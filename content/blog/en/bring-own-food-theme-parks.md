We compared the house rules and website FAQs of fifteen parks in Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium and France.

Heide Park, Efteling, Toverland, Walibi Holland, Futuroscope and Disneyland Paris explicitly allow your own food.
Disneyland Paris rules out anything you'd need a cooler or a table for, though.

At Bobbejaanland, Walibi Belgium, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland you can picnic, but only in the areas the park
sets aside for it.

There's nothing about food from home in the rules of Europa-Park, Phantasialand, Hansa-Park, Legoland Deutschland and
Movie Park. What they ban is grills, cooking equipment or alcohol.

"No rule" means the house rules neither ban your own food nor allow it.

## Germany

### Europa-Park

Yes, you can bring food and drinks into [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park): the park rules don't ban them. Grilling and cooking
equipment are banned, and so are large amounts of alcohol and high-proof spirits. Large suitcases go in the lockers at
the main entrance.

The park lists picnic areas on its page of visitor tips: in the historic Balthasar castle park in the Germany themed
area, next to the Wodan Timburcoaster.

At the Rulantica water park, on the other hand, food from home is banned.

In the Europa-Park & Rulantica app you can order food and drinks ahead, pay in the app and collect the order at the time you chose. It works at the snack bars CMAK Russian Food & Burger and Moby Dick.

### Phantasialand

Food and soft drinks are fine at [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand). Alcohol stays out, in any packaging.

At the entrance, the park uses detectors and checks bags at random. Its list of banned items
includes knives and scissors, pepper spray, pyrotechnics, weapons, skateboards, inline skates, bicycles and Segways.
Alcohol in any packaging is on it too, so a sealed can of beer counts.

Food, water bottles, coolers and glass aren't on the list.

At Restaurant Uhrwerk in Rookburgh, The Navigator chicken burger with fries costs €26.00 and a 0.2-litre Coca-Cola €3.30. The €14.00 kids' burger is only sold together with an adult dish. At Rutmor's Taverne, the Vesperbrett platter for one costs €21.50.

Next to Guest Relations on Kaiserplatz in Berlin there is a nursing room, and staff help with warming up baby food.

### Heide Park

In its help center, [Heide Park](ref:heide-park) says you can bring your food
for the day and eat it in the park. Glass bottles are the one thing it asks you to leave at home. Under the park rules,
grilling is banned across the whole site.

If you have a severe intolerance such as coeliac disease, the park recommends bringing your own food. Its kitchens
can't fully rule out cross-contamination, and they don't take special requests.

Staff warm up baby food for free at Biergarten Rafting, Wild Wild Waffel, Mucho Pollo and Frau Mümmels Kiosk. According to the park, almost every restaurant has vegetarian dishes, Hot Dog Freestyle sells a vegan hot dog and Piratenburger a plant-based burger.

### Hansa-Park

The park rules of [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park) ban grilling, grills, cooking equipment, alcohol and drugs. They say nothing about food from home. Glass bottles are on the rules' list of dangerous items, alongside
knives and laser pointers.

You can't eat or drink on the rides or in the indoor shows. That applies to every guest, including food bought in the
park.

Picnic areas are in Wikingerland next to Viking Odin and between Elins Luftreise and the Wikinger-Bootsfahrt, next to the Skandinaviske Hytter opposite Pow Wow, at Peterhof station within earshot of the Bärenkapelle, and in Roanoke. The park also offers the Skandinaviske Hytter themselves, huts right next to Schwur des Kärnan, as a place to rest.

### Legoland Deutschland

The park and business rules of [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland) are part of the terms and conditions on
legoland.de. They ban open fire and grills anywhere in the resort, and your own cooking appliances in the accommodation.
There's nothing in them about food, drinks, glass or cooler bags in the park.

There is a picnic lawn with benches, number 45 on the park map. The first aid station is there too, between the observation tower and Drachenjagd. The mobile stands take cash only, the restaurants cards as well. The park's Baby Service sells baby and toddler food.

### Movie Park Germany

[Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany) doesn't stop you bringing food and soft drinks either. Under the park rules, you can't bring alcohol, and grilling is banned
across the whole site. There's nothing in the rules about food from home. Large suitcases and other bulky items stay outside.

The Halloween Horror Festival has rules of its own. According to the festival FAQ, alcohol and any drink in a glass
bottle are banned, and so is spray deodorant, while roll-on and stick are fine. People and bags are checked before
entry.

At the Coca-Cola Freestyle stations you fill a cup you've bought yourself. The cup is redeemed at Studio Sip, the drink station in the Hollywood Street Set. Some restaurants take cards only.

The crew warms up food and baby food at Hollywood Snack, the Bakery, Subway and the Langnese Happiness Station, and at Trattoria Hollywood only with a reservation.

### Plopsaland Deutschland

At [Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland), picnics are allowed only in the areas the park provides for
them. Under the park regulations you can't bring bulk packs of food or drink, and there's no size given for a bulk pack.

Grilling, picnicking and camping are banned on the rest of the grounds and in the car parks. You can't eat or drink on
rides or in queues, and you can't bring alcohol.

Its FAQ lets you eat small amounts of your own food outside the restaurants, on a park bench for example, but not inside the restaurants or on their outdoor areas. Baby food and the birthday cake of a booked children's party are exceptions. Microwaves for baby food are in the restaurants Pfalzgraf and Maja-Burger and in Plopsa Indoor.

You may leave the park briefly to fetch something from the car and get back in the same day with a stamp on your hand.

## The Netherlands

### Efteling

Under the heading "Eigen eten en drinken" (own food and drink), the park regulations of [Efteling](ref:efteling) allow you to bring your own food and drink. The park's FAQ has the same line.

When it's busy, the park asks you to eat what you've brought at one of the picnic areas, away from the restaurants and
their terraces.

You can refill your water bottle for free at taps in the park, for example in the Symbolica queue or at De Ganzenhoedster on the Anton Pieck Plein. There are more taps at the Kleine Boodschap and 't Gemack toilets and near Fata Morgana, and the water from the taps in the toilet buildings is drinking water too.

As a shady spot for a picnic, the park names a narrow path next to Het Spookslot. If you want to fetch something from the cloakroom by the car park in between, get a stamp on the way out.

### Toverland

[Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) allows your own food and has two microwaves you can use for free, at the
Waldstube and at Katara Plaza. The park mentions them for warming baby bottles too.

The park regulations are available at the entrance and as a download on the website.

Drinks come in reusable cups with a €1.50 deposit. You return cups, bottles and cans at deposit machines in four places in the park.

### Walibi Holland

[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) explicitly allows your own food and drink and marks picnic areas in the park. Under
the park rules you can't bring glass or alcohol. Everything off the paths and picnic areas is closed off.

Several restaurants have microwaves for warming bottles and food.

## Belgium

### Bobbejaanland

At [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland), the park regulations have you eat what you've brought at the picnic areas in the
park. Large packs of food and drink need the management's permission.

You can't take food or drinks into rides or shops. Camping, grilling and picnicking are banned in the car park.

Drinks come in reusable cups with a €2 deposit, refunded when you return the cup. You may also keep the cups.

### Walibi Belgium

The park regulations of [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) have an article of their own on picnics. They're allowed in
the areas marked on the park map and banned in the restaurants and on their terraces.

Alcohol is banned in the park. Bags, boxes and containers can be 55 cm wide, 40 cm long and 20 cm deep at most, and a
cooler has to fit within those limits too. Eating and drinking are banned on rides and in queues.

The restaurants have self-service microwaves for warming bottles and baby food.

## France

### Parc Astérix

[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) allows picnics only in the zones signposted for them. Glass objects are banned, and so
are alcohol the park didn't sell you and any kitchen appliance. The park explicitly names stoves, grills and coolers.

You can't eat or drink on rides, for safety reasons, or in queues, for reasons of hygiene.

If you want to leave the park for a short while, tell the staff at the exit checkpoint first and get a stamp on your arm. Without it you cannot get back in that day.

### Disneyland Paris

[Disneyland Paris](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park) allows your own food. Meals
that need a cooler, a table or bulky containers are banned in Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World (Walt Disney Studios Park until 29 March 2026). For a picnic
like that, there's a picnic area between the car park and the entrance.

Glass bottles and alcohol are on the list of items that stay outside. You can sit at the restaurants' outdoor tables,
but guests who bought their food there come first. On rides, in theatres and in queues, the park asks you not to eat or
drink.

### Futuroscope

According to its FAQ, you can picnic on any lawn at [Futuroscope](ref:futuroscope).

Rigid coolers and glass are banned, and so is alcohol you didn't buy in the park. Bulky bags should stay outside, and bags are checked at the entrance. Because you're allowed to leave the park during the day and come back in, the cooler can stay in the car.

Restaurants serve food and drinks on reusable crockery. Every restaurant has a children's menu and vegetarian and gluten-free dishes. Futuroscope does not accept meal vouchers such as Ticket Restaurant or Chèque déjeuner.

## The water parks

All four water parks whose rules we compared ban food from home.

| Water park                | Food from home                                                                      | Park's rules                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rulantica (Europa-Park)   | banned for reasons of hygiene                                                       | [Swimming and Car Park Rules](https://www.europapark.de/en/rulantica/info/safety/swimming-and-car-park-rules-and-regulations)       |
| Aqualibi (Walibi Belgium) | picnics, food and drink strictly banned, except in the restaurant area              | [Park regulations](https://www.walibi.be/en/regulations/park-regulations)                                                           |

Rulantica also bans glass bottles, porcelain and jars, and drinks without a sturdy container have to stay in the food
and drink areas. At the Aquascope, every exit is final, even for a break at the car.

## What goes in the backpack

Pack sandwiches, fruit and snacks in a backpack or a soft cooler bag. Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris ban coolers,
Futuroscope bans rigid ones, and Walibi Belgium only lets in bags up to 55 × 40 × 20 cm. For drinks, take a plastic or
metal bottle, because glass is banned in six of the fifteen parks and in Rulantica.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you bring your own food into a theme park?

At most parks, yes. Heide Park, Efteling, Toverland, Walibi Holland, Futuroscope and Disneyland Paris explicitly allow
it, Disneyland Paris without a cooler or a table. Bobbejaanland, Walibi Belgium, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland
allow picnics at designated areas. There's nothing about it in the rules of Europa-Park, Phantasialand, Hansa-Park,
Legoland Deutschland and Movie Park.

### Can you bring food and drinks into Europa-Park?

Yes. The park rules only ban grills and cooking equipment, large amounts of alcohol and high-proof spirits. There are
picnic spots in the Historic Balthasar Castle Park in the German themed area, next to Wodan Timburcoaster. In the
Rulantica water park, food from home is banned.

### Can you bring food and drinks into Phantasialand?

Yes, just no alcohol, not even the sealed beer sitting so innocently in your backpack. Bags are checked at the entrance,
with detectors and at random.

### Can you bring food and drinks into Movie Park Germany?

Yes, except alcohol. During the Halloween Horror Festival, all drinks in glass bottles stay out as well, and bags are
checked before entry.

### Can you take glass bottles into a theme park?

Not at Heide Park, Hansa-Park, Walibi Holland, Parc Astérix, Disneyland Paris, Futuroscope or Europa-Park's Rulantica water park,
and not at the Halloween Horror Festival at Movie Park either. The other parks don't mention glass in their rules. None
of the fifteen parks bans a plastic or metal water bottle.

### Are coolers allowed in theme parks?

Not at Parc Astérix, Disneyland Paris or Futuroscope. At Futuroscope the ban covers rigid coolers. Walibi Belgium
doesn't let in bags or boxes larger than 55 × 40 × 20 cm. The other parks don't mention coolers.

### Can you bring your own food into a water park?

No. Rulantica, Aqualibi, Plopsaqua De Panne and Futuroscope's Aquascope all ban food from home. Rulantica and Plopsaqua
give hygiene as the reason.

### Where can you eat food you've brought?

At the picnic areas. That's the rule at Bobbejaanland, Walibi Belgium, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland, and at
Efteling the park asks for it when it's busy. Walibi Holland marks picnic areas and closes off everything away from the
paths. Disneyland Paris has a picnic area between the car park and the entrance. At Futuroscope you can picnic on any
lawn.

### Can you bring alcohol into a theme park?

Mostly not. Phantasialand, Hansa-Park, Movie Park, Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland, Walibi Belgium and
Disneyland Paris ban it. Parc Astérix and Futuroscope ban alcohol they didn't sell themselves, and Europa-Park bans large amounts and
high-proof spirits. At Phantasialand the ban covers unopened bottles and cans too.

### Can you grill at a theme park?

No. Every park with a rule on it bans grilling or cooking equipment: Europa-Park, Heide Park, Hansa-Park, Legoland
Deutschland, Movie Park, Plopsaland Deutschland, Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris. At Bobbejaanland and Walibi Belgium,
grilling is banned in the car park as well.

### Where can you find a park's rules?

In the house rules, which go by Parkordnung, Parkreglement, park rules or Règlement intérieur depending on the park, and
often in the FAQ on the park's website too.

### Where can you warm up baby food at a theme park?

Toverland, Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland and Walibi Belgium have microwaves for it, free at Toverland. At Heide Park, Movie Park and Phantasialand staff warm it up for you, at Heide Park also for free.

### How much do lockers cost at a theme park?

Between €1 for a small locker at Europa-Park and €8 for a large one at Movie Park Germany. A common price is €5 for a small locker and €7 for a large one.
