[Efteling](ref:efteling) is selling an evening ticket for Winter Efteling at 33 euros, valid from 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. Also new are a show in a mirror tent, a caterpillar ride and a partly covered ice rink. Winter Efteling runs from Monday, 16 November 2026, to 31 January 2027. The park published the programme on 8 October 2026.

## The evening ticket is valid from 4 pm

The evening ticket is available from 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. It's valid from 4 pm until closing and costs 33 euros. It isn't valid on 31 December.

Anyone driving pays 10 euros for parking if they buy the parking ticket together with the evening ticket. Otherwise parking costs 15 euros a day, according to the park's parking page. The announcement puts day tickets at 40 to 56 euros and doesn't say which price applies on which day. The evening ticket is 7 euros cheaper than the cheapest day ticket and up to 23 euros cheaper than the most expensive.

Over the Christmas holidays the park stays open until 8 pm, except on 31 December. Arriving at 4 pm leaves time until 8 pm. According to the parking page, you can enter the car park until an hour and a half before closing. Otherwise the park opens from 11 am to 6 pm at the least, often from 10 am to 7 pm. Tickets for visits up to 31 December are on sale, and the dates from January follow later.

## New Year's Eve is separate

Efteling is holding its own Oud & Nieuw on 31 December. Tickets aren't on sale yet; the park's page lets you sign up by email to be notified. The accommodations at Efteling are fully booked for that night.

Guests staying overnight get unlimited access to Winter Efteling. The park also offers Christmas packages with breakfast, dinner or both.

## Cirque Miragio and Cirque Papillon

The new show Cirque Miragio runs on the Warme Winter Weide. It's on daily, lasts 20 minutes and is a musical acrobatics show in a nostalgic mirror tent with seating. It tells what happens to the wicked queen after the Snow White fairy tale. The park says nothing about showtimes.

The caterpillar ride Cirque Papillon is new, too. The announcement gives no age limit or minimum height.

The ice rink is called De Gladde Piste. It's new and partly covered, and the skating tent has seating. The park hasn't published what skating costs.

## The rest of the winter programme

The Winterdorp shows new and earlier Luville miniatures. For photos there's a sleigh with Pardijn, several chalets run prize games, and there are winter food specials that are only on offer during this period, plus souvenirs. At the fire pit you can see the nine-metre-high Wonder Winterboom.

Pardoes greets guests in a new covered pavilion in the Hartenhof area, on the square in front of Symbolica. The Nikkelen Nelissen from Symbolica join him. The Carrousel Theater shows "De Man met de Toverlantaarn" until 3 January 2027, a musical with singing, puppets and visual effects. On the Carnaval Festivalplein, Jokie & Jet perform their winter show, and on the Max & Moritzplein there's the Anderberg Liederfestival with Frau Schmetterling and Bakker Krümel. The Sprookjesbos adds a winter walk, snow-covered mushrooms for photos and the Sprookjesboom winter show in the open-air theatre.

On the Anton Pieckplein, the Anton Pieck Molen runs, along with the Carolling Carousel trio and the game "Uyt den Losse Pols".
