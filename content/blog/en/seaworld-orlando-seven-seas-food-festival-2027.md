[SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando) is putting on 29 concerts at Bayside Stadium for the Seven Seas Food Festival, from January 30 to May 16, 2027. There's a show every Saturday and on most Sundays, always at 7 pm. The concerts come with park admission, but only a reservation guarantees you a spot. Flo Rida opens the lineup, which SeaWorld announced on November 19.

## Festival and concert dates

The festival itself starts the day before the first concert, on Friday, January 29, and runs until Sunday, May 16. The food booths open only on selected days, Thursday through Sunday in 2026, and SeaWorld hasn't published the 2027 list yet. The park closes at 6 pm on the opening Friday.

A set usually runs 60 to 75 minutes. After that, the park stays open until 9 pm on the first concert weekend and most concert nights in February and March, and until 10 pm on the Sundays in March. February 13 and 27 are shorter: SeaWorld closes at 7 pm, just as the concert starts, and at 8 pm on March 27.

SeaWorld knows better than to book against football, the Easter Bunny or Mom, so there's no show on Super Bowl Sunday (February 14), Easter Sunday (March 28) or Mother's Day (May 9).

The festival marks Mardi Gras (Tuesday, February 9), St. Patrick's Day (Wednesday, March 17) and Cinco de Mayo (Wednesday, May 5) midweek, on days without a concert. Viva La Música adds a few days in May.

## Open and reserved seating

With a park ticket or an annual pass, you can sit in the stadium's open seats if you get there early enough. Once it's full, entry can close. Reserved Concert Access is sold on top of your park ticket, in three tiers:

- Standard from $9.99, a seat in the middle of the lower bowl
- Up-Close from $14.99, the second section on the floor behind Stagefront, with access to the Floor Bar
- Stagefront from $24.99, the front section on the floor right in front of the stage, also with the Floor Bar

Once a night's allocation sells out, you're left with the open seats, for as long as entry stays open. Check-in for all three tiers is to the left of the stage, near Pipeline: The Surf Coaster, and opens at 2 pm. Spots aren't numbered, so within your section it's first come, first served again. At 13 concerts, among them Flo Rida, Shaggy, Lil Jon and Luísa Sonza, Stagefront and Up-Close are standing areas without chairs. Drinks at the Floor Bar cost extra.

Rain alone doesn't end a concert. If a thunderstorm moves in, the show is paused, pushed back or moved indoors, to the Nautilus Theater for example. It can get tighter there, but anyone with a reserved spot is guaranteed to get in.

The Seven Seas VIP Tour also comes with a reserved spot, plus a lanyard good for five festival samples, food or drinks, and unlimited priority access to the rides. There's no 2027 price yet, and in 2026 the tour started at $199.99.

## Tickets, annual passes and blockout dates

In the Black Friday sale, a single-day ticket costs from $61.99 online instead of $151.99, for anyone three and older. The 2027 Fun Card costs $104.99 instead of $151.99 and is valid on every day except blockout dates. Two day tickets cost $123.98 online, so the card pays off from the second visit.

Among the annual passes, what matters for the festival is the gap between Bronze at $171 (or $14.25 a month), which has blockout dates, and Silver at $198, which doesn't. During the festival, Bronze and the Fun Card are blocked out on the four concert Saturdays from March 13 to April 3. If you hold Bronze and need more cowbell from Blue Öyster Cult, you'll need a day ticket that night, or Silver. Food and drink discounts are 5 percent with Silver, 10 with Gold ($222) and 15 percent with Platinum ($351), and none with Bronze.

Parking costs from $35, and the entrance booths don't take cash. From I-4, Central Florida Parkway takes you to 7007 Sea World Drive, and without a car, Lynx bus route 50 stops nearby. Our guide [Planning Disney World and Universal Orlando in one trip](/blog/orlando-itinerary-disney-universal) covers combo tickets and how to fit SeaWorld in alongside Disney and Universal.

## What's changed since 2026

The 2026 lineup had 32 dates, five of them with a surprise guest, and there were shows on seven Fridays too. In 2027 it's weekends only. Besides Flo Rida, Grupo Manía, Proyecto Uno and Soulja Boy are back from 2026. The reserved spots were called Reserved Seating in 2026 and also started at $9.99.

Park president Brad Gilmour says some of the 2027 dishes will take their cue from the music. SeaWorld will announce the 2027 booths and menus separately. In 2026, more than 200 dishes and drinks were spread across 29 booths, eight of them new.

## Concerts in San Diego and San Antonio, SEAQuest in Orlando

With the sister parks, the 2027 series adds up to more than 50 concerts. [SeaWorld San Diego](ref:seaworld-san-diego) has concerts on Saturdays at 6 pm at Bayside Amphitheater from March 20 to August 28, with reserved seats there from $69.99. [SeaWorld San Antonio](ref:seaworld-san-antonio) has seven dates on Saturdays and Sundays from April 11 to May 23 at Nautilus Amphitheater, included with admission as in Orlando.

Orlando is also set to open SEAQuest: Legends of the Deep in 2027, a suspended dark ride in an expanded area of the park. Through Passport to Thrills, Pass Members who visit three times between November 18 and December 31 get a one-time Quick Queue for SEAQuest. Make those three visits in December and you'll also catch the [Christmas Celebration](/news/seaworld-orlando-christmas-celebration-2026) with the orca show Miracles, which runs until January 4.
