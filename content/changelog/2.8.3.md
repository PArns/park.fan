## New

- A maintenance page instead of an error when our data service can't be reached (28 May).

## Improved

- The rain warning is calm while the rain is still coming and says to find shelter once it is
  close (2 June).
- Upcoming quiet days are spread over the next 30 days, one or two a week, and a day that has
  passed is never recommended (2 and 3 June).
- Safari remembers that you allowed your location and stops asking on every page.
- The park's time zone sits in the opening-hours card, and the live ticker is left out on phones.
