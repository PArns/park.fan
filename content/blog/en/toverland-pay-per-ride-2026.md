[Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum won't charge admission on 15 days between 28 November 2026 and 17 January 2027. On those Wednesdays and weekend days you pay only for the rides, with strips at €2 each. The park calls it Pay per Ride. Depending on the ride, a go costs one, two or three strips. Free entry is only for those who've reserved one of the free places beforehand.

## Dates and opening hours

Pay per Ride runs on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays only, in one block before the Christmas holidays and one after:

- November: Saturday 28 and Sunday 29
- December: Wednesday 2, Saturday 5, Sunday 6, Wednesday 9, Saturday 12, Sunday 13 and Wednesday 16
- January 2027: Wednesday 6, Saturday 9, Sunday 10, Wednesday 13, Saturday 16 and Sunday 17

On Wednesdays the park opens from 11 am to 6 pm, at weekends from 11 am to 7 pm.

## What a ride costs

- 3 strips (€6): [Troy](ref:attractiepark-toverland/troy), [Booster Bike](ref:attractiepark-toverland/booster-bike), [Fēnix](ref:attractiepark-toverland/fenix), [Dwervelwind](ref:attractiepark-toverland/dwervelwind) and the [ice rink](ref:attractiepark-toverland/schaatsbaan)
- 2 strips (€4): Toos-Express, Scorpios, Merlin's Quest, Dragonwatch, Expedition Zork, Djengu River, Pixarus and the Toos & Morrel show
- 1 strip (€2): Paarden van Ithaka, Morrels Truckjes, Vliegend Tapijt, Djinn, Wirbelbaum, Karussell, Tolly Molly, Jumping Juna, Drakenslangen, Theekopjes, Klokhuis, Alpenrutsche, Villa Fiasko, Coco Bolo, Garden Tour and Tubingbaan

So a session on the ice costs the same as a ride on the wooden coaster.

The strip shop lets you pick any amount and suggests 10 strips for €20. From 25 strips the price drops to €1.90 a strip, and from 50 to €1.85. Every card in one order counts towards the discount, so a family that orders together reaches it sooner than four people ordering separately.

## Buying and using strips

You can buy strips online in the strip shop, in the Toverland app on the day of your visit and in the souvenir shops inside the park. Once you've paid, the card arrives by email and can go into your phone's wallet. In the park you can top it up and pass strips to another card by its card number, say to the child who suddenly wants one more go on Booster Bike. Strips are checked at the start of the queue or by the operator at the ride.

Strips aren't tied to a date and are valid for the whole period. Toverland won't swap them back for cash, so anyone who bought too many in December rides off the rest in January.

## Reservation

On these days strips won't get you into the park without a reservation. It costs €0.00, covers up to 15 people per booking and doesn't include a single ride. You book it at toverland.com/payperride. Places are limited each day, and Toverland hasn't announced how many there are. If your day is full, a regular day ticket still gets you in.

## Day tickets, companions and Magic Members

Toverland keeps selling its regular day ticket on Pay per Ride days, with unlimited rides, shows and winter activities. In the calendar, a Wednesday ticket costs €37.00 for adults and children from 140 cm, €30.00 for children from 90 to 140 cm and €34.50 for visitors aged 60 and over. At weekends it's €43.00, €34.50 and €40.50. Children under 90 cm go in free. Buying at least seven days ahead saves up to €7.

An adult day ticket on a Wednesday costs as much as 18.5 strips, a little over six rides on the big attractions. At weekends it's 21.5 strips, a little over seven big rides. If you want more big rides than that, the day ticket comes out cheaper. The discount from 25 strips moves that line up a bit.

In a group, day tickets and Pay per Ride can be mixed. If you go with a child who's only allowed on a ride with an adult because of the height limit, you'll need a day ticket or strips yourself. Birthday and return tickets, and tickets from the spring offer "2. Besuch gratis" (second visit free), stay all-in day tickets.

Magic Members with a valid pass ride as much as they like on Pay per Ride days at no extra cost and need neither a reservation nor strips. Parking isn't part of the pass. A parking pass costs €50 a year or €4.50 a month.

## Parking and getting there

Only the entry is free. A parking ticket costs €13.50 online in the ticket shop, and you can also buy one at the machines by the main entrance. The address for your satnav is Toverlaan 2, 5975 MR Sevenum.

Without a car, take the train to Horst-Sevenum station and then bus 79 or 679 to the Toverland stop, across the road from the park.

## Winter Feelings and the Christmas holidays

The Pay per Ride days are part of Winter Feelings, the park's winter season from 28 November 2026 to 17 January 2027. The whole park is decorated for winter, six themed areas get lights, and Port Laguna becomes the winter hub with the winter activities. Toverland has also announced winter food and extra entertainment acts.

From 19 December to 3 January Toverland opens every day, mostly until 7 or 8 pm, and until 6 pm on 24 and 31 December and 1 January. Pay per Ride skips exactly those two weeks, when most people would have the time. Then you need a regular all-in ticket to get in. ## 25 years of Toverland

Toverland welcomed its first visitors on 19 May 2001 and turns 25 in 2026. Pay per Ride falls in the last weeks of that anniversary year, and the January block runs into the next one. We've written up how the park has grown since then, and the time of day when Troy's queue is shortest, in our [Toverland guide](/blog/toverland-troy-wait-times-tips).
