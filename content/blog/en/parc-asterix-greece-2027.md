[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) is expanding Grèce, its ancient Greece area, by spring 2027. The plans cover two new family rides, a playground, a big restaurant and two kiosks, the park announced in Plailly on 2 November. The same area is home to the wooden coaster [Tonnerre 2 Zeus](ref:parc-asterix/tonnerre-2-zeus?bare), and that's getting new track along its whole layout in 2027.

## Two rides and a playground

Tournikotis Tournikotas is a family ride with two drop towers, and you'll need a couple of attempts before the name comes out right. Riders get several goes too, up and down a few times per ride. On La Flotte de Poséidon you sit in one of eight boats that spin while they climb a central column.

The park hasn't announced who's building the two rides or where exactly in Grèce they'll go. Between them they're meant to carry 800 people an hour. For the youngest visitors there's also L'Aire de Jeu d'Ulysse, a playground themed to match the area.

## Dinner with Dionysus

The new restaurant is called La Table de Dionysos. It has table service and 460 seats, 350 inside and 110 on the terrace. The existing Taverne de Dionysos is being renamed La Taverne de Poséidon, which leaves Poseidon with his own fleet of boats and his own place to eat in Grèce.

The first kiosk, Chez Nikos Bouldeglas, sells handmade ice cream. The second kiosk, Elis, serves granita. Elis is a name the area already uses, for the ride La Rivière d'Elis.

## Tickets and opening days

The 2027 season starts on Saturday 27 March, with the park open from 10 am to 7 pm. It's open from 27 to 29 March, closed from 30 March to 2 April, and open daily from 3 April until early September. The park hasn't published day ticket prices for 2027 yet. [Hotel L'Odyssée](/news/parc-asterix-hotel-odyssee-2027) opens a day before the season, on 26 March 2027.

This year the park is closed from 9 November to 18 December. Before and after that, it's open on these days:

- Saturday 7 November, Peur sur le Parc: 9 am to 6 pm, plus a night opening from 7 pm to 1 am
- Sunday 8 November, the last day of Peur sur le Parc
- Noël Gaulois from 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027: daily from 11 am to 8 pm, until 7 pm on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve

## New track for Tonnerre 2 Zeus

Over the winter of 2025/26, Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC) replaced around 350 metres of the track. The park aims to finish that work this season. In the 2027 season, the ride then gets new track along its whole layout. The park hasn't announced whether Tonnerre 2 Zeus will close for part of the 2027 season while that happens.

The coaster opened on 5 April 1997 as Tonnerre de Zeus, built by Custom Coasters International. The old version ran for the last time on 7 November 2021. From the winter of 2019, The Gravity Group had relaid the track in stages and taken out the double helix. The ride came back with new Gravitykraft trains on 9 April 2022. Since then there's been a 2 in the name, which in French sounds almost like the old "de". It's 30 metres tall, and you can ride on your own from 1.20 metres.

The park is 35 kilometres north of Paris and has five themed worlds: Gaul, the Roman Empire, ancient Greece, the Vikings and Egypt. Grèce currently has seven attractions. Besides Tonnerre 2 Zeus, they're [Pégase Express](ref:parc-asterix/pegase-express?bare), [Discobélix](ref:parc-asterix/discobelix?bare), [Le Vol d'Icare](ref:parc-asterix/le-vol-dicare?bare), [Le Cheval de Troie](ref:parc-asterix/le-cheval-de-troie?bare), [L'Hydre de Lerne](ref:parc-asterix/lhydre-de-lerne?bare) and [La Rivière d'Elis](ref:parc-asterix/la-riviere-delis?bare). The two new ones bring that to nine. Which hour gives you the shortest queue at which ride is something our [Parc Astérix guide](/blog/parc-asterix-wait-times-tips) goes through area by area, along with how to get to Plailly.

## More plans up to 2030

The park plans to invest €250 million by 2030. It reworked Egypt in 2026: the Oxygénarium became La Descente du Nil, and the Chaises Volantes became L'Envol d'Ibis. Londinium is announced for 2028, a covered area based on the album "Asterix in Britain", with a big roller coaster and an interactive family dark ride. Once every project is done, the park should have 20 percent more capacity, two thirds of it under cover.
