On 18 November Disneyland in Anaheim announced the dates for Disneyland After Dark 2027: four separately ticketed parties at [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park) between January and June. Sweethearts' Nite, Star Wars Nite and Pride Nite are back, and Disneylandia Nite joins them in April. Sweethearts' Nite goes on sale first, to everyone from 10 December. Disney announced the line-up on the Disney Parks Blog.

## The nights

- Sweethearts' Nite: 24, 26, 28 and 31 January, 2, 4, 7, 9, 11 and 16 February
- Disneylandia Nite: 11, 13 and 15 April
- Star Wars Nite: 27 and 29 April, 4 and 6 May
- Pride Nite: 17 June

All 18 nights fall on a Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday. Valentine's Day isn't one of them, even though 14 February 2027 is a Sunday. If you want to take your sweetheart to the park that day, you'll need a regular day ticket; the last Sweethearts' Nite comes two days later.

## Hours and what the ticket includes

Each party runs from 9 p.m. to 1 a.m. local time, after the park has closed to day guests. A party ticket gets you in from 6 p.m., though, so you can spend seven hours in the park, a full shift for an evening ticket. For the first three of them the park is still running its normal day.

The ticket covers every digital Disney PhotoPass photo taken of you during the party, with no cap. You collect them by scanning the QR code on your wristband in the Disneyland app. You also get a party map, meant as a keepsake. Only selected attractions are open, and Disney often promises shorter waits on them. Disney hasn't said yet which rides those will be in 2027.

The announcement doesn't give a price. A ticket is valid only on the date you booked and for the hours of the party, and each night has a limited number of tickets.

## Tickets for Sweethearts' Nite

Sales run through Disneyland.com, and for now only Sweethearts' Nite is on sale. On Tuesday, 8 December, from 9 a.m. California time, Inspire Key holders can buy from a small allocation; on Wednesday, 9 December, every Magic Key holder, also from a limited allocation. General sale opens on Thursday, 10 December, at 9 a.m. Pacific, which is 6 p.m. CET. Disney plans to announce on-sale dates and details for Disneylandia Nite and Star Wars Nite in early 2027, and for Pride Nite in the spring.

## Sweethearts' Nite

Disney has couples in mind, but says just as clearly that a best friend or the family counts too. On Main Street, U.S.A. the "Celebrate Love Cavalcade" rolls past with Mickey, Minnie and their friends. In front of Sleeping Beauty Castle, singers perform love songs from Disney films live in "Once Upon a Dream", and afterwards you can meet royal couples there. Both are coming back.

If you'd rather sing yourself, the Golden Horseshoe in Frontierland runs "Disney Duets", a karaoke for two. At Tomorrowland Terrace a DJ plays the Valentine's Dance Party, with Disney characters on the dance floor. For something slower, board the [Mark Twain](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/mark-twain-riverboat?bare), which becomes the Lou-Ray Jazz Club and cruises the Rivers of America in the dark with jazz on board. Disney projects WALL-E and EVE onto the front of [Space Mountain](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/space-mountain?bare), and photo backdrops around the park show "Aladdin", "The Aristocats", "Tangled" and "Lady and the Tramp".

The party merchandise, including a hoodie, a headband with a Minnie-style bow and a tumbler, is sold next to a smugglers' ship of all places: you'll find it at the [Millennium Falcon](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/millennium-falcon-smugglers-run?bare) in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge.

New on the menus are fries with chicken and za'atar, at Galactic Grill among others, and fried Hawaiian rolls at Little Red Wagon. Blue Bayou, River Belle Terrace and Carnation Café serve their own party dishes. Disney recommends booking those, with online reservations opening closer to the dates. Specialty items from the quick-service spots can be ordered through mobile order in the app before the party starts.

## Disneylandia Nite and 50 years of Star Wars

Disneylandia Nite celebrates Hispanic and Latin American cultures, with characters from "Coco" and "Encanto". It sits on almost the same April dates as Disney Channel Nite did in 2026, which is missing from Disney's 2027 list.

Star Wars Nite in 2027 marks 50 years of Star Wars, and one of its four nights falls on 4 May. Disney asks guests to come in Star Wars-style costumes, within the park's costume rules.

Pride Nite on 17 June celebrates the LGBTQIA+ community, with rainbow projections around the park. It started in 2023, ran on two nights in 2026 and has one in 2027.

## Fewer parties than in 2026

Disneyland held five After Dark parties in 2026. "70 Years of Favorites" was new in March, on two nights, and Disney Channel Nite ran in April. Neither is on the 2027 calendar; Disneylandia Nite takes their place.

If you're planning a trip around Southern California, the [California itinerary guide](/blog/california-itinerary-disneyland-universal-san-diego) puts the party nights at Disneyland, Universal, Knott's and SeaWorld San Diego side by side, with the time day guests have to leave at each.
