## New

- The homepage counted parks, themed areas and rides, ranked the busiest and quietest parks and the
  longest and shortest waits, and broke the numbers down by continent and by the top five
  countries. A refresh button fetched them again from api.park.fan, which already supplied all of
  it.
- Below the dashboard sat a tree of pages from continent to country to park to ride. A park page
  listed its themed areas with a wait badge on each ride, how many rides were open, the crowd
  level and the current weather.
- Waits showed as "Walk On", a number of minutes or "Closed", in five colour bands that ended at
  120 minutes and more.
- On 25 June a search field in the header started suggesting parks and rides from the third
  letter on, with country flags, arrow keys and `/` to jump into it.
- A light, dark and system theme, and the version number in the footer, were there from the first
  dashboard on.

## Fixed

- Park and country URLs that the API did not resolve returned a 404; the page now looked the park
  up through the search instead (26 June).
