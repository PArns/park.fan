[Gardaland](ref:gardaland) in Castelnuovo del Garda is the biggest theme park in Italy. It opened in 1975, belongs to Merlin Entertainments, and according to the Themed Entertainment Association drew about three million visits in 2024, which is seventh place in Europe.

The park’s big coasters are [Oblivion](ref:gardaland/oblivion-the-black-hole), a dive coaster with an 87-degree first drop, [Raptor](ref:gardaland/raptor), the prototype of Bolliger & Mabillard’s wing coasters, and [Blue Tornado](ref:gardaland/blue-tornado), an inverted coaster with five inversions. We went through the last twelve months of wait times, and at most rides the queues stay short.

## From Livio Furini to Merlin

In the early 1970s Livio Furini, a businessman from Verona, wanted to build a theme park on Lake Garda. He had been inspired by Edenlandia in Naples and a visit to Disneyland in California. In 1971 he bought a 60,000-square-metre plot between Lazise and Castelnuovo del Garda, on the shore of the lake.

The park opened on 19 July 1975. There were 21 attractions on opening day, including a fishing pond, and about 100,000 visitors came in the first year. Admission was 1,750 lire. The narrow-gauge railway TransGardalandExpress is still there and is the only attraction left from the opening year. Furini himself stepped down as chairman in 1975 after disputes with the other shareholders.

The park grew fast in the eighties. Fininvest, Silvio Berlusconi’s media company, advertised it on television, and from 1984 to 1992 it ran an entertainment show hosted by Paolo Bonolis. In 1985 the steel coaster Magic Mountain, the first in Italy, opened together with Colorado Boat, a log flume from Mack Rides. The mascot Prezzemolo, a young dragon who still turns up all over the park, dates from the same period.

The nineties added the pirate area with the dark ride I Corsari (1992), Blue Tornado and the drop tower Space Vertigo (both 1998) and the river rapids Jungle Rapids (1999). Fuga da Atlantide followed in 2003 and the Gardaland Hotel, the first themed hotel in Italy, in 2004. Merlin Entertainments took over in October 2006, and in 2008 the SEA LIFE Aquarium and the coaster Mammut opened.

Since then Merlin has been turning the park into a resort: Raptor (2011), Oblivion (28 March 2015), the Adventure Hotel (2016), the Magic Hotel (2019) and the LEGOLAND Water Park (2021). The pandemic interrupted that for a year. Gardaland reopened on 13 June 2020, the first Italian theme park to do so, with a cap of 10,000 guests a day.

Attractions based on the film "Jumanji" have stood there since 2022. In 2024 the drop-and-twist tower Wolf Legend replaced the coaster Sequoia Magic Loop, which was taken down in autumn 2023. In 2025 the park celebrated its 50th birthday with Animal Treasure Island, a water dark ride on 6,000 square metres into which Merlin put about ten million euros.

### Oblivion: The Black Hole

[Oblivion](ref:gardaland/oblivion-the-black-hole?full)

At the top the train hangs over the edge for a moment, then drops 42.5 metres at an angle of 87 degrees, into a tunnel. It’s a dive coaster from Bolliger & Mabillard with seats in a single row, six people side by side, 18 per train. The track is short at 566 metres. After the drop come an Immelmann and a heartline roll, and the ride is over after about a minute.

Oblivion opened on 28 March 2015, for the park’s 40th birthday. According to English Wikipedia, attendance rose after the opening. The minimum height is 140 centimetres.

### Raptor: the first wing coaster

Raptor is the prototype of the wing coasters. Bolliger & Mabillard opened it on 1 April 2011. The seats hang to the left and right of the track, four people side by side, with nothing above or below you. It’s 33 metres tall and 90 km/h fast, with three inversions: a corkscrew, a zero-g roll and an inline twist.

Here too the limit is 140 centimetres.

### Blue Tornado and Shaman: Vekoma, 1998 and 1985

Blue Tornado is a suspended looping coaster in the standard 765-metre length, with a helix. The train hangs below the track, there are five inversions, and the ride lasts 1:42 minutes. A train holds 20 riders, and according to RCDB the ride manages 1,500 people an hour. Oblivion has three trains of 18 seats each.

Shaman is the oldest coaster in the park. It opened in 1985 as Magic Mountain, then the first steel coaster in Italy. In 2008 it got new chassis from Vekoma, and new train bodies for 2009. Over the winter of 2020/21 a large part of the track was replaced; only the station, lift hill and first drop stayed. The train runs two loops and a double corkscrew sequence, and the ride takes two minutes. In 2017 you could ride it with VR headsets. According to German Wikipedia the loading times caused queues, and the idea was dropped after one season.

### Mammut, Dragon Rush and Ortobruco Tour

[Mammut](ref:gardaland/mammut) is a mine-train coaster from Vekoma with three lift hills (12, 13 and 14 metres) and a ride time of 2:45 minutes. A statue of a mammoth stands at the entrance, and the theme is arctic. It runs at 50 km/h and from 110 centimetres.

[Dragon Rush](ref:gardaland/dragon-rush) is a spinning coaster from the Fabbri Group, opened in 2016 in what was then the Kung Fu Panda area. It was reworked in 2025 after the DreamWorks licence for Kung Fu Panda had ended, and the area is now called Dragon Empire. Dragon Rush runs from 110 centimetres and, by our measurements, has the longest queue in the park.

[Ortobruco Tour](ref:gardaland/ortobruco-tour) is a caterpillar coaster from Pinfari for children from 100 centimetres. According to the Roller Coaster DataBase it has seven booster lift hills, and the trains hold 28 people.

## Water, dark rides and towers

You get wet on three rides. [Colorado Boat](ref:gardaland/colorado-boat) is the log flume from Mack Rides, built in 1985, with two drops, the higher one 15 metres. [Jungle Rapids](ref:gardaland/jungle-rapids) by Intamin (1999) is a river rapids ride with round boats that passes Asian scenery. [Fuga da Atlantide](ref:gardaland/fuga-da-atlantide) is an Intamin Super Splash from 2003. The boat goes up a lift and plunges into a basin that, according to German Wikipedia, was the largest water basin in Europe when it opened. The minimum height is 100 to 110 centimetres.

[Animal Treasure Island](ref:gardaland/animal-treasure-island) replaced the dark ride I Corsari in the same spot, which had run underground on three levels since 1992. The Intamin ride system and the set are still there, the story is new, and a young cat called Captain Nine Lives is one of the main characters. Quality Travel calls it the longest water dark ride in Europe, and the minimum height is 107 centimetres.

[Jumanji – The Adventure](ref:gardaland/jumanji-the-adventure) opened in 2022 where Ramses: Il Risveglio stood, the park’s first interactive dark ride, which had itself replaced the old Valle dei Re from 1988 in 2009. According to English Wikipedia it’s the first attraction in the world based on the Jumanji franchise, with a large animatronic figure. Gardaland gives 107 centimetres for it. In 2023 Jumanji – The Labyrinth was added, a walk-through maze of mirrors and jungle.

The park also has two towers. [Space Vertigo](ref:gardaland/space-vertigo) by Intamin is a 40-metre drop tower from 1998, minimum height 130 centimetres. [Wolf Legend](ref:gardaland/wolf-legend) is the drop-and-twist tower by SBF Visa from 2024, from 105 centimetres. The Flying Island from 2000 is a 50-metre observation tower with views of the park and Lake Garda, and the Magic House (2002) is an underground madhouse by Vekoma.

## For small children

Children under 90 centimetres get into Gardaland free. There are three areas for them. The Fantasy Kingdom has the Albero di Prezzemolo, a walk-through house of the mascot, the Volaplano with its aeroplane gondolas, the Doremifarm and the Funny Express. The Prezzemolo area has a medieval look, with playgrounds and slides. And Peppa Pig Land at the main entrance, opened in 2018, has Peppa Pig’s house, Grandpa Pig’s train, a balloon and a pirate ship.

In these areas the minimum height is 80 to 105 centimetres, where there is one at all. If you come with children under 110 centimetres you can rent an Inglesina stroller at the entrance, for a fee and against ID. Parents can get the wristband with the child’s name and mobile number at the Welcome offices. The Fantasy Kingdom has a baby room with high chairs, a bottle warmer and changing tables.

Rides with a minimum height have the Baby Switch: one parent waits with the child while the other rides, then they swap without queuing again. Pets are allowed in the park on a lead, but not on the rides, at the shows, in the SEA LIFE Aquarium or in the LEGOLAND Water Park.

## Shows and meet and greet

The Gardaland Theatre has had about 1,300 seats since 2006 and shows musicals and shows in changing seasonal programmes. The children’s shows run on the stage in the Teatro della Fantasia, the music shows on the Buffalo Stage in the Western area. The programme changes with the season, and the times are in the Gardaland app on the day of your visit.

The Halloween programme runs until 1 November. "Halloween Manor" lasts 25 minutes and starts at 2:30 and 4:30 pm; it doesn’t run on Wednesdays. "Frankentiger – Monströse Experimente" is a ten-minute family show at 11:30 am, 3:30 pm and 6:30 pm. "Sound of Shadows" on the Buffalo Stage runs at 12:30 pm, and "Mirror – Spiegelung der Dunkelheit" at 7 pm only at weekends until 25 October. Children can meet Prezzemolo and Zenda at 10:00 am, 2:15 pm and 5 pm at the Piazza Camelot, Peppa Pig at her house several times in the morning and late afternoon, and Captain Nine Lives at Animal Treasure Island at 1:15 and 3:15 pm.

### The hour after opening

In high season the park opens at 10 am. Blue Tornado, Mammut, Raptor and Fuga da Atlantide peak in the hour from eleven and are much shorter for the rest of the day. Jumanji – The Adventure, Dragon Rush and Colorado Boat do the opposite: they build up during the day and peak in the afternoon. There are no wait times for Jumanji before eleven or for Dragon Rush before twelve.

The rope-drop analysis on the park page gives the same order for every ride. The best time for Blue Tornado, Mammut, Raptor and Fuga da Atlantide is the first 15 minutes after opening. Oblivion follows after about 30 minutes. Wolf Legend is worth it after roughly an hour, Jumanji – The Adventure after an hour and 15 minutes, Shaman and Colorado Boat after an hour and a half. Dragon Rush is shortest two hours after opening, so around noon.

Compared with the peak, the saving at most rides is between 20 and 45 minutes.

## A day in the park from 140 centimetres

Say you’re at the entrance at 10 am and can ride everything, so you’re at least 140 centimetres tall.

Blue Tornado, Mammut and Raptor come first, in the first quarter of an hour while the queues there are still short, then Fuga da Atlantide. Oblivion is on the list for around half past ten, before its queue builds up.

At eleven the four early rides peak. That’s the time for Wolf Legend, and a quarter of an hour later for Jumanji – The Adventure. If it’s warm, Space Vertigo and Jungle Rapids are next, or the SEA LIFE Aquarium (separate ticket). Shaman and Colorado Boat are shortest half an hour later, Dragon Rush around twelve, and after that its queue stays about the same length until evening. There’s time for lunch. In the early afternoon the queues at Blue Tornado, Mammut, Raptor and Fuga da Atlantide are shorter again than at eleven, good for a second ride.

You need two days if you have children who want to ride the smaller areas out, or if you add the aquarium and the LEGOLAND Water Park.

## Gardaland Express: the fast track

Gardaland calls its Fast Pass Gardaland Express. It applies on top of admission and lets you use a separate entrance at the included rides, with a shorter wait. It’s valid only on the day of your visit and can’t be transferred.

There are three packages. "Give me 5" allows one ride each on five attractions out of 15 and costs from 27 euros online, from 30 euros in the park. "Epic 17" is good for one ride each on 17 attractions, including Blue Tornado, Oblivion, Raptor, Mammut, Fuga da Atlantide and Jumanji – The Adventure, from 57 euros online and from 60 euros in the park. "No Limits" allows unlimited rides on those 17 attractions and costs from 87 euros online, from 90 euros in the park. All three give 20 percent off in the park’s shops.

You reserve through the "Gardaland Express" app. You import the voucher or ticket there by QR code, then see the estimated wait times and join the queue for a ride. A notification ("You’re up soon") arrives about five minutes before your ride, followed by a QR code that stays valid for about five minutes. You buy it online or at the Express Points in the park, and the number of passes per day is limited.

If a ride’s median is a quarter of an hour, the pass saves little. On Saturday, the busiest weekday, the queues are longer than the weekly average, and then it saves more.

## Tickets, prices and season pass

Day admission starts at 32 euros per adult if you book early online. The undated 1-day ticket for the park, valid on any day between 28 March and 1 November 2026, costs from 59 euros online. During Magic Halloween there’s the "One more day" offer: a 1-day ticket from 44 euros, with a second park admission free that you can redeem by 1 November. The offer ends on 29 October. The combination ticket "2 days park and 1 day aquarium" costs from 59 euros online. The ticket offices close two hours before the park does.

Children under 90 centimetres pay nothing. If you stay at one of the three Gardaland hotels, children up to and including twelve get admission to two park days and one day at the SEA LIFE Aquarium free. The offer applies to bookings made by 29 October 2026 for arrivals up to 1 November, except 30 and 31 October, from 78 euros per person.

The 2026 season pass is valid from 28 March 2026 to 6 January 2027 and includes Magic Halloween and Magic Winter. There are four tiers:

This year the Platinum pass includes free admission on two consecutive days to all Merlin theme parks in Europe, among them LEGOLAND Deutschland, LEGOLAND Billund and Heide Park, plus a day’s admission to many other Merlin venues, including Madame Tussauds in Amsterdam, Berlin and Vienna. Platinum costs 50 euros more than Gold (149 against 99 euros), and for that you get the Merlin admissions.

## Getting there and parking

The Gardaland Resort is at Via Derna 4 in Castelnuovo del Garda, near the A4 motorway between Milan and Venice. From Milan you take the Peschiera del Garda exit, from Venice Sommacampagna or Peschiera del Garda, and either way it’s a few kilometres from there. From the Brenner motorway A22 you leave at Affi and follow the expressway towards Peschiera del Garda. From Modena you take the A22 to the A4 towards Milan.

According to Gardaland the car park is free, opens an hour before the park, and its ticket booths close two hours before the park does. A covered space can be booked online for an extra charge; the park’s site says "from €7" in the shop and "from €5" in the FAQ. Motorhomes aren’t allowed in the main car park. They park at the coach park in Via Ronchi, where overnight parking is not allowed. Hotel guests enter via Via Palù and pay 12 euros for 72 hours in the hotel car park.

By train you reach Peschiera del Garda station, two kilometres from the park. A free shuttle bus runs every 30 minutes, the first 30 minutes before opening, the last 30 minutes after closing. FlixBus and Busforfun serve Gardaland from all over Italy. The nearest airport is Verona Villafranca, only a few kilometres away.

## Hotels, food and the other parts of the resort

The resort has three themed hotels with 475 rooms between them, 264 of them themed. The **Gardaland Hotel** (2004) is rated three stars superior and has 236 superior rooms, five junior suites and six suites in a New England style, some of them with Peppa Pig, princess and pirate themes. The **Adventure Hotel** (2016) has four stars and 100 rooms in four areas: Arctic, Arabia, jungle and Wild West. In the evening you eat at the Tutankhamon, a restaurant styled like an Egyptian temple. The **Magic Hotel** (2019) has four stars, 128 rooms in three worlds, the wizard’s house, the enchanted forest and the magic ice castle, and a wizard’s hat at the entrance. A free shuttle bus links the hotels with the park.

The park has 25 restaurants, kiosks and bars, among them the Ristorante Aladino, which opened in 1986 as the first big self-service restaurant, the Pizzeria Saloon and a fast-food outlet at Blue Tornado. The "Kid’s Box" with a menu and a surprise is sold at the Fast Food Blue Tornado and at the Covo dei Bucanieri, cheaper online. Gardaland also sells restaurant menus as an extra with the ticket.

The LEGOLAND Water Park is inside the park, on about 15,000 square metres. It opened in 2021 as the first of its kind in Europe, with slides, water play and a Miniland of more than four million LEGO bricks in which Italian landmarks are rebuilt. Entry is possible only together with the park ticket, for a supplement. Children under 14 may only enter with an adult, teenagers from 14 to 17 with or without one.

The SEA LIFE Aquarium is the first fully themed aquarium in Italy, opened in 2008, with 37 tanks and about 5,000 animals, an ocean tunnel and sea lions. It’s outside the park at the end of the car park and has its own ticket.

## What’s new, and what has closed

Five new things arrived in 2025: Animal Treasure Island, the reworked Dragon Empire, the live show "Bim Bum Bam Live" with the puppet Uan, the multimedia show "A.I. – The Future is Here" and the 4D cinema "Prezzemolo e l'Enigma Svelato". In return I Corsari closed in 2024, and Wolf Legend has stood since 2024 where the coaster Sequoia Magic Loop used to be.

Magic Winter follows in winter, from 5 December to 6 January 2027. From 4 to 8 December the magic show Supermagic Elementi runs in the Gardaland Theatre with eight performances of about two hours each, tickets from 24 euros. Season pass holders get a discounted ticket for themselves and up to nine companions.

Gardaland hasn’t published anything about novelties for 2027.

## Frequently asked questions about Gardaland

### How long are the wait times at Gardaland?

At most rides the median is between ten and 25 minutes, measured over the past twelve months. Saturday is the busiest day and Wednesday the emptiest. The big coasters peak shortly after opening and then drop. The current wait time of every ride is on the park page.

### When is Gardaland least crowded?

On a Wednesday outside the holidays. The quietest days of the coming weeks are marked green in the calendar on the park page. March, April, June, July and October are on average less crowded than May and September.

### Do I need the Express pass at Gardaland?

On a Wednesday, probably not. On a Saturday in high season "Give me 5" from 27 euros can pay off. Raptor is only in the "Epic 17" package, and Dragon Rush and Wolf Legend are in none of the three.

### How tall do you have to be for Oblivion, Raptor and Blue Tornado?

140 centimetres for all three. For Shaman it’s 120, for Mammut, Dragon Rush and Fuga da Atlantide 110, and for Jumanji – The Adventure and Animal Treasure Island 107 centimetres.

### How much does Gardaland cost?

The 1-day ticket starts at 32 euros online, the earlier you book the better. The undated ticket costs from 59 euros, a Silver season pass 79 euros. Children under 90 centimetres get in free, and parking is free.

### When is Gardaland open?

The 2026 season runs from 28 March to 1 November, Magic Winter from 5 December to 6 January 2027. In high summer, from 28 June to 6 September, it’s open daily from 10 am to 11 pm, in spring and autumn usually from 10 am to 5 or 6 pm.

### Can you do Gardaland in one day?

Yes, at the measured wait times the big coasters and water rides fit into one day. With small children, SEA LIFE and the LEGOLAND Water Park you need two.

### How do you get to Gardaland without a car?

By train to Peschiera del Garda station, and from there a free shuttle bus runs to the park every 30 minutes. FlixBus and Busforfun serve Gardaland too.
