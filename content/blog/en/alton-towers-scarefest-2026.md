[Alton Towers](ref:alton-towers) in Staffordshire opened **Scarefest**, the
park’s Halloween event, on Saturday 26 September. This year’s new scare maze is
**Final Exhibit**, and in the evenings the big coasters run in the dark.

## Dates and opening hours

Scarefest doesn’t run every day. These are the dates the park gives:

- 26 and 27 September
- 2 to 4 October
- 9 to 11 October
- 16 October to 1 November, every day

Until mid-October that means weekends only, Friday to Sunday in October, and
after that every day through to 1 November. The combination tickets give park
entry until 9 pm, and the park calendar lists **10 am to 9 pm** local time for
the October weekends. On the days in between, such as Monday 5 to Thursday
8 October, the park closes at 4 pm and there’s no Scarefest. England is an hour
behind Germany, so 9 pm in Staffordshire is 10 pm in Berlin.

## Final Exhibit and the other mazes

In [Final Exhibit](ref:alton-towers/final-exhibit) you walk through the
forgotten collection of a man the park only ever calls “The Curator”. According
to the park’s page, every room has a dark secret, not every exhibit is as
lifeless as it looks, and once you’re past the archive doors there’s only one
way to go. The maze is recommended for **ages 12 and up**, like all four thrill
mazes.

The other three are returning. [COMPOUND](ref:alton-towers/compound) is a lab
where scientists want to turn your DNA into a weapon.
[Edge of the Forest](ref:alton-towers/edge-of-the-forest) has a cabin on the
edge of the Dark Forest and a creature called Leshwall, and
[Altonville Mine Tours: Tiny’s Revenge](ref:alton-towers/altonville-mine-tours-tinys-revenge)
takes you down into the old Altonville mines.

The scare zone [Dark Hollow](ref:alton-towers/dark-hollow) has no time slots.
It’s an open-air wood you wander through at your own pace. In
[Burial Grounds](ref:alton-towers/burial-grounds) a cult called The Unchosen
waits among burning shrines. Families get two walkthroughs for ages 6 and up,
[Trick O’ Treat Town](ref:alton-towers/trick-o-treat-town-with-haribo-and-maoam)
and [Amigos in the Afterlife](ref:alton-towers/amigos-in-the-afterlife), plus
shows in CBeebies Land.

## Riding in the dark

In early October the sun sets in Staffordshire shortly before 7 pm, so if you
stay until 9 pm you get a good two hours of riding in the dark. The park singles
out [The Smiler](ref:alton-towers/the-smiler),
[Nemesis Reborn](ref:alton-towers/nemesis-reborn) and
[Wicker Man](ref:alton-towers/wicker-man), the wooden coaster that races towards
a burning figure six storeys high. There’s also
[Oblivion](ref:alton-towers/oblivion), with its vertical drop into the black
hole, and [Toxicator](ref:alton-towers/toxicator).

## What it costs

The mazes are **not included** in admission. The prices below come from the
park’s Scarefest page, and annual pass holders get 20% off each of them.

| Ticket                 | Price    | Includes                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day ticket             | from £37 | park and rides, £68 at the gate                           |
| Final Exhibit only     | from £16 | one timed entry, admission ticket required                |
| Other mazes, each      | from £14 | one timed entry per maze                                  |
| Thrills Combi          | from £43 | all four thrill mazes once, admission ticket required     |
| Day ticket + 3 mazes   | from £80 | admission plus three mazes, **without** Final Exhibit     |
| Ultimate Thrills Combi | from £95 | the four thrill mazes as often as you like, with Fastrack |

The combined admission-and-mazes ticket only covers COMPOUND, Edge of the Forest
and Tiny’s Revenge. If you want Final Exhibit, book the day ticket and the
Thrills Combi separately. That also comes to £80, the same price as the combined
ticket, with one more maze.

Time slots start every 15 minutes. For a Saturday in October, book yours before
you go.

## Dinner with the Vile family

Also new this year is **Dine with the Dead**, a dinner at the Vile family’s
table with three courses and performers around you. The evening show starts at
7:30 pm and ends with a party, which the 12:30 pm matinee leaves out. You have
to be at least 14, and under-18s need to come with an adult. Evening tickets
start at £99, the matinee at £69.

## Getting there

Alton Towers is in Staffordshire, between Manchester and Birmingham. From
Manchester Airport it’s a good hour by car. If you’d like to stay two days, the
park sells short breaks with an overnight stay, with a choice of five places to
stay on site. For Halloween on the continent, Movie Park, Plopsaland, Walibi and
Toverland are in the [Halloween overview](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026).
