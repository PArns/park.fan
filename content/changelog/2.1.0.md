## New

- Every park, ride, show and restaurant has a star, and starred ones appear in a favourites
  section on the homepage (7 January). They live in a cookie on your device for a year.
- Favourite rides carry their crowd level. Without your location the section shows a hint and
  sorts alphabetically.
