Halloween has been on at [PortAventura Park](ref:portaventura-park) since
19 September and runs until 15 November. The new addition is **El Carnicero de
Penitence**, “the Butcher of Penitence”, the park’s fifth horror passage.
PortAventura calls a walk-through with live actors a passage, and last year it
had four.

## What the new passage is about

Penitence is the Wild West town in the park’s Far West area, the one where
[Uncharted](ref:portaventura-park/uncharted) stands, and the story is set in the
19th century. People in town are going missing. The sheriff suspects the
butcher, and you help him investigate by walking through a butcher’s shop and
an old slaughterhouse. The Diari de Tarragona describes the tone as explicitly
gory. The park itself only says that something doesn’t smell right around the
butcher’s shop.

According to the conditions on the ticket page, El Carnicero has the same age
limit as the other four passages, 12 and over if accompanied and 14 to go in
alone. So a child under twelve can’t get into any of the five. The older Angkor
passage also turns away people with heart problems, high blood pressure or
epilepsy, and pregnant women.

## The five passages and what they cost

None of the passages is included in park admission. The park’s ticket page
lists the prices as “from”, and depending on the day you may pay more.

| Ticket                    | From   | Includes                                      |
| ------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| El Carnicero de Penitence | €10    | one entry, new                                |
| La Isla Maldita           | €12    | one entry, ghost pirates                      |
| REC® Experience           | €8.90  | one entry, based on the REC films             |
| La Muerte Viva            | €6.90  | one entry                                     |
| Angkor                    | €6.90  | one entry, cannibal king                      |
| Halloween Passport 2      | €11.90 | El Carnicero and REC                          |
| Halloween Passport 4      | €21.90 | all except La Isla Maldita                    |
| Halloween Passport 5      | €30.90 | all five                                      |
| Express Miedo             | €10    | express entry to La Isla Maldita, add-on only |

If it’s the new passage you’re after, take Passport 2, which adds REC for
€1.90 more than El Carnicero on its own. Bought separately, all five come to
€44.70, and Passport 5 saves you just under €14 of that. La Isla Maldita is
staged in Caribe Aquatic Park. REC is based on the Spanish horror film series,
and PortAventura is the only place with a passage built on it. For what horror
houses cost at other parks in Europe, see the
[Halloween overview](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026).

## Included with admission

For families, Truco o Trato, Día de los Muertos and the Halloween Parade are
back, along with new characters walking around the park. The Saloon has a new
mystery and dance show, **Resurrection**. In the evening the scare zone La
Maldición del Emperador opens.

The water park has dropped out of the programme. Caribe Aquatic Park only opens
from May to September and has now closed.

Between January and March 2026, [Dragon Khan](ref:portaventura-park/dragon-khan)
got new track in its loop and its cobra roll. According to Valencia Extra, it
has vibrated less since then.

## Opening hours

According to the calendar on park.fan, the park is open from **10:30 am to
6 pm** on weekdays in early October. On Saturday 3 October and over the weekend
of 10 and 11 October it stays open until 11 pm, and on Sunday 4 October and on
12 October, Spain’s national holiday, until 7 pm. Spain is one hour ahead of
the UK.
