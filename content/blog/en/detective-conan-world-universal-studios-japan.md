Detective Conan World returns to [Universal Studios Japan](ref:universal-studios-japan) in Osaka from 10 February to 30 June 2027, with an escape game, a Live Restaurant and a Story Ride on the Hollywood Dream – The Ride coaster. The park announced the programme on 20 October as part of "Universal Cool Japan 2027". Detective Conan World has been held there since 2017. All three parts run for the whole period.

## Escape game, restaurant and Story Ride

"Detective Conan: The Escape" is a Real Escape Game in Stage 18. The "Detective Conan Live Restaurant" takes place in the Peacock Theater, which USJ calls a dining theater, a restaurant with a show. Until now it was held in Lombard's Landing. The "Detective Conan × Story Ride" runs on [Hollywood Dream – The Ride](ref:universal-studios-japan/hollywood-dream-the-ride-2) in the Hollywood area.

## Prices and tickets

The restaurant needs a separate paid ticket. Depending on the day, adults aged 12 and over pay 7,000 to 8,400 yen, and children from age 4 to the end of elementary school pay 2,600 to 3,200 yen, tax included in both. A 12-year-old who's still in elementary school pays the child price. Show times change from day to day, and on some days the restaurant is closed.

Park admission isn't part of the restaurant price. A 1-Day Studio Pass costs from 8,400 yen for adults. So an adult pays at least 15,400 yen for the restaurant and a day ticket together. The Twilight Pass, valid from 3 p.m., starts at 5,900 yen and only works for a show that starts after 3 p.m.

The announcement doesn't say whether the escape game and the Story Ride are included in the Studio Pass in 2027. USJ plans to announce the content, running times and on-sale date later.

## What's different from 2026

In 2026 Detective Conan World ran from 30 January to 30 June, again with the escape game in Stage 18 and a Story Ride on Hollywood Dream. The restaurant was called the "Detective Conan Mystery Restaurant" then and cost the same on every day; in 2027 the price depends on the day.

## Universal Cool Japan 2027

The 2027 edition starts in stages, with Sailor Moon on 2 December 2026. Until 5 April 2027 it brings "The Miracle 4-D – Moon Palace Deluxe" to the Cinema 4-D Theater and, for the first time, a Sailor Moon Story Ride on Hollywood Dream. That means riders on the coaster can choose between two Story Rides from 10 February to 5 April, Sailor Moon and Conan.

## Hollywood Dream – The Ride

Hollywood Dream is a Bolliger & Mabillard hyper coaster, 43 metres tall, and it's been running since 2007. The minimum height is 132 cm.

Before boarding, each rider picks one of five music tracks, which then plays from speakers in the headrest. A Story Ride is one of those tracks, except that a character tells the rider a story in their ear. The coaster also runs backwards, as the [Backdrop](ref:universal-studios-japan/hollywood-dream-the-ride-backdrop-2).

Hollywood Dream is included in the Express Pass. The Express Pass 4 costs from 6,800 yen and the Express Pass Premium from 27,600 yen.

## Conan all year round

Detective Conan (Case Closed) is a manga by Gōshō Aoyama. Since 22 March 2024 the park has also had a permanent Conan attraction outside the event: the [Detective Conan 4-D Live Show: Jewel Under the Starry Sky](ref:universal-studios-japan/detective-conan-4-d-live-show-jewel-under-the-starry-sky) in the New York Area. The show lasts about 30 minutes, and the Express Pass is valid there too.

## Getting there

Universal City station is on the JR Yumesaki Line, and you change from the JR Osaka Loop Line at Nishikujo. From Osaka and Umeda it's about eleven minutes, and some trains run through without a change.
