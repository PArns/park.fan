A child under four is too small for most roller coasters. If you're taking one
to a theme park, you want to know beforehand at what age or height admission
starts to cost money, whether you can rent a stroller or bring your own, where
to change a diaper and whether two adults can take turns on a ride without
queuing twice. Every park puts that information in a different corner of its
website.

For this guide we looked it up for ten parks: Europa-Park, Phantasialand,
Legoland Deutschland, Heide Park and Plopsaland Deutschland in Germany,
Efteling, Toverland and Walibi Holland in the Netherlands, Walibi Belgium in
Belgium and Parc Astérix in France. We read the parks' websites on 8 October 2026. Whatever we couldn't find is stated as an open question in the text, not
guessed. That mostly affects quiet zones, shaded spots and whether you may bring
baby food.

## How we searched

For each park we looked for seven things: up to what age a child doesn't pay,
where you can change a diaper and warm up baby food, whether you can rent a
stroller and bring your own, whether there's Baby Switch or Rider Switch,
whether you may bring food for your child, which rides and play areas the park
itself names for the youngest, and whether there are quiet zones. The answers
come from the parks' own pages: ticket page, help and FAQ, park rules, ride
pages. Blogs and forums don't count.

On park.fan, minimum heights appear on each ride's page where we've entered
them. For Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland and Walibi Belgium the value is
missing in our database for every ride, and for Efteling for 29 of 36. There we
give what the park itself says. An empty field on our side doesn't mean a ride
has no minimum height.

The limits are built differently. Efteling, Europa-Park, Phantasialand and Parc
Astérix go by age, Heide Park, Toverland, Walibi Belgium and Plopsaland by
height, and according to Plopsaland and Walibi Belgium, children are measured
with their shoes on. A child can be free under one park's age rule and not under
another's height rule, and the other way round. If your child is tall for their
age, check each park.

## Efteling

At [Efteling](ref:efteling) in Kaatsheuvel, children up to and including age 3
get in free. In 2026 a ticket costs between €40 and €56, depending on the day.

The park has the most detailed baby page of the ten. Every restroom has at least
one changing cubicle with a diaper bin, though the park doesn't sell diapers.
There are three rooms for nursing and pumping: in Volk van Laaf, at Station de
Oost and in the Efteling Grand Hotel. In Volk van Laaf and the hotel the room is
always open, at Station de Oost a staff member opens it. The Efteling app shows
exactly where they are. Bottles, jars and warm water are available on request in
a restaurant, and most restaurants have high chairs.

You can rent wooden strollers for €5 at the main entrance, a seat for one
toddler. You may bring your own, but not into every ride and restaurant. The
ride pages for Sirocco and Piraña say explicitly that strollers aren't allowed.

Baby Switch applies on 19 rides. One parent rides while the other waits at the
exit with the baby. When the first gets off, staff hand them a Baby Switch
ticket that lets the second board through the exit without queuing again. The 19
rides are spread like this: in Marerijk Anton Pieckplein, Droomvlucht,
Stoomcarrousel, Villa Volta and the Volk van Laaf, in Reizenrijk Carnaval
Festival, Vogel Rok and Pagode, in Ruigrijk Baron 1898, De Oude Tufferbaan, De
Vliegende Hollander, Halve Maen, Joris en de Draak and Python, in Anderrijk Danse
Macabre, Fata Morgana, Max & Moritz and Piraña, in Fantasierijk Symbolica.

For children under 4 there are two playgrounds, Kleuterhof in Reizenrijk and
Kindervreugd in Marerijk. On the park's pages, several rides give no minimum
height, only a rule: children under 1.00 m ride with adult supervision on the
[Stoomcarrousel](ref:efteling/stoomcarrousel), in Villa Volta, on the steam train
and in Sirocco. With a companion aged 16 or older, children under 1.00 m may go
on [Droomvlucht](ref:efteling/droomvlucht), in the [Pagode](ref:efteling/pagode)
and on the Anton Pieckplein. In Symbolica, babies must be able to sit up on
their own, and nobody may ride on a lap. The park doesn't mention quiet zones or
nap spots.

The rest of the park is covered in our guide
[Efteling: the Disney of the Netherlands](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).

## Europa-Park

At [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) in Rust, toddlers from 0 to 3 years get in
without paying. For children from 4 to 11, the ticket page lists between €56.50
and €65 online for a day between 28 March 2026 and 9 January 2027. Birthday
children up to their 12th birthday get in free, with ID at the box office by the
main entrance.

The park names changing rooms in seven places: at the main entrance, at the info
point by the lake, in Ireland (Kinderwelt), in Italy next to dm family & friends,
in Spain, in the Fairytale Forest and in the Kingdom of the Minimoys. It doesn't
say how they're equipped or whether there's a nursing room. At dm family &
friends the park sells selected baby food, and according to the page you can only
warm it up with the help of guest services.

The rental stroller costs €5 plus a €50 deposit and is handed out at the main
entrance or at the entrance to the hotel car park. By the park's own account it
only suits children who can already sit, because nobody can lie down in it. The
park doesn't rent out wagons. The park rules don't mention wagons, they ban
non-motorised vehicles such as skates, bikes and scooters, so whether your own
wagon gets through isn't clear. If you want to bring one, ask guest services
beforehand.

Baby Switch is called the same thing at Europa-Park and, according to the park,
applies on many of the larger coasters. blue fire Megacoaster and Alpenexpress
Coastiality are named, but there's no complete list. Whoever waits stays with the
child, say at the playground, and the rider gets a card for the exit or for the
Baby Switch entrance.

For the youngest, the park's list includes Little Lamb's Land as a "Tiny tots
playground", the Würmchen Wies'n Playground, the Water Playground, the ball pit,
the Adventure Playground, Paul's Playboat and the Mul-Muls Carousel. In our
database the Quipse Paddle Boats, the Mini-Scooters and Red Baron are each at 90
centimetres and the Mul-Muls Carousel at 100. The park doesn't publish official
minimum ages for each children's ride on the pages we read. More on the rides is
in our guide [Europa-Park: Germany's biggest park, and where everybody went](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips).

## Phantasialand

At [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl, too, children from 0 to 3 years
get in free, without a pre-booked ticket. On the day of your visit you need proof
of age, meaning an ID or birth certificate, a copy will do. Without it, the child
price for ages 4 to 11 applies. The price isn't on the page, only "Tickets from
€44". Birthday children up to their 11th birthday get in free if they've booked
ahead online.

Next to guest services there's a quiet and nursing room with changing rooms, and
staff will warm up jars of baby food. In the winter park Wintertraum, guest
services is at Kaiserplatz, with its own nursing corner, changing table and
kettle.

The rental stroller costs €3 a day plus a €20 deposit and is handed out at guest
services in the Berlin themed area. The number is limited, you can reserve by
e-mail, and the stroller carries children up to 22 kilograms. With
your own wheels, Phantasialand is the strictest of the ten parks: only narrow,
classic strollers and buggies up to 105 centimetres long, 75 centimetres wide and
125 centimetres high are allowed, and only for carrying children. Wagons, bike
trailers and dog carts are banned. An exception on medical grounds has to be
e-mailed to guest services at least 14 days ahead.

There is a Baby Switch: one parent rides, the other stays with the toddler, then
you swap without the second one queuing again. You ask for it at the ride. The
park doesn't say which rides take part.

In the official table in its terms of use, the park lists under "Attractions for
children" Der lustige Papagei, Die fröhliche Bienchenjagd, Bolles Riesenrad,
Wözl's Duck Washer, Bolles Flugschule, Winni Splash, Wolke's Luftpost, Bumper
Klumpen and Wupi's Wabi Wipper. We couldn't read the height bars in the table.
According to our data, Bolles Riesenrad, the Papagei and the Bienchenjagd have
90 centimetres, Bolles Flugschule, Bumper Klumpen, Wupi's Wabi Wipper and
Wözl's Duck Washer 100. The park writes that children from 1.00 m can ride more
than three quarters of the attractions. As a play area for the little ones it
names Mopti's Monkey Depot.

Our [Phantasialand guide](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips) has the rest.

## Legoland Deutschland

At [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland) in Günzburg, children under 2
get in free. In the 2026 season (28 March to 8 November), children from 2 to 11
pay €58 at the gate and anyone from 12 pays €64. Online, the day ticket starts at
€39. In one search result we found "under three years" for another page of the
park, but we couldn't check that in the full text. The price page says under 2.

The Baby Service powered by Müller is in the Imagination themed area and has a
changing table, a separate nursing area, seating and a small kitchen for warming
food and preparing baby food. You can also buy baby food, diapers and care
products there. Since 1 October you can only reach it through the terrace of the
Pizza Mania restaurant, because the Duplo playground is being rebuilt. According
to the FAQ, every restroom in the park has a changing room.

Buggies are available in limited numbers at the entrance for €6 plus a €4
deposit, and you return them there too. They have no mount for infant car seats.
According to the FAQ you're welcome to bring your own wagon, and there are
dedicated parking spots in the park for buggies and strollers. Neither the FAQ
nor the toddler page mentions a Rider Switch. Whether the park has an
arrangement anyway, we don't know, and it's worth asking before your visit.

The park's toddler page names the Junior Driving School (from 3 years), the Duplo
Express, the Safari Tour, the Harbour Cruise, the Airport, the Knight's Tournament,
the Canoe X-pedition and Miniland. According to our data, with a companion Airport,
Canoe X-pedition, Captain Nick's Splash Battle, Duplo Express, Harbour Cruise,
Legoland Express and the Observation Tower are open from 80 centimetres, and
Caterpillar Ride, Pedal-A-Car, Pirate School, Pyramid Rallye, Royal Joust and
Temple X-pedition from 90 centimetres. There are also play areas with no value in
our database: the Ninjago World Toddler Area, the Duplo Playground and the
playgrounds of Pirate Land, Shipyard and LavaLand. The park publishes the full
list with age and height as a PDF, which we didn't retrieve.

There's a quiet room and loungers in the first aid station, meant for guests with
circulation problems. Our guide
[Legoland Deutschland: tips for a day with kids from three to twelve](/blog/legoland-deutschland-tips)
has the details on the rides.

## Heide Park

At [Heide Park](ref:heide-park) in Soltau, children under 90 centimetres get in
free. Birthday children of any age get in free with proof. We couldn't check
ticket prices, because the price page returned an error when we loaded it.

Here the swap system is called the Baby-Switch-Pass. Parents of children up to
and including age 3 get it free at the Service Center, only on days when the
Express Ticket is available. It applies to coasters. The park doesn't say which
ones.

You can warm up baby food at the Wirtshaus des Admirals and in Lucky Land, among
other places, and the app shows all the stations. The park map also marks
changing stations. The park's help pages returned a 403 when we loaded them, so
on changing rooms, nursing, wagons and bringing food we can only report what
search results summarise, and we didn't use that. Renting a stroller or bringing
your own: not mentioned on the pages we could read.

On its "Theme park with children" page, the park names Peppa Pig Land, Drachenzähmen
– Die Insel and Ghostbusters 5D as children's attractions, and among others
Floßfahrt, Heide Park Express, Grottenblitz, Indy Blitz, La Ola, Monorail,
Topilaula, Wildwasserbahn, Bounty and the vintage cars. For George's Dino
Adventure in Peppa Pig Land we have 86 centimetres, for Raffnuss & Taffnuss
Wasserflieger 95 and for Indy-Blitz, La Ola, Screamie and Wüstenflitzer 100
each. For Peppas Ballonfahrt, Peppas Bootsfahrt, Opa Pigs Zugfahrt, Peppas
House, Käpt'ns Törn, Heide Park Express and Monorail our database has no value.
That's a gap on our side, not a statement from the park.

Our guide [Heide Park: Colossos in wood, surrounded by one German first after another](/blog/heide-park-wait-times-tips)
has more.

## Plopsaland Deutschland

[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) in Haßloch gives only a
height for free entry: children under 85 centimetres pay nothing. From 85 to 99
centimetres the ticket costs €22, and from one metre the adult price applies. The
price page gives no year. The family ticket for three to six people costs between
€139 and €259, depending on group size.

According to the park, baby changing rooms are in all restroom blocks. The
restaurants Pfalzgraf, Maja-Burger and Plopsa Indoor have microwaves for warming
baby food. You may bring your own food in small amounts, outside the restaurants.
Baby food is the exception: it may also be eaten in restaurants and on outdoor
terraces.

Strollers are allowed, but at rides and shows they have to stand in the
designated areas, and on busy days they aren't allowed in Plopsa Indoor. According
to our sources the park doesn't rent out strollers. Wagons cost €10 a day plus a
€50 deposit at the Plopsaland Shop, as the FAQ says. The park rules, on the other
hand, name an ID or a €100 deposit. Only the park knows which is right. Neither
the FAQ nor the park rules mention Rider Switch or swapping, and every guest has
to leave the ride after the trip.

On the other hand, Plopsaland is the park where each ride's height bands are
stated most precisely on the park's page. A companion must be at least 15 years
old. Without a minimum height, but with a companion, children under 100
centimetres ride, for example, the ball pit, Fischerboote, Frösche, Tanzende
Fontänen, Red Baron, the Historisches Karussell (under 105 centimetres with a
companion) and the playgrounds of Heidi, Maja, Wickie and at the beach. Under
120 centimetres with a companion, children can go on Die Schlümpfe Abenteuer,
Flip der Grashüpfer, Balloon Race and in the Super Wings water playground. From
85 centimetres children ride Bienchenwirbel, Willis Floßfahrt and Laras
Marienkäferflug without a companion. The park's page filters by "Ideal for
babies" and "Ideal for toddlers" but doesn't name any areas. When it rains, the
indoor attractions stay open, and there's more on that in our guide
[Theme parks in the rain](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain).

More on the rides is in the guide
[Plopsaland Deutschland: the park that used to be Holiday Park](/blog/plopsaland-deutschland-wait-times-tips).

## Toverland

At [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum, children under 90
centimetres pay nothing but still need a reservation in the ticket shop. From 90
to 140 centimetres the ticket costs €32.50, from 140 centimetres €39.50,
depending on the day class. On your birthday you get in for €0 with ID.

All restrooms except those in Ithaka have changing tables. Microwaves are free in
the Waldstube and on Katara Plaza, and the park doesn't mention a bottle warmer.
If you want to pump, ask the staff at the main entrance.

You may bring a buggy or wagon, and the park recommends a chain lock. Toverland
doesn't rent anything out. Baby Switch applies on selected rides, with no age
limit: you pick up a special ticket at the entrance, and one parent may ride
through the exit. The park doesn't say which rides they are.

For ages 0 to 4, Toverland is the easiest to navigate of the ten parks. In Land
van Toos, an area with halls, children ride with a companion from 0 centimetres
in the Klokhuis and in Morrels Truckjes, alone from 90. The Toos-Express,
Theekopjes and Drakenslangen start at 90 with a companion, Djinn at 100
centimetres. On top of that come Villa Toverhoed, Tovertuin, Dwaalhof, Sim sa la
Klim and the Speelkasteel with no height given. In Avalon, Little Dragons is a
playground for toddlers, the Garden Tour runs from 0 with a companion and Jumping
Juna from 0 centimetres, and in the Wunderwald the carousel from 0. The water
playgrounds are Sparky's Splash Dock and Waku Waku. The park never goes by age,
only by height. The park describes itself as partly covered. Our figure for that
is in the guide [Theme parks in the rain](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain).

[Toverland: Troy, the wooden coaster everyone drives past](/blog/toverland-troy-wait-times-tips).

## Walibi Holland

[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) in Biddinghuizen gives two different age
limits. The 2026 ticket page says: free under 2, ages 3 to 5 €23, ages 6 to 11
€36, ages 12 and up €46, as the price for the low season. The FAQ from the 2025
season says: free under 3. If the ticket page applies, a two-year-old probably
isn't free. According to the FAQ there's no minimum age for visiting.

Changing tables are in several restrooms, and microwaves for bottles and food in
several restaurants, with the locations on the park map. If you want your child
to wear a wristband with their name and your mobile number, you can get one at
Guest Service.

Buggies cost €10 plus a €50 deposit at the Gift Shop and can't be booked in
advance. Whether your own stroller or wagon is allowed isn't on the pages we
loaded. You may bring food and drinks. The swap system is called "baby change":
one adult waits with the child at the exit, the other rides, then they swap, and
the second goes through the exit without queuing. The park doesn't say which
rides take part.

For the little ones, the kids page lists Merrie Go'Round (from 0 with a
companion, alone from 120 centimetres), Walibi's Fun Recorder, Garage, Bubble
Swirl and Walibi's World Tour (from 0 with a companion, alone from 105) and
Walibi's Shuttle (from 0, alone from 120). Wind Seekers starts at 90 with a
companion, and children ride Mini Taxi's, Stunt Flight and Space Kidz alone from
90 or 105 centimetres respectively. Our database has no minimum height for any of
the 39 rides, so only the park's figures count here. When we queried it, almost
all rides showed as closed. You'll find more on the park in the post
[Walibi Holland: "hard gaan", Untamed and a field where fish swam in 1957](/blog/walibi-holland-untamed-hard-gaan).

## Walibi Belgium

[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) in Wavre lets children under one metre in
free, measured with shoes on. From 1 to 1.40 metres a reduced price applies, €47
on a standard day and €51 on an extended day, from 1.40 metres €57 and €61
respectively. That applies to the Ibilaw days from 10 October to 8 November 2026.
Children under 13 may only enter the park with adults and are under constant
supervision.

Baby Switch is free and can't be combined with the Speedy Fast-Pass or the
Easy-pass. How it works isn't spelled out on the page. Neither the services page
nor the FAQ nor the park rules mention a changing room. Nursing is allowed
everywhere in the park, and if you want it quiet, go to the first aid post. The
restaurants have microwaves for bottles and baby food. According to the park
rules, strollers are exempt from the pedestrian principle and so allowed. The FAQ
mentions "trolleys" for €10 in limited numbers, but doesn't say whether those are
strollers.

The park has no named children's areas, but it does have a "Kids" category. The
smallest figure is 50, with a companion, on Stormy, 4x4 Adventure, Mini Tour,
Spinning Taxi and Tchou-Tchou Express. The park's page gives the number without
a unit, and it means centimetres. So there are no rides with no minimum height at
all. During the Ibilaw days, Kondaala, Spinning Taxi, Guitar Riff, Kids Airlines,
Tchou-Tchou Express and Little Swing, among others, are closed. Our database has
no minimum height for any ride. More background in the guide
[Walibi Belgium: the Walibi nobody thinks of, with the tallest Benelux coaster](/blog/walibi-belgium-wait-times-tips).

## Parc Astérix

At [Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) in Plailly, children under 3 are free, on
proof of age. Children from 3 to 11 pay the child price. For 2026 the park lists
the Malin Ticket at €49, Early Bird from €53 for ages 3 to 11 and the Night Ticket
at €53 (10 October to 7 November 2026).

Changing tables are in several restrooms. The park doesn't mention a nursing room,
a microwave or a bottle warmer in the park itself, only in the hotel, where the
reception has bottle warmers. Rental strollers cost €15. Picnics are only allowed
in designated areas, and whether you may bring baby food isn't on the pages we
loaded.

Baby Switch has its own explanatory page: one parent rides alone, the other stays
with the child, then both go to the ride operator, who lets the second ride
without queuing again. It applies to children below the minimum height. There's
no list of rides.

The children's area is called "Attractions for Little Gauls". With an age, the
park names The Mini Train and Ceasar's Carrousel from 2 years, and The Mini Flying
Chairs, Ace Flying Squadron and Laundromatix from 3. The three playgrounds Golden
Boar, Getafix and Little Oaks are open to everyone, the page says "no age or
height restrictions". For Aerodynamix, Enigmatix and Hydrolix the park gives
from 5 years in one place and in another says they're accessible to children under
one metre. Which is right is best settled at the entrance, where children are
measured and get a wristband with the matching rides. Our guide
[Parc Astérix: Toutatis, OzIris and the Christmas queues](/blog/parc-asterix-wait-times-tips)
covers the rest.

## What the parks' pages add up to

No park mentions quiet zones, naps or shade on the pages we read. The exceptions
are the quiet and nursing room at Phantasialand, the three nursing rooms at
Efteling and the first aid post at Walibi Belgium. Legoland has a quiet room in
the first aid station, meant for circulation problems. So where a child sleeps is
mostly decided by the stroller. The rental stroller at Europa-Park doesn't allow
lying down, the rental stroller at Efteling is a wooden single seat for one
toddler, and at Phantasialand your own strollers are limited to 105 by 75 by 125
centimetres. If you want your child to sleep in the stroller, your own is the
easiest.

The food rules differ. Plopsaland explicitly allows baby food, even in
restaurants. At Walibi Holland you may bring your own food. For Europa-Park,
Phantasialand, Legoland, Toverland and Parc Astérix we found nothing on baby
food, only bans on grills and cooking equipment at Europa-Park. According to the
pages, there's a microwave at Plopsaland, Toverland, Walibi Holland and Walibi
Belgium, and staff will warm food at Phantasialand, Heide Park and Efteling.

Baby Switch lets you split the day. It goes by different names in the parks,
namely Baby Switch, Baby-Switch, Baby-Switch-Pass or "baby change", and works
the same way: one rides, one waits with the child, the second rides without
queuing. At Efteling the number of rides is given, at Europa-Park and Parc Astérix
the type of rides, and at Heide Park, Toverland, Walibi Holland and Phantasialand
you ask at the ride. Legoland and Plopsaland say nothing about it. Riding a
second time is only possible at Plopsaland by queuing again, according to the park
rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### Up to what age are children free at a theme park?

It varies. At Efteling it's up to and including age 3, at Europa-Park and
Phantasialand ages 0 to 3, at Parc Astérix under 3. At Legoland Deutschland
children under 2 are free, and at Walibi Holland too according to the 2026 ticket
page. Heide Park and Toverland go by height, under 90 centimetres, Walibi Belgium
under one metre and Plopsaland Deutschland under 85 centimetres. The date is 8
October 2026.

### Which theme park is best for a baby?

According to the parks' pages, Efteling has the most detailed information: changing
cubicles in all restrooms, three nursing and pumping rooms, a rental stroller for
€5 and Baby Switch on 19 rides. Legoland Deutschland has its own area in the Baby
Service, with a changing table, nursing area and kitchen. A park with a 0
centimetre minimum height for several rides with a companion is Toverland.

### What is Baby Switch?

One parent rides while the other stays with the child who's too small, then rides
afterwards without queuing again. Parks handle it differently: at Efteling staff
give you a ticket when you get off, at Europa-Park a card, and at Heide Park it's
a pass you get at the Service Center.

### Can I take a stroller into a theme park?

At most of the ten parks, yes, but not onto every ride. Phantasialand only allows
narrow strollers and buggies up to 105 by 75 by 125 centimetres and bans wagons.
At Toverland, Legoland and Plopsaland you can bring your own wagon, going by the
information we found. At Plopsa Indoor, strollers aren't allowed on busy days.

### How much does it cost to rent a stroller at a theme park?

At Phantasialand €3 plus a €20 deposit, at Efteling €5, at Europa-Park €5 plus a
€50 deposit, at Legoland €6 plus a €4 deposit, at Walibi Holland €10 plus a €50
deposit and at Parc Astérix €15. Toverland has no rental.

### How small can a child be to ride a roller coaster?

Each park sets that ride by ride. The lowest figures we read for family rides are
80 centimetres at Legoland Deutschland and 85 centimetres at Plopsaland. For small
children there are rides that run from 0 centimetres with a companion, at
Toverland and at Walibi Holland. The minimum height of each ride is on its page at
park.fan.
