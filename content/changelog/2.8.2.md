## New

- A banner on the park page warns when rain or a storm is coming (21 May), from 25 May up to an
  hour ahead, with bars for how hard it will rain and when.
- A °C/°F switch. Fahrenheit is the default only in the United States and the five other places
  that use it.

## Improved

- Ride pages and "Parks near you" refresh their live data every five minutes.
- The weather card shows wind with a compass, fog and snow, and a weather animation behind it.
