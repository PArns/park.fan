## New

- The glossary started with 90 terms in all six languages, each on its own page under a
  localised address (`/glossar`, `/glossaire`, `/woordenlijst` …), searchable from the site search.
- Wherever a glossary term appears on a park or ride page, the homepage or the guide, it is
  underlined, and hovering shows its definition.
- Park pages say when to go: the quietest weekdays, the next quiet days, and waits by month and by
  weekday from the park's own history (6 April). An "In the area" section lists the parks nearby.
- The calendar marks recommended days (31 March).
- Rides have a chart of today's waits with the day's low, high and trend (5 April), drawn in
  15-minute steps from 20 April, and their cards name the best time to ride.
- Headliners get a section of their own on the park page, and a badge marks rides that only run part of
  the year.
- Restaurant cards show whether they are open, their wait, their hours and whether you need to
  book.
- The homepage reports how accurate the predictions are: the average error, how many were made and
  how many rides they cover.
- Where a park has not published its hours, the hours card shows an estimate and says so.

## Improved

- The weather card has a forecast strip with day and night icons and a "feels like" temperature.
- The language switch keeps you on the same page, including the glossary's localised addresses.
- Walt Disney Studios Park is called Disney Adventure World, and its old address redirects.

## Fixed

- Opening times and virtual-queue return times were shown in your own time zone instead of the
  park's.
- A map pin could name a show that had already taken place.
