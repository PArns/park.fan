On Wednesday 11 November both Disneyland Paris parks were full, [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park) and [Disney Adventure World](ref:disney-adventure-world). Armistice Day is a public holiday in France and in Belgium, and plenty of families apparently had the same idea. At the entrance it said "Parcs complets – sauf avec billet et réservation" in French and English, so nobody got in without a ticket and a reservation. The fan site DLP Report posted a photo of it on X that day. The parks didn't close: both stayed open until 10 pm, an hour longer than on the Tuesday and the Thursday. Disneyland Paris hasn't announced how many guests were turned away at the gate.

## How busy the first Christmas week was

The Christmas season began on Saturday 7 November, and DLP Report only reported a full park for the holiday. In our calendar, Disneyland Park is on "High" on two days of that first week, the opening Saturday and the holiday. On the Monday and Tuesday in between it was on "Low".

"High" means you queue noticeably longer at the big rides than on a typical day at the park. Above it come "Very High" and "Extreme", and Disneyland Park hasn't reached either on a single day this year. From January to 12 November it has 68 days on "High", and the holiday is one of them, like the five days from 27 to 31 October during the French autumn school holidays. Disney Adventure World was even on "Normal" that Wednesday.

The way I read it: on a day like that, the entrance decides how your visit goes. Once you're in, you queue about as long as during the autumn holidays.

## Which tickets get you in on a full day

Without a reservation you get in on a full day with

- a dated ticket, which already has the date on it,
- any ticket if you're staying at a Disney hotel; you show your Magic Pass at the gate, and it also works on the days you check in and out,
- a hotel-and-ticket package booked directly with Disney.

An undated ticket is valid for a year from purchase, but never on 14 July, 31 October or 31 December. You register your visit date online while there's still space on it. Until 10 am on the day you can cancel the reservation and pick another date. With the Disneyland Pass, the annual pass, you register visit dates the same way, up to three at a time.

The ticket desks at the entrance only sell in exceptional cases, for cash, Chèques-Vacances or gift cards, and only while the park isn't at capacity. So once the parks were full on 11 November, there was nothing left to buy there.

## Christmas and New Year's Eve

The Christmas season runs until 6 January. We cover the parade, the tree and the first Christmas in World of Frozen in our post on [Christmas and New Year's Eve in Paris and Orlando](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026). The next public holidays in France are Friday 25 December and Friday 1 January. The French Christmas school holidays start on Saturday 19 December, and school goes back on Monday 4 January. On New Year's Eve both parks are open until 1 am. An undated ticket isn't valid that day at all, and no 6 am alarm will change that.

If you want to come during the holidays on an undated ticket or the annual pass, register the date now. If you don't have a ticket yet, buy a dated one for a holiday date, and getting in is settled the moment you pay.
