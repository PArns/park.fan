## New

- Favourites have a page of their own at `/favorites` (16 September). The menu's "view all" used to
  lead to the top of the homepage, where the band sat two screens down. `/favorites` and `/alerts`
  are linked from the header menu in every state and from the footer.
- In the calendar's day view, each headliner's forecast carries its spread in the planner's
  notation, and says so when the model reported none (15 September).
- The estimate for how long a ride stays down is drawn as well as written: a bar on a fixed scale
  from now to four hours, and a meter for the probability (15 September).

## Improved

- Each section behind "More" in the header is a card with an icon that links as a whole, and the
  blog has its own entry in the bar again, under Backstage (16 September).
- The footer's links sit in three named columns, content, tools and legal, where one row broke
  wherever the width ran out (18 September).
- With a mouse, a planner block moves in five-minute steps instead of fifteen, and the ghost shows
  its time however short the block is (18 September).

## Fixed

- On 18 September every map tile on every park page came back as "403 Access blocked", because
  OpenStreetMap's own tile server is not meant for embedding. Both maps draw from CARTO's basemap
  CDN, credited to OpenStreetMap and CARTO.
