[SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando) is putting on 29 concerts at Bayside Stadium for the Seven Seas Food Festival, from January 30 to May 16, 2027. There's a show every Saturday and on most Sundays, always at 7 pm. The concerts come with park admission, and a reserved spot costs from $9.99 on top. Flo Rida opens the lineup, which SeaWorld announced on November 19.

## Festival and concert dates

The festival itself starts the day before the first concert, on Friday, January 29, and runs until Sunday, May 16. The food booths are only open on selected days. SeaWorld hasn't published which days those are in 2027. In 2026 the booths opened Thursday through Sunday. On the opening Friday, the park closes at 6 pm.

On the first concert weekend, SeaWorld is open from 9 am to 9 pm. A set usually runs 60 to 75 minutes. On most concert nights in February and March, the park closes at 9 pm, and at 10 pm on the Sundays in March. On February 13 and 27 it closes at 7 pm, just as the concert starts, and on March 27 at 8 pm.

There's no show on Super Bowl Sunday (February 14), Easter Sunday (March 28) or Mother's Day (May 9). SeaWorld isn't competing with football or with Mom. That leaves 16 Saturday concerts and 13 on Sundays.

The festival also includes Mardi Gras on February 9, St. Patrick's Day on March 17, Cinco de Mayo on May 5 and Viva La Música on selected days in May.

## Open and reserved seating

With a park ticket or an annual pass, you can sit in the stadium's open seats if you get there early enough. Once it's full, entry can close. To be sure of getting in, you can buy Reserved Concert Access on top of your park ticket, in three tiers:

- Standard from $9.99, a seat in the middle of the lower bowl
- Up-Close from $14.99, the second section on the floor behind Stagefront, with access to the Floor Bar
- Stagefront from $24.99, the front section on the floor right in front of the stage, also with the Floor Bar

Each concert has a limited number of reserved spots. Check-in for all three tiers is to the left of the stage, near Pipeline: The Surf Coaster, and opens at 2 pm. Spots aren't numbered, so within your section it's first come, first served again. At 13 concerts, among them Flo Rida, Shaggy, Lil Jon and Luísa Sonza, Stagefront and Up-Close are standing areas without chairs. Drinks at the Floor Bar cost extra.

Rain alone doesn't end a concert. If a thunderstorm moves in, the show is paused, pushed back or moved indoors, to the Nautilus Theater for example. It can get tighter there, but anyone with a reserved spot is guaranteed to get in.

The 2026 Seven Seas VIP Tour also came with a reserved spot, plus a lanyard good for five samples of food or drinks and priority access to the rides. It cost from $199.99, and SeaWorld hasn't published the 2027 price yet.

## Tickets, annual passes and blockout dates

In the Black Friday sale, a single-day ticket costs from $61.99 online instead of $151.99, for anyone three and older. The 2027 Fun Card costs $104.99 instead of $151.99, less than two day tickets, and is valid on every day except blockout dates. Annual passes cost $171 a year or $14.25 a month for Bronze, $198 for Silver, $222 for Gold and $351 for Platinum.

During the festival, Bronze and the Fun Card are blocked out on the four concert Saturdays from March 13 to April 3. If you hold Bronze and need more cowbell from Blue Öyster Cult, you'll need a day ticket that night, or Silver, since Silver and up have no blockout dates in 2027. Food and drink discounts are 5 percent with Silver, 10 with Gold and 15 percent with Platinum, and none with Bronze.

Parking costs from $35. SeaWorld doesn't take cash at the entrance. The park is at 7007 Sea World Drive, reached from I-4 via Central Florida Parkway. Lynx bus route 50 stops nearby. ## What's changed since 2026

The 2026 lineup had 32 dates, five of them with a surprise guest, and there were shows on seven Fridays too. In 2027 it's weekends only. Besides Flo Rida, Grupo Manía, Proyecto Uno and Soulja Boy are back from 2026. The reserved spots were called Reserved Seating in 2026 and also started at $9.99.

Park president Brad Gilmour says some of the 2027 dishes will take their cue from the music. The 2027 booths and menus are still to come. In 2026, more than 200 dishes and drinks were spread across 29 booths, eight of them new.

## Concerts in San Diego and San Antonio, SEAQuest in Orlando

With the sister parks, the 2027 series adds up to more than 50 concerts. [SeaWorld San Diego](ref:seaworld-san-diego) has concerts on Saturdays at 6 pm at Bayside Amphitheater from March 20 to August 28, with reserved seats there from $69.99. [SeaWorld San Antonio](ref:seaworld-san-antonio) has seven dates on Saturdays and Sundays from April 11 to May 23 at Nautilus Amphitheater, included with admission as in Orlando.

Orlando is also set to open SEAQuest: Legends of the Deep in 2027, a suspended dark ride in an expanded area of the park. There's no date yet. Through Passport to Thrills, Pass Members who visit three times between November 18 and December 31 get a one-time Quick Queue for SEAQuest. Until January 4, SeaWorld Orlando is still running its [Christmas Celebration](/news/seaworld-orlando-christmas-celebration-2026) with the orca show Miracles.
