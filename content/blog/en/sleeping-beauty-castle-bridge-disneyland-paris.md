For the 35th anniversary of [Sleeping Beauty Castle](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/sleeping-beauty-castle) in [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), Disneyland Paris is set to reopen the bridge in front of it. It had been closed since May 2026. The park announced this on 12 October without giving a date for the reopening.

## What's new on the bridge

The granite setts are easier to maintain than the old stamped concrete. The 2,735 stones follow the curve of the arch.

The balusters of the parapet, in Champagny limestone, were rebuilt from scratch and waterproofed against spray from the fountains below. The wooden posts of the balustrade are new, and the drawbridge has a new deck of tropical hardwood.

The structure that carries the decorative pieces was rebuilt too, along with the arches, the corbels and the ornamented spandrels between the arches. What looks like masonry on the side walls and under the arches is artificial stone, a paste sprayed onto a metal frame and shaped into stones by hand. The pieces are painted in five shades to match the castle's stone. Last came the patina and fresh paint, and the beds along the bridge were replanted.

## The compass rose

The compass rose, natural stone with brass inlays, carries the colours of the three good fairies from "Sleeping Beauty". Merryweather's blue rays and Flora's red ones run in opposite directions. Disney ties this to the scene where the two of them squabble over the colour of Aurora's dress while she dances with Prince Philip. A green arc for Fauna, the one who keeps the peace in the film, joins the two colours.

At the centre is a brass medallion with Aurora's crown, ringed by thorns. A brass fleur-de-lis points north. It recalls four brass letters that marked the points of the compass on the bridge from the day the park opened. They were removed during a renovation in 2010. A craftsman made every brass piece, each worked on three levels with different finishes.

One of the castle windows refers to the same scene and, since June 2024, keeps changing colour in the evening.

> [!QUOTE]
> This renovation was made possible thanks to the involvement of numerous trades and the wealth of their expertise. We called upon experts in the restoration of historic monuments to take care of Sleeping Beauty Castle, which is, in its own way, a landmark of Disneyland Paris. Our aim was to restore the bridge to its former glory, whilst preserving the details that give it its character, so that it may continue to form an integral part of the Castle for many years to come.
>
> Stefano Servi, Stage Architect at Walt Disney Imagineering Paris, in [Disneyland Paris's announcement of 12 October 2026](https://news.disneylandparis.com/en/sleeping-beauty-castle-bridge-refurbishment/)

## The castle at 35

The castle was unveiled on 12 October 1991 at an event called "Rendez-Vous au Château", in front of the press, cast members and their families. The park opened half a year later, on 12 April 1992. For the resort's fifth birthday in 1997 the castle wore carnival decorations for "The Hunchback of Notre Dame".

In 2021 the whole castle was given its first major renovation since 1992. Nine French companies worked on it, covering carpentry, roofing, painting and metalwork. The roof and its timber frame went to Le Bras Frères, a company that usually restores historic monuments. Adrien Beaugendre is a roofer there and has held the title "Meilleur Ouvrier de France" for roofing since 2015. In a piece published by the park he said the frame and the slates were made exactly as on a real French château.

Since June 2024 more than 200 new LED floodlights have lit the castle from all sides. Disney Tales of Magic, a show projected onto the Main Street facades and the castle, has run in the evenings since 2025.

The castle is about 51 metres (167 ft) tall, with towers under royal blue roofs. It's modelled on the castle in the animated "Sleeping Beauty" and on real French châteaux and historic buildings.

## The gallery and the dragon's lair

Stairs lead up to the first floor and [La Galerie de la Belle au Bois Dormant](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/la-galerie-de-la-belle-au-bois-dormant), where Aubusson tapestries and nine stained-glass windows show scenes from the fairy tale, with storybooks on display. The windows are by Paul Chapman, who has also restored windows at Notre-Dame de Paris. One of them shows Aurora and Philip kissing.

Below the castle is [La Tanière du Dragon](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/la-taniere-du-dragon), a dark dungeon where a defeated dragon lies chained to the rock. Every so often it wakes with a roar and puffs smoke.

The gallery and the dragon's lair stayed open throughout the work, and so did La Boutique du Château and the Boutique Merlin l'Enchanteur.

## Around the castle this autumn

The castle stands at the end of Main Street, U.S.A. In front of it is Central Plaza, where the paths split off to the other four lands. Walk across the bridge and through the castle and you are in Fantasyland.

The Disney Halloween Festival runs until 1 November, and its decorations stretch from Main Street to the foot of the castle. At the Royal Castle Stage, thorns, ravens and the crests of Disney villains decorate the gates, and a ruin has been built at the foot of the castle as a photo backdrop. The Théâtre du Château beside the castle is dressed as a haunted ruin as well. On 31 October a special programme runs until 11 pm with its own fireworks, and that's the only day of the year when guests of all ages may visit in costume.

[Disney Enchanted Christmas](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026) follows from 7 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, with Main Street decorated and a 24-metre Christmas tree. From 14 November Minnie and Mrs. Claus appear at the Royal Castle Stage. On New Year's Eve fireworks go up over Sleeping Beauty Castle at midnight.
