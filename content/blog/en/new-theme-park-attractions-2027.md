As of 5 October 2026, parks in Europe and the USA had confirmed a good two dozen new attractions for
2027 themselves, among them a spinning coaster in New Jersey that Six Flags says will be the world's tallest, and an Efteling fairytale set inside a library. So far only Plopsaland Belgium has put a firm date on a new
attraction, and its Flying Cinema opens on Saturday, 13 March 2027. Everyone else gives the year,
a few give a season, and for more than half of them the source doesn't say who's building the ride.

Germany is having a quiet year. None of the big parks has announced a new coaster for 2027, and
Europa-Park's Euro-Mir is going. The big projects are in France, England and Denmark, and in the
USA Six Flags alone has six parks building something.

The next big chance for announcements is the IAAPA Expo, the industry trade show in Orlando from
17 to 20 November 2026. Anything that's confirmed there goes onto this page afterwards.

## What makes the list

A new attraction goes on the list when the park, its operator or the manufacturer names it on
their own site or in their own press release. Rumours, planning applications and photos of
building sites don't count, even when the trade press picks them up. Whatever is doing the rounds
that way sits further down under "What isn't on the list", with the source it came from.

Timing, manufacturer and figures appear the way the source gives them. "2027" means 2027, with no
month. Where two official pages disagree, you'll find both figures here. For the American rides
I've kept the feet, mph and inches the parks use and put rounded metric figures in brackets. Each
section links its source, and the full list is at the end. I opened every page on 5 October 2026.

## Germany

### Hansa-Park: Buddenbrook-Turm

[Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park) lists the Buddenbrook-Turm on its website as new for 2027. It's due
to open at some point during the season, and the park hasn't given a month. The tower is going up
in the Hansa-Garten themed area, and its attraction page carries two icons, one for indoor or
covered and one for guests with disabilities. There's no height, manufacturer or capacity on the
page, and nothing about whether you ride to the top or climb up. The other rides on the site each
have a box of facts and figures, and the tower doesn't have one yet.

The park doesn't explain the name either. "Buddenbrooks" is Thomas Mann's novel
about a merchant family in Lübeck, and Lübeck is a little over 30 kilometres south of Sierksdorf.
Hansa-Park opened on 15 May 1977, which makes 2027 its fiftieth season. The park plans to run it
from 25 March to 24 October 2027.

In 2026 the park opened its eighth coaster, the Cornwall Coaster, on 23 May. According to its
attraction page, the Gerstlauer ride has seven launches over about 570 metres, reaches up to
50 km/h and stands 18 metres tall. In the March press release it was still six launches. We don't
measure wait times at Hansa-Park, and the park only puts them in its own app. Everything else you
need to know there is in the [Hansa-Park guide](/blog/hansa-park-tips).

### Europa-Park and Rulantica

[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) has no new ride for 2027. Instead it opens earlier than usual, with
its first Pre-Opening Weeks and cheaper tickets from 7 to 19 March, and the summer season starts
on 20 March. The details are in
[our report on the Pre-Opening Weeks](/blog/europa-park-pre-opening-weeks-2027).
[Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir) only runs until 9 January 2027, and its section is further
down under "What goes away in 2027".

At the [Rulantica](ref:rulantica) water park, a two-storey building called Nørd Havn is going up.
There's a restaurant on the ground floor, and upstairs you'll find lounging areas in three places:
indoors, in a conservatory and on outdoor terraces. It doesn't include a slide. The park says Nørd
Havn will open its doors at the end of 2027.

### Phantasialand, Heide Park, Movie Park and the others

For [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand), [Heide Park](ref:heide-park),
[Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany), [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland)
and [Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) I found no confirmed new attraction for
2027 on 5 October. At Phantasialand anything bigger depends on the expansion into the land by the
Ententeich, the duck pond, which still has no development plan, and a petition against it has been
collecting signatures since 28 September. What the park wants to build there is in
[our report on the petition](/blog/phantasialand-expansion-petition). Movie Park is up for sale
along with its parent company, Parques Reunidos, and there's been no announcement for 2027 from
that side.

## Benelux

### Plopsaland Belgium: Flying Cinema

The Flying Cinema at [Plopsaland Belgium](ref:plopsaland-belgium) has the earliest date of any new
attraction here. It opens to everyone on Saturday, 13 March 2027. From 19 December 2026 it already
runs on selected days for annual pass holders and guests staying overnight at the park.
Construction has been under way since the end of September 2025.

It's a flying theatre. Riders sit in a gondola that moves in front of a large projection dome,
with wind and scents added. According to the attraction page a flight lasts about five minutes and
passes over Iceland, Venice and Paris before arriving at Plopsaland itself. The August press
release has the tulip fields of the Netherlands and the Plopsa park in the Ardennes in place of
Iceland. The ride system comes from Mack Rides, according to the Dutch-language press release of
September 2025, and Barco from Kortrijk supplies picture and sound. The attraction is under cover,
sits right by the entrance, and you board at ground level.

The Flying Cinema is part of Plopsaland Plaza, which at 25 million euros is the biggest investment
in the park's history. The Plaza also gets a grand café, a bowling alley, arcade machines and a new
station for the Plopsa Express. On summer evenings it stays open to anyone after the park closes,
and the Flying Cinema then needs a ticket of its own. The first announcement said different films
would run in the evening than during the day. With the Plaza, Plopsa wants to reach two million
visitors a year.

### Efteling: the Fairytale Library

[Efteling](ref:efteling) is building its 32nd fairytale in the Fairytale Forest, the Fairytale
Library. It stands where the Chinese Nightingale stood until March 2026. Visitors walk into a
nine-metre-tall building full of books with stories a fairytale collector gathered on his travels,
and seven busy dwarfs are part of it. The park says the building's framework is already up. Its "New in Efteling" page has the library as new from 2027, with no month.

Ton van de Ven designed the Chinese Nightingale in its last form, and it had stood in the forest in that form since 1999. The building was in poor condition, and work started on 9 March 2026. The Nightingale
will come back in a new form near its old spot, but Efteling hasn't said when or how. The
ornamental garden in front of it stays. It's one of the ten fairytales the Fairytale Forest opened
with in 1952.

Before the library, Efteling reopens its Raveleijn area on 1 December 2026 after almost a year of
rebuilding, with a stunt show the park developed with Puy du Fou and Vekoma's Ravenring, for
20 million euros in total. That's in [our report on Raveleijn](/blog/efteling-raveleijn-ravenring).
Efteling's next coaster, Missie Luminar, isn't planned until 2029 and will be the park's first
suspended launch coaster. For how busy Efteling gets on which day, see the
[Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).

### Bobbejaanland: a new area

[Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) is building a new themed area for 2027. So far the park's
website has a single sentence about it, a note for drivers: the charging points in the car park
are out of service because of work on the "nieuwe zone van 2027", the new zone of 2027. The park
hasn't yet published on its own pages which attractions the area will have, who's building them or
what it costs. It's on the list because the park names it for 2027, and the source
has nothing more. Like Movie Park, Bobbejaanland belongs to Parques Reunidos, whose sale is covered
in [a separate post](/blog/parques-reunidos-sale-movie-park).

## France

### Disney Adventure World: Wilderness Explorers Sky Swings

Disneyland Paris's second park, now called [Disney Adventure World](ref:disney-adventure-world),
gets the first attraction based on Pixar's "Up" in any Disney park in 2027. Wilderness Explorers
Sky Swings is a family swing ride that spins up in the air, with a view out over the park. It sits
on Adventure Way opposite Raiponce Tangled Spin, and a garden styled on the film goes with it.
Disney hasn't named a month or a manufacturer.

The "Lion King" area Disney is building in the same park still has no year. The
[Disneyland Paris guide](/blog/disneyland-paris-wait-times-tips) has what you need to know for a day
in both Disney parks.

### Parc Astérix: expanding the Greek area

[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) is enlarging its Greek area in 2027 with two family attractions,
a playground and La Table de Dionysos, a restaurant with 450 seats. In its press area the park
doesn't give the names or manufacturers of the two attractions. Next to the park, the 300-room
Hotel L'Odyssée also opens in spring 2027, designed around Asterix and Obelix's travels to the
Orient. The [Parc Astérix guide](/blog/parc-asterix-wait-times-tips) lists where you queue longest
there today.

### Nigloland: Supersonic 1887

In [Nigloland](ref:nigloland), in Dolancourt in the Champagne region, Supersonic 1887 is going up, a Mack Rides coaster, 47 metres tall, and the park gives its top speed as
almost 100 km/h. The project costs 18 million euros, the biggest investment in the park's history,
and goes back to 2022. The park's own team designed the train.

The track is 355 metres long, but the train runs it forwards and backwards, so it covers twice the
distance. It's launched electrically six times in all, across three sections. According to the
graphic on the park's page, the ride has three inversions, one of them a vertical loop, six
moments of airtime and forces from −1.6 to +4.1 g. There's also an element called the "Supersonic
Spin", based on a double Immelmann, eight "Wing Seats" mounted beside the track, and an extra car
at the front, the "0 car".

The coaster stands in a new area themed to a World's Fair of 1887, where existing attractions are
being turned into national pavilions. The story revolves around a fictional engineer called Émile
Delaroche. Trade outlets give 28 March 2027 as the opening day, but the park's page only says
"2027". Nigloland's calendar currently runs to 11 November 2026.

### Futuroscope

[Futuroscope](ref:futuroscope) near Poitiers turns forty in 2027 and opens the season on
6 February 2027 with a new IMAX film in the Kinemax, "ATHOS", about the Patrouille de France
aerobatic team. The park hasn't announced a new ride for 2027.

## Britain

### Chessington World of Adventures: Minecraft World

[Chessington](ref:chessington-world-of-adventures) in south-west London opens Minecraft World in
2027, which Merlin and Mojang describe as the first area themed to the game at a major theme park.
The project costs £50 million. It includes a Minecraft coaster that Merlin says exists nowhere
else, interactive attractions, play areas built from blocks, shops and restaurants. On
26 September 2026 Merlin also announced a Minecraft hotel at the park for 2027. Neither press
release gives the coaster's name, manufacturer or ride stats.

### Paultons Park: Serpent's Curse

[Paultons Park](ref:paultons-park) near Southampton is building a Mack Rides water coaster, in its
own words one of the biggest rides the park has ever built. Serpent's Curse has a lift hill and
fast turns, and it ends with a splash into the water. It's going into an extension of Valgard, the
park's Viking area, and is aimed at families with older children and at teenagers.

The park hasn't set a date ("The exact date is yet to be confirmed") or a minimum height yet. The
ride is included in standard admission. Seal Falls, the children's water ride from 2002, moves to
Valgard as part of the same work and becomes Little Serpents, for children aged seven and under
with no minimum height.

## Scandinavia

### Djurs Sommerland: Midgårdsormen

Midgårdsormen at [Djurs Sommerland](ref:djurs-sommerland) is an Intamin Hot Racer, a coaster on a
single rail with cars in which riders sit in a line, one behind the other. The park's figures are
603 metres of track, a height of 23 metres, 75 km/h, two launches, three inversions, a ride of
about 107 seconds and a minimum height of 120 centimetres. The park calls it the tallest and
fastest ride of its kind in Europe.

It's the park's tenth coaster and its first with inversions. Djurs Sommerland puts the investment
at more than 100 million Danish kroner, a little over 13 million euros, in a press release that
also lists a restaurant, a playground and a shop in the enlarged area. The Viking area,
Vikingeland, grows by 15,000 square metres for it, next to Thors Hammer, the coaster from 2002.
The theme is the sea serpent of Norse mythology that gives the ride its name. In spring 2027 the
park will raffle nine places as test pilots, and it hasn't given an opening date.

### Gröna Lund: SCORE!

Behind a construction fence in the middle of [Gröna Lund](ref:grona-lund) in Stockholm, SCORE! is
taking shape, an indoor interactive dark ride that opens in 2027. The vehicles come from Vekoma, run without a track and are steered by radar, and JoraVision designed the hall together with the park's own design team. Everyone sharing a car collects points over the four and a half minutes and plays against the others. The park calls the attraction the only one of its kind in the world and expects up to 540 riders an hour.

### Liseberg: Oceana

[Liseberg](ref:liseberg) in Gothenburg is building Oceana, a water park of about 13,600 square
metres, 6,000 of them indoors and 4,000 outdoors. Four larger slides are planned, along with three
children's areas, a wave pool and a river for families. Liseberg says Oceana will open in early 2027.

## USA

### Six Flags Great Adventure: Bakunawa

The tallest new ride of the year is in New Jersey. Bakunawa at
[Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) is a spinning launch coaster from Mack
Rides, 382 feet (116 m) tall with a top speed of 100 mph (161 km/h). Six Flags calls it the
world's tallest and fastest spinning coaster and lists six records, among them the first launch
with the train hanging upside down and the first floorless spinning coaster. Six Flags doesn't name
the manufacturer itself, but Mack Rides confirmed it in an announcement of its own.

The track is 3,163 feet (964 m) long, and a ride lasts 2 minutes 17 seconds. The train is launched
three times, to 60 mph (97 km/h), then upside down to 80 mph (129 km/h) and finally to 100 mph
(161 km/h). Two trains of five cars run, each car with four seats back to back, and the cars spin
freely. The minimum height is 48 inches (122 cm). Bakunawa is named after a sea serpent from
Philippine mythology and will be the main ride of The Boardwalk, an area that's being rebuilt.

Two Six Flags pages give different heights. A blog post of 3 July 2026 reported that the tower had
reached its full height of 375 feet, while the park page and Mack Rides give 382 feet, or
116 metres.

### Six Flags Magic Mountain: Thrill Glider

The Thrill Glider at [Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain) is a new ride type
from Vekoma, the first "Thrillglider". Riders lie on their stomachs on a "hovercycle", facing
forward, and hang below the track. The ride is 107 feet (just under 33 m) tall and 3,380 feet
(1,030 m) long, reaches up to 50 mph (80 km/h) and has five inversions and twelve moments of
airtime. The park page lists two launches and two trains of 16 seats each. The minimum height is
48 inches (122 cm).

The ride time comes in two versions, 80 seconds in the press release of 10 September 2026 and
1 minute 24 seconds on the park page. The coaster is going up on the site of the former Golden
Bear Theatre, between Full Throttle and Lex Luthor: Drop of Doom. There's more in
[our report of 3 October](/blog/thrill-glider-six-flags-magic-mountain), including the story told
in the queue. In the same park, [X2](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain/x2) hasn't run since 12 July
2026, and on 29 September Six Flags retired it for good. In neither announcement does Six Flags
link the closure to the Thrill Glider. The background is in
[our report on the closure](/blog/x2-six-flags-magic-mountain-closed).

### Universal Studios Florida: Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift

The coaster that opened in Hollywood on 16 September 2026 is coming to Orlando in 2027 as well. At
[Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida), Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift
replaces the former Hollywood Rip Ride Rockit coaster. Universal describes cars that spin
360 degrees as if they were drifting, and a 170-foot (52 m) vertical "spike" that lifts riders out
over the edge of Universal CityWalk.

Universal announced the ride on 20 January 2026 and hasn't been more precise than 2027 since, nor
has it said whether the Orlando ride will match Hollywood's stats. The California version is by Intamin, reaches up to 72 mph (116 km/h) and has four launches,
as covered in [our report on the Hollywood opening](/blog/hollywood-drift-universal-studios-hollywood).

### Six Flags Fiesta Texas: Werewolf Gorge

[Six Flags Fiesta Texas](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas) in San Antonio celebrates its 35th season in
2027 and is building Werewolf Gorge for it, a family launch coaster from Vekoma. At 4,120 feet
(1,256 m) it's the longest of its kind, according to Six Flags and Vekoma. The ride lasts
2 minutes 36 seconds and has four launches with linear motors, a rollback partway along the track
and 32 moments of airtime. It never goes faster than 45 mph (72 km/h), and the first launch takes
the train from 6 to 43 mph (10 to 69 km/h) in three seconds.

The minimum height is 39 inches (99 cm). The biggest
drop is 40 feet (12 m), and the track rises and falls 85 feet (26 m) in all. Three trains with 16
seats each run on 2.47 acres (about one hectare). The story is set in the old quarry the park
really does sit in, and it revolves around a werewolf and a roadside attraction.

### Silver Dollar City: Miner's Mountain Express

[Silver Dollar City](ref:silver-dollar-city) in Missouri is building a family coaster with Mack
Rides for spring 2027. Miner's Mountain Express is 3,553 feet (1,083 m) long, which the park says makes it the longest family coaster in the American Midwest. It has two lift hills, the higher one 97 feet (30 m), and 15
moments of airtime, more than any other ride in the park. A ride lasts 3 minutes 18 seconds, and
children can ride from 39 inches (99 cm) with an adult.

The ride runs through Daredevil Pass, a new area between Wildfire, Powder Keg and American Plunge.
Above it stands a 15-storey headframe from a real Missouri mine. The story goes back to the Marble
Cave Mining Company, which worked the site in the 1880s. Mack Rides built Time Traveler for the
park, too.

### Six Flags Great America: Camp Timber Trail

At [Six Flags Great America](ref:six-flags-great-america) near Chicago, Yukon Territory, an area
that's been there since the park opened in 1976, becomes Camp Timber Trail, with nine attractions.
The new ones are Sky Hawk, a suspended family coaster the park calls the longest, tallest and
fastest of its kind in the Midwest, a trail with animals called Trail Trotters and the Forest Grove
Play Park playground.

Four existing rides get a new theme and new names. Sprocket Rockets becomes Chirp Chaser, Crazy
Bus turns into Wilderness Wagon, Yahoo River into Creek Cruiser and Buzzy Beez into Bee Boppin'.
The wooden coaster Little Dipper and the log flume
[Logger's Run](ref:six-flags-great-america/loggers-run) stay as they are. Six Flags hasn't named
Sky Hawk's manufacturer or given its stats. The park calls Camp Timber Trail the first step in a
rebuild spread over several years.

### Carowinds: Rip Roarin' Falls

[Carowinds](ref:carowinds), on the border between North and South Carolina, is building a water
ride set to take three world records. Rip Roarin' Falls is a WhiteWater Super Flume with boats for
eight. At the end it drops 100 feet (30 m) at up to 50 mph (80 km/h). Along the way two switches
change the direction of travel, so the boat also runs backwards. The records are the tallest drop
on a flume, the tallest backwards drop at 42 feet (13 m) and the tallest backwards hill at 32 feet
(just under 10 m).

The course is 2,240 feet (683 m) long, and a ride takes six and a half minutes. It's going up on
the site of Rip Roarin' Rapids, a river rapids ride that closed in 2019, with its entrance in the
Thrill Zone near Fury 325. Children can ride from 35 inches (89 cm) with an adult and from
41 inches (104 cm) on their own. The fact box in the same press release gives one inch more.

### Disney's Hollywood Studios: Monstropolis

Part of Monstropolis, the "Monsters, Inc." area at
[Disney's Hollywood Studios](ref:disneys-hollywood-studios), opens in 2027. The coaster with the
doors isn't part of it yet, and Disney says work on it continues. Disney gives no year for the
coaster. All it has said is that it will be the first suspended coaster in a Disney park and the
first with a vertical lift.

What opens in 2027 is the Glob Theater show "Welcome to Monstropolis", hosted by Mike and Sulley
with a song by Randy Newman, the restaurant Archie's Scare Pig Pizza with the Monsters University
Alumni Club next door, the restaurant Harryhausen's with an Audio-Animatronics sushi chef, and the
Scareporium shop. To make room, Disney closed the Grand Avenue area in 2025. Muppet*Vision 3D
played there for the last time on 7 June 2025, and the Muppets have taken over the
[Rock 'n' Roller Coaster](ref:disneys-hollywood-studios/rock-n-roller-coaster-starring-aerosmith).

### Disney's Animal Kingdom: Tropical Americas

[Disney's Animal Kingdom](ref:disneys-animal-kingdom-theme-park) is building Tropical Americas, an
area themed to Central and South America with the village of Pueblo Esperanza at its centre. It
gets two ride-throughs, Antonio's Fiesta de Encanto, set in the Madrigal family's house from
"Encanto", and Indiana Jones and the Myth of the Jade Serpent in a Maya temple, plus a carousel
with animals. On the Disney Parks Blog, Disney writes "when it opens in 2027", and
disneyexperiences.com has "Set to open in 2027". Disney gives no month.

### More new attractions and refurbishments in the USA

The [Knott's Soak City](ref:knotts-soak-city) water park in California gets two raft slides in
2027 for up to five riders each. Coral Craze has an element called the "Flying Saucer", and Six
Flags says Kelp Kraze is the first "Family Hive" in North America.

[Busch Gardens Williamsburg](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg) is bringing back an old attraction in
a new form. Return to Corkscrew Hill is in the park's Irish section, where guests on a castle tour
are shrunk to the size of the little folk and have to escape a witch. The park's page says "Coming
2027" and gives no ride type.

At [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park), Walt Disney's Carousel of Progress has been closed
since 6 July 2026. Disney is reworking the show and expects it to reopen in late spring 2027.

At [Hersheypark](ref:hersheypark) the sooperDooperLooper, a coaster from 1977, gets a new station
with light and sound effects. It was due to close after Labor Day 2026 and is set to run again in summer 2027.

## What goes away in 2027

Two coasters in Europe run for the last time this winter, and both are being replaced.

### Europa-Park: Euro-Mir

[Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir) has run in Europa-Park's Russian area since 12 June 1997.
Mack Rides built it over three years. Nine trains with rotating cars circle the mirrored towers.
The ride is 28.3 metres tall, reaches up to 80 km/h and carries 16 riders per train. According to
the park, about 1.5 million people rode it in its first year.

Its last laps come in the winter season, which runs from 28 November 2026 to 9 January 2027. On
that last day the park says goodbye with a big event, and dismantling starts afterwards. In its
place comes a space-themed area of its own with a new Mack Rides family coaster, due to be ready
for the 2028 season. The park is working with the European Space Agency, ESA, on it, and the
partnership was signed on 8 June 2026. There's more in
[our report on the farewell](/blog/europa-park-euro-mir-farewell).

### Walibi Belgium: Loup-Garou

[Loup-Garou](ref:walibi-belgium/loup-garou) at [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) is a wooden
coaster, 28 metres tall and 1,035 metres long, with a top speed of 80 km/h. It runs in this form
for the last time on 3 January 2027, the last day of the park's winter opening, which starts on
5 December 2026. Work begins in January 2027, and Rocky Mountain Construction will rebuild the
ride as a hybrid coaster due to open in 2028.

The new ride is meant to be 49 metres tall, reach 106 km/h and, at 1,231 metres, be the longest in
the Benelux, with a first drop of 47 metres at 89 degrees and three elements found nowhere else.
One passage in the press kit says 48 metres, the table of figures 49. The ride is the start of
Festival World, an area styled on New Orleans. The 4D cinema goes in 2028 to make room for it, and
by 2030 the area gets redesigned family rides, family drop towers, a water playground and a
restaurant. Walibi hasn't published a name for the new ride yet.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which new coaster is the tallest in 2027?

Bakunawa at Six Flags Great Adventure, at 382 feet (116 m), and at 100 mph (161 km/h) it's also
the fastest. In Europe it's Supersonic 1887 at Nigloland, with 47 metres and almost 100 km/h.

### Is there a new roller coaster in Germany in 2027?

Not as of 5 October 2026. Hansa-Park is building a tower, Rulantica a restaurant, and Europa-Park
plans its next new coaster for 2028. The nearest new coasters are at Nigloland in Champagne and
Djurs Sommerland in Denmark.

### Which new attraction opens first in 2027?

The Flying Cinema at Plopsaland Belgium, on 13 March 2027. If you have an annual pass or stay
overnight at the park, you can fly from 19 December 2026 on selected days.

### Which coasters close in 2027?

Loup-Garou at Walibi Belgium runs in its current form for the last time on 3 January 2027, and
Euro-Mir at Europa-Park on 9 January 2027. Both are being replaced, Loup-Garou by a rebuild in 2028
and Euro-Mir by a new family coaster the same year.

### When will the next new attractions be announced?

The next chance is the IAAPA Expo trade show in Orlando, with the exhibition running from 17 to
20 November 2026. The Six Flags parks presented their 2027 additions between May and September
2026, and Merlin presented its own in March and September.
