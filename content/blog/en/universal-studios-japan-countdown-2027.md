[Universal Studios Japan](ref:universal-studios-japan) in Osaka stays open for 26 hours in a row over New Year, from 7 p.m. on 31 December to 9 p.m. on 1 January 2027. For that whole stretch you only get in with a Party Pass. It costs from 16,900 yen including tax. General sale opens on 4 November at around 9 p.m. Japan time, which is 1 p.m. in Germany and noon in the UK, and it only gets the passes the presales haven't sold. The event is called "NO LIMIT! Countdown 2027 ~Discover U!!!~" and is part of the park's 25th birthday. Before that, the winter event Universal Holiday Wonders starts on 13 November.

## What the Party Pass gets you

The Party Pass lets you leave and come back as often as you like during those 26 hours. Arrive at 7 p.m., stay until closing on New Year's Day, and you'll be as wrecked as after a long-haul flight. The Party Pass is sold on the USJ website, through Lawson Ticket and at partner hotels, where only guests can buy it. The presale for Grand Royal annual pass holders is over. An online presale through Lawson Ticket runs until 11:59 p.m. on 3 November, first come, first served. In bad weather USJ can cut the night's programme short or call it off, even if you already hold a Party Pass.

On top of the Party Pass, the park sells spots in reserved areas in front of the shows, seated or standing, plus the Universal Express Pass and packages with dinner at one of the park's restaurants. The most expensive package costs 1,500,000 yen and is sold exactly once, to one group of up to four. It includes a room at the Park Front Hotel, a pickup from Kansai or Itami airport or a major station in Osaka, and your own spot for the fireworks. I go through how the Express Pass works and when it pays off in our [guide to wait times and the Express Pass at Universal Studios Japan](/blog/universal-studios-japan-wait-times-tips).

## With a regular ticket on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day

A Studio Pass for 31 December is only valid until 5 p.m., and that goes for annual passes too. Between 5 and 7 p.m. the park is closed to everyone. A regular ticket for 1 January gets you in from 2 a.m., an hour when most people's New Year plans involve a bed.

## The programme on the night

The Countdown Special Stage runs at Gramercy Park in the New York area from 7:05 p.m. on 31 December until 12:20 a.m. On stage are the performers from the 25th anniversary parade, the Blues Brothers and the Sesame Street street show, plus special versions of shows from the summer festival and from Halloween. A DJ show plays music by Zedd, who also did the music on Space Fantasy – The Ride from January to August 2026.

The Countdown Moment follows from 11:50 p.m. to 12:05 a.m., with about 5,000 fireworks over the lagoon, over Gramercy Park and across the rest of the park, with lights and music. After the countdown the park's characters greet the new year in traditional Japanese clothes.

New this year is the "USJ Grand Music Revival", a kind of New Year's Eve singing contest in the [Universal Monsters Live Rock and Roll Show](ref:universal-studios-japan/universal-monsters-live-rock-and-roll-show). From 11:45 p.m. to 12:30 a.m., Reika Ayanokoji, Deacon, Beetlejuice and Woody Woodpecker sing old songs from the park's 25 years. You need a separate add-on ticket for the show, and it runs right across the Countdown Moment.

## What's closed that night

Super Nintendo World, and according to USJ other areas too, will close at times during the countdown for maintenance. Area Timed Entry and Standby Entry for the land are only available through the official app, and during the countdown each person gets just one of those tickets.

## Universal Holiday Wonders from 13 November to 17 January

USJ calls the Universal Food Festival its biggest yet, by number of dishes and areas. Snoopy and Elmo turn up unannounced in the food areas. If you're eating with a toddler, keep a hand on the plate. USJ will name the restaurants taking part later.

From 25 November to 11 January, Gramercy Park has two evening shows, with a reserved area from 1,700 yen:

- Light Up the Night ~Christmas Celebration~, about 30 minutes, with Elmo, Snoopy, Hello Kitty and falling snow. The show also ran last winter.
- Joy of Lights, about 10 minutes, back after nine years. Messages from guests to the people who matter to them appear on a screen, and snow falls at the end. You can send yours in until 14 December, and a draw decides which ones are shown.

In the Wizarding World of Harry Potter, "Hogwarts Magical Night ~Winter Magic~" runs in the evenings from 13 November to 17 January, a projection of about 10 minutes on the castle. Hogsmeade gets winter decorations. The [Hogwarts Castle Walk](ref:universal-studios-japan/hogwarts-castle-walk) through the castle runs until 17 January.

The Minions come out in winter clothes for the Minion Jingle Bello Meet & Greet, and Charlie Brown and Hello Kitty dance in [Universal Wonderland ~Let's Smile Together!~](ref:universal-studios-japan/universal-wonderland-lets-smile-together). Super Nintendo World is turning five, and as the finale of that birthday, stars are hidden around the land from 12 November to 11 January. Find one, hand it to a staff member, and you get a sticker with a new design showing Mario and colourful Yoshis.

## Free entry for children, but only if you live in Japan

For the first time, USJ is letting children from all over Japan in for free. From 13 November to 30 December, the Holiday Kids Free Pass lets one child aged 4 to 11 in free with each paying adult, and the same goes for a 12-year-old who's still at primary school. The pair costs from 8,400 yen instead of from 13,900 yen; the Express Pass is extra. Only people who live in Japan can buy it, and they have to upload official proof of the child's age when they do.

## 25 years of Universal Studios Japan

Universal Studios Japan opened on 31 March 2001. It was the first Universal park outside the US and is now one of six Universal Studios parks. Universal Cool Japan 2027 starts on 2 December 2026 with Sailor Moon, and Fullmetal Alchemist and Yomi no Tsugai join in the spring. [Detective Conan World 2027](/news/detective-conan-world-universal-studios-japan) is part of the same programme.
