[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) opens its fourth hotel, L'Odyssée, on 26 March 2027, and starts selling rooms on 30 October. The park announced it on 26 October. The hotel has four stars and 300 rooms, as many as Les Quais de Lutèce and La Cité Suspendue put together.

It's designed as one big oriental caravanserai. The theme is four ancient cultures, Persia, Egypt, Mesopotamia and Phoenicia, and the four suites are named after them. Thierry Retif designed the scenography. The park is also planting a lot of greenery; the press release calls it an oasis.

## Booking and prices

You can book on parcasterix.fr. Prices start at €328.30 for two adults and two children, covering one night in the hotel and one day in the park. That's around €82 a head. The park sells its hotels as a package of park ticket, room, breakfast and parking.

The tourist tax is on top: in 2026 it's €2.60 a day for every guest over 18.

## Rooms for four to ten people

Family rooms come for four, five and six people, and in each one at least one guest has to be twelve or under. The room for four has a double bed and a bunk bed, the room for five adds a sofa bed, and the room for six has a double bed and two bunk beds. All three can also be booked with a view.

Two families travelling together can take a "Chambre Tribu": two rooms for five people each. The Phénicie and Mésopotamie suites are 53 square metres each, Égypte is 63 and Perse 90. Each one is meant for four people.

## Inside the hotel

The hotel has two restaurants, the Restaurant des Marchands and the Restaurant des Sables, and the bar L'Oasis. The buffet is à volonté, all you can eat.

Pets aren't allowed, not even Dogmatix. Check-in is from 5 pm, check-out by 10 am. If you arrive in the morning, you can leave your bags at the luggage storage and head straight into the park.

## Early entry

Hotel guests get into some rides 30 minutes before official opening: [Toutatis](ref:parc-asterix/toutatis?bare), [La Tour de Numérobis](ref:parc-asterix/la-tour-de-numerobis?bare), [Cétautomatix](ref:parc-asterix/cetautomatix?bare), [Attention Menhir !](ref:parc-asterix/attention-menhir?bare), [OzIris](ref:parc-asterix/oziris?bare) and [Chez Gyrofolix](ref:parc-asterix/chez-gyrofolix?bare). That depends on the weather. Hotel guests can also meet characters from the park. Early entry doesn't run on days with evening opening for Peur sur le Parc, on special event days or at the Christmas parties, the "arbres de Noël".

In our [Parc Astérix guide](/blog/parc-asterix-wait-times-tips) you'll find the time of day when the longest queues build at Toutatis and OzIris, and how to get to Plailly without a car.

## The other three hotels

Per person, the other three start at €99 (Les Trois Hiboux, three stars, in the woods, with a large fireplace), €102 (La Cité Suspendue, three stars, 150 rooms, built as a Celtic town with three hamlets) and €111 (Les Quais de Lutèce, four stars, 150 rooms). All four hotels are in the Oise regional nature park, and you can walk into the park from each one.

The €82 or so is a quarter of a price for two adults and two children, and the park hasn't published which night it applies to. You can only compare them in the booking process, with the same date for each hotel.

The hotel is part of a €250 million investment plan the park is running until 2030. For companies there's a 1,326-square-metre seminar area. The hotel holds the French environmental label HQE at the "Excellente" level. The park is hiring around 200 staff to run the hotel, with a recruitment day on 10 December 2026.
