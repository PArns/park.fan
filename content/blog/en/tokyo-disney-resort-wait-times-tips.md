In Tokyo you choose your park when you buy the ticket, before you've looked at a single ride. [Tokyo Disneyland](ref:tokyo-disneyland) and [Tokyo DisneySea](ref:tokyo-disneysea) sit side by side on reclaimed land in Urayasu, Chiba Prefecture, but a ticket is only ever good for one of them, and you choose which one when you buy it, along with the date. If you fancy hopping over to the other park after lunch, you'll need a second ticket, and tickets are sold online only.

Both parks belong to the Oriental Land Company (OLC). It pays Disney licence fees for the characters and stories, and Walt Disney Imagineering designs the rides. The Walt Disney Company holds no stake in the parks, though, and doesn't run them either. They're the only Disney parks in the world where that's the case. The OLC's largest shareholder, with 22 percent, is the railway company Keisei.

Tokyo Disneyland counted around 15.1 million guests in 2024, fourth in the world and second in Asia. Tokyo DisneySea had 12.4 million, seventh in the world. In our measurements since October 2024, the queues at DisneySea have been clearly longer on average than at Disneyland. The longest waits are for Soaring: Fantastic Flight and Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey.

## Tokyo Disneyland

The park covers 51 hectares and follows the plan of Disneyland in California and the Magic Kingdom in Florida, with a shopping street leading up to a castle and seven areas arranged around it. Cinderella Castle is an almost exact copy of the one in Florida.

### World Bazaar

What's called Main Street, U.S.A. elsewhere is the World Bazaar here, done up as a small American town around 1900. A permanent glass roof covers the main street and the cross street, Center Street, so you stay dry underneath even on a rainy day in Tokyo. The one ride is the Omnibus, a double-decker; otherwise the area is there for shopping and eating.

### Adventureland and Westernland

Adventureland is split into a New Orleans-style quarter and a jungle. That's where [Pirates of the Caribbean](ref:tokyo-disneyland/pirates-of-the-caribbean) and the [Jungle Cruise: Wildlife Expeditions](ref:tokyo-disneyland/jungle-cruise-wildlife-expeditions) run. The [Western River Railroad](ref:tokyo-disneyland/western-river-railroad) is a steam train that takes 15 minutes to circle through Adventureland, Westernland and Critter Country, with room for 140 people per train.

Westernland is dominated by a mountain of red artificial rock modelled on Monument Valley. Inside it, [Big Thunder Mountain](ref:tokyo-disneyland/big-thunder-mountain) has been running since **4 July 1987**, a mine train coaster designed by Tony Baxter and built by Vekoma. The ride lasts 3 minutes 40 seconds, height requirement 102 cm. The backstory of the abandoned gold-rush town is a tsunami in Tokyo, an earthquake in California and a flash flood in Florida. Whichever disaster you get, the train still has no driver.

On the river beside the mountain you'll find the Mark Twain Riverboat, a paddle steamer, and the rafts to Tom Sawyer Island, and in the Country Bear Theater a band of bears sings.

### Critter Country

[Splash Mountain](ref:tokyo-disneyland/splash-mountain) opened on **1 October 1992**, a day before the version at the Magic Kingdom. The California and Florida versions have both closed since and came back in 2024 with a new theme as Tiana's Bayou Adventure. In Tokyo the ride carries on as before. The last drop is 16 metres and the only one outdoors; the first is in a dark cave. Eight people sit in a log and the height requirement is 90 cm. Because every seat in Tokyo has its own lap bar, the limit is lower than on the American versions. Since summer 2022 it has run as "Get Wet MAX" in the hot season, a shower with a queue that soaks riders more than usual on purpose, to cool them down in Tokyo's muggy summer.

### Fantasyland

In our measurements the longest queue in the park is for [Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast](ref:tokyo-disneyland/enchanted-tale-of-beauty-and-the-beast). The ride opened in September 2020 as part of the biggest expansion in the park's history and sits in a castle of its own, with Maurice's cottage and Gaston's fountain out front. The vehicles run without a track, ten people sit in each, and the ride takes eight minutes. Premier Access costs 2,500 yen here, more than at any other ride in Tokyo Disneyland.

[Pooh's Hunny Hunt](ref:tokyo-disneyland/poohs-hunny-hunt) runs without a track too, in vehicles for five people, for four and a half minutes. Aquatopia over at DisneySea uses the same system.

The rest of Fantasyland is classic dark rides through scenes from Disney films: [Peter Pan's Flight](ref:tokyo-disneyland/peter-pans-flight) (two and a half minutes), [Snow White's Adventures](ref:tokyo-disneyland/snow-whites-adventures), [Pinocchio's Daring Journey](ref:tokyo-disneyland/pinocchios-daring-journey) and ["it's a small world"](ref:tokyo-disneyland/its-a-small-world). Dumbo, the teacups of Alice's Tea Party, the Castle Carrousel and Mickey's PhilharMagic are in Fantasyland too. From 15 September 2026 to 7 January 2027 the Haunted Mansion runs as [Haunted Mansion "Holiday Nightmare"](ref:tokyo-disneyland/haunted-mansion-holiday-nightmare), and during that time it has Premier Access for 1,000 yen.

The Fantasyland Forest Theatre opened in 2020 with 1,500 seats and plays "Mickey's Magical Music World".

### Toontown

Toontown is based on the film "Who Framed Roger Rabbit" and is the area for small children. [Gadget's Go Coaster](ref:tokyo-disneyland/gadgets-go-coaster) is a junior coaster by Vekoma from 1996, with 207 metres of track, a height of 8.5 metres and a top speed of 35 km/h, and it's all over after 56 seconds. It's the standard model mirrored, with a chain lift, which this type rarely has. Height requirement 90 cm. The spinning taxis of [Roger Rabbit's Car Toon Spin](ref:tokyo-disneyland/roger-rabbits-car-toon-spin) are here too, and you can walk through the houses of Minnie, Goofy, Donald and Chip 'n' Dale.

### Tomorrowland

Much of Tomorrowland's architecture comes from Florida's Tomorrowland as it stood from 1971 to 1993. [The Happy Ride with Baymax](ref:tokyo-disneyland/the-happy-ride-with-baymax) arrived in September 2020 as the first Disney attraction based on "Big Hero 6". The cars spin in circles to loud music, height requirement 81 cm.

[Monsters, Inc. Ride & Go Seek!](ref:tokyo-disneyland/monsters-inc-ride-and-go-seek), a four-minute ride, and [Star Tours: The Adventures Continue](ref:tokyo-disneyland/star-tours-the-adventures-continue) are in Tomorrowland as well.

Space Mountain had been there since opening day in 1983 and closed on **31 July 2024**. The new building is going up 80 metres from the old one, so the old ride could keep running while it was built. On 13 August 2026 the OLC announced the name, "Space Mountain Earthrise", with an opening in 2027. It'll be a roller coaster in the dark again, and the first in the resort with music from speakers in the car. Tomorrowland Plaza is being built where the old hall stood.

### Height requirements at Tokyo Disneyland

- 81 cm: The Happy Ride with Baymax
- 90 cm: Splash Mountain, Gadget's Go Coaster
- 102 cm: Big Thunder Mountain, Star Tours: The Adventures Continue

## Tokyo DisneySea

DisneySea opened on **4 September 2001** and cost 335 billion yen. Instead of a main street with a castle, it has eight "Ports of Call", harbours from around the world arranged around a lagoon, with a volcano in the middle. The park did get a castle in the end, in Fantasy Springs in 2024, except that Elsa lives in it.

### Mediterranean Harbor

The entrance leads into an Italian harbour that opens out in a "V", to the right towards the volcano and to the left towards New York. Some of the buildings on the water are a real hotel, the Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta, modelled on Portofino and Venice. The Venetian Gondolas run along the canal, and Fortress Explorations by the harbour is a fort you walk through, with rooms to explore and things to try out.

[Soaring: Fantastic Flight](ref:tokyo-disneysea/soaring-fantastic-flight) is a flying theatre. Riders hang in front of a dome screen, 87 at a time, and fly for five minutes over landmarks and landscapes from around the world. Height requirement 102 cm.

### American Waterfront

The American Waterfront is a New York harbour and a Cape Cod fishing village around 1900. The ocean liner S.S. Columbia lies in the harbour with a restaurant on board. Above it all stands the Hotel Hightower, the façade of the [Tower of Terror](ref:tokyo-disneysea/tower-of-terror). The ride opened in 2006 and tells a story of its own, without the Twilight Zone, which hardly anyone in Japan knows. The hotel's owner, Harrison Hightower III, stole an idol on his travels and vanishes in the lift on New Year's Eve 1899. Imagineer Joe Rohde lent him his face. The lift seats 22 people in three rows, the ride takes two minutes, and the height requirement is 102 cm. From January to March there's usually a special version with its own drop sequence.


[Toy Story Mania!](ref:tokyo-disneysea/toy-story-mania) opened on 9 July 2012. With 3D glasses on, you spend seven minutes shooting at fairground games, twelve people per vehicle. Turtle Talk is a 30-minute show, the Big City Vehicles drive through the harbour streets, and the DisneySea Electric Railway takes guests over to Port Discovery.

### Port Discovery

Port Discovery is a retro-futuristic harbour built around a made-up marine research institute. [Aquatopia](ref:tokyo-disneysea/aquatopia) is a ride in trackless boats that spin through whirlpools and past waterfalls. [Nemo & Friends SeaRider](ref:tokyo-disneysea/nemo-and-friends-searider) is a simulator for 122 people that replaced StormRider on 12 May 2017. Height requirement 90 cm.

### Lost River Delta

At the very back of the park stands a Mayan pyramid, and inside it [Indiana Jones Adventure: Temple of the Crystal Skull](ref:tokyo-disneysea/indiana-jones-adventure-temple-of-the-crystal-skull) has run since opening day. It's a dark ride with twelve people per vehicle, three minutes long, height requirement 117 cm. From 30 November 2026 the park adds the ride to Premier Access, at 1,500 yen.

[Raging Spirits](ref:tokyo-disneysea/raging-spirits) opened on **21 July 2005**. Walt Disney Imagineering designed it, Intamin built it together with Sansei Technologies, and it resembles Indiana Jones et le Temple du Péril at Disneyland Paris. It's 600 metres long and 16 metres tall, reaches 60 km/h, and the ride lasts one minute 38 seconds. Its loop is the only inversion in the entire resort. Twelve people per train, height requirement 117 cm, maximum 1.95 metres.

The steamers of the DisneySea Transit Steamer Line stop here, at Mediterranean Harbor and at the American Waterfront. If your feet are tired, the steamer takes you across the park.

### Arabian Coast and Mermaid Lagoon

Arabian Coast is a harbour from the Arabian Nights, themed on "Aladdin". [Sindbad's Storybook Voyage](ref:tokyo-disneysea/sindbads-storybook-voyage) is a boat ride with its own song by Alan Menken, "Compass of Your Heart". The [Caravan Carousel](ref:tokyo-disneysea/caravan-carousel) has two storeys and room for 190 people, and Jasmine's Flying Carpets and the Magic Lamp Theater are there too.

Most of Mermaid Lagoon is inside a hall with dimmed lighting, as if you were under water, which makes the area the place to go on rainy days. Its rides are built for children. [Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster](ref:tokyo-disneysea/flounders-flying-fish-coaster) is a family coaster by Togo, 240 metres long, 32 km/h, 66 seconds, height requirement 90 cm. The other rides are Scuttle's Scooters, Jumpin' Jellyfish, Blowfish Balloon Race, The Whirlpool and the Ariel's Playground play area there.

### Mysterious Island

The volcano is called Mount Prometheus and is the backdrop for two rides based on Jules Verne. [Journey to the Center of the Earth](ref:tokyo-disneysea/journey-to-the-center-of-the-earth) was there on opening day. It was first meant to be a free-fall tower for a "Discovery Mountain" at Disneyland Paris, and in Tokyo it was built with the technology of Test Track from Epcot. After a lift ride into the depths, six people ride a mine vehicle through crystal caves and a mushroom forest until a lava monster shoots them upwards in an eruption. Three minutes, height requirement 117 cm. The music is by Buddy Baker. The second dark ride in the volcano is [20,000 Leagues Under the Sea](ref:tokyo-disneysea/20000-leagues-under-the-sea).

### Fantasy Springs

The eighth port opened on **6 June 2024**. At around 140,000 square metres and 320 billion yen, it's the biggest expansion since 2001. You reach it along a path between Lost River Delta and Arabian Coast. It has three sections, based on "Frozen", "Tangled" and "Peter Pan".

[Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey](ref:tokyo-disneysea/anna-and-elsas-frozen-journey) is a boat ride through Arendelle, six and a half minutes, 16 people per boat. [Rapunzel's Lantern Festival](ref:tokyo-disneysea/rapunzels-lantern-festival) is a boat ride as well, five minutes to the lantern festival. [Peter Pan's Never Land Adventure](ref:tokyo-disneysea/peter-pans-never-land-adventure) is a 3D ride, six minutes long, twelve people per vehicle, height requirement 102 cm. The fourth ride, [Fairy Tinker Bell's Busy Buggies](ref:tokyo-disneysea/fairy-tinker-bells-busy-buggies), is a two-minute ride for four people and mainly for small children.

### Height requirements at Tokyo DisneySea

- 90 cm: Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster, Nemo & Friends SeaRider
- 102 cm: Soaring: Fantastic Flight, Tower of Terror, Peter Pan's Never Land Adventure
- 117 cm: Indiana Jones Adventure, Journey to the Center of the Earth, Raging Spirits (maximum 1.95 metres)

## Disney Premier Access

```glossary-widget slug=premier-access

```

Parades and shows have Premier Access too, and there it buys you a fixed spot in the viewing area. At DisneySea the evening show "Believe! Sea of Dreams" and the two daytime shows cost 2,500 yen each. At Tokyo Disneyland the shows and parades are 2,500 yen, while the Halloween parade "The Villains' Halloween 'Into the Frenzy'" and the Christmas parade "Toys Wondrous Christmas!" are 3,500 yen.

On one park ticket you can buy Premier Access several times, one booking after the other. You can book the next ride 60 minutes after buying or once the booked time comes round, whichever is sooner, and you can hold a ride and a show at the same time. Children up to three ride free if the adult with them has Premier Access. You can't cancel or change a booking, and on busy days the allocation can sell out. Payment is by credit, debit or prepaid card. If you don't have a smartphone or a card, you can buy at counters in the park throughout the day, for example at the Main Street House in Tokyo Disneyland and at Guest Relations in DisneySea.

The **Standby Pass** runs through the same app. On some days you need one to get into a shop or a character greeting at all, and it gives you a time window for it; in autumn 2026 that applied to several souvenir shops with capsule toy machines, for example. With the free **Entry Request** you apply for a place at certain shows, once per day and show, and you see in the app straight away whether you got one. If a ride breaks down while you're queuing, the park sometimes hands out a Multiple Experience, which gets you into another attraction once.

## A day at one of the parks

An example for DisneySea on an ordinary autumn day with a 9 am opening:

1. Get to Maihama station around 8 am, take the Disney Resort Line to the entrance and join the queue. Every ticket in your group should already be in the app.
2. Once you're in, go straight to Journey to the Center of the Earth, then over to Raging Spirits.
3. Right after entry, buy a Premier Access in the app for the ride you care about most, Soaring or Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey, say. Buy the next one as soon as you're allowed to.
4. Tower of Terror and the Toy Story shooting galleries in the morning, then lunch ordered through Disney Mobile Order.
5. In the afternoon, Fantasy Springs with the rides you have Premier Access for, plus the quieter rides in Mermaid Lagoon and Arabian Coast.
6. Finish with "Believe! Sea of Dreams". If you want a fixed spot, buy Premier Access for it.

Tokyo Disneyland works the same way, with Big Thunder Mountain and Splash Mountain in the morning and Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast either with Premier Access or in the evening.

## Tickets and prices

The ticket is called the **1-Day Passport**. The price depends on the date, and on any given day both parks cost the same. As of 3 October 2026, for dates from October 2026 to February 2027:

| Price tier | Adults (18 and over) | Juniors (12 to 17) | Children (4 to 11) |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| low        | 9,900 yen            | 8,200 yen          | 5,600 yen          |
| standard   | 10,900 yen           | 9,000 yen          | 5,600 yen          |
| high       | 11,900 yen           | 9,800 yen          | 5,900 yen          |
| peak       | 12,400 yen           | 10,200 yen         | 5,900 yen          |

Children up to three get in free. In October and November 2026 most days are at the standard tier, and the peak tier mostly falls on a Saturday. For the first days of February 2027 the calendar mostly shows the low tier.

If you're arriving later, you can buy one of two shorter passes. The **After 3 Passport** is valid at weekends and on public holidays from 3 pm and costs adults 7,900 to 9,900 yen, and the **After 5 Passport** is valid Monday to Friday, except public holidays, from 5 pm and costs 6,000 or 6,600 yen. Guests with disabilities and one companion get their own 1-Day Passport, at 7,900 to 9,900 yen for adults. The resort's ticket page doesn't list an annual pass. If you're coming on several days, you buy a separate pass for each one.

Tickets are sold online only, on the website and in the app, and the ticket booths at the entrance are closed. Guests at a Disney hotel can also buy them at the hotel. Sales open every day at 2 pm Japan time for the day two months later, which is 6 am in Central Europe in winter and 7 am in summer. You can buy up to ten tickets per order, and there's no refund if you cancel. For re-entry on the same day, tell a staff member at the exit as you leave, and you can come back in later on the same ticket.

## Getting there

The resort's station is **Maihama**, on the JR Keiyō and Musashino lines. From there it's about a five-minute walk to the entrance of Tokyo Disneyland. For DisneySea you take the Disney Resort Line. The monorail loops past four stations in about 13 minutes, starting at Resort Gateway by Maihama station, then Tokyo Disneyland, Bayside with the hotels and Tokyo DisneySea. To get to DisneySea it's a two-minute walk to the station, nine minutes on the train and one minute to the gate. A single ride costs 300 yen from age 12 and 150 yen for children, and the line runs from 6:03 am to 11:30 pm.

According to the resort, the trip from Tokyo Station takes about 20 minutes without a change. From Narita Airport it's about 80 minutes with one change, starting with an hour on the Narita Express to Tokyo Station. From Haneda Airport it's about 50 minutes with two changes, on the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsuchō and the Yamanote Line to Tokyo Station.

### Parking

By car, you leave the expressway at the Kasai exit coming from Tokyo and at Urayasu coming from Chiba. The resort asks guests to come by train, because traffic backs up around the car parks, especially after closing time. You pay the fee on the way in, as of 12 October 2026:

| Vehicle                       | Fee per day |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| Car under five metres         | 4,000 yen   |
| Vehicle five metres or longer | 6,000 yen   |
| Motorcycle                    | 1,000 yen   |

The car park has a height limit of 2.2 metres, opens before the park does and closes an hour after it. With your parking ticket you can drive back in with the same car on the same day, including into the other park's car park. A car can stop for ten minutes free of charge to drop off or pick up passengers.

## Food

Table-service restaurants can be booked as **Priority Seating** one month ahead at the earliest, from 10 am Japan time, which for readers in Europe lands in the middle of the night. On the day of your visit you can book online from 9 am and at the restaurant from 10 am, as long as tables are free. You get one reservation per meal, for up to eight people. It works at five restaurants in Tokyo Disneyland, including Restaurant Hokusai and the Blue Bayou Restaurant, and at six in DisneySea, including the S.S. Columbia Dining Room on the ocean liner and Restaurant Sakura.

At the quick-service restaurants you order ahead in the app with **Disney Mobile Order** and pick up your food at the time you chose, at 18 locations in Disneyland and 17 in DisneySea. The resort recommends the curry at Casbah Food Court in Arabian Coast, the popcorn from The Big Pop in Tomorrowland, which opened in 2020 alongside Baymax, and, as a snack, the Ukiwa Bun from Seaside Snacks in DisneySea. Fantasy Springs has the Royal Banquet of Arendelle in the castle, Oaken's OK Foods, The Snuggly Duckling and Lookout Cookout.

## Hotels

Two of the six Disney hotels are right inside DisneySea. The **Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta** opened on the same day as the park, and its rooms look out on the lagoon, the gondola canal or the entrance. The **Tokyo DisneySea Fantasy Springs Hotel** came in 2024 with the new port, with 419 rooms in the Fantasy Chateau and 56 in the pricier Grand Chateau. Its guests get straight into Fantasy Springs through their own entrance.

The **Tokyo Disneyland Hotel** is at Tokyo Disneyland station, and the **Disney Ambassador Hotel** at Resort Gateway station. The **Tokyo Disney Resort Toy Story Hotel** at Bayside station is the mid-price option, and the **Tokyo Disney Celebration Hotel** is the cheapest. A free bus gets you there in 15 minutes. Bayside station also has six partner hotels from other chains, including the Hilton Tokyo Bay and the Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel.

Resort Gateway station is also where you'll find Ikspiari, an OLC shopping centre with a cinema that opened on 7 July 2000. Bon Voyage, the big Disney shop outside the parks, is reached from Tokyo Disneyland station.

## What's new in 2027

Besides "Space Mountain Earthrise", Tokyo Disneyland is due to open "Sugar Rush: Sweet Rescue" in spring 2027, a ride based on "Wreck-It Ralph". It was originally planned for 2026.

## The resort's history

The ground both parks stand on is reclaimed land in Tokyo Bay. In the 1960s the area was set aside for leisure facilities, and in 1960 the Oriental Land Company was founded to develop the Maihama area. Two years later it approached Disney for the first time.

The idea of a Japanese Disneyland is older. In the late 1950s, businessman Kunizo Matsuo pitched a park in Nara to Walt Disney. Disney pulled out, Matsuo built it anyway, and in 1961 he opened Nara Dreamland, an almost exact copy of Disneyland, just without the Disney characters. The park ran until 2006 and was torn down in 2017.

In February 1974 the OLC invited Disney managers on a fact-finding trip to Japan, and in April 1979 OLC president Masatomo Takahashi signed the contract. Construction began a year later. The budget was 100 billion yen; in the end the park cost 180 billion. Tokyo Disneyland opened on **15 April 1983** as the first Disney park outside the US, with Space Mountain from day one. The OLC paid off its 1.4 billion US dollar construction loan in three years.

A second park had been planned since 1987, first as a film studio park modelled on the Disney-MGM Studios in Florida. After Japan's speculative bubble burst in 1991, that concept was dropped. Instead, Disney and the OLC revived "Port Disney", a marine park in Long Beach next to the Queen Mary that Disney had cancelled after its losses on Euro Disney. Ground was broken for DisneySea on 22 October 1998. The park reached ten million guests after 307 days, faster than any park before it. The record until then had been held by Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, at 338 days. In 2002 DisneySea received a Thea Award from the Themed Entertainment Association.

In October 2019 both parks closed because of Typhoon Hagibis, and from 29 February 2020 they were closed for the pandemic until 1 July 2020. The OLC had announced Fantasy Springs in June 2018 for the 2023 financial year. Because of supply delays from abroad and the pandemic, in October 2022 it pushed the opening back to 6 June 2024.

## Frequently asked questions about Tokyo Disney Resort

### How much does admission cost?

A 1-Day Passport costs adults between 9,900 and 12,400 yen depending on the date, juniors aged 12 to 17 between 8,200 and 10,200 yen, and children aged 4 to 11 pay 5,600 or 5,900 yen, for visits from October 2026 to February 2027. Children up to three go free.

### Can I visit both parks on one ticket?

No. A 1-Day Passport is good for one park on one date, and both are fixed when you buy. For two parks you need two tickets, ideally for two days.

### How much is Premier Access?

Between 1,000 and 2,500 yen per person and ride, depending on the ride. The cheapest are the Monsters, Inc. ride and, until 7 January 2027, the Haunted Mansion in Tokyo Disneyland; the most expensive are Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast, Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey and Soaring: Fantastic Flight. A spot for a parade or show costs 2,500 or 3,500 yen.

### How tall does my child need to be for the rides?

At Tokyo Disneyland a child can ride The Happy Ride with Baymax from 81 cm and every ride with a height limit from 102 cm. At Tokyo DisneySea, Indiana Jones Adventure, Journey to the Center of the Earth and Raging Spirits require 117 cm, the highest requirement in the resort.

### Which park is busier?

Tokyo DisneySea. In our measurements the longest queues in the whole resort are for Soaring: Fantastic Flight and Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey.

### When is it least busy?

In September, going by our measurements, and at Tokyo Disneyland in June and July as well. February and March are the busiest months at both parks.

### How do I get from the airport to the resort?

From Narita it takes about 80 minutes with one change at Tokyo Station, from Haneda about 50 minutes with two changes. The station you want is Maihama on the JR Keiyō Line, and from there it's a five-minute walk to Tokyo Disneyland.

### Is Space Mountain open?

No. The ride has been closed since 31 July 2024, and the new "Space Mountain Earthrise" is due to open in 2027.

### How long does the Tokyo DisneySea anniversary run?

The 25th birthday celebration, "Sparkling Jubilee", runs from 15 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
