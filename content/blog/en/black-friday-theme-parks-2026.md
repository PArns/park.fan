Around Black Friday on 27 November, [Heide Park](ref:heide-park), [LEGOLAND Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland), [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany) and 14 other parks in Europe are selling their 2027 annual passes for less. Fårup's hotel deal is gone after Friday itself, and eight other deals close on Monday, 30 November. Phantasialand, PortAventura, Energylandia, Bellewaerde and Nigloland are cutting the price of day tickets as well, and Gardaland, Fårup and the four Merlin parks in England are selling hotel nights with park entry.

Europa-Park, Efteling, Toverland, Disneyland Paris and the two Plopsaland parks in Germany and Belgium have no Black Friday deal. Even at full price, the cheapest annual pass at those parks pays off after four to six visits; our [annual pass comparison](/blog/theme-park-annual-pass-comparison) has the sums park by park.

## Germany

A Heide Park annual pass is valid for 365 days from the day you buy it. In 2027 the Basic has 35 blackout days at Heide Park and 36 at LEGOLAND, while the Gold at Heide Park has only two. LEGOLAND's Gold also gets you into PEPPA PIG Park Günzburg and WinterWonder LEGOLAND. Renewing a Basic or a Gold costs as much as a new pass; renewing a Platinum costs €99.

The Merlin Platinum annual pass is the same pass at the same price in both shops. LEGOLAND just crosses out a higher regular price, so the same €119 looks like a bigger saving there. The pass gets you into both parks and eleven other Merlin attractions in Germany, with parking included at Heide Park and LEGOLAND. Heide Park lists two friend tickets per pass term and one single Express ride per visit day; LEGOLAND, in its own dialect, promises two Bring-a-Friend tickets for 2027 and one Fastrack One per visit. On 30 and 31 October 2027, Platinum holders also need a reservation ticket for Heide Park, which is free.

Both parks also sell cheaper overnight stays with two days' entry. At Heide Park you stay at the Adventure Hotel or the Holiday Camp, with breakfast and dinner buffets, from €60 instead of €80 per person, arriving by 30 June 2027. LEGOLAND charges from €72 instead of €96 per person, with breakfast and parking, for stays from 20 March to 30 June 2027.

The Movie Park annual pass is valid from the day you buy it, so it covers the rest of 2026, [Hollywood Christmas](/news/movie-park-hollywood-christmas-2026) included, and then runs until 7 November 2027. You have to register every visit in advance, because each day has a quota. If you already have an annual pass, you can only renew it at the park, from 27 to 29 November and from 4 to 6 December at the info desk or the Visitor Welcome Center. Silver, Gold and Platinum then cost €96.90, €111.90 and €129.90, €5 under the price of a new pass in the shop. Bronze only comes as a renewal, for €74.90.

## Belgium

Bellewaerde opens its sale on Wednesday, 25 November, at 12 noon, and it closes early if the allocations sell out. There are 3,500 Combi passes for the park and the aquapark, 2,000 park passes and only 500 aquapark passes, so anyone after one of those should set an alarm for Wednesday lunchtime. If you want both, the Combi is cheaper than two separate passes. What you buy is a voucher, which you activate at the ticket desk on an opening day by 3 January 2027; otherwise the pass starts running by itself on 4 January 2027.

Bellewaerde also sells 5,000 cut-price day tickets for each of these periods:

- Bellewaerde Kerst, 5 December to 3 January 2027: €33.60 instead of €48 (more winter openings: [parks in winter](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026))
- the park, 27 March to 30 May 2027: €35.70 instead of €51
- the aquapark from 25 November: €17.50 instead of €25, valid until the end of February according to the offer page and until 31 March 2027 according to the terms

## France

Nigloland's spring ticket costs the same for adults and children, is valid on one day of your choice from 27 March to 2 May 2027 and is only sold online. Children under one metre get in free. The digital Pass Saison Argent costs as much as three spring tickets, so if you're coming three times in spring, buy the pass straight away; it then covers the whole 2027 season too. The plastic card costs €109 instead of €112. The code NIGLOBLACK gets you the hotel discount, for nights in the 2027 season at Cabaïana and the Hôtel des Pirates.

## Spain

With the code BLACKPAW26, dated and flexible adult tickets for PortAventura World are half price if they cover several days or more than one of its three parks, PortAventura Park, Ferrari Land and [Caribe Aquatic Park](ref:caribe-aquatic-park). The resort is selling 1,000 tickets this way in total, on portaventuraworld.com. The last day you can visit is 9 January 2028. The blocked dates are 5 to 8 December, 12 and 13 December, and 19 December to 6 January 2027.

Hotel Roulette puts you in a four-star hotel in the resort, and you find out which one the day before you arrive. There are at most 20 rooms a night, bookings can't be refunded, and the last night you can stay is 7 January 2028. The Ponient hotels are outside the resort, and there your stay has to end by 6 January 2028 at the latest, depending on the hotel.

At Parque Warner and Parque de Atracciones near Madrid, the Bono Parques is valid from the day you buy it, so whatever's left of 2026 comes free on top of all of 2027. The Platino works in 27 parks and includes free parking, and you can pay in three monthly instalments. Neither park has published a quota, unlike Bellewaerde or PortAventura.

## Italy

At Gardaland the discount covers nights at the Gardaland Hotel, the Magic Hotel and the Adventure Hotel, with breakfast. You arrive between 30 March and 30 June 2027, except on 1 May and 2 June.

Mirabilandia's VIP and Gold passes also work at the Mirabeach water park, but only the VIP pass comes with free parking. During the Black Friday Weeks, park-and-hotel packages are 10% cheaper, and tickets without a fixed date are up to €15 cheaper.

## Britain

On an Alton Towers short break, the price applies to hotel nights from 22 January to 7 March 2027, and children swim for free. Thorpe Park puts a day's entry and a day of Unlimited Fastrack in the package, and the code SAVE20 takes £20 off selected 2027 dates. At Chessington it's £30 off with SAVE30, on selected days of the February half-term. LEGOLAND Windsor adds a round of Adventure Golf, for nights from 27 December 2026 to 18 March 2027.

## Denmark, Sweden and Poland

LEGOLAND Billund's 2027 season passes are already valid from December 2026. Renewing a 2026 Platinum pass costs 899 DKK. Overnight stays with two-day tickets from 1 April to 30 June 2027 cost from 576 DKK instead of 768 DKK per person. Fårup Sommerland hasn't published yet which nights the Black Nights cover; it sends out the calendar in its newsletter.

The Guldkortet is valid all through summer 2027 at Gröna Lund, concerts in the park included.

At [Energylandia](/blog/energylandia-wait-times-tips) in Poland the Black Weeks prices cover reduced tickets and two- and three-day tickets too; you can buy ten at most, and only on ticket.energylandia.pl. They're all valid from 4 December 2026. The peak-season ticket is valid until 31 October 2027, the off-peak ticket until 25 June and from 1 September to 31 October 2027, and the Winter Kingdom ticket until 31 January 2027. Gift tickets and afternoon tickets for Winter Kingdom, the Energy Pass, annual passes and parking tickets aren't part of the deal.
