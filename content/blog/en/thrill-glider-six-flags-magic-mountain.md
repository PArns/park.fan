Six Flags announced the **Thrill Glider** for
[Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain) on 10 September 2026.
Vekoma is building it, construction has started, and it's due to open in 2027.
The park hasn't named a date.

## What's being built

Six Flags describes the Thrill Glider as a mix of flying, motorbike and
suspended coaster technology. Riders lie face down on a "hovercycle" and hang
beneath the track.

The figures from the press release:

- Track: 3,380 feet, just over 1,030 metres
- Height: 107 feet, about 32 metres
- Speed: up to 50 mph, around 80 km/h
- Ride time: 80 seconds
- Launches: several
- Inversions: five, namely a wing over, a zero-g roll, an overbanked stall turn,
  a barrel roll and an upward barrel roll
- Airtime: twelve moments
- Minimum height: 48 inches, or 122 cm

Six Flags also lists six records the ride is meant to hold at opening. It calls
it the world's first Thrillglider, and the fastest, longest and tallest one.
Add the first with several launches and the most inversions. Since no other
exists yet, the list mostly says that this is the first ride of its kind.

## Where it stands

The Thrill Glider is going up on the site of the former Golden Bear Theatre,
between Full Throttle and LEX LUTHOR: Drop of Doom, next to Looney Tunes Land.
It covers nearly 3.5 acres, about 1.4 hectares.

The ride has a backstory. It's set at "QuantumPulse Propulsion Systems", a
fictional technology company. The queue runs through the "Futurist Pavilion"
with displays, holograms and exhibits, and sound and lighting on board are
synchronised to the ride.

Park president Brian Oerding called it another milestone in Magic Mountain's
"renaissance".
