[Energylandia](ref:energylandia) in Zator opens its Winter Kingdom on Saturday 28 November. Until 31 January 2027 the park is open on 28 days, always from 11:30am to 6:30pm. There's a light garden, an ice rink, snow tubing, a Christmas market, Santa and shows with elves. The coasters run too, as long as the weather lets them.

## Which days the park is open

- in November on Saturday and Sunday, the 28th and 29th
- in December from the 4th to the 6th and the 11th to the 13th (Friday to Sunday), on the 19th and 20th, and from the 26th to the 30th (Saturday to Wednesday)
- in January from the 2nd to the 6th (Saturday to Wednesday), then on the weekends of the 9th and 10th, 16th and 17th, 23rd and 24th, and 30th and 31st

The gates stay shut from 21 to 25 December, on New Year's Eve and on New Year's Day. Last winter, Winter Kingdom ran from 29 November 2025 to 8 February 2026, so this time it ends a good week earlier.

## What's on

The light garden takes you along winding paths past 5 million lights and through light tunnels, by glowing sleighs, snowmen and oversized baubles. At the far end stands a Christmas tree almost 17 metres tall. The ceremony where it's switched on for the first time starts on Saturday at 4pm at the main entrance. The park says the lights are on all day, and you don't need an extra ticket for the garden.

The ice rink has a small and a large surface. Everyone has to wear a helmet and protective gloves, and children aged three to seven only go on the ice with someone accompanying them. On opening Saturday, DJs from the Energy 2000 club play a disco on skates there from 1pm to 3pm. Snow tubing has two hills, one for adults and one for kids, and on both you slide down on an inflatable tube.

New this year is Arctic Loop, a ride with rubber rings that turn slowly on the ground. The park gives an age range of two to 13 and a height range of 100 to 200 centimetres, and children ride with someone accompanying them. There's also a reindeer enclosure where, according to Energylandia, the reindeer can talk. You can decorate gingerbread in a workshop that's open from 12:30pm to 5:30pm, and write letters to Santa in a second one that's open all day. Santa himself sits at the Lancelot Shop from 12:30pm to 5:30pm and poses for photos.

At 11:20am Santa, the elves and the mascots welcome the first guests outside the gate. In Teatr Egypt, elves put on two shows of 15 minutes each. In "Następny przystanek: Święta!" (Next stop: Christmas) Santa's trip to Lapland looks like it's about to go wrong, and "Fabryka Elfów" (The elf factory) is set in the gift factory. Elf René does magic five times a day in the Town Hall Theatre in "Elfastycznie!" (roughly: Elftastic). The day ends at 6:15pm with the "Santa Express" parade and Santa's train in the Formuła area.

## Which rides are running

The park says more than 30 attractions are open, among them roller coasters, flat rides and part of Bajkolandia for small children. In its blog post about last winter, Energylandia names [Hyperion](ref:energylandia/pepsi-hyperion) and [Zadra](ref:energylandia/zadra-made-in-malopolska). A coaster only runs when the track is dry, though, with no ice and no snow lying on it. Whether Hyperion is running on your day, you'll see in the morning on the [park page](ref:energylandia). After a night of frost it's worth checking twice.

## What winter costs

Euros and złoty have separate price lists, each in its own shop. In the euro shop at tickets.energylandia.pl, a day at Winter Kingdom costs €38, or €33 reduced. The reduced price is for children under 140 centimetres, seniors from 65 and people with disabilities. From 3pm there's an afternoon ticket for €21, or €19 reduced. You don't need to book a date: the ticket is valid on any opening day until 31 January. According to the FAQ it covers every attraction and show that's open that day, the ice rink included.

In the Polish shop at ticket.energylandia.pl, the winter ticket normally costs 149 zł, and during Black Weeks, until Sunday 29 November, only 99 zł. The offer is online only, with no more than ten tickets in all. These tickets only become valid on 4 December, though. For the opening weekend you pay the normal price. The afternoon ticket isn't part of the offer. We've collected the [Black Friday deals from other parks](/news/black-friday-theme-parks-2026) in a separate post.

Parking in front of the entrance costs 30 zł a day. For clothing, the park recommends layers, ski wear and ski goggles with an elastic strap, so think ski slope rather than Christmas market.

## By train from Kraków or Oświęcim

From 28 November to 12 December, a train runs to Zator Park Rozrywki station for Winter Kingdom. A little road train picks you up there and takes you back in the evening:

- from Kraków Główny at 9:18am, road train at 10:25am, back to the station at 6:20pm, train to Kraków at 7:03pm
- from Oświęcim at 10:27am, road train at 10:47am, back to the station at 6:45pm, train to Oświęcim at 7:27pm

So if you're heading back to Kraków, you'll see the 6:15pm parade from behind at best.

## What happens in 2027

The 2027 summer season starts on 3 April. Along with the new Volana area, Energylandia is also getting the [Rokan wing coaster](/news/energylandia-rokan-wing-coaster), though the park hasn't named a date yet.
