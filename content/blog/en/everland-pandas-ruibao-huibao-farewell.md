The last day you can see the panda twins Rui Bao and Hui Bao in public at [Everland](ref:everland) in Yongin is 11 December. In the third week of December they leave for China. Until then you get the whole family at once: the parents Aibao and Le Bao, the twins, and since 30 October also Shubao, the cub born in June. Samsung C&T, which runs the park, announced the last viewing day on 19 October and Shubao's first appearance on 27 October.

## Shubao, panda number five

Shubao was born in Panda World on 3 June 2026, a daughter of Aibao and Le Bao conceived naturally. She weighed 171 grams at birth, roughly what a large apple weighs, and she's now past nine kilos. She got her name on her 100th day and took her first steps at about 120 days old. So she knew what she was called before she could walk anywhere. More than 10,000 people sent in ideas, a panel picked five of them, and Shubao took 42 per cent of 220,000 votes cast online and in the park. The characters 曙宝 mean dawn and treasure.

After Fu Bao and the twins, she's the fourth panda born in Korea. Everland plans to show her for longer each day and to more visitors, step by step.

## Where to see the pandas, and for how long

Aibao and Le Bao live in Panda World, which is where Shubao now has her audience too. Rui Bao and Hui Bao live next door in the Panda Second House, which opened on 3 October 2025. It covers 1,200 square metres, with an indoor enclosure glazed from floor to ceiling and an outdoor one.

Since 1 October, you can stay ten minutes per visit in Panda World and five in the Panda Second House. When the crowd there gets big, Everland switches to a walk-through, and you pass the enclosure without stopping. The hours of both houses can change on the day without notice.

On 13 November the twins start their pre-transport quarantine, which lasts about a month and takes place in the Panda Second House. Fu Bao's quarantine in 2024 was closed to the public. This time you can still see Rui Bao and Hui Bao on a limited basis, in the indoor enclosure. It's sealed off completely from the outside, so it meets the quarantine rules. Everland hasn't announced yet at what times or in what form. That depends on how the twins are doing and how far the travel preparations have got.

As a send-off, Everland plans a dedicated day in November with a talk concert by the keepers Kang Cheol-won and Song Young-gwan, including unreleased footage from raising the twins. The date and how to sign up haven't been announced. The park also plans to show a film about the twins' first years.

## Tickets and getting there

Everland raised its prices on 6 October, and what a day ticket costs depends on the month. In November, adults and teenagers from 13 to 18 pay 65,000 won, while children from 36 months to 12 years and seniors from 65 pay 55,000 won. In December it's 55,000 and 45,000 won. So December is the cheaper month, but only eleven of its days still have the twins. Children under 36 months get in free.

From 9 November the park is open from 10 am to 8 pm Sunday to Thursday, and until 9 pm on Fridays and Saturdays. Friday 11 December is scheduled for 10 am to 9 pm, and as with the panda houses, Everland can still change that on the day.

By metro, take the Bundang Line to Giheung and change to the EverLine, which runs from 5:30 am to 11:30 pm. From its last stop, a free shuttle bus runs to Everland every five to ten minutes, the first at 7:30 am and the last an hour after the park closes. Regular buses also run from Seoul to the park, such as the 5002 via Gangnam and Yangjae or the 4105 from Myeong-dong and Seoul Station.

## What else is on until December

The autumn festival runs until 22 November, with Blood City Zero: Hell Gate and the autumn show line-up. The circus show Wings of Memory closes sooner, on 15 November, and the Wild Savanna Expedition is running too.

Over the winter, Everland is taking down some older rides. Work on the Helicycle started on 2 November. The Play Yard follows from 23 November, Car Kingdom and Kids Village from 1 December and the Spooky Fun House from 3 December. The dates depend on the weather, so with small children it's safer to plan Kids Village and Car Kingdom before the end of November.

## Why the twins are moving to China

Aibao and Le Bao came to Korea from the Dujiangyan Panda Habitat in Sichuan for the opening of Panda World on 21 April 2016. Around 18 million people have visited it since. Pandas born outside China move there before their fourth birthday, before they reach sexual maturity.

Fu Bao was born on 20 July 2020, the first panda born in Korea. She made her last public appearance on 3 March 2024, and on 3 April 2024 she left Everland for the Shenshuping base in the Wolong Nature Reserve in Sichuan. Rui Bao and Hui Bao were born on 7 July 2023 as Korea's first panda twins, at 180 and 140 grams. Today each weighs about 90 kilos, more than most of the visitors watching them. Exactly when they leave and where in China they go depends on the temperature and the situation there. All that's fixed so far is the third week of December.
