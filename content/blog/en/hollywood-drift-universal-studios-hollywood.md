**Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift** officially opened at
[Universal Studios Hollywood](ref:universal-studios-hollywood) on
16 September 2026. It’s the park’s first roller coaster to run outdoors, and
Universal says it’s the fastest anywhere in the Universal Destinations &
Experiences portfolio. The occasion is the film series’ 25th anniversary.

The soft opening had already started on 27 July, just over seven weeks before
the official date.

## What the ride does

The track is 4,100 feet long, about 1,250 metres, and the cars reach up to
**72 mph** (roughly 116 km/h). Universal quotes forces of up to 3.6 g. There
are four launches, and the layout has an inverted dive loop, a twisted pretzel
loop and an inverted stall, so three inversions in all.

Intamin built it to a design by Universal Creative, and linear motors power the
launches.

The ride vehicles are cars from the films: a Dodge Charger, a Mazda RX-7, a
Nissan Skyline GT-R and a Toyota Supra. Each car can turn a full 360 degrees
during the ride, and each one is programmed separately. A train is four cars
with two rows of two seats each, which makes 16 riders per train.

The station is on the Upper Lot, where Animal Actors on Location and the
Special Effects Stage used to be. One stretch of track runs through a
see-through enclosure inside the queue.

## Who can ride

The height requirement is **51 inches**, or 129.5 cm. There’s a test seat where
you can check that before you queue. Bags and loose items go into a free
locker, and everyone walks through a metal detector before the platform.

## For your visit

[Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift](ref:universal-studios-hollywood/fast-and-furious-hollywood-drift?full)

Universal is building a second version of the ride for Universal Studios
Florida. It’s due to open in 2027, with a different layout and a 170-foot
(52 m) tower.
