[Rulantica](ref:rulantica), the indoor water park at the Europa-Park resort in Rust, is holding a winter season of its own for the first time, from 28 November to 10 January 2027. It's called Vinterglanz, and in the evenings a 20-minute Christmas show plays at the Surf Fjørd wave pool. The Mack group announced it on 19 November. Until 27 November the water park is closed for maintenance.

Once it reopens, a big Christmas tree stands in the lobby and garlands hang between decorated fir trees across the water park. The Hyggedal sauna area, with its three saunas and a steam room, gets the Christmas treatment too.

## The evening show at the Surf Fjørd

"VINTERGLANZ – das Leuchten des Heimatsterns" (roughly "the glow of the home star") is a Christmas story starring Snorri and Bubba, and Svalgur turns up in it as well. It's told with acrobatics and puppets. At the end of every show, a child from the audience makes the home star light up, and Junior Club members can book a part in the ensemble for 750 club stars.

The park says the best view is from inside the wave pool, which is not where most people sit for a Christmas story. You can't go past the red barrier in the pool, though. The park warns in advance that the show uses strong light effects, loud sound effects, fog and water.

There's more than one performance each evening: the page for the midnight party mentions the "last evening show". Rulantica hasn't published the times yet, for the show or for the daytime programme. Until it does, nobody can say whether the Moonlight ticket from 7 pm gets you in early enough.

## The daytime programme

Most of it happens at Skip Strand. In the Vinter calendar, children open a door each day with Snorri and the Nordic winter fairy, which takes eleven minutes. Bubba's Christmas Presents, the bubble show Snorri in a Christmas Bubble, "Die Wikinger & das Geheimnis der Weihnacht" (the Vikings and the secret of Christmas), the kids' disco Jingle Beats and the Vinter-Circus, where the Talent Academy runs acrobatics you can join in with, last 16 minutes each. The Snorri slide badge, open to children aged 4 to 11, is scheduled for 20 minutes.

The treasure hunt "Die Wikinger & die verzauberten Sterne" (the Vikings and the enchanted stars) is listed under Lumåfals, runs 16 minutes and goes round Skip Strand. At Frigg Tempel, a heated outdoor pool, children spend an hour making things around the home star in Snorri's star workshop. Rulantica Vibes brings five hours of music and cocktails to the same spot, and the craft group only gets to listen.

## Vikingløp and Tønnevirvel

[Vikingløp](ref:rulantica/vikinglop) has eight tubes in the 30-metre Nordiskturn tower, and the park calls it the biggest speed slide in Europe. It opened in 2023 and handles 600 people an hour; you need to be 10 years old and 110 centimetres tall. The water carousel [Tønnevirvel](ref:rulantica/tonnevirvel) is now indoors. Nine boats with up to six seats spin for three minutes. Riders must be three and 90 centimetres, and anyone under 120 centimetres rides with an adult. The minimum heights for the other slides are in our [Rulantica guide](/blog/rulantica-wait-times-tips).

## Admission and tickets

The show and the programme are included in normal admission. A day ticket costs €41 to €54 for adults aged 12 and up, depending on the day, and €38 to €51 for children aged 4 to 11 and seniors from 60. Children up to three get in free. From 5 pm the evening ticket costs €37.50 to €46.50, or €35 to €43.50 at the reduced rate. The Moonlight ticket from 7 pm is €28 to €34 for everyone aged four and up. A day with the Hyggedal sauna costs €55 to €68, or €52 to €65 for seniors. The price for a specific day only shows up in the ticket shop's calendar.

Every ticket is for one date. The park recommends booking online: the ticket desk only sells on the day when there's capacity left, and charges €6 extra. A locker costs €1 a day, and your winter coat goes in it too, since the air inside is 30 to 34 degrees and the water 27 to 32.

If you want a day at Winterzauber next door and the evening at Rulantica, there's Ride & Slide. The combined ticket, a Europa-Park day pass plus a Rulantica evening ticket, is valid Monday to Friday and costs from €75 per person between 30 November and 22 December. Our [Europa-Park guide](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips) helps with the day over there.

## Opening hours and two special days

Rulantica opens every day from 9:30 am to 10 pm, and hotel guests and Adventure ticket holders get in from 9 am. It's closed on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, and on New Year's Eve it closes at 7 pm.

The first day of Vinterglanz is also Rulantica's seventh birthday; it opened in 2019. From 10 am to 4 pm there's a small Snorri party every hour at Guest Relations in Lumåfals, and at 4:30 pm a birthday disco with Snorri at Skip Strand. Airbrush tattoos run from 10 am to 6 pm, and a photo spot with the home star is set up all day. Share the photo with @rulantica and #Vinterglanz and you could win two tickets and a plush Snorri. On that Saturday a day ticket costs from €41, or from €38 for children and seniors.

On Saturday 5 December, Rulantica stays open until midnight for the midnight party, a little over 14 hours. The show opens the evening, then a DJ takes over, and Vikingløp hosts a late-night slide challenge with a prize-giving. Hyggedal offers sauna infusions, scrubs and face masks. The party is included in admission. That day a day ticket costs €48, €45 for children, the evening ticket €44, €41 for children, and the Moonlight ticket €32 for everyone. The park hasn't said yet whether the evening and Moonlight tickets are valid until midnight that day.

## Swimming outdoors in December

Open in winter are the heated outdoor pool Svømmepøl, added in 2025, Frigg Tempel and Vildstrøm. Svømmepøl is partly roofed over in winter, and its Svømme bar fully. You can get mulled wine in the pool, so you drink it standing in warm water. Svalgurok with its ten slides, Snorri Strand, Dynstrønd and the Strand Kök food court don't reopen until 1 May 2027.

New on the menu this winter are fresh pasta, bowl salads, a mushroom poutine and wintry hot drinks.

## Getting there

Rulantica is at Roland-Mack-Ring 1 in Rust, directly opposite Hotel Krønasår. On the A5, take exit 57b Rust. The visitor car park costs €10 a day, €20 for motorhomes, and electric cars charge in row 10. By train, go to Ringsheim/Europa-Park, where the Eurocity-Express between Frankfurt and Milan also stops twice a day. From there a public bus runs via Rulantica and the hotels to Europa-Park, at €3.20 single and €6.40 return, or €2.10 and €4.20 for children. Guests at the resort hotels park free at their hotel and take the free shuttle to the water park.

From January to March, Rulantica sells an off-season special in certain weeks, for example the adult day ticket for €41. It applies from 11 to 15, 18 to 22 and 25 to 29 January, from 1 to 4 February, and from 1 to 5, 8 to 12 and 15 to 18 March 2027.

## What's new compared with last winter

In winter 2025/26 Rulantica had no season name of its own and no Christmas programme. Back then the Mack group advertised the slides, the wave pool, Vikingløp and the new Svømmepøl, and the opening hours were the same as now. Europa-Park has had a winter season for 25 years. This year its Winterzauber runs from 28 November to 9 January, to 10 January for hotel guests, every day from 11 am until at least 7 pm. Europa-Park is closed on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day too. Vinterglanz is already in the calendar for next winter, from 27 November 2027 to 9 January 2028.

On 11 January Rulantica moves straight on to Nordlyfest, which runs until 19 March and is included in admission. The Nordic festival of lights has run before under another name. This time it has light installations, a beacon at Frigg Tempel and an evening show at the Surf Fjørd with aerial acrobatics above the water. The other parks open this winter are in our [winter guide 2026](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026). After Winterzauber, Europa-Park doesn't reopen until 20 March 2027.
