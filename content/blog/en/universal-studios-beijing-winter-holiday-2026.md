Winter Holiday, the Christmas event at [Universal Studios Beijing](ref:universal-studios-beijing), runs from November 20, 2026, to January 3, 2027. For the occasion, a new Christmas tree 72 feet (22 meters) tall stands at the end of Hollywood Boulevard. Over in The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Hogsmeade celebrates Christmas again. Universal spread nine themed events across 2026 for the resort's fifth birthday, and Winter Holiday is the last of them.

## The new tree on Hollywood Boulevard

The Spectacular Christmas Tree comes in rose gold, warm silver and blue, with touches of red, gold and champagne gold, styled after classic Hollywood. It hasn't grown, mind you: last year's tree was 72 feet tall as well. In the evening the tree hosts the Sparkling Christmas Tree Spectacular, a show of light and music.

The boulevard gets Winter Holiday flower beds, and wreaths and bells hang from the lampposts and shop windows. Fairy Godmother, Prince Charming, Shrek and Fiona walk around Hollywood in their winter outfits.

## Santa, the shows and the food

At Spectacular Santa's Village you can meet Santa and have your photo taken with him. Elves, Mrs. Claus and the village Postmaster are around too. The village stage is in the Hollywood Amphitheatre, where Gift of the Season plays, a show you'll only see in winter. In the music show Universal Pop: Winter Holiday, the cast also performs in winter costumes.

The village menu has a hot dog with cheese and meat sauce, a turkey and vegetable quesadilla, and a beef burger with pineapple and cheese. Anyone who's spent years arguing about pineapple on pizza can now take the debate to a burger in Beijing. To drink there's mulled wine and a hot chocolate with strawberry toffee. Sunset Grill in Hollywood and Hammond's in Jurassic World Isla Nublar cook Winter Holiday dishes as well. Universal CityWalk Beijing serves a Winter Platter, a roast turkey leg and apple pie with cookie ice cream.

## Christmas in Hogsmeade

Hogsmeade is hung with wreaths and fir garlands, the shop windows are lit and the roofs are covered in snow. You switch on the Enchanted Christmas Tree yourself with a wand, and then the lights come on and decorations float. The Holiday Frog Choir is back with its own set list of Christmas songs, and the Three Broomsticks has winter dishes.

In the evening Universal projects The Magic of Christmas at Hogwarts Castle onto the castle: a choir of ghosts, the Yule Ball, owls delivering presents and a Christmas tree. The rides in this land are [Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey](ref:universal-studios-beijing/harry-potter-and-the-forbidden-journey) and [Flight of the Hippogriff](ref:universal-studios-beijing/flight-of-the-hippogriff).

## Winter Holiday 2025

Last year Winter Holiday ran from November 14, 2025, to January 4, 2026. That was the first Christmas in Hogsmeade, with the wand-lit tree and the castle projection. Gift of the Season was new in 2025 as well, on a stage built like an elves' toy factory, with Puss in Boots, Kitty Softpaws, Po, Zhen, Gingy and Xile. In 2025 you met Santa at Santa's Cottage, and Universal Pop: Winter Holiday played the Lakeside Stage.

## Opening hours and tickets

Universal Beijing hasn't published opening hours from November 16 onward yet, nor ticket prices for December and January. Until November 15 the park is open daily from 9 am to 8 pm. Your ticket gets you into every land and attraction, though the park keeps the right to make exceptions. The Winter Holiday announcement doesn't say whether the shows, the tree lighting and the castle projection are included.

The price calendar already lists the November event days. On those days the One-Day Dated Ticket costs 418 yuan Monday to Friday and 473 yuan on Saturdays and Sundays. On November 20 the 1.5-Day Dated Ticket costs 625 yuan and the 2-Day Flex Ticket 705 yuan. Children aged 3 to 11 and seniors 65 and over pay about a quarter less.

Every ticket is issued in one person's name and is only valid with ID. Until midnight two days before your visit you can cancel for free or change the date once. After that, cancelling costs 80 yuan until 11:59 pm the day before, and on the day itself you get nothing back. Universal Express starts at 190 yuan depending on the date and version, admission not included. The standard version gets you in faster once at each participating attraction.

## Getting there and hotels

The resort sits on the East 6th Ring Road in Beijing's Tongzhou district. Metro Line 7 and the Batong Line stop at Universal Resort Station, and from exits B, C and D it's about a seven-minute walk to the Universal CityWalk Beijing security check. Bus routes 589 and T116 also run to the resort. If you drive, a standard parking lot costs 100 yuan if you arrive by 2 pm, 50 yuan by 6 pm and 25 yuan after that, on days without Scares @ Universal.

The Universal Studios Grand Hotel stands right at the park entrance, and the NUO Resort Hotel is a five-minute walk from it. Guests at both hotels with a valid ticket get into selected areas of the park up to an hour before opening. In winter the NUO serves afternoon tea in the lobby bar and a Christmas Eve dinner. On New Year's Eve the NUO throws a party in the lobby bar, and the Grand puts on a New Year's Eve buffet.

## Five years of Universal Beijing

Universal Studios Beijing opened on September 20, 2021, and now has seven lands. The resort also includes Universal CityWalk Beijing and the two hotels. For the anniversary, Universal Beijing announced in September an indoor Fast & Furious ride, the first in Asia based on the films, and a nighttime show on the Lagoon in the middle of the park. Universal hasn't given an opening date for either.
