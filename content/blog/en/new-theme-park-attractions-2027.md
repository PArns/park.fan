On 4 October 2026, parks in Europe and the USA had confirmed twelve novelties for 2027 themselves.
Only one of them comes with a date so far: Plopsaland Belgium opens its Flying Cinema on 13 March 2027. For all the others the park's page says just the year, and for six of the twelve it doesn't even say who builds the ride.

Until the IAAPA announcements in November, most of this sits in individual press releases. This page collects what the park, its operator or the manufacturer has published itself, region by region, each with a link to the source.

## What goes into the tables

A novelty is included if the park, its operator or the manufacturer names it on its own site or in its own press release. Rumours, building permits and photos of construction sites don't count, even when trade media pick them up. Timing and manufacturer are given the way the source gives them. "2027" means 2027, with no month. I've only entered a manufacturer where the source names one, the rest say "not named".

## What goes away in 2027

Two rides run out after this season, and both are being replaced.

At Europa-Park, Euro-Mir (Mack Rides, 1997) says goodbye on 9 January 2027 with a farewell event. Dismantling starts after that. A new family coaster from Mack is going up in the space-themed area, and the park plans it for the 2028 season. The details are in [our report on the farewell](/blog/europa-park-euro-mir-farewell).

Loup-Garou at [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) is in its last season in its current form in 2026. Work starts in January 2027, Rocky Mountain Construction rebuilds the ride, and the park gives 2028 as the opening. The press release of 1 June 2026 is on [Walibi Belgium's press page](https://walibibelgium.prezly.com/walibi-belgium-devoile-sa-future-attraction-majeure).

## What's missing

The list leaves out reports where I couldn't find a page from the park or manufacturer, such as a building application from Walibi Holland or a post on X about a new ride at Cedar Point. For Tropical Americas at Disney's Animal Kingdom it's unclear whether 2027 still holds, so the area isn't here.

The tables are as of 4 October 2026. If a park changes its date, this page changes with it.
