## Improved

- Every page loads about 30 KB less before it can draw, one font fewer, and the animation library
  is out of the first download (about 40 KB compressed). Background photos on a computer are 30 to
  40 % smaller.
- A thin bar at the top shows that the next page is on its way.
- The guide page uses the real park header, weather card and statistics instead of drawings of
  them.

## Fixed

- An open park could carry a "Closed" badge. The badge is worked out from the opening hours and
  the park's clock now, and appears together with the hours.
- An address for a park or ride that does not exist answered with a server error; it is a proper
  404 page now, translated and inside the site's own header and footer.
- A closed park's ride list disappeared on phones.
- Times on park cards, showtimes and the best time on ride cards pushed the layout when they
  arrived.
- When our data service is back, the maintenance page notices within 15 seconds and reloads on its
  own.
- Phones got a real 180 × 180 home-screen icon.
