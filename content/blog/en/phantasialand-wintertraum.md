Since 14 November there's ice on the Kaiserplatz at [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) again, right under the big tree of lights. Wintertraum runs until 24 January 2027, every day from 11 am to 8 pm, and at about 7:50 pm the fireworks of MAGIC SYMPHONY go up over Berlin. In between come seven shows, mulled wine at the stalls and [Taron](ref:phantasialand/taron) in the dark.

There's a lot of new decoration in the park this winter, and the stalls sell plenty of new treats. Neither is on the park's own list of what's new. In Mexico, for example, the Chiapas logo now glows in colour. You can't get to the water ride without passing it, and if you're planning to get wet, you've got time for a photo first.

## Dates and opening times 2026/27

- Season: 14 November 2026 to 24 January 2027 inclusive
- Opening times: 11 am to 8 pm daily
- Closed: 24 and 25 December 2026, 1 January 2027, 12 and 13 January 2027
- New Year's Eve: 31 December, 11 am to 6 pm only
- Totensonntag: on 22 November no show starts before 6 pm

On Totensonntag the rides open at 11 am like on any other Wintertraum day. Only the show programme moves into the evening, because in North Rhine-Westphalia it's a "quiet" public holiday. There's more on that in the post about [Totensonntag at Phantasialand](/blog/phantasialand-totensonntag).

Of the nine hours the park is open, you'll ride just under three to three and a half of them in the dark. In Brühl the sun sets at about 4:45 pm in mid-November, at 4:29 pm on 21 December, the shortest day, and at 5:09 pm on the last Wintertraum day in January. It's properly dark a good half hour later.

## What's new in 2026/27

The park lists these as new for winter 2026/27:

- Phenie's Shakes & Pancakes by the Mondsee lake in Fantasy, marked "NEU" on the park's restaurant page.
- Winni Snow, a snow playground for the youngest in the Tal der Wuze that only exists during Wintertraum.
- Tiempo de Fuego, the 3D mapping show on the rocks of Mexico, runs with new projection technology and new fire effects this winter.
- The park has restaged Der goldene Faden in the Wintergarten for this winter.
- The Quick Pass is now sold in the online shop too, as a QR code on your phone.

The 4D film [Die 3 Mausketiere](ref:phantasialand/die-3-mausketiere) in the Residenz Theater in Berlin has been around since 2025 and runs all year, but the park still advertises it as a "Weltneuheit in 4D", a world first in 4D. The picture comes from an LED wall instead of a projector, and the seats move with it, which the park says is a first in Europe. Lottie, Otto and Herbert chase a rat boss who's out to steal the "Kugel des Spaßes", the ball of fun.

## The seven winter shows

Three shows play in theatres, four outdoors in the themed areas. Each theatre show has one day off a week, and on its website the park gives a fixed time only for the finale. All other show times are posted day by day on the screens in the park and in the Phantasialand app. The theatres open 30 minutes before the show and seating is limited.

| Show                        | Type                           | Where                     | Length  | Day off    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Spectaculum Phantastère     | Singing and acrobatics         | Silverado Theatre, Mexico | ~30 min | Mondays    |
| Der goldene Faden           | Acrobatics and dance           | Wintergarten, Berlin      | ~40 min | Tuesdays   |
| Arctaris – The Hidden World | Ice show                       | Arena de Fiesta, Mexico   | ~30 min | Wednesdays |
| Kaiserplatz Illumination    | Light show with music and fire | Kaiserplatz, Berlin       | –       | –          |
| Tiempo de Fuego             | 3D mapping and fire            | Plaza Mariachi, Mexico    | ~25 min | –          |
| Pulse of Rookburgh          | 360-degree light and laser     | Rookburgh                 | ~10 min | –          |
| MAGIC SYMPHONY              | Closing show with fireworks    | Kaiserplatz, Berlin       | ~10 min | –          |

**Spectaculum Phantastère** is a jesters' festival in a glittering castle, with trampoline acts, acrobatics and live singing, aimed at children of primary school age and up.

**Der goldene Faden** opens in a costume store. The tailor's assistant pulls loose a golden thread, and from then on the show jumps back and forth between past and present, with acrobatics, dance and a lot of comedy.

**Arctaris – The Hidden World** is the ice show in the Arena de Fiesta, with video walls, pair skating and fire acts on skates. An ice show in December sounds like being cold twice over, but the arena is a theatre with a roof.

The park recommends the **Kaiserplatz Illumination** to families with small children, for whom a finale at ten to eight is too late. It starts at dusk, when the tree of lights and the houses of Berlin come on to a song written for the show.

For **Tiempo de Fuego**, get there a quarter of an hour early. The show projects the changing seasons onto the rocks and buildings of Mexico, at the end flames several metres high shoot up around the bell tower, and in bad weather it may be cancelled.

At **Pulse of Rookburgh**, beams of light cut through the Rookburgh fog, through the track of [F.L.Y.](ref:phantasialand/fly) and over the Hotel Charles Lindbergh. The show uses fast, bright strobe effects, so if you're sensitive to them, watch from further back.

Outside the programme, sway pole artists perform on tall poles on the Kaiserplatz, and the park's dragons meet children in their own themed areas: Drago in Berlin, Wang in China Town, Kroka in Deep in Africa and Phenie in Fantasy.

### MAGIC SYMPHONY and the Good Night Wish

MAGIC SYMPHONY was voted Germany's best show by theme park visitors for the second year running. It lasts about ten minutes, with light, sparks, a song written for it and fireworks over the roofs of Berlin.

Not everybody fits on the Kaiserplatz, so the park advises heading to Berlin from 6:50 pm. From then on, staff guide every guest in through separate entrances and assign the spots, and the entrances close at 7:40 pm at the latest, earlier on busy days. Pushchairs go to their own parking areas. The stalls around the ice rink stay open until the show and close completely while it's on, and the area right next to it is cordoned off because of the fireworks. So take your mulled wine and crêpe with you into the Berlin street beforehand. Wheelchair users have their own viewing area, which you can book for free at Guest Service until 6 pm.

In strong wind or other bad weather MAGIC SYMPHONY is cancelled for safety reasons. The park then says goodbye with the "Good Night Wish", a short scene of music and light without fireworks. The screens in the park show in the evening whether the fireworks are on.

## Lights and LIGHTS OF MEMORIES

At dusk each themed area switches on its own lighting. The park talks of millions of lights. I didn't count them.

- Berlin: the big tree of lights above the ice rink on the Kaiserplatz
- China Town: a glowing dragon above the pagodas
- Mystery and Klugheim: red fog in the gorges under Taron
- Rookburgh: floodlit zeppelins
- Deep in Africa: glowing masks above the jungle
- Mexico: fire in the rocks of Chiapas
- Fantasy: balls of light on the Mondsee lake

LIGHTS OF MEMORIES is the park's name for the light shows that run in several places in the evening: Kaiserplatz Illumination, Tiempo de Fuego, Pulse of Rookburgh and MAGIC SYMPHONY, plus two that aren't in any show schedule. In **Mystic Winter Castle**, the tower of [Mystery Castle](ref:phantasialand/mystery-castle) uses light to look as if its walls are bursting apart, with rushing water and trolls popping up. And at **Water. Music. Light.**, fountains dance around the [Wellenflug](ref:phantasialand/wellenflug) in Berlin.

## What runs and what takes the winter off

Everything runs during Wintertraum except three water attractions. [River Quest](ref:phantasialand/river-quest) has been closed since 2 November, Wakobato and Wözl's Wassertreter since 14 November, all three until 24 January. The splash playgrounds Winni Splash and Wavy Battle aren't on the park's winter overview, but there's Winni Snow in the Tal der Wuze instead.

[Taron](ref:phantasialand/taron?full)

The big rides are outdoors. The park says Taron runs down to minus ten degrees, [Black Mamba](ref:phantasialand/black-mamba) and [Chiapas](ref:phantasialand/chiapas-die-wasserbahn) down to minus five. Chiapas brakes with magnets at the end instead of in the water, which the park says gets you "usually less wet". At four below zero I'd take that "usually" seriously and pack a dry jacket to change into.

You can ride in the dry in the Wuze Town hall, where [Winja's Fear](ref:phantasialand/winjas-fear), [Winja's Force](ref:phantasialand/winjas-force) and Wözl's Duck Washer run. Die 3 Mausketiere plays in the air-conditioned Residenz Theater, and the Geister Rikscha runs underground. That's thin for a whole wet afternoon, which is where the three theatre shows come in handy.

## For children: ice rink, snow and height limits

**Berliner Eislaufen** is the ice rink that's only on the Kaiserplatz in winter, right under the tree of lights. There's no minimum height, you hire skates right next to it, and beginners can push penguin skating aids. For the youngest there's Winni Snow in Fantasy, plus Avoras to climb on and Mopti's Monkey Depot in Deep in Africa, none with a height limit. The face painters are free.

At Guest Service on the Kaiserplatz, staff measure your child and write the height on a wristband, so you don't have to get measured again at every ride. There and at the entrances you can also pick up free wristbands for a parent's phone number. With Baby Switch, one parent rides while the other waits with the child, then they swap without queuing twice. It works at Taron, F.L.Y., Black Mamba, Chiapas and Maus au Chocolat, among others.

Height limits for the rides open during Wintertraum:

- [Maus au Chocolat](ref:phantasialand/maus-au-chocolat) and Die 3 Mausketiere: from 0.90 m, accompanied up to 1.20 m
- [Colorado Adventure](ref:phantasialand/colorado-adventure), [Raik](ref:phantasialand/raik), Crazy Bats and Wellenflug: from 1.00 m, accompanied up to 1.20 m
- Chiapas: from 1.20 m, accompanied up to 1.30 m
- Winja's Fear and Force: from 1.20 m, accompanied up to 1.40 m and between ages 6 and 10, maximum 2.00 m
- Taron: from 1.30 m, accompanied up to 1.40 m
- F.L.Y.: from 1.30 m and age 8, maximum 2.05 m
- Mystery Castle: from 1.30 m and age 10, maximum 1.95 m
- Black Mamba and Talocan: from 1.40 m and age 10, maximum 1.95 m

Children up to three get in free and don't need a ticket, only an ID card or birth certificate, and a copy will do. Children up to and including eleven also get in free on their birthday, provided they book the visit online in advance.

## Food and drink at Wintertraum

Only in these weeks can you get salmon cooked over an open fire and, on the Kaiserplatz, a kale pan. Next to the mulled wine there's eggnog punch and Hot Chardonnay, and alcohol-free punch for children. On the sweet side there are concha doughnuts with pistachio or nougat and crêpe brûlée, and the park's patisserie bakes its own winter tartlets.

**Phenie's Shakes & Pancakes** sits on the shore of the Mondsee lake and looks like a round magic mushroom, with a floor that looks like dough, a topping machine and dripping lamps. The menu has pancake towers, shakes and, for children up to eleven, Cold Pancakes with soft serve, which won't warm anyone up after a turn on the ice but do cheer them up.

At the Parkscout Publikums Award 2025/26, visitors voted Phantasialand's food the best of any German theme park. For a proper meal somewhere warm, my favourite is Rutmor's Taverne in Klugheim, with Eierknöpfli. Restaurant Uhrwerk in Rookburgh is another warm, themed place to eat.

## How busy Wintertraum gets

We measured last year's Wintertraum from 26 December 2025 to 25 January 2026. We have no figures from last winter for November and early December. During the Christmas holidays people queued at Phantasialand as long as in the August summer holidays. January was quieter, but only on weekdays. On 6 January 2026, the last day of the holidays, crowds were very low. On the weekdays after the holidays they were low, and on the weekends of 10/11 and 17/18 January very high.

This winter the North Rhine-Westphalia Christmas holidays run from 23 December 2026 to 6 January 2027, and I'd plan the days between Christmas and New Year like a day in August. Monday to Friday after the holidays was the quietest stretch last January, and on many of those days the park charges €29, the lowest price in the calendar.

On the first Wintertraum weekend of 2026, crowds were high on Saturday, 14 November, and very low on the Sunday, in drizzle. That's only one Sunday, but in that kind of weather the Winja's rides run in their hall and the three theatre shows are under a roof, so I wouldn't call off a day like that. I expect Totensonntag on 22 November to be busy, because it falls on the second Wintertraum weekend and all seven shows are squeezed into two evening hours.

## The Quick Pass during Wintertraum

With a Quick Pass you skip the queue once at one ride, through a separate entrance. You can buy it at Guest Service on the Kaiserplatz and in the online shop.

- Taron After Dark, from 5 pm: €12 (online, it's the only single pass for Taron)
- Black Mamba: €10
- Winja's Fear, Winja's Force, Colorado Adventure: €7 each
- Maus au Chocolat, Mystery Castle, Talocan: €5 each
- Chiapas: €10, only at Guest Service, according to the info page
- Quick Pass Ultimate: €80, once at every Quick Pass entrance and once at F.L.Y., which has no single pass

Every Quick Pass is only valid on the day you book it, so buy it in the morning of your visit, not the evening before. Sales in the shop start at 11 am when the park opens, and the park only sells a limited number. How booking in the shop and the QR code at the Quick Pass entrance work is in our news item on the [Quick Pass going online](/news/phantasialand-quick-pass-online).

On a Tuesday in January I'd keep my money, since those days were quiet last winter. On a Saturday in the Christmas holidays I'd buy a Taron After Dark pass right at eleven and use it for my first ride in the dark.

## A Wintertraum day from 11 am to 8 pm

Say you're coming on a Saturday in December, with a dated ticket on your phone, and you want everything that only exists in winter.

You're at the gate just before eleven, and at eleven you head straight to Taron in Klugheim. After that, Black Mamba in Deep in Africa and Chiapas in Mexico while it's still light and your jacket can dry. In the early afternoon, when the queues are longest, sit down in one of the theatre shows; none of them has its day off on a Saturday. Children can go skating or into the Wuze Town hall in between.

By about five it's dark. Now ride Taron again, through fog lit red and past basalt columns that have turned into outlines. Then F.L.Y. through floodlit Rookburgh, where Pulse of Rookburgh plays every so often, and Tiempo de Fuego in Mexico. With small children, the Kaiserplatz Illumination at dusk makes a better finale.

At 6:50 pm head to Berlin, get your last mulled wine from a stall and be on the Kaiserplatz by 7:35 pm at the latest. MAGIC SYMPHONY starts at about 7:50 pm, and the park closes at 8. If you came by shuttle, the last bus back to Brühl leaves at 9:15 pm.

## Tickets and prices

Phantasialand sells tickets only in its online shop. There's no ticket office at the gate, and every ticket is for a fixed date. On the day of your visit you can still buy one until 2 pm, at that day's price and as long as the day isn't sold out. Tickets can't be changed or cancelled.

The price depends on the day and can go up at any time, because each price tier has a limited allocation per day. Until Monday, 16 November, the park is running an opening discount with tickets from €30, otherwise up to €78.

## Getting there in winter

Phantasialand is in Brühl, between Cologne and Bonn. The address for your sat nav is Berggeiststraße 31-41, 50321 Brühl. By car, leave the A553 at exit 2, Brühl-Süd. The Bornheim exit, which the park says sat navs like to suggest, is the wrong one. You park in the Mystery (P1) and China (P2) car parks for €8 per car per day, cash or card. You can't reserve a space, and there are no motorhome pitches.

By train, take the RE5, RB26 or RB48 to Brühl (Rheinland), or KVB light rail line 18 to Brühl Mitte. From there the park's shuttle bus takes you there and back for €6 per person, and children up to 1.00 m ride free. Regional transport tickets aren't valid on the shuttle. At Brühl station the shuttle currently stops on Comesstraße because of roadworks, about two minutes' walk from the station forecourt.

During Wintertraum the shuttle only runs on days the park is open. The first bus leaves the station at 10:25 am, then every half hour. The first one back leaves the park at 10:45 am, the last at 9:15 pm. Without the shuttle, bus 985 goes to the Berggeiststraße stop, about ten minutes' walk from the park.

From Cologne Bonn Airport it's 40 to 50 minutes to Brühl by train, or about 20 minutes by car.

## Staying overnight in winter

The park has three themed hotels right next to it: Ling Bao in China Town, Matamba in Deep in Africa and Charles Lindbergh in Rookburgh, which won "Europe's best themed hotel" at the Parkscout|plus Award 2025.

For a short winter break the park advertises up to 45 % off for early bookings. Breakfast is included, parking at the hotel is free, and you walk straight into the park through the hotel gardens or a gate of your own. Guests at Matamba and Ling Bao get faster access once per person per night to one of these rides: Chiapas, Maus au Chocolat, Talocan, Winja's Fear & Force or Colorado Adventure, but only with a valid park ticket. Charles Lindbergh guests get faster access to F.L.Y. as Explorers, and the hotel also has an Explorer package with a night's stay, a three-course dinner, breakfast and two park days. The new pool and spa area at Ling Bao is for its own guests only.

The New Year's Eve packages with an overnight stay are fully booked.

## New Year's Eve at Phantasialand

On New Year's Eve the park closes at 6 pm, almost two hours before the usual fireworks. The park hasn't said yet whether MAGIC SYMPHONY starts earlier as a result.

For the evening there are four events without an overnight stay, booked separately:

- Asian Celebration, an evening of dim sum and Cantonese barbecue with a children's buffet of its own: adults €245, ages 12 to 17 €210, ages 4 to 11 €105
- African Journey, with twelve stations from street food to wine tasting and a children's buffet: adults €250, ages 12 to 17 €215, ages 4 to 11 €110
- Kaiser-Silvester, seven courses of Asian food, age 12 and up: adults €285, ages 12 to 17 €250
- Fantissima Silvester-Gala, the dinner show with a four-course menu, age 14 and up: from €390 per person

## Wintertraum's history since 2002

Until 2001, Phantasialand shut from November to March. In 2002 it opened for a few weeks in winter for the first time, at first only until around 6 January. In the early years there was a walk-in snow globe, Father Christmas's office, a living nativity scene, a snowboard simulator and a toboggan run on the hill between Mystery Castle and River Quest, plus an ice rink even back then. Of all that, only the ice rink is left.

From 1996 to 2006 the Festival der Lichter ran every October, with opening hours extended to 9 pm, thousands of lights and, to close, the light parade "The Miracle of Light" in Alt-Berlin. From 2002 to 2006 the two programmes ran side by side, and then the park dropped the festival.

Up to the 2019/20 season the winter programme kept changing its name. Since then it's been called Wintertraum, and under that name visitors voted it Germany's best event at the Parkscout Publikums Award 2025/26, as in the two years before. Since 2021 the park no longer closes in autumn. It converts to winter while it's open and only shuts for two months after Wintertraum. That's why the 2026 season began on 28 March and runs without a break until 24 January 2027.

## Frequently asked questions about Phantasialand's Wintertraum

### When is Phantasialand Wintertraum 2026/27?

From 14 November 2026 to 24 January 2027, daily from 11 am to 8 pm. The park is closed on 24 and 25 December, 1 January and 12 and 13 January. On New Year's Eve it closes at 6 pm.

### What time do the fireworks start at Phantasialand?

MAGIC SYMPHONY with fireworks starts every evening at about 7:50 pm on the Kaiserplatz in Berlin and lasts about ten minutes. The entrances close at 7:40 pm at the latest. In strong wind the fireworks are cancelled and the "Good Night Wish" of music and light takes their place.

### Which shows are on at Wintertraum?

Seven: Spectaculum Phantastère in the Silverado Theatre, Der goldene Faden in the Wintergarten, the ice show Arctaris in the Arena de Fiesta, and outdoors Kaiserplatz Illumination, Tiempo de Fuego, Pulse of Rookburgh and MAGIC SYMPHONY. The theatre shows take turns to rest on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

### Which attractions are closed during Wintertraum?

River Quest, Wakobato and Wözl's Wassertreter. Taron, F.L.Y., Black Mamba, Chiapas, Maus au Chocolat and the Winja's rides are running. The park says Taron runs down to minus ten degrees, Black Mamba and Chiapas down to minus five.

### How much does Phantasialand Wintertraum cost?

An adult day ticket costs between €29 on weekdays in January and €42 on some weekend days such as 27 December, depending on the date. Tickets are sold online only, until 2 pm on the day. Children up to three get in free.

### When is Wintertraum quietest?

Monday to Friday in January, after the Christmas holidays. Last winter the Christmas holidays were as busy as the August summer holidays, the January weekends were very busy, and the weekdays in between were quiet.

### Is there a Quick Pass during Wintertraum?

Yes, at Guest Service on the Kaiserplatz and in the online shop. Each pass is only valid on the day you book it, and online sales start at 11 am. In the shop, Taron After Dark from 5 pm costs €12, Colorado Adventure €7 and the Ultimate with F.L.Y. €80. Chiapas is only sold at Guest Service.
