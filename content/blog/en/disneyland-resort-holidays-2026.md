The holiday season at the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim starts on 13 November and runs until 6 January 2027 at both [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park) and [Disney California Adventure](ref:disney-california-adventure-park). This year Santa sits in the Trolley Barn in Mickey's Toontown at Disneyland Park, next to [Roger Rabbit's Car Toon Spin](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/roger-rabbits-car-toon-spin?bare).

In 2025 he was still part of "Holiday Fun with Santa and Friends!" at the Fantasyland Theatre. That stage now belongs to "Bluey's Best Day Ever!", so Santa lost his theatre to a cartoon dog.

## Dates

- Holiday season in both parks: 13 November to 6 January 2027
- "World of Color – Season of Light" and the Disney Festival of Holidays: from 13 November
- Santa in Toontown and at the Redwood Creek Challenge Trail: 13 November to 24 December
- Chip and Dale's Ornament Trail at Downtown Disney: 11 November to 6 January 2027
- Holiday character dining at the hotels: 16 November to 3 January 2027

The season carries on until 6 January, but from 25 December Santa's no longer around. If you'd rather spend Christmas in Florida or near Paris, the dates are in the [guide to Christmas and New Year in Orlando and at Disneyland Paris](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026).

## Disneyland Park

A Christmas tree 60 ft tall stands on Main Street, U.S.A. Mickey, Minnie and their friends come out on Town Square in new outfits themed on winter travel and the Alps. Sleeping Beauty's Winter Castle carries more than 80,000 lights, and every evening artificial snow falls on Main Street during "Wintertime Enchantment".

"A Christmas Fantasy Parade" brings toy soldiers, dancing reindeer and gingerbread men through the park, with Santa riding at the very end. During the "Believe… In Holiday Magic" fireworks, snow falls on parts of Main Street, and like the parade they're carried over from last year. You can watch them in front of the castle, at "it's a small world" Holiday or along the Rivers of America.

Jolly Holiday Bakery Cafe sells a milk-and-cookies macaron shaped like a Christmas tree, and Galactic Grill a crispy chicken sandwich with cranberry.

## Haunted Mansion Holiday and "it's a small world" Holiday

[Haunted Mansion Holiday](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/haunted-mansion-holiday) has been running since Halloween Time began and stays through the holidays. Disney posts the access rules for each day only on the day itself, in the Disneyland app and on Disneyland.com. Jack Skellington from "Tim Burton's The Nightmare Before Christmas" plays "Santa Jack" there. The holiday version has run since October 2001, and Disney has built a new gingerbread house for the ballroom every year since. In 2026 it's a music box more than 10 ft tall. A key topped with Santa Jack turns on top while a carol plays, and when the roof lifts, something made of bones comes out. Disney doesn't describe it any further.

In the séance room, 13 fortune-telling cards spin with the "13 Days of Christmas". The Madame Leota there is Kim Irvine, daughter of Leota Toombs, the original Madame Leota. Nearly 7,500 sq ft of artificial snow covers the graveyard.

["it's a small world" Holiday](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/its-a-small-world-holiday) has been at Disneyland since 1997. The facade glows with 400,000 lights and the clock wears a Santa hat. The song you already couldn't shake is mixed with "Jingle Bells" and "Deck the Halls". At the exit of the [Jungle Cruise](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/jungle-cruise?bare) there's a tree decorated in skipper style.

## Disney California Adventure

In Cars Land, [Mater's Jingle Jamboree](ref:disney-california-adventure-park/maters-jingle-jamboree?bare) and [Luigi's Joy to the Whirl](ref:disney-california-adventure-park/luigis-joy-to-the-whirl?bare) run in their holiday versions. Santa sits at the [Redwood Creek Challenge Trail](ref:disney-california-adventure-park/redwood-creek-challenge-trail?bare) in this park, with games beside him. In the evening, "World of Color – Season of Light" shows scenes from "Beauty and the Beast" and "Inside Out".

For the Festival of Holidays, the "Mickey's Happy Holidays" cavalcade, with Mickey and the Holiday Toy Drummers, travels from Paradise Gardens Park to Hollywood Land several times a day. On some days there's also "Mirabel's Gifts of the Season", "A Musical Christmas with Mariachi Alegría de Disneyland & Miguel!" on Buena Vista Street and the "¡Viva Navidad! Street Party" with the Three Caballeros. Six marketplaces sell festival food, including braised pork belly adobo. "Schmoozies!" has the Mickey Elf Shake with mint and chocolate cookie.

## Park reservations and extras

Besides a ticket, you need a park reservation for the same park on the same day.

The Sip and Savor Pass costs extra: six digital coupons for food and non-alcoholic drinks at the marketplaces and some restaurants in Disney California Adventure. It's valid for the whole festival and can be shared. You can only buy it in the park on the day of your visit, not in advance online or in the app. Magic Key holders get a discount on some versions. The "Holiday Time at the Disneyland Resort" tour with reserved parade seating, the Plaza Inn dining package for "A Christmas Fantasy Parade" and the "World of Color – Season of Light" dessert party each need their own booking and their own payment.

## Hotels and Downtown Disney

Santa also drops in at the Disneyland Hotel, the Pixar Place Hotel and the Grand Californian Hotel & Spa, though Disney gives no fixed times. The Great Hall of the Grand Californian has a gingerbread house a little over 7 ft tall, in the hotel's Craftsman style. Holiday dining at Goofy's Kitchen in the Disneyland Hotel is called "Goofy's Kitchen Celebrates the Holidays!". Storytellers Cafe has "Daisy's Holiday Pajama Party" for breakfast and "Mickey's Christmas Carol Feast" in the evening. Reservations aren't open yet, and Disney hasn't announced a date.

For nights from 11 October to 18 December, the hotels take up to 20 percent off if you stay at least three nights and book by 15 December. From 13 November, snow falls every evening at the "Downtown Disney LIVE!" stage at Downtown Disney, with bands and DJs playing.
