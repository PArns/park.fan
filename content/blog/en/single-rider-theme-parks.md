Single rider means you join a separate queue and take a seat that is left over in the vehicle, next
to strangers. In return you usually wait less than in the standard queue.

On park.fan, 66 rides in 18 parks have a single rider queue.

## Where the single rider queue comes from

Disneyland in Anaheim opened on 17 July 1955. Three years later Walt Disney sent the park’s
Operations Committee a memo, which the English Wikipedia dates to 31 July 1958:

> [!QUOTE]
> Perhaps during peak hours we should […] establish a special line for single riders, with the request that they double up.
>
> Walt Disney, memo from 1958, quoted by Marcy Carriker Smothers in an [interview with D23 on 15 December 2021](https://d23.com/inside-walts-disneyland-an-immersive-guide-to-the-park-walt-helped-create/)

Smothers published the memo in her book “Walt’s Disneyland”, which came out in 2021 from Disney
Editions.

## How a single rider entrance works

A train has fixed seat groups, and not every group in the standard queue fills them. Where a seat
stays empty, the park puts someone from the single rider queue into it. The ride runs fuller, and the
groups in the standard queue lose nothing, because nobody from their row is moved ahead. Efteling
writes that this shortens the wait for groups and single riders alike.

The single rider queue only moves as fast as gaps appear. Efteling writes that single riders usually wait less, but that how much depends on the number of visitors and the free seats.

At every park whose page we could read:

1. **You don’t pick your seat.** Efteling assigns a seat, together with other guests. Walt Disney
   World guarantees neither immediate boarding nor your choice of seat.
2. **The usual boarding requirements apply.** Walt Disney World and the Disneyland Resort write that
   single riders must meet all of the ride’s requirements. Efteling lets children enter the single
   rider entrance alone if they meet the ride’s height and other requirements.
3. **The queue can be closed.** According to Efteling, it may stay shut on quiet days. Walt Disney
   World writes that the service depends on availability.

## Who it is for, and when it is not

The single rider entrance is worth it in three cases: you are alone in the park, your group can be
split, or you badly want one particular ride and the standard wait is too long for you. In the second
case you split up, ride separately and meet at the exit.

It isn’t worth it in these cases:

- **You are riding with a child who needs a companion.** For Eurosat at Europa-Park the park’s page
  gives 120 to 195 centimetres, and below 130 centimetres only with an adult. If you need the child
  next to you, you can’t board separately. For parents who ride one after the other, Europa-Park
  offers Baby-Switch. One adult rides, the other waits with the child and then boards through the exit
  or a separate entrance. According to the park this is only available on the bigger roller coasters.
- **You want to ride together.** Efteling lets groups use the single rider entrance, but they ride one
  after another. Only if several seats happen to be free do people from the single rider queue sit
  side by side.
- **You want to ride the same version.** On Symbolica at Efteling you cannot
  choose which of the three palace tours you get as a single rider.
- **The standard queue is short anyway.** Then the advantage is gone, and the group is split for no
  reason.

## Single rider on the attraction page

**Does the ride have a single rider queue?** If it does, park.fan shows a “Single rider” badge on
the attraction page. A missing badge doesn’t mean there is none, often we just don’t know for that
ride. The badge doesn’t say whether the queue is open today.

**How long is it right now?** On 8 October 2026, twelve of the 18 parks
reported 41 single rider queues between them. 19 of them were open, in seven parks. Not one
of the 41 carried a wait time. The parks tell us the queue is open, but not how long it is. On the
attraction page park.fan then shows “Single rider” without a time, and the tables in this post show
the standard queue.

## Europa-Park

At [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park), six rides have a single rider entrance: ARTHUR in the Minimoys
Kingdom themed area, WODAN – Timburcoaster and blue fire Megacoaster in Iceland, Eurosat – CanCan
Coaster in France, the Voletarium in Germany and Voltron Nevera powered by Rimac in Croatia.

The park lists all six under “Single Rider” on its page of tips for your visit. On the pages for
[Voltron Nevera](ref:europa-park/voltron-nevera-powered-by-rimac) and
[Eurosat – CanCan Coaster](ref:europa-park/eurosat-cancan-coaster), single rider also appears among
the ride’s features, as a special queue for individual guests. Voltron Nevera admits riders from
130 centimetres, and a train holds 16 people. On Eurosat the limit is 120 to 195 centimetres and six
years of age, and under eight only those with an adult ride along.

blue fire, WODAN, the Voletarium and Voltron Nevera also offer the VirtualLine. You reserve a
time slot in the app and spend the time until then elsewhere in the park.

All six rides have a minimum height of 120 or 130 centimetres, according to the figures in our
database.

## Efteling

[Efteling](ref:efteling)’s page on the single rider entrance lists six rides under “Attractions with a single rider entrance”: Danse Macabre, Joris en de Draak,
Symbolica, Baron 1898, Max & Moritz and Python. Each has two queues, one for groups and families, one
for single riders, plus an entrance for visitors with disabilities, usually near the exit. The answers
higher up on the same page leave out Baron 1898. In the standard queue Efteling guarantees groups that
they sit together. Our data
additionally lists De Vliegende Hollander, which the park’s page doesn’t name.

According to the page, a single rider chooses neither the seat nor, on Symbolica, which of the three palace tours they get. Groups may use the entrance but ride one after another, and children may
enter alone if they meet the ride’s requirements. The page doesn’t state the requirements, they differ
by attraction. According to the figures in our database, the minimum height is 90 centimetres on
Max & Moritz, 110 on Joris en de Draak, 120 on Python, De Vliegende Hollander and Danse Macabre, and
132 on Baron 1898. Symbolica has no figure.

## How to plan a day with single rider

Pick two or three rides beforehand. On the park page at park.fan you can see which rides carry the
badge. The entrance is worth most on the ride where the standard queue is longest.

Check that the queue is open before you split up. On the attraction page the “Single rider” badge
appears with the status of the queue. It can be closed without the ride being closed.

For two adults the fastest option is for one to take the standard queue
and the other the single rider entrance. Whoever rides first waits at the exit.

## Frequently asked questions about single rider

### What does single rider mean in a theme park?

Single rider is a separate queue for individual guests. Whoever uses it takes the seats left over in a
vehicle and sits next to strangers.

### Is the single rider queue always shorter?

No. You usually board sooner, but at Efteling, for one, how much sooner depends on how busy the park
is and how many seats are left over. On quiet days the queue can be closed altogether.

### Can I use single rider with my family?

Only if everyone is willing to ride separately. At Efteling a group may go through the single rider entrance,
but then it’s one rider after another. A child who needs a companion has to sit next to the adult, so the single
rider entrance is ruled out.

### Which rides at Europa-Park have single rider?

Six: ARTHUR, WODAN, blue fire, Eurosat, the Voletarium and Voltron Nevera.

### Which rides at Efteling have single rider?

Six by the park’s page, in alphabetical order: Baron 1898, Danse Macabre, Joris en de Draak, Max &
Moritz, Python and Symbolica. In our data De Vliegende Hollander is listed as well.

### Where can I see whether a ride has single rider?

On the attraction page at park.fan, from the “Single rider” badge. If the badge is missing, the value
may be unknown.

### Do I have to meet the minimum height on single rider?

Yes. At Walt Disney World and the Disneyland Resort every requirement of the ride applies to single
riders, minimum height included, and Efteling holds children who go in alone to the same.

### Can I choose my seat?

No. Efteling assigns a seat, and Walt Disney World doesn’t guarantee the choice of seat.

## Which lists are complete

At Europa-Park and Phantasialand it is recorded for almost every attraction whether it has a single
rider entrance, so the lists there are complete. At the other parks this is missing for most
attractions, at Disneyland Park in Paris for 41 of 43, at Disneyland Park in Anaheim for 54 of 56.
There may be more single rider rides there, and in the 185 parks where we know of none.

For Phantasialand, PortAventura, Universal and the Asian Disney parks we could not read a park page
that confirms the list.
