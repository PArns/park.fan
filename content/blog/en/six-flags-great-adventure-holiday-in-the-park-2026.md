Holiday in the Park starts at [Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) in New Jersey on Saturday, 21 November. This year, for the first time, the park splits the event into four Christmas areas of its own. New Jersey's own devil gets the holidays off, of all times: [Jersey Devil Coaster](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/jersey-devil-coaster?bare) won't run at any point during the event, and neither will [El Toro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/el-toro?bare) or [Runaway Mine Train](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/runaway-mine-train?bare).

## Dates and opening hours

On Friday, 20 November, passholders have the park to themselves from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m. at the Season Passholder Preview Night. Everyone else can come from the following day, and every Holiday day runs from 1 p.m. to 8 p.m.:

- November: 21, 22, 27, 28 and 29
- December: 5, 6, 12, 13, 19, 20, 23 and 26 to 31
- January 2027: 1, 2 and 3

The park is closed on Thanksgiving, 26 November, and on 24 and 25 December. New Year's Eve and New Year's Day are open, though. The water park and Wild Safari stay closed on every Holiday day.

After Fright Fest, the park still opens before the event from 5 to 8 November and on 14 and 15 November, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. each day, with no programme.

## The four areas and the shows

At the entrance, in Glittering Gateway, there's a 65-foot Christmas tree, just under 20 metres. That's where the Tree Lighting Spectacular happens every evening, along with the Glistening Snow Show and its falling snow, which means New Jersey gets snow on schedule in at least one spot. Santa waits in Santa's Arctic North Pole, which also has fire pits and "S'mores with Santa's Helpers".

Coastal Christmas sits on Shoreline Pier, which the park has just rebuilt, among glittering palm trees. Sweet Street has the Sweet Tooth Stage with a silent disco. Outside the four areas, the Showcase Theater puts on "Holiday Road: LIVE on Tour!". More fire pits for s'mores burn behind the Big Wheel, and there's the Elf Craze Mirror Maze. Carolers walk the park, more shows run on the main stage, and there's holiday food and drink.

## Which rides stay shut

Besides the three coasters, Tango and the rides in Bugs Bunny National Park won't run during Holiday in the Park. The other rides are meant to run if weather and temperature allow. The [Nitro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/nitro?bare) page currently says "Temporarily Closed".

## Tickets, parking and getting there

Holiday in the Park is included in the Gold Season Pass and in an active membership. That can also apply to buyers from the 2025 MVP Sale, to the All-Park Add-On, to the Prestige Pass and to Gold passholders at some other Six Flags parks on the East Coast. If you buy a 2027 Season Pass now, you get the rest of 2026 thrown in, Holiday in the Park included. Parking in the general lot is free with it.

The 2027 Gold Pass costs $99 instead of $115, and the price goes up from 2 November. The Prestige Pass is $249. Memberships cost $8 or $20 a month plus a $20 initiation fee. Six Flags adds a processing fee of up to $9.99 to all three.

Six Flags hasn't announced a day-ticket price for Holiday evenings yet. General parking starts at $45 a day and Priority Parking at $65, each plus a fee. For the satnav, the address is 1 Six Flags Blvd, Jackson Township, NJ 08527.
