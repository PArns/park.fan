[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) in Haßloch opened for the winter as Majaland Deutschland on Friday, 6 November. Until 14 March 2027, the park opens just Majaland with its children's rides themed on Maya the Bee, the Plopsa Indoor hall and the dark ride [The Smurfs' Adventure](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/the-smurfs-adventure). On almost every Friday, a dated ticket bought online costs €16. Plopsa announced the programme on 9 November.

## Opening days and hours

The park opens on 72 days, always from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Outside the school holidays, that means the weekends from Friday to Sunday, and during the holidays the days in between as well:

- November: 6 to 8, 13 to 15, 20 to 22 and 27 to 29
- December: 4 to 6, 11 to 13, 18 to 23 and 26 to 31
- January: 1 to 10, 15 to 17, 22 to 24 and 29 to 31
- February: 4 to 14, 19 to 21 and 26 to 28
- March: 5 to 7 and 12 to 14

The gates stay shut on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, when Father Christmas has a busier round to make anyway. By 26 December he's back in the Indoor Theater for his last day of the season. On 27 November, Plopsa Indoor closes at 4 p.m. for a private event.

## What a ticket costs

Online, a dated ticket costs €27 or €29 on Saturdays and €29 on Sundays. On weekdays in the school holidays it's anywhere from €16 to €29. The Friday price of €16 applies every Friday except New Year's Day (€24) and 5 February (€22). An undated ticket is €32, the same as at the gate.

These prices apply from one metre in height. Children under 85 centimetres get in free. Children between 85 and 99 centimetres pay from €19 with a date and €22 without. Visitors aged 70 and over pay €22. The family ticket has no fixed date and is valid until 14 March: €75 for three people, €95 for four, €115 for five and €130 for six. For four people over one metre, single tickets work out cheaper on a Friday, while on a Sunday the family ticket is the better deal. The annual pass costs €150 and covers a year of both Plopsaland and Majaland. The other parks open this winter and their prices are in our [winter overview](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

## Rides and shows this winter

Majaland has twelve outdoor attractions, among them the Bienchenwirbel and the Blumenturm. The heated Plopsa Indoor hall has seven more, alongside slides and climbing areas. One of them is [Tabaluga's Rollercoaster](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/tabalugas-rollercoaster), with 444 metres of track, a height of 9 metres and a speed of 40 km/h. Riders need to be at least 100 centimetres tall. The Smurfs' Adventure sits in what used to be Burg Falkenstein. The ride lasts five minutes. On both rides, children under 120 centimetres can only ride accompanied.

Maya the Bee turns 50 as a cartoon character in 2026. Her show "Der Ball der Bienenkönigin" (The Queen Bee's Ball) plays in the Indoor Theater at 1 p.m. and 4 p.m. on every opening day. Maya, Willy and Flip get ready for a costume ball, but only one of them can go with the queen. Singing along is explicitly allowed, and that includes parents who haven't got the theme tune out of their heads since they were kids. The show is included in admission. At 1:45 p.m. and 3:15 p.m. you can meet Maya and her friends. Heidi and Peter, Holly, Tabaluga and the Smurfs are out in the park too. The Pfalzgraf restaurant in the Palatine Village is open on Saturdays and Sundays.

Expedition GeForce, Sky Scream and the other big rides from our [guide to Plopsaland Deutschland](/blog/plopsaland-deutschland-wait-times-tips) stand still in winter and run again from the start of the season on 19 March 2027.

## Father Christmas, the St Nicholas run and carnival

From 4 December, Father Christmas sits in the Indoor Theater on every opening day. Children can hand him their wish lists, and every child gets a chocolate from the Wawi chocolate factory. On Saturday, 5 December, the second Plopsa Nikolauslauf, a St Nicholas run with courses for children and adults, starts at the Platz der Fontänen. Registration will open shortly. From 4 to 10 February 2027, Plopsa Indoor celebrates carnival with a kids' disco. If your child's birthday falls in winter, you can book the "Majas Geburtstagszauber" package, and a visit from Maya is included in the price.

## What's different from last winter

In winter 2025/26 the same part of the park was called Majaland Pfalz. It was open from 7 November to 22 March then, while this season ends on 14 March, and the cheapest ticket was €20 against €16 now. The park had already dropped the name Holiday Park at the end of June 2025. The Maya show and the first winter run of The Smurfs' Adventure were that season's new additions.

## Getting there and parking

The park is at Holiday-Park-Straße 1–5, 67454 Haßloch, close to the A65 and the B9. Parking costs €10 online. If you're only dropping someone off or picking them up, you can stay in the car park for 45 minutes free of charge. The barrier opens by number plate recognition. The shuttle bus from Haßloch station to the park only ran until 1 November this year. For the winter, Plopsa only describes getting there by car.
