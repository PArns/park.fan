## New

- An open hour on the weather chart is drawn four times as wide as a closed one, so the hours of a
  visit take 74 % of the chart instead of 42 % (19 August). The scale changes exactly at the dashed
  edges of the opening-hours band, which show a door icon with the opening and closing time.
- The header menu opens as one wide band: countries arrive with their flags, parks with their
  photos (21 August).
- A park page lists its address, phone number, website, ticket shop, Wikipedia article, social
  accounts, opening year and area, from eleven fields we fill in ourselves, because none of our
  three data feeds has them (20 August). Where a park has none of them, the section is left out.
- The park header shows two crowd figures, one above the other: today's level, measured the same
  way as the forecast beside it, and the crowd right now (17 August). At Phantasialand a live
  "normal" had stood next to a forecast of "very high", because the forecast was for the whole day
  and the live figure for that moment.
- An agent that knows only the hostname finds `/llms.txt`, an API catalogue under
  `/.well-known/api-catalog`, three published skills with their SHA-256 digests, and an MCP server
  at `/api/mcp` with three read-only tools (25 August). A park that publishes no wait times is
  reported as such, not as a park averaging zero minutes.

## Improved

- The guide to how park.fan works was rewritten around one question, what a wait time means, in
  nine chapters (25 August). Each card on it is the one a park page uses, so you will know it when
  you open that page in the park.
- Next to "Closed", a ride out of season shows that it runs only in winter or only in summer
  (21 August). Before, the season was a greyed-out word with nothing to tie it to the closure.

## Fixed

- Pages jumped in seven places while they loaded, and our measuring script could not see two of
  them from the top of the page (19 August).
- The live column in the blog's wait-time tables disappeared at opening time, and four of those
  tables still pointed at a park that had been renamed (16 August).
- Park names in the quiet-day card were cut off, and the quietest weekday went missing when two
  days tied (24 August).
- Ride pages could show a wait from most of an hour earlier. On 25 August the page for Hagrid's
  Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure said 35 minutes while the park page said 60, because a
  cache in front of our server kept serving an old copy.
