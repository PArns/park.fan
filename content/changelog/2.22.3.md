  A ride that starts at set times instead of running all day now shows its next show and how long
  until it, where other rides show a wait. The first is Phantasialand's 4D cinema Die 3
  Mausketiere, every hour from 13:00 to 17:00 during Wintertraum. On the four trip region hubs the
  chapter on when to travel lets you choose the parks and the length of the trip, and each hub ends
  with one place to start planning instead of two.
mode: published

## New

- A ride that starts at set times shows "Next show" with the start time and how long until it,
  and every show of the day below, the past ones struck through. Its badge reads "Set showtimes"
  instead of "Closed", which the park's feed reports between two shows. The ride's page shows the
  same under "Wait time right now".
- In chapter 04 of the trip hubs you can switch the parks (in Orlando all seven, Disney's four or
  Universal's three) and the length of the trip, and planning one of the trips takes your choice of
  parks along.

## Improved

- The trip length follows the parks: in Orlando 10 days for all seven parks, 6 for Disney World
  and 5 for Universal, 6 days in Paris and Southern California and 7 in Japan. Before, every hub
  and best-time page counted 10 days.
- A trip card in chapter 04 says why it ranks ahead of one with a shorter wait, such as the
  hurricane season at the other, and marks heat and storm season. A strip above the cards shows the
  three trips across the months, and each card shows its peak wait and its length in days.
- Each hub ends with one block to start planning instead of two, and the heading of chapter 01
  names the region.
- The resort cards in chapter 03 list their parks as rows with an arrow, 44 px tall on phones.

## Fixed

- A resort card without a photo of its own showed the region's Parky picture, in Orlando twice side
  by side. It now opens with a tinted area and the resort's name.
