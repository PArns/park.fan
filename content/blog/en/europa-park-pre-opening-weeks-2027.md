[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) will open before the start of its season for the
first time in 2027: the Pre-Opening Weeks run from 7 to 19 March. The summer
season begins on 20 March. The park announced it on 17 September 2026.

The model is the Pre-Opening Day 2026, a single Sunday before the season. By the
park's account it was a complete success, so now there are two weeks.

## What a day costs

The park lists these prices for the online day ticket, adults, including VAT:

| Day                 | Without an overnight stay | With a stay in the resort |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Monday to Friday    | €59.50                    | €59.50                    |
| Saturday and Sunday | €70.00                    | €59.50                    |

If you sleep in the resort, you pay the weekday price at the weekend too. The
page doesn't list prices for children or seniors. For normal days the park's
calendar gives €76 (price category 1) and €67 (price category 2) as examples
for the online day ticket, so you'd save €7.50 to €16.50 a day during these two
weeks. ResortPass annual passes are valid too; Silver and Gold holders book
through the ResortPass portal.

## What's open and what isn't

The park says itself that not every attraction and show will run, because it's
still getting ready for the season. Which ones is stated nowhere. The app shows
the available attractions and show times for each day, and the page gives no
separate opening hours for the two weeks. On a few days the park is shut
entirely for third-party events; the closing days are in the calendar on the
Pre-Opening page.

## With an overnight stay

From 6 March, according to the hotel page, all 4-star superior hotels and Silver
Lake City are open, and so is Rulantica. Restaurants and bars open seasonally.
Two offers are already listed: a promotional rate with 20% off on selected
dates, each with admission to Europa-Park or Rulantica and breakfast, and the
"Alles erleben" package with one day at Europa-Park, one day at Rulantica and
one night including breakfast from €173.50 per person in a five-bed room.

7 March is a Sunday and 19 March a Friday. That makes three of the 13 days cost
€70 (7, 13 and 14 March) and the other ten €59.50, before the closing days are
taken off. There's only one full weekend in the two weeks.

## Who it suits

The park promotes shorter queues. We haven't measured anything in March, so we
can't say how busy it will be.

It should pay off if you arrive with no particular ride in mind and just want a
cheap day in the park, on a weekday or with an overnight stay. If you're after
one specific ride or show in March, check the app first or wait until 20 March.
Tickets for the summer season go on sale on 10 November 2026. What waits look
like in high season is in the
[Europa-Park guide](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips).
