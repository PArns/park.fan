On 5 November, Universal Orlando announced a new indoor coaster for [Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe). It's going into Dark Universe, the land of the classic Universal Monsters, right beside [Monsters Unchained: The Frankenstein Experiment](ref:universal-epic-universe/monsters-unchained-the-frankenstein-experiment). Construction is already under way, but Universal hasn't announced when it opens.

## What we know about the ride

Universal calls it an indoor thrill coaster with a new story built around the Universal Monsters. It doesn't have a name yet, or at least not one Universal is sharing. Universal says more details will follow later. On the opening, the announcement says "in the years ahead", a phrase that rules out next Tuesday and not much else. No year is given.

## What's in Dark Universe today

Dark Universe is the village of Darkmoor, one of five worlds in the park, which opened on 22 May 2025. The other four are Celestial Park, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic, Super Nintendo World and How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk.

Monsters Unchained takes you down into the catacombs of Frankenstein Manor. Dr. Victoria Frankenstein is showing off an experiment when Dracula leads the monsters in a revolt, the Wolf Man and the Mummy among them. Universal calls it the most terrifying attraction Universal Orlando has built so far. The Phantom of the Opera's organ supplies the soundtrack, and at twelve points flames shoot up about a metre in time with the music, which is more pyrotechnics than most organ recitals get. Riders need to be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall.

[Curse of the Werewolf](ref:universal-epic-universe/curse-of-the-werewolf) is a family coaster with spinning cars, where riders try to get away from the werewolves before they turn into one. The minimum height is 40 inches (102 cm), and children between 102 and 122 cm ride only with a companion. For younger children, Darkmoor also has the Darkmoor Monster Makeup Experience with make-up and wash-off tattoos, the restaurant Das Stakehaus, and meet-and-greets with the Bride of Frankenstein, the Invisible Man and Ygor.

Whether children under 122 cm will be allowed on the new coaster won't be clear until Universal gives its minimum height.

## The neighbour has awards on the shelf

Monsters Unchained won Amusement Today's 2025 Golden Ticket Award for best dark ride. In November 2025, Universal also announced four Thea Awards for Epic Universe: one for the park itself, one for Harry Potter and the Battle at the Ministry, one for the Isle of Berk, and one for Monsters Unchained in the "Outstanding Achievement – Attraction" category.

Universal Express is valid on Monsters Unchained. What tickets and Express cost at Epic Universe, which pass works where, and every minimum height are in the [Universal Orlando guide](/blog/universal-orlando-wait-times-tips).

## What else Universal Orlando is opening

The same announcement lists the resort's projects for the coming year. Later this year, Universal Studios Florida gets a reworked Universal Horror Make-Up Show with Blumhouse. In 2027, the same park adds the coaster Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift, and Islands of Adventure gets a new table-service restaurant in Jurassic Park. Luke Combs' Category 10, a music and entertainment venue, is due to open at CityWalk in late 2027. The Dark Universe coaster is the only project on that list without a year.
