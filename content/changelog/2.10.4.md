## New

- Click a day in the crowd calendar and it opens with that day's hours, the forecast, the waits of
  the headliners, an hourly chart, the weather and the holidays around it (20 July). School
  holidays in neighbouring regions are marked.
- "Forecast today" in the park header opens the same view for today, and the arrow keys step
  through the days (21 July).
- Fancast, a page with the prediction model's live accuracy (21 July), and a hub for the best
  time to visit, in six languages (22 July).
- A card lists the day's skip-the-queue prices (23 July).

## Improved

- A park's crowd level measures the whole park, where it used to follow the busiest ride.
  Phantasialand read "high" while Taron and F.L.Y. sat at 20 minutes against typical waits of 45
  and 40; on a day like that it now reads "low" (27 July).
