## New

- A new logo and icon set, with a large version of the logo on the homepage (20 to 23 February).
- The imprint credits the photographers of the park photos.

## Improved

- The header is transparent over the homepage photo and fades in as you scroll, and on park and
  ride pages the photo reaches up behind it.
- Live data reloads every five minutes for open parks and every hour for closed ones, and again
  when you come back to the tab or your connection returns (15 February).
