[Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) is in Jackson Township, New Jersey, on Interstate 195, between New York City and Philadelphia. Six Flags counts 14 roller coasters there. The Hurricane Harbor water park and the Wild Safari share the same grounds. The theme park itself takes up 160 acres, about 65 hectares, and the Wild Safari 350, just over 140 hectares, so the giraffes, rhinos and lions have more room than any coaster.

Kingda Ka ran here from 2005 until November 2024, and for its entire life it was the tallest roller coaster in the world. Since it was torn down, [Nitro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/nitro?bare) has been the tallest coaster in the park, and in October Six Flags closed it after the fatal accident on Goliath in Georgia. Bakunawa is due in 2027, and at 116 metres it'll take over from Nitro.

## The rides by area

### The Pine Barrens

Nitro is a hyper coaster by Bolliger & Mabillard (B&M), and the first train went out on 7 April 2001. It climbs 70 metres and drops 66, reaching 129 km/h at the bottom. The track is 1,644 metres long without a single inversion, and a ride takes 2:20 minutes. The park says you can see Philadelphia from the first hill, assuming your eyes are open at that moment.

After the [fatal accident on Goliath](/news/goliath-six-flags-over-georgia-fatal-incident) at Six Flags Over Georgia, Six Flags closed nine coasters at six parks in October, Nitro among them. Six Flags lists them as temporarily closed. On 23 October chief executive John Reilly announced [seat belts](/news/six-flags-seat-belts-goliath) for Goliath and for rides with a similar lap bar. There's no reopening date for Nitro yet.

The [Jersey Devil Coaster](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/jersey-devil-coaster?bare) twists through the Pine Barrens on a single rail, Rocky Mountain Construction's (RMC) Raptor track, and went into service on 13 June 2021. Its 914 metres of track hold one drop at 87 degrees and three inversions, and from a height of 40 metres it reaches 93 km/h.

If you want to get wet in the Pine Barrens, there's [Roaring Rapids](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/roaring-rapids?bare), an Intamin river rapids ride from 1981.

### Plaza Del Carnaval

From 55 metres up, the train on [El Toro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/el-toro?bare) plunges 54 metres at an angle of 76 degrees, and Six Flags says no wooden coaster in the US drops more steeply. It has run since 12 June 2006 on Intamin's prefabricated wooden track, built by Cordes Holzbau to a design by the engineering firm Stengel. The bull charges over 1,341 metres of track at 113 km/h and is back in its pen after 1:42 minutes. In USA Today's 10 Best Awards, El Toro came first in 2022. Before the 2026 season the park relaid long stretches of the track.

### Frontier Adventures

[Medusa](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/medusa?bare) opened on 2 April 1999 as B&M's first floorless coaster. From 2009 to 2021 it went by Bizarro, then it got its old name back. The train climbs 43 metres, drops 40 and goes upside down seven times. At up to 98 km/h it covers 1,215 metres in 2:20 minutes.

The [Runaway Train](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/runaway-mine-train?bare) opened on 4 July 1974, three days after the park, which makes the Arrow Dynamics mine train the oldest coaster in Jackson. Its highest point is 18 metres, its longest drop 12 and its track 740 metres, and along the way it gets up to 61 km/h.

The [Saw Mill Log Flume](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/saw-mill-log-flume?bare) by Arrow Development dates from the same summer, and you ride it downhill in a hollowed-out log. Frontier Adventures is also where the [Safari Off-Road Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/safari-off-road-adventure?bare) starts, the guided tour through the Wild Safari. You board it near Runaway Mine Train and Medusa.

### Movietown and Metropolis

[BATMAN The Ride](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/batman-the-ride?bare) has run since 1 May 1993, a B&M inverted coaster that leaves the riders' feet dangling below the track. From a height of 32 metres it goes upside down five times, at up to 80 km/h and 4 G, and after 821 metres and a minute and a half it's all over.

[THE FLASH: Vertical Velocity](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/the-flash-vertical-velocity?bare) is a Vekoma Super Boomerang that fires forwards and then backwards through the same two inversions. It opened on 29 March 2025. The track is 436 metres long, and the train hits up to 95 km/h. You can ride alone from 142 cm, or with a companion from 122.

[THE DARK KNIGHT Coaster](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/the-dark-knight-coaster?bare) is a Mack Rides wild mouse that has run indoors since 15 May 2008. In two minutes the car covers 370 metres and six 180-degree turns, with its highest point at 14 metres.

[WONDER WOMAN Lasso of Truth](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/wonder-woman-lasso-of-truth?bare) in Movietown takes riders from 132 cm. Zamperla put it up in 2019. Over in Metropolis, Sally Corp's [JUSTICE LEAGUE: Battle for Metropolis](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/justice-league-battle-for-metropolis?bare) has been running since 2017.

### Lakefront

[THE JOKER](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/the-joker?bare), an S&S Worldwide 4D Free Spin, came to Lakefront on 28 May 2016. On a track only 311 metres long, the car shoots up twelve storeys, which is 37 metres, and reaches 61 km/h. You can't be taller than 196 cm here, for once a limit that catches the tall people rather than the kids.

The family coaster [HARLEY QUINN Crazy Train](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/harley-quinn-crazy-train?bare), Blackbeard's Lost Treasure Train until 2015, is a Zierer Tivoli from 1999. At 8 metres high and 36 km/h over 360 metres, it's the coaster kids can ride from 104 cm with an adult.

### Adventure Alley

The family coaster [Skull Mountain](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/skull-mountain?bare), an Intamin from 3 June 1996, runs in the dark inside a building. The track is 420 metres long, and from its highest point at 12 metres the train drops 11 and reaches 53 km/h. As on the Joker, the cut-off is 196 cm.

The 74-metre [SkyScreamer](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/skyscreamer?bare) spins riders round at up to 64 km/h. The [Giant Wheel](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/giant-wheel?bare) is in Adventure Alley as well, and here even the Ferris wheel is on the Fast Lane list.

### Boardwalk and Shoreline Pier

On [SUPERMAN: Ultimate Flight](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/superman-ultimate-flight?bare) you lie flat under the track, face to the ground. That's been the view since 17 April 2003, when the B&M flying coaster opened. It's 32 metres tall and drops 30. The train needs 2:06 minutes for its 841 metres with two inversions, and its top speed along the way is 82 km/h.

```glossary-widget slug=flying-coaster

```

The [Slingshot](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/slingshot?bare) on the Boardwalk costs extra.

Six Flags announced the rebuilt Shoreline Pier on 26 March 2026. After a preview opening in June, it officially opened on 1 July 2026. It has five new rides: Wave Swinger, Super Round Up, Flying Scooters, Hypno Twister and [Barrels O' Fun](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/barrels-o-fun?bare). Four of them come from Six Flags America, which Six Flags said in May 2025 would close after the 2025 season.

Barrels O' Fun was the last of the five and has been running since 8 July 2026. It's a Reverchon spinning coaster with cars for four. It used to stand at Six Flags Great America and then at Six Flags America, both times as Ragin' Cajun. It's only 13 metres tall, tops out at 47 km/h and has exactly as much track as Skull Mountain, 420 metres, yet at 150 cm it asks for the highest minimum height of any coaster in the park.

### Dream Street and the kids' areas

Dream Street has the Patrina Williams Carousel, which Frederick Savage built in 1881, so it's almost a hundred years older than the park. The Enchanted Teacups are here too, and so is Houdini's Great Escape, where Six Flags asks for 122 cm, the same as for El Toro.

In the first of the two kids' areas, the Zamperla family coaster [Lil' Devil Coaster](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/lil-devil-coaster?bare), formerly Road Runner Railway, is 80 metres long, climbs only 4 metres and ambles along at 24 km/h. Barnstormer, Sky Zooma, Storm Chaser and the Tree Top Hopper run there as well. The second one, Bugs Bunny National Park, has five kids' rides, two of them from 91 cm.

## Height requirements

Six Flags gives the limits in inches, with the metric conversion in brackets. Kids get measured at the Ride Information Center by the entrance, and they get a wristband there.

- 59 inches (150 cm): Barrels O' Fun, with a companion from 48 inches (122 cm)
- 56 inches (142 cm): THE FLASH, with a companion from 122 cm
- 54 inches (137 cm): Nitro, SUPERMAN, BATMAN, Medusa
- 52 inches (132 cm): WONDER WOMAN
- 48 inches (122 cm): El Toro, Jersey Devil Coaster, THE JOKER (196 cm at most), Houdini's Great Escape, Wave Swinger, Bakunawa from 2027
- 48 inches (122 cm), lower with a companion: THE DARK KNIGHT (from 107 cm), JUSTICE LEAGUE (from 107 cm), Skull Mountain (from 112 cm, 196 cm at most), HARLEY QUINN (from 104 cm)
- 46 inches (117 cm): Saw Mill Log Flume
- 44 inches (112 cm): Runaway Train, SkyScreamer, Slingshot, Flying Scooters
- 42 inches (107 cm): Lil' Devil Coaster, Giant Wheel, Carousel, Enchanted Teacups, Hypno Twister, Super Round Up, Barnstormer, Sky Zooma, Storm Chaser, Porky Pig's Camp Wagons, Daffy Duck Hot Air Balloons, Wile E. Coyote Canyon Blaster
- 36 inches (91 cm): Tree Top Hopper, Bugs Bunny Camp Carousel, Ranger Pilots

With Rider Swap, two adults can ride one after the other while one stays with the child, without queuing twice.

### Fright Fest

Fright Fest runs on selected evenings from 18 September to 1 November. Six Flags doesn't recommend it for children under 13. In 2026 four Thursdays were added, the 8th, 15th, 22nd and 29th of October. On 30 October the park is open from 5 p.m. to midnight.

Halloween itself, 31 October, starts at 10:30 a.m. with Oktoberfest and the family Boo Fest, which lasts until 5 p.m. From 6 p.m. until midnight it's Fright Fest. On 1 November the park is open from 10:30 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Fright Fest starts again at 6 p.m. On Fright Fest evenings Hurricane Harbor and the Wild Safari are closed.

New this year are the mazes "JASON: Blood Reign" and "Final Destination: Death's Playground". The Conjuring Universe, Army of the Dead, Asylum, Twisted Holidays, Deadwood Cabin, Big Top Terror and The Witch's Reflection are running too. On top of the mazes there are two shows, "Dr. Fright's Party" in Adventure Alley and the "Freak Show" on Dream Street. The Fright Fest Bundle with admission and mazes starts at $80, the All Season Haunted Attractions Pass at $75.

### November and Holiday in the Park

After Fright Fest the park opens without an event on 5 to 8 and 14 and 15 November, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. each day. [Holiday in the Park](/news/six-flags-great-adventure-holiday-in-the-park-2026) starts on 20 November with a Preview Night for pass holders from 4 to 8 p.m. For everyone else it runs on selected days from 21 November to 3 January 2027, from 1 to 8 p.m. Thanksgiving on 26 November and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day stay closed. New Year's Eve is open until 8 p.m., early enough to wait for midnight at home.

Jersey Devil Coaster, El Toro, Runaway Train, Tango and Bugs Bunny National Park don't run during the event. Six Flags decides each holiday evening, by weather and temperature, which of the other rides run.

## Fast Lane

Six Flags sells Fast Lane for the whole day in two tiers. **Fast Lane Reserve** holds your place in the queue virtually and gives you a return time. **Fast Lane Ultimate** books you in straight away, and Six Flags promises 90 percent less waiting with it. Day Fast Lane starts at $45. For 2027 there's the All Season Fast Lane at $599 instead of $899, Bakunawa included.

Then there's Single-Use Fast Lane, good for exactly one ride on one particular attraction. You can only buy it inside the park, through a QR code, and Six Flags offers it only on selected rides and at selected times.

Fast Lane is valid on 22 rides:

- BATMAN, Buccaneer, Giant Wheel, HARLEY QUINN, Houdini's Great Escape, JUSTICE LEAGUE, Nitro, Skull Mountain, SkyScreamer, SUPERMAN, THE DARK KNIGHT, THE FLASH, THE JOKER, WONDER WOMAN
- one ride only: Barrels O' Fun, Hypno Twister
- in season only: Jersey Devil Coaster, Medusa, Roaring Rapids, Runaway Train, Saw Mill Log Flume
- with Ultimate only: El Toro

You can only hold one reservation at a time, and you need a smartphone with a browser. Fast Lane isn't transferable and only works on the day you bought it for. Admission is paid separately, and pass holders don't get a discount. For the Fright Fest mazes, Six Flags sells the Haunted Attractions Pass and the Haunted Attractions Express instead of Fast Lane. Hurricane Harbor has no Fast Lane at all.

I'd buy Reserve on a day the calendar shows a high crowd level, and Ultimate only if El Toro is the reason you're making the trip.

## A day in the park

Say you're at the gate for opening on a summer day, Nitro is running again, and you don't have Fast Lane. Here's the order I'd ride in:

- At opening, SUPERMAN: Ultimate Flight on the Boardwalk.
- Then El Toro on the Plaza Del Carnaval.
- In Movietown, THE FLASH and WONDER WOMAN, then BATMAN and THE DARK KNIGHT.
- In the Pine Barrens, the Jersey Devil Coaster and Nitro.
- Around midday, over to Frontier Adventures for Medusa, the Saw Mill Log Flume and the Safari Off-Road Adventure. Half an hour sitting down among lions and rhinos isn't a bad break in the midday heat.
- In the afternoon, Lakefront with THE JOKER and HARLEY QUINN, plus Skull Mountain in Adventure Alley, which runs indoors.
- Last, the Shoreline Pier, on summer evenings with Boardwalk Nights.

On El Toro, THE JOKER, the Jersey Devil Coaster and THE FLASH, no loose items are allowed in the queue, so no bags and no backpacks. Fast Lane and tickets run on your phone, and so that this works in the queue too, the park installed more than 600 new Wi-Fi access points before the 2026 season. Payment is cashless only, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. If you've brought cash, you can swap it for a card at kiosks. Guest Relations answers questions by live chat every day from 8 a.m. to 2 a.m. Eastern Time.

## Tickets

A day ticket costs from $45 online, and the park also sells day tickets at the gate. Six Flags adds a fee of up to $9.99 plus tax to every purchase. Children aged two and under get into the park and Hurricane Harbor free. Six Flags doesn't refund tickets and doesn't hand out rain checks for another day.

The 2-Park Combo for $79 covers one visit to Dorney Park until 7 November and one to Great Adventure until 29 November, though not on a Saturday there.

The **2027 Gold Pass** costs $99 instead of $115, or $89 as a renewal. It's valid for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027, and the price goes up on 2 November. It includes:

- admission to every Six Flags park on the East Coast, Dorney Park included, and their water parks
- Fright Fest and Holiday in the Park
- 10 percent off food and merchandise
- a discount for friends you bring along

As a **Gold Membership** you pay a $20 joining fee and $8 a month. It renews itself after twelve months.

The **Prestige Pass** currently costs $249, or $239 as a renewal. As a membership it's a $20 joining fee and $20 a month. The Prestige Pass gets you:

- admission to every Six Flags park
- fountain drinks on every visit
- two friend tickets a year
- 15 percent off
- a VIP entrance
- one Single-Use Fast Lane per visit

Renew or sign up for a membership by 1 November and you get a Bring-A-Friend ticket on top. If you drive, the Gold Pass beats a day ticket plus parking from the second visit. For a single day a year, I take the online ticket.

The park rents out wheelchairs for $25 and electric scooters (ECVs) for $55, to adults aged 18 and over.

## Parking

General parking costs from $45 a day, the same as the cheapest online ticket. Priority Parking starts at $65. The Wild Safari has a separate lot for $20. With the Gold Pass or a membership, general parking is free. The Prestige Pass includes Preferred Parking while spaces last.

## Getting there

The address for your satnav is 1 Six Flags Blvd, Jackson Township, NJ 08527, and it's the same for the park, Hurricane Harbor and the Safari Base Camp. The park is in Ocean County on Interstate 195, along Monmouth Road (County Route 537). The I-195 links Central Jersey with the New Jersey Turnpike (I-95) and the Garden State Parkway.

Without a car, the bus company OurBus runs once a day at 9:30 a.m. from the George Washington Bridge Bus Station (Level 3, Slip 4) to Bus Lot 19 at the park. The bus takes about an hour and a half for roughly 109 kilometres, and the trip costs from $37.39.

## Food

The park has 55 restaurants and snack stands. In 2026 Six Flags turned Best of the West into "Best of the West: Smokehouse" and refurbished the Main Street Pub.

For a single day the park sells these meal plans:

- Single Meal Deal for $25
- All Day Dining for $35, with one meal every 90 minutes
- Premium All Day Dining for $45
- drink plans from $20

If you eat two meals, All Day Dining already comes out ahead of two single deals.

For the whole season there are these plans, all three with one meal every two hours:

- All Season Dining Access with 20 meals for $109
- Dining Plus with 30 meals for $139
- Dining Unlimited for $399, valid at every Six Flags park

Season drink plans start at $39.

## Where to stay

The **Savannah Sunset Resort & Spa** is inside the Wild Safari, on one of the highest points in Jackson, according to Six Flags. It has 20 glamping suites and opened in mid-2024. Stays are booked for two nights minimum. Guests have to be at least five, so younger kids only get to see the giraffes from the safari truck.

The price covers breakfast, a giraffe feeding, marshmallows in the evening and a safari tour, plus one ticket per night for the three parks. Besides the spa with two treatment rooms, there's a restaurant and a bar. You only get in with a reservation, and a shuttle runs from the car park. Pass holders get up to 10 percent off.

Off site there's the Courtyard Hamilton, about 24 kilometres from the park, and the Home2 Suites Bordentown, about 33 kilometres away.

## Wild Safari

Around 1,200 animals from six continents live on the Wild Safari's 350 acres.

One way in is the [Safari Off-Road Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/safari-off-road-adventure?bare) from inside the park, which is back in 2026 and included in park admission. A guide drives you in an open off-road truck past elephants, rhinos, giraffes, lions and tigers, and you can also see JJ, the brown bear cub. The tour takes about 30 minutes.

The other way starts outside, at the Safari Base Camp, with a separate ticket from $25. Juniors between 91 and 107 cm and seniors from 65 pay from $15, children under 91 cm nothing. The ticket isn't valid for the park or Hurricane Harbor, but the Season Pass does include the safari. From Base Camp the tour takes about 45 minutes. Base Camp has animal presentations. The Tortoise Encounter and the Lorikeet Feeding with the parrots are closed for this season.

In 2026 the Wild Safari is still open on 31 October from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., and on 1 November and from 5 to 8 November from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. each day.

## Hurricane Harbor New Jersey

Hurricane Harbor opened in 2000 as a $40 million expansion. The water park is open from mid-May to early September. It has its own entrance and needs its own admission, and a ticket at the gate costs $60.

Its 22 acres, just under 9 hectares, hold Jurahnimo Falls with a 23-metre tower, the Blue Lagoon wave pool and the lazy river Taak It Eez Ee Creek, a name you have to read out loud once. For kids there are Discovery Bay and Splash Island. Hurricane Harbor doesn't rent out strollers or wheelchairs.

## What's new and what's gone

### Bakunawa 2027

Bakunawa is due to open in 2027 on a day Six Flags hasn't named yet, as the main ride of the rebuilt Boardwalk. Mack Rides is building it as an Xtreme Spinning Coaster. It'll stand 116 metres (382 feet) tall and reach up to 161 km/h. The track is 964 metres long, and a ride lasts 2:17 minutes.

The first launch takes the train from a standstill to 97 km/h, the second, upside down, to 129 km/h and the third to 161 km/h. The steepest bank is 180 degrees. Two trains with five cars each are planned, and every car has four back-to-back seats that spin freely. The ride takes its name from a sea serpent in Philippine mythology.

Six Flags and Mack claim six world records for Bakunawa before a single rider has boarded:

- the fastest spinning coaster
- the tallest spinning coaster
- the fastest inversion, at 129 km/h
- the longest stall inversion
- the first floorless spinning coaster
- the first launch with the train hanging upside down

```glossary-widget slug=xtreme-spinning-coaster

```

On 14 November 2024, the same day Six Flags confirmed the end of Kingda Ka, it announced a new launch coaster with several world records for 2026. On 28 July 2025 Six Flags pushed the opening beyond 2026. The first piece of track arrived on 18 February 2026, construction began in March, and the top piece was in place by early July. Exactly one year after the delay, on 28 July 2026, Six Flags revealed the name Bakunawa. Until then the construction site had gone by "Project Purple".

In October 2026 nine of the ten cars were being assembled, for one green and one purple train, at roughly a week's work per car. Bakunawa replaces Green Lantern, the Parachute Training Center drop tower and Twister.

### What's gone

Kingda Ka, an Intamin accelerator coaster, opened on 21 May 2005. The tower was 139 metres tall, and the train went from zero to 206 km/h in 3.5 seconds. When it opened, Kingda Ka was also the fastest coaster in the world, until Formula Rossa took that record in November 2010.

Kingda Ka ran for the last time on 10 November 2024, and the tower was blown up on 28 February 2025. Zumanjaro: Drop of Doom, the Intamin drop tower that had hung off Kingda Ka's tower since 4 July 2014, went with it.

Green Lantern also had its last day of operation on 10 November 2024. The B&M stand-up coaster had stood at Kentucky Kingdom as Chang and came to Jackson in 2011. It was torn down in November and December 2024. Also in 2024, the Dare Devil Dive Skycoaster, which had run since 1997, closed, and so did Twister, a Huss Top Spin from 1999.

Besides Nitro, Six Flags currently lists the Runaway Train and the Big Wheel as closed or under refurbishment.

## History

Warner LeRoy came up with the concept for Great Adventure in 1972. The park opened for 4,500 invited guests on 30 June 1974 and for everyone on 1 July. LeRoy had built it for about $10 million, and back then it was simply called Great Adventure.

Six Flags bought the park in autumn 1977. On 11 May 1984 the Haunted Castle, a haunted house in the park, burned down, and eight teenagers died.

From 1990 to 1997 Time Warner was a major shareholder in Six Flags. In 1993 LeRoy sold his stake to Time Warner. Premier Parks bought Six Flags in 1998.

Of the older coasters, Viper went first (last ridden in 2004, taken down in 2005), then Batman & Robin: The Chiller in 2007 and the Great American Scream Machine in 2010. Rolling Thunder, which had run since 1979, was the last to close, in 2013.

In 2012 the Wild Safari and the park were joined into one site. Cedar Fair and Six Flags completed their merger on 1 July 2024. The park president of Great Adventure is Mike Fehnel.

Six Flags doesn't publish attendance figures. The TEA and AECOM Theme Index lists Six Flags Great Adventure for 2019 with an estimated 3,451,000 visitors. That was 1.5 percent more than the year before and 18th place among the parks in North America.

The status of every ride is live on the [park page](ref:six-flags-great-adventure). Nitro has been temporarily closed since mid-October 2026, with no reopening date. During Holiday in the Park, from 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, Jersey Devil Coaster, El Toro, Runaway Train, Tango and the rides in Bugs Bunny National Park don't run either.

### How busy is Six Flags Great Adventure today?

After Fright Fest ends on 1 November, the park is open on only six more days before Holiday in the Park. Today's crowd level, from very low to extreme, is on the [park page](ref:six-flags-great-adventure).

### Is there a crowd calendar for Six Flags Great Adventure?

Yes, the [wait-time calendar](ref:six-flags-great-adventure?calendar) gives the expected crowd level for every day of the season. The last days of 2026 are the holiday dates up to 3 January 2027. Six Flags hasn't listed any operating days for the 2027 season yet.

### How much is a ticket for Six Flags Great Adventure?

Online, a day ticket starts at $45, plus a fee of up to $9.99 and tax. Children aged two and under go free. The 2027 Gold Pass costs $99 until 1 November and is already valid for the rest of 2026, general parking included. The Wild Safari from Base Camp costs from $25 extra, Hurricane Harbor $60 at the gate.

### How tall do you have to be for the roller coasters?

Nitro, SUPERMAN, BATMAN and Medusa require 54 inches (137 cm), El Toro and the Jersey Devil Coaster 48 inches (122 cm). THE FLASH takes riders from 142 cm, Barrels O' Fun from 150 cm. For kids there's the Lil' Devil Coaster from 107 cm, and rides from 91 cm in the two kids' areas.

### How much is Fast Lane at Six Flags Great Adventure, and is it worth it?

Day Fast Lane starts at $45 and comes as Reserve, with a return time, or as Ultimate, which books you in straight away. Only Ultimate works on El Toro. The 2027 All Season Fast Lane costs $599. If you arrive at opening, you'll ride Superman, THE FLASH and WONDER WOMAN with short queues even without it.

### When is Six Flags Great Adventure open?

The season usually runs from March to December. In 2026, after Fright Fest, the park is still open on 5 to 8 and 14 to 15 November from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., and on the holiday days from 21 November to 3 January 2027 from 1 to 8 p.m., but not on Thanksgiving or on 24 and 25 December. Six Flags hasn't published the hours for the 2027 season yet.

### When does Bakunawa open?

In 2027, and Six Flags hasn't named a day yet. The Mack Rides spinning coaster will be 116 metres tall, reach 161 km/h and take riders from 122 cm. It's included in the 2027 All Season Fast Lane.
