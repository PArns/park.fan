[Shanghai Disneyland](ref:shanghai-disneyland) runs its Disney Winter Frostival from 19 November 2026 to 20 February 2027. The new addition this winter is a sing-along show in which Olaf and Anna perform among the audience in front of Enchanted Storybook Castle.

## The new show at the castle

It's called "Star Squad: Join Olaf for a Cozy Celebration", with the Shanghai Disneyland Band playing live. Olaf and Anna set out to gather winter magic, which for a snowman is roughly as demanding as a fish going looking for water. The Star Squad gets everyone singing and dancing, and the crowd helps light up "magic stars". The interactive part is called "With You, It's Magic+", after the motto of the resort's tenth birthday.

## What's back from earlier winters

At Mickey's Snow Magic, Mickey Avenue turns into a snow globe again, with you standing inside it and flakes swirling above the street. In the Disney Winter Magic Cavalcade, Disney characters walk alongside floats dressed as winter scenes. Once it's dark, "A Celebration of Disney Winter Frostival" projects onto the castle, and the light and sound carry into the Gardens of Imagination. Rain, strong wind, poor air quality, thick fog or thunderstorms can lead the resort to delay or cancel the shows.

For the winter photo, there are snow figures and ice sculptures of Disney characters on Mickey Avenue and in the Gardens of Imagination, and in the Zootopia land the animals from the film stand around as snow figures.

## December in the park and in Disneytown

From 1 December to 3 January, Mickey and his friends wear new ski outfits, and the matching Ski Party collection is in the shops. Santa Goofy is back on Mickey Avenue, and Duffy and his friends turn up in new Christmas outfits. You'll meet princesses, queens and princes at the Castle Encounters throughout the Frostival, sometimes in the winter costumes from the Cavalcade.

At Mickey's Magical Tree Lighting Ceremony in the Gardens of Imagination, Mickey, Minnie, Duffy and LinaBell switch on the resort's tallest Christmas tree while a live band plays. The resort hasn't given a date for it yet. Three more Christmas shows join in: Light Up the Holidays with Magic, Christmas Swing and the Mickey Avenue Carolers.

On 31 December and 1 January, Mickey and his friends change into New Year outfits. The castle show for the turn of the year is "Ignite the New Year".

If you've got kids with you, go to Disneytown on a weekend or a public holiday. That's when Santa welcomes them in his cabin at the Disneytown Christmas Market, between craft and food stalls. The Christmas Tree Trail there leads to a tree decorated for the tenth birthday this year, and the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel and the Toy Story Hotel are decorated too. When the Disneytown tree is switched on, the Magic Glee Club sings.

## Tickets, packages and food

The resort is selling two winter offers and hasn't published prices for either:

- Disney Dream Vacation – Winter Frostival Package: one night at the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel or the Toy Story Hotel, breakfast and dinner buffet for two adults, two date-specific one-day tickets, dining vouchers for the park and Disney Premier Access to the Cavalcade for two
- One-day ticket with an 88-yuan voucher: valid on a day of your choice up to 3 January 2027, with the voucher good for a combo meal at dinnertime in selected quick-service restaurants in the park

New on the menu are spring rolls filled like a wagyu cheeseburger and Mickey-shaped youdunzi, which you'll only get at breakfast. Against the cold there's hot chocolate and milk tea, plus Christmas cookies, muffins, cotton candy and tanghulu made to look like Mickey and his friends. Royal Banquet Hall and Aurora have new festive menus, and both hotels serve dinner buffets.

## Getting there by metro, and no ferry in November

Metro Line 11 ends at Disney Resort station, a few steps from the park, Disneytown and Wishing Star Park. Trains run every few minutes; the first arrives at 07:08, the last at 23:39. The Wishing Star Ferry is being overhauled from 4 to 30 November, and until then a free shuttle runs between the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel, the park and Disneytown.

On weekends and public holidays the car parks are full or close to it, so on those days the resort recommends the metro, a taxi or the bus.

## Ten years of the resort, a new hotel and a partner hotel

Shanghai Disney Resort opened on 16 June 2016 and has counted more than 100 million visits since. The Frostival is the winter chapter of that birthday celebration. Right in the middle of it, on 16 December, the [Shanghai Disney Enchanted Star Hotel](/news/shanghai-disney-enchanted-star-hotel) opens as the resort's third hotel.

For stays from 16 November there's also a package at the Meliá Shanghai Parkside, the resort's first official partner hotel, a short walk from the resort. It includes the room, a date-specific one-day ticket and an Early Park Entry Pass, and it's on sale while the allocation lasts.
