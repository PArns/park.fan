On 20 November, [Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) in Buena Park turns into Knott's Merry Farm, and it stays that way until 3 January 2027. New this winter is the ice show "Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies" in the Walter Knott Theater.

## Snoopy on skates

A vocal quartet sings Christmas standards, and a line of Follies dancers high-kicks across the stage. In between, Snoopy and his friends take to the ice with the figure skaters. The theater seats about 2,000, which is a decent crowd for a beagle. Showtimes are only in the Six Flags app, and the schedule changes from day to day.

Six Flags can't quite settle on the name: the press release of 4 November calls it "Snoopy's Christmas Follies", while the park's event guide says "Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies". Snoopy has skated at Merry Farm in earlier winters too, for instance in "Snoopy's Night Before Christmas".

## Lights, shows and Santa

Thousands of lights hang between garlands and big snowflakes. The Holiday Lighting Moment happens every evening across the park. The lights on Main Street and Schoolhouse Road in Ghost Town dance to Christmas music during Snow and Glow, while snow falls. In Calico Park, the Christmas tree is switched on during the "'Tis the Season Tree Lighting Ceremony".

The other stages around Calico and in Fiesta Village have shows of their own:

- Home for the Holidays: a musical revue with dancing and a winter set on the Calico Mine Stage
- The Gift of the Magi, 1885 and A Calico Christmas Carol: two plays after O. Henry and Charles Dickens in the Bird Cage Theatre
- Krazy Kirk and the Hillbillies at Wagon Camp, the Wandering Wranglers on the Calico Park Stage and the Fireman's Duo at the Fireman's BBQ Stage
- the Calico Carolers in the streets of Calico, and holiday bands in Fiesta Village

Ghost Town becomes the Christmas Crafts Village, with dozens of local artisans. Myles Freedman blows glass there every day, and Nathan Howard carves with a chainsaw.

Santa is in Santa's Christmas Cabin, the Wilderness Dance Hall made over for the season. It opens every day about half an hour after the park, and photos cost extra. Inside there are cookies, hot chocolate, hot toddies and mulled wine. You can get alcohol from 21 with ID. On some days, kids can decorate a cupcake in Mrs. Claus' Kitchen for a small fee and take it home.

## Park hours

The park is open every day except 25 December, Thanksgiving (26 November) included. The hours day by day:

- 20 to 28 November (Thanksgiving on the 26th): 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- 29 November: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
- 30 November to 4 December and 7 to 10 December: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- 5, 11 and 12 December: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- 6 December and 13 to 17 December: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- 18 to 23 December: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- 24 December: 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.
- 26 to 30 December: 9 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- 31 December: 9 a.m. to 1 a.m., with a New Year's Eve party
- 1 to 3 January: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Knott's hasn't listed any scheduled ride closures for the holidays.

## What the day costs

Shows, lights, the Christmas Crafts Village and most rides are included with admission. You pay extra for food, drinks and souvenirs, the photos with Santa, cupcake decorating in Mrs. Claus' Kitchen and Fast Lane. A date-specific one-day ticket starts at $65 plus tax online, and kids two and under get in free. You don't need a reservation. There are no refunds for rain.

The 2027 Season Pass costs $120, and renewing it $110. And because it already covers the rest of 2026, it's the one Christmas present to yourself you're allowed to unwrap before the 25th. On a payment plan it's $30 down and ten payments of $9. Two day tickets at the lowest price come to $130, so the pass pays for itself by your second visit. Fast Lane takes you past the regular line on participating rides and starts at $75 for one day. The park sells only a limited number each day.

The holiday menu has a Cranberry Bacon Jam Cheeseburger with crispy onions at Sutter's Grill, a Hot Chocolate Churro from the Gourmet Churro Factory, and a flatbread with cranberry, mint and goat cheese at Mrs. Murphy's. The Merry Farm souvenir cup is $13.99, and each refill $3.99.

The Knott's Hotel sits right next to the park and sells packages that include admission. For Black Friday, Six Flags is giving a third night free when you book two at participating hotels, and the Knott's Hotel is one of them.

## Getting there and parking

The park is at 8039 Beach Boulevard in Buena Park. Parking is $40, or $60 in the Grand Preferred Lot right by the park. The park doesn't take cash. The lots open about an hour before the park. Without a car, take Metrolink to Buena Park station on Lakeknoll Drive and an OCTA bus down Beach Boulevard from there. The Karmel Shuttle runs from hotels in Anaheim, where the Disneyland Resort celebrates its [holiday season](/news/disneyland-resort-holidays-2026), and from Garden Grove and Buena Park if you book it ahead. The park puts John Wayne Airport in Orange County 20 minutes away and LAX 30.

## From berry farm to Six Flags

From 1923, the Knott family sold berries, jam and pies at a roadside stand. Next to it, Walter Knott opened Ghost Town in 1941, and the whole place has been called Knott's Berry Farm since 1947. Snoopy has been the park's mascot since Camp Snoopy was built in 1983. Gary Salisbury dreamed up the first Merry Farm in 1985. In the fall, before Merry Farm, the park ran Scary Farm, which features in our roundup of [Halloween in the USA](/blog/halloween-usa-theme-parks-2026). The family sold the park to Cedar Fair in 1997, and Cedar Fair merged with Six Flags on 1 July 2024.

Six Flags is celebrating in eleven parks this winter. [Six Flags Great Adventure](/news/six-flags-great-adventure-holiday-in-the-park-2026) and Six Flags Over Georgia have redesigned Holiday in the Park, and Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia has seven new shows from 26 November. The new free admission for guests 65 and over and the Black Friday ticket, which gets you the whole spring and summer 2027 season for the price of one day, aren't available at Knott's.
