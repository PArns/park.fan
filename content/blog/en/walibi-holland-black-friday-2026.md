[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) is selling its annual passes for the 2027 season at a discount online until Monday, 30 November. The cheapest of the three, the Explore, is down from €95 to €75. Walibi calls it Black Friday, and the sale started on Friday, 20 November. Each pass and the Family & Friends Deal has its own allocation, and once that runs out the discount on it ends early.

The three passes, which Walibi calls abonnementen, are valid for the 2027 season only, not for a year from the day you buy.

## What the three passes cost and include

| Pass       | Black Friday | Regular price | Fright Nights           | Parking      | Fast Lane   | Food and shop discount |
| ---------- | ------------ | ------------- | ----------------------- | ------------ | ----------- | ---------------------- |
| Explore    | €75          | €95           | none                    | €12.50 a day | no          | 10%                    |
| Experience | €95          | €115          | two evenings            | €12.50 a day | no          | 10%                    |
| Exclusive  | €365         | €395          | all, with a reservation | included     | Gold Single | 20%                    |

All three get you into the park on every regular opening day, plus the event days Walibi Play, LEKKERGAAN and Zomerse Zaterdagen. They don't cover special events, days when the park is hired out, or sold-out days if you haven't reserved. During the Halloween Spooky Days, the Explore and the Experience only work on days without Fright Nights. The Exclusive's Fast Lane Gold Single lets you use the Fast Lane entrance once per visit on every ride that takes part, and at Fright Nights on two evenings of your choice. The Exclusive's 20% doesn't apply to alcohol or to attractions that cost extra.

Children up to two get in free; from three they need their own pass at the regular price. The pass is personal, carries your photo and can't be passed on or resold. If you lose it or leave it at home, a replacement costs a €7.50 admin fee.

Instead of paying €12.50 a day, pass holders can buy a season parking pass for €35. It works for any car, one per day, and pays for itself from the third visit. Parking at Fright Nights is free for everyone. I'd buy the Experience, since €20 on top of the Explore gets you two Fright Nights evenings. The Exclusive costs €235 more than the Experience and the parking pass together. That's worth it if you want the Fast Lane on busy days or plan to go to Fright Nights more than twice.

## The Family & Friends Deal

Buy four, five or six passes of the same kind in one order and the Explore costs €60 per person, the Experience €76. Five of you pay €300 for the Explore with the deal, the same as four Explore passes without it. You pick the deal separately in the shop. It isn't available for the Exclusive.

The Black Friday price is only available online at walibi.nl and can't be combined with another discount. You can't switch to a different pass after buying, not even if you realise at Christmas that you'd like more Fright Nights after all.

## Other parks with the pass

Walibi Belgium and Aqualibi aren't included in any of the three passes. In 2027 all three do get you discounts at other parks, though:

- 50 percent off a regular adult ticket at Walibi Belgium, Bellewaerde, Parc Astérix, Walibi Rhône-Alpes, Futuroscope, Family-Park, Belantis, Grévin and France Miniature, except on Halloween days and days when the park is hired out
- 40 percent at Movie Park Germany, the Plopsa parks, Bobbejaanland, Diergaarde Blijdorp and GaiaZOO
- 20 percent at Toverland if you book online

Walibi Belgium in Wavre is selling its own annual passes for less [during Black Week, until 29 November](/news/walibi-belgium-black-week-2026).

## When you can first use it

Walibi Holland stays closed until the season opens on Friday, 26 March 2027. Walibi says it'll announce the 2027 dates for the Halloween Spooky Days and Fright Nights later. For the discounts at Movie Park Germany, Toverland and the other partner parks, our [2027 season start overview](/blog/theme-park-season-start-2027) has each park's first day.

With a pass you can skip the busy days; the [crowds guide](/blog/walibi-holland-crowds) lists the weeks and school holidays when Walibi Holland is quiet. On a quiet day, start with the rides from the ["hard gaan" guide](/blog/walibi-holland-untamed-hard-gaan).
