In early October, 25 of the US parks on park.fan are running a Halloween event, and nearly all of them last until October 31 or November 1. SCarowinds in Charlotte is the last to close, on November 7. Halloween Horror Nights, Knott's Scary Farm and Howl-O-Scream in Tampa and Orlando each need a ticket of their own. At some parks the event comes with regular admission, and both Disney parties are already sold out for 2026.

After dark there are haunted houses, scare zones full of actors, and shows, and almost every park advises against bringing children under 13. In the daytime it's candy, pumpkins and costumes, often in the same park, just a few hours earlier. Europe is covered in our [Halloween guide](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026), and Cedar Point in Ohio has its own post on [HalloWeekends](/blog/cedar-point-halloweekends-2026).

> [!NOTE]
> The dates, houses, age guidance and prices below are as of October 5, 2026, taken from the parks' and operators' websites that day. Prices are starting prices in US dollars before tax. They depend on the night and usually go up the closer it gets.

## Evening horror events

### Universal Studios Florida: Halloween Horror Nights

Universal says [Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida) in Orlando has 49 nights between August 28 and November 1, Wednesday through Sunday plus Monday, October 12. Event gates open at 6:30 pm, and Universal hotel guests have their own entrance until 7:30 pm. On event nights the park ends its regular day at 5 pm. If you'd rather be there in the afternoon, Scream Early costs from $70 on top. You go in from 2 pm and no later than 5 pm, then stay in the park until the event starts.

There are ten houses on the program. Five of them run in the same form in Hollywood:

- Stranger Things, the fifth and final season of the Netflix series
- Sinners, based on the 2025 horror film
- Hellraiser, based on the first three films in the series
- Ozzy Osbourne: Prince of Darkness, a house about the heavy metal singer, designed for Horror Nights
- Evil Dead Burn, based on the latest Evil Dead film

Universal came up with the other five itself:

- Jack & Oddfellow: Chaos & Control, the backstory of Jack the Clown
- INVASION: Alien Abduction, in which a spaceship abducts a family along with its livestock
- H.R. Bloodengutz Presents: A Halloween Fright-Tacular!, one last Halloween special on the TV station WKNB
- MADLANDS: Caged Cannibals, a wasteland after the end of the world
- Cybergoria, where people wake from cryosleep thousands of years later

There are four scare zones, all of them new: Fortnitemares, Infernal Carnival of Nightmares, Downtown Clowntown and Sideshow of Decay. Out on the streets you'll also run into Mel's Die-In: Zombies, Club Horror and the Chainsaw Hordes. Two shows run, one about Stranger Things on the lagoon, and Nightmare Fuel: Blood Noir. Some rides stay open at night, including Revenge of the Mummy, Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, TRANSFORMERS: The Ride-3D and MEN IN BLACK Alien Attack.

In Universal's online store one night starts at $94.99, with some dates costing more. On October 5, October 14 was $95 and October 31 was $140. For several nights there's the Rush of Fear Pass, good for up to 18 nights, and the Frequent Fear Pass for up to 31. The ticket page lists them at different prices in two places, $229.99 and $274.99 on one line and $239.99 and $289.99 on another. The Express Pass, which gets you into each house once without using the regular queue, starts at $279.99, and the guided R.I.P. Tour at $529.99. In 2026 Universal no longer sells multi-night passes that include Express.

Universal doesn't recommend the event for children under 13. Costumes and costume masks aren't allowed. The one exception is Premium Scream Night on Monday, October 19, which has its own ticket. Costumes are allowed that night, but masks still aren't.

### Universal Studios Hollywood: Halloween Horror Nights

[Universal Studios Hollywood](ref:universal-studios-hollywood) has more nights in 2026 than ever before, 42 according to Universal, from September 3 to November 1. The event starts at 7 pm, and when it ends depends on the night.

Eight houses are running. Five are the same as in Orlando: Stranger Things, Sinners, Hellraiser, Ozzy Osbourne: Prince of Darkness and Evil Dead Burn. Only Hollywood has **Killer Klowns from Outer Space**, based on the film; **KILLceañera**, with music by Slash, in which Lola's quinceañera gets out of hand; and **Dead, Deader, Deadest**, set in an abandoned funeral home.

The Terror Tram is Hollywood's alone. On event nights Art the Clown takes over the tram that tours the studio backlot during the day, with music by the band Ice Nine Kills. According to the press release there's also a show, The Purge: Dangerous Waters, and five scare zones: Fortnitemares, Hackerz, El Circo de la Muerte, Blood Bog and Murder of Crowz. In the evening Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge runs until 10 pm and Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey until 11:15 pm, along with Flight of the Hippogriff, TRANSFORMERS, The Simpsons Ride and Revenge of the Mummy.

One night costs from $84, and up to $109 depending on the date. The Frequent Fear Pass for up to 35 nights starts at $179, the Ultimate Fear Pass at $299 and Universal Express at $279. If you want to be in the park from 2 pm, the day and night ticket starts at $124. Early Access for $20 lets you into the first houses before the event begins. The website gives 5 pm for that, the press release 5:30 pm. There's no minimum age, but Universal doesn't recommend the event for children under 13. Costume masks aren't allowed.

### Knott's Berry Farm: Knott's Scary Farm

[Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) in Buena Park near Los Angeles holds Scary Farm on select nights from September 17 to October 31. In October it's open Thursday through Sunday from 7 pm to 1 am, and until 2 am on Fridays and Saturdays.

There are ten mazes, two of them new. In **Inked** the murderous characters of a washed-up 1930s cartoonist come after you, and in **Unearthed** a dig has uncovered something old and evil. The other eight are Widows, Eight Fingers Nine: The Boogeyman, Chilling Chambers with scenes from more than 50 years of Scary Farm, Room 13, Cinema Slasher, Mary - The Haunting of Worth Home, The Zoo and Origins: The Curse of Calico.

Five scare zones are spread across the park, among them CarnEVIL and the Ghost Town Streets with the Sliders, who have been part of it for years. Two of the three shows are new. **Occultum** is a magic show put together by the Magic Castle in Hollywood and staged in the Bird Cage Theatre, and the other is **Voodoo: Bayou's Edge**. Also new is a lantern whose six colors you collect at stations around the park.

One night costs from $65 and the pass for every night $164, both sold online only. The mazes carry no extra charge. For Fright & Fast Lane, which lets you skip the regular queue at the mazes and some rides, the event page has "from $139" and the ticket page "from $159", both without admission. The park doesn't recommend the event for children under 13, and every guest needs their own ticket, whatever their age.

### Six Flags Magic Mountain: Fright Fest

Fright Fest at [Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain) near Los Angeles runs Friday through Sunday from September 18, adds Thursdays from October 8 and ends on Sunday, November 1. The park calendar has the event starting at 7 pm and running until 11 pm on Thursdays and Sundays, midnight on Fridays and 1 am on Saturdays. Anyone who leaves the park after 9 pm can't come back in.

Of the seven mazes, **Final Destination: Death's Playground** and **Wolves' Blood**, a rock concert in a town full of werewolves, are new. The others are The Conjuring Universe, Trick 'r Treat, Carnage, Vault: 666 Unlocked and Willoughby's: Resurrected, and there are seven scare zones and five shows on top. You need the Haunted Attractions Pass for the mazes. It costs from $25 for one night and from $69 for the season, and the bundle with park admission starts at $74.

In the daytime the same park runs Tricks and Treats for families from noon to 5 pm, on the same days as Fright Fest.

### Six Flags Great Adventure: Fright Fest

[Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) in New Jersey has nine mazes, more than any other Six Flags park in this guide except Knott's. **JASON: Blood Reign** and **Final Destination: Death's Playground** are new. The rest are The Conjuring Universe, Army of the Dead, Asylum, Twisted Holidays, Deadwood Cabin, Big Top Terror and The Witch's Reflection, plus five scare zones and five shows.

Fright Fest runs Thursday through Sunday from September 18 to November 1. The bundle with admission starts at $80, the pass for every night at $75. For kids there's Kids Boo Fest in the daytime, from September 12 to November 1.

### Kings Island and Kings Dominion: Halloween Haunt

[Kings Island](ref:kings-island) near Cincinnati holds Halloween Haunt from September 25 to October 31, on Thursdays and Sundays from 6 to 11 pm and on Fridays and Saturdays until midnight. Of the six mazes, **JASON: Blood Reign** and **Metal Massacre** are new, and there are four scare zones and three shows. The Conjuring: Beyond Fear costs extra and isn't part of the Haunted Attractions Pass. The bundle with admission starts at $64, the pass for the whole season at $49. On Thursdays and Fridays you can't leave the park and come back in, and on weekends you can only until 6 pm. For families, Tricks and Treats runs on Saturdays and Sundays from 11 am to 5 pm.

[Kings Dominion](ref:kings-dominion) near Richmond, Virginia, opens Halloween Haunt Friday through Sunday from September 18 to November 1, from 6 pm to midnight on Fridays and Saturdays and until 10 pm on Sundays. **Diablo: The Infernal Path** is the new one among its six mazes, and four of the five shows are new for 2026. Bundles start at $55, the Haunted Attractions Pass at $10.

### Carowinds: SCarowinds

[Carowinds](ref:carowinds), on the line between North and South Carolina, has the event in this guide that ends last. SCarowinds runs from September 11 to November 7, Thursday through Saturday from 6 pm to midnight and on Sundays until 11 pm. New this year are the maze **JASON: Blood Reign**, three of the five scare zones and four of the six shows. The Conjuring: Beyond Fear costs extra here too. The bundle with admission starts at $55, the Haunted Attractions Pass at $10.

### Texas: Fright Fest at Six Flags Over Texas and Fiesta Texas

[Six Flags Over Texas](ref:six-flags-over-texas) near Dallas opens Fright Fest Friday through Sunday from September 11 to November 1. Five mazes are running, among them The Strangers: No Escape and SAW: Legacy of Terror. The new scare zone, Doc Varnum's Hybrid Hell Pit, needs the Haunted Attractions Pass, while the other five scare zones come with admission. The bundle costs $84, the pass for one night from $30.

[Six Flags Fiesta Texas](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas) in San Antonio has seven mazes. The new ones are **Nosferatu** and **Cinema Slasher**, a maze modeled on the one at Knott's Scary Farm. Eight shows and five scare zones run as well. Fright Fest is open there on weekends from September 11 to November 1, and on Thursdays too from October 22. The bundle starts at $84.

### Six Flags Great America: Fright Fest

[Six Flags Great America](ref:six-flags-great-america), between Chicago and Milwaukee, has five mazes, including the new **Blair Witch: No Signal** and **Nightmares**, seven scare zones and nine shows, more shows than any other park in this guide. For the park's 50th anniversary the Necropolis scare zone is back. Fright Fest runs from September 19 to November 1, and the bundle starts at $75. Nobody is let back into the park after 8 pm, and from 6 pm the park doesn't recommend the event for children under 13.

### Howl-O-Scream: Busch Gardens and SeaWorld

All four parks run by United Parks call their event Howl-O-Scream, but they're four different nights with different rules.

At [Busch Gardens Tampa](ref:busch-gardens-tampa), Howl-O-Scream runs on select nights from September 11 to October 31, with entry from 5 pm and a 7 pm start. The event is a separate ticket, from $48.99 for one night. Of the six houses, **Anaconda: Fear Has No Escape** is brand new and three others have a new story. There are also six scare zones and two shows. The park writes that the event is meant for a mature audience and contains violence and gore.

[SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando) opens its gates at 6:30 pm, and the event starts at 7 pm. Five houses are running, including **I Know What You Did Last Summer: The Final Catch**, the first house in this park based on a film. The park also has six scare zones, five new bars, four rides in the dark and two shows. The website lists one night from $47.99, and the press release gives $45.99 when you buy two tickets or more.

At [Busch Gardens Williamsburg](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg) in Virginia and [SeaWorld San Antonio](ref:seaworld-san-antonio), Howl-O-Scream comes with regular admission. Williamsburg starts at 6 pm with five houses, two of them new, and asks families with young children to think carefully about staying past 6 pm. San Antonio marks the 25th year of Howl-O-Scream with five houses, three of them new, and eight scare zones, Friday through Sunday until November 1.

### Hersheypark: Dark Nights

At [Hersheypark](ref:hersheypark) in Pennsylvania, Dark Nights is part of Hersheypark Halloween, which runs for eight weekends from September 12 to November 1. From 6 pm five haunted houses and five scare zones open, and the new scare zone is Reese's Depot of Doom. It's all in the Halloween ticket, which costs $56.99 for a day or $39.99 from 5 pm. Kids up to 12 get a trick-or-treat trail with 14 stops.

### Kennywood: Phantom Fall Fest

[Kennywood](ref:kennywood) in Pittsburgh has seven haunted houses, including the new Ghostwood Estate: Open House, and four scare zones, all included in admission, which starts at $37.49 bought in advance. Phantom Fall Fest runs from September 12 to November 1, on Fridays from 6 pm and on weekends from noon. There's no minimum age, but after 6 pm the event is aimed at older guests, and anyone 17 or younger needs a chaperone aged 21 or over.

## Family-friendly: Halloween in the daytime

### Disney: two parties, both sold out

**Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party** at [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park) has 38 nights in 2026 between August 7 and October 31. The nights still to come are October 6, 8, 9, 13, 15, 16, 18, 22, 23, 25, 27, 29 and 31. Disney says every date is sold out. The party runs from 7 pm to midnight, and with a ticket you can enter the park from 4 pm. The program includes Mickey's Boo-To-You Halloween Parade, the fireworks show Disney's Not-So-Spooky Spectacular with lasers, and the stage show Hocus Pocus Villain Spelltacular in front of the castle. Candy is handed out at stations in every area of the park. New this year are A Masquerade with Stitch, a dance party with Stitch, Lilo and Angel in Tomorrowland, and Captain Jack Sparrow with a pirate program in Adventureland. According to Disney, Space Mountain runs in complete darkness on party nights, with Halloween music.

**Oogie Boogie Bash** at [Disney California Adventure](ref:disney-california-adventure-park) in Anaheim has 33 nights from August 18 to October 31, each from 6 to 11 pm, with entry from 3 pm. Prices ranged from $139 for a night in August to $199 for October 31, and every ticket is gone here too. Madame Leota's Swinging Wake, a street party themed to the Haunted Mansion, is new. Guests 14 and older can't wear masks, and capes are allowed only if they don't drag on the ground.

You get the decorations without a party ticket, too. At Magic Kingdom pumpkin garlands hang over Main Street during the day, and at the Disneyland Resort, Halloween Time runs until October 31 with regular admission. There the Haunted Mansion becomes Haunted Mansion Holiday, Cars Land turns into Radiator Screams in the evening, and Disneyland Park has the nighttime show Halloween Screams, with fireworks on some nights. To plan a day at Magic Kingdom, use our [Magic Kingdom guide](/blog/magic-kingdom-wait-times-tips).

### Knott's Spooky Farm and the daytime events at Six Flags

Spooky Farm at Knott's Berry Farm runs in the daytime, Thursday through Sunday from September 24 to October 31 and on Columbus Day, October 12. Under the motto "All Fun. No Fear." there's candy in Ghost Town, the Peanuts characters, a pumpkin patch and shows. The pumpkin patch and cookie decorating cost extra.

The other Six Flags parks have their own days for families. It's called Tricks and Treats at Magic Mountain, Kings Island, Carowinds, Over Texas, Fiesta Texas and Kings Dominion, and Kids Boo Fest at Great Adventure and Great America. Mostly that means weekends until November 1, in the daytime until about 5 pm, before the evening program starts.

### SeaWorld and Busch Gardens: Spooktacular

The four United Parks parks have a daytime program for kids, included in regular admission. At Busch Gardens Tampa, Halloween Spooktacular runs on Saturdays and Sundays from September 12 to November 1, noon to 4 pm, with a Sesame Street Halloween added from October 3. SeaWorld Orlando holds its Spooktacular on select days from August 29 to November 1. In Williamsburg it's aimed at kids up to nine, and San Antonio has a new Baby Shark show.

### Dollywood and Silver Dollar City: harvest festivals with pumpkins

[Dollywood](ref:dollywood) in Tennessee holds its Harvest Festival from September 14 to October 31, included in regular admission. In the evening more than 12,000 pumpkins glow at Great Pumpkin LumiNights, along with a pumpkin tree 12 meters (39 feet) tall. New this year is Harvey's Boo Bash, an evening in costume on October 16, 23 and 30 from 9 pm to midnight, for $129.99 extra.

[Silver Dollar City](ref:silver-dollar-city) in Missouri has its Harvest Festival from September 11 to October 31. From 5:30 pm more than 20,000 pumpkins light up, the Garden of Giants has pumpkins weighing more than 450 kilograms (1,000 pounds), and during the day craftspeople demonstrate their work. You don't need a separate event ticket.

### Legoland and Knoebels

[Legoland Florida](ref:legoland-florida) and [Legoland California](ref:legoland-california) hold Brick or Treat, for families with kids from two to twelve, included in the day ticket. In California it's on weekends until November 1, with candy from 3 pm. New at both parks is the Brick or Treat Street with monster houses built from Lego. For Florida, the event page and the park's blog list different days, so check the calendar before you go.

There's no admission charge at [Knoebels](ref:knoebels-amusement-park) in Pennsylvania, where you pay per ride. During Hallo-Fun on October weekends more than 50 rides are open in Halloween decorations, on Fridays from 6 to 10 pm, Saturdays from 2 to 10 pm and Sundays from noon to 6 pm.

## With kids: age guidance and costumes

Nearly every park recommends its evening events for ages 13 and up. Six Flags says so for each of its parks, and Busch Gardens Tampa and SeaWorld Orlando speak of a mature audience, though SeaWorld does let in teens under 18. At Great America the recommendation applies from 6 pm, and at Kennywood anyone 17 or under needs a companion aged 21 or older. Hersheypark gives no age for Dark Nights.

At Oogie Boogie Bash, masks are banned from age 14, costumes can't drag on the ground or include weapons, and if you dress as a character you can't pose for photos the way that character would. SeaWorld Orlando bans masks from age 13 at Spooktacular, and so does Hersheypark. At Halloween Horror Nights in Orlando, costumes and costume masks are banned except on Premium Scream Night, and in Hollywood it's costume masks that are banned.

## Two events on one trip

In Orlando, Universal Studios Florida and SeaWorld Orlando are seven kilometers (4 miles) apart as the crow flies, and Magic Kingdom is 13 kilometers (8 miles) from Universal. You can fit Halloween Horror Nights and Howl-O-Scream into one trip on two evenings, with a day at one of the Disney parks in between, where the Halloween decorations are up without a party ticket. Busch Gardens Tampa is a good 100 kilometers (over 60 miles) farther west in a straight line.

In the Los Angeles area, Knott's Berry Farm and Disney California Adventure are eight kilometers (5 miles) apart, Universal Studios Hollywood is 46 kilometers (29 miles) from Knott's, and Six Flags Magic Mountain another 39 kilometers (24 miles) farther north. Knott's Scary Farm and Fright Fest at Magic Mountain overlap on Fridays and Saturdays in October, when both stay open until midnight or later.

The Six Flags bundles include park admission for the whole day, so you can ride in daylight and simply stay on for the event instead of arriving in the evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Halloween event in the US is the biggest?

By number of nights it's Halloween Horror Nights at Universal Studios Florida, with 49. Orlando and Knott's Scary Farm both have ten houses, and among the other Six Flags parks Great Adventure has the most, with nine.

### Can you still get tickets for the Disney Halloween parties?

No. Disney lists every 2026 date of Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party and Oogie Boogie Bash as sold out. The decorations at Magic Kingdom and Halloween Time at the Disneyland Resort come with a regular ticket.

### What age are the horror events for?

Six Flags recommends its evenings for ages 13 and up, and Busch Gardens Tampa and SeaWorld Orlando speak of a mature audience. Younger children usually aren't banned, but they need their own ticket.

### Which event ends last?

SCarowinds at Carowinds, running until November 7. Nearly all the others end on October 31 or November 1.

### What is there for small kids?

The daytime events suit them better than the evenings, for example Knott's Spooky Farm, Tricks and Treats and Kids Boo Fest at Six Flags, Spooktacular at SeaWorld and Busch Gardens, Brick or Treat at Legoland and the pumpkins at Dollywood and Silver Dollar City. Most of it comes with regular admission.
