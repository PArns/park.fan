Shanghai Disney Resort's third hotel, the Shanghai Disney Enchanted Star Hotel, opens on Wishing Star Lake on 16 December 2026. You can book from midnight on 6 November through the resort's Reservation Center, its official channels and authorised travel partners.

## Rooms and prices

The hotel has 400 rooms over nine floors. Four room types go on sale when bookings open:

- Enchanted Star Deluxe Room
- Enchanted Star Deluxe Room (Park View)
- Enchanted Star Dream Room
- Enchanted Star Club Room

More types will be released closer to the opening. The resort hasn't announced yet what a night will cost.

The artwork in the rooms is about wishes and wishing stars, with small Disney details mixed in. The hotel takes its name from Disney's Wishing Star, and the motif runs from the facade through to the interiors.

## Where it is and what it looks like

It stands right next to the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel on the lakeshore. From the hotel you look out over [Shanghai Disneyland](ref:shanghai-disneyland) and Wishing Star Park, the lakeside green space that both hotels use as a shared garden. The building is Art Nouveau, modelled on Shanghai's architecture of the early 20th century.

Since 9 October, three Siberian crane sculptures have stood on the shore near the new hotel. They went up on the eve of World Migratory Bird Day and point to Poyang Lake, an important habitat for these critically endangered birds. The Disney Conservation Fund and the International Crane Foundation have helped the population on the eastern flyway almost double. Wishing Star Park is itself a wintering ground for migratory birds, and the three cranes on the shore stay through the summer too.

## Pools, play areas and restaurants

Inside there are swimming pools and several play areas themed on Disney films. Outside is the first outdoor pool at any of the resort's hotels. It only opens in some seasons, and even then only when the weather and operations allow. The resort hasn't announced which months those are. For a stay in December, I'd plan around the indoor pool.

One of several restaurants is called Enchanted Seasons. Its theme is the seasons, and it serves Chinese and international food. You'll also meet Disney characters in the hotel, and there's entertainment and shopping.

A dedicated corridor connects the hotel to the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel. Guests of both share the gyms, the pools and the indoor and outdoor play areas, and can shop and eat in either hotel.

## What else the resort is building

The resort announced the hotel's name in June 2026, on its tenth birthday. So far it has two hotels, the Shanghai Disneyland Hotel and the Toy Story Hotel. A fourth is planned a few steps from the main entrance of Shanghai Disneyland.

Inside the park, the ninth themed land is going up, built around Spider-Man and the W.E.B. inventors' workshop. All of the ride's track was in place by June, and the first vehicles for test runs were due in August. There's no opening date yet. Soaring Over the Horizon is being expanded as well.
