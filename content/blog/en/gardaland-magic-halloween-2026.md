[Gardaland](ref:gardaland) is running Magic Halloween for 51 days this year, from
12 September to 1 November. Last year it was 31 days, according to Il Giornale.
New is **Preda**, a maze you have to walk through in silence.

## The Preda maze

In the park’s story, the Ravages have come out of the Black Hole, fast alien
creatures that any noise draws in. Earth is their hunting ground, and you’re the
prey, “preda” in Italian. To get through, you move quietly. The park gives you
three rules: don’t stop, don’t turn around, don’t scream.

Preda costs extra. You need a separate ticket from €5.90 per person, valid for
the time slot you book, and the number of tickets is limited. Gardaland
recommends Preda for ages 14 and up and advises against it for younger children.
According to the park, people with heart problems, asthma or a pacemaker are
not allowed in, nor are people with claustrophobia or epilepsy, pregnant women
and wheelchair users. The full list is in the rules on the park’s Preda page.

## Dates and prices

A day ticket costs from €44 during Magic Halloween. With the “One more day”
offer, a 1-day ticket comes with a free second park entry that you can use up to
1 November.

| Event            | When                                       | Ticket   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| Venerdì da Paura | 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30 October, 5 pm to 10 pm | from €29 |
| Halloween Party  | 31 October, music until midnight           | €29      |

On the five Friday evenings, zombie areas and creepy characters take over parts of
the park. Seven artists play at the Halloween Party, and Gardaland hasn’t said
who yet.

According to Il Giornale, the programme includes a welcome show called
“Gardaland Halloween Mania”. On 3 October Lorenzo De Pretto does a meet and
greet at the Camelot Shop. The Boo Bag costs €13.90 and sends you to seven
stops to collect sweets, and make-up with a choice of eight designs
costs €12.90.

Daniela Bricola, Vice President of Gardaland Resort, explains the earlier start
with the “Summerween” trend: Halloween is more than 31 October, she says, and
visitors want the mood to last longer.
