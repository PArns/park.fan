## Improved

- When you tell Parky a forecast is wrong, he first says which reading you are looking at: the
  crowd right now, the forecast for the whole day, or how the day has gone so far. Then he explains
  how Fancast forecasts and offers its track record. He passes it on to the team only if the same
  number still shows two ways.
- On the trip hubs for Orlando, Southern California, Japan and Paris, Parky's offer under "How the
  trip planner plans your days" has one button, "Plan it with Parky", that opens the chat. The blue
  button there used to start the planner by hand. The card no longer runs across the full width.
- Parky stays in the language you write in when you answer with only a ride's name.
- One reply suggestion that runs too long no longer takes the others with it.

## Fixed

- Today's day view on a park's crowd calendar labelled the day so far "Crowds now", which the
  today panel uses for the current moment. At Europa-Park around noon that read "high" in one place
  and "normal" in the other. The day view now says "So far today".
- Parky called a day a bridge day that the calendar never marked: Thursday 12 November at
  Disneyland Paris, the day after a Wednesday holiday. He now names holidays, bridge days and school
  breaks only where park.fan has them.
- A ride with no measured wait in the past year, such as Lanzadera at Parque de Atracciones de
  Madrid, no longer gets best visit times or an hourly forecast on its page.
