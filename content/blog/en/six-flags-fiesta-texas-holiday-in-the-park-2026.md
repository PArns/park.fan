[Six Flags Fiesta Texas](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas) in San Antonio has been running Holiday in the Park since Saturday, 21 November. From 26 to 30 December, every evening there ends with fireworks. On New Year's Eve the New Year's 360 Fireworks Celebration sends thousands of rockets up from the quarry walls that ring the park, so the bangs come from every side. The park is open on 28 days through 4 January 2027, and regular admission covers the shows, the lights and most of the rides.

## Fireworks after Christmas and on New Year's Eve

Six Flags calls the five evenings from 26 to 30 December Countdown to New Year's and only promises the fireworks if the weather plays along.

In the park calendar, New Year's Eve is listed as the Rockin' New Years Celebration. The park closes at 10 p.m. local time, so the fireworks are over at least two hours before midnight, and anyone who wants to count into the new year does it on IH-10. In Germany it's already 5 a.m. on New Year's Day by then. Fiesta Texas hasn't announced yet what time the rockets start on any of the six evenings, or whether New Year's Eve needs a separate ticket.

This winter Six Flags is announcing New Year's Eve evenings at five parks: Fiesta Texas, [Carowinds](/news/carowinds-winterfest-2026), Kings Island, Knott's Berry Farm and [Magic Mountain](/news/six-flags-magic-mountain-holiday-in-the-park-2026).

## Shows, the tree and the lights

Until 23 December, the 70-foot (just over 21-metre) Christmas tree in Rockville lights up at 6 p.m. on every event evening. After that the lights of the Holiday Illuminations switch in time to the music there, without a break. The H2Ho Ho Ho Holiday Fountain does the same with jets of water, and the Singing Saps get you singing carols with an entire family of Christmas trees. Santa takes wish lists at Santa's Cottage, with photo packages at extra cost. The Looney Tunes and DC's heroes and villains pose for photos at set times.

## Which rides are running

On the event days in November and December, 38 rides are running (the January list isn't out yet), among them [Iron Rattler](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas/iron-rattler), the [Superman Krypton Coaster](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas/superman-krypton-coaster), the [Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas/wonder-woman-golden-lasso-coaster), Batman The Ride and Poltergeist. For families, Fiesta Texas recommends [METROPOLIS Transit Authority](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas/metropolis-transit-authority). Of the two railways, only the Fiesta Texas Railroad runs. Gully Washer and Bugs' White Water Rapids sit out Holiday in the Park, so on these evenings the only way to get soaked is a cocoa that goes down the wrong way.

## Tickets, parking and getting there

Park tickets are sold online only, from $45. The Good Any Day ticket, valid on any day you like, is $69. Both come with a processing fee of up to $9.99 plus tax. Parking starts at $39 a day, Preferred Parking at $49 and valet at $55, again plus a fee and tax. For the satnav: 17000 IH-10 West, San Antonio, TX 78257.

Some holiday add-ons cost extra: s'mores over an open fire, cocoa in a souvenir mug and a bubble wand. All three can be bought online too, though the park doesn't give a price. The seasonal menu sticks with turkey, as a holiday feast with honey ham, stuffing and one side or as a turkey leg with a pineapple ring. If you've had enough of that, there's Korean fried chicken bao or a sugar cookie or gingerbread parfait, and adults can order a cocktail with it. The Holiday Hand Pies fit Thanksgiving into an empanada you can carry around, with turkey, stuffing and gravy inside and a cranberry dip on the side.

## Black Friday: the 2027 Silver Pass for the price of a Good Any Day ticket

Until 27 November, Fiesta Texas is selling the 2027 Silver Pass for $69. It's good for unlimited visits, with parking and entry to Hurricane Harbor, but only at Fiesta Texas. Six Flags describes it as covering all of 2027 in one place and spring and summer in another. The park's Silver Pass page doesn't mention visits this December. Buy it as part of the Black Friday deal and you get a Bring-A-Friend ticket, which has to be used by 4 January 2027, so it still works for Holiday in the Park. With a food and drink plan, the package costs $159 instead of $287.

The Gold Pass at $99 is valid this winter and through 2027 at every Six Flags park in Texas and Oklahoma, which includes Holiday in the Park at [Six Flags Over Texas](/news/six-flags-over-texas-holiday-in-the-park-2026) in Arlington.

## Free admission at 65 and over

If you were born on or before 31 December 1961, you can visit Fiesta Texas free as often as you like until 4 January 2027. Sign-up is online only, not at the park, and the admission is activated on your first visit. You show ID with your date of birth at the gate every time. The free admission only works at the park you registered for, and not at the water park. You pay for parking yourself, and discounts, Bring-A-Friend tickets and season pass perks aren't included. Anyone coming with you needs a ticket of their own, except children up to two. This winter is the first time Six Flags has offered free admission.

## After 4 January

Fiesta Texas reopens on Saturday, 9 January 2027, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., and after that on the January weekends and on Monday, 18 January. The Mardi Gras Festival runs on selected days from 6 February to 15 March. For 2027 the park is building Werewolf Gorge, which Six Flags calls the world's longest launched family coaster, with 1,256 metres of track and no opening date so far.
