## New

- Pages from continent to country to park to ride. A park page listed its themed areas with a
  wait badge on each ride, how many rides were open, the crowd level and the current weather.
- Waits showed as "Walk On", a number of minutes or "Closed", in five colour bands up to 120
  minutes and more (21 June).
- The search in the header suggested parks and rides from the third letter on, with country flags
  and arrow keys, and `/` jumped into it (25 June).

## Fixed

- Park and country addresses that the API did not resolve led to a 404; the page looked the
  park up through the search instead (26 June).
