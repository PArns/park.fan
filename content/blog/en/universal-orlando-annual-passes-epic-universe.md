In December, Universal Orlando starts selling the first annual and seasonal passes that include [Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe). If you want to use one of them at the new park, you'll have to reserve each visit in advance. The first day you can book is January 4, 2027. The resort announced the overhaul of its pass program on November 19.

Until now, no annual pass covered Epic Universe. From October 24, 2024, Universal let passholders buy a separate day ticket for it before everyone else and threw in 10% off the Darkmoor Monster Makeup Experience. As annual pass perks go, that was a consolation prize. Universal's reason for the new passes is that the resort has had four theme parks since Epic Universe opened on May 22, 2025. The other three are [Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida), [Islands of Adventure](ref:universal-islands-of-adventure) and the [Volcano Bay](ref:universal-volcano-bay) water park.

## Who gets to upgrade first

Before general sales start later in December, current passholders get their own upgrade window in the coming weeks. They can then switch to a pass with Epic Universe, as long as their pass is active and, in Universal's words, "in good standing". The window runs in phases by tier, and Epic Universe reservations should already be bookable in each phase. Universal plans to publish the dates in early December.

What an upgrade costs depends on when your current pass expires. If you're already in your renewal window, you pay a difference of $125 to $410, depending on the tier, and renew straight into a pass with Epic Universe. Universal hasn't matched the amounts to the tiers yet. Everyone else gets a special price based on their tier and the unused value left on their pass.

## Prices and the new tiers

Florida residents pay from $329.99 for the new passes and from $449.99 for one with Epic Universe, plus tax. Universal will announce prices for everyone else and for each tier from December.

Premier becomes Platinum, Preferred becomes Gold, Power becomes Silver, and the Seasonal pass will be called Bronze. Each tier keeps its own set of benefits, as it does now, and you combine it with the parks you want on the pass. Universal hasn't announced yet what the four tiers get you for parking, Express Pass and discounts, or whether Volcano Bay is one of the parks you can pick. The water park doesn't reopen after its winter break until April 1, 2027 anyway.

The UOAP Lounge, passholder-only sweepstakes and Passholder Appreciation Days stay. The plastic card is being phased out bit by bit, and entry and benefits will run through a digital pass. Passholders also become Pass Members, as they already are at Universal's other US parks.

## Two reservations at a time

Universal uses the reservations to control how many people come into Epic Universe. You can hold two reservations at most, and you can only book a third Epic day once one of them frees up. Spots are limited, go to whoever books first and depend on your pass's blockout dates. The blockout dates are still open, and so is whether the Bronze seasonal pass gets the same Epic days as the annual passes.

Since you only ever get two at a time, I wouldn't waste either of them on a day when half of Florida has booked Epic too.

## Who the pass is for

For now, the pass is for people from Florida who come several times a year and can get by with two Epic days at a time. For a holiday from Europe, I'd stick with day tickets until Universal publishes the remaining prices. Compare the $449.99 with an Epic day ticket and Express Pass; our [Universal Orlando guide](/blog/universal-orlando-wait-times-tips) lists both prices.
