The magic show Supermagic Elementi plays the Gardaland Theatre at [Gardaland](ref:gardaland) from 4 to 8 December 2026. You need a separate ticket from €24, because neither the park ticket nor the season pass covers it.

It's the second year running that Supermagic has come to the park. Last year's edition was called Supermagic Arcano, and the cast changes from one edition to the next. Gardaland bills Supermagic as "Europe's biggest magic show".

## Eight performances

| Day                  | Start         |
| -------------------- | ------------- |
| Friday, 4 December   | 9 pm          |
| Saturday, 5 December | 5 pm and 9 pm |
| Sunday, 6 December   | 5 pm and 9 pm |
| Monday, 7 December   | 5 pm and 9 pm |
| Tuesday, 8 December  | 5 pm          |

Each performance runs about two hours with a 15-minute interval. Seats are numbered, and there's a bar in the foyer. You can't take photos or film during the show. The theatre's regular show still goes ahead alongside Supermagic.

## Who's on stage

Remo Pannain created Supermagic and organises it. From Italy come Massimiliano Cardone and Matteo Fraziano, who won the talent show "Tu Sì Que Vales" in 2024. Nathalie Romier from France sings and does quick-change. Charlie Mag has won the Mandrake d'Or, and Jerome Murat took the Golden Wand at the Monte-Carlo Magic Stars. Mag Marin from Spain performs large-scale illusions.

## Tickets and discounts

A show-only ticket costs from €24 per person online. The exact price depends on how many seats are left and where you sit. It's valid for one performance, on the date and time you pick when you buy, and you can't change it afterwards. Tickets aren't issued in anyone's name. Parking is included.

Children up to nine, guests over 65 and people with disabilities pay a reduced price. Bring proof to the show, or you'll pay the difference on the spot. If you hold a Gardaland season pass or a Gardaland Club card, you can buy up to ten discounted tickets, for yourself and up to nine others. You have to come to the performance yourself and show your card.

Magic Winter is a separate event, running from 5 December 2026 to 6 January 2027. A combined Magic Winter and Supermagic ticket costs from €48 online. It covers one entry to the park and LEGO Miniland on a single day between 5 and 8 December, the show that same evening, and the SEA LIFE Aquarium on the same day or the next, with parking included. That means there's no combined ticket for the Friday performance on 4 December. A dated day ticket for Magic Winter alone costs from €29. For the other parks open in December, see the [winter overview](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

## Dinner at Aladino

The Aladino restaurant serves a show dinner from €49.90 per person, with a meat, vegetarian or children's menu. It starts at 7 pm, so if you've booked a 5 pm performance you eat afterwards, and with a 9 pm one you eat first.

## On the day

Show the documents you got by email at ticket desks 24 to 27, no later than 60 minutes before the show or the dinner. The park closes at 6 pm. If you spent the day inside and hold a 9 pm ticket, you still have to leave, then check in at desks 25 to 27 from 8 pm.

On show days, parking is free from 3:30 pm; show your ticket at the bus entrance. The car park closes an hour after the last performance ends at the latest.

There are up to eight wheelchair spaces, which you reserve in advance by emailing the park. Wheelchair users see the show free, and their companion pays the reduced price.

## Getting there

Gardaland is in Castelnuovo del Garda, in the province of Verona. On the A4 from Milan, take the Peschiera del Garda exit; from Venice, Peschiera del Garda or Sommacampagna. Coming from the Brenner on the A22, leave at Affi and follow the road towards Peschiera.

The park is two kilometres from Peschiera del Garda station. A free shuttle bus runs every 30 minutes, the first leaving 30 minutes before opening and the last 30 minutes after closing. Verona Villafranca airport is a few kilometres away. Hotels next to the park and motorhome pitches are in the [Gardaland guide](/blog/gardaland-wait-times-tips).
