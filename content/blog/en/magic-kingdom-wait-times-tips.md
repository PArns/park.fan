The TEA Global Experience Index puts Magic Kingdom at **17.8 million visits** in
2024, 0.7 percent more than the year before. That made it the most visited theme
park in the world, just ahead of Disneyland Park in California with 17.3 million.
Going by Wikipedia's attendance table it was the eighteenth year running, and
you would expect the longest queues on earth to come with a number like that.
They don't. Of the four Disney parks in Florida, [Magic
Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park) has the lowest typical wait in our data, and
EPCOT the highest.

We can't say why. The crowd queues for a handful of rides instead.

## The six lands and the utilidors

The park opened on **1 October 1971** as the first part of Walt Disney World, on
the same day as the Contemporary and Polynesian hotels. It's run by Disney
Experiences, the company's parks division, and the resort turned 55 on 1 October 2026. At opening the park had 23 attractions, twenty of them copies of rides
from Disneyland in California, and six lands. Five of those lands were Anaheim's Main Street,
Adventureland, Frontierland, Fantasyland and Tomorrowland. The sixth, Liberty
Square, exists only in Florida, and it is where the Haunted Mansion sits.

Only one land was ever added. It opened in 1988 as Mickey's Birthdayland, ended
up as Mickey's Toontown Fair, and closed on 11 February 2011 to make room for
New Fantasyland. That project finished on 28 May 2014 with
the Seven Dwarfs Mine Train.

Underneath it all runs a second floor. Florida's water table is too high for
tunnels, so the service corridors, called utilidors, were built at ground level
and the park was piled on top with earth from the Seven Seas Lagoon. The park
stands on its own second story, 33 metres above sea level, and staff can cross
it without ever being seen. The upper floors
of the buildings on Main Street are shorter than the ground floor, so the street
looks taller than it is.

Cinderella Castle stands at the end of the street. Measured from the water in
its moat it's 56 metres tall, and it took about 18 months to build, finished in
time for opening day. It uses the same forced perspective as Main Street, with
stones, windows and doors getting smaller toward the top. On the fourth floor
there's a suite that sleeps up to six, and from outside you can pick it out by
the stained-glass windows on the north and east sides.

## The queue is in Tomorrowland

```

The top spot belongs to **[TRON Lightcycle / Run](ref:magic-kingdom-park/tron-lightcycle-run)**.
It opened on 4 April 2023, after a soft opening in February, and it is a
launched coaster from Vekoma that reaches 59 mph, about 95 km/h. The ride lasts
around a minute. At that length the queue is the longer part of the
experience, and how much longer is in the table.

Near the top of the table sit **[Seven Dwarfs Mine
Train](ref:magic-kingdom-park/seven-dwarfs-mine-train)**, the 2014 coaster that
completed New Fantasyland, and **[Tiana's Bayou
Adventure](ref:magic-kingdom-park/tianas-bayou-adventure)**, the log flume that
replaced Splash Mountain on 28 June 2024.

The older rides sit further down, with one exception. **[Peter Pan's
Flight](ref:magic-kingdom-park/peter-pans-flight)** is a dark ride from 1971
that opened two days after the park, and in the table it's ahead of Space
Mountain.

## The five coasters

Magic Kingdom has five roller coasters, and none of them is fast. TRON, the
fastest, reaches 59 mph (95 km/h), and the other four stay under 60 km/h
(37 mph). On the other hand, a child of 35 inches (89 cm) can already ride the first
one, and at 38 inches (97 cm) three of the five. The height requirements below
are the ones on Disney's ride pages, and the technical figures come from the
Roller Coaster DataBase.

**[TRON Lightcycle / Run](ref:magic-kingdom-park/tron-lightcycle-run)** in
Tomorrowland is the newest and the biggest. Vekoma built it, and the track is
966 metres long and climbs to 23.8 metres. You ride hunched forward as if on a
motorbike, and right after the station a linear-motor launch fires the train out
under a large canopy of light arcs. Minimum height 48 inches (122 cm). TRON
dropped its virtual queue on 9 September 2024, so you either join the standby
queue or buy a Lightning Lane Single Pass.

**[Seven Dwarfs Mine
Train](ref:magic-kingdom-park/seven-dwarfs-mine-train)** in Fantasyland is a
family coaster by Vekoma that opened on 28 May 2014. The track is 610 metres
long, and the cars reach 34 mph (55 km/h) and rock from side to side in the
curves. Halfway through, the train slows down for a pass through the dwarfs'
mine, past figures sorting gemstones. Minimum height 38 inches (97 cm), and the
ride lasts 2:50 minutes.

**[Space Mountain](ref:magic-kingdom-park/space-mountain)** has run in
Tomorrowland since 15 January 1975, under a white dome and almost entirely in
the dark. A top speed of 27 mph (43 km/h) sounds tame, but without light you
can't see the next curve coming. The track is just under a kilometre long, and
a ride lasts a little over two and a half minutes. Minimum height 44 inches
(112 cm), the highest in the park after TRON.

**[Big Thunder Mountain
Railroad](ref:magic-kingdom-park/big-thunder-mountain-railroad)** in
Frontierland is a mine train coaster from 1980, originally built by Arrow. It
was closed from January 2025 to early May 2026 while the track was rebuilt with
parts from Vekoma. It reopened on 3 May 2026 with new effects and a lower height
requirement, 38 inches (97 cm) instead of 40 inches (102 cm). The track is 847
metres long and the top speed is 36 mph (58 km/h).

**[The Barnstormer](ref:magic-kingdom-park/the-barnstormer)** in Storybook
Circus is the small one to start with, a Vekoma junior coaster from 1996. It's
242 metres long and nine metres high, and it reaches 25 mph (40 km/h). Anyone
35 inches (89 cm) or taller can ride.

## The water ride, the dark rides and the 1971 classics

[Tiana's Bayou Adventure](ref:magic-kingdom-park/tianas-bayou-adventure) is the
only water ride in the park. The boats float through the Louisiana bayou to a
party from _The Princess and the Frog_, and at the end comes a drop Disney gives
as 50 feet, a little over 15 metres. Elsewhere Disney says 55 feet, and
Wikipedia has 16 metres. Minimum height 40 inches (102 cm), and you will get
wet. The ride is closed for maintenance from 2 November into December 2026.

Several dark rides are as old as the park. The [Haunted
Mansion](ref:magic-kingdom-park/haunted-mansion) in Liberty Square, "it's a
small world" in Fantasyland and the Jungle Cruise in Adventureland opened on 1
October 1971, and Peter Pan's Flight two days later. [Pirates of the
Caribbean](ref:magic-kingdom-park/pirates-of-the-caribbean) followed on 15
December 1973. None of the five has a height requirement. They're the rides for
the afternoon, once TRON and the Mine Train are done, and of the classics the
Haunted Mansion usually ranks highest in the table above.

## Parades, fireworks and shows

The day at Magic Kingdom ends with **Happily Ever After**, an 18-minute
fireworks show with lasers and projections on the castle and the buildings of
Main Street. Before or after it comes the nighttime parade **Disney Starlight:
Dream the Night Away**, which premiered on 20 July 2025. During the day the
**Disney Festival of Fantasy Parade** takes twelve minutes to pass through the
park, the shorter Disney Adventure Friends Cavalcade runs as well, and Mickey's
Magical Friendship Faire plays in front of the castle.

Show times change daily and are listed in Disney's app. They matter for the
queues, because while a parade or the fireworks are on, a lot of guests are
lining the route instead of the rides. On Halloween and Christmas party nights
the regular fireworks are cancelled, and a separate show runs for party guests
only.

## Rope drop, or the evening

This is how the eight longest queues move between 8:00 and
22:00 local time:

```

**TRON does not get shorter.** Its queue is at its lowest in the first hour,
grows through the afternoon and peaks in the evening. If you missed it at opening,
don't come back after dinner expecting a bargain. Our rope-drop analysis
rates the payoff of an early start at TRON as high, the only ride in the park
with that rating.

**Tiana's Bayou Adventure starts almost empty.** In the first hour the queue is
a fraction of what it becomes by lunch, and it stays at that level until
early evening. Buzz Lightyear's Space Ranger Spin behaves the same way. Both
get a moderate rope-drop rating.

**Seven Dwarfs Mine Train is the one that gives in.** It holds its level from
midday into the evening and drops in the last hours before closing. If TRON and
Tiana's fill your morning, this is the ride for the end of the day.

Take TRON at opening, Tiana's directly after it and the Mine
Train in the last hour. Space Mountain peaks around midday, the Jungle Cruise
holds its level from mid-morning into the early evening, and both fall off
toward closing.

### Early Entry and Extended Evening Hours

If you're staying at a Disney hotel, you can get into the park 30 minutes ahead
of everyone else every day, and Disney says that will continue until the end of 2027. At Magic Kingdom the rides running in that half hour include Seven Dwarfs
Mine Train, Space Mountain, Peter Pan's Flight and The Barnstormer, while TRON,
Tiana's, Big Thunder and the Jungle Cruise stay shut. For hotel guests that
means the half hour belongs to the Mine Train, and TRON comes right at the
official opening.

Guests at the Deluxe hotels and a few others get Extended Evening Hours on
selected nights, up to two hours after the park closes. TRON, Space Mountain,
Big Thunder and the Mine Train all run then. Disney's calendar lists which
nights they are.

### A day the way I'd plan it

With the hotel perk, be at the gate 45 minutes before opening and spend the half
hour of Early Entry on the Mine Train, then Peter Pan's Flight. At the official
opening, head to Tomorrowland for TRON, then across the park to Frontierland for
Tiana's and Big Thunder. Without a Disney hotel, start straight with TRON and
leave the Mine Train for the evening.

Spend midday in the dark rides of Adventureland and Liberty Square, in an
air-conditioned restaurant or at the Festival of Fantasy Parade. In the
afternoon, when the queues at the big rides are at their longest, ride the
classics. During the fireworks, stay away from Main Street, where the crowd is
thickest, and use the time for one more big ride. The last hour before closing
is for the Mine Train, if it didn't work out in the morning.

To see how long all of that takes, enter your rides in the [trip
planner](/blog/trip-planner). You get an order for them based on the day's
forecast wait times.

## With small children

For families with children under eight, the day happens in Fantasyland. That's
where you'll find Peter Pan's Flight, "it's a small world", the Mine Train, the
Mad Tea Party teacups and, in Storybook Circus, Dumbo the Flying Elephant, where
each child decides how high their elephant flies. Dumbo has no height
requirement. The Barnstormer next to it takes riders from 35 inches (89 cm), and
for a lot of children it's their first roller coaster.

If a child is too small for a ride, the parents don't have to queue twice. With
Rider Switch one adult rides while the other waits with the child, and then the
second adult boards without queuing again. It also works when someone in the
group doesn't want to ride but can't wait outside alone. The park's Baby Care
Center has changing tables, bottle warming and a quiet corner, and it's also
where cast members bring children who have lost their parents.

With small children you're better off planning a shorter day. Go to the
air-conditioned shows at midday, take an afternoon break at the hotel if it's on
the monorail, and come back to the park for the fireworks.

## Lightning Lane

Disney's paid queue skip in Florida is called **Lightning Lane** and comes in
three forms. The Single Pass covers one ride, and at Magic Kingdom it's sold
only for TRON and Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, at most two a day. The Multi Pass
covers most other rides, and the Premier Pass covers every participating ride
in one park once. The Multi Pass and Single Pass replaced Genie+ on 24 July 2024.

With the Multi Pass you pick three time slots in advance. One of them has to
come from the first group, which is Big Thunder, Jungle Cruise, Peter Pan's
Flight, Space Mountain or Tiana's. The other two come from the second group,
which includes the Haunted Mansion, Pirates of the Caribbean, "it's a small
world" and Buzz Lightyear. Disney hotel guests can book seven days before their
stay, everyone else three days before their park visit.

```glossary-widget slug=lightning-lane

```

Prices move by the day. On 30 September 2026, a Wednesday with a low crowd
level, our live data listed the Multi Pass at $27 and the Premier Pass at $329.
The next day they were $23 and $299. The Single Pass for TRON cost $19 on both
days, and for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train $11 on 30 September. Both were marked
unavailable that day. TRON's came back for 1 October.

TRON is also the ride where our rope-drop analysis sees the biggest saving, so
the $19 competes with getting to the gate early.

### Halloween and Christmas

Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party runs on 38 nights between 7 August and 31
October 2026, and Disney lists every date as sold out. On those nights the park
closes to regular guests at 18:00, and the party runs from 19:00 to midnight.
The October dates still to come are the 6th, 8th, 9th, 13th, 15th, 16th, 18th,
22nd, 23rd, 25th, 27th, 29th and 31st. Turn up on one of them without a party
ticket and you get a short day. What the party includes is in our overview of
[Halloween in the US](/blog/halloween-usa-theme-parks-2026).

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party follows on 25 nights from 8 November to 22
December 2026, also from 19:00 to midnight, with entry from 16:00. Tickets cost
$189 to $229 plus tax depending on the night, and children aged three to nine
pay $10 less. The party has its own parade, Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime
Parade, and its own fireworks, Minnie's Wonderful Christmastime Fireworks Show.
On these nights, too, the park closes early for everyone else.

## What is closed, and what is coming

The Rivers of America section of Frontierland opened for the last time on 6
July 2025 and has been closed since 7 July, and Tom Sawyer Island and the
Liberty Belle Riverboat went with it. In its place Disney is building Piston
Peak National Park, an area themed to the Pixar _Cars_ films with two rides,
Cars Ridge Run Rally and Miss Fritter's Daredevil Spin-Along. Disney says
construction is under way but hasn't given an opening year. The same goes for
Villains Land, the land for the Disney villains. Its name was confirmed in
August 2026, along with a Maleficent coaster and a dark ride about the Magic
Mirror.

The Carousel of Progress in Tomorrowland has been closed since 6 July 2026.
Disney is reworking the show with a new Walt Disney figure and scenes from more
recent decades, and Jamie Lee Curtis and Bryan Cranston voice the parents.
Disney expects it to reopen in late spring 2027.

The Walt Disney World Railroad has been closed for maintenance since 28
September 2026 and is due back in November. Since the Frontierland rebuild it no
longer circles the park and only runs between Main Street and Fantasyland. The
first version of this guide still had it going all the way round.

The castle itself got new colours in 2026. And for 2027 Disney has announced
Disney After Hours, separately ticketed nights after closing on selected dates
from 11 January to 19 July.

## Getting there, hours, tickets and food

**Getting there.** The park is more than a mile from its parking lot, on the far
side of the Seven Seas Lagoon. Imagineers chose that on purpose, to keep the
backstage lot out of sight of the park. From the lot you take a tram to the
Transportation and Ticket Center, then either the monorail or one of three
ferries, named after the Disney executives Joe Potter, Richard Irvine and Joe
Fowler. The monorail's Express Line runs directly to the park. Disney hotels in
other parts of the resort run buses that stop at the main gate, while city
buses and rideshare drop you at the Transportation and Ticket Center.

Parking at Magic Kingdom costs $35 a day, and spaces closer to the entrance of
the Transportation and Ticket Center cost $50, $55 or $60. The monorail runs
from 30 minutes before the park opens until an hour after it closes, and the
buses start 45 minutes before opening. From Orlando airport, Mears Connect runs
to the Disney hotels from $16 per adult each way, according to the operator,
and the trip takes 30 to 60 minutes. Inside the resort there's also the Minnie
Van, a car service you book through the Lyft app.

**Opening hours.** Year-round, every day. The hours change daily and are kept
current on the [park page](ref:magic-kingdom-park). Between 4 October and 14
December 2026, Disney has the park opening at 8:00 or 9:00 and closing at 22:00
or 23:00, or at 18:00 on party nights.

**Tickets.** Disney sets the prices by date, and they're in the calendar on
[disneyworld.com](https://disneyworld.com/). We don't track admission, only
what Lightning Lane costs on top of it. With Park Hopper you can switch to any
of the other three parks at any time of day after your first one, as long as
there's room.

**Food.** Our database lists 23 places to eat in the park as of 30 September
2026, from Cinderella's Royal Table to the counter at Casey's Corner. Alcohol,
banned in the park until 2012, has been sold in all table-service restaurants
since 2018. Inside the castle itself you eat at Cinderella's Royal Table, and in
the Beast's castle from _Beauty and the Beast_ at Be Our Guest Restaurant. Both
are in Disney's most expensive price category, over $60 per adult, and you
should reserve for both. At the quick-service restaurants you can order ahead in
Disney's app, which works at more than 60 locations across the resort.

**Hotels.** The closest are the three hotels on the monorail: the Contemporary,
with the monorail running through its main hall, the Polynesian Village, and the
Grand Floridian from 1988. Bay Lake Tower belongs to the Contemporary. All three
are Deluxe hotels, so they get Early Entry and Extended Evening Hours. Disney's
Lakeshore Lodge opens on Bay Lake in summer 2027 and can be booked from 8
October 2026. The hotels on the bus network elsewhere in the resort are cheaper.

## Frequently asked questions about Magic Kingdom

### Does the most visited park have the longest queues?

No. In the comparison above, Magic Kingdom has the lowest typical wait of the
four Disney parks in Florida, and EPCOT the highest. We can't say why.
One ride, TRON, has by far the longest queue in the park.

### Which ride has the longest queue at Magic Kingdom?

TRON Lightcycle / Run, in our data. The current ranking is in the table above.

### When is TRON shortest?

In the first hour after the gates open. The queue then grows through the
day and peaks in the evening, so an evening visit is worse.

### Is Splash Mountain still there?

No. Tiana's Bayou Adventure replaced it on 28 June 2024, with a new story
from the 2009 film _The Princess and the Frog_.

### What does Lightning Lane cost?

It depends on the ride and the date. On 30 September 2026 our data had a Multi
Pass at $27, a Premier Pass at $329, and single rides at $11 and $19.

### How do I get to Magic Kingdom without a car?

By bus from a Disney hotel, straight to the gate, or by monorail or ferry from
the Transportation and Ticket Center. City buses and rideshare drop you at that
same center.

### How tall does my child have to be for the roller coasters?

The Barnstormer takes riders from 35 inches (89 cm), Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and
Big Thunder Mountain from 38 inches (97 cm), Space Mountain from 44 inches (112
cm) and TRON from 48 inches (122 cm). The water ride, Tiana's Bayou Adventure,
needs 40 inches (102 cm). The dark rides have no height requirement.

### When does Magic Kingdom close early?

On the nights of the Halloween party in autumn and the Christmas party in
November and December, when the park closes to regular guests at 18:00. The
dates are further up, under "Halloween and Christmas".

### Is there still a virtual queue for TRON?

No. Since 9 September 2024 you can only ride TRON through the standby queue or
with a Lightning Lane Single Pass.
