## New

- Wait-time alerts (8 September): the bell on a ride card sends a push notification once the wait
  falls to the number of minutes you set, and the bell on a show reminds you before it starts.
  `/alerts` lists every alert this browser has, across parks. From 9 September the favourites menu
  lists them too, each with a button to remove it.
- Pick two days in the crowd calendar and a dialog compares them: the verdict in one sentence, then
  each reason with both values and the difference, and from there straight into the planner
  (10 September).
- The day view in the calendar draws the hour-by-hour curve for today and tomorrow, in the park's
  own time zone (14 September). The chart had been there since it was written and never drawn,
  because the data it reads was never requested.

## Improved

- The planner works under a finger (10 September). On the shortest block, the grip had a touch area
  20 px tall.
- A ride page says since when a ride has been down and for how long (9 September). Its card on the
  park page carried the first half, while the ride page said only "temporarily closed".

## Fixed

- The bell beside a show time signed you up for every performance of that show, while the dialog
  named one. It registers the one you pressed (9 September).
