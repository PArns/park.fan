[Cedar Point](ref:cedar-point) in Sandusky, Ohio, is holding **HalloWeekends**
for the 29th time. The event began on 17 September and runs on selected days
until **1 November**. During the day there’s an autumn festival for families,
and in the evening the haunted houses open. New this year is
**Diablo: The Infernal Path**, a house based on a video game.

To get into the houses you need a separate pass on top of park admission. That
goes for every visitor, season pass holders included.

## Diablo: The Infernal Path

Blizzard’s Diablo series turns 30 this year. Six Flags has taken the licence
for two parks, Cedar Point and Kings Dominion in Virginia. According to the
park, [Diablo: The Infernal Path](ref:cedar-point/diablo-the-infernal-path)
takes you through cursed cathedrals into a gothic world where Diablo, Lilith,
Mephisto and Baal are waiting. Reports on the announcement also name the
Butcher and Duriel. The Butcher has been greeting players with "Ah, fresh
meat!" since 1996.

Diablo is part of the Haunted Attractions Pass, along with the other five
houses:

- [Midnight](ref:cedar-point/midnight), the house of Mr. Midnight, the character
  the whole event revolves around
- [Slaughter House](ref:cedar-point/slaughter-house), a meat-packing plant with
  a guided tour
- [The Haunting of Eerie Estate](ref:cedar-point/the-haunting-of-eerie-estate),
  a decaying mansion
- [Fearground Freak Show](ref:cedar-point/fearground-freak-show), a sideshow of
  curiosities
- [CornStalkers 2.0](ref:cedar-point/cornstalkers-20-revenge-of-the-pumpkin-heads),
  a cornfield with pumpkin heads, closed on Sundays

## The Conjuring costs extra

[The Conjuring: Beyond Fear](ref:cedar-point/midnight-2) ran last year and is
back. You play a ghost hunter, meet Annabelle the doll and lock cursed objects
away. It takes just under 15 minutes. This house is **not** part of the
Haunted Attractions Pass and has its own ticket.

## What it costs

All prices are from the park’s add-on page, in US dollars, as of 28 September.
Every purchase adds a fee of up to $9.99, plus tax.

| Ticket                              | Price    | Includes                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Haunted Attractions Pass            | from $15 | the six houses on one night, no admission   |
| All Season Haunted Attractions Pass | from $59 | the six houses on every night               |
| HalloWeekends Bundle                | from $70 | admission and the houses on the same night  |
| The Conjuring: Beyond Fear          | from $25 | this house only, on one night, no admission |

The one-night pass is only sold online. If you read about a $35 All Season Pass
in August, the park’s own page now says $59. The Gold Pass, the more expensive
season pass, includes HalloWeekends. You still need the Haunted Attractions
Pass for the houses.

## When the houses are open

According to the event page, the houses open from 8 pm to midnight on Thursdays
and Fridays, 6 pm to midnight on Saturdays and 5 pm to 8 pm on Sundays. For the
first two weeks of October, the park calendar lists only the evening from 6 pm
on Thursdays, 11 am to midnight on Fridays and Saturdays, and 11 am to 8 pm on
Sundays. From Monday to Wednesday the park is closed.

Ohio is six hours behind Central European time. From 25 to 31 October, after
Europe puts its clocks back, it’s five. On 1 November, the last day, the US puts
its clocks back too, and the gap is six hours again.

The four scare zones run Thursday to Saturday from 8 pm and don’t open at all on
Sundays. The new one is **Crystal Rock Massacre**, a summer camp in the woods
with a secret. Cut Throat Cove with its pirates, Clownz: Hall of Infamy and
Tombstone Terrortory are back. There are also eight shows, from the opening
with Mr. Midnight to a ska band made of skeletons.

After 8 pm, Thursday to Saturday, the park says some areas are too intense for
young children. Costumes, masks and horror make-up aren’t allowed on any night.

## Rides renamed for Halloween

Six rides go by other names until November.
[GateKeeper](ref:cedar-point/gatekeeper), above the main entrance, is now
GateKreeper, [Rougarou](ref:cedar-point/rougarou) becomes RougaBOO! and the
[Midway Carousel](ref:cedar-point/midway-carousel) the Midway Scareousel.
[Maverick](ref:cedar-point/maverick) runs as MaverEEK!, the pirate ship
[Ocean Motion](ref:cedar-point/ocean-motion) as Ocean Potion and the wooden
coaster [Blue Streak](ref:cedar-point/blue-streak) as Boo Streak.
