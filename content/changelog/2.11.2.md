## New

- The crowd calendar left the park page's tabs for a page of its own, in all six languages
  (27 August). A day tile carries a bar along its top edge with one segment per signal, so a Friday
  in the summer holidays with holidays next door shows both, and the predicted wait sits in its
  corner (28 August).
- Where a park sells skip-the-queue access, the ride carries a badge with the product's name and
  price in your currency format (28 August). A price of zero means it is included, as at
  Europa-Park. Six brand names joined the glossary: QuickPass, Virtual Line, Fast Lane, Speedy Pass,
  Fastrack and Premier Access.
- The star in the header opens your favourites from any page, as cards with a photo and the current
  wait (29 August). Until then they appeared only on the homepage, two screens below the photo.

## Improved

- Park and ride pages open onto a row of tiles with icons. The tab bar they replace was a pale strip
  under the weather card, below the fold on most screens (27 August).
- The sitemap reports the day a page last changed for a reader. A daily crawl fingerprints what is
  on each page, instead of stamping all of its URLs (44,000 at the time) with today's date
  (27 August).

## Fixed

- Going from a park page to its calendar and back cost a jump to the top and an animated scroll back
  down, in both directions. The tile row keeps its position (27 August).
