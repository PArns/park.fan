A child under four is too small for most roller coasters. If you're taking one
to a theme park, you want to know beforehand at what age or height admission
starts to cost money, whether you can rent a stroller or bring your own, where
to change a diaper and whether two adults can take turns on a ride without
queuing twice. Every park puts that information in a different corner of its
website.

The ten parks in this guide are in four countries: Europa-Park, Phantasialand,
Legoland Deutschland, Heide Park and Plopsaland Deutschland in Germany,
Efteling, Toverland and Walibi Holland in the Netherlands, Walibi Belgium in
Belgium and Parc Astérix in France.

## Where the information comes from

What the parks say comes from their own websites: ticket page, help and FAQ,
park rules and the ride pages. Blogs and forums don't count.

On park.fan, each ride's page shows its minimum height, but not every ride has
one yet. For Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland and Walibi Belgium it's
missing for every ride, and for Efteling for 29 of 36. For those parks we give
the heights the park itself lists. So if a minimum height is missing on our
side, that doesn't mean the ride has none.

Efteling, Europa-Park, Phantasialand, Parc Astérix, Legoland Deutschland and
Walibi Holland base free entry on age. At Heide Park, Toverland, Walibi Belgium
and Plopsaland it's height that counts, and Plopsaland and Walibi Belgium
measure children with their shoes on. A child can be free under one park's age
rule and not under another's height rule, and the other way round. If your
child is tall for their age, check each park.

## Efteling

At [Efteling](ref:efteling) in Kaatsheuvel, children up to and including age 3
get in free. In 2026 a ticket costs between €40 and €56, depending on the day.

The park has the most detailed baby page of the ten. Every restroom has at least
one changing cubicle with a diaper bin, though the park doesn't sell diapers.
There are three rooms for nursing and pumping: in Volk van Laaf, at Station de
Oost and in the Efteling Grand Hotel. In Volk van Laaf and the hotel the room is
always open, at Station de Oost a staff member opens it. The Efteling app shows
exactly where they are. Bottles, jars and warm water are available on request in
a restaurant, and most restaurants have high chairs.

You can rent wooden strollers for €5 at the main entrance, with a seat for one
toddler. You may bring your own, but not into every ride and restaurant. The
ride pages for Sirocco and Piraña say explicitly that strollers aren't allowed.

Baby Switch applies on 19 rides. One parent rides while the other waits at the
exit with the baby. When the first gets off, staff hand them a Baby Switch
ticket that lets the second board through the exit without queuing again. The 19
rides are spread like this: in Marerijk Anton Pieckplein, Droomvlucht,
Stoomcarrousel, Villa Volta and the Volk van Laaf; in Reizenrijk Carnaval
Festival, Vogel Rok and Pagode; in Ruigrijk Baron 1898, De Oude Tufferbaan, De
Vliegende Hollander, Halve Maen, Joris en de Draak and Python; in Anderrijk Danse
Macabre, Fata Morgana, Max & Moritz and Piraña; in Fantasierijk Symbolica.

For the youngest there are two playgrounds, Kleuterhof in Reizenrijk and
Kindervreugd in Marerijk. For several rides the park gives no minimum height,
only a rule: children under 1.00 m ride with adult supervision on the
[Stoomcarrousel](ref:efteling/stoomcarrousel), in Villa Volta, on the steam train
and in Sirocco. With a companion aged 16 or older, children under 1.00 m may go
on [Droomvlucht](ref:efteling/droomvlucht), in the [Pagode](ref:efteling/pagode)
and on the Anton Pieckplein. In Symbolica, babies must be able to sit up on
their own, and nobody may ride on a lap. The park doesn't mention quiet zones or
nap spots.

The rest of the park is covered in our guide
[Efteling: the Disney of the Netherlands](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).

## Europa-Park

At [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) in Rust, toddlers from 0 to 3 years get in
without paying. Children from 4 to 11 pay between €56.50 and €65 online for a
day between 28 March 2026 and 9 January 2027. Birthday
children up to their 12th birthday get in free, with ID at the box office by the
main entrance.

The park names changing rooms in seven places: at the main entrance, at the info
point by the lake, in Ireland (Kinderwelt), in Italy next to dm family & friends,
in Spain, in the Fairytale Forest and in the Kingdom of the Minimoys. It doesn't
say how they're equipped or whether there's a nursing room. At dm family &
friends the park sells selected baby food. The website doesn't say where you
can warm it up and sends you to guest services instead.

The rental stroller costs €5 plus a €50 deposit and is handed out at the main
entrance or at the entrance to the hotel car park. By the park's own account it
only suits children who can already sit, because nobody can lie down in it. The
park doesn't rent out wagons, and they don't appear in the park rules. The rules
ban non-motorised vehicles such as skates, bikes and scooters, and it isn't
clear whether a wagon counts as one. If you want to bring one, ask guest
services beforehand.

At Europa-Park the swap is called Baby-Switch and, according to the park, it
applies on many of the larger coasters. The park names blue fire Megacoaster and
Alpenexpress Coastiality, but there's no complete list. Whoever waits stays with
the child, at the playground for instance. Whoever rides first gets a card for
the exit or for the separate Baby Switch entrance.

On the park's attraction list, the ones apparently meant for the youngest are
Little Lamb's Land, which the list calls a "Tiny tots playground", the Würmchen
Wies'n Playground, the Water Playground, the ball pit, the Adventure Playground,
Paul's Playboat and the Mul-Muls Carousel. According to our data, children need
at least 90 centimetres for the Quipse Paddle Boats, the Mini-Scooters and Red
Baron, and 100 for the Mul-Muls Carousel. The park's website gives no minimum
age for its children's rides. More on the rides is
in our guide [Europa-Park: Germany's biggest park, and where everybody went](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips).

## Phantasialand

At [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl, too, children from 0 to 3 years
get in free, without a pre-booked ticket. On the day of your visit you need proof
of age, meaning an ID or birth certificate, a copy will do. Without it, the child
price for ages 4 to 11 applies. The ticket page doesn't give that price, only
"Tickets from €44". Birthday children up to their 11th birthday get in free if
they've booked ahead online.

Next to guest services there's a quiet and nursing room with changing rooms.
Staff will warm up jars of baby food. In the winter park Wintertraum, guest
services is at Kaiserplatz, with its own nursing corner, changing table and
kettle.

The rental stroller costs €3 a day plus a €20 deposit and is handed out at guest
services in the Berlin themed area. The number is limited, and you can reserve
one by e-mail. A stroller carries children up to 22 kilograms. Phantasialand is
very strict about strollers you bring and gives exact dimensions: only narrow,
classic strollers and buggies up to 105 centimetres long, 75 centimetres wide and
125 centimetres high are allowed, and only for carrying children. Wagons, bike
trailers and dog carts are banned. An exception on medical grounds has to be
e-mailed to guest services at least 14 days ahead.

There is a Baby Switch: one parent rides, the other stays with the toddler, then
you swap without the second one queuing again. You ask for it at the ride. The
park doesn't say which rides take part.

In a table in its terms of use, the park lists under "Attractions for children"
Der lustige Papagei, Die fröhliche Bienchenjagd, Bolles Riesenrad, Wözl's Duck
Washer, Bolles Flugschule, Winni Splash, Wolke's Luftpost, Bumper Klumpen and
Wupi's Wabi Wipper. According to our data, children need at least 90
centimetres for Bolles Riesenrad, the Papagei and the Bienchenjagd, and 100 for
Bolles Flugschule, Bumper Klumpen, Wupi's Wabi Wipper and Wözl's Duck Washer.
The park writes that children from 1.00 m can ride more
than three quarters of the attractions. As a play area for the little ones it
names Mopti's Monkey Depot.

Our [Phantasialand guide](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips) has the rest.

## Legoland Deutschland

At [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland) in Günzburg, children under 2
get in free. In the 2026 season (28 March to 8 November), children from 2 to 11
pay €58 at the gate and anyone from 12 pays €64. Online, the day ticket starts at
€39.

The Baby Service powered by Müller is in the Imagination themed area and has a
changing table, a separate nursing area, seating and a small kitchen for warming
food and preparing baby food. You can also buy baby food, diapers and care
products there. Since 1 October you can only reach it through the terrace of the
Pizza Mania restaurant, because the Duplo playground is being rebuilt. According
to the FAQ, every restroom in the park has a changing room.

Buggies are available in limited numbers at the entrance for €6 plus a €4
deposit, and you return them there too. They have no mount for infant car seats.
According to the FAQ you're welcome to bring your own wagon, and there are
dedicated parking spots in the park for buggies and strollers. Neither the FAQ
nor the toddler page mentions a Rider Switch. If you want to know whether
there's an arrangement anyway, ask before your visit.

The park's toddler page names the Junior Driving School (from 3 years), the Duplo
Express, the Safari Tour, the Harbour Cruise, the Airport, the Knight's Tournament,
the Canoe X-pedition and Miniland. According to our data, children need at
least 80 centimetres for Airport, Canoe X-pedition, Captain Nick's Splash
Battle, Duplo Express, Harbour Cruise, Legoland Express and the Observation
Tower, and at least 90 for Caterpillar Ride, Pedal-A-Car, Pirate School, Pyramid
Rallye, Royal Joust and Temple X-pedition. There are also play areas: the
Ninjago World Toddler Area, the Duplo Playground and the playgrounds of Pirate
Land, Shipyard and LavaLand. The park publishes a full list with the age and
height for each ride as a PDF.

There's a quiet room and loungers in the first aid station, meant for guests with
circulation problems. Our guide
[Legoland Deutschland: tips for a day with kids from three to twelve](/blog/legoland-deutschland-tips)
has the details on the rides.

## Heide Park

At [Heide Park](ref:heide-park) in Soltau, children under 90 centimetres get in
free. Birthday children of any age get in free with proof, and you have to pick the €0 ticket for that in the online shop.
The dated day ticket costs from €37 online and €64 at the box office.

At Heide Park the swap is called the Baby-Switch-Pass. Parents of children up to
and including age 3 get it free at the Service Center, only on days when the
Express Ticket is available. It applies to coasters. The park doesn't say which
ones.

You can warm up baby food at the Wirtshaus des Admirals and in Lucky Land, among
other places, and the app shows all the stations. The park map also marks
changing stations. There's nothing on the park map, the top tips page or the
ticket page, or in the park rules, about stroller rental, bringing your own
stroller or wagon, nursing or bringing food. The park rules ban bicycles, scooters and tricycles, kick scooters, skateboards,
roller skates and sleds.

On its "Theme park with children" page, the park names Peppa Pig Land, Drachenzähmen
– Die Insel and Ghostbusters 5D as children's attractions, and among others
Floßfahrt, Heide Park Express, Grottenblitz, Indy Blitz, La Ola, Monorail,
Topilaula, Wildwasserbahn, Bounty and the vintage cars. According to our data,
children need at least 86 centimetres for George's Dino Adventure in Peppa Pig
Land, 95 for Raffnuss & Taffnuss Wasserflieger and 100 each for Indy-Blitz, La
Ola, Screamie and Wüstenflitzer. For Peppas Ballonfahrt, Peppas Bootsfahrt, Opa
Pigs Zugfahrt, Peppas House, Käpt'ns Törn, Heide Park Express and Monorail, the
minimum height is still missing on our side. That doesn't mean the park doesn't
set one.

Our guide [Heide Park: Colossos in wood, surrounded by one German first after another](/blog/heide-park-wait-times-tips)
has more.

## Plopsaland Deutschland

[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) in Haßloch gives only a
height for free entry: children under 85 centimetres pay nothing. From 85 to 99
centimetres the ticket costs €22, and from one metre the adult price applies. The
price page gives no year. The family ticket for three to six people costs between
€139 and €259, depending on group size. The family ticket page still lists €170
to €250 for the 2025 summer season.

According to the park, there are changing rooms in all restroom blocks. The
restaurants Pfalzgraf, Maja-Burger and Plopsa Indoor have microwaves for warming
baby food. You may bring your own food in small amounts, outside the restaurants.
Baby food is the exception: it may also be eaten in restaurants and on outdoor
terraces.

Strollers are allowed, but at rides and shows they have to stand in the
designated areas. On busy days they can't go into Plopsa Indoor. The park
doesn't mention stroller rental. Wagons come with a fee, according to the park
rules, against an ID or a €100 deposit. The rules don't say how much the fee is.
Neither the FAQ pages nor the park rules mention a Rider Switch or swapping.
Every guest has to leave the ride after each trip, and anyone who wants a second
go queues again.

On the other hand, Plopsaland gives the height bands on each ride's page. A
companion must be at least 15 years old. Without a minimum height, but with a companion, children under 100
centimetres ride, for example, the ball pit, Fischerboote, Frösche, Tanzende
Fontänen, Red Baron, the Historisches Karussell (under 105 centimetres with a
companion) and the playgrounds of Heidi, Maja, Wickie and at the beach. Under
120 centimetres with a companion, children can go on Die Schlümpfe Abenteuer,
Flip der Grashüpfer, Balloon Race and in the Super Wings water playground. From
85 centimetres children ride Bienchenwirbel, Willis Floßfahrt and Laras
Marienkäferflug without a companion. The park's page filters by "Ideal for
babies" and "Ideal for toddlers" but doesn't name any areas. When it rains, the
indoor attractions stay open, and there's more on that in our guide
[Theme parks in the rain](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain).

More on the rides is in the guide
[Plopsaland Deutschland: the park that used to be Holiday Park](/blog/plopsaland-deutschland-wait-times-tips).

## Toverland

At [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum, children under 90
centimetres pay nothing but still need a reservation in the ticket shop. From 90
to 140 centimetres the ticket costs €32.50, from 140 centimetres €39.50,
depending on the day class. On your birthday you get in for €0 with ID.

All restrooms except those in Ithaka have changing tables. Microwaves are free in
the Waldstube and on Katara Plaza, and the park doesn't mention a bottle warmer.
If you want to pump, ask the staff at the main entrance.

You may bring a buggy or wagon, and the park recommends a chain lock. Toverland
doesn't rent anything out. Baby Switch applies on selected rides, with no age
limit: you pick up a special ticket at the entrance, and one parent may ride
through the exit. The park doesn't say which rides they are.

In Land van Toos, an area with halls, children ride with a companion from 0 centimetres
in the Klokhuis and in Morrels Truckjes, alone from 90. The Toos-Express,
Theekopjes and Drakenslangen start at 90 with a companion, Djinn at 100
centimetres. On top of that come Villa Toverhoed, Tovertuin, Dwaalhof, Sim sa la
Klim and the Speelkasteel with no height given. In Avalon, Little Dragons is a
playground for toddlers, the Garden Tour runs from 0 with a companion and Jumping
Juna from 0 centimetres, and in the Wunderwald the carousel from 0. The water
playgrounds are Sparky's Splash Dock and Waku Waku. On its website the park
gives no age limits, only heights. It describes itself as partly covered. How
many of its attractions are under a roof is in our guide
[Theme parks in the rain](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain).

[Toverland: Troy, the wooden coaster everyone drives past](/blog/toverland-troy-wait-times-tips).

## Walibi Holland

[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) in Biddinghuizen words the age limit
differently in two places. The 2026 ticket page lists children up to 2 as free,
ages 3 to 5 at €23, ages 6 to 11 at €36 and ages 12 and up at €46, as the
box-office price for the low season. In the FAQ from the 2025 season, it's free
under 3. Both mean the same: a child is free until their third birthday.
According to the FAQ there's no minimum age for visiting.

There are changing tables in several restrooms and microwaves for bottles and
food in several restaurants. The park map marks where they are. If you want your child
to wear a wristband with their name and your mobile number, you can get one at
Guest Service.

Buggies cost €10 plus a €50 deposit at the Gift Shop and can't be booked in
advance. The park's website doesn't say whether your own stroller or wagon is
allowed. You may bring food and drinks. The swap system is called "baby change":
one adult waits with the child at the exit, the other rides, then they swap, and
the second goes through the exit without queuing. The park doesn't say which
rides take part.

For the little ones, the kids page lists Merrie Go'Round (from 0 with a
companion, alone from 120 centimetres), Walibi's Fun Recorder, Garage, Bubble
Swirl and Walibi's World Tour (from 0 with a companion, alone from 105) and
Walibi's Shuttle (from 0, alone from 120). Wind Seekers starts at 90 with a
companion, and children ride Mini Taxi's, Stunt Flight and Space Kidz alone from
90 or 105 centimetres respectively. On park.fan the minimum height is still
missing for all 39 of the park's rides, so these heights all come from the park.
You'll find more on the park in the post
[Walibi Holland: "hard gaan", Untamed and a field where fish swam in 1957](/blog/walibi-holland-untamed-hard-gaan).

## Walibi Belgium

[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) in Wavre lets children under one metre in
free, measured with shoes on. From 1 to 1.40 metres a reduced price applies:
€47 on a standard day and €51 on an extended day. From 1.40 metres it's €57 and
€61 respectively. These prices apply to the Ibilaw days from 10 October to 8
November 2026.
Children under 13 may only enter the park with adults and are under constant
supervision.

Baby Switch is free and can't be combined with the Speedy Fast-Pass or the
Easy-pass. The page's text doesn't explain how it works. Neither the services
page nor the FAQ nor the park rules mention a changing room. Nursing is allowed
everywhere in the park. If you'd like somewhere quieter, go to the first aid
post. The restaurants have microwaves for bottles and baby food. According to
the park rules, strollers are exempt from the pedestrian principle and so
allowed. The FAQ mentions "trolleys" for €10 in limited numbers. It doesn't say
whether those are strollers.

The park has no named children's areas, but it does have a "Kids" category. The
lowest limit is 50, with a companion, on Stormy, 4x4 Adventure, Mini Tour,
Spinning Taxi and Tchou-Tchou Express. The park's page gives the number without
a unit, and it means centimetres. Every ride has a minimum height on the park's
website. During the Ibilaw days, Kondaala, Spinning Taxi, Guitar Riff, Kids
Airlines, Tchou-Tchou Express and Little Swing, among others, are closed. More
background in the guide
[Walibi Belgium: the Walibi nobody thinks of, with the tallest Benelux coaster](/blog/walibi-belgium-wait-times-tips).

## Parc Astérix

At [Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) in Plailly, children under 3 are free, on
proof of age. Children from 3 to 11 pay the child price. For 2026 the park lists
the Malin Ticket at €49, Early Bird from €53 for ages 3 to 11 and the Night Ticket
at €53 (10 October to 7 November 2026).

Changing tables are in several restrooms. The park doesn't mention a nursing
room, a microwave or a bottle warmer. There are bottle warmers only in the
hotel, at reception. Rental strollers cost €15. Picnics are only allowed in
designated areas. The park's website doesn't say whether you may bring baby
food.

Baby Switch has its own explanatory page: one parent rides alone, the other stays
with the child, then both go to the ride operator, who lets the second ride
without queuing again. It applies to children below the minimum height. The
page doesn't give a complete list of rides.

The children's area is called "Attractions for Little Gauls". The park gives a
minimum age for The Mini Train and Ceasar's Carrousel (2 years) and for The Mini
Flying Chairs, Ace Flying Squadron and Laundromatix (3). The Baby Switch page
lists the playgrounds Golden Boar, Getafix and Little Oaks with no age or height
restriction. The activities page gives Golden Boar from 2 years. For
Aerodynamix, Enigmatix and Hydrolix, one page says from 5 years and another says
they're accessible to children under one metre. Which is right is best checked
at reception. Children are measured there and get a wristband listing the rides
they're allowed on. Our guide
[Parc Astérix: Toutatis, OzIris and the Christmas queues](/blog/parc-asterix-wait-times-tips)
covers the rest.

## What the parks' pages add up to

No park mentions quiet zones, naps or shade on its website. The closest are the
quiet and nursing room at Phantasialand, the three nursing rooms at Efteling,
the first aid post at Walibi Belgium and the quiet room in Legoland's first aid
station. So a child will mostly sleep in the stroller. Nobody can lie down in
Europa-Park's rental stroller, and Efteling's is a wooden single seat. At
Phantasialand, your own stroller can't be larger than 105 by 75 by 125
centimetres.

Plopsaland explicitly allows baby food you bring, even in the restaurants. At
Walibi Holland you may bring your own food. For the other eight parks, the
website doesn't say whether you can bring baby food. Europa-Park only bans
grills and cooking equipment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Up to what age are children free at a theme park?

It varies. At Efteling it's up to and including age 3, at Europa-Park and
Phantasialand ages 0 to 3, at Parc Astérix under 3. At Legoland Deutschland
children under 2 are free, and at Walibi Holland children up to 2, so until
their third birthday. At Heide Park and Toverland it's height that counts, and
children under 90 centimetres are free. Walibi Belgium (under one metre) and
Plopsaland Deutschland (under 85 centimetres) go by height too. As of
8 October 2026.

### Which theme park is best for a baby?

Efteling has the most detailed information on its website: changing cubicles in
all restrooms, three nursing and pumping rooms, a rental stroller for €5 and
Baby Switch on 19 rides. Legoland Deutschland has its own area in the Baby
Service, with a changing table, nursing area and kitchen. At Toverland, children
with a companion can go on several rides from 0 centimetres, so with no minimum
height at all.

### What is Baby Switch?

One parent rides while the other stays with the child who's too small, then rides
afterwards without queuing again. Parks handle it differently: at Efteling staff
give you a ticket when you get off, at Europa-Park a card, and at Heide Park it's
a pass you get at the Service Center.

### Can I take a stroller into a theme park?

Yes, at every park that says anything about it, but not onto every ride.
Phantasialand only allows narrow strollers and buggies up to 105 by 75 by 125
centimetres and bans wagons. At Toverland, Legoland and Plopsaland you can bring
your own wagon. At Plopsa Indoor, strollers aren't allowed on busy days.

### How much does it cost to rent a stroller at a theme park?

At Phantasialand €3 plus a €20 deposit, at Efteling €5, at Europa-Park €5 plus a
€50 deposit, at Legoland €6 plus a €4 deposit, at Walibi Holland €10 plus a €50
deposit and at Parc Astérix €15. Toverland has no rental.

### How small can a child be to ride a roller coaster?

Each park sets that ride by ride. On children's and family rides, kids with a
companion can ride from 0 centimetres at Toverland and Walibi Holland, and from
50 at Walibi Belgium (the page gives no unit). At Legoland Deutschland, minimum
heights start at 80 centimetres according to our data, and at Plopsaland
children can ride on their own from 85 centimetres. The minimum height of each
ride is on its page at park.fan.
