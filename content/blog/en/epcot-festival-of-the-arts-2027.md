The Festival of the Arts at [EPCOT](ref:epcot) runs from 15 January to Monday 1 March 2027. At 46 days it's a week longer than in 2026, when it ran from 16 January to 23 February. The trading card game Disney Lorcana presents it for the second year running.

## Collecting Lorcana cards

The quest booklet is free at the Creations Shop while supplies last. The Collection Quest asks for four cards, and you get them by trading with other players in the park, so it's not much fun if you're shy. You trade the full set for the card "Minnie Mouse – Musical Artist" at the Illumineer's Lounge in the Morocco pavilion or at Figment's Inspiration Station in The Odyssey. The Ink & Find Scavenger Hunt has you looking for oversized festival cards around the park, and the reward is "Belle – Reflective Writer" at normal card size.

Disney sells the Curator's Collection, six cards in a limited run, only at the festival. One of them shows Figment, his first Lorcana card ever. An Illumineer's Trove from the Hyperia City set comes with the card "Alice – Tea Alchemist", one per purchase. That only works in store: at Disney Traders, at Souk-al-Magreb in Morocco and at the Disney Lorcana TCG Festival Market. Disney hasn't published prices for the Curator's Collection or the Trove yet.

Lorcana artists and the game's two designers are at the festival on twelve days between 20 January and 24 February, from 11:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. each time. If you want a particular card signed by the person who drew it, the [Walt Disney World artists page](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/events-tours/epcot/epcot-international-festival-of-the-arts-artists/) lists the day they're there.

## Broadway concerts at the America Gardens Theatre

The concerts run every evening at 5:15, 6:30 and 7:45 p.m. at the America Gardens Theatre next to The American Adventure and are included in park admission. In 2026 the whole festival came with park admission; for 2027 Disney has so far confirmed that only for the concerts. Seats are first come, first served. Six pairs of performers take turns, changing almost every day:

- Michael James Scott and Marisha Wallace: 15, 17, 18, 21, 22, 25 and 26 January
- Ashley Brown and Josh Strickland: 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 27 and 28 January
- Adam Pascal and Sherie Rene Scott: 29 and 31 January, 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9 February
- Sierra Boggess and Norm Lewis: 30 January, 2, 3, 6, 7, 10 and 11 February
- Kara Lindsay and Mason Reeves: 12, 14, 15, 18, 19, 22 and 25 February
- Caissie Levy and Ryan McCartan: 13, 16, 17, 20, 21, 23, 24 and 26 February
- Finale "Seize the Day! A Newsies Celebration": 27 and 28 February and 1 March, cast still to be announced

Marisha Wallace, Adam Pascal, Sherie Rene Scott, Norm Lewis, Mason Reeves and Ryan McCartan are singing at the America Gardens Theatre for the first time. Brown and Strickland mark 20 years of "Mary Poppins" and "Tarzan" on Broadway. Michael James Scott has played the Genie on Broadway longer than anyone else.

At CommuniCore Hall, Disney shows the exhibition "Once Upon A Stage: The Music of Disney on Broadway". In 2026 it was "Once Upon A Stage: The Magic of Disney on Broadway", so Disney swapped one word, and this time it's about the songs. The hall also has Disney on Broadway merchandise for the first time, from "Hercules", "Aladdin", "The Lion King" and "Beauty and the Beast".

## Dining packages with a guaranteed seat

You can book the dining packages from 3 December 2026, two weeks earlier than last year. A meal at one of 14 restaurants comes with a guaranteed seat at a concert the same day, with its own queue. The restaurant hands you a badge with the date and showtime. The package covers a main course with dessert or a buffet, plus one drink, which can be alcoholic if you're 21 or over.

Adults aged ten and up pay from $38 at Regal Eagle Smokehouse to $104 at Le Cellier Steakhouse. For children from three to nine it's $23 to $52, all before tax and tip. Regal Eagle only takes bookings for the same day, and park admission is on top. You'll need a credit card on file to book.

## Art to look at and join in

Among the artists exhibiting at the festival, Haley Talton, Nicky Nicotera and Robert Post are new, Darren Wilson is back, and Stephanie Laberis and William Silvers are exhibiting too. At the Animation Academy in the America Gardens Theatre, Disney artists show you how a character comes together on paper. In the Expression Section you help paint by numbers on five murals, one of them with a Lorcana motif.

The festival turns ten, and for the occasion the bridge to World Showcase gets two new Lorcana photo backdrops, "Champion Card Collage" and "Merlin's Shop and Smithy". Right next to them are the Chalk Art Galleries, full of pictures in pavement chalk. Festival food comes from 19 Food Studios, whose menus are still to come.
