## New

- Press the bell on a ride card, set a number of minutes, and you get a push notification once the
  wait falls to it (8 September). The bell beside a show time reminds you before it starts.
  `/alerts` lists every alert this browser has, and from 9 September the favourites menu lists them
  too, each with a button to remove it.
- Pick two days in the crowd calendar and a dialog compares them: the verdict in one sentence, then
  each reason with both values and the difference, and from there straight into the planner
  (10 September).
- The day view in the calendar draws the hour-by-hour curve for today and tomorrow, in the park's
  own time zone (14 September). The chart had been built but never appeared, because the page
  never asked for its data.

## Improved

- The planner works under a finger (10 September). On the shortest block, the grip had a touch area
  20 px tall.
- A ride page shows since when a ride has been down and for how long (9 September). Its card on the
  park page had the first half, while the ride page said only "temporarily closed".

## Fixed

- The bell beside a show time signed you up for every performance of that show, while the dialog
  named one. It registers the one you pressed (9 September).
