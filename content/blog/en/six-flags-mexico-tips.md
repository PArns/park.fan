[Six Flags México](ref:six-flags-mexico) sits on the southern edge of Mexico City, in the borough of Tlalpan, on the road that climbs up towards the Ajusco. The address is Carretera Picacho-Ajusco km 1.5. The park opened in 1982 as Reino Aventura and has been called Six Flags México since 14 April 2000. It covers about 45 hectares, and since the Speedway Stunt Coaster opened on 31 January 2026, ten roller coasters have been running there.

On the big rides the queues are shortest when the gates open at ten, and they grow right into the evening. If you don't turn up until after lunch, the busiest part of the day is still ahead of you.

## Opening hours

Six Flags México is open all year round, just not every day. Outside the school holidays and events the park skips some weekdays. The open days are in the calendar on the Six Flags website and in our [wait-time calendar](ref:six-flags-mexico?calendar). The park usually opens at 10 a.m., and the closing time changes with the event.

### Christmas in the Park 2026

From 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027, the park's hours for Christmas in the Park are:

- 26 to 29 November: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- 30 November and 1 December: closed
- 2 to 6 December and 8 to 23 December: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., closed on 7 December
- 24 December: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- 25 December: 11 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- 26 to 30 December: 10 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- 31 December: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- 1 January 2027: 11 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- 2 to 17 January: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., closed on 7 January and from 11 to 14 January

Christmas in the Park is included in regular admission and in every season pass. A few attractions cost extra, the ones with snow among them.

Before the event starts, the park still opens in November on the 20th and 25th from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and on the 21st and 22nd from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.

### Festival del Terror

The Halloween event is called Festival del Terror, and in 2026 it ran from 3 September to 16 November. Eight Atracciones de Terror cost extra, with no exception for pass holders either. Two of the eight houses were new in 2026, JASON Blood Reign and THE STRANGERS No Escape, and THE CONJURING Universe came back rebuilt. Three Zonas de Espanto, the Cortejo Siniestro parade and, for kids, the Kids Boo Fest came at no extra charge. Six Flags recommends the festival for ages 13 and up, and anyone older than twelve isn't allowed to wear a mask or a costume.

The five busiest days we've measured at Six Flags México all fell during the festival, between 24 October and 16 November 2026.

## When it's quietest

```stats-widget slug=six-flags-mexico show=weekdays

```

Your shortest queues are from Monday to Wednesday, and on a Tuesday they're a little shorter still than on the other two. The weekend is busiest, Saturday more than Sunday, and Thursday and Friday sit in between, closer to the start of the week. If you get to choose, make it a Tuesday, and check the calendar beforehand to make sure the park is actually open that day.

```stats-widget slug=six-flags-mexico show=months

```

Except for December, which we haven't measured on enough days yet, September, February and June are the months with the smallest crowds. October and November, with Festival del Terror, are the busiest, followed by May and January.

### School holidays and public holidays

It gets busier during the SEP school holidays, and in winter they cover almost the whole Christmas season. For the 2026/27 school year the dates are:

- winter break: 21 December 2026 to 5 January 2027, with 6 January off school as well and classes back on 7 January
- spring break: 22 March to 3 April 2027, with Easter on 28 March
- summer holidays: from 10 July 2027

In 2027 the public holidays on Monday 1 February and Monday 15 March make for long weekends. There's no school on 5 May either.

### Rainy season

In Mexico City most of the rain falls from May to October, the most in July and August, and the south of the city, where the park is, gets more than the north. The storms usually arrive in the afternoon. If a storm closes the rides, Six Flags gives you neither your money back nor another day. November to April is the dry season, and December is the driest month. Mornings up on the Ajusco are chilly then, though, and without a jacket you'll feel it first on Superman El Último Escape at 120 km/h.

## How busy it gets, ride by ride

You'll queue longest at Medusa Steel Coaster and Crazanity, then at Superman El Último Escape. Medusa and Crazanity are where walking straight over at opening pays off most. At Superman, Batgirl Batarang, Batman The Ride, the Triple Torre Kilahuea, The Dark Knight Coaster and The Joker you also queue less in the first hour than later on, but the gap is smaller than at Medusa and Crazanity. At the weekend, an early start gets you a lot more than it does on a weekday.

At Medusa the queue jumps around midday and then stays at almost the same length until evening. Crazanity, on the other hand, keeps getting busier into the early evening. On most rides the busiest hour doesn't come until the evening. So Medusa and Crazanity belong in the morning, because in the evening you'll queue at least as long for both as at midday.

## A day in the park

Say you're at the gate at ten on a Saturday and you don't have a Flash Pass. Here's the order I'd ride in:

- First Medusa Steel Coaster, the ride whose queue climbs most steeply in the morning.
- Then Crazanity.
- Next, Superman El Último Escape and Batgirl Batarang.
- Batman The Ride, The Joker and The Dark Knight Coaster before noon.
- Early in the afternoon, Río Salvaje, and in the rainy season before the storms roll in.
- In the evening, when almost all the queues are at their longest, shows and dinner.

Loose items aren't allowed on any ride, so no phone, cap, camera or bag. Small things go in a bag with a zip or a belt bag. If something falls into the restricted area on any coaster except the Speedway Stunt Coaster, or in Justice League, you won't get it back until the park closes.

## The roller coasters

[Superman El Último Escape](ref:six-flags-mexico/superman-el-ultimo-escape?bare) is the tallest, fastest and longest roller coaster in Mexico. D.H. Morgan Manufacturing built it, and it went into service on 19 November 2004, though Six Flags itself says 2005. The train is hauled up 67 metres, drops 62.5 metres at an angle of 60 degrees and is doing 120 km/h at the bottom. It needs a little over three minutes for its 1,700 metres of track, all without a single inversion. The ride was supposed to open in 2002, but a dispute between Six Flags and the Mexican government held up construction for two years.

[Medusa Steel Coaster](ref:six-flags-mexico/medusa-steel-coaster?bare) stood in the park from 2000 to 2013 as a wooden coaster by Custom Coasters International. Rocky Mountain Construction rebuilt it with its IBox track, and since 14 June 2014 it has run as a hybrid coaster, according to Six Flags the only one in Mexico. RCDB lists 29.9 metres of height, 914 metres of track, 93 km/h and three inversions, while Six Flags gives 36 metres and about a kilometre.

```glossary-widget slug=hybrid-coaster

```

[Batman The Ride](ref:six-flags-mexico/batman-the-ride?bare), a Vekoma Suspended Looping Coaster, has been part of the park since it reopened as Six Flags México. Hanging below the 33-metre track, you go upside down five times, and no coaster in Mexico has more inversions. At up to 80 km/h the train covers 689 metres in 1:36 minutes.

[Batgirl Batarang](ref:six-flags-mexico/batgirl-batarang?bare) is a Vekoma Boomerang, and according to RCDB the very first one the manufacturer built. It ran in Puebla from 1984 and has stood in the park since 1988, where it was called Boomerang at first, then Escorpión, and Boomerang again from 2000. Since 2025 it has gone by Batgirl Batarang, with a new train and new brakes. The train climbs 35.5 metres and runs at up to 76 km/h through a cobra roll and a loop, first forwards, then backwards. Six Flags counts both directions, and that's how it gets to six inversions.

```glossary-widget slug=boomerang

```

The [Wonder Woman Coaster](ref:six-flags-mexico/wonder-woman-coaster?bare) is an S&S Worldwide 4D Free Spin and has run since 1 June 2018. The seats hang off the sides of the track and flip head over heels during the ride. A vertical chain lift pulls the car 36.6 metres up, the track is 310 metres long, and the car's top speed is 61 km/h. If you want to ride a 4D Free Spin in Latin America, Six Flags says you have to come here.

[The Dark Knight Coaster](ref:six-flags-mexico/the-dark-knight-coaster?bare) is a Mack Rides wild mouse inside a building. It stood at Six Flags New England until 2008 and has run in Mexico City since 19 March 2009. One lap of 370 metres takes two minutes.

[The Joker](ref:six-flags-mexico/the-joker?bare), a Gerstlauer spinning coaster, also moved here from the US and ran at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom from 2008 to 2012, first as Tony Hawk's Big Spin, then as Pandemonium. Since 6 March 2013 it has been spinning in Pueblo Francés, 16 metres tall, 412 metres long and at up to 50 km/h. Before you board, you walk through a hall of mirrors.

```glossary-widget slug=spinning-coaster

```

The [Speedway Stunt Coaster](ref:six-flags-mexico/speedway-stunt-coaster?bare) is the tenth and newest coaster in the park and has run since 31 January 2026. It's a Vekoma Family Boomerang with one train for 20 riders that goes forwards and backwards. It drops 23 metres, reaches 60 km/h and runs on more than 200 metres of track.

[Tsunami](ref:six-flags-mexico/tsunami?bare), a Zierer family coaster, was built in 1981 according to RCDB, a year before Reino Aventura opened, and was called Catarina Voladora until 2001. It's 8 metres tall and 360 metres long, and its top speed is 36 km/h. The [Superman Krypton Coaster](ref:six-flags-mexico/superman-krypton-coaster?bare) is a Vekoma junior coaster from 1993, formerly Roller Skater, with 207 metres of track and a height of 8.5 metres. It runs at 35 km/h according to RCDB, 45 according to Six Flags.

### Towers, a pendulum and water

[Crazanity](ref:six-flags-mexico/crazanity?bare) is the tallest pendulum ride in the world, according to Six Flags. It swings up to 50 metres high and reaches 120 km/h. It opened on 12 March 2020.

```glossary-widget slug=pendulum-ride

```

The [Triple Torre Kilahuea](ref:six-flags-mexico/triple-torre-kilahuea?bare), an S&S drop tower, has stood in the park since the reopening in 2000, just like Batman The Ride. It's more than 60 metres tall. On [Supergirl Sky Flight](ref:six-flags-mexico/supergirl-sky-flight?bare) you spin round a 74-metre tower at up to 64 km/h. If you look for older videos, you'll find the swing ride under SkyScreamer, its name until 2018.

You'll get wet on [Río Salvaje](ref:six-flags-mexico/rio-salvaje?bare), a river rapids ride that only runs in some seasons. Inside the building of [JUSTICE LEAGUE: Battle for Metropolis](ref:six-flags-mexico/justice-league-battle-for-metropolis?bare) you've been riding in a car and shooting at villains since 2016. The SlingShot and the Skycoaster X-Flight cost extra.

- 1.40 m: Triple Torre Kilahuea
- 1.32 m: Crazanity
- 1.30 m: Medusa Steel Coaster, Superman El Último Escape, Batman The Ride
- 1.22 m: Wonder Woman Coaster

- 1.20 m alone, 90 cm with a companion: Superman Krypton Coaster, Río Salvaje, Pato Lucas Blaster

- 1.22 m alone, 1.07 m with a companion: Justice League: Battle for Metropolis

- extra charge: SlingShot from 1.15 m, X-Flight from 1.07 m, go-karts from 1 m as a passenger and from 1.50 m behind the wheel

## Flash Pass

Six Flags México sells THE FLASH Pass, which gets you into the Fila preferente. It comes in two tiers:

- Flash Pass Regular for three rides, from 300 pesos
- Flash Pass Platino for ten rides, from 700 pesos

The price depends on the day, and online it can differ from the one in the park. The pass is sold in the souvenir shops and online, in limited numbers. It's only valid on the day you bought it for, it's tied to one person, and you can't return it.

The Flash Pass works on every coaster except the Superman Krypton Coaster and the Speedway Stunt Coaster, plus Crazanity, Supergirl Sky Flight, Río Salvaje and Justice League: Battle for Metropolis. The website also shows the Flash Pass for the Triple Torre Kilahuea and the Speedway Stunt Coaster.

At opening you don't need one. I'd buy the Regular on a Saturday if I weren't arriving until midday, for Medusa and Crazanity, which have the longest queues by then. The Platino only on a day during Festival del Terror or between Christmas and New Year.

## Tickets and season passes

Online, a day ticket costs from 1,149 pesos, and the price changes with the date. At the ticket office Six Flags charges 1,500 pesos for everyone over 1.20 metres and 599 pesos for children between 91 centimetres and 1.20 metres. Children under 90 centimetres get in free. INAPAM members and people with disabilities pay 299 pesos and pregnant women 599 pesos, but those discounts are only available at the ticket office.

Six Flags doesn't refund tickets or move them to another date. If you leave the park, you need a new ticket to get back in.

The 2027 Pases Anuales are valid from the day you buy them until 31 December 2027, on all regular operating days, and include Festival del Terror and Christmas in the Park, though not the extra-charge attractions:

- Silver for 1,700 pesos: Six Flags México and Hurricane Harbor Oaxtepec, 10 percent off food and souvenirs, no parking
- Gold for 1,800 pesos: ten parks, with Six Flags Magic Mountain, Knott's Berry Farm, California's Great America, Six Flags Discovery Kingdom and four water parks in the US on top of Six Flags México and Hurricane Harbor Oaxtepec, plus parking for one car per visit
- Prestige for 2,999 pesos: every Six Flags park in Mexico, the US and Canada, preferred parking, 15 percent off, one Flash Pass ride per visit and the drinks plan

A day ticket that cost 750 pesos or more can be turned into a season pass on the same day, at the Módulo de Servicio a Socios in Pueblo Mexicano.

> [!PARKY] Gold over Silver
> If we're driving, we take Gold: it costs only 100 pesos more than Silver, and the parking that Silver makes you pay for is worth 300 pesos on the very first visit. From the second visit of the year, Silver and Gold both work out cheaper than two day tickets, and because a day ticket can still be converted on the same day, the decision can wait until after the first churro.

## Parking

Parking costs 30 pesos an hour for cars, or 300 pesos for the whole day. You only get the day rate if you pay before 2 p.m. Buses pay 40 pesos an hour or 330 pesos a day. Once the car park is full, Six Flags closes it, to pass holders as well. You can still walk into the park then.

## Getting there

By car, you take the Periférico Sur. Coming from the north, take the exit for TV Azteca and Six Flags after the Hospital Ángeles, turn right onto the Carretera Picacho-Ajusco and then left at the second set of traffic lights. Coming from the south, drive past Perisur to the Blvd. de la Luz–Picacho exit, go under the Periférico and again turn left at the second set of lights. From the Zócalo it's about 23 kilometres.

Without a car, take Metro Line 3 to the end of the line at Universidad, which from the Zócalo means one change, from Line 2 at Hidalgo. From Universidad, buses and microbuses run to the park, and the city's tourism site puts the "Cruz del Farol" microbús at about 40 minutes. Buses that pass the park show destinations such as Sto. Tomás, Bosques, Cuchilla and Santa Teresa. From the Central del Sur bus station, the DM3 trolleybus runs to the corner of Av. Copilco and Av. Universidad, and from there lines 87 and 125 stop at the entrance.

## Food and what you can bring

You can't bring your own food or drinks. One bottle of water of up to 1.5 litres per person is allowed, and so are baby food and food for a medically necessary diet. Cool boxes stay outside, and so do glass, selfie sticks, GoPros and drones.

Inside the park you can only pay by card, contactless included. If you've brought cash, you can swap it for a cashless card at kiosks.

For 2027 Six Flags sells the Plan de Bebidas for 759 pesos, good for one soft drink every 15 minutes. The Plan de Alimentos Básico, with 20 meals at one every two hours, starts at 1,999 pesos, but you can only get it with a 2027 season pass.

There are lockers in Pueblo Mexicano opposite the teacups, in Pueblo Francés opposite the carousel, in Pueblo Vaquero opposite the Saloon Victoria and in Villa Hollywood by the Justice League exit. They cost extra, and there aren't many of them.

## History

Reino Aventura opened on 3 March 1982. What made the park famous was the killer whale Keiko, who lived there from 1985. In 1993 "Free Willy" was filmed in the park with him, and in January 1996 he flew to the Oregon Coast Aquarium in the US.

On 4 May 1999 Premier Parks, which had owned Six Flags since 1998, bought the park for $59 million. At the time, according to the buyer, it drew about 1.7 million visitors a year. On 14 April 2000 it reopened as Six Flags México, with Batman The Ride, the Triple Torre Kilahuea and the wooden Medusa. Six Flags leases the land from the city, until 2034. Since the merger with Cedar Fair on 1 July 2024, the park has belonged to Six Flags Entertainment Corporation. When Six Flags sold seven parks in March 2026, Six Flags México wasn't one of them.

In the TEA and AECOM index for 2019 it came first in Latin America, with 2,803,000 visitors. Beto Carrero World in Brazil has been ahead since 2023. In both 2024 and 2025 Six Flags México had a little over two million visitors and came second.

## Frequently asked questions about Six Flags México

### What time does Six Flags México open?

Usually at 10 a.m., and at 11 a.m. on 25 December and 1 January. The park is open in every month of the year, but outside the school holidays and events not on every day of the week. The operating days are in the [wait-time calendar](ref:six-flags-mexico?calendar).

### What time does Six Flags México close today?

During Christmas in the Park, from 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027, usually at 10 p.m. From 26 to 30 December and on 1 January it closes at 11 p.m., and on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve at 6 p.m. Today's opening hours are on the [park page](ref:six-flags-mexico).

### How busy is Six Flags México today?

Today's crowd level and the current wait time for every ride are live on the [Six Flags México park page](ref:six-flags-mexico). The park is busiest on Saturdays, in October and November during Festival del Terror, and in the winter school holidays from 21 December to 5 January.

### What is the least busy day at Six Flags México?

Tuesday, followed by Monday and Wednesday. Across the year, September, February and June are the quietest months. Whether the park is open on the day you have in mind is in the calendar.

### How much is a ticket to Six Flags México?

From 1,149 pesos online, depending on the date. At the ticket office everyone over 1.20 metres pays 1,500 pesos, and children between 91 centimetres and 1.20 metres pay 599 pesos. Children under 90 centimetres go free. The 2027 Silver season pass costs 1,700 pesos, and Gold, with parking, 1,800 pesos.

### How much is the Flash Pass at Six Flags México?

From 300 pesos for three rides with the Regular, and from 700 pesos for ten with the Platino, depending on the day. You can only redeem it on the day you bought it for, at Medusa, Crazanity and Superman El Último Escape among others.

### How much is parking at Six Flags México?

30 pesos an hour, or 300 pesos for the day if you pay before 2 p.m. With the Gold and Prestige season passes, parking is free.

### Can you bring food into Six Flags México?

No. The only things allowed are water, up to 1.5 litres per person in one bottle, baby food and food you need for medical reasons. Cool boxes aren't allowed.

### How do you get to Six Flags México without a car?

Take Metro Line 3 to Universidad and then a bus or microbús to the park, which takes about 40 minutes according to the city. From the Central del Sur bus station, the DM3 trolleybus runs to Copilco, and from there bus line 87 or 125.

### When is Christmas in the Park 2026?

From 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027, mostly from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. The park is closed on 30 November, 1 and 7 December, 7 January and from 11 to 14 January. The event is included in regular admission.
