## New

- 119 parks with enough measured days (as of 21 September) have a statistics page: crowds by month
  and by weekday over two years, the rides ranked by typical wait, and the typical day hour by
  hour, with a note on how many opening days the numbers come from.
- The info card on a park page links to Google Maps and Apple Maps. Both links are built from the
  park's coordinates, so a park we have no address or website for gets a route to its gate as
  well. In a sample of 40 parks, 19 had none of those facts and showed no card before.
- This changelog. Until 21 September the only record of what changed was an internal log in the
  repository, written for the people who work on the code.

## Improved

- The live crowd badge in a park's panel opens the whole scale: all six levels with the percentages
  behind them, the current one highlighted.
- A ride closed for a refurbishment has a badge that says so, beside its season badge, on the card
  and on its ride page. Before, a headliner behind hoardings until March looked like a ride having
  a bad morning.
- Efteling's steam train station has a transport badge, so a queue for the next departure is not
  read as a queue for a popular ride.
- In the planner, the overview counts down to the next planned day, and a ride you tick off shows
  how the measured wait compared with the forecast.
- Eleven fixes to the planner on phones: the sheet rests flush under the header, and delete sits on
  the selected block's own card (20 September).
- Photos that are not our own show a credit badge in their corner.
