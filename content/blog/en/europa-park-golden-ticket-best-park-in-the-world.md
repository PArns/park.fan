[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) won the Golden Ticket Award for Best Park for
the eleventh time on 12 September 2026, the title for the best theme park in
the world. The ceremony took place in Austin, Texas, at COTALand, a new park
on the grounds of the Circuit of the Americas that hadn't opened yet. Dollywood
came second, Universal Epic Universe third.

## Who hands out the award

The Golden Ticket Awards have existed since 1998 and are run by the trade
magazine Amusement Today. In 2026 there were 22 categories, decided by an
international poll with more than 500 ballots. The voters are people from the
industry, not the public. Dollywood took four Golden Tickets, the most of the
evening, among them Most Beautiful Park and Best Guest Experience.

The same month, the magazine Kirmes & Park Revue named the park "Europe's Best
Theme Park" again at the European Star Awards, according to Europa-Park.

## Three coasters in the steel ranking

Besides the awards, Amusement Today publishes a ranking of the best steel
coasters. Fury 325 at Carowinds is No. 1 for the tenth year in a row. Three
coasters from parks on park.fan made the top ten:

- No. 5: The Ride to Happiness at [Plopsaland De Panne](ref:plopsaland-belgium)
- No. 8: [Taron](ref:phantasialand/taron) at Phantasialand
- No. 9: [Voltron Nevera](ref:europa-park/voltron-nevera-powered-by-rimac) at Europa-Park

According to Amusement Today, places 1 to 9 are the same as in 2025, so
Voltron Nevera keeps last year's rank. At Phantasialand, Chiapas was also
named best water ride in a park for the fourth time.
