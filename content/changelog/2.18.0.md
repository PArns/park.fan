## New

- The "next twelve months" overview opens a panel for a day on hover or tap: date, crowd level,
  expected wait, school holidays and public holidays. A click opens that day in the wait time
  calendar. The weekdays in the park header show their next six dates in the same way.
- A park that closed for good keeps its page and states the date and the reason.
- The browser tab of a ride page shows the current wait in front of the page title while the ride
  is running.
- A ride page with a wait of 15 minutes or more names up to three open rides in the same park
  with a shorter queue, each with its distance and the minutes saved.
- A ride page says what is usual at this time of day, for example "Usually about 40 min at this
  hour, rarely over 80".
- The ride list of a park page can be sorted by shortest wait.
- Country pages with six parks or more have an "Open only" filter and can order their parks
  quietest first or nearest first.
- The weather forecast shows the opening hours of each day, or "Closed".
- The search palette has tabs. Under "All", parks and rides come first, then guides and news.
- The day planner offers a reminder with your plan on the morning of the park day.
- A ride that does not run on the current weekday says "Not on the program today" and lists the
  days it usually runs.
- The glossary explains the median (P50) and P90 that every wait time table prints, and has 32
  new terms.

## Improved

- The cards in the shows tab list the next show first.
- When it rains, covered headliners come first in the weather banner.
- The day planner says when the school holidays of a past comparison day are not published yet.
- An update to a news post sits at the top of the post, and the menu and `/news` show an Update
  badge.
- The new-posts toast says what a post is, such as news or a new guide.
- A tile in the news and blog lists is one link as a whole.
- The quietest weekdays follow one rule everywhere, and the park header says "Monday to Friday"
  instead of "Werktags" in German. In 6 of 18 comparison tables the quietest-day column used to be
  dashes only, and none is now.
- Every page of a park has the same header, with its links in three bands.
- The card glass and "Opens at" sit in one row.
- The search box grows and shrinks over 300 ms instead of jumping.
- The glossary matches the rest of the site and opens a term at its definition. Ride lists on a
  term link every ride, including the 43 of 58 wooden coasters that had no link before.
- The changelog page shows which version you are reading.
- On a park page the burger menu opens in 352 ms instead of 728 ms, the filter sheet in 248 ms
  instead of 600 ms and the alert dialog in 240 ms instead of 616 ms, measured at 4x CPU slowdown.
- A park page loads 133 KB less JavaScript.

## Fixed

- "Not on the program today" no longer swaps in after the page has loaded. It used to appear first
  as "Not open yet today".
- Park cards no longer rebuild their grid when the distance appears.
- The AI forecast sparkline shows month names in the language of the page. On `/de` it said "Oct".
- The glossary names Red Force as the only giga coaster in Europe, and about 50 more statements
  in it were corrected, among them founding years and the maker of several rides.
- Figures and dates: the rope drop saving on a card and its badge show the same number, a median
  outage of 2 minutes no longer reads "0", averages no longer round to Disney's walk-on 13, "Opens
  in N weeks" rounds instead of rounding up, and two opening windows on one day show as one
  span.
