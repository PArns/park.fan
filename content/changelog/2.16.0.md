## New

- `/compare?parks=a,b` puts two to four parks side by side: typical wait, longest queue and quietest
  weekday. The choice is in the link, so you can share it.
- The bell on a ride offers a second alert: a message when the ride runs again after a closure or a
  breakdown. It needs no wait time, so a ride with no wait shown can have one too.
- The park page offers the opening hours as a calendar file, one event per open day. For
  Europa-Park that is 31 events on 6 October.
- Every ride card on the park page has a switch for "ridden". The park header counts "x of y rides
  ridden". The marks stay in your browser, with no account.
- Halloween mazes carry an orange badge and sit in their own group on the park page, after the
  countries.
- The day planner shows days beyond the forecast as "Earlier years": curves from comparable days of
  previous years, with a note that it is a look back and not a forecast.
- The park and ride pages say when a ride opens later than the park, "Opens at 10:00". At
  Phantasialand that is 23 of 34 rides. The park page also names Early Entry where the park has it.
- Inside a park, the park page shows the compass that was only on the home page, right under the
  title card.

## Improved

- The note about wait time alerts sits in the heading of the headliner section instead of on a card
  of its own. On Movie Park the page is 138 px shorter on a phone.
- The bell on a ride opens with that ride chosen. A ride that is standing offers both alerts, with
  "tell me when it reopens" preselected, and a running ride offers the wait time alert only.
- The planner says why a day has no curves, in six languages, instead of an empty grid.
- A running breakdown has a block of its own, on the ride card and on the ride page, in place of
  two small grey lines.
- Temperature, wind and rain show in the unit you picked, also in the weather tile, the home page
  hero and the calendar day dialog.
- "Guides" in the menu has a search field. It looks through 32 articles and shows at most eight
  hits.
- The new-posts toast shows the most recent news first, ordered by day and time.
- The chapters of the park page keep the same spacing on a phone as the other pages: 24 px.
- The planner's push settings have a label for the rain topic in all six languages.

## Fixed

- Disney's 13 minutes stay 13 minutes. They were rounded up to 15, so a ride you could walk onto
  looked like a short queue.
- A photo inside an article shows its photographer's credit, as the title image does.
- `/news` no longer slides sideways on a 360 px phone. The page was 362 px wide and is 360 px now.
- The credit line of the title image stays on the image on a phone. It used to sit below the first
  paragraph.
- An unknown blog, news or park address shows the 404 page. `/de/blog/gibt-es-nicht-xyz` and three
  other addresses answered with a 500.
