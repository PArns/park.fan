Christmas Studios, the winter season at [Parque Warner Madrid](ref:parque-warner-madrid) in San Martín de la Vega, opens on Saturday 21 November, a good two weeks earlier than last year. It runs until 10 January 2027. From 5 December the park adds a new bubble show, "El Reino de Cristal".

## New this winter

"El Reino de Cristal" lasts about 25 minutes and only plays in December. The show tells of an old kingdom that hope, anticipation and magic kept safe for centuries. Every bubble brings it back a memory or a wish. At the end a mime tries to light an old lantern, the kingdom's last light. He can't manage it alone, so he brings up a volunteer from the audience.

ACME Factory: Christmas Candies is open from 21 November. In the Christmas version of the children's attraction that opened for Halloween, kids help the Looney Tunes recover the secret recipe for the Christmas sweets. So the recipe has gone missing again: at Halloween it was the pumpkin sweets, after the chaos the Coyote had left behind.

Also new is the Estación Energía de Repsol, a hands-on walk through three rooms. First you get lost in a mirror maze, then you play with a light installation, and at the end you walk through recreated rays of sunlight.

## Shows, parade and Santa

The 22-metre Christmas tree is back at the entrance. The Looney Tunes switch it on in a show of their own, and lights and fake snow go up in all five themed areas.

On stage there's the musical The Santa's Magical Bell, the Looney Tunes Dance Festival in its Christmas Edition and, in the Western area, Una Navidad en Dodge City. From 5 December "Happy Holiday!", last year's dance show, is back on Hollywood Boulevard. In Christmas Town, a small Christmas village with elves and reindeer, Santa shows off his workshop in El Taller de Papá Noel, which the park pitches at the youngest visitors. The Three Kings receive visitors there too. Bugs Bunny, Tweety, Daffy Duck, the DC superheroes and Gizmo from Gremlins pose for photos in festive outfits. The day ends with the Superstars of Christmas Parade, where the same characters ride past once more on floats, Santa and the Three Kings included.

## Dates and opening hours

Christmas Studios is open on 29 days, always from noon:

- November: 21, 22, 28 and 29, until 8 p.m.
- December: 5 to 8, 12 and 13, 19 to 23 and 26 to 30, until 9 p.m.
- January 2027: 1 to 5 and 7 to 10, until 8 p.m.

Until mid-December the park opens only at weekends and on 7 and 8 December. Of all days, it's closed on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and 6 January, Three Kings' Day.

## Tickets

Online, a day ticket costs from €32.90 depending on the date, the same for everyone 100 cm and taller. At the gate you pay €62.90 over 140 cm and €56.90 from 100 to 140 cm; guests over 65 pay the children's gate price too. Children under 100 cm get in free. For several visits there's the Bono Parques annual pass. We've put its prices and the park's rain guarantee in our [Parque Warner Madrid guide](/blog/parque-warner-madrid-wait-times-tips). Parking costs from €16 online.

## What's changed since 2025

This is the third Christmas Studios. The second ran from 6 December to 5 January in 2025.

There were bubbles last winter as well. Back then, Bubble Christmas was new at the Teatro Chino, with the elf Bublinna, who collects the yawns children make before falling asleep and turns them into bubbles. Neither that show nor the Repsol Christmas Music Studio, where families could record their own Christmas song in 2025, appears on the 2026 programme. Repsol's slot now goes to the Estación Energía.

## Getting there

By car from Madrid, head south on the A-4 to exit 22; from there the park is signposted. Without a car, take Cercanías line C-3 to Pinto and then La Veloz bus 413, which stops at the entrance. Line 412 runs to the park from Villaverde Bajo. From Méndez Álvaro and Príncipe Pío, the park sells admission and the bus ride together.

## Queues and the Pase Correcaminos

Our [guide](/blog/parque-warner-madrid-wait-times-tips) breaks the queues down by ride and time of day. To skip them, there's the Pase Correcaminos, named after the Road Runner and sold on top of admission.
