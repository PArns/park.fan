[Efteling](ref:efteling) has put tickets on sale for its New Year's Eve party on 31 December 2026, which runs from 7 pm to 1 am and costs €62 a head. The event page promises rides in the dark, thousands of lights, the winter attractions, entertainment and fireworks.

On 8 October the park's blog still said tickets for the "Oud & Nieuw feest" would be "binnenkort verkrijgbaar", available soon. The evening can now be booked in the ticket shop.

## What the ticket covers

The ticket gets you an oliebol, something sparkling for the toast and the fireworks. The Dutch event page calls the drink a "blikje bubbels", a can of bubbly, and the German page translates it as "eine Dose Sekt". On the page for annual pass holders, though, the park writes "een glas bubbels", a glass.

Parking is included on New Year's Eve. On other days the Efteling car park costs €15, and guests at the hotels and holiday parks park free at their accommodation.

Each ticket admits one person aged four or over, once. On day tickets, children up to and including three get in free with ID. The ticket page doesn't say whether that also applies on New Year's Eve, so if you're bringing a toddler, check with the park first.

You can buy up to 19 tickets per order, and they can't be returned. Efteling normally gives 14 days to return tickets bought online at efteling.com, but it explicitly excludes dated and event tickets, and its FAQ names the New Year's Eve tickets as an example.

## With an annual pass

An Abonnement, Efteling's annual pass, gets you 25 percent off. The ticket then costs €46.50 and is a separate product in the ticket shop, limited to six per order. Every one of those tickets has to be shown at the gate together with a valid pass.

The pass itself isn't valid during the party ("Je abonnement is tijdens het Oud & Nieuw feest niet geldig", the park writes on its page of pass holder benefits), so even with an annual pass you need a New Year's Eve ticket.

Efteling only mentions the discount in Dutch, on the event page and on the pass holder page. The German and English event pages leave that section out. The discounted ticket is booked through the Dutch ticket shop.

## 31 December during the day

The New Year's Eve ticket only starts at 7 pm. During the day Efteling is open as usual on 31 December, from 10 am to 6 pm. The day ticket is sold separately and costs €56 for that date in the ticket shop. There's an hour between the end of the normal day and the start of the party.

From 19 to 30 December the park is open daily from 10 am to 8 pm, while on New Year's Eve it closes at 6 pm. The evening ticket, valid from 4 pm between 21 December and 3 January at €33, isn't sold for 31 December.

The day falls within Winter Efteling, which runs from 16 November 2026 to 31 January 2027. The outdoor coasters run as long as the weather allows. The park's maintenance list has no ride down for 31 December. Droomvlucht is closed for maintenance from 9 November to 4 December, Joris en de Draak from 30 November to 18 December, and the Pagode from 15 to 17 December.

For the other parks open between Christmas and New Year, see our [winter round-up](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

## How earlier New Year's Eves went

Efteling hasn't yet published which rides will run in the evening, what shows there will be or when the fireworks start in 2026.

For 2022 the park described the programme on its blog. From 11 pm DJs played at the Siervijver, the countdown began at 11:45 pm, then came fireworks with laser effects, and the DJs carried on until 1 am. Tickets cost €49 including parking that year, and pass holders paid €36.75. Admission ran from 7 pm to 1 am in 2022 as well.

The official map for 2024/25 marked, among other things, a silent disco, "Grootmoeders Bingo", "Bubbels en bollen" and the best spot to watch the fireworks.

The evening has sold out more than once. In 2022 the park announced the start of sales on 9 November, and on 3 December it added a line to the post saying all tickets were gone. In 2018 Efteling added the same line to a post about the party as early as 16 November.

## Staying overnight

There's a New Year's Eve package for overnight guests. It includes entry to the party, unlimited park admission during the stay, park entry half an hour early and breakfast. In the holiday homes at Efteling Bosrijk and Efteling Loonsche Land you arrive on 30 December and stay three nights, with breakfast delivered. Hotel rooms at the Grand Hotel, the Wonder Hotel, Bosrijk and Loonsche Land start on 31 December for two nights, with breakfast and a three-course dinner, drinks not included. Grand Hotel guests can pay extra to see in the countdown on the terrace of the Mystique restaurant-bar, with a snack and a drink; places are limited.

In its blog post of 8 October, however, Efteling wrote that no accommodation was left for Oud & Nieuw. The package page is still online, with a search form, no price and nothing to say it's fully booked.

## Getting there

Efteling is at Europalaan 1, 5171 KW Kaatsheuvel. By public transport, take the train to 's-Hertogenbosch or Tilburg and then, depending on direction, bus 300, 301, 136, 800 or 801 to the "Efteling" stop. From Brussels and Antwerp, Flixbus 811 runs direct to the park on Mondays and from Thursday to Sunday. Efteling's travel page lists no buses after 1 am on New Year's night. There's more on travel, tickets and hotels in our [Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).

The free Lijn 7 shuttle links the bus station at the main entrance with the Wonder Hotel, Villa Pardoes, Loonsche Land and Bosrijk. It runs Wednesday to Sunday from 8 am to noon and from 4:30 pm to 7 pm, so it's no longer running when the party ends at 1 am.
