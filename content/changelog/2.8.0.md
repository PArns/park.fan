## New

- Ride and park cards have a photo area, the trend, a small chart of today, the day's average
  wait, the best time to go and a countdown to closing (24 April). The homepage lists of the
  busiest and quietest parks and the longest and shortest waits use the same cards.
- A ticker along the bottom of the homepage photo runs through live waits with their trend, and
  the photo's credit panel links to the park it shows.

## Improved

- A closed park or ride has a grey card (1 May).
- The header is lower, and the homepage search was redrawn.
