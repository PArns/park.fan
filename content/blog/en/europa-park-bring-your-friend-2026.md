If you have a ResortPass, you can take a companion into [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) for free on any weekday until 18 December. The offer is called "Bring Your Friend". ResortPass Silver lets you do it once, Gold twice. The park announced it on the evening of Thursday 19 November in its Facebook group "Europa-Park Resort Freunde", and it's valid straight away.

## The free ticket is in the ResortPass portal

Your friend's ticket comes from the ResortPass portal at resort-pass.de. Log in with your MackOne account. The graphic that came with the announcement lists it as a "For friends" ticket at €0.00. There's a daily limit on these tickets, and some days may already be sold out. If you want to come with your friend on one particular day, say 18 December, the last day of the offer, book their ticket now rather than the week before.

You book your own visit in the portal as usual, because as a day guest you need a reservation for every visit with the ResortPass. You can hold up to five days at once, even on the day itself while places are left, and cancel any you won't use. Once a day is fully booked, neither the phone line nor the gate can help you. If you're staying in one of the resort's hotels or at Silver Lake City, your own reservation is already part of the hotel booking.

## 21 days to choose from

The park is open every day until 23 December. HALLOWinter runs until 27 November and Winterzauber starts on 28 November, both from 11 am until at least 7 pm. Hotel guests go in earlier. Because the announcement came out around closing time, Friday 20 November is the first full day of the offer. That leaves 21 weekdays up to Friday 18 December: six during HALLOWinter and 15 during Winterzauber.

Silver isn't blocked on any of the 21 days, since its blackout dates don't start until 26 December, after the offer ends. Gold has none at all.

## What your friend would pay otherwise

Every day of the offer falls in the cheaper of the two price categories, where an online day ticket costs €67 for adults and €56.50 for children aged 4 to 11 and for seniors aged 60 and over. At the ticket desk, add €10. With Gold, two adult friends save €134 between them, which makes the ticket price a thin excuse. Bringing a child under four wastes the offer, though, since they get in free anyway.

## HALLOWinter and Winterzauber

During HALLOWinter, Halloween pumpkins and Christmas trees share the park. From 28 November, Winterzauber adds:

- the Christmas market in the Historic Balthasar Castle Park
- an ice rink
- the Bellevue Ferris wheel
- a Christmas circus
- the Eurosat mapping show in the French area
- the "Alpin" children's ski school with snow tubes, and Skitty World Nordic, a cross-country skiing area for kids

The exhibition "Monaco im Europa-Park" is new. If one park isn't enough, you'll find the others opening this winter in the [overview of theme parks in winter 2026/27](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

The rides I'd start with on a winter day are in our [Europa-Park wait times guide](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips). If your friend brings kids, Arthur runs all winter. They can ride from 100 centimetres, under 120 only with an adult.

## You can't buy a ResortPass right now

The allocation has run out, so the park isn't selling new passes until further notice, not by phone, email or at the ticket desk either. If you have one, you'll be told before it expires and can renew it in the portal during the last 14 days.

In our [annual pass comparison](/blog/theme-park-annual-pass-comparison) we work out how many visits each pass needs to pay off. Silver is valid for day guests on more than 230 set days a year. Gold also comes with two day tickets for [Rulantica](/news/rulantica-vinterglanz-2026), which you reserve in the portal as well.
