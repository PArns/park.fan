[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) now sells its Quick Pass in the [park's online shop](https://shop.phantasialand.de/en/quick-pass?utm_source=park.fan&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=quick-pass-online). Once you've paid, it arrives as a PDF by email, and at the ride's Quick Pass entrance you show the QR code from it on your phone. Until now you could only get one at Guest Services on the Kaiserplatz, in the Berlin area.

A Quick Pass is valid only on the day you book it, and the top of the shop says "Valid today only" with the day's date. There's no rebooking and no refund either. So buy it on the day of your visit, in the morning on the way to Brühl or right after you're through the gates. A pass you order from the sofa the evening before is a nice-looking PDF the next morning and nothing more.

## How to buy the Quick Pass online

In the shop you pick the rides you want a pass for, or go straight for the Ultimate. The basket then holds the passes for ten minutes, and you finish the order within that time. Each pass is for one person, so a family of four needs four for Black Mamba. The passes are valid as soon as you've paid. The shop is in German, English, French and Dutch.

Open the QR code while you're in the queue and turn the brightness all the way up, so nobody at the Quick Pass entrance has to wait behind you while you scroll through your inbox. The pass for Taron After Dark only comes up in the evening, when after a cold Wintertraum day full of photos your phone often has hardly any battery left. So save a few percent.

The Quick Pass Ultimate costs €80. It gets you through every Quick Pass entrance once, and onto [F.L.Y.](ref:phantasialand/fly) faster once, which has no single pass. For Taron, the shop says the Ultimate covers "Daylight & After Dark".

## What else you need

The Quick Pass costs extra and only works with a valid day ticket. Day tickets are sold online only, since there's no ticket office, and for the day itself the park sells them until 2 pm. Turn up at the gate at half past two without one and you have a bigger problem than the queue for Black Mamba.

Even with a pass you don't get to pick your seat on the train, and anyone too short for Taron is just as short with a Quick Pass.

## When the entrance pauses or the ride is down

With a Quick Pass you queue for less time, not for no time, and the Quick Pass entrance may pause for a while when the ride is short on capacity. If a ride closes for technical reasons or because of the weather, you aren't entitled to a refund. Reselling is prohibited, and the park won't replace a lost pass.

## At Wintertraum

Wintertraum has been running since 14 November and goes on until 24 January 2027, daily from 11 am to 8 pm. The park is closed on 24 and 25 December, 1 January and 12 and 13 January.

The single pass for Taron After Dark is valid from 5 pm, when it has long been dark at Wintertraum. On New Year's Eve the park closes at 6 pm, and the Taron After Dark pass is then only worth it if you're at the ride by five.

## How many passes there are, and when one pays off

At Guest Services there were only so many, and at Wintertraum they were often gone by midday on weekends. Online the allocation is limited too: next to "Also available online", the screens in the park say "Limited availability". According to the park's info page, Guest Services sells them as before.

In my experience you don't need a Quick Pass on a green day in the calendar if you're at the gate when the park opens. On a deep-red Saturday in the school holidays I'd rather spend the €10 on Black Mamba than join its queue at midday. The order I ride in without one is in the [Phantasialand tips](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips).
