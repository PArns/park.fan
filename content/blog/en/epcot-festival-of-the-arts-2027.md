The Festival of the Arts at [EPCOT](ref:epcot) runs from 15 January to 1 March 2027, which takes it into March for the first time, if only for a single Monday. At 46 days it's a week longer than in 2026, when it ran from 16 January to 23 February. The trading card game Disney Lorcana presents it for the second year running, and the Disney on Broadway concert series is included in park admission. Disney announced the line-up on the Disney Parks Blog on 19 November.

## Collecting Lorcana cards

The quest booklet is free at the Creations Shop while supplies last. The Collection Quest asks for four cards, and you get them by trading with other players in the park, so it's not much fun if you're shy. Turn in the full set at the Illumineer's Lounge in the Morocco pavilion or at Figment's Inspiration Station in The Odyssey and you get the card "Minnie Mouse – Musical Artist". The Ink & Find Scavenger Hunt has you looking for oversized festival cards around the park, and the reward is "Belle – Reflective Writer" at normal card size.

The Curator's Collection is sold only at the festival, six cards in a limited run. One of them shows Figment, his first Lorcana card ever. Buy an Illumineer's Trove from the Hyperia City set at Disney Traders, at Souk-al-Magreb in Morocco or at the Disney Lorcana TCG Festival Market and you also get the card "Alice – Tea Alchemist", one per purchase and in store only. Disney hasn't published prices for the collection or the trove yet.

Each week a different Lorcana artist or one of the game's two designers comes to the festival from 11:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Cristian Romero starts on 20 January, and Malia Ewart is the last, on 24 February. On their days the artists sign the cards they drew.

## Broadway concerts at the America Gardens Theatre

The concerts run every evening at 5:15, 6:30 and 7:45 p.m. at the America Gardens Theatre next to The American Adventure. Seats are first come, first served. Six pairs of performers take turns, changing almost every day:

- Michael James Scott and Marisha Wallace: 15, 17, 18, 21, 22, 25 and 26 January
- Ashley Brown and Josh Strickland: 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 27 and 28 January
- Adam Pascal and Sherie Rene Scott: 29 and 31 January, 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9 February
- Sierra Boggess and Norm Lewis: 30 January, 2, 3, 6, 7, 10 and 11 February
- Kara Lindsay and Mason Reeves: 12, 14, 15, 18, 19, 22 and 25 February
- Caissie Levy and Ryan McCartan: 13, 16, 17, 20, 21, 23, 24 and 26 February
- Finale "Seize the Day! A Newsies Celebration": 27 and 28 February and 1 March

Wallace, Pascal, Sherie Rene Scott, Lewis, Reeves and McCartan are on this stage for the first time. Brown and Strickland mark 20 years of "Mary Poppins" and "Tarzan" on Broadway, and Disney introduces Michael James Scott as the longest-running Genie on Broadway. Disney hasn't said yet who sings at the Newsies finale.

At CommuniCore Hall, Disney shows the exhibition "Once Upon A Stage: The Music of Disney on Broadway". In 2026 one with almost the same name stood there, about the magic rather than the music. It's also where Disney sells merchandise from "Hercules", "Aladdin", "The Lion King" and "Beauty and the Beast" for the first time.

## Dining packages with a guaranteed seat

You can book the dining packages from 3 December 2026, two weeks earlier than last year. A meal at one of 14 restaurants comes with a guaranteed seat at a concert the same day, with its own queue. The restaurant hands you a badge with the date and showtime. The package covers an entrée with dessert or a buffet, plus one drink, which can be alcoholic if you're 21 or over.

For adults, meaning ten and up, prices range from $38 at Regal Eagle Smokehouse to $104 at Le Cellier Steakhouse. Children from three to nine pay $23 to $52, in both cases before tax and tip. Regal Eagle can only be booked on the same day. Park admission isn't part of the package, and you'll need a credit card on file to book. For 2027 Disney names only the concerts as included in admission, whereas in 2026 the whole festival counted as part of park admission.

## Painting, chalk and 19 Food Studios

New among the festival artists are Haley Talton, Nicky Nicotera and Robert Post, and returning ones include Darren Wilson, Stephanie Laberis and William Silvers. At the Animation Academy in the America Gardens Theatre, Disney artists teach drawing classes. In the Expression Section you help paint by numbers on five walls, one of them with a Lorcana motif.

The festival started in 2017. For its tenth anniversary, the bridge to World Showcase gets two new Lorcana photo backdrops, "Champion Card Collage" and "Merlin's Shop and Smithy", alongside the chalk drawings of the Chalk Art Galleries. There are 19 Food Studios serving festival food, and Disney hasn't published the dishes or prices yet.

School holidays, public holidays and the other events in these weeks are in our [Orlando itinerary guide](/blog/orlando-itinerary-disney-universal), along with Disney and Universal tickets.
