By road, [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) in Rust and [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl are about 395 kilometres apart, just under four hours by car without traffic, which is long enough for the back seat to argue out which of the two is better. Rust sits north-west of Freiburg right on the Rhine, with France on the other bank, while Brühl lies between Cologne and Bonn.

Europa-Park opened in 1975 as a showground for Mack, a ride manufacturer from Waldkirch. Its themed areas are European countries, with Monaco added in 2026, and at 95 hectares it's the bigger of the two parks.

Phantasialand started out in 1967 as a fairy-tale forest in the Grube Berggeist, a worked-out open-cast lignite mine in the south of Brühl, and it has never moved out of that pit. Car parks and hotels included, it comes to about 28 hectares, the park itself to around 12, and on those the rides stand shoulder to shoulder in themed areas such as Klugheim and Rookburgh.

With children under 1.30 metres, or for a two-day visit, I'd go to Europa-Park. For a single day of big coasters I'd pick Phantasialand, on a weekday outside the school holidays in North Rhine-Westphalia.

Every day has a price of its own at Phantasialand, and the ranges in the table cover the days you can book up to 23 December. ## Where the queues are shorter

Since the end of December 2025 we've read off the wait time of every single ride at both parks every five minutes. What that looks like by month, weekday and time of day is on the statistics pages for [Europa-Park](/en/parks/europe/germany/rust/europa-park/average-wait-times) and [Phantasialand](/en/parks/europe/germany/bruehl/phantasialand/average-wait-times), and the [park comparison](/en/compare?parks=europa-park,phantasialand) puts every ride at both parks side by side.

Part of the reason the queues are shorter at Europa-Park is that it has twice as many coasters and its headliners [move more riders an hour](/blog/the-art-of-waiting). The park states 1,600 riders an hour for Voltron and 1,620 for Silver Star. According to the roller coaster database RCDB, Taron manages 1,200 and F.L.Y. 1,440.

Phantasialand’s Quick Pass lets you buy your way past the regular queue. For a single ride it costs

- €5 on Colorado Adventure, Mystery Castle, Talocan, Maus au Chocolat or Die 3 Mausketiere,
- €7 on Winja’s Fear or Winja’s Force,
- €10 on Black Mamba or Chiapas,
- €12 on Taron or River Quest.

F.L.Y. comes only with the Quick Pass Ultimate, which costs €80 and includes one go on each of those rides plus F.L.Y. You can only buy it at Guest Services in the Berlin themed area, and there are a limited number.

At Europa-Park you book a free time slot in the park app instead, for blue fire, Euro-Mir, Piraten in Batavia, Poseidon, Voletarium, Voltron or Wodan and go somewhere else in the meantime. One ticket holds a slot at only one attraction at a time, the app works only inside the park, and if you turn up late for your slot you lose it. On busy days, the park says, the slots go quickly. Arthur, blue fire, Eurosat, Voletarium, Voltron and Wodan also have a single rider queue for anyone riding alone and happy to fill an empty seat.

## When both parks are quiet

From 20 July to 7 August 2026, 14 to 16 of Germany’s states were on holiday at the same time. In those weeks the queues for the big rides at Phantasialand were 96 per cent longer than on a weekday outside the holidays, and 37 per cent longer at Europa-Park ([every holiday week compared](/blog/school-holidays-theme-parks-germany)).

Saturday is Europa-Park’s busiest day, while a Sunday outside the holidays costs you only 9 per cent more waiting there than a weekday with no holidays. At Phantasialand, switching to Sunday doesn't buy you much: the queues there ran 36 per cent longer on Sundays and were just as long on Saturdays. The weekdays barely differ.

### Europa-Park

In the calendar below, Europa-Park's holidays include those in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Hesse and Rhineland-Palatinate and also the school holidays in Alsace, Basel and Aargau. In the 2026 summer season, May and June were the quietest months and August the busiest.

In 2027 Europa-Park opens from 7 to 19 March for two Pre-Opening Weeks. Not every attraction and show will be running, and day tickets cost €59.50 on weekdays and €70 at weekends. The summer season starts on 20 March, and the park puts tickets for it on sale from 10 November 2026.

At Europa-Park a child needs someone with them up to 1.30 metres on every ride in the 0.90, 1.00 and 1.20 metre bands, and up to 1.20 on Arthur and the Ba-a-a Express ([every ride by the height for riding without an adult](/en/parks/europe/germany/rust/europa-park/with-kids)). At Phantasialand that applies up to 1.40 metres on Taron, F.L.Y. and the Winja’s, up to 1.30 on Chiapas, and up to 1.20 on Colorado Adventure, Raik and Crazy Bats. Our page on [Phantasialand with kids](/en/parks/europe/germany/bruehl/phantasialand/with-kids) sorts its rides by the minimum height instead, like the table above.

Phantasialand offers Baby Switch on 13 rides, Taron, F.L.Y., Chiapas and Crazy Bats among them. Europa-Park offers it on its big coasters.

## For coaster fans

The tallest and fastest ride in either park is Europa-Park’s [Silver Star](ref:europa-park/silver-star), at 73 metres and 127 km/h according to the RCDB; the park itself says 130. Europa-Park also has the only wooden coaster of the two, the 40-metre [Wodan](ref:europa-park/wodan-timburcoaster). On [Voltron Nevera](ref:europa-park/voltron-nevera-powered-by-rimac) you're launched four times, once backwards after the train has been turned round on a turntable. It takes [blue fire](ref:europa-park/blue-fire-megacoaster) 2.5 seconds to go from 0 to 100 km/h. If you want one more ride on [Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir), this winter season is your last chance, and after that the park is building a new space-themed area in its place, due by 2028.

![Looking up into Wodan’s timber structure where two stretches of track cross. | 21,000 beams and two million nails hold this together. | right](/media/europa-park/wodan-timburcoaster-4x3.jpg)

At Phantasialand, [Taron](ref:phantasialand/taron) races through the Klugheim area with two launches and a top speed of 117 km/h. Face down beneath the track, in roughly the pose Superman flies in, you lie on [F.L.Y.](ref:phantasialand/fly), a launched flying coaster and the only ride of its kind at the two parks. Like Silver Star, [Black Mamba](ref:phantasialand/black-mamba) was built by Bolliger & Mabillard, but it hangs below the track and turns you upside down four times. The spinning coasters [Winja’s Fear & Force](ref:phantasialand/winjas-fear) run indoors.

![A rock arch and waterfall in Deep in Africa, Black Mamba’s lift hill behind. | Black Mamba’s track runs low between the rocks and huts. | left](/media/phantasialand/black-mamba-4x3.jpg)

Two of Europa-Park’s 14 coasters are water coasters, Poseidon and Atlantica SuperSplash. On [Arthur](ref:europa-park/arthur) you hang beneath the track for four and a half minutes through the kingdom of the Minimoys. At Phantasialand, [Crazy Bats](ref:phantasialand/crazy-bats), which opened in 1988 as Space Center, has run with VR headsets since 2019, and [Mystery Castle](ref:phantasialand/mystery-castle) shoots you up a 65-metre tower at 72 km/h before dropping you back down in free fall.

## When it rains

On rainy days the wait times at Phantasialand were 11 per cent shorter than on dry days, and 5 per cent shorter at Europa-Park ([weather and wait times in twelve parks](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain)).

![Eurosat’s silver sphere above the Moulin Rouge facade. | The whole ride runs inside that sphere. | right](/media/europa-park/eurosat-cancan-coaster-4x3.jpg)

Ten of the covered attractions at [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?bare) are stations of the EP-Express, the Panoramabahn and the monorail. Among the others under a roof are the Eurosat CanCan Coaster, Voletarium, Piraten in Batavia, Madame Freudenreich Curiosités, Castello dei Medici and Abenteuer Atlantis. At [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand?bare), Winja’s Fear & Force run in Wuze Town, an indoor area, Crazy Bats in a closed hall and Mystery Castle inside its tower. On a wet day the "Covered" filter on each park's page finds all of them.

## In winter

During Winterzauber, Europa-Park has a Christmas market in the Historischer Schlosspark Balthasar, an ice rink, the Bellevue Ferris wheel and a children’s ski school with snow tubes. The park promises up to 23 hours of shows a day over the winter, a Christmas circus among them. From November it sells an evening ticket for entry from 4 pm. Adults pay €41.50 until 22 December and €47 after that, children €35 to €40, and there's no extra charge at the gate. On 10 January the park opens only to hotel guests and invited guests.

At Europa-Park, Fjord-Rafting and the Tiroler Wildwasserbahn run only until the end of the Halloween season on 1 November, and Atlantica SuperSplash until HALLOWinter ends on 27 November. Poseidon is on the schedule in all four seasons.

![A lit swing carousel turning, a light tree at the left. | Wintertraum, Phantasialand’s winter season. | left](/media/phantasialand/background-4x3.jpg)

Every evening at around 7:50 pm during Phantasialand’s Wintertraum, the "Magic Symphony" show plays on the Kaiserplatz in the Berlin area, and there's an ice rink in Berlin too. According to the park, Taron runs down to minus ten degrees, Black Mamba, Chiapas and Colorado Adventure down to minus five. River Quest, Wakobato and Wözl’s Wassertreter are closed during Wintertraum. A day at Phantasialand costs adults €35 to €59 in November and December.

## One day or two, and the hotels at the parks

A second day at Europa-Park costs adults only €60 to €67.50 more online, with the two-day ticket at €127 to €143.50. Phantasialand gives no discount on a second day.

For a second day in Rust there's also Rulantica, the resort’s water park, open all year. It has its own tickets: for visits up to 19 March 2027, €38 to €54 during the day and €35 to €46.50 from 5 pm.

Europa-Park has six hotels, from El Andaluz in 1995 to Krønasår in 2019, plus Silver Lake City, a western town with tipis, covered wagons and a campsite. In the summer and Halloween seasons hotel guests get into the park from 8:15 am, 45 minutes before everyone else, and from 10 am during HALLOWinter and winter. If you stay overnight you get tickets at the lowest price: €67 for one day, €127 for two and €160 for three.

Phantasialand has three hotels: Ling Bao with an Asian theme, Matamba with an African one, and Charles Lindbergh in Rookburgh, where no one under eight can stay. Phantasialand doesn't mention earlier park entry for its hotel guests. The Charles Lindbergh ticket gives you faster access to F.L.Y. once a day through a separate entrance. At Matamba and Ling Bao each person gets one faster entry per night to Chiapas, Maus au Chocolat, Talocan, Winja’s Fear & Force or Colorado Adventure.

By our measurements, early entry at [Europa-Park](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips) saves you the most time at Voletarium. At [Phantasialand](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips) it's Taron and Winja’s Fear, and there the rides open in the summer season at 10 am, an hour after the gates.

## Getting there

At weekends the RailCoaster runs to Europa-Park from Basel and Zurich. If you fly, Lahr is the closest airport at 20 kilometres, Strasbourg and Karlsruhe/Baden-Baden are 64 kilometres away, Basel 90. Drivers can reserve a space at the main entrance for €32, motorbikes park for free, and motorhomes over 5.50 metres pay €20 a day.

The shuttle bus from Brühl station takes about 20 minutes to Phantasialand. The ticket covers the return trip, and regional transport tickets aren't valid on the shuttle. Without the shuttle, take bus 985 to the Berggeiststraße stop and walk about ten minutes from there. Satnavs sometimes send you to the Bornheim exit on the way to the park. Overrule yours and leave at Brühl-Süd, which is what the park asks. Phantasialand has no pitches for motorhomes.

Europa-Park has lockers at the main entrance: a small one costs €1, a large one €4, and every time you open it again it's another €1. A €5 wristband from Guest Services on the Kaiserplatz lets you use a locker at Phantasialand all day, in Berlin or in Mexico at the Colorado Adventure exit.

## Both parks on one trip

By car the drive between the two parks takes just under four hours without traffic, going by OpenStreetMap’s routing. In the summer season Phantasialand closes at 6 pm at the earliest, during Wintertraum at 8 pm, so you can drive in the evening and be in Rust the next morning.

I'd use a Friday and the weekend for it. Go to Phantasialand on the Friday, before its busy weekend starts. I'd spend Saturday, Europa-Park’s busy day, in the car, where at least you're sitting down while you wait, and go to Europa-Park on Sunday.

## What’s coming next

On 8 October 2026 Europa-Park announced three new rides for 2027, all for families, among them a Wild Swing XL giant swing in Portugal. The park hasn’t named opening dates yet.

Phantasialand wants to grow by about 15 hectares to the west, onto land set aside for it in the regional plan since 2013. According to the park the plans include an aqua park hotel resort, rides, a theatre, multi-storey car parks and green space. The Netzwerk Naturschutzgebiet Ententeich is collecting signatures for a [citizens’ petition](/news/phantasialand-expansion-petition) against the decisions on the expansion that Brühl’s planning and urban development committee took on 9 July 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better, Europa-Park or Phantasialand?

Europa-Park with children under 1.30 metres, because more rides are open to them there. At 1.30 metres only Silver Star is still off limits at Europa-Park, while Black Mamba, River Quest and Talocan are still closed to them at Phantasialand. Europa-Park for two days as well, because a second day there costs €60 to €67.50, while at Phantasialand it costs the full day price.

### Where are the queues shorter, Europa-Park or Phantasialand?

At Europa-Park. Voltron Nevera usually has the longest queue there, Taron at Phantasialand.

### Which park is bigger?

Europa-Park, at 95 hectares against about 28 for Phantasialand, car parks and hotels included.

### Does Europa-Park have a fast pass like Phantasialand’s Quick Pass?

No. It has a free Virtual Line in the park app at seven attractions, Voltron, Wodan and blue fire among them, and single rider queues at six.

### How far apart are Europa-Park and Phantasialand?

288 kilometres as the crow flies and about 395 by road, just under four hours by car without traffic. Europa-Park is on the A5 at exit 57b Rust, Phantasialand on the A553 at exit Brühl-Süd.

### Is there an annual pass for Europa-Park or Phantasialand?

Europa-Park isn't selling its ResortPass in October 2026. Phantasialand no longer sells an annual pass. The park took its Club-Karte off sale during the pandemic, and the last ones ran out in summer 2022.

### Are Europa-Park and Phantasialand open in winter?

Yes, both are, apart from a few closing days. Europa-Park is open until 9 January 2027, Phantasialand until 24 January 2027.

### How much does it cost to get into Europa-Park and Phantasialand?

At Europa-Park an adult day ticket costs €67 to €76 online, at Phantasialand €35 to €69 depending on the day. Children under four go free at both parks.
