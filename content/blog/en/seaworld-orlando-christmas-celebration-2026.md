[SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando) opens its Christmas Celebration on November 6, and with it comes Miracles, a nighttime orca show set to Christmas music that the park hasn't run since 2019. The event runs until January 4, 2027, on 16 days in November alone. It's included with regular park admission and with an annual pass, and you don't need to reserve anything.

Pass Members, as SeaWorld calls its annual pass holders, get a performance of Miracles all to themselves. SeaWorld hasn't published a time or place for Miracles yet. The event's showtimes are in the park's free app.

## Event days and hours in November

The Christmas Celebration runs on 16 days in November:

- Friday, November 6, 13 and 20: 10 am to 9 pm
- Saturday, November 7, and Sunday, November 8: 9 am to 9 pm
- Saturday, November 14: 9 am to 10 pm; Sunday, November 15: 9 am to 9 pm
- Saturday, November 21: 9 am to 10 pm
- Sunday, November 22, through Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving): 9 am to 9 pm
- Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28: 9 am to 10 pm
- Sunday, November 29: 9 am to 9 pm

On the remaining days, November 9 to 12, November 16 to 19 and November 30, the park is open only from 10 am to 6 pm and there's no Christmas program.

Every event night through January 1 ends at park close with Holiday Reflections: Fireworks Finale over the lagoon. Plenty of the program is indoors and survives a Florida downpour, though the fireworks over the lagoon don't count.

## What admission covers

Elmo and friends lead the Sesame Street Christmas Parade through Sesame Street Land, and the Seaport Theater has Elmo's Christmas Wish Show. O Wondrous Night puts more than 30 Christmas songs on stage with life-size puppets and live animals. The ice show Winter Wonderland on Ice plays on selected days from November 7 to January 5, past January 4. Clyde and Seamore get a Christmas show of their own, Clyde & Seamore's Countdown to Christmas.

At the lagoon, the trees of Sea of Trees light up in time to Christmas music around a 70-foot (21-meter) tree in the middle. Snow falls along the Waterfront, not something Orlando usually manages in November. There's a miniature Christmas train layout at Dolphin Nursery.

Santa is at Waterway Grill, with photographers on hand. Rudolph and Bumble are in Rudolph's Christmastown on Pipeline Pathway. SeaFire Grill shows Christmas movies, and you don't have to order anything to watch.

## Extras, food and tickets

There's an ice rink in Bayside Stadium right on the lagoon, at an extra charge. Dinner with Santa in the Sea Harbor Pavilions and the Sesame Street Christmas Breakfast at SeaFire Grill also cost extra. The dinner runs on selected days, though not on opening weekend.

For the first time there are fire pits where you can toast marshmallows and build s'mores with chocolate and graham crackers. The new dishes include ropa vieja festiva with tostones, three-cheese ravioli in a chardonnay cream sauce, and beignets with Lotus Biscoff cookie butter. The dishes cost extra, outside food and coolers stay outside, and a water bottle is fine.

For shopping, there's a Christmas Market this year for the first time.

In the online Black Friday sale a day ticket starts at $59.99, valid for ages three and up; the regular price is $151.99. If you plan on more visits in 2027, the same sale has the Fun Card from $104.99, good for unlimited visits all year apart from blockout dates. Free military tickets are valid during the event as well.

## Holidays in December and January

- Hanukkah, December 4 to 12: the menorah is lit every evening in Sesame Street Land, and Big Bird tells Hanukkah stories
- Kwanzaa, December 26 to January 1: the kinara is lit every evening in Sesame Street Land
- New Year's Eve, December 31: fireworks to ring in the new year
- Three Kings Celebration, January 3 to 6: food and performances for Epiphany in the Nautilus Theater

The park is at 7007 Sea World Drive. The Christmas programs at Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando are in our guide to [Christmas and New Year's in Orlando and Paris](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026).
