## New

- Italian became the sixth language on 27 February, with 564 translated keys.
- A new logo and icon set, with a large version of the logo on the homepage (20 to 23 February).
- A guide page, "How it works", walks through search, favourites, the badges, the calendar, the
  predictions and location, with small working copies of the real controls. It went online in
  German and English on 10 March and in the other four languages a day later.
- The homepage has a band of featured parks with their live status, crowd and waits, a different
  selection for each language (5 March), and a short text about park.fan.

## Improved

- Live data reloads when you come back to the tab or your connection returns: every five minutes
  for open parks, every hour for closed ones (15 February).
- The header is transparent over the homepage photo and fades in as you scroll, and on park and
  ride pages the photo reaches up behind it.
- On narrow screens the middle of a long breadcrumb folds into "…", which opens on a tap (3 March).
  A park's name no longer appears twice in it.
- Status and crowd badges share one soft style, the search dialog shows status badges in its
  results and keyboard hints, and light mode got more contrast.

## Fixed

- Park pages could be scrolled sideways on phones.
- City addresses used names instead of slugs, so Brühl is `bruehl` now.
- The hover values on the small wait charts.
