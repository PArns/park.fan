Of the **nine theme parks in Germany** on park.fan, we measure the wait times at six,
ride by ride. Not a single number comes in for [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park), because the park
only shows them in its own app. [Traumatica](ref:traumatica), for which we have only four measured days so far, and the water world
[Rulantica](ref:rulantica) belong to the Europa-Park resort and
so don’t appear as parks of their own.

## How I sort them

For coaster fans, what counts is the rides I want to ride once I’ve arrived, how they’re built
into the grounds and how much waiting they cost. For families, what counts is the height at which
kids can ride and what stays open on a rainy day. The numbers next to it
are our own measurement, and the technical data comes from the parks, from
the Roller Coaster DataBase and from our guides to the individual parks, which are linked further
down.

I leave prices out.

## For coaster fans

**1. [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park), Rust.** On 12 September 2026 the park won the Golden Ticket
Award for “Best Park” in Austin for the eleventh time. Industry people voted, not the public.
The park has fourteen roller coasters, among them
[Voltron Nevera](ref:europa-park/voltron-nevera-powered-by-rimac), which is ninth in Amusement
Today’s steel coaster ranking, and the [Silver Star](ref:europa-park/silver-star). The Silver
Star is 73 metres tall, runs at 127 km/h and shares both titles with Kärnan at Hansa-Park. With
6.2 million guests in 2024 according to the TEA index, Europa-Park is the biggest park in the
country. The [Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir) runs for the last time this winter, and the
park has announced a farewell event on 9 January 2027.

Voltron opened in April 2024 and launches you four times, once of them backwards, after the train
has been turned round on a turntable halfway through the course. That’s 1,385 metres, 100 km/h
and seven inversions, with nothing but a lap bar holding you in. Next to it stands the wooden
coaster [Wodan](ref:europa-park/wodan-timburcoaster) from 2012, forty metres tall, built by Great
Coasters International out of 21,000 beams and two million nails. The list goes on with
[blue fire](ref:europa-park/blue-fire-megacoaster), the park’s first launch coaster from 2009,
the [Eurosat](ref:europa-park/eurosat-cancan-coaster), which has been running in the dark inside
its silver sphere since 1989, and the water coaster
[Poseidon](ref:europa-park/water-rollercoaster-poseidon). The themed areas are built as countries and strung out in a long chain. If you want to
ride all fourteen coasters in a day, you walk a long way, from Croatia to Ireland for instance.

The park doesn’t sell a fast pass. There are single-rider queues at six attractions, a free
Virtual Line in the app for some rides and, for hotel guests, early entry 45 minutes ahead of
everyone else in the summer season. In 2026 the season runs without a break from 28 March to
9 January 2027, with two days closed at Christmas. By car it’s the A5, exit 57b Rust; by train
you go to Ringsheim and take the shuttle bus from there. Six themed hotels belong to the resort,
plus Silver Lake City with tipis, covered wagons and log cabins.

**2. [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand), Brühl.** The theming is the best of the parks on this list.
[Taron](ref:phantasialand/taron) is eighth in the steel coaster ranking, and
[Chiapas](ref:phantasialand/chiapas-die-wasserbahn) was named best water ride in a park by
Amusement Today for the fourth time. Its 2.1 million guests a year are spread across eight headliners, so the queues are long. The park
still gets second place because its rides are built into the themed areas.

Taron launches you twice through Klugheim, a village of basalt columns that doubles as the queue.
[F.L.Y.](ref:phantasialand/fly) in Rookburgh is a flying coaster you board standing up and ride
lying on your stomach, through a Victorian aviators’ workshop. [Black Mamba](ref:phantasialand/black-mamba) is an inverted
coaster from 2006 that passes close to rocks and huts. Add the mine train
[Colorado Adventure](ref:phantasialand/colorado-adventure) from 1996, the two spinning coasters
Winja’s Fear and Winja’s Force and the 65-metre tower [Mystery Castle](ref:phantasialand/mystery-castle).

Seven themed areas are packed into a small space: Berlin, China Town, Deep in Africa, Mexico,
Fantasy, Mystery and Rookburgh. Tickets are sold only online and for a set date; there’s no ticket
desk any more. For an extra charge, the **Quick Pass** gets you onto a single ride faster, or the Quick
Pass Ultimate onto every ride once, and only Ultimate covers F.L.Y. Three themed hotels stand at
the park: Charles Lindbergh in Rookburgh, Ling Bao and Matamba. Unlike in Rust they don’t get you
in earlier, but the Hotel Charles Lindbergh has its own entrance to F.L.Y. once a day. By car it’s the
A553, exit Brühl-Süd, or take the train to Brühl and the shuttle bus from there.

**3. [Heide Park](ref:heide-park), Soltau.** [Colossos](ref:heide-park/colossos-kampf-der-giganten)
is the tallest pure wooden coaster in Europe and has run since 2001. The 103-metre
[Scream](ref:heide-park/scream) stands next to it, and the park was the first to bring four
coaster types to Germany. In spring and autumn it regularly stays closed on Mondays.

The four firsts are Limit from 1999, now [Toxic Garden](ref:heide-park/toxic-garden), the
country’s first suspended looping coaster; Desert Race from 2007 as the first launch coaster;
[Krake](ref:heide-park/krake) from 2011 as the first dive coaster; and
[Flug der Dämonen](ref:heide-park/flug-der-daemonen) from 2014 as the first wing coaster.
[Big Loop](ref:heide-park/big-loop) from 1983 is, at 43, the oldest coaster in the park, and for
the 2026 season it got new trains styled on the original cars. Colossos stood still from 2016 to
2018 because the glued wooden track was worn out after fifteen years. Since then it has run as
“Kampf der Giganten”, straight through a giant who breathes fire on the last hill.

Here the paid queue skip is called the **Express Ticket** and comes in four tiers from Bronze to
Platinum, with one ride per attraction in the lower three. It’s only sold on selected days, in
2026 mostly at weekends and in the school holidays. The season ends on 31 October. By car it’s the
A7 between Hamburg and Hanover, exit Soltau-Ost, and a bus runs from Hamburg and Hanover right to
the gate. At the park there’s the Abenteuerhotel and the Holiday Camp with 81 wooden cabins.

**4. [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park), Sierksdorf.** On 46 hectares you’ll find the
[Oath of Kärnan](ref:hansa-park/the-oath-of-kaernan) and the
[Highlander](ref:hansa-park/highlander), the tallest gyro drop tower in the world. In the 2026
season, from 26 March to 25 October, there isn’t a single closing day. The park is fourth because
I can’t say how long you’ll queue there.

Kärnan, built by Gerstlauer, hauls you straight up in a tower, on your back and in the dark,
and at more than 60 metres lets the train drop backwards a little before the first drop from
67 metres. [Flucht von Novgorod](ref:hansa-park/escape-of-novgorod) from 2009 goes from zero to
100 km/h in 1.4 seconds and later drops at 97 degrees, past vertical.
[Nessie](ref:hansa-park/nessie) by Anton Schwarzkopf has run since 1980 and was Europe’s biggest
looping coaster at the time. The Highlander is 120 metres tall and took the record off Scream in
Soltau in 2019. For Herbstzauber am Meer around 2,000 illuminated figures stand in the park, in 2026 from
10 to 25 October, open until 9 pm. You
can get there on the regional train from Lübeck to Sierksdorf and a ten-minute walk, or by car on
the A1. In 2027 the park turns fifty and is building an 86-metre observation tower for it.

**5. [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany), Bottrop.** Opened in 1996 as Warner Bros. Movie
World, and under its current name since 2005. In our measurement the queues are shorter than at
Phantasialand, and Sunday is one of the quieter days here.

The biggest of the park's eight roller coasters is
[Star Trek: Operation Enterprise](ref:movie-park-germany/star-trek-operation-enterprise) by Mack
Rides from 2017: 40 metres tall, 90 km/h, 130 centimetres minimum, and before it sets off the
train is flung forwards and back three times until it has enough speed for the first hill.
[The Bandit](ref:movie-park-germany/the-bandit) from 1999 was Germany’s only wooden coaster when it
opened, and after 27 years it rattles hard. The
[Movie Park Studio Tour](ref:movie-park-germany/movie-park-studio-tour) from 2021 is a coaster dark
ride by Intamin with two launches and a turntable, and
[Van Helsing’s Factory](ref:movie-park-germany/van-helsings-factory) has the longest queue in the
park, because the ride only gets through 848 people an hour.

In 2026 the Halloween Horror Festival runs from 26 September
to 8 November, and 8 November is also the last day of the season. Since September the park opens
only Thursday to Sunday outside the holidays. From 27 November to 5 January Movie Park’s Hollywood
Christmas follows.

Feldhausen station is about 400 metres from the entrance, closer than at any other park on this
list, served by the RE14 from Essen and the RB43 from Dortmund.

## For families with children under ten

**1. [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland), Günzburg.** The park has been open since 2002. In winter 2026/27 it opens on selected days between November and January for the fourth
time, with an ice rink and shows, and most outdoor rides then say “open depending on weather”.
There are individual closing days in May, June and September 2026.

The park has eleven themed areas, the newest being Lego Mythica from 2023. That’s where you’ll find
[Maximus](ref:legoland-deutschland/maximus-the-guardians-flight), a wing coaster by Bolliger &
Mabillard and the first in any Legoland: 17 metres tall, 54 km/h, from 120 centimetres with an
adult, and alone from 130 centimetres and age eight. Before that come the [Feuerdrache](ref:legoland-deutschland/fire-dragon) by Zierer
from the opening year, from 110 centimetres and age six with an adult, and the
[Drachenjagd](ref:legoland-deutschland/dragon-hunt) by Gerstlauer, which takes four-year-olds from
one metre. [Flying Ninjago](ref:legoland-deutschland/flying-ninjago) is a Sky Fly from 2012 where
you spin your own gondola round its axis.

According to Wikipedia, Miniland is made of more than 25 million bricks, and around two million guests came in 2024. The 2026 season runs from 28 March to 8 November, with Halloween from
3 October to the end of the season. Here the paid queue skip is called **Fastrack**, in three
tiers, for 18 attractions and without admission. You can stay in the holiday village right next
to the park, in the Pirate Island Hotel, in three castles, in the Ninjago quarter or, since 2024,
in the Waldabenteuer Lodge. By car it’s the A8, exit Günzburg, or take the train to Günzburg and
the shuttle bus from there.

**2. [Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland), Haßloch.** Until June 2025 the park was
called Holiday Park. In our measurement it has the shortest queues of the six parks in the table,
by a wide margin. In October the Halloween Fright Nights run here on ten evenings, and the autumn
festival for kids runs during the day.

The Schneider family opened the park in 1971. In November 2010 it was bought by Studio 100’s
Belgian Plopsa group, and since 28 June 2025 it has carried their name. Its 40 hectares hold
Majaland, Wickieland and the other areas built around the children’s TV characters the parents
remember from their own childhood. New this season is
[100% Wolf](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/family-coaster-100percent-wolf), a family coaster by
Gerstlauer with 760 metres of track, a switch and a backwards section, from one metre with an
adult. [Tabalugas Achterbahn](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/tabalugas-rollercoaster) from 2018 is
the smaller of the two.

For the parents there’s [Expedition GeForce](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/bigfm-expedition-geforce),
an Intamin from 2001 that reaches 120 km/h and, according to the park, has collected more than 40
awards, among them the Golden Ticket Award for best coaster in Europe. It turns twenty-five in
2026 and asks for 140 centimetres. Then there’s [Sky Scream](ref:plopsaland-deutschland/sky-scream)
by Premier Rides, from 140 centimetres and age fourteen.

In 2026 the park is open every day until 1 November, which it says is the longest season in its
history. It sells its queue skip as the **Express Pass**, for eight attractions. There’s no hotel
at the park; it lists about 25 partner hotels in the area. In season a shuttle bus runs from
Haßloch station, and by car the park is close to the A65.

**3. [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park).** Of the eight roller coasters, four are family or kids’
coasters. The Kleine Zar and the Schlange von Midgard take children from 90 centimetres, and so
does the Cornwall Coaster, which opened in 2026.

The [Cornwall Coaster](ref:hansa-park/new-2026-cornwall-coaster) opened on 23 May 2026, 18 metres
tall, 50 km/h, and the park counts seven launches per lap, forwards and backwards. At Hansa-Park many rides set an age next to the height, from which a child may
ride alone: six on the Schlange von Midgard, eight on the Cornwall Coaster, and not until ten on
Nessie and the Royal Scotsman. A seven-year-old who is 1.25 metres tall can therefore ride seven of
the eight coasters, but four of them only with an adult alongside.

**[Heide Park](ref:heide-park?bare), Soltau.** It isn’t a family park, but it has a corner for the youngest in Peppa Pig
Land, and Schorschs Dino-Abenteuer takes children from as little as 86 centimetres. The big rides
there ask for 140 centimetres.

## Where you queue the shortest

The comparison is calculated the same way for every park, across all measured days. The rows are in the order of the 3 October 2026 measurement and aren’t re-sorted.

```

Europa-Park, Heide Park and
Movie Park are close together, even though Europa-Park has the largest visitor numbers. It spreads them across more rides, so even its longest queue doesn’t get as long.

For Legoland the column with the longest queue is missing. We only put a ride in that column from 100 measured days, and at Legoland none of the ten rides
with the highest median reaches that.

How long a queue gets also depends on how many riders a ride can carry in an hour. According to the park, Voltron sends out a train every 36 seconds and handles 1,600. Van Helsing’s Factory in Bottrop runs at 36 km/h with single cars of four and manages 848.

A paid queue skip makes the wait longer for everyone without one. At Europa-Park, where there isn’t one, our median is therefore close
to what everybody experiences. At Phantasialand and Heide Park two groups stand in front of the
same ride on busy days, and without a pass you wait longer there than our median.

```

Across all measured days at Phantasialand, Taron is busiest at opening and in the last hour, when everyone wants to squeeze in one more
ride. F.L.Y. is short in the first hour and peaks an hour later, once the rest of the park has
made it to Rookburgh. Both have their quietest hour in the afternoon. So ride F.L.Y. at opening and Taron in the afternoon.

At Europa-Park the Eurosat has its longest queue as early as ten in the morning, because it’s near
the entrance and runs indoors, and when it rains everyone heads there at once.
At Heide Park, Colossos draws the crowds around midday, and at Movie Park the queue for Van
Helsing’s Factory doesn’t get shorter until evening.

## When to go

The quietest weekday is different for each park, and the table above names it. It
can be empty: for parks that close on several weekdays outside the main season, we don’t compare
days that rest on different parts of the year.

Saturday is the busiest day at Europa-Park, Phantasialand, Heide Park and Legoland, and in Brühl
it stands furthest above the rest of the week. There, Sunday is almost as busy; in Rust, Soltau
and Bottrop it’s one of the quieter days. For families who can only go at the weekend, Sunday is the better day, except in Brühl. At Plopsaland every day is equally quiet, Saturday included.

July and August are the busiest months at almost every park,
and at Phantasialand by a long way. September is one of the quietest in Brühl, Soltau and Bottrop,
because the summer holidays are over in most German states and the autumn holidays haven’t
started yet. The long weekends after
Ascension Day and Corpus Christi, on the other hand, are packed, and in our data for Europa-Park
that holds even when it rains.

At five of the six measured parks, a weekday in the home state's school holidays was busier
than an average weekend day. Which German states fill which park is in the
[guide to school holidays and crowds](/blog/school-holidays-theme-parks-germany).

## Two parks on one trip

The distances are straight lines between the entrances.

- Phantasialand and Movie Park, **91 kilometres.** Both
  are in North Rhine-Westphalia, both can be reached by train. It works especially
  well in autumn, because Phantasialand doesn’t do Halloween.
- Heide Park and Hansa-Park, **131 kilometres.** Soltau is south of Hamburg on the A7, Sierksdorf
  north of it on the A1. Hamburg sits in between, and if you stay there, each day starts with a
  drive of a good hour. In October this only works until the 25th, when Hansa-Park closes.
- Europa-Park and Plopsaland, **124 kilometres.** Rust and Haßloch both lie in the Upper Rhine
  valley. With a child, this beats two days in Rust, because the second day in Haßloch is shorter
  and calmer.
- Europa-Park and Rulantica, **in the same resort.** The water world needs its own ticket
  and has its own car park. For most people two days at Europa-Park are still the better choice,
  because with fourteen roller coasters and 96 attractions, there’s almost always something left over after
  one day.

I’d advise against Europa-Park and Phantasialand in one weekend. The 288 kilometres as the crow
flies mean a good four hours’ drive on the A5 and A61.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best theme park in Germany?

Europa-Park in Rust. In 2026 it won the Golden Ticket Award for best park in the world for the
eleventh time, with 6.2 million guests in 2024 it’s the biggest park in the country, and it has
fourteen roller coasters. For theming Phantasialand comes first, for small children Legoland.

### Europa-Park or Phantasialand?

If you want to fit as many roller coasters as possible into one day, go to Rust. If you want
Taron, F.L.Y. and themed areas where the queue is part of the set, go to Brühl and accept longer
waits for it. Phantasialand is smaller and has fewer rides to spread the crowd across.

### Which theme park in Germany is best for small children?

Legoland in Günzburg, followed by Plopsaland in Haßloch. Both are built for children up to about
ten, and at Plopsaland we measure the shortest queues of all the German parks we track. At
Hansa-Park, children from 90 centimetres can ride three of the eight roller coasters.

### Where are the wait times shortest?

At Plopsaland Deutschland, by a wide margin. They’re longest at Phantasialand. Europa-Park, Heide
Park and Movie Park sit in between and close together.

### What is the tallest roller coaster in Germany?

Silver Star at Europa-Park and the Oath of Kärnan at Hansa-Park share the title, both at 73 metres.
At 127 km/h Silver Star is also the fastest, level with Kärnan. The tallest wooden coaster is
Colossos at Heide Park.

### Which theme parks are open in winter?

Europa-Park until 9 January 2027, Phantasialand with Wintertraum until 24 January, Movie Park with
Hollywood Christmas until 5 January, and Legoland on selected days until 10 January. Heide Park
and Hansa-Park stay closed. Last winter Plopsaland opened part of the park at weekends, but it
hasn’t announced anything for this winter yet.

### Which theme park is easiest to reach without a car?

Movie Park. Feldhausen station is about 400 metres from the entrance. Europa-Park, Phantasialand
and Heide Park are served by a shuttle or bus from the nearest station, and Hansa-Park is a
ten-minute walk from Sierksdorf station.
