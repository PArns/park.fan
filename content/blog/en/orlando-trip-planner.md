On 8 December 2026 you'll have to leave [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park) at 6 pm if you hold an ordinary day ticket. Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party starts at 7 pm, and it needs a ticket of its own. The day before, the park stays open until 10 pm. We expect about the same crowds at the big rides on both days, which is what makes the 8th a trap: in an ordinary crowd calendar it looks as good as the 7th, it just has four hours fewer, and they're the ones with the fireworks.

For days like that, [park.fan/orlando](/orlando) now has a trip planner. You say when you're in Orlando and which parks you want to see. For ten days from 5 to 14 December, Magic Kingdom then gets Monday the 7th, one of the five days without a party.

## Every Orlando park in one calendar

The same page puts Walt Disney World, Universal with Epic Universe, SeaWorld, their water parks and three day trips into one calendar, 16 parks in November 2026. Each square is one park on one day, and the first column is today in Orlando. The number in it is the minutes we expect at the park's big rides at the busiest time of day, and that's what you compare parks by. The colour is set another way, by comparing the day with a typical day at the same park, which is why a park with longer queues can still be green.

A folded corner means the park closes early for day guests, at Magic Kingdom for Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party and at Hollywood Studios for Disney Jollywood Nights. Hatched squares are parks closed for the season, in winter 2026/27 Typhoon Lagoon and Volcano Bay among them.

![The Orlando Crowd Calendar with 16 rows for the Disney, Universal and SeaWorld parks and the day trips, one square per day with the expected minutes. | On 13 November, Animal Kingdom gets the frame, not Magic Kingdom with the smaller number, because Magic Kingdom closes at 6 pm that evening for the party. | wide](/media/reiseplaner/orlando-kalender-en.webp)

At the very top you'll find a single day in Orlando time, with the park that has the shortest queues at each resort and a note when one of them closes early. Tap 8 December and you get the party with its start time, plus Early Entry, the drive and the five big rides. "Plan this day" opens the [day planner](/trip-planner) with the park and the date filled in.

Without traffic, EPCOT to Universal Studios is a 25-minute drive, LEGOLAND in Winter Haven just under an hour, and Busch Gardens and Adventure Island in Tampa about an hour and a half. Strictly speaking you don't drive to Magic Kingdom at all. You drive to its car park at the Transportation and Ticket Center, and from there it's another quarter of an hour by monorail or ferry, which is built into every trip there.

The three best-time pages for [Orlando](/orlando/best-time-to-visit), [Disney World](/orlando/best-time-to-visit/disney-world) and [Universal Orlando](/orlando/best-time-to-visit/universal-orlando) open on the three quietest ten-day trips of 2027, and every September they move on a year. Their links open the trip planner straight inside German, Dutch or English school holidays.

## What the plan takes into account

You enter your dates, up to 21 days, and the parks. If you leave the selection alone, it's the big seven: Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom at Disney, and Universal Studios, Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe at Universal. With "Split my days for me" on, a park gets a second day where your trip has room for one. Hotel, hotel area, rest days and park hopping sit under "Fine-tune".

Each day is weighed by the hours you actually get as a day guest. A party day that sends you out at 6 pm instead of 10 pm weighs up to one and a half times as much as a full day with the same waits. It only comes up when its waits are short enough to make up for the missing hours. Disney After Hours starts after the regular close, so the day loses no hours to it. Answer "Do you want to see Magic Kingdom's fireworks?" with yes, and a party day only comes up when nothing else fits.

The hotel counts too. A Disney hotel brings Early Entry, a Disney deluxe hotel Extended Evening Hours on top, and a Universal hotel Early Park Admission. The Universal Premier hotels, which are the Royal Pacific, the Hard Rock and the Portofino Bay, include Express at Universal Studios and Islands of Adventure, and on those days the queues there count for a lot less.

With a Park Hopper, two parks end up on one day when your trip has fewer days than park visits and the two are under 30 minutes apart door to door. When a park closes early, you get an idea for the evening: a second park if you have a hopper, otherwise Disney Springs or CityWalk, which are free to enter. Rest days fall on the busiest days of your trip, and you won't get more than four park days in a row unless your parks don't fit any other way. On a rest day you don't queue anywhere, or at least nowhere we measure.

The answer opens with minutes: how long you'll queue for a big ride on your days on average, how long at the same parks on an average day of your trip, and how far a forecast for one ride on one day can be off. For the ten December days, the best order saves about three minutes a ride, against a possible forecast error of about 17 minutes. The two numbers sit side by side on purpose. Three minutes a ride is also the line below which all you're told is that the order barely matters on these dates. A bigger number would have sold better.

You can view the plan as an agenda, a week or a month. You can pin a day or swap it with another one, and before you swap, you see how many minutes it costs you. Single days or the whole trip go into the day planner, into a calendar file in Orlando time, or out as a link to exactly this plan.

## When your dates are still open

Under "Find the best dates" you pick a trip length of 3 to 21 days and a period: the next 12 months, a year, a spring, summer or autumn, or dates of your own. Back come the three best stretches, each with the wait at the big rides and how far that is below the period's average. With "Only in our school holidays" ticked, only stretches that lie entirely inside your region's school holidays are left, and you see how far our holiday dates reach. "Plan these dates" turns a stretch into a plan.

Heat and the hurricane season show up as short notes on each stretch. A day counts as hot from a forecast of 35 °C, and further ahead, where there's no forecast yet, from a long-term average of 31.5 °C. The hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November, with its peak from mid-August to mid-October. A hurricane doesn't show up in any wait time, so during the season we add up to eight minutes per park day, for the ranking only. A stretch in September therefore ends up behind an equally busy one in May and is marked "ranked lower", while its minutes stay as they are.

## Tickets you already have

The planner's first step asks "Do you already have tickets?". If you do, you pick them by name for Disney, Universal, SeaWorld or LEGOLAND. US tickets include Disney's date-based ticket for 1 to 10 days and Universal's for 1 to 5 days, both with or without the hopper, and at Universal, Epic Universe only comes in from three days. If you're not sure which one you have, the plan uses the strictest version of that length.

The 14-day tickets sold outside the US are in the list too, tagged "Sold outside the US". Disney's 14-Day Magic Ticket is good for 14 park days within 18 days, park hopping and water parks included. Universal's 14-day ticket is valid every day for 14 days in a row, with park-to-park and Epic Universe. So on the Universal ticket, the day by the pool counts too.

The Disney and Universal Combo Ticket, as Attraction Tickets sells it, fills in both at once, and one first day counts for both. If you arrive between 20 October 2026 and 31 March 2027, the Universal half is the Three Park Adventure Ticket, without Volcano Bay, because the water park is closed until the end of March.

![The trip planner's ticket step with two tickets of your own, Disney's 14-Day Magic Ticket and Universal's 14-day ticket, both tagged "Sold outside the US", each with its first day and dates. | A Combo Ticket starting Saturday 6 February 2027. Leave the first day open and the plan picks the start that fits best. | wide](/media/reiseplaner/tickets-uk-en.webp)

With 6 February as the first day, the Disney ticket runs to 23 February and the Universal one to the 19th. Both windows sit as bars under the days in the calendar, and every park day stays inside its ticket's window. With the big seven, that's eight of the 14 Disney days in those 18 days, two per park. They don't need more. A water-park day doesn't come off those days on the Magic Ticket. Disney and Universal days only get mixed where that saves the same three minutes a ride.

On days when a park hasn't published its opening hours yet, the plan has no hours for it, and the block says "no hours yet". For a trip that's still months away, that's often every day. Open a saved link again later and it plans with whatever hours have come out by then.

## No tickets yet

If you answer "Not yet, just plan", the plan comes with a suggestion of which tickets you need. For the ten December days with the big seven, it reads: 4 days Disney from Sunday 6 December, then 3 days Universal from Friday the 11th.

A four-day Disney ticket is good on any four of the seven days from its first, a three-day Universal ticket on any three of five. Under "Ticket details" you also see what a hopper would add, and on which date. "Worth adding" only appears where it opens up an evening in a second park, fits in a park that otherwise wouldn't fit, or saves the same three minutes a ride. In December it's a single evening. On the 10th, Animal Kingdom closes at 6 pm, and with a Park Hopper you can head over to Hollywood Studios afterwards. I'd take it for that evening: 6 pm in Orlando is too early for me to call it a day. For the three Universal days, park-to-park doesn't save a minute, it only gets you onto the Hogwarts Express.

You'll have to look up the extra cost yourself, though, because the planner shows no prices. Instead, each ticket comes with links to where you buy it: the cheapest authorised seller we found, Attraction Tickets and the park's own shop, with the pages in your language first. If one of them is a partner link, it's marked as one, and if you buy through it, park.fan earns a commission and your price stays the same. Because the trip is ten days long, there's also a line about the 14-day tickets sold outside the US, including the Combo.

## "Disney day 2 of 4" in the week view

Take both suggestions as your tickets, add the Park Hopper on the Disney one, and the week view lays the plan on the same hour axis as the day planner. A block runs from opening to the time you have to leave as a day guest, so for Animal Kingdom on Thursday it ends at 6 pm. Above each column you see which day of your ticket it is.

Monday at Magic Kingdom is "Disney day 2 of 4" and runs from 9 am to 10 pm. On Thursday, Animal Kingdom is followed after a 17-minute drive by the evening at Hollywood Studios. On Friday, Universal starts with Islands of Adventure, and the evening at CityWalk is dashed because it isn't a park. On a party day, the time after the block would be hatched, with the party's name and "separate ticket". Nothing is hatched, because Magic Kingdom is on the 7th and not the 8th.
