Winter Holiday, the Christmas event at [Universal Studios Beijing](ref:universal-studios-beijing), runs from November 20, 2026, to January 3, 2027. For the occasion, a new Christmas tree 72 feet (22 meters) tall stands at the end of Hollywood Boulevard. Over in The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Hogsmeade celebrates Christmas again. Universal spread nine themed events across 2026 for the resort's fifth birthday, and Winter Holiday is the last of them.

## The new tree on Hollywood Boulevard

The Spectacular Christmas Tree is dressed in rose gold, silver and blue, which is Universal's idea of classic Hollywood. It hasn't grown, mind you: last year's tree was 72 feet tall as well. In the evening the Sparkling Christmas Tree Spectacular plays light and music around it.

Fairy Godmother, Prince Charming, Shrek and Fiona stroll down the boulevard in winter outfits, past flower beds and lampposts hung with wreaths and bells.

## Santa, the shows and the food

At Spectacular Santa's Village you can meet Santa and have your photo taken with him. Elves, Mrs. Claus and the village Postmaster are around too. The village stage is in the Hollywood Amphitheatre, where Gift of the Season is playing again. The music show Universal Pop: Winter Holiday is back too, with the cast in winter costumes.

The village menu has a hot dog with cheese and meat sauce, a turkey and vegetable quesadilla, and a beef burger with pineapple and cheese. Anyone who's spent years arguing about pineapple on pizza can now take the debate to a burger in Beijing. To drink there's mulled wine and a hot chocolate with strawberry toffee. Sunset Grill in Hollywood and Hammond's in Jurassic World Isla Nublar cook Winter Holiday dishes as well. If you eat at CityWalk after the park, there's a roast turkey leg or apple pie with cookie ice cream, and a Winter Platter if you're hungrier.

## Christmas in Hogsmeade

Hogsmeade gets wreaths and fir garlands, and the shop windows glow under snowy roofs. You switch on the Enchanted Christmas Tree yourself with a wand, and then the lights come on and the decorations float. The Holiday Frog Choir is back, singing Christmas songs, and the Three Broomsticks has a winter menu.

In the evening Universal projects The Magic of Christmas at Hogwarts Castle onto the castle: a choir of ghosts, the Yule Ball, owls delivering presents and a Christmas tree. The rides in this land are [Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey](ref:universal-studios-beijing/harry-potter-and-the-forbidden-journey) and [Flight of the Hippogriff](ref:universal-studios-beijing/flight-of-the-hippogriff).

## Winter Holiday 2025

Last year Winter Holiday ran from November 14, 2025, to January 4, 2026. That was the first Christmas in Hogsmeade, with the wand-lit tree and the castle projection. Gift of the Season was new in 2025 as well, on a stage built like an elves' toy factory, with Puss in Boots, Kitty Softpaws, Po, Zhen, Gingy and Xile.

## Opening hours and tickets

Universal Beijing hasn't published opening hours from November 16 onward yet, nor ticket prices for December and January. Until November 15 the park is open daily from 9 am to 8 pm. According to the resort's FAQ, your ticket gets you into every land and attraction, with exceptions Universal doesn't list. Universal Beijing hasn't announced whether the shows, the tree lighting and the castle projection are included.

On the November event days the One-Day Dated Ticket costs 418 yuan Monday to Friday and 473 yuan on Saturdays and Sundays. On November 20 a day and a half costs 625 yuan (1.5-Day Dated Ticket) and two days 705 yuan (2-Day Flex Ticket). Children aged 3 to 11 and seniors 65 and over pay about a quarter less.

Every ticket is issued in one person's name and is only valid with ID. Until midnight two days before your visit you can cancel for free or change the date once. After that, cancelling costs 80 yuan until 11:59 pm the day before, and on the day itself you get nothing back. Universal Express starts at 190 yuan depending on the date and version, admission not included. The standard version gets you in faster once at each participating attraction.

## Getting there and hotels

The resort sits on the East 6th Ring Road in Beijing's Tongzhou district. Metro Line 7 and the Batong Line stop at Universal Resort Station, and from exits B, C and D it's about a seven-minute walk to the Universal CityWalk Beijing security check. Bus routes 589 and T116 also run to the resort. If you drive, a standard parking lot costs 100 yuan if you arrive by 2 pm, 50 yuan by 6 pm and 25 yuan after that, on days without Scares @ Universal.

The Universal Studios Grand Hotel stands right at the park entrance, and the NUO Resort Hotel is a five-minute walk away. Guests at both hotels with a valid ticket get into selected areas of the park up to an hour before opening. In winter the NUO serves afternoon tea in its lobby bar. Both hotels have something planned for Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve: the NUO a Christmas dinner and a party in the lobby bar, the Grand a New Year's Eve buffet.

## Five years of Universal Beijing

Universal Studios Beijing opened on September 20, 2021, and now has seven lands. For the anniversary, Universal Beijing announced in September an indoor Fast & Furious ride, the first in Asia based on the films, and a nighttime show on the Lagoon in the middle of the park. Universal hasn't given an opening date for either.
