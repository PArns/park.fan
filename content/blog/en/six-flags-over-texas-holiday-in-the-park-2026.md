Holiday in the Park at [Six Flags Over Texas](ref:six-flags-over-texas) in Arlington opens on Sunday, 22 November, with five new shows. For the first time, the giga dive coaster [Tormenta Rampaging Run](ref:six-flags-over-texas/tormenta-rampaging-run) keeps running into the winter as well. The event lasts 22 days, through 30 December, and regular admission covers the shows, the lights and most of the rides.

## The five new shows

| Show                           | Location           | What's on                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Santa's Brass Brigade          | Six Universe Stage | a brass band dressed as toy soldiers             |
| Polar Pop                      | Mexico Gazebo      | an a cappella concert with current pop hits      |
| Cool Yule                      | Star Mall          | Christmas hits as a stage show                   |
| Merry Melodeon                 | Boomtown           | ragtime and Christmas music on a piano bicycle   |
| Holidaze Rave w/ DJ Bugs Bunny | Star Mall          | a dance party with DJ Bugs Bunny at the big tree |

After its set on the Six Universe Stage the Brass Brigade marches through the park's walkways, and the Merry Melodeon's pianist pedals the piano around Boomtown himself. Six Flags hasn't published show times yet and points to its app instead.

Tinker's Toy Factory is billed as new too, but it already ran at the Southern Palace in 2025, back then as a circus show with acrobats. This winter it's a musical with Santa, Tinker and the elves. The theatre has new lighting and sound, and new seats, more of them than before.

## What else is on

- TWAS: A Merry Mishap on the Six Universe Stage, a comic version of "Twas the Night Before Christmas"
- The Mistletones, a trio singing in the style of the 1950s
- the Looney Tunes Holidaze Dance Party and the Tree Lighting Ceremony with the Six Flags Over Texas Holiday Singers, both on the Star Mall
- Midway Merriment, with Jack Frost, the Sugar Plum Fairy and other characters out on the walkways
- Story Time with Mrs. Claus at the Texas Arcade
- meet-and-greets with the Looney Tunes

Last year's Holly Jolly Trolley has been dropped from the programme.

## Cookies, cocoa and Santa

St. Nick's Pics has photos with Santa from 12:30 p.m. until the park closes; the photo packages cost extra. Kids can write him a postcard at the North Pole Post Office. At Mrs. Claus' Kitchen you decorate four large sugar cookies with icing and sprinkles and take them home in a box, for $21.99 plus tax and fees. Places are limited each evening.

S'mores at the fire pits cost extra. A cocoa mug is $12.99: on your first visit, refills of regular cocoa are free, and on later visits they're discounted. $29.99 buys a Bugs Bunny magic wand, which you pick up in the park.

## Which rides are running

Six Flags lists 37 rides for event days, among them Titan, Mr. Freeze, the New Texas Giant and the [Runaway Mine Train](ref:six-flags-over-texas/runaway-mine-train). [Aquaman: Power Wave](ref:six-flags-over-texas/aquaman-power-wave) closes at 6 p.m. on event days. On most event days, Dive Bomber Alley and the Six Flags Speedway go-karts don't open until 1 p.m. Roaring Rapids and the Six Flags and Texas Railroad sit out Holiday in the Park. Fast Lane starts at $30 a day.

## Event days and hours

On every event day the park opens at noon.

- 22 to 25 November: until 9 p.m.
- 26 November, Thanksgiving: closed
- 27 and 28 November: until 9 p.m.; 29 November until 8 p.m.
- 30 November to 4 December: closed
- 5 and 12 December (Saturdays): until 9 p.m.; 6 and 13 December (Sundays): until 8 p.m.; closed on the days in between
- 18 to 23 December: daily until 9 p.m.
- 24 and 25 December: closed
- 26 to 30 December: daily until 9 p.m.

New Year's Eve is in the calendar from noon to 8 p.m., so midnight won't find you in the park. Back in February, Six Flags Over Texas announced a New Year's Eve Celebration for 31 December; Six Flags hasn't published a programme for it.

## Tickets, the Silver Pass and parking

Online, admission starts at $45, plus a processing fee of up to $9.99 and tax. A Good Any Day ticket is $69. You can also buy day tickets at the gate, no reservation is needed, and children under three get in without a ticket.

Until 30 November the park sells the Silver Pass for the same $69 as a Black Friday deal. The park's event page says it covers unlimited visits for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027, with parking and entry to the water park. Six Flags' press release, on the other hand, talks of the 2027 spring and summer season. Buy it by 30 November and you get a Bring-A-Friend ticket on top, which has to be used by 5 January 2027. With a dining plan, the package costs $159 instead of $277.

Parking starts at $39, Preferred Parking at $55, each plus a fee and tax. For the satnav: 2201 Road to Six Flags, Arlington, TX 76011.

## Free admission at 65 and over

Since this winter, anyone born on or before 31 December 1961 gets into the park free. You can only sign up online; the admission is then activated at the gate, and you need ID showing your date of birth on every visit. The first visit has to take place by 31 December 2026. The ticket page gives the offer's end as 31 December 2026 in one place and 5 January 2027 in another, and the offer's FAQ says 5 January 2027.

The free admission only works at the park you registered for, and not at the water parks. Parking, discounts, Bring-A-Friend tickets and season pass perks aren't included. Anyone coming along needs a ticket of their own.

## Tormenta Rampaging Run in winter

[Tormenta Rampaging Run](ref:six-flags-over-texas/tormenta-rampaging-run?full)

Tormenta is a Bolliger & Mabillard dive coaster in Rancho de la Tormenta, the new Spanish village in the Mexico & Spain area. It stands 94 metres (309 ft) tall. At the top the train hangs over the edge for three seconds, just long enough to spot the Christmas lights below. Then it drops 87 metres at 95 degrees, slightly past vertical, and reaches 140 km/h. An Immelmann 66 metres high and a 55-metre loop follow, and the 1,280-metre track has four inversions in all.

A train seats 24, eight abreast in three cars. Riders must be between 122 and 198 centimetres tall. On event days Tormenta runs from opening until the park closes.

## Getting there with Arlington On-Demand

Arlington On-Demand is a ride service the city runs with Via, with no fixed routes and no timetable. You book it in the Arlington Transportation app or on 817-784-7382, and the average wait is 12 to 15 minutes. A ride costs $3 to $8 depending on distance, $3 to CentrePort station. On weekdays the service starts at 6 a.m. and on Saturdays at 9 a.m., and on both it stops at 9 p.m., the very minute the park closes on most event days and everyone heads for the exit at once. The city gives no Sunday hours, and six of the 22 event days are Sundays, opening day included.

## The oldest Six Flags park turns 65

2026 is the 65th season at Six Flags Over Texas, which Six Flags calls the oldest Six Flags theme park in the world. The park's oldest coaster is the Runaway Mine Train from 1966, so anyone getting in free on the senior offer this winter has a few years on the ride and can still board it. Holiday in the Park ran here for the 40th time in 2025.

This winter Six Flags celebrates at eleven parks, five of them with a Holiday in the Park, including [Six Flags Over Georgia](/news/six-flags-over-georgia-holiday-in-the-park-2026) and [Magic Mountain](/news/six-flags-magic-mountain-holiday-in-the-park-2026).
