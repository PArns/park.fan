Five big Halloween events in the USA are still running until October 31 or
November 1. Every one of them costs extra for the evening, and each has a
different program: horror houses in Orlando and Buena Park, Fright Fest at Six
Flags, candy trails at Disney. The overview for Europe is in the
[Halloween guide](/blog/halloween-freizeitparks-2026), and Cedar Point in Ohio
has its own post about [HalloWeekends](/blog/cedar-point-halloweekends-2026).

## Halloween Horror Nights in Orlando

According to Universal's press release, [Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida)
has **49 nights between August 28 and November 1**. Ten houses are on the
program:

- Stranger Things
- Sinners
- Hellraiser
- Ozzy Osbourne: Prince of Darkness
- Evil Dead Burn
- Jack and Oddfellow: Chaos & Control
- INVASION: Alien Abduction
- H.R. Bloodengutz Presents: A Halloween Fright-Tacular!
- MADLANDS: Caged Cannibals
- Cybergoria

There are also four scare zones: Fortnitemares, Infernal Carnival of Nightmares,
Downtown Clowntown and Sideshow of Decay. Admission is good for one evening and
is a separate ticket. According to ticket sellers, tickets start at $87.99 plus
tax and cost up to $129.99 on the busiest nights. We couldn't pull up the
park's own price list.

## Scary Farm at Knott's Berry Farm

[Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) in Buena Park near Los Angeles is
running the 53rd season of **Knott's Scary Farm**: 26 nights from September 17
to October 31, Thursday through Sunday. The gate opens at 7 p.m. The event ends
at 1 a.m. on Thursdays and Sundays and at 2 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

There are ten mazes, and **Inked** and **Unearthed** are new. **Origins: The
Curse of Calico** and **The Chilling Chambers** are in their last season. Add
five scare zones and three shows, two of them new. A ticket for one evening
starts at $69, and the Scary Farm Pass for every night costs $164. The event
isn't recommended for children under 13, and costumes that hide the face are
banned.

## Fright Fest at Six Flags

Fright Fest runs at several Six Flags parks from September 18 to November 1.
Here are the two parks park.fan measures.

According to the park, [Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain)
in California is open Friday through Sunday, and Thursdays too from October 8,
always from 6:30 p.m. The mazes cost from $25 for one evening, the pass for the
whole season from $69, and the bundle with park admission from $74. New this
year are **Final Destination: Death's Playground** and **Wolves' Blood**.

[Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) in New Jersey shows
at least nine mazes, including the new **JASON: Blood Reign** and **Final
Destination: Death's Playground**. The bundle with admission starts at $80, the
season pass for the mazes at $75. From October 8, Fright Fest runs on Thursdays
too.

Both parks advise against Fright Fest for children under 13. At both locations
the mazes need their own ticket, while the scare zones and shows are included in
park admission.

## Halloween parties at Disney

The Disney parties are separate from the park day. If you want to go, you buy a
separate ticket.

[Magic Kingdom Park](ref:magic-kingdom-park) in Florida holds **Mickey's
Not-So-Scary Halloween Party** on select evenings between August 7 and
October 31, always from 7 p.m. to midnight. In October, according to the date
lists on travel portals, those are the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 9th, 13th, 15th,
16th, 18th, 22nd, 23rd, 25th, 27th, 29th and 31st. We couldn't pull up the dates
on Disney's own site.

[Disney California Adventure Park](ref:disney-california-adventure-park) in
Anaheim holds **Oogie Boogie Bash** on 33 evenings between August 18 and
October 31, from 6 to 11 p.m. If you have a ticket, you can get into the park as
early as 3 p.m. New this year is **Madame Leota's Swinging Wake**, a street
party themed around the Haunted Mansion.

Costumes are allowed at Oogie Boogie Bash, and according to Disney the queues
are shorter at select rides.
