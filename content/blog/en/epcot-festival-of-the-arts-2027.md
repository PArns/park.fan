The Festival of the Arts at [EPCOT](ref:epcot) runs from 15 January to 1 March 2027, and the last day is a Monday. At 46 days it's a week longer than in 2026, when it ran from 16 January to 23 February. The trading card game Disney Lorcana presents it for the second year running.

## Collecting Lorcana cards

The quest booklet is free at the Creations Shop while supplies last. The Collection Quest asks for four cards, and you get them by trading with other players in the park, so it's not much fun if you're shy. You trade the full set for the card "Minnie Mouse – Musical Artist" at the Illumineer's Lounge in the Morocco pavilion or at Figment's Inspiration Station in The Odyssey. The Ink & Find Scavenger Hunt has you looking for oversized festival cards around the park, and the reward is "Belle – Reflective Writer" at normal card size.

Disney sells the Curator's Collection, six cards in a limited run, only at the festival. One of them shows Figment, his first Lorcana card ever. An Illumineer's Trove from the Hyperia City set comes with the card "Alice – Tea Alchemist", one per purchase. That only works in store: at Disney Traders, at Souk-al-Magreb in Morocco and at the Disney Lorcana TCG Festival Market. Disney hasn't published prices for the Curator's Collection or the Trove yet.

Lorcana artists and the game's two designers come to the festival on twelve days from 11:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., starting with Cristian Romero on 20 January and ending with Malia Ewart on 24 February. On their days the artists sign the cards they drew.

## Broadway concerts at the America Gardens Theatre

The concerts run every evening at 5:15, 6:30 and 7:45 p.m. at the America Gardens Theatre next to The American Adventure and are included in park admission. In 2026 that went for the whole festival, but for 2027 Disney has so far announced it only for the concerts. Seats are first come, first served. Six pairs of performers take turns, changing almost every day:

- Michael James Scott and Marisha Wallace: 15, 17, 18, 21, 22, 25 and 26 January
- Ashley Brown and Josh Strickland: 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 27 and 28 January
- Adam Pascal and Sherie Rene Scott: 29 and 31 January, 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9 February
- Sierra Boggess and Norm Lewis: 30 January, 2, 3, 6, 7, 10 and 11 February
- Kara Lindsay and Mason Reeves: 12, 14, 15, 18, 19, 22 and 25 February
- Caissie Levy and Ryan McCartan: 13, 16, 17, 20, 21, 23, 24 and 26 February
- Finale "Seize the Day! A Newsies Celebration": 27 and 28 February and 1 March

Wallace, Pascal, Sherie Rene Scott, Lewis, Reeves and McCartan are on this stage for the first time. Brown and Strickland mark 20 years of "Mary Poppins" and "Tarzan" on Broadway. Michael James Scott has played the Genie on Broadway longer than anyone else. Disney hasn't announced yet who sings in the Newsies finale.

At CommuniCore Hall, Disney shows the exhibition "Once Upon A Stage: The Music of Disney on Broadway". In 2026 there was one with almost the same name, about the magic rather than the music. The hall also has Disney on Broadway merchandise for the first time, from "Hercules", "Aladdin", "The Lion King" and "Beauty and the Beast".

## Dining packages with a guaranteed seat

You can book the dining packages from 3 December 2026, two weeks earlier than last year. A meal at one of 14 restaurants comes with a guaranteed seat at a concert the same day, with its own queue. The restaurant hands you a badge with the date and showtime. The package covers a main course with dessert or a buffet, plus one drink, which can be alcoholic if you're 21 or over.

Adults aged ten and up pay from $38 at Regal Eagle Smokehouse to $104 at Le Cellier Steakhouse. For children from three to nine it's $23 to $52, all before tax and tip. Regal Eagle can only be booked on the same day. Park admission isn't part of the package. You'll need a credit card on file to book.

## Painting, chalk and 19 Food Studios

Haley Talton, Nicky Nicotera and Robert Post are exhibiting at the festival for the first time. Darren Wilson is back, and Stephanie Laberis and William Silvers are exhibiting too. At the Animation Academy in the America Gardens Theatre, Disney artists teach drawing classes. In the Expression Section you help paint by numbers on five murals, one of them with a Lorcana motif.

The festival started in 2017. For its tenth birthday, the bridge to World Showcase gets two new Lorcana photo backdrops, "Champion Card Collage" and "Merlin's Shop and Smithy". The Chalk Art Galleries are next to them. Festival food comes from 19 Food Studios, whose menus Disney hasn't revealed yet.

School holidays, public holidays and the other events in these weeks are in our [Orlando itinerary guide](/blog/orlando-itinerary-disney-universal), along with Disney and Universal tickets.
