## New

- The crowd calendar moved off the park page's tabs to a page of its own, in all six languages
  (27 August). A day tile has a bar along its top edge with a segment for each of school holidays,
  public holidays, bridge days and holidays in nearby regions, so a Friday in the summer holidays
  when a nearby region is also on holiday shows both. The predicted wait sits in its corner
  (28 August).
- Where a park sells skip-the-queue access, the ride has a badge with the product's name and price
  in your currency format (28 August). A price of zero means it is included, as at Europa-Park. Six
  brand names joined the glossary: QuickPass, Virtual Line, Fast Lane, Speedy Pass, Fastrack and
  Premier Access.
- The star in the header opens your favourites from any page, as cards with a photo and the current
  wait (29 August). Until then they appeared only on the homepage, two screens below the photo.

## Improved

- Park and ride pages open onto a row of tiles with icons. The tab bar they replace was a pale strip
  under the weather card, below the fold on most screens (27 August).
- The sitemap gives each page the date its visible content last changed, found by a daily crawl
  that compares what each page shows with the crawl before (27 August). Until then all of its URLs
  (44,000 at the time) were stamped with the current date every day.

## Fixed

- Going from a park page to its calendar and back cost a jump to the top and an animated scroll back
  down, in both directions. The tile row keeps its position (27 August).
