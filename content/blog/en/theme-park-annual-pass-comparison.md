[Efteling](ref:efteling) in the Netherlands, [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) in Rust and [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum sell an annual pass, [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl doesn't.

All prices come from the parks' own pages, linked at the end.

## The four parks at a glance

| Park                                          | Annual pass             | Adult price      | Adult day ticket          | Blocked days                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| [Efteling](ref:efteling?bare)                 | Classic, Plus, Premium  | €240, €310, €400 | €40 to €56 (2026)         | 2027: Classic 68, Plus 25, Premium none         |
| [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?bare)           | ResortPass Silver, Gold | €325, €495       | €67 to €76 (2026 season)  | Silver only on over 230 set days, Gold none     |
| [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland?bare) | Magic Member            | €160             | €37 to €49.50 (base rate) | none, only days the park is closed or hired out |
| [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand?bare)       | none                    | not available    | €44 to €78 (October 2026) | not applicable                                  |

The day ticket ranges don't cover the same period. Efteling gives its range for the whole of 2026.
For Europa-Park it's the season from 28 March 2026 to 9 January 2027. For Toverland it's the base rate
in the ticket calendar from 6 October 2026 to 17 January 2027, so only the autumn and winter months. For
Phantasialand it's the October calendar of the ticket shop. None of the four parks has published
prices for 2027. Europa-Park has announced that tickets for the 2027 summer season go on sale on
10 November 2026.

## How the break-even works

The break-even is the smallest whole number of visits at which the annual pass costs no more than the same number of day tickets. The day price moves. Between the cheapest and the dearest day the gap is €16 at Efteling, €9 at Europa-Park, €12.50 at Toverland and €34 at Phantasialand. So I calculate with both ends and give a range. If you mostly go on Saturdays, you pay the higher prices and reach the break-even with fewer visits.

Each of the three parks with a pass sells a parking permit or discounts the parking ticket. If you come by train, that part drops out and the "without car" columns in the tables below apply.

I haven't counted the discount on food and souvenirs, because it depends on what you buy, or the visits you wouldn't have made without the pass.

All passes are tied to a person.

## Efteling's three annual passes

Efteling has sold three annual passes since 11 June 2024, differing in the number of days they are
valid. A discount for visitors aged 65 and over no longer exists, and existing 65+ passes ran until the end of their minimum term.

Efteling surveyed the holders in 2023, and according to the park most wanted more choice. From 15 April to 10 June 2024 the park sold no new passes, only renewals. In the Netherlands the number of pass holders had risen in 2023, Efteling writes in its review of the year.

| Tier    | Annual price | Monthly | Access            | Parking discount | Food, drinks, souvenirs | Friend tickets at half price |
| ------- | ------------ | ------- | ----------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Classic | €240         | €21     | at least 290 days | 40%              | no discount             | 2 per calendar year          |
| Plus    | €310         | €28.50  | at least 340 days | 50%              | 5%                      | 2 per calendar year          |
| Premium | €400         | €36     | all days          | 60%              | 10%                     | 4 per calendar year          |

All three tiers give 25% off CARO tickets and the New Year's Eve party, up to 25% off overnight stays in
the park and free or reduced entry to partner parks and zoos. Premium adds a keepsake and access to an
event for pass holders only. Paying monthly commits you for at least twelve months and costs more.
Twelve months of Classic come to €252, €12 more than paying for the year up front. For Plus and Premium
it's €32 more each. You set the start date of the pass when you buy, up to one month ahead.

Visitors with a disability get €50 off any tier when paying for the year, or €5 a month. Children up to and including three get the pass free. On your birthday you get a pastry with a drink you buy.

### The blocked days in the park's calendar

Efteling publishes the blocked days as a calendar for Classic and Plus that you can subscribe to in
Google, Apple or Outlook. The figures below are counted from both calendars.
Premium has no blocked days and doesn't appear in them.

| Period                         | Classic blocked | Plus blocked |
| ------------------------------ | --------------- | ------------ |
| 10 October to 31 December 2026 | 44 days         | 10 days      |
| Calendar year 2027             | 68 days         | 25 days      |
| Open days 2027                 | 297             | 340          |

Classic is valid on 297 days in 2027, seven more than the promised 290, and Plus on exactly the promised 340. Blocking falls mostly on weekends and in blocks of several weeks: in 2027, 42 of the 68 Classic days are a Saturday or Sunday, and 16 of the 25 for Plus. Monday to Friday account for 26 Classic days.

The Classic blocked days in calendar order:

- Autumn and winter 2026: 10 and 11 October, 17 October to 8 November, 14 and 15 November, 22 and 29 November, 5 and 6 December, 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027
- January 2027: 10, 16, 17, 23 and 24 January
- Spring 2027: 6 and 7 March, 14 March, 20 and 21 March, 28 and 29 March, 6 and 7 May, 16 and 17 May
- Autumn 2027: 11, 12, 18, 19, 25 and 26 September, 2, 3, 9 and 10 October, 16 October to 7 November, 13 and 14 November, 21 November, 27 and 28 November
- December 2027: 4, 5, 12 and 14 December, 25 to 31 December

Far fewer days are blocked on Plus: 22 and 29 November, 5 and 6 December and 26 to 31 December 2026, and in 2027 10, 16, 23 and 24 January, 6 and 7 March, 20 and 21 March, 6, 7, 16 and 17 May, 21, 27 and 28 November, 4, 12 and 14 December and 25 to 31 December. The biggest difference is the three weeks from mid-October to early November: 17 October to 8 November in 2026, 16 October to 7 November in 2027. At Easter 2027, on 28 and 29 March, Plus is valid and Classic isn't. Ascension (6 May) and Whitsun (16 and 17 May) are blocked for both.

### What pays off

A day ticket at Efteling costs between €40 and €56 in 2026, plus €15 for parking per visit.

| Tier    | Price | Break-even without car | Break-even with car |
| ------- | ----- | ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Classic | €240  | 5 to 6 visits          | 4 to 6 visits       |
| Plus    | €310  | 6 to 8 visits          | 5 to 7 visits       |
| Premium | €400  | 8 to 10 visits         | 7 to 9 visits       |

The "with car" column counts the parking discount as savings: €6, €7.50 and €9 per visit. The price
applies per person on every tier. Two adults on Classic pay €480.

The step from Classic to Plus costs €70, the step from Plus to Premium €90. In 2027 Plus is valid on 43 more days than Classic, and Premium on 25 more than Plus. Per extra day, Plus costs €1.63 more than Classic and Premium €3.60 more than Plus. That's less than any day price.

By [our measurements](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands), waits at Efteling are only a little longer on Saturdays than on other days. Classic does block mostly weekends, but also 26 days from Monday to Friday. If you can come on weekdays, you lose little with Classic.

The park is a good one and a half to two hours' drive from Düsseldorf, a good two from Cologne and the Ruhr.

## ResortPass Silver and Gold at Europa-Park

Before the ResortPass the park sold the Europa-Park Club card. In March 2020, when the park had to close because of Covid, it took the card off sale until further notice. It announced the ResortPass on 8 March 2022, and the pass went on sale in the Mack International ticket shop in mid-May 2022. In March 2023 Silver cost €240 for adults and Gold €420, and both were sold out, according to the Stuttgarter Zeitung. A spokeswoman told the paper at the time that new allotments would go on sale after the first expiry dates in May and June 2023. Since then Silver has gone up by €85 and Gold by €75.

Europa-Park sells the ResortPass in two tiers, both valid for one year:

| Tier   | Adults 12+ | Children 4 to 11, seniors 60+ | Access as a day guest            | Rulantica                |
| ------ | ---------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Silver | €325       | €275                          | on over 230 pre-set opening days | not included             |
| Gold   | €495       | €430                          | on all opening days              | two day tickets included |

As an overnight guest in the park's hotels and in Silver Lake City, both passes give entry on every day
of the stay. Both give €2 off at the evening cinema, free access to content on the VEEJOY streaming
platform and free or reduced entry to partner parks. A ParkingPass costs €39 extra on either tier. At a
€10 parking fee per day it's cheaper from the fourth visit by car. The printed card costs €5 extra on
Silver and comes with Gold.

Once during the term, both tiers give free entry to Efteling, Pleasure Beach Resort in England, Liseberg in Sweden, PortAventura Park and Isla Mágica in Spain, Emerald Park in Ireland and Le Pal in France. The eight parks and pools of the Plopsa group, Plopsaland Deutschland among them, give a one-off 40% off the regular admission at the ticket desk.

At the moment no new pass can be bought. The park writes on its page that the allotment is used up and the ResortPass is unavailable until further notice, also by phone, by e-mail or on site. Holders get a message 30 days before expiry and can renew online in the ResortPass portal during the last 14 days of the term, at the price that applies then. An expired pass can be reactivated within two years if there is allotment left. You can only switch from Silver to Gold when renewing, and only if Gold is available. The park hasn't announced when sales will reopen.

A day ticket costs €67 to €76 for adults and €56.50 to €65 for children and seniors.

| Tier   | Adults        | Children and seniors | Family of two adults and two children             |
| ------ | ------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Silver | 5 visits      | 5 visits             | 5 visits (€1,200 against €247 to €282 a day)      |
| Gold   | 7 to 8 visits | 7 to 8 visits        | 7 to 8 visits (€1,850 against €247 to €282 a day) |

The five visits have to fall on days Silver covers. Which days those are is in the ResortPass portal, and the price page gives only their number, over 230. The 2026 season runs from 28 March 2026 to 9 January 2027, which is 288 calendar days, less the two rest days at Christmas.

Gold costs €170 more, €155 for children. That's a little over two extra adult day tickets at the top
price. In return Gold is valid on every opening day, and the two Rulantica tickets are included. The
Rulantica ticket has a price of its own, which I haven't counted here because it depends on the day and
the time slot.

Gold pays off if you don't want to fix days in advance or if you visit the water world twice. Silver
pays off if you come five times and can plan your visit days around the park's calendar. If you come only
two to four days a year, stay with the day ticket.

The park has announced the Pre-Opening Weeks from 7 to 19 March 2027, two weeks before the season opens. On the ResortPass page the park lists the cheapest tickets of the year for them. Day reservations for them go through the ResortPass portal. That's the first date on which you could use a new pass.

Europa-Park is emptiest on a Sunday
or Thursday in May or June and fullest on Saturday.

## Magic Member at Toverland

Toverland in Sevenum has the cheapest annual pass of the four, Magic Member.

| Group                       | Annual price | Monthly (minimum twelve months) | Parking per year | Parking per month |
| --------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Adults and children 140 cm+ | €160         | €14                             | €50              | €4.50             |
| Children 90 to 140 cm       | €120         | €10.50                          | €50              | €4.50             |
| Seniors 60+                 | €130         | €11.50                          | €50              | €4.50             |

Paying monthly comes to €168 instead of €160. Included is entry on all opening days, 5% off food, drinks
and souvenirs at the permanent outlets, discounts at other parks and zoos and a birthday pastry with a drink you buy. Entry to
the Halloween Nights comes at no extra charge, while the Haunted Experiences cost extra. A child under
90 cm gets a free pass if a parent holds an annual pass. If the child outgrows the height class, the
difference is payable for each remaining month.

When the pass is bought on a visit day and paid for the whole year, the park refunds that day's admission. The partner list of March 2026 includes Walibi Holland at 20% except during Halloween Fright Nights, Attractiepark Hellendoorn at 35%, the Plopsa parks at 40% for visitors from 1 m, Bobbejaanland at 20% and Freizeit-Land Geiselwind at 40%.

### The terms

The price page lists no blocked days. The pass terms, version of 21 March 2024, say: the pass entitles you
to one day visit per opening day. Days on which the park is closed or hired out are excluded and are
listed on the opening-times page. On busy days the park may refuse entry without refunding the price.

Further points from the terms:

- One pass per person, not transferable, with a photo that is checked at the entrance.
- Bought at the desk, it runs for one year from the date of purchase. Bought online, it runs from the date of collection, provided it is collected within two months.
- Online purchases carry a 14-day right of withdrawal as long as the pass hasn't been used or collected.
- The park sends a renewal invitation two months before expiry and may adjust the price at expiry.
- With monthly payment the pass can be cancelled after the first twelve months, with one month's notice.
- The parking pass covers one car per day, for one person in the family, and isn't valid in the last hour before closing.

### What pays off

The prices below are the base rate in the ticket calendar for adults and children 140 cm and over.
Anyone who books online at least seven days ahead gets a €7 early-bird discount, a shorter lead time gets
€2. Toverland has four price levels, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum, set by the opening hours and
programme of the day.

| Day price                           | Break-even for €160 | Break-even with car (pass plus parking €210) |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| €37, cheapest base rate             | 5 visits            | 5 visits                                     |
| €49.50, dearest base rate           | 4 visits            | 4 visits                                     |
| €30, cheapest day with early bird   | 6 visits            | 5 visits                                     |
| €42.50, dearest day with early bird | 4 visits            | 4 visits                                     |

In the "with car" column we compare a parking fee of €13.50 per day with annual parking at €50. Annual
parking pays for itself after four visits by car.

In 2026 the park sold a Summer Pass for the first time: €79 for adults, €59 for children from 90 to
140 cm, unlimited visits from 1 July to 30 September. Whether it returns in 2027 the park hasn't announced.
If you come only in summer, you pay €81 less than for the annual pass.

Weekdays at Toverland hardly differ, and it's busier in the Christmas holidays.

## Phantasialand without an annual pass

Phantasialand took its Club card off sale during the pandemic, and the last cards ran out in summer 2022.
Neither an annual pass nor a Club card comes up in the ticket shop's FAQ, and tickets are only sold
dated and online.

In the ticket shop's October calendar a ticket for adults from 12 years costs between €44 and €78. The gap between the cheapest and the dearest day is €34, which is 77% on top of the cheap day. Three visits a year cost between €132 and €234. At Phantasialand a Saturday is noticeably fuller than a Tuesday.

If you can come during the week, you pay least with Classic at Efteling and Magic Member at Toverland. If you're only free at weekends, you're better off with Plus or Premium at Efteling and with Gold at Europa-Park.

## Who needs which pass

At Efteling, Classic is enough if you can come outside the holidays and are at the park at least six
times a year. If you want to go between mid-October and early November, you need Plus. Between Christmas
and New Year Plus is blocked too, which leaves Premium. It only pays off from eight to ten visits.

## When to buy

An annual pass runs for one year from purchase or from its start date. At Efteling you set the start and can place it up to one month ahead. If you start in October, you begin in the middle of the blocked weeks.

For the Europa-Park ResortPass the start date must fall within 14 days of purchase and can't be changed afterwards.

At Efteling the 2026 prices stand until the park names new ones. At Toverland the park can only change the price of a pass when it expires.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a theme park annual pass worth it?

At Efteling, Classic pays off from five to six visits a year, and at Europa-Park the ResortPass Silver
from five. At Toverland, Magic Member pays off from four to five visits. Phantasialand doesn't sell an
annual pass.

### How many blocked days does the Efteling annual pass have?

According to the park's calendar the Classic is blocked on 68 days in 2027, Plus on 25 days and Premium on none.

### Can you still buy the Europa-Park ResortPass?

Not at the moment. According to Europa-Park the allotment is used up.

### Does Toverland have blocked days for the annual pass?

No. Under the pass terms of 21 March 2024 the pass is valid on every opening day. The only exceptions
are days when the park is closed or hired out. On busy days the park may refuse entry.

### Does Phantasialand still have an annual pass?

No. The Club card ended during the pandemic, and the last cards ran out in summer 2022.
