The Swedish private equity firm **EQT** is preparing to sell Parques Reunidos,
the Madrid-based group that has owned [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany)
in Bottrop since 2010. The Spanish business daily **Expansión** reported it on
Thursday, citing several sources in the market. JPMorgan has been hired to run
the sale. Neither EQT nor JPMorgan would comment.

It is the whole group that is up for sale, so [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland?bare)
in Belgium, Slagharen in the Netherlands and Tropical Islands south of Berlin go
with it.

The reports disagree on the price. Expansión puts the valuation at around
€1.7 billion; elEconomista, which also carries the story, says more than
€1.5 billion. Both call the process early, and neither gives a timetable.

## What Parques Reunidos owns

The group was founded in 1967. According to Expansión it runs more than 30
theme parks, water parks, zoos and aquariums in ten countries: nine in Europe,
plus Australia. Besides the four above, that includes
[Mirabilandia](ref:mirabilandia?bare) near Ravenna,
[Parque Warner](ref:parque-warner-madrid?bare) and the
[Parque de Atracciones](ref:parque-de-atracciones-de-madrid?bare) in Madrid,
Tusenfryd outside Oslo and Weltvogelpark Walsrode, a bird park in Lower Saxony.

Revenue in 2025 was €550 million, down 4.7 percent on a like-for-like basis,
with EBITDA above €170 million. Germany is almost as big as Spain: €172 million
in sales against €196 million, with Italy a distant third at €49 million. Close
to a third of what a buyer gets is German business, mostly Movie Park, Tropical
Islands and the bird park.

EQT bought the group through a takeover bid in 2019 and took it off the Madrid
stock exchange. Expansión says the deal valued it at just under €2.1 billion
including debt, though that was with the American parks still in it. EQT now
holds 52 percent, the Spanish investment company Corporación Financiera Alba
25 and the Belgian holding Groupe Bruxelles Lambert 23. None of the reports say
whether Alba and GBL will sell their stakes too.

## A smaller group than the one EQT bought

In March 2025 Parques Reunidos sold its US arm, Palace Entertainment, to
Herschend: more than 20 parks and attractions in ten states. elEconomista puts
the deal at around €1 billion, and the US had brought in about a third of group
revenue. CEO Pascal Ferracci said at the time: "Our future is in Europe, where
we see tremendous potential for expansion."

Two weeks after the US announcement, Belantis near Leipzig went to Compagnie
des Alpes for an enterprise value of about €22 million. In September 2025 EQT
refinanced a little over €1 billion of debt, and according to elEconomista the
shareholders took a €50 million dividend out of it.

## Who might buy

Expansión names two operators as possible bidders, Merlin Entertainments and
Compagnie des Alpes, while stressing that there is no shortlist yet. Merlin runs [Heide Park](ref:heide-park?bare)
and [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland?bare); Compagnie des Alpes
owns [Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland?bare),
[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium?bare), [Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix?bare)
and, since April 2025, Belantis.

A Compagnie des Alpes deal would put Movie Park back in a group with some old
neighbours. From 1999 to 2006 Bottrop, Walibi Belgium and what is now Walibi
Holland had the same owner, first Six Flags and then the private equity firm
Palamon. In 2006 the French bought the Walibi parks from Palamon, which kept
Movie Park until it sold to Parques Reunidos.

## What it means if you visit

Nothing for now. The Halloween Horror Festival opens as planned tomorrow,
26 September, and runs until 8 November; the dates are in
[our festival post](/blog/movie-park-germany-halloween-horror-festival-2026).
Tickets and season passes you already hold stay valid. It is the parent company
being sold, and the park remains the one you bought from.

The sale doesn't touch the season pass either. Gold and Platinum cover the rest of this season and all of 2027, and Movie Park's site already lists the 2027 pass benefits: three free visits each to Bobbejaanland, Slagharen and Weltvogelpark Walsrode, one to Tropical Islands, and one each to other European parks in the group, naming Mirabilandia and Parque Warner. The rest of the 2027 Bonus Club offers will be published "at the start of the 2027 season". Since the group is being sold as a whole, its parks stay sister parks after a sale.

Belantis shows how the park handles a change of owner mid-season. The 2025 list gave pass holders three free visits there, valid until 9 November, and the sale to Compagnie des Alpes in April didn't change that. Only the 2026 list moves Belantis to the discounts. If you're thinking about the 2027 Gold pass, the sale is no reason to wait. Whether anything changes for the partner parks is a question for 2028 at the earliest.

Parques Reunidos has built in Bottrop more than once since taking over in 2010. A month after the deal the park's manager pitched [Van Helsing’s Factory](ref:movie-park-germany/van-helsings-factory) to the new owners, the group signed off €5 million, and the ride opened in 2011. A 45-metre coaster on the meadow right behind the entrance, presented to the town in 2012, was stopped in September that year, officially because of "a change in our investment strategy". [Star Trek: Operation Enterprise](ref:movie-park-germany/star-trek-operation-enterprise), now the park's biggest coaster, opened on that plot in 2017. [Excalibur](ref:movie-park-germany/excalibur-secrets-of-the-dark-forest) followed in 2018 and the [Movie Park Studio Tour](ref:movie-park-germany/movie-park-studio-tour) in 2021. The park's full history is in the [Movie Park guide](/blog/movie-park-germany-wait-times-tips).
