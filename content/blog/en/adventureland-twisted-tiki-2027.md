[Adventureland](ref:adventureland-resort) in Altoona, Iowa, is building a new slide called Twisted Tiki at its Adventure Bay water park for summer 2027. It's the park's first slide where a whole family rides together in one raft. Adventureland announced it on 17 November, and construction has already started. It's the first expansion of Adventure Bay since 2012, and it adds almost an acre (about 0.4 hectares) to the water park.

## Five storeys up, in a closed tube

ProSlide is building Twisted Tiki. A raft holds up to three people, the start is five storeys up, and the tube runs for about 150 metres. It's enclosed all the way, so you won't see any of the Iowa sky on the way down. The track spins through two full 360-degree turns, and coloured rings and light effects glow in the curves.

Adventureland hasn't published a height requirement for Twisted Tiki yet. Hyperlight, Bermuda Quadrangle and the Gang Plank & Pirates' Plummet slides all require 48 inches (122 cm).

> [!QUOTE]
> As our first-ever family raft slide, it gives our visitors a new way to experience the water park together, with thrilling twists, vibrant lights and plenty of surprises along the way. We can't wait to welcome guests aboard in summer 2027.
>
> Mike Lusky, general manager of Adventureland, in the [press release of 17 November 2026](https://adventurelandpark.com/press/all-new-twisted-tiki-water-slide/)

There's no opening day yet. The park says the 2027 calendar and the year's events will follow soon.

## What 2027 season passes cost right now

The Black Friday sale takes up to $60 off 2027 season passes bought online. Adventureland hasn't given an end date; the sale runs "for a limited time". You can pay for any pass in eight instalments.

| -------- | ------------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bronze   | $69.99             | $119.99       | summer, Sunday to Friday, 5 % off food and merchandise                     |
| Silver   | from $129.99       | $169.99       | the full season with Oktoberfest and Phantom Fall Fest, half-price parking |
| Gold     | from $159.99       | $219.99       | free parking, two friend tickets, one tube a day at Adventure Bay          |
| Platinum | from $199.99       | $259.99       | Neon Nights, early entry to Adventure Bay, ten more parks                  |

A 2027 day ticket costs $69.99 too. So the same money buys you either one day or a whole summer, except that the Bronze summer has no Saturdays. Children aged three and under get in free. Both tickets and season passes include Adventure Bay whenever the water park is open, though at peak times it only lets in a limited number of guests. If you had a season pass in 2024, 2025 or 2026, you save up to $80 when you renew. Platinum also gets you into ten other parks besides Altoona, Noah's Ark Waterpark in Wisconsin among them.

## What else is at Adventure Bay

Children under 48 inches have to wear a life jacket in the Breaker Beach wave pool. The Caribbean Cruiser lazy river floats you once around the whole water park, and on Reef Racer six people race each other side by side. Gang Plank & Pirates' Plummet are the fastest slides in Adventure Bay. For kids there's Kokomo Kove with its tipping buckets, where the smallest slides are open from 36 inches (91 cm) and the rest from 40 inches, and the pirate ship Shipwreck Shores. Adventure Bay closes during thunderstorms as soon as lightning is detected within ten miles (16 km).

The two additions before Twisted Tiki were about light as well. Hyperlight opened on Memorial Day weekend 2025, a slide with changing colours and surround sound. Neon Nights followed in 2026: on Fridays and Saturdays from 10 July to 15 August, more than ten attractions lit up in the evening, with walk-around acts and music in between. Silver and Gold passes get you discounted tickets for it in 2027.

## Getting to Altoona

Adventureland is a few minutes from Des Moines, right off Interstate 80, at 3200 Adventureland Drive. The Caribbean flair the press release promises is reached by a Midwest highway exit. Parking is $21 a day, or $26 for Preferred Parking.
