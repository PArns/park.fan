## New

- Dutch, French and Spanish joined English and German on 15 January, and a banner in your
  browser's language offers the matching version of the page from 24 January.
- A shared link to any page shows a picture made for that page, from the homepage down to a single
  ride, with its sparkline or its region on a map (13 January).
- `/parks` lists the continents as the start of the whole tree, and the search finds cities and
  countries as well as parks (15 January).
- Park and ride pages and the homepage answer common questions in a FAQ at the bottom, in all five
  languages (17 January).
- The crowd calendar was rebuilt as a month grid of our own with month-by-month navigation, and
  each month has a link of its own (30 January). Days without published hours are grey and say
  so, while days the park is confirmed closed are red (3 February).
- If you are in a park, the homepage welcomes you to it and shows its details under the greeting,
  and the header shows the nearest park when it is within 5 km (7 February).
- "Parks near you" works without location permission, from an approximate position based on your
  connection, and says it gets more accurate with your location (5 February).
- A countdown on the homepage ran to 28 March, the start of the 2026 season, over a photo of Taron
  (27 January).

## Improved

- A park out of season says so on its card and page, with the date it reopens (31 January).
- The crowd scale got a new set of colours and icons, and its middle level is called "Normal"
  instead of "Moderate" (1 February).
- Park cards on country and city pages count down to opening or closing (10 February).
- Ride and park names lost the "NEW" prefix some parks put in front of them.

## Fixed

- A crowd level was shown for parks that were closed.
- Several malformed URLs and the links in "Parks near you" ended on a 404.
- Moving between pages asked for your location and the park data again every time: four requests
  per navigation before, none within the cache time after (25 January).
