[Six Flags Over Georgia](ref:six-flags-over-georgia) in Austell, just outside Atlanta, opens a reworked Holiday in the Park on Saturday, 21 November. There are three new Christmas areas and a new show, "Tinker's Toy Factory". The event runs on selected days until 3 January 2027.

The park calls this the first phase of a makeover that'll take several years. [Holiday in the Park at Six Flags Great Adventure](/news/six-flags-great-adventure-holiday-in-the-park-2026) in New Jersey gets the same treatment this year. The big productions play indoors on a main stage, which nobody minds in December, while carolers and street performers bring live music to the paths outside.

## The new Christmas areas

So far, the event page names Merry Magnolia Lane and Santa's Toy Town. The page doesn't say whether these are two of the three new areas, but they're where you'll find Santa and the cocoa. In Merry Magnolia Lane, Santa sits at Coastal Gifts, and the photos with him go by "St. Nick's Pics". In Santa's Toy Town, the Fenders Gift Shop turns into Tinsel's Merry Mixer, a holiday lounge serving cocoa and other seasonal drinks. Behind the bar, says the event page, is an elf "with very big dreams", which is a lot of ambition for a cocoa counter. The Toy Soldier Trio also marches through Toy Town with fanfares.

## Tinker's Toy Factory and the other shows

The new show is set in a toy factory at the height of the Christmas rush. The plot is the one every Christmas musical runs on: Santa, Tinker and the elves work out how much family and friends mean to them. Tinker's Dance Party is on the bill too. The Tree Lighting Spectacular starts at 6 p.m. on event days.

The rest of the line-up:

- Christmas Carol's Chaotic Choir Concert: the Off-Key Carolers sing out of tune on purpose, which most families only manage by accident
- Jingle Jazz: Christmas classics arranged for jazz
- The Mistle Tones: Christmas songs in harmony, with stories in between
- Holiday Road: the Crystal Pistol Holiday Review
- Twas a Merry Mishap: a comic retelling of a Christmas classic

## Beyond the shows

Sweets come from Mrs. Claus' Confectionery, a holiday kitchen, and s'mores from the S'mores Zone. On the terrace of Dee Jay's Diner, The Spirits Meter pours holiday drinks, and Artisan Alley is a market for handmade goods. Cookie decorating and gingerbread house building are on here just as at Great Adventure, along with tasting menus. The cocoa also comes in a souvenir mug, for anyone with a bit of cupboard space left at home. For adults, there are Christmas cocktails on the menu.

Admission covers the lights, the shows and whichever rides run, which Six Flags so far describes only as selected thrill rides, family rides and the kids' areas. Food, drinks, souvenirs and some experiences cost extra.

## Opening days and showtimes

The park hasn't published which days between 21 November and 3 January it's open, or at what times. Showtimes are only in the Six Flags app, and the event page still lists the digital event guide as "Coming Soon".

## Tickets and free entry from 65

Day tickets start at $35. Until 30 November, the park sells the Silver Pass as a Black Friday deal for $69, the price of a 2027 Good Any Day ticket. It's valid for Holiday in the Park 2026 and then through Labor Day 2027. It also covers the water parks Hurricane Harbor Atlanta and Six Flags White Water, and parking.

The Gold Pass costs $79 and covers 2026 and 2027 at every Six Flags park on the East Coast, with parking, Fright Fest and Holiday in the Park included. If you already hold one, renewal is $69, and if you'd rather pay monthly, the Gold membership is $7 a month plus a $20 initiation fee. All of these carry a processing fee of up to $9.99 plus tax. Buy or renew a pass, or take out a new membership, by 30 November and you get a Bring-A-Friend ticket, which has to be used by 3 January 2027.

New this winter, Six Flags lets everyone 65 and over in free, to get the grandparents along, while the grandchildren still pay from $35. Register online and make your first visit by 3 January 2027, and you get in free until then. You'll need ID showing your date of birth at the gate every time.

Without a pass, parking costs $39, or $49 for Preferred Parking, each plus a fee. For the satnav, the address is 275 Riverside Parkway, Austell, GA 30168.

## Goliath stays closed

On 12 October, one person died and several were injured in an [incident on Goliath](/news/goliath-six-flags-over-georgia-fatal-incident). [Goliath](ref:six-flags-over-georgia/goliath?bare) stays closed until the required safety inspections and approvals are complete. Six Flags hasn't given a date for it to reopen. The ride and other coasters with a similar restraint are being fitted with [seat belts](/news/six-flags-seat-belts-goliath).

## Christmas at the other Six Flags parks

Six Flags is celebrating Christmas at eleven parks this winter, and Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, gets going on 26 November with seven new shows. In Texas, the event runs at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio from 21 November to 4 January 2027, and at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington from 22 November to 30 December. Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park puts on a new ice show with Snoopy for [Merry Farm](/news/knotts-merry-farm-2026).
