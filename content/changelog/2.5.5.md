## New

- A banner in your browser's language offers the matching version of the page, and remembers
  when you close it (24 January).

## Improved

- Moving between pages cost four requests each time, two for your location and two for the data.
  Now it costs none within the cache time.
- Pages are fetched ahead of a click only for parks and rides that are open.

## Fixed

- The links in "Parks near you" used names instead of addresses and led to a 404.
