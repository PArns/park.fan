[Everland](ref:everland) in Yongin starts its Christmas season, Christmas Wish, on 21 November, with a wish tree about ten metres tall at the main entrance. The Bling Bling X-mas Parade and the night show Magic in the Sky join it on 27 November, and the whole programme runs until New Year's Eve. Samsung C&T, which runs the park, announced it on 16 November. Christmas Wish is the winter chapter of the Whatever Series, the run of monthly themes Everland has been putting on through 2026.

## The wish tree at the entrance

The Giant Wish Tree stands in Global Fair, with figures of the panda twins Rui Bao and Hui Bao next to it. Unlike the real pair, the figures don't have to go into quarantine.

In the first week's evenings the old fireworks show, The Guardians of Light, carries on until 23 November, and from 24 to 26 November Everland has no night show on the schedule at all.

## Parade and night show from 27 November

The Bling Bling X-mas Parade takes about half an hour and runs from the Rose Garden entrance to Carnival Square. Between the floats, several dozen performers dance to Christmas songs as Santa, Rudolph or a snowman. The parade already ran under that name in 2025, when its floats were shaped like a tree, a sleigh and a cake.

During Magic in the Sky, Christmas-themed video runs while several thousand fireworks go up above it, with lasers and music. Last winter the evening slot went to a K-Pop Demon Hunters sing-along that Everland ran every night with Netflix. The crowd sang along to "Golden" and "Soda Pop". Everland hasn't announced the show again for this winter.

Everland hasn't yet published the times for the parade and the show, or where the show can be seen. Once the times are set, they'll be in the daily schedule on everland.com.

## Snow Buster and the Winter Spa

The sledding slope Snow Buster opens at some point in December, with a family run where parents slide down with their children and a racing run. Last year the first snow attractions started on 12 December. Back then Snow Buster sat in Alpine Village, right beside Carnival Square, where the parade also ends.

Next door at the Caribbean Bay water park, Winter Spa Cabi starts on Thursday 3 December, two days earlier than in 2025. Every pool is heated then, the lazy river and the Aquatic Center included. To warm up there's also an outdoor bath made of hinoki cypress and a Finnish sauna.

Caribbean Bay has its own ticket. Until the end of December adults pay 45,000 won and children and seniors 36,000 won, or 39,000 and 30,000 won from 2:30 pm. On busy days the water park can stop letting people in. Until 2 December it's closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. You can rent a jacket for 8,000 won and a large towel for 6,000. Last winter the Ever to Cabi combo ticket let you switch between the two parks on the same day; Samsung C&T hasn't announced it for this winter so far.

## Tickets and opening hours

In November an Everland day ticket costs 65,000 won for adults and teenagers from 13 to 18. Children from 36 months to 12 years and seniors from 65 pay 55,000 won. In December it's 55,000 and 45,000 won. Wait until 1 December and you see the parade and the show at the December price. Children under 36 months get in free. Toddler-only attractions still cost extra for under-threes, as do a few other things in the park.

On opening day, Saturday 21 November, the park is open from 10 am to 9 pm, and on the Sunday and Monday after that until 8 pm. Everland publishes opening hours only a week ahead.

Some rides take a winter break, depending on the weather: the Play Yard from 23 November, Amazon Express from 7 December and Thunder Falls from 14 December, all until February or March. Safety inspections also close Hurricane from 23 to 26 November and Flying Rescue from 23 November to 2 December. Sky Cruise is shut from 30 November to 4 December and again from 14 to 17 December.

## Getting there

By metro, take the Bundang Line to Giheung and change to the EverLine, which runs from 5:30 am to 11:30 pm. From its last stop, a free shuttle bus runs to Everland and Caribbean Bay every five to ten minutes, the last one an hour after the park closes. Regular buses also run straight from Seoul, such as the 5700 via Jamsil and Suseo, or the 1500-2 from Sadang via Pangyo.

## Rui Bao and Hui Bao until 11 December

The panda twins are expected to make their last public appearance on 11 December, and in the third week of December they move to China. Since 13 November they've been in quarantine in the Panda Second House, where you can see them only on a limited basis in the indoor enclosure. That leaves three weeks in which you get the tree and the twins on the same day, and only 27 November to 11 December if you want the parade too. Our post on [Rui Bao and Hui Bao's farewell](/news/everland-pandas-ruibao-huibao-farewell) explains how the panda visits work.
