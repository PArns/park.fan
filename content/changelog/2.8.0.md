## New

- Ride and park cards have a photo area, the trend, a small chart of today, the day's average
  wait, the best time to go and a countdown to closing (24 April). The homepage's lists of the
  busiest and quietest parks and the longest and shortest waits use the same cards.
- A ticker along the bottom of the homepage photo runs through live waits with their trend. From
  28 May it is left out on phones.
- A banner on the park page warns when rain or a storm is coming (21 May), from 25 May up to an hour ahead, with
  bars for how hard it will rain and when. Close to the start the warning tells you to find shelter.
- A °C/°F switch. Fahrenheit is the default only in the United States and the five other places
  that use it.
- When our data service is down, the site shows a maintenance page instead of an error (28 May).

## Improved

- Ride pages and "Parks near you" refresh their live data every five minutes (21 May).
- The weather card shows wind with a compass, fog and snow, and a weather animation behind it.
- A closed park or ride has a grey card. The header is a little lower.
- Safari remembers that you allowed your location and stops asking on every page.

## Fixed

- A day that had already passed could still be marked as recommended.
- Upcoming quiet days bunched together; they are spread over the next 30 days now, one or two a
  week.
