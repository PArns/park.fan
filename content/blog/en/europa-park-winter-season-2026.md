[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?bare) starts its winter on Saturday 28 November and celebrates 25 years of winter seasons. In Winterland, the indoor hall called WinterWunderWelt has grown, and there's a new show in the ice stadium. The DINO MATES now have a play area of their own in the Spanish Arena. The MACK Group announced all of this in its winter factsheet.

## When the park is open

The winter season runs until Saturday 9 January 2027. The park opens at 11am every day and closes at 7pm at the earliest. It's shut on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. If you're staying at one of the resort hotels, you get in before the regular opening, and you also get one extra day on Sunday 10 January. That day the park opens only to hotel guests and invited guests, and the ticket booths stay closed.

## What's new

Winterland opened in winter 2025 on the Traumatica site, and you reach it through the Croatia area. The WinterWunderWelt hall inside it has more room this year: a kids' disco, a main stage with a show programme, a play area for children and adults, and a few shops.

For the DINO MATES Winter Adventure, the park has filled the Spanish Arena with dino bouncy castles and oversized building blocks. Parents get a lounge where bouncing is optional. The dino cinema shows episodes of "DINO MATES – Ferien bei Madame Freudenreich" on a big screen all day. MACK Magic produced the cartoon series for the ride [Madame Freudenreich Curiosités](ref:europa-park/madame-freudenreich-curiosites) together with Edel Kids and KiKA.

The new ice show is called "Surpr´Ice presents Christmas Spirit". It lasts 30 minutes and plays in the ice stadium in the Greek area, starting on 25 November, three days before the winter season itself. The park hasn't published the times for the ice show or the circus yet.

## Shows and lights

In the circus tent in Winterland, "Monte Carlo Circus Festival meets Europa-Park" runs for an hour. The park puts it on with the International Circus Festival of Monte-Carlo. Wolf Fisher hosts, and the Flying Martini perform on the flying trapeze.

More than 60 works from the [James Rizzi meets Rollercoasters](/news/europa-park-james-rizzi-exhibition) exhibition hang in the Croatia area until 9 January. From 5pm, a three-minute 3D mapping about Nikola Tesla plays every five minutes on the front of the [Voltron Nevera](ref:europa-park/voltron-nevera-powered-by-rimac) station in the same area. Over in the French area, [Eurosat – CanCan Coaster](ref:europa-park/eurosat-cancan-coaster) gets a mapping show of its own, "Miraculeux", every half hour from 5pm. On the Europa-See the park stages "Everglow", a show with water and fire, and the Magic Forest light installations glow in Balthasar Castle Park and on the meadow in the Austrian area. The Globe Theater has singing and dancing in "Season of Light". The Christmas market goes up around the Europa fountain and in Balthasar Castle Park.

## What winter costs

An adult day ticket (age 12 and up) costs €67 online until 22 December and €76 from 23 December. Children aged four to eleven and seniors from 60 pay €56.50 to €65 depending on the day, and younger children go free. At the ticket booth every day ticket costs €10 more. The evening ticket is valid from 4pm and costs adults €41.50 until 22 December and €47 after that, with no surcharge at the booth. Both mapping shows start at 5pm, so the evening ticket covers them.

I'd go before 23 December. The visitor car park costs €10 a day and opens at 9am in winter, and hotel guests park free at their hotel. If you'd rather spend the evening sitting down, book "The Grand Club", the dinner show in the Europa-Park Teatro: four courses from €152 per person, starting at 7:30pm, with park entry from 5pm. For Christmas and New Year's Eve, our [overview of winter 2026/27](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026) rounds up the parks that stay open.

## What runs and what doesn't

[Silver Star](ref:europa-park/silver-star), [blue fire Megacoaster](ref:europa-park/blue-fire-megacoaster), [Wodan – Timburcoaster](ref:europa-park/wodan-timburcoaster), Voltron Nevera, Eurosat and [ARTHUR](ref:europa-park/arthur) all keep running in winter, and so does even the [Poseidon water coaster](ref:europa-park/water-rollercoaster-poseidon). [Atlantica SuperSplash](ref:europa-park/atlantica-supersplash), [Fjord-Rafting](ref:europa-park/fjord-rafting) and the [Tiroler Wildwasserbahn](ref:europa-park/tirol-log-flume) take a break. At Fjord-Rafting the park builds its Nordic winter village with a big ice rink, so the only way to get wet there is to fall over. In ice, snow, strong wind or severe cold the park may close rides and shows at short notice.

The Bellevue Ferris wheel in the Portuguese area recovers energy when it brakes, and the park uses it to light and heat its 42 gondolas. Cold fingers in winter can thaw out 55 metres up.

This is the last winter for [Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir). On 9 January, the last day for day visitors, the park says goodbye to it with an event, and then it comes down. By 2028 a space-themed area with a new family coaster from MACK Rides is due to take its place. The park hasn't announced the programme for the [Euro-Mir farewell](/news/europa-park-euro-mir-farewell) yet. I wouldn't save my last ride for that Saturday, though: it already falls in the higher price band.

## Rulantica in winter

The [Rulantica](ref:rulantica) water park celebrates [Vinterglanz](/news/rulantica-vinterglanz-2026) for the first time, from 28 November to 10 January, with an evening show at the wave pool. It turns seven on 28 November. Unlike Europa-Park, Rulantica lets in visitors without a hotel stay on 10 January. Like the park, it's closed on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
