Christmas Town opens at [Busch Gardens Tampa Bay](ref:busch-gardens-tampa) in Florida on Friday, November 13, and this year it comes with a new drone show. On top of that there's the acrobatics show A Very Merry Cirque. The event runs until January 4, 2027, though not every day, and on event days it lasts from park opening to close. It's included with regular admission.

## New this year

The drone show is called Lights of Wonder. The drones fly to classic Christmas songs above Festival Field, whose entrance is near Pass Member Landing. The show runs until December 31, though not on every event day. GEICO sponsors it. On New Year's Eve the park puts on new fireworks, best seen from Festival Field as well.

A Very Merry Cirque plays on some event days between November and January, on a stage Busch Gardens hasn't named yet. For shopping there's the Christmas Town Market.

## Event days and hours in November

After the start on the 13th, Christmas Town takes a break from November 16 to 18 and on November 30. On the other 14 days in November the park opens at 10 am and closes at these times:

- Friday, November 13, and Sunday, November 15: until 9 pm
- Saturday, November 14: until 10 pm
- Thursday, November 19: until 8 pm
- Friday, November 20, through Thursday, November 26: until 9 pm, with a Thanksgiving Feast on Thanksgiving Day (the 26th)
- Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28: until 10 pm
- Sunday, November 29: until 9 pm

On opening night the Christmas tree on Cheetah Hunt Plaza lights up at 7 pm. Before that, local station Mix 100.7 broadcasts live from the plaza between 5 and 7.

## What else is on

Christmas on Ice plays the Moroccan Palace Theater for the whole event, with figure skaters, which in Tampa's climate counts as a minor achievement all by itself. Seating is first come, first served, and if the theater fills up, latecomers can find the doors shut before the show has even started.

In the Serengeti Light Show, thousands of lights move to music outside Serengeti Overlook Restaurant. Across the park, almost 70 miles (about 113 km) of lights hang for the Celebration of Lights, with stilt walkers in between. The Holly Jolly Express, a train playing Christmas songs, leaves [Nairobi Station](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/serengeti-express-nairobi-station) in the evening, with views over the 65-acre (26-hectare) savanna where giraffes, rhinos and zebras live.

The Ginja Ninjas breakdance on Jungala Plaza on some nights in November and December, weather permitting, to a story about Mrs. Claus's missing cookie recipe.

The Three Kings Journey at Dragon Fire Grill runs until January 4. The park gives two different start dates, November 21 on its show page and December 21 on its event page. From December 4 to 12, the menorah between the Moroccan Palace and the cheetah habitat is lit every evening at 6 pm. On December 17 the Coca-Cola Holiday Caravan stops on Festival Field with a free sample while supplies last.

## For kids

Elmo's Christmas Wish starts ahead of the event, on November 6, and plays the Sunny Day Theater in Sesame Street Safari of Fun until January 4. On some days you can take photos with the Sesame Street characters at 123 Smile.

Festival Walkway has life-size scenes from the Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer picture book until December 24, and you'll meet Rudolph and Bumble there. Santa, too, poses for photos with kids in his study at Santa's North Pole Experience only until December 24. Mrs. Claus, who reads stories at the Canopy, and the princesses Ice, Sugar Plum and Poinsettia stay until January 4, well after Santa has left.

## What costs extra

The Welcome Center and the holiday food stands open at 4 pm on event nights. A sampler lanyard with 5 or 10 tastings starts at $45, and pass members get 12 tastings for the price of 10. This year there's also a $35 dessert sampler with four desserts and a hot chocolate in a souvenir mug, valid for the whole event. The Savory Sleigh hut serves braised short rib, pumpkin soup and eggnog bread pudding, among other things, and Holiday Spice has a beef Wellington.

The Rudolph & Friends Character Dine costs $42.99. Two VIP tours last four hours each:

- Holly VIP Tour, private, $150: front-of-line access to Santa, reserved seats at Christmas on Ice, a flamingo feeding and a meal
- Jolly VIP Tour, guided, $125: bites at the huts, front-of-line access on some coasters, a giraffe feeding on the Serengeti Safari and reserved seats at Christmas on Ice

## Tickets and parking

In the Black Friday presale a day ticket starts at $71.99 instead of the regular $150.99, or $111.99 with All-Day Dining. The Ultimate Bundle includes unlimited Quick Queue, except on [Iron Gwazi](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/iron-gwazi) and [Phoenix Rising](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/phoenix-rising-now-open). The 2027 Fun Card costs $89.99 and is good for unlimited visits until December 31, 2027, blockout dates aside, with the rest of 2026 thrown in for free. Parking at 10165 N McKinley Drive in Tampa starts at $35 plus tax.
