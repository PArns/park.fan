# A `revalidate` at a call site is somebody else's page (REQUIREMENT)

One standing rule. It is indexed from the repo's [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), which carries the rule in one line and links here for the reasoning, the measurements and the counter-examples.

Next takes the **shortest** `revalidate` among the fetches a route executes, and `export const revalidate` does not override it — it is a default any shorter fetch beats, which was verified by declaring a week on the blog route and watching the manifest not move a single page. So a number typed into a component decides how often a whole class of pages rebuilds, and every rebuild is an ISR write **and** a full SSR render. `lib/blog/park-resolver.ts` asked for `getGeoStructure(3600)` to index park slugs for `ref:` links and thereby held **all 60 blog posts** to 24 rebuilds a day; `hero-stats` and `hero-world-panel` did the same to the homepage with `getGlobalStats(3600)` / `getGeoLiveStats(3600)`. `lib/api/ml.ts` had it written down backwards — „cached 1h — anything lower would pin the homepage shell's ISR window below its 3600s", as if the hour protected the homepage, when `MLStatsSection` reaches it through the AI story chapter and that fetch **was** the window, for a figure that changes once a day at 06:00 UTC. So: **never pass a numeric TTL at a call site**, put it in `CACHE_TTL` or the helper's default, and **set it from the data's cadence rather than as a floor under some page**. A seed with a client overlay may cache for a long time — `getGlobalStats`/`getGeoLiveStats` say „Only an SSR SEED" in their own docstrings and are replaced on mount — but where there is **no** overlay and the value is compared against a threshold, stale is _wrong_ rather than old, which is why the hottest-parks heat banner was removed instead of slowed down. A parameter is still right when the window belongs to the caller (`getParkWeatherNowcast` is load-bearing on one page and decoration in a blog post), provided the constant is named and the docstring says why. Finding these by grepping components missed the homepage four times; what works is walking the route's **import graph** for every reachable `revalidate` literal, then proving it against `initialRevalidateSeconds` in `.next/prerender-manifest.json` before and after a build. Measured: **5,032 → 657 regenerations/day, −86.9 %**. See [caching strategy](docs/architecture/caching-strategy.md#the-invisible-clock-a-hard-coded-ttl-argument-sep-2026).
