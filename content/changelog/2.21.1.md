## New

- A park's wait-time calendar marks each day of a season with an icon: a ghost for Halloween, a
  fir tree for Christmas, a snowflake for winter seasons such as Phantasialand's Wintertraum, a moon
  for summer nights and a sparkle for other events. The key under the month lists only the icons
  that month shows, and a day's details name the season, whether it is confirmed and whether it
  needs its own ticket. A season the park runs on selected days marks only those days.
- A ticket in the trip planner lists every country shop that sells it, such as "Attraction Tickets ·
  UK (£)", "· Ireland (€)" or "· USA ($)". The shop for your country comes first. Under two or more
  shops a line says that prices differ by the shop's country and that it pays to compare them.

## Improved

- On a long trip the planner spreads the park days over the trip instead of bunching them, at the
  same waits. It never costs a ticket day: a ticket you entered keeps its days within its window,
  and a suggested ticket keeps its days or gets fewer, so Disneyland Paris's consecutive days stay
  together.
- A ticket from a shop abroad, such as a UK 14-day ticket for Walt Disney World, counts like any
  other: the planner suggests it when it covers your days best, wherever it is sold.
- The hurricane and typhoon season sits at the top of the hub's timeline, under each date, instead
  of in the last lane under the calendar.
- A pinned day shows its lock in the week and month views too, and "Swap" says what a swap does to
  that day.
- When Parky sets your trip's dates, the date picker jumps to that month.
- The regions in the Plan menu open their hub at the top.
- Parky's box in the planners shows his face, and its text is larger.
- `/` opens the search even while a link or a chip has the focus.

## Fixed

- Parky sometimes wrote his tap replies as a "Replies" list into the answer, instead of showing
  them as buttons under it.
- Tickets named their hopper option "with .h"; they now read, for example, "1 Park Per Day or Park
  Hopper".
- Long dates in the day planner could show the next day for readers east of UTC+12.
