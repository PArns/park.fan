Christmas Studios, the winter season at [Parque Warner Madrid](ref:parque-warner-madrid) in San Martín de la Vega, opens on Saturday 21 November, a good two weeks earlier than last year. It runs until 10 January 2027. From 5 December the park adds a new bubble show, "El Reino de Cristal".

## New this winter

"El Reino de Cristal" lasts about 25 minutes and only plays in December. Its story is about an old kingdom that three gifts kept safe for centuries: hope, anticipation and magic. Every bubble holds a memory or a wish, and bubble by bubble the kingdom gets back what it had forgotten. At the end a mime tries to light an old lantern, the kingdom's last light. He can't manage it alone, so he brings up a volunteer from the audience.

ACME Factory: Christmas Candies is open from 21 November. In the Christmas version of the children's attraction that opened for Halloween, kids help the Looney Tunes recover the secret recipe for the Christmas sweets. So the recipe has gone missing again: at Halloween it was the pumpkin sweets, after the Coyote had torn through the factory.

Also new is the Estación Energía de Repsol, a hands-on walk through three rooms. You get lost in a mirror maze, play along with a light installation and walk through an installation modelled on rays of sunlight.

## Shows, parade and Santa

The 22-metre Christmas tree is back at the entrance. All five themed areas get lights and fake snow: Cartoon Village, Hollywood Boulevard, DC Super Heroes World, Movie World Studios and Old West Territory. The Looney Tunes switch on the Christmas tree in a show of their own.

On stage there's the musical The Santa's Magical Bell, the Looney Tunes Dance Festival in its Christmas Edition and, in the Western area, Una Navidad en Dodge City. From 5 December the Hollywood Boulevard dance show that premiered in 2025 is back. In Christmas Town, a small Christmas village with elves and reindeer, Santa shows off his workshop in El Taller de Papá Noel. The Three Kings receive visitors there too. Bugs Bunny, Tweety, Daffy Duck, the DC superheroes and Gizmo from Gremlins pose for photos in festive outfits. The day ends with the Superstars of Christmas Parade, with the Looney Tunes, superheroes, Santa and the Three Kings riding through the park on floats.

## Dates and opening hours

Christmas Studios is open on 29 days, always from noon:

- November: 21, 22, 28 and 29, until 8 p.m.
- December: 5 to 8, 12 and 13, 19 to 23 and 26 to 30, until 9 p.m.
- January 2027: 1 to 5 and 7 to 10, until 8 p.m.

Until mid-December, then, the park only opens at weekends and on 7 and 8 December. It's closed on 24, 25 and 31 December and on 6 January, so you won't spend Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve under the 22-metre tree.

## Tickets and children

Online, a day ticket costs from €32.90, the same for everyone 100 cm and taller. The online price depends on the day you visit. At the gate you pay €62.90 from 140 cm and €56.90 from 100 to 140 cm; guests aged 65 and over pay the children's gate price too. Children under 100 cm get in free. If you're coming more than once, look at the Bono Parques annual pass instead; our [Parque Warner Madrid guide](/blog/parque-warner-madrid-wait-times-tips) lists its prices along with the park's rain guarantee. Parking costs from €16 online.

Most of the Christmas programme is aimed at children, and the park pitches Santa's workshop explicitly at the youngest.

## What's changed since 2025

This is the third Christmas Studios. The second ran from 6 December to 5 January in 2025.

There were bubbles last winter as well. Back then, Bubble Christmas was new at the Teatro Chino, with the elf Bublinna, who collects the yawns children make before falling asleep and turns them into bubbles. Neither that show nor the Repsol Christmas Music Studio, where families could record their own Christmas song in 2025, appears on the 2026 programme. This time Repsol is there with the Estación Energía.

## Getting there

By car from Madrid, head south on the A-4 to exit 22; from there the park is signposted. Without a car, take Cercanías line C-3 to Pinto and then La Veloz bus 413, which stops at the entrance. Line 412 runs to the park from Villaverde Bajo. From Méndez Álvaro and Príncipe Pío, the park sells admission and the bus ride together.

## A day in the park

That leaves you eight to nine hours before the parade for rides, shows and characters. Queue times by ride and time of day are in our [guide](/blog/parque-warner-madrid-wait-times-tips). To skip them, there's the Pase Correcaminos, sold on top of admission.
