## New

- **A day that does not fit opens an assistant.** Pressing "plan all headliners" used to
  answer in an 11 px grey line under the buttons, and the ride that fell out was whichever
  one cost the most minutes of queueing, which is the one people came for. The planner now
  opens a three-step assistant: what would make room without dropping anything, the full
  list in the order the optimiser gives up on it, and the result with the dropped rides by
  name. Every suggestion is a measured difference between two plans. Across 35 park days at
  six parks, five did not fit, and two of those were solved by shortening a break.
- **The day ends when the park closes.** The grid read a closing hour of 18 as "open until
  18:59" and planned a ride at 18:15 in a park that shuts at 18:00. It now draws the hour
  after closing as slack you can drag into, and plans nothing into it. Joining a queue late
  still counts: the start time decides whether a stop happens, so a 40-minute queue at 17:45
  is a slot you can take on purpose.
- **Ride pages open the way park pages do.** "Today at this ride" is the park page's panel
  one page type over, with the live wait and its trend, today's range and peak, the
  recommended slots, the typical and busy pair, and the park's own status and opening hours,
  which a ride page never stated at all. The 30-day history is a chapter now, drawn in the
  crowd calendar's colours with the queue curve along the floor of each tile.
- **A height filter on park pages.** Every ride card already carried "from 120 cm", one card
  at a time. The filter appears only where the park has supplied minimum heights, because a
  slider whose every position shows the same 40 rides is worse than none, and its scale comes
  from that park's own limits.
- **Park pages can say where the park is.** Eleven curated fields now cover the address,
  phone number, website, ticket shop, Wikipedia, the park's social accounts, the year it
  opened and its area. None of the three upstream feeds carries any of it. A park with
  nothing filled in shows nothing rather than an empty frame.
- **Fastpass on the ride.** Where a park sells skip-the-queue access, the ride carries a badge
  with the product's name and price, formatted in the reader's language. A price of zero means
  it is included, which is the case at Europa-Park. Six brand names joined the glossary:
  QuickPass, Virtual Line, Fast Lane, Speedy Pass, Fastrack and Premier Access.
- **The site answers machines as well.** An agent that has only the hostname now finds
  `/llms.txt`, an API catalogue under `/.well-known/api-catalog`, three published skills with
  their SHA-256 digests, and an MCP server at `/api/mcp` with three read-only tools. A park
  that publishes no wait times is reported as exactly that instead of as a park averaging zero
  minutes.

## Improved

- **The weather chart is built around the opening hours.** An open hour is drawn four times as
  wide as a closed one, so the hours of a visit take 74 % of the chart instead of 42 %. Both
  changes of slope sit on the dashed edges of the opening-hours band, which now carry a door
  icon and the opening and closing time. There is room for an hourly tick through the visit,
  the temperature on arrival and on leaving, and a mark on the two wettest runs of the day.
- **The header became a band.** Countries arrive with their flags, parks with their photos, and
  the panel behind "More" reads as a menu: each section is a card with an icon, and the whole
  card is the link. The blog is a separate entry in the bar again, under Backstage.
- **The °C / °F switch moved into the header.** It governs temperatures in the weather calendar,
  in blog posts and on the best-time hub, and it used to sit in the weather card's own header,
  so it existed on park pages only. It is next to the language and theme controls now, on every
  page, and it keeps a fixed width so pressing it does not shift the row.
- **The sitemap says which pages changed.** A daily crawl fingerprints what a reader sees and
  reports the date it last moved, rather than stamping all 44,000 URLs with today.

## Fixed

- **The park map went blank.** OpenStreetMap's own tile server is not for embedding, and on
  18 September its edge started answering every tile on every park page with "403 Access
  blocked". Both maps now draw from CARTO's basemap CDN, credited to OpenStreetMap and CARTO.
- **The tile row stayed where it was.** Going from a park page to its calendar and back cost a
  jump to the top and an animated scroll back down, every time, in both directions. The row now
  keeps its position across the navigation.
- **The live column disappeared at opening time** in the blog's wait-time tables, and four of
  those tables still pointed at a park that had been renamed.
- **Seven layout shifts,** two of which the measurement harness had been unable to see from the
  top of the page.
- **The favourite star sits in its ring again,** park names in the quiet-day card are no longer
  truncated, the quietest weekday is back when two days tie, and the icon next to park.fan in
  Google's results is legible at the size it is drawn.
