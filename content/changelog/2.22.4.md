title: The wait-time history stops calling open days closed, and rides with set showtimes drop best visit times
summary: >
  The wait-time history on a ride's page showed its oldest day as closed on every ride, and a ride
  without a queue, such as a playground, as closed on every day it ran. Both now read as they
  were. A ride that starts at set times no longer suggests best visit times, since its next show is
  the answer.
mode: published
---

## Improved

- A ride that starts at set times, such as Phantasialand's 4D cinema Die 3 Mausketiere during
  Wintertraum, no longer shows best visit times on its page, its card or its day chart. The next
  show stands in their place.
- Parky names a ride's next show instead of a quiet time for rides that start at set times.

## Fixed

- The wait-time history on every ride's page drew 31 days while the history holds 30, so its oldest
  day always read "Ride Closed". It now covers the last 30 days.
- A ride without a queue, such as a playground, read "Ride Closed" in its history on the days it ran,
  because its wait is always 0 minutes. Those days now read "Open, no wait".
