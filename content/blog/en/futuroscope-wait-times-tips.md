Anyone who arrived in Chasseneuil-du-Poitou on 31 May 1987 found a pavilion with a sphere on a
sloping surface, and a cinema. According to the French Wikipedia, the surface was meant to show a
sunrise over a changing world, which is how the architect Denis Laming designed it. The cinema was
called Kinémax and was in the park from its first season. [Futuroscope](ref:futuroscope) didn't
start with a roller coaster but with a screen, and you can still tell: almost every attraction
here is a show, a walk-through in the dark or a seat that moves with the film.

The park is 20 minutes from Poitiers and belongs to the resort Futuroscope Xperiences, which is
what the whole site has been called since May 2024. The resort puts its own visitor count at
around 2.5 million a year, while the Themed Entertainment Association counts 2.05 million for the
park alone in 2024. We've been measuring wait times here since 6 October 2024, currently at 55
attractions.

## From cinema pavilion to resort

On 11 December 1984, René Monory, the president of the Vienne departmental council, laid the
foundation stone. He had dreamed up a city of the future in the early 1980s, and the park was its
first part. It opened on 31 May 1987 with the pavilion, the Kinémax and a play area. Cinema after
cinema followed: the first Cinéma Dynamique in 1988, the Omnimax and the Gyrotour observation
tower in 1990, the IMAX 3D in 1996, and in 2000 the TGV station with a footbridge straight into
the park.

The park hit its peak in 1997 with 2.9 million visitors, according to the French Wikipedia. After
that it went downhill, and by 2003 it was down to 1.16 million. Since 14 January 2011 it has been
run by the Compagnie des Alpes, which also operates Parc Astérix, the Walibi parks in France,
Belgium and the Netherlands, and Bellewaerde. Arthur, l'aventure 4D opened at Christmas 2009, and
in February 2010 the French Wikipedia records 48 percent more visitors than a year earlier.

On 3 July 2020 the Vienne département adopted a plan called Vision 2025: around 300 million euros
over ten years, split between the park and a resort on part of the old car parks. It produced
Chasseurs de Tornades (2022), the Station Cosmos hotel and the Space Loop restaurant (both opened
on 29 April 2022), the Ecolodgee hotel (2023), the Aquascope (15 July 2024) and Mission Bermudes
(June 2025). The whole site has been called Futuroscope Xperiences since 29 May 2024.

## The big attractions, and what each one asks for

The park sets minimum heights ride by ride, and there are usually two: one for children with an
adult and one for children on their own. A child below the first can't ride. At the info points
in the park the child is measured and gets a wristband, which tells staff at every ride where it's
allowed. Depending on height, a child who can't ride may be able to watch from the side at some
attractions.

### Objectif Mars

[Objectif Mars](ref:futuroscope/destination-mars) has been running since 2020 and, apart from the
junior coaster Le Balancier in Futuropolis, is the park's only roller coaster. Intamin built it
as a launched coaster with rotating vehicles, a spinning coaster. The park calls it a family
coaster and gives a top speed of 55 km/h, and the track runs through a building and out into the
open. It was due to open on 28 March 2020; because of the pandemic closure it started in June
2020, at first on weekends only.

With an adult you can ride from 1.10 metres, alone from 1.20 metres, and the upper limit is
1.95 metres. The park excludes heart problems, epilepsy, back or neck trouble and pregnancy. There
is a test seat at the entrance where you can check whether the restraints will close.

### Mission Bermudes

[Mission Bermudes](ref:futuroscope/mission-bermudes) arrived in June 2025 and replaced the Jardin
des Énergies. The park describes it as a cross between a water ride and a roller coaster: a boat,
rapids, unexplained phenomena and a drop at the end. The French Wikipedia lists it as a Mack
Rides rocking boat with a ride time of just under five minutes. You can get wet, and in bad
weather it stays closed.

From 1.05 metres with an adult, from 1.40 metres alone. The same exclusions apply as at Objectif
Mars (heart, back, epilepsy, pregnancy), and prostheses are allowed.

### Chasseurs de Tornades

[Chasseurs de Tornades](ref:futuroscope/tornado-chasers) opened in summer 2022 in the Images
Studio pavilion and won the Themed Entertainment Association's Thea Award the same year. The
platform you stand on rises and falls, tilts and turns in front of a circular LED screen where a
tornado forms, with effects and scenes that are played live. It lasts five minutes.

From 1.05 metres with an adult, from 1.40 metres alone. The park advises against it for people
with epilepsy and those prone to dizziness.

### Danse avec les Robots

[Danse avec les Robots](ref:futuroscope/dances-with-robots) is a Robocoaster: you sit on the arm
of an industrial robot that dances to hits by the DJ Martin Solveig. The ride lasts about a
minute. The robot pavilion opened in 2006, the year of the robots, and the version with Solveig
dates from December 2012. The park talks about a robot nine metres tall.

There's just one limit here: from 1.20 metres, up to 2.00 metres. Anyone shorter can't ride, not
even with an adult.

### L'Extraordinaire Voyage

[L'Extraordinaire Voyage](ref:futuroscope/the-extraordinary-journey) is a flying theatre by
Dynamic Attractions in the Tapis Magique pavilion. You hang in a seat with your feet dangling
above the floor and fly over footage of places around the world. It opened in 2017, the year of
the park's 30th birthday, and lasts about five minutes.

From 1.05 metres with an adult, from 1.20 metres alone, up to 2.00 metres.

### The rest of the seated rides

- [Arthur, l'aventure 4D](ref:futuroscope/arthur-the-4d-adventure) in the IMAX dome: you're
  shrunk to the size of the Minimoys and fly through their world in a moving seat. From 1.05 and
  1.20 metres.
- [La Machine à Voyager dans le Temps](ref:futuroscope/the-time-machine): a dark ride with the
  Raving Rabbids, about six minutes, from 0.90 metres with an adult and from 1.20 metres alone.
- [Dynamic!](ref:futuroscope/dynamic) and [La Vienne Dynamique](ref:futuroscope/dynamic-vienne):
  moving seats synchronised to a film. The first shows the film EcoDingo in 2026, from 1.20
  metres; La Vienne Dynamique dates from 1994, a rally film, from 1.05 metres.
- [Hydro Dynamo](ref:futuroscope/hydro-dynamo): a swing that seats several guests and swings up
  to twelve metres. From 1.05 and 1.20 metres.
- [Étincelle](ref:futuroscope/etincelle): a 4D film with lasers in which a young heroine takes on
  the villain Ténèbre, running since February 2023.
- The [Kinémax](ref:futuroscope/kinemax) shows IMAX films in laser projection, alternating
  between "Dans les Yeux de Thomas Pesquet" and "Antarctica". Under domes there's also Chocs
  Cosmiques, a film with Lorànt Deutsch under a planetarium dome, and since 2026 the film T. Rex.
- The Gyrotour, an observation tower by Intamin from 1990, lifts guests to 45 metres. The Aérobar,
  a bar at 35 metres where your feet hang free, has the same limits as most of the seated
  rides: from 1.05 metres with an adult, from 1.20 metres alone.

## Futuropolis and the wristbands

Futuropolis is the outdoor area for children, three hectares in size, opened in 1988 as Monde des
Enfants and under its current name since 2018. The French Wikipedia counts 21 attractions and
play areas, including carousels by Heege, Zierer and Zamperla.

The wristband decides what a child can do. There are four colours, graded by height. These are
the limits the park gives at the attractions:

Among the larger rides are [Sauvetage Academy](ref:futuroscope/rescue-academy), a jet-ski ride by
Zierer, and [Aventure Éclabousse](ref:futuroscope/splash-adventure), an airboat with water
cannons where you shoot at targets. Both are water rides, so you'll get wet.
[La Maison à l'envers](ref:futuroscope/upside-down-house) is a house standing on its head, with
photo spots; the park gives no height limit for it.

For children too small for a ride there's the "Solution Jeunes Enfants", Baby Switch in English.
You ask for it at the entrance to the attraction. Children aged 5 to 12 pay the child rate,
under fives get in free, and every restaurant has a menu for children under twelve.

## The evening in the park

Futuroscope doesn't shut when the rides do. On every evening the park is open there's **La Clé
des Songes**, the night spectacle on the Lac aux Images. According to the park it lasts about
25 minutes, is included in the ticket and goes ahead in the rain unless the weather is
exceptional. It has 3D water images, light and pyrotechnics. The show has run since June 2021 and
replaced La Forge aux Étoiles, which people behind Cirque du Soleil had designed.

There are two more shows during the day. **Éclipse, robotic show** was made in 2024 with Cirque
Éloize in the former IMAX 3D pavilion and mixes video mapping, lasers and drones. **IllusiO, un
destin magique** is a 25-minute illusion show that runs on weekends and in school holidays.

If you only want the evening, buy a Soirée ticket (entry from 5 pm) or a Nocturne ticket (from
8 pm). Both include the evening show and the attractions that are open in the evening.

### Passes that get you past the queue

The park sells three passes on top of admission. They're only available on certain dates and in
limited numbers.

- **Pass Premium+**: five priority-lane entries for one day, to be spread over the rides of your
  choice and over several people (one entry per person and ride). 40 to 60 euros depending on
  the date, from age five. Futuropolis, Pulse, La Maison à l'envers, Les Yeux Grands Fermés and
  the Aérobar are excluded. It also gives ten percent off in the park shops.
- **Pass Gold**: priority access to all attractions without limit, one entry to three attractions
  in Futuropolis, the Gold seats at the evening show, parking and 15 percent off in the shops.
  99 to 149 euros depending on the date.
- **VIP Expérience**: personal welcome, priority access, VIP seats for the evening show, unlimited
  beer and soft drinks and a VIP parking space, from 500 euros for adults.

Whether the Premium+ pays off depends on how wide the top of the table above is. Where the most
popular rides share a ranking, five entries mostly buy you predictability, and that's worth
something different on a Saturday in August than on a Thursday in September.

### The weekday

Saturday is the busiest day, by a clear margin over all the others. Thursday, Friday and Sunday
have the shortest waits, with Monday to Wednesday in between. Each weekday rests on 30 to 35 days
in our data.

One caveat: in autumn the park is often closed from Monday to Wednesday, for three weeks in
September and from 12 to 14 in October 2026. The open days of those weekdays therefore fall
mostly in spring and summer, when it's busier anyway. The comparison with Thursday isn't entirely
clean for that reason.

### The month

```stats-widget slug=futuroscope show=months

```

August is the busiest month, with April and the Easter holidays second. July isn't busier than
May despite the start of the holidays. September has the shortest waits of the main season, and
February is low as well. October has too few measured days to be assessed.

The 2026 season runs, according to the Carte Saison calendar, from 7 February 2026 to 3 January 2027,
but not every day: in November the park opens, per the calendar, only on weekends once 1 November
is past, in December likewise until the 18th, and daily from then until 3 January. That explains
why our readings for November and December are thin.

### Futuroween 2026

Futuroween, Futuroscope's Halloween, runs from 17 October to 1 November 2026. The theme is
aliens collecting pumpkins. The programme includes **Galactic Talents**, a dance contest between
zombies, monsters and pumpkins (18 to 31 October). In Kinékid "Famille Monstre" plays in 3D, and
Grocauchemar appears in the evening show La Clé des Songes.

Les Yeux Grands Fermés, the walk through darkness with a blind guide, has a Futuroween variant in
which a magician disrupts the journey. The park recommends it from age twelve. The visit costs a
contribution in support of visually impaired people, which the ticket doesn't cover.

Futuroween starts on Saturday 17 October, a day before the autumn holidays. From 18 October the
park is open daily from 9:45 am to 10 pm according to the calendar.

## Tickets, cards and prices

At the ticket office a dated day ticket costs 59 euros for adults and 49 euros for children aged
5 to 12. Online the price depends on the date. For 2026 the park is advertising the Billet Tribu
at 37 euros per person for groups of four or more, valid from 25 May 2026 to 3 January 2027 and
bookable up to three days before the visit. A dated ticket can be changed or cancelled free of
charge up to three days before the visit.

A package with two days, one night, the evening show and breakfast starts at 126.50 euros per
adult. The "3rd day free" offer runs until 3 January 2027. Students, people with disabilities and
large families get 15 percent off the ticket.

A **Carte Saison** costs 132 euros for adults and 112 euros for children in 2026. It's valid from
7 February 2026 to 3 January 2027 and, according to the park, has paid for itself from the third
visit. With the card, the day ticket at the gate of Parc Astérix, Grévin Paris, France
Miniature, Walibi Rhône-Alpes, Walibi Belgium, Bellewaerde, Walibi Holland and Family Park is
half price. Anyone who had a card in 2025 gets 20 percent off the renewal. You get the card at
the Futuroscope Destination agency at the main entrance, and the photo is taken on site.

The Aquascope, the covered water park on the site, isn't included in the park ticket (more on
that further down, in the section on the resort).

## Getting there

**By car.** From the A10 take exit 28 "Futuroscope", which brings you onto the Avenue René
Monory. Parking is 13 euros for the day (from 8 am to 8 am the next morning) and 8 euros from
2 pm. If you don't want to queue at the exit, pay at the machine or at the booth in advance.
Motorhomes may park in the car park, overnight too, but without electricity, water or waste
disposal; caravans aren't allowed. Charging points for electric cars cost extra.

**By train.** Futuroscope station has a footbridge straight into the park, open according to
train times. OUIGO runs classic trains between Paris and Bordeaux that call at the station every
weekend, from 10 euros. From Poitiers station it's 20 minutes by taxi, by bus on lines 1 and 21
(E on Sundays) or by TER via Futuroscope. Poitiers-Biard airport is 15 minutes away by taxi.

**Luggage, pets, strollers.** Left luggage at the main entrance and at the station costs 8 euros
a day. Strollers can be rented for 10 euros a day while stock lasts. Dogs aren't allowed in the
park; at the entrance to the car park there's a free kennel, for which a reservation and a
vaccination record are needed. Wi-Fi is free.

**Language.** Translations inside the attractions are available in English and Spanish, through
the app "ListenWIFI". The park doesn't mention German. The park app comes in French, English and
Spanish.

## Eating and staying overnight

There are about a dozen restaurants and snack bars in the park, and the park sorts them by level
of service. You're served at the table in **L'Atelier des Saveurs**. The fixed-price buffet is
**La Table d'Arthur**. Self-service is at the **Quai Gourmand** and **MIAM !**, quick and
takeaway at the **Food Court Festival**, **La Crêpe Volante** and **Bermuda Bar & Food**. All
restaurants have a children's menu for under-twelves. With the Carte Saison you get ten percent
off.

The operator markets the **Space Loop** between the park and the Aquascope as a "Rollercoaster
Restaurant". It opened on 29 April 2022 together with the Station Cosmos hotel.

Three hotels belong to the resort: the **Station Cosmos**, with rooms fitted out like cabins on a
space station (hotel and ticket package from 196 euros per adult), the **Ecolodgee**, with lodges
and terraces (from 166.50 euros), and the **Hôtel du Futuroscope**, with direct access to the
park (from 126.50 euros). The prices are for 2026, for a room in the lowest category with two
adults, without promotions and without tourist tax. Nine more partner hotels with one to four
stars are less than ten minutes' walk from the entrance.

## What's new

For the 2026 season Futuroscope has rebuilt three pavilions. **La Serre des Mondes**, in the
former 360° pavilion, is a walk-through of a plant world with interactive projections, 16 minutes
long according to the French Wikipedia. **T. Rex** is a film in the Omnimax, now projected with
laser, 24 minutes. **Pulse ! L'Odyssée électrique**, in the historic Pavillon du Futuroscope, is
about energy and was made with EDF and Dalkia; entry is free. The Food Court Festival on the
square in front of the Kinémax is new as well.

Les Yeux Grands Fermés was relaunched in 2025. Mission Bermudes has been the newest ride since
June 2025.

## Aquascope, ZerOGravity and Arena

Three facilities next to the park belong to the resort and need their own ticket.

The **Aquascope** is a covered water park that opened on 15 July 2024. The park lists 24 entries
there. The slides section has nine, and eight of them have a minimum height (Reflex and Flash from
1.10 metres, Matrix, Sprint and Alien from 1.20, Spiral and Quick from 1.35, Rocket from 1.40).
There's also a children's area with tentacles, a water maze and a music grotto, and a summer
beach with terraces. Because capacity is limited, there are only tickets with a fixed time slot
and no ticket office; the adult ticket starts at 39 euros. Strollers aren't allowed in the
Aquascope, and there's no left luggage. The park names two awards for it, from the World
Waterpark Association and from the Themed Entertainment Association.

**ZerOGravity** is opposite the park and the Aquascope and is a wind tunnel for free fall. The
operator describes two flights with an instructor that are supposed to equal two jumps from
4,200 metres. The Xperience takes 1:30 hours and starts from age five; children aged 5 to 12
need an adult with them, teenagers aged 12 to 17 need written parental consent, and weight is
capped at 120 kilograms. The price is 69 euros for adults and 55 euros for children. With the
Carte Saison you get ten percent off the "Adrénaline" flight and the "Kid" flight.

The **Arena Futuroscope** is a hall for shows and sport with 6,144 seats, which opened on 7 April 2022. It has nothing to do with a park visit, but it's on the same site.

## A day in the park, roughly planned

In October 2026 the park opens at 10 am on weekdays and 9:45 am on weekends, and daily at 9:45 am during the autumn holidays. It closes at 8:45 pm or 10 pm depending on the day. The evening belongs to the show, which is a reason to take the
rides early and the films later.

1. **Before opening** Pick up the wristband for the children at an info point. Then straight to
   Objectif Mars or Mission Bermudes.
2. **Morning** Chasseurs de Tornades and L'Extraordinaire Voyage. Both start at 1.05 metres, and
   both are near the top of our ranking.
3. **Midday** Lunch, then a film in the Kinémax or the dome, Chocs Cosmiques for example if it's
   raining.
4. **Afternoon** Danse avec les Robots, Arthur and the Time Machine. With children, Futuropolis
   after that, and when it rains La Serre des Mondes or Pulse.
5. **Late afternoon** IllusiO on weekends and in the holidays, Éclipse otherwise. The times are in
   the park's calendar on the day itself.
6. **Evening** La Clé des Songes on the Lac aux Images.

A second day is mostly worth it with children, because Futuropolis then carries the whole day.
With the "3rd day free" offer it costs little.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many attractions does Futuroscope have?

We list 55 entries, including films, shows and walk-throughs without a queue. The French
Wikipedia names 40 attractions in the park, 21 of which are in Futuropolis.

### How tall does my child have to be for the rides?

The lowest limit among the big attractions belongs to the Time Machine, at 0.90 metres with an
adult. At 1.05 metres with an adult you can ride Mission Bermudes, Chasseurs de Tornades,
L'Extraordinaire Voyage and Arthur, alone from 1.20 metres; at Mission Bermudes and Chasseurs de
Tornades alone from 1.40 metres. Objectif Mars starts at 1.10 metres with an adult and 1.20
metres alone, Danse avec les Robots at 1.20 metres without one.

### What does a day at Futuroscope cost?

At the ticket office, 59 euros for adults and 49 euros for children aged 5 to 12. Children under
five pay nothing. Online there's the Billet Tribu at 37 euros per person from four people, and a
package with a hotel night starts at 126.50 euros. Parking is 13 euros, or 8 euros from 2 pm.

### When is Futuroscope least busy?

On Thursdays, Fridays and Sundays, and in September. Saturday is the busiest day, August the
busiest month. In autumn the park is often closed from Monday to Wednesday.

### Is the Premium+ pass worth it?

It pays off on days with many guests, so on a Saturday or in the holidays, if you want to be sure
of riding five particular rides. It costs 40 to 60 euros depending on the date, only applies to
the attractions the park doesn't exclude, and is sold only on certain dates in limited numbers.
On a Thursday in September it saves you little.

### Is the evening spectacle included in the ticket?

Yes. La Clé des Songes is included in every admission, every package and every stay, on the day
of the visit, and runs on every evening the park is open. Only Les Yeux Grands Fermés and a few
special events are excluded.

### What applies to visitors with disabilities?

With the Carte Mobilité Inclusion, the Carte d'invalidité, the Carte Priorité or the EU Disability
Card you get a wristband at the info point behind the main entrance that gives the person and up
to three companions priority access to the attractions, provided the person rides along. The
ticket costs 15 percent less at the ticket office, for a companion too. The German
Schwerbehindertenausweis isn't on the list of accepted proofs.

### Can you get there without a car?

Yes. Futuroscope station is right at the park and has a footbridge into the entrance area. TGV
and TER stop there, and on weekends OUIGO too, from 10 euros. From Poitiers station it's
20 minutes.

### Do you have to book the Aquascope separately?

Yes. The Aquascope has its own ticket with a time slot, because capacity is limited, and there's
no ticket office. The adult ticket starts at 39 euros. It isn't included in the park ticket.

### Are dogs and strollers allowed?

Dogs aren't allowed in the park; they can stay free of charge in the kennel at the entrance to
the car park, with a reservation and a vaccination record. The park rents out strollers for
10 euros a day.
