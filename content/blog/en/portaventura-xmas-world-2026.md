From Friday 20 November, Christmas at [PortAventura Park](ref:portaventura-park) in Salou goes by the name Xmas World. Until 6 January 2027, each themed area is decorated after a different Christmas tradition, and Far West and México get two new musicals. This winter is the last outing for the lake show La Llegada de los Emisarios Reales, which the park will rework for next year. PortAventura announced all of this on 16 November.

## A different Christmas in each area

Mediterrània is dressed up as a Catalan village, with a giant Tió de Nadal. In the street show El Tió de Nadal, children sing and beat the huge log with sticks until surprises tumble out. In Catalan that's called "fer cagar el tió", and you'd better look up the verb before you put it on a Christmas card. China hangs red and gold lanterns that already point to Chinese New Year, and in Winter Imperial China a dragon and its dancers parade across the Plaza Imperial.

México is decked out with piñatas and papel picado. In México suena a Navidad, the villagers get a party ready for Woody Woodpecker, and ¡Viva la Posada! passes in front of La Hacienda restaurant as a procession in the tradition of the posadas, where people go asking for shelter. It ends with a Christmas piñata being smashed.

Far West gets the classic Christmas look, with garlands, lights and decorated trees. At 5:15 p.m. the characters on Penitence's main street count down in Christmas Lights, the big tree lights up and fake snow falls over the town. In What a Crazy Christmas, the Crazy Brothers are out with Holly and a troupe of dancers, though Santa Claus hasn't turned up yet. Each of these street shows runs for eight minutes. In SésamoAventura, Grover, Elmo and Ernie celebrate Christmas on stage in the theatre show Ya es Navidad en SésamoAventura.

## Two new musicals, both with a minimum spend

The saloon in Penitence puts on La Gran Rifa de Navidad. Pianist Sarah Joplin and Lilly Parsonn have been rigging the town's Christmas raffle for years, and this time the winning ticket lands with a penniless beggar. At La Cantina in México, Navidad a la Mexicana works through the country's customs up to Christmas Eve, with piñatas, villancicos and pastorelas.

Both last 20 to 30 minutes, and both come with a minimum spend, so you'll be ordering something to watch. In a saloon, that's at least in character. Shows you can see without buying anything include Circo de Navidad at the Gran Teatro Imperial in China, about a girl who dreams of the circus, and Bubble Magic Christmas at the Templo Mágico, where an alchemist blows soap bubbles. There's also Cuidemos el Planeta, and El Bosque Encantado, a path in front of Serpiente Emplumada in México that leads to Santa's house. Up to 25 December your photo there is with Santa Claus; from the 26th it's with one of the Three Kings.

## The parade and the last lake show

Towards the end of the day, the Christmas Parade brings floats and characters along the Paseo in Mediterrània. Then, in La Llegada de los Emisarios Reales, the envoys of the Three Kings land on the lake, with fireworks, flyboard stunts and dancing on the water. It's modelled on Spain's Cabalgata de Reyes. PortAventura hasn't published what will play on the lake after the rework, or from when.

## Opening and closing times

From 20 November to 6 January the park opens every day at 10:30 a.m. and usually closes at 6 p.m. Closing time moves to 7 p.m. on 5, 6 and 7 December, from 27 to 30 December and from 1 to 4 January.

The parade and the lake show don't run every day. The park's daily calendar lists them at 5:45 and 6 p.m. on Saturday 12 December, Christmas Eve, and 5 and 6 January, and at 6:45 and 7 p.m. on Sunday 27 December. Neither is listed for Wednesday 2 December, though Christmas Lights at 5:15 p.m. is. For opening day on Friday, the calendar so far shows only four shows, none of the new ones and no parade. If you want to catch the lake show one last time, pick a day it's listed.

From 7 January the park is closed, all through February too, which makes the winter break longer than Xmas World itself. According to the calendar it reopens on 5 March, from 4 p.m. to 9 p.m. that day.

## Tickets

There's no separate Christmas ticket on the ticket page; you buy the regular park ticket. Online, a day costs from €43 for adults aged 11 to 59 and from €39 for children aged 4 to 10 and visitors 60 and over. With the promo code REGALAPAW, that drops to €37 and €33. The code only works when you buy on the website, for purchases and visits up to 6 January, and for 1,000 tickets in total. Two days in the park cost from €75, or from €41 with the code. All prices depend on the day you visit, and a dated ticket can be cancelled up to three days ahead.

Reduced tickets for people with a disability of 33 percent or more are sold only at the ticket office and the Guest Service Office.

## Ferrari Land and Caribe in winter

In November and early December, [Ferrari Land](ref:ferrari-land) opens only on Fridays from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. and at weekends from 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m., plus 7 and 8 December. From 18 December to 5 January it opens daily, mostly from 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m., and from 1 p.m. to 7 p.m. on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. The same 1 p.m. to 7 p.m. applies on 6 January, after which Ferrari Land closes too. Since PortAventura Park usually shuts at 6 p.m. and Ferrari Land stays open until 10 p.m., the one-day ticket for both parks pays off on those days, from €55 or from €44 with the code. A day at Ferrari Land alone costs from €19.

Caribe Aquatic Park is closed in November, December and January.

## Which rides are running

On Saturday 12 December and Sunday 27 December, the daily calendar lists 39 attractions, among them [Shambhala](ref:portaventura-park/shambhala?bare), [Dragon Khan](ref:portaventura-park/dragon-khan?bare), [Furius Baco](ref:portaventura-park/furius-baco?bare), Stampida, [Uncharted](ref:portaventura-park/uncharted?bare) and Hurakan Condor. On 20 November it's 23, on 2 December 25, and on Friday Stampida and Furius Baco are among those missing. Tutuki Splash, Silver River Flume, Angkor and Sea Odyssey aren't listed on any of those four days, whereas Grand Canyon Rapids is, on 12 and 27 December until 5 p.m. PortAventura hasn't published a list of rides closed for the winter.

## Getting there

The park is on Avinguda Alcalde Pere Molas in Vila-seca, and by car you drive straight there from the A-7. Parking costs from €15 per car per day and covers every park in the resort, but you can only book it online together with your tickets.

By train, ride to the resort's own PortAventura World station, served by Rodalies lines R-17 and RT2. The combined ticket with Rodalies includes the return trip. Because of Adif engineering works between Garraf and Sitges, a replacement service runs between Barcelona and Sant Vicenç de Calders, as the park points out on its travel page. From elsewhere in Spain, take the AVE or another long-distance train to Camp de Tarragona and the Plana L4 bus from there. Reus airport is 15 minutes away, Barcelona-El Prat an hour.

## What's changed since 2024

In 2024, Christmas at PortAventura ran from 23 November to 6 January. Christmas Dream at the Gran Teatro Imperial, La Navidad de Woody in Polynesia and Christmas Time at the Long Branch Saloon appear in neither the 2026 press release nor the calendar, and the Gran Teatro Imperial now plays Circo de Navidad. Bubble Magic Christmas, Ya es Navidad en SésamoAventura, the parade and the lake show are still there.

La Posada de los Gnomos in Far West, next to The Iron Horse restaurant, also opened only at Christmas back in 2024. This time it serves a menu of seafood cream soup, meloso de ternera and tronco de Navidad. Racó de Mar in Mediterrània has canelones and sopa de Navidad, La Cantina and Taquería La Catrina serve esquites and tacos de cochinita pibil, and Cantón has tanghulu, the Chinese New Year sweet. The 2024 season included a Cabalgata de Reyes in the resort on 5 January. PortAventura hasn't announced whether there'll be one on 5 January 2027.
