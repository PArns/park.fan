You can now message Parky, our chat assistant on park.fan. He lives in the
bottom right corner, a green speech bubble with big eyes and a Ferris wheel for
a hat, and he looks things up for you in park.fan's live data. Tap him and ask
where the queues are short right now. Ask the same thing twenty minutes later
and he'll check again without a sigh.

Not everyone sees him yet, because Parky is only switched on for some visitors
so far. If he isn't in your bottom right corner, you can bring him in on
[his own page](/parky) with the "Ask Parky now" button. He's free and needs no
account, like the rest of park.fan.

## What you can ask Parky

For every answer, Parky looks at the same data you see on park.fan: the waits
right now, the forecast for the next hours and days, opening hours and the
weather. When he wants to show you a ride or a park, he drops it into the chat
as a card with its status, crowd level and wait. Tap the card and you're on the
ride's or the park's page.

![Parky in the chat on a phone. Asked about the queue at Taron, he gives the wait time and adds a card for Taron below, marked open with a normal crowd level. | Taron first, and the next question is already about what else is quick.](/parky/en/chat.webp?align=right)

A few questions to start with:

- "Which ride at Europa-Park has the shortest wait right now?"
- "How busy will Phantasialand be on Saturday?"
- "When is Efteling quietest in December?"
- "Will it rain at Movie Park this afternoon?"
- "How fast is Silver Star?"

You don't have to ask as neatly as this list does. Parky also knows the
park.fan glossary: ask "What is airtime, anyway?" and he looks it up there. On
"What's new?" he goes through our posts and news, so he'll tell you about this
one too, in case you skipped it to get here.

## On a park's page

On a park's page, "here" means that park. Under his greeting you'll then find
questions that fit it, such as "Which rides are down right now?", "When does
the park close today?" or "When is it quietest here?". Tap one and it's asked,
without you having to spell the park's name. With "Disney's Hollywood Studios"
that saves a fair bit of thumb work.

If you don't know where to go yet, ask "Which parks are near me?". Parky needs
your location for that, and he asks for it in the chat with two buttons, "Share
location" and "Rather not". He only sees it once you share it. Say no and the
conversation carries on without it.

## Parky helps plan your day

Tell Parky which park you want to visit on which day and what matters to you.
He adds the rides to your day planner and tells you where to start. If you ask,
he slots in breaks, food or a show as blocks of their own and sorts the day for
you. Under his answer it then says what he did, like "In your day planner" or
"Sorting your day". After that you move and share the plan like any other.

![Parky in the chat on a phone. Asked for a Saturday at Europa-Park with the big coasters, he replies that Silver Star, Blue Fire, Voltron Nevera and Wodan are in the day plan and that the day should start with Voltron. Below it, a card for Europa-Park with its opening hours and the rides' waits. | Four big coasters on one Saturday. Whether your legs are still up for it after Wodan isn't something Parky plans for.](/parky/en/plan.webp?align=right)

How to start a plan:

- "Plan me a day at Efteling."
- "Plan me a day at Europa-Park with kids."
- "Plan my Saturday at Europa-Park with the big coasters."
- "Plan me a day here." (on a park's page)

How the planner works the forecast waits and the walks into your day is in
[our post on the planner's launch](/blog/trip-planner). The water ride straight
after lunch is something you push back there yourself; Parky doesn't know your
stomach.

## He watches the search with you

In the search, Parky holds a magnifying glass up to his eye in the bottom right
corner. The results arrive as before, he just watches them very closely.

![The search for Taron on park.fan, with the ride, three guides and one news item as results. In the bottom right corner, Parky looks through a magnifying glass. | Taron in the search, Parky with his magnifying glass in the corner.](/parky/en/search.webp?align=right)

## Parky between two questions

Even when you ask nothing, Parky reacts to what you do on park.fan. He's got a
line ready for plenty of occasions and a few tricks we won't give away here,
because anyone who spends a while on the site runs into most of them. He
doesn't like being flung around, by the way, in case you were going to try.

If he gets too chatty, tap the ✕ in one of his speech bubbles and he stays
quiet until the end of your visit. If "Reduce motion" is switched on in your
system, his animations stay off.

## When Parky gets it wrong

Parky answers with Anthropic's language model Claude, and a language model gets
things wrong now and then. That's why the line under the input field reads
"Parky is an AI assistant and sometimes gets it wrong." The figures on the
cards in the chat come straight from park.fan and not from the model, so when
in doubt, believe the card.

If an answer seems off, give it a thumbs down and we'll take a look. What's
missing or not working goes into the "Feedback" tab, with your email address if
you'd like a reply. And anyone who fires off a lot of questions in a short time
will at some point read "Phew, that was a lot of questions. Give me a short
break and ask again in a moment." and has to wait a little.

We store your questions without your IP address and delete them after 90 days.
Everything else is in the [privacy policy](/datenschutz). Parky speaks all six
languages of park.fan and answers in the language of the page you're on.
