## New

- A glossary of 90 terms in all six languages, each on its own page under a localised address
  (`/glossar`, `/glossaire`, `/woordenlijst` …), and searchable from the site search.
- Wherever a glossary term appears on a park or ride page, the homepage or the guide, it's
  underlined, and hovering shows its definition (16 to 19 March).
- Restaurant cards show whether they're open, their wait, their hours and whether you need to
  book (21 March).

## Improved

- The search puts exact matches first, whatever the category.
- The language switch keeps you on the same page, including the glossary's localised addresses
  (22 March).
