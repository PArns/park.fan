# Card photos are two layers (REQUIREMENT)

One standing rule. It is indexed from the repo's [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), which carries the rule in one line and links here for the reasoning, the measurements and the counter-examples.

`object-fit: cover` scales to the **larger** of the two ratios it needs, so it overflows on one axis and fits exactly on the other — paint a photo across a whole card (405×404, aspect ≈1.0) and a 4:3 photo fills its _height_ exactly, leaving `object-position`'s Y with nothing to move. The focal point then renders byte-identical pixels however far you drag it, with a green build and no error. So the framing reference is the **strip between the glass panels**, not the card: `CardPhotoFrame` lives inside the card's photo-spacer row (405×220, aspect 1.84) and is what a visitor sees; `CardPhoto` still covers the whole card underneath so the frosted panels have something to blur. Same URL → one request, one decode. Where a card renders no bottom panel (ride with no live wait time) the spacer takes `row-span-2`, or the framed layer's lower edge sits exposed mid-card as a crop seam. Never widen the frame layer back to the full card. `pnpm check:card-framing` (needs a running site) asserts the box stays wider than 1.5. See [media database](docs/features/media-database.md).
