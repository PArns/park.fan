[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) is inviting everyone whose main residence is registered in Brühl to spend a day at Wintertraum for free. The occasion is the park's 60th birthday on 30 April 2027, and the invitation opens the anniversary year. You can sign up until 15 December 2026 using a form on the "Euer Brühler Wintertraum" page (roughly "your Brühl winter dream"), and you choose the day of your visit yourself when you do.

## Who can take part

The invitation goes to every resident whose main home is in Brühl, once per person. Each form covers exactly one person, so a family of four fills it in four times. Pupils who go to a school in Brühl but live outside the town get a separate form, and their school sends them the link.

If Brühl is only your second home, or you live next door in Cologne, you're not included and need a normal ticket. The same goes for friends and relatives who want to come along.

## Which days you can pick

You can visit until 24 January 2027, except during the Christmas school holidays, which in North Rhine-Westphalia run from Wednesday, 23 December, to Wednesday, 6 January. Days when Wintertraum is closed anyway are out as well. That leaves:

- in December, the 5th to the 11th and the 13th to the 22nd; the form doesn't accept 30 November to 4 December or Saturday the 12th
- 7 to 24 January, except Tuesday the 12th and Wednesday the 13th, when the park is closed

Phantasialand hasn't announced how long the town of Brühl takes to check each application. So if you want to go in December, you're better off signing up now than waiting until the 14th. In our measurements at last year's Wintertraum, the shortest queues were on weekdays in January, so my tip is a day from Monday to Friday after the holidays.

## How to sign up

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth and the day you'd like to visit. Type your name exactly as it appears on your ID card, special characters included: a Jürgen on the card can't be a Juergen on the form. You also agree that Phantasialand passes your details to the town of Brühl, which checks them against the residents' register. Only once that check is done does the ticket arrive by email.

Each visiting day has a limited number of places. The park hasn't announced whether you'll be offered another day if yours is already full.

## At the gate and in the park

The ticket can't be passed on and is only valid together with valid proof of where you live. Phantasialand doesn't say which document it wants to see. Your ID card has your address on it, and the form already asks you to spell your name the way the card does, so that's the one to bring.

Wintertraum is open daily from 11 am to 8 pm. The evening shows and the rides that run in winter are in our [Wintertraum guide](/blog/phantasialand-wintertraum). The day ends at around 7:50 pm with MAGIC SYMPHONY on Kaiserplatz, weather permitting.

## Friends from outside Brühl

Anyone who doesn't live in Brühl buys a normal day ticket, and only online: Phantasialand has no ticket office at the gate. In the [Black Deal](/news/phantasialand-black-deal-wintertraum), a Wintertraum day costs from €29, or from €33 at weekends and in the holidays, but only until Monday, 30 November. On the day itself, the online shop sells tickets until 4 pm, which is good news for the relative who says no all week and changes their mind over breakfast.

## 60 years in Badorf

Phantasialand opened on 30 April 1967 as a fairy-tale forest; what it looks like today is in our [Phantasialand guide](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips). It was founded by the showman Gottlieb Löffelhardt and the puppeteer Richard Schmidt. The park sits in the south-west of Badorf, a district of Brühl, on the Ville ridge. With car parks and hotels, the site covers about 28 hectares, the park itself about 12.
