From 9 November up to and including 6 December, [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) in Kasterlee is selling its annual passes at up to 40 percent off. A pass runs for 365 days from the day you buy it, so anyone who buys in those four weeks will also get into Nickelodeon Land, which opens just behind the park entrance in April 2027. The park calls the sale Black November even though it runs into December, and at 40 percent off nobody's going to check its calendar.

## What the three passes cost

- PREMIUM: €98.45 instead of €164.90
- PLUS: €83.95 instead of €139.90
- BASIC: €80.45 instead of €114.90

Only PREMIUM includes the parking pass and the pass for the shuttle bus. PLUS and BASIC come without either, and you can't add them separately. During Black November PLUS and BASIC are almost the same price, so the real choice is between PREMIUM and the other two.

PREMIUM gets you 20 percent off selected products in the restaurants and shops, cheaper friend tickets with no limit on how many (the price depends on the period), and a discount on the Speedy Pass Diamond, Gold and Silver, whose price depends on the day you visit. Up to two companions aged 65 or over get in free on each visit if they show ID at the ticket desk. You get three free visits each to [Movie Park Germany](/blog/movie-park-germany-wait-times-tips) and to Slagharen by showing the digital pass, plus one visit to another Parques Reunidos park. Partner parks let you in free or at a lower price, and the Corsendonk Hotels take 10 percent off the room rate.

PLUS has nearly the same, except that it's two free visits to Movie Park and Slagharen instead of three, and Bobbejaanland doesn't list a visit to another Parques Reunidos park for it. BASIC is entry without the extras, with 10 percent off in the restaurants and shops.

None of the three passes is valid on red days, and discount codes don't work on those days either. If no day is marked red in the park's calendar, Bobbejaanland hasn't set any yet. Days with a private event are excluded too, and no tickets are sold for them.

## Pass or day ticket

A day ticket for anyone taller than 140 centimetres costs from €27.90 online and from €48.90 at the ticket desk, with the standard rate at €49.90. Between 100 and 140 centimetres you also pay from €27.90 online, and from €44.90 at the desk. Children under one metre get in without a ticket or a booking, so they don't need a pass. Visitors aged 65 and over pay €21.90 at the desk. Parking costs €13 per visit by car, €7 by motorbike and €19 by motorhome.

If you'd pay at the desk twice, the BASIC pass already works out cheaper; if you book online, it does by the third visit at the latest. If you come by car, I'd go for PREMIUM, because you stop paying for parking every time. ## When the park is open

Halloween runs until 15 November inclusive. Bobbejaanland Wintert, one of the [winter seasons in Europe](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026), starts on 5 December and lasts until 3 January 2027, open from 11 am to 7 pm, and until 5 pm on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. The park is closed on 25 December and 1 January. The pass works on Wintert days like on any other day, as long as the day isn't red. Black November is still on during the first Wintert weekend, 5 and 6 December, so you can buy the pass before your first winter visit.

In winter the park is split into six winter zones, with Father Christmas, a Christmas village and Land of Fire & Ice, and the pond turns into an ice rink. Among the rides running are Sledge Hammer, [Typhoon](ref:bobbejaanland/typhoon), [Fury](ref:bobbejaanland/fury), Dreamcatcher, Naga Bay, Bob Express, Revolution and The El Paso Special. Fury joined the line-up in 2019.

## Nickelodeon Land from April 2027

On its annual pass page, Bobbejaanland announces Nickelodeon Land for 2027, with three new attractions. CEO Yves Peeters told VRT more about it. The area gets Nickelodeon characters such as Garfield and SpongeBob and sits right behind the park entrance. Construction has started, existing attractions are being taken down to make room, and the site is fenced off. The area costs around €15 million and is due to open in April 2027.

The Wild Swing takes 18 riders at a time and swings them up to 12 metres. The drop tower is 15 metres tall, which a cat as fond of the sofa as Garfield would probably call far too high. The water ride Big Wavez lifts riders more than 20 metres, drops them and pushes up a 10-metre wave as it does. Peeters says it's the first of its kind in Europe in this form. Bobbejaanland has grown from an attraction park into a theme park with heavily themed areas, he told the broadcaster. What else opens in Europe in 2027 is in our [new attractions for 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027).

The park was founded by Bobbejaan Schoepen. Since 2004 Bobbejaanland has belonged to Parques Reunidos, which also owns Movie Park Germany. The park has more than 40 attractions.
