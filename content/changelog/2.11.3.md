## New

- The homepage was rebuilt to answer "what is park.fan?" (31 August). Returning visitors get a row
  of shortcuts first; after that come three steps for planning a park day and a chapter for each
  thing the site does: live wait times, the forecast, the calendar, shows and restaurants, and the
  dictionary. The figures on it are live readings: the counters, the day curve and the forecast
  model's scorecard.
- Park pages have a height filter, shown only where the park publishes minimum heights, with a scale
  taken from that park's own limits (2 September). Until then a ride's minimum height ("from
  120 cm") was on its card and nowhere else.

## Improved

- The °C/°F switch sits in the header next to the language and theme controls, on every page
  (2 September). It governs the weather calendar, blog posts and the best-time hub, and it used to
  exist only in the weather card of a park page. Its width is fixed, so pressing it does not shift
  the row.

## Fixed

- Shows and restaurants came from a copy cached for a day, so a park page showed them as they were
  at the hour that copy was made, all day long. They are reloaded when you open the page, every half
  hour after that, and when the park opens or closes (1 September).
- The park.fan icon in Google's results was a blur at the size Google draws it (31 August).
