## New

- Dutch, French and Spanish, next to English and German (15 January).
- A shared link to any page shows a picture made for that page, from the homepage down to a single
  ride, with its sparkline or its region on a map (13 January).
- `/parks` lists the continents as the start of the tree, and the search finds cities and
  countries as well as parks.
- Queue types have names in every language (Single Rider, Lightning Lane, Virtual Queue and the
  rest), and each prediction carries an accuracy rating, from "Poor" to "Excellent".
- A page that does not exist shows a 404 page of our own.
