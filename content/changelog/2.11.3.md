## New

- The homepage was rebuilt to answer "what is park.fan?" (31 August). Returning visitors get a row
  of shortcuts first; after that come three steps and a chapter for each thing the site does, from
  live wait times and the forecast to the calendar, shows, restaurants and the dictionary. The
  figures on it are live readings: the counters, the day curve and the forecast model's scorecard.
- Every ride card already said "from 120 cm", one card at a time. Park pages have a height filter,
  shown only where the park publishes minimum heights, with a scale taken from that park's own
  limits (2 September).

## Improved

- The °C/°F switch sits in the header next to the language and theme controls, on every page
  (2 September). It governs the weather calendar, blog posts and the best-time hub, and it used to
  exist only in the weather card of a park page. Its width is fixed, so pressing it does not shift
  the row.

## Fixed

- Shows and restaurants came from a copy cached for a day, so the hour that copy was made decided
  the whole day. They are reloaded when a tab opens, every half hour after that, and when the park
  opens or closes (1 September).
- The park.fan icon in Google's results is legible at the size Google draws it (31 August).
