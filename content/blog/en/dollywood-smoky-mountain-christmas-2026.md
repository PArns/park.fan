Smoky Mountain Christmas starts at [Dollywood](ref:dollywood) in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, on Friday, November 6. Until November 22, a one-day ticket costs everyone the kids' price, adults included. The holiday season runs until January 3, 2027.

## Lights and snow

More than six million lights hang in the park, and each area has a color scheme of its own: red and green in Santa's Village, blue and white in Glacier Ridge, warm candlelight in Craftsman's Valley. Glacier Ridge, a light landscape done up as the Arctic, is back and stretches through Timber Canyon, Wildwood Grove, Wilderness Pass and the upper end of Craftsman's Valley. By the pond in Rivertown Junction stand the Christmas Reflections, five colorful trees of light that show up a second time in the water once it's dark.

New this year is Snow in the Grove, in [Wildwood Grove](ref:dollywood/wildwood-grove). Snow falls over Wildwood Creek every evening, and [The Wildwood Tree](ref:dollywood/the-wildwood-tree) lights up in color, set to music. It's probably the only snow in the Smokies that keeps to a timetable. The park's holiday characters are around too: Roly and Poly, Benjamin Bear, and Flit and Flutter.

Peppermint Valley is also new. For it, the Country Fair area is strung with red and white lights shaped like candy canes. Most of the rides keep running through the holiday season, and from up top you see the park's lights spread out below you.

## Shows and Santa

The main show is "Christmas in the Smokies" at the Celebrity Theater. In "Heidi Parton's A Christmas To Remember," Dolly's niece Heidi sings and tells stories from her family's Christmases. Also back this year:

- "'Twas the Night Before Christmas"
- "Christmas with the Kingdom Heirs"
- "O' Holy Night"
- "Appalachian Christmas with the Smoky Mountain String Band"
- the Candlelight Carolers

At [Santa's List... Naughty or Nice?](ref:dollywood/santas-list-naughty-or-nice), Santa sits in his cabin checking who's been good and who hasn't. Kids write their wish lists there with the elves and hand them to Santa in person. His last day in the park is December 23, since he has a fairly well-known overnight flight around the world on the 24th.

Before that, he has breakfast with guests on December 12, 13, 19 and 20 at Aunt Granny's Restaurant, with seatings at 8:30 and 10 a.m. Breakfast with Santa needs its own ticket and costs $24.99 for adults and $12.99 for kids.

## What a day costs

The "Everyone Pays Kids' Price" deal covers date-specific 1-Day Tickets between November 6 and 22, 2026. You have to come on the day you pick when you buy. After that, the regular adult prices apply, all before tax:

- Pick-A-Day (fixed date): one day from $94.99, two days from $124.99
- Good-Any-Day (any operating day in 2026): one day from $99.99, two days from $129.99

Kids and seniors get a discount. Pick-A-Day prices depend on the date.

A one-day ticket gets you into the festival, but food and parking cost extra. The Festival Tasting Pass gives you up to five festival dishes for $45.99 plus tax, or $42.99 if you hold a Gold or Diamond pass. You can buy it online or at the food stands in the park. Five dishes won't get you through the whole menu. The savory dishes are buffalo chicken stuffed baked potatoes, jambalaya, Stuffing Stackers and potato soup. For afterwards there's a mint chocolate chip brownie sundae, red velvet hot cocoa, vanilla eggnog, candy cane cupcakes, apple dumplings and gingerbread men.

The Smoky Mountain Christmas Guided Tour takes you through the festival and comes with a lanyard to keep, reserved seats on the [Dollywood Express](ref:dollywood/dollywood-express-train-depot), character meet-and-greets, a snack and a hot drink.

If rain stops the rides and you have to leave early, the Weather Guarantee applies. Before you go, pick up a return ticket at a ticket window for another operating day this season. Everyone in your group has to be at the window with you.

## The 2027 season pass covers this Christmas

Dollywood has been selling [season passes for 2027](/news/dollywood-season-2027) since November 2. Three of the four already get you into Smoky Mountain Christmas this year. Gold and Diamond passes are good through January 3, Silver through December 13. The Summer Fun Pass doesn't start until April 19, 2027. If you already have a one-day ticket, you can upgrade it to a season pass on the day of your visit, and the value of a regular one-day ticket counts toward it.

Buy a season pass between November 2 and December 13 and you get a Bring-A-Friend ticket for $45, which brings one guest to the festival between November 6 and December 13. Active and retired military save $35 per pass on Silver, Gold and Diamond from November 2 to 15, and so does their immediate family. They verify their status with id.me first. Kids born in 2022 or 2023 get in free with the Pre-K Imagination Season Pass.

## Getting there and parking

Dollywood is in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, at the edge of Great Smoky Mountains National Park. The nearest airport is McGhee Tyson in Knoxville, less than an hour's drive from the park.

Parking is $25 per vehicle. Free trams run from lots A to E to the main entrance; from lot F you walk. Preferred Parking right by the park is $55 and comes with its own ticket window and entrance. RVs also park in lot F, for $30. Gold and Diamond passholders park free.

## The two resorts

At DreamMore Resort and Spa, a Christmas tree two floors tall stands in the lobby, and the Christmas Tree Show plays by the pool. The Song & Hearth restaurant serves a holiday meal on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve. DreamMore guests ride a shuttle to the park and get TimeSaver Passes as well. HeartSong Lodge & Resort is decorated after the Great Smoky Mountains, and its restaurant Ember & Elm serves dinner on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve. Guests at both resorts roast marshmallows, and the youngest do crafts at Camp DW.

## Golden Ticket Awards and a new coaster

Smoky Mountain Christmas has won the Golden Ticket Award for best holiday event at an amusement park 15 times. This year it's nominated in the USA Today 10Best Awards. New in the park is [NightFlight Expedition](ref:dollywood/nightflight-expedition), an indoor coaster.
