The Rainforest Café in Disney Village at Disneyland Paris serves its last meals on Sunday 15 November 2026. After that it's closed for good, 27 years after it opened. Disneyland Paris confirmed the date to the British fan site Mickey From The UK. The French site Disneyphile had already heard it from the restaurant's staff back in August. If you want one more lunch next to a gorilla, you have until Sunday.

> [!QUOTE]
> Rainforest Café will permanently close its doors on November 15, 2026.
>
> Disneyland Paris, November 2026, quoted by [Mickey From The UK](https://mickeyfromthe.uk/news/rainforest-cafe-at-disneyland-paris-closes-15th-november-2026)

## Why it's closing

The closure itself has been settled since 17 September. That day Disneyland Paris presented the next phase of its Disney Village makeover and added that, as part of it, the Rainforest Café would close permanently by the end of 2026. It hasn't announced which restaurants and shops come next, nor what happens to the building, whether it gets refitted or torn down.

In spring the talk was still of 15 September as the last day, a date Disneyland Paris never confirmed. At the end of August word came from the team that the restaurant would stay open until mid-November. Disneyland Paris hasn't given a reason for the delay. By the end of September part of the shop had already been cleared, and strollers were parked where the plush toys used to sit.

## Where the staff go

The Rainforest Café is run by Groupe Flo, a subsidiary of Groupe Bertrand. Nobody is being laid off, Disneyphile reports: the team moves to Casa Giulia, an Italian restaurant from Groupe Bertrand that opens on 1 December at the entrance to Disney Village. Planet Hollywood stood on that spot until January 2023. Casa Giulia has 440 seats over two floors and a terrace with another 120. Next door, Giulia Express sells pizza by the slice, focaccia, Italian pastries and ice cream, with just over 80 seats. Casa Giulia isn't taking over the Rainforest Café building. Groupe Bertrand also runs Brasserie Rosalie, in the space that used to be Café Mickey.

## 27 years of thunderstorms at dinner

The Rainforest Café opened in 1999 on the site of the former Key West Seafood, and it's the only one in France. You eat among artificial plants, animatronic gorillas and mechanical crocodiles, and every so often a fake thunderstorm rolls through the dining room, lightning and all.

## What else is changing in Disney Village

The makeover started in December 2023 with Brasserie Rosalie. The first phase is almost finished, and the second is due to start in 2027. The Disney Wonders shop opened on 22 September. Annette's Diner is closed for refurbishment and should reopen in the first quarter of 2027. Vapiano closes at the end of 2026 for a new interior, and the cinema is being refurbished in stages until 2027 while staying mostly open.

You don't need a park ticket for Disney Village. It's a few minutes' walk from Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy station, where the RER A and the TGV stop. [Disney Enchanted Christmas](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026) has been running in both parks since 7 November, and from 14 November Minnie and Mrs. Claus appear at the Royal Castle Stage in [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park).
