Our crowd calendar has 16 parks around Orlando, and you don't have to change hotels for any of them. Walt Disney World has four theme parks and two water parks, Universal three theme parks and a water park, and SeaWorld runs Aquatica and Discovery Cove next to its marine park. Three more are far enough out that you plan a day trip for them: LEGOLAND in Winter Haven, and Busch Gardens with the Adventure Island water park in Tampa.

The parks are quick to pick and the days less so, because on 25 evenings in November and December 2026 Magic Kingdom shows its day guests the door three to five hours earlier than usual, so the Christmas party guests can have the park to themselves. A day with short queues that ends that early is often the worse day. In the [Orlando crowd calendar](/orlando) the best park at each resort is marked for every day, with the early close counted in, and with the [trip planner](/orlando#planner) you put your parks on your trip days.

Our guides to [Magic Kingdom](/blog/magic-kingdom-wait-times-tips) and [Universal Orlando](/blog/universal-orlando-wait-times-tips) go through the rides with the longest queues park by park. At a Disney deluxe hotel you can stay at EPCOT two hours longer on Monday evenings into January 2027, and from a hotel on Hotel Plaza Boulevard you get into the four Disney parks half an hour early every day.

## The parks around Orlando and their resorts

### Walt Disney World

Walt Disney World lies south-west of the city and runs its own buses, boats, a monorail and a cable car, the Skyliner. The resort's theme parks are [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park?calendar) (1 October 1971), [EPCOT](ref:epcot?calendar) (1 October 1982), [Hollywood Studios](ref:disneys-hollywood-studios?calendar) (1989) and [Animal Kingdom](ref:disneys-animal-kingdom-theme-park?calendar) (1998), and its water parks [Typhoon Lagoon](ref:disneys-typhoon-lagoon-water-park) (1989) and [Blizzard Beach](ref:disneys-blizzard-beach-water-park) (1995). Typhoon Lagoon is closed for refurbishment until 26 January 2027.

![A packed open-air theatre seen from the back, the audience waiting in the dark. | The Fantasmic! theatre at Hollywood Studios has about 6,900 seats, and they fill up long before the show.](/media/disney-hollywood-studios/fantasmic-crowd.jpg)

### Universal Orlando

[Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida?calendar) (1990) and [Islands of Adventure](ref:universal-islands-of-adventure?calendar) (1999) sit gate to gate, with CityWalk's restaurants, bars and shops between them, and they share the same parking garages. [Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe?calendar), open since 22 May 2025, is just under six kilometres further south-east by car, with its own garage and its own hotel, the Helios Grand. The [Volcano Bay](ref:universal-volcano-bay) water park opened in 2017 and has no car park of its own. It's been on its winter break since 26 October 2026, and Universal says it'll reopen by 1 April 2027 at the latest.

![A VelociCoaster train in a turn, the towers of Hogwarts behind it. | The VelociCoaster runs through Jurassic World, and the towers behind it already belong to Hogwarts, both at Islands of Adventure.](/media/universal-islands-of-adventure/welcome-cover.jpg)

### SeaWorld Orlando

[SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando?calendar) has been on International Drive since 15 December 1973, and the [Aquatica](ref:aquatica-orlando) water park across the road since 2008. At [Discovery Cove](ref:discovery-cove-orlando) you book a fixed date and the number of guests is capped, so it's the one park around Orlando where you don't need to hunt for a day with short queues. SeaWorld's multi-park tickets work at Busch Gardens and Adventure Island in Tampa too, because all five parks belong to United Parks & Resorts.

### Day trips to Winter Haven and Tampa

Merlin Entertainments owns [LEGOLAND Florida](ref:legoland-florida?calendar) in Winter Haven, which opened on 15 October 2011 on the grounds of the old Cypress Gardens. Next to it are the LEGOLAND Water Park, on its winter break until 12 February 2027, and the Peppa Pig Theme Park, with a ticket of its own. [Busch Gardens Tampa Bay](ref:busch-gardens-tampa?calendar) dates back to 31 March 1959, when it opened at Anheuser-Busch's brewery in Tampa. The [Adventure Island](ref:adventure-island-tampa) water park next door starts its 2027 season on 6 March.

## What the numbers in the crowd calendar mean

Each of the 16 parks has a square for every day in the crowd calendar, with a number and a colour. The water parks and Discovery Cove only show whether they're open, because their queues aren't worth a forecast.

### The peak wait

The number in the square is the park's **peak wait** for that day. Each of the park's big rides goes in with its wait at the busiest time of day, and we average across all the big rides. It's the one number we compare parks and days by.

It doesn't hold for every hour. If you're at the gate at nine, most rides have shorter waits, and if you're still at breakfast at nine, you'll notice the difference at your first queue. In many parks the queues get shorter again in the evening, while TRON's at Magic Kingdom only gets really long then. At Magic Kingdom every ride has its longest queue at a different hour anyway.

### What the colour means

For the colour we compare the day with an ordinary day at the same park. Ordinary means the median of all the park's open days we've measured, going back at most a year and a half.

That's why a green EPCOT can have a longer peak wait than a red Magic Kingdom. Green only means EPCOT is quieter than on an ordinary EPCOT day, and an ordinary EPCOT day is busier than an ordinary one at Magic Kingdom. To see where you'll queue less, compare the minutes.

### Where the numbers come from and how far ahead they reach

park.fan records the wait every park posts for each of its rides every five minutes. Our [forecast models](/how-park-fan-works) learn from those measurements together with opening hours, school holidays and the weather. Opening hours, Early Entry and the separately ticketed evenings come from the parks' own calendars. We enter seasons by hand, festivals and parties too, and check them against the parks' pages.

For the next 60 days our model works out every day ride by ride. Further ahead, a day shows the same weekday a year earlier as we measured it, marked "last year", and in the trip planner and on the best-time pages you see last year's figure for those days too. Our long-range forecast for those days came out well below what we'd measured on the same days a year before, and it barely told one day from the next. A dot in the square means the park hasn't published its hours for that day yet. The peak wait is already there, but an early close for a party may not be.

The day after, we compare every forecast with the measured wait, and the average miss in minutes is public on the page of [Fancast](/fancast), our forecast model. The further away a day is, the bigger it gets. Next to every plan in the trip planner you can see by how many minutes the forecast for one ride on one day can be off.

We round to five minutes, so two days less than five minutes apart count as equally good, whichever of them is listed first among the best days.

## Party nights, weekdays and the ring in the calendar

Within a resort the parks are a few minutes' drive apart. You can move a Disney day from Tuesday to Wednesday without any fuss, and it pays to give each park its best day of the trip. At each resort, the park with the lowest peak wait that day gets a ring in the crowd calendar, and an early close counts against it.

### Why an early close counts

On the 25 evenings of Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party, anyone without a party ticket has to leave Magic Kingdom at 6 pm. On days without a party the park usually closes between 9 and 11 pm. Hollywood Studios closes at 7 pm instead of 9 pm on the 15 evenings of Disney Jollywood Nights. Both parties need a ticket of their own.

The hours a party night takes from you are hours you can't spend on rides, and that's why a day in the calendar and the trip planner counts by the hours you get as a day guest. A party day weighs up to one and a half times as much as a full day with the same peak wait. On most party nights in November and December 2026, the ring goes to Animal Kingdom, even when Magic Kingdom has a slightly shorter peak wait that day. Animal Kingdom closes at 6 pm almost every day, so for that park it's a normal day.

Disney After Hours also costs extra, but it starts after the normal close, on selected evenings from January to August 2027 at Magic Kingdom, EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. Since no day guest has to leave early, an After Hours evening doesn't count against the park in the calendar.

At Universal it's the autumn evenings that are short. On Halloween Horror Nights evenings in 2026, Universal Studios closed to day guests at 5 pm, on 49 evenings between 28 August and 1 November. The Halloween party at Magic Kingdom ran on 38 evenings from 7 August to 31 October 2026. Neither series is set for 2027 yet, and until the parks publish the evenings, the calendar shows no early close.

### Weekdays and good days

Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Epic Universe are about as busy on every day of the week. Animal Kingdom, Islands of Adventure, SeaWorld, Busch Gardens and LEGOLAND get noticeably more guests on Saturdays than during the week, and there it's worth moving your visit to a day from Monday to Friday.

## Where to stay and what the hotel gets you

### At a Disney hotel

Stay at one of the Disney hotels and **Early Entry** gets you into any of the four Disney theme parks half an hour before everyone else, every day, including the days you arrive and leave. Disney offers it through the end of 2027. The same goes for 15 other hotels, among them the Swan, the Dolphin and the Swan Reserve, the Four Seasons, the Waldorf Astoria and Shades of Green. Lightning Lane, Disney's way of paying to skip the queue, opens to hotel guests seven days before arrival and to everyone else three days before the park day.

Disney's Deluxe and Deluxe Villa hotels, plus the Swan, the Dolphin, the Swan Reserve and Shades of Green, add **Extended Evening Hours** on selected nights, two hours after the normal close with some of the rides running. Until the end of December 2026 they're on Mondays at EPCOT and Wednesdays at Animal Kingdom, and in January 2027 on Mondays at EPCOT and Wednesdays at Magic Kingdom.

With Early Entry I count on one extra big ride on an ordinary morning, two on a good one and one extra coffee on a bad one. What matters more to me is that free buses, boats, the monorail or the Skyliner run from every Disney hotel to the parks, so I don't need a car.

### At a Disney Springs hotel

On Hotel Plaza Boulevard next to Disney Springs there are hotels Disney doesn't own that are still on its Early Entry list, among them the Hilton Orlando Lake Buena Vista, the Hilton Orlando Buena Vista Palace, the Drury Plaza Hotel, the DoubleTree Suites by Hilton, the Holiday Inn Orlando, the Renaissance Orlando Resort and Spa, formerly the B Resort, and the two Wyndhams. Their guests get into the four Disney theme parks half an hour early every day, but they don't get the Extended Evening Hours.

For Disney and Universal on one trip, that's where I'd stay. From there it's 13 to 20 minutes to the car parks of the four Disney parks, 20 to Universal Studios and 24 to Epic Universe, and Early Entry comes with the room.

### At a Universal hotel

Five of Universal's eleven hotels make up the Signature Collection: the Hard Rock Hotel, Loews Portofino Bay, Loews Royal Pacific, Loews Sapphire Falls and the Helios Grand at Epic Universe. The others are three Prime Value hotels and three Value Inns. Every guest gets **Early Park Admission**, entry to one of the parks up to an hour before opening, for selected rides. Universal decides which parks open early on each day. In November and December 2026 it's Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe.

At three hotels, the Hard Rock, Portofino Bay and Royal Pacific, **Express Unlimited** comes with the room. It gets you onto every Express ride at Universal Studios and Islands of Adventure faster as often as you like, but it isn't valid at Epic Universe. The Helios Grand has its own entrance to Epic Universe instead. Free buses or water taxis run to the parks, and from some hotels you walk.

### Hotels outside the resorts

Outside the resorts you'll mostly stay on International Drive between Universal and SeaWorld, in Lake Buena Vista around Disney Springs or in Kissimmee on Highway US-192 east of the Walt Disney World entrance. There's no early entry from any of them, except at the hotels on Disney's list.

If Universal is your focus, I'd stay on International Drive or at a Universal hotel, both under ten minutes from all three Universal parks. Kissimmee is closer to Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom than any other area apart from the Disney hotels themselves, but it's the furthest from Universal.

### From the airport to the hotel

Orlando International Airport, MCO for short, lies south-east of the city. None of the five areas is more than a good half hour from there, and from Terminal C the times in the table change by three minutes at most. Disney's free bus to the hotels, Disney's Magical Express, stopped on 1 January 2022. Since then Mears Connect has run paid shuttles from the airport to the Disney hotels and the Disney Springs hotels. For International Drive, the Universal hotels and Kissimmee you'll need a rental car, a taxi or a ride-hailing car.

### Between the parks

Disney's buses run between every hotel and park. The monorail links the Transportation and Ticket Center with Magic Kingdom and with EPCOT, and park to park, with the change, it takes about half an hour. The Skyliner runs from Caribbean Beach Resort to EPCOT's back entrance, the International Gateway, in about a quarter of an hour, and to Hollywood Studios in the same time. Between EPCOT and Hollywood Studios there's also a boat, and on foot it's about 30 minutes.

At Universal you walk from the Studios to Islands of Adventure through CityWalk in about ten minutes. The other way across is the Hogwarts Express from Diagon Alley to Hogsmeade, and for that you need a park-to-park ticket. Universal's shuttle buses run to Epic Universe, and by car it's eleven minutes from the Universal Studios garages.

Between the resorts you drive or take a ride-hailing car. EPCOT to Universal Studios is 25 minutes, Universal Studios to SeaWorld eleven. In the evening Disney Springs charges neither for entry nor for parking, and CityWalk is free to enter but not to park at. For LEGOLAND and for Busch Gardens I'd set aside a whole day each, because Tampa and back is about three hours in the car.

## Tickets

Disney and Universal sell tickets for fixed dates. SeaWorld and LEGOLAND also sell tickets whose period starts with your first visit.

### Walt Disney World

Disney's tickets come in lengths of one to ten days. The date you choose when you buy is day one, even if you spend it by the pool, and days you don't use are lost. The days spread over a window that grows with the length. A four-day ticket is used within seven calendar days, a seven-day ticket within ten and a ten-day ticket within fourteen. A one-day ticket is for one park.

With **Park Hopper** you switch to a second park on the same day, any time after you've entered the first, and it uses up only one day of your ticket. You'll find an example with ten days in December, with and without the hopper, in our [post on the trip planner](/blog/orlando-trip-planner). The Water Park and Sports option, called Park Hopper Plus together with the hopper, adds as many water park and mini golf visits as the ticket has days. Since 9 January 2024 you no longer need a park reservation with a ticket. For arrivals from 3 January to 22 May 2027 there's also the 4-Park Magic Ticket, one day at each of the four parks within six days, with no hopping.

### Universal Orlando

At Universal you buy one to five days, and the period starts on the date you choose: two days within four, three days within five and five days within seven. Tickets of three days or more get you into Epic Universe. With one or two days you need a separate Epic Universe ticket for that, or the package of one day at the Studios or Islands of Adventure and one day at Epic Universe. You'll find the prices of the tickets and of Express Pass in our [Universal guide](/blog/universal-orlando-wait-times-tips). With **park-to-park** you switch between the parks on the same day and ride the Hogwarts Express.

### SeaWorld, Busch Gardens and LEGOLAND

Besides the single-day ticket, SeaWorld sells combination tickets for two, three or four parks, depending on the ticket with Busch Gardens and Adventure Island too. The visits fall within 14 days of the first, and with the Four Parks ticket you go as often as you like in that time. Discovery Cove comes on its own or with 14 days of SeaWorld and its sister parks around it. LEGOLAND sells one- and two-day tickets, and Merlin's 7 Florida Attractions Ticket includes one LEGOLAND visit within 14 days.

### 14-day tickets from the UK

The tickets sold outside the US are made for long trips. Disney's 14-Day Magic Ticket is for residents of the UK and Ireland. It's good for 14 park days within 18 days, with park hopping, the water parks and mini golf, and a water park day doesn't use up any of the 14 park days. Universal's 14-day tickets are also on sale from retailers in other countries. They're valid on 14 days in a row, every day, with park-to-park and Epic Universe. For arrivals up to the end of March 2027 the ticket is called the Three Park Adventure Ticket and comes without Volcano Bay, and after that the All Parks Ticket with the water park is back.

A pool day costs you one of the 14 days on Universal's ticket and none on Disney's, as long as you stay within the 18 days.

### January to April

After New Year the parks empty out. January to April is mild, and February and March are the driest months of the year. It got busy in 2026 all the same, most of all in February and March. Those weeks bring Presidents' Day, Orange County's spring break, the Festival of the Arts at EPCOT and Mardi Gras at Universal Studios. In April the Disney parks were a little quieter than in March and the Universal parks about as busy.

### May to August

The summer break from 27 May brings the families, and the busiest weeks of the 2026 summer were the end of May and early June, and the second half of July. It's hot and wet then, and the four months from June to September are the wettest of the year.

The hurricane season starts on 1 June. According to the National Hurricane Center, the first named storm usually forms in mid to late June and the first hurricane in early to mid-August. When Orange County's new school year began, on 11 August in 2026, the parks emptied out again. The Halloween party at Magic Kingdom starts in August, and Halloween Horror Nights in late August.

### September and October

The quietest days of our first year were at the end of August and in early September 2026, just before the Labor Day long weekend. All through September, Saturdays stayed busier than the weekdays. The hurricane season peaks on 10 September, and most storms form between mid-August and mid-October.

A trip that touches any day between 15 August and 15 October drops down the list on the best-time pages and in the trip planner's date search, and the quietest week of our first year is in none of the suggested trips. If you plan those weeks anyway, the trip planner marks each day "peak hurricane season".

The week around 12 October 2026, when Orange County schools were off, was one of the busiest of the autumn. By the end of October it was quieter again.

### November and December

The hurricane season ends on 30 November. Thanksgiving week starts the holiday season, and in our forecast the weeks around Thanksgiving and between Christmas and New Year sit above the weeks of Advent in between.

## Frequently asked questions about Orlando

### How far is Universal from Walt Disney World?

From the Universal Studios garages to the EPCOT car park is a 25-minute drive without traffic. To Magic Kingdom it's 26 minutes by car to the Transportation and Ticket Center, plus about 15 minutes by monorail or ferry.

### Do I need Early Entry?

No, but on busy days it gets you a ride or two before the day guests are let in. Guests at the Disney hotels and at 15 other hotels, including the ones on Hotel Plaza Boulevard by Disney Springs, get into the four Disney theme parks half an hour early. Guests at the eleven Universal hotels get in up to an hour early at the parks Universal picks for that day.

### Which Disney park works on a Magic Kingdom party night?

EPCOT, Hollywood Studios or Animal Kingdom. Without a party ticket you have to be out by 6 pm on those evenings in November and December 2026, while on other days Magic Kingdom is open until 9, 10 or 11 pm. A party ticket lets you in from 4 pm and you stay until midnight.

### Is Epic Universe busier at the weekend?

Hardly. In our measurements since December 2025, the days of the week differ only a little at Epic Universe, unlike Islands of Adventure, where Saturday is the busiest day.

### How do I get from Orlando airport to my hotel?

By car it's 25 minutes from Orlando International Airport to International Drive without traffic, 27 to the Universal hotels and 31 to Caribbean Beach Resort in the middle of Walt Disney World. Disney hasn't run a free bus since January 2022, and Mears Connect serves the Disney hotels and the Disney Springs hotels for a fare.

### When are the school holidays in Orlando?

In 2026/27, Orange County schools have Thanksgiving break from 23 to 27 November, winter break from 21 December to 1 January and spring break from 15 to 19 March. The last day of school is 26 May 2027. Schools in other US states have their own dates.

### When is hurricane season in Orlando?

From 1 June to 30 November. Most storms form between mid-August and mid-October, and the National Hurricane Center puts the peak on 10 September.

### How many park days do I need for Disney and Universal?

One day each for the four Disney parks and the three Universal parks, so seven. With ten park days I'd give Magic Kingdom, EPCOT and Epic Universe a second day, in that order, and plan a day without a park in between.
