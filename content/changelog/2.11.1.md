## New

- An open hour on the weather chart is drawn four times as wide as a closed one, so the hours of a
  visit take 74 % of the chart instead of 42 % (19 August). Both changes of slope sit on the dashed
  edges of the opening-hours band, which carry a door icon with the opening and closing time.
- The header menu opens as one wide band: countries arrive with their flags, parks with their
  photos (21 August).
- Eleven curated fields put the address, phone number, website, ticket shop, Wikipedia, the park's
  social accounts, the year it opened and its area on a park page, none of which any of our three
  data feeds carries (20 August). Where a park has none of them, the section is left out.
- The park header shows two crowd figures, one above the other: today's level, measured the way
  the forecast beside it is, and the crowd right now (17 August). At Phantasialand a live "normal"
  had stood next to a forecast of "very high", and both were right.
- An agent that knows only the hostname finds `/llms.txt`, an API catalogue under
  `/.well-known/api-catalog`, three published skills with their SHA-256 digests, and an MCP server
  at `/api/mcp` with three read-only tools (25 August). A park that publishes no wait times is
  reported as such, not as a park averaging zero minutes.

## Improved

- The guide to how park.fan works was rewritten around one question, what a wait time means, in
  nine chapters (25 August). Each card on it is the real component from a park page, so you meet
  the same cards an hour later in the park.
- A ride out of season says that it runs only in winter or only in summer, next to "Closed". The
  season used to be a greyed-out word nobody connected to the closure (21 August).

## Fixed

- Seven layout shifts are gone, two of which our measuring script could not see from the top of the
  page (19 August).
- The live column in the blog's wait-time tables disappeared at opening time, and four of those
  tables still pointed at a park that had been renamed (16 August).
- Park names in the quiet-day card are no longer cut off, and the quietest weekday shows again when
  two days tie (24 August).
- Hagrid's wait on its ride page stood still for 40 minutes (25 August).
