## New

- Italian, the sixth language (27 February).

## Improved

- The brand blue became the main colour, and status and crowd badges share one soft style. The
  search dialog shows status badges in its results, plus keyboard hints.
- On narrow screens the middle of a long breadcrumb folds into "…", which opens on a tap
  (3 March), and a park's name no longer appears twice in it.
- Tabs and the ride, show and restaurant cards on park pages sit on glass.

## Fixed
