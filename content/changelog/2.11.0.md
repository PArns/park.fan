## New

- The homepage photo shows the live number of open parks, a search that works in place, and a
  world map on wide screens (6 August).
- Park and ride pages show up to three blog posts about them (3 August).
- The top-ten table of longest waits has a column with each ride's current wait
  (15 August).

## Improved

- Park pages stopped jumping as their late sections arrived: the median layout shift went from
  0.245 to 0.0005 (11 August). The live update every five minutes shrank from 90 KB to 40 KB.
- Searching for "fly" puts F.L.Y. first.
- Hansa-Park looked like a park with every ride open and no queue anywhere. Its page now says that the park publishes no wait times (14 August).
