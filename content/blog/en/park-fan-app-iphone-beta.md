From today you can test the park.fan iPhone app on TestFlight, Apple's programme for beta versions. We're handing out 50 places in the public beta for now. The app only runs on iPhones with iOS 26 or later, the iPhone Duo included. Once every place is taken, TestFlight tells you the beta is full when you open the link.

## How to join

First get the TestFlight app from the App Store. Then open the [invitation link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/W3b54H4U) on your iPhone, tap "Accept" and then "Install". You don't need a park.fan account. Each beta build runs for 90 days in TestFlight, and if you like, TestFlight installs the next one for you.

## What the app does

Before your visit, the crowd calendar shows you the expected crowds and the headliners' peak wait for every day, months ahead. The day planner puts your rides and shows in the order with the shortest expected waits, walking time included.

In the park you see live wait times, with breakdowns and closures. The compass points to the rides in your plan and tells you how far away they are, and "Take me there" walks you to the entrance in metres and minutes. Your lock screen and the Dynamic Island show the waits of your favourite rides and the next stop in your plan. Notifications only come if you switch them on, for example when a ride drops below the wait you set. Parky answers your questions in the app too.

You don't need an account, you won't see ads, and nobody tracks you. Favourites and plans stay on your iPhone. The app speaks the same six languages as the website.

## Bugs and wishes

Take a screenshot in the beta and TestFlight offers to send it to us with a comment. If the app crashes, TestFlight asks whether you want to send us the report. We read both.
