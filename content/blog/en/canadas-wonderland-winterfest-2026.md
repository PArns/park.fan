[Canada's Wonderland](ref:canadas-wonderland) in Vaughan, Ontario, is throwing its first New Year's Eve party on Thursday, 31 December. At 8 p.m. the PEANUTS gang and the WinterFest characters lead the countdown on International Street, and fireworks go up over Wonder Mountain afterwards. WinterFest itself runs on 23 days, from Saturday 21 November to 3 January 2027.

## New Year's Eve at 8 p.m.

On 31 December the park is open from noon to 8 p.m. and closes once the party is over. That puts the fireworks four hours ahead of midnight, so this year even the kids who never stay up until twelve get to see them. The park runs the evening together with Tourism Vaughan. It's included in the regular WinterFest ticket and in the 2027 season passes. VIP packages with food and reserved seats for the fireworks cost extra; the park hasn't published a price for them yet.

## The new Countdown to Christmas Square

New this winter is Countdown to Christmas Square, presented by Hallmark Channel, W Network and STACKTV. The Wonderland Players perform Small Town Snowmance there, a show the audience joins in. If the name sounds like a Hallmark Christmas movie, look at who's paying for the square. There are fire pits with Muskoka chairs, photo backdrops and hot chocolate, and for the fire pits you buy a Campfire S'Mores kit on top.

## Shows, lights and Santa

Every evening two Christmas trees about 15 metres tall light up on International Street, and seven million lights hang around the park. After a few years away, the Holly Jolly Trolley is running again. Tinker's Toy Factory brings music and acrobatics to the Canterbury Theatre, and toy soldiers bang the drums in Four Drummers Drumming. Charlie Brown, Snoopy and the rest of the PEANUTS take the stage in It's Christmas, Charlie Brown! On top of that there's Polar Party with the Tannenbaums, The MistleTones, Cool Yule Christmas and live bands. The park hasn't published show times yet.

Local makers sell their work at the Christmas Market. You'll find Santa at St. Nick's Pics, where the photo package costs extra, and the North Pole Post Office takes letters to him until 23 December. There are also the Dancing Lights, Charlie Brown's Christmas Tree Lot and an Elf on the Shelf photo spot.

## Dates and opening hours

- Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November: 3 to 9 p.m.
- Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November: 3 to 9 p.m.
- Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December: 3 to 9 p.m.
- Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December: 3 to 9 p.m.
- Friday 18 to Wednesday 23 December: 3 to 9 p.m.
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day: closed
- Saturday 26 December to Sunday 3 January: noon to 8 p.m.

The Splash Works water park stays shut all winter.

## Tickets and season passes

A park ticket with WinterFest starts at 39 Canadian dollars, plus a processing fee of up to $9.99 and tax. There's no ticket booth in winter; everything is sold online. Children under three don't need a ticket.

The 2027 Silver Pass costs $89, or $75 a pass if you order four or more online in one purchase by 30 November. It covers Canada's Wonderland and Splash Works until Labour Day 2027, and WinterFest 2026 is included as a limited-time bonus. Buy or renew a 2027 pass by 30 November and you get a free Bring-a-Friend ticket to use by 3 January. The Gold Pass costs $115 and also works at the Six Flags parks in the Midwest, Cedar Point included. It only gets you into WinterFest on selected days, and those dates aren't published yet.

## What costs extra

Skating on [Snow Flake Lake](ref:canadas-wonderland/snow-flake-lake) in front of Wonder Mountain is paid separately and booked online in advance; skates can be rented on site. A Canadian winter festival without a rink would be a hard sell anyway. Cookie decorating in Mrs. Claus' Kitchen and food and drinks in the igloos of Jack Frost's Igloo Village cost extra as well. Mrs. Claus' Kitchen has a new recipe this year, made with local ingredients. A locker costs $17 to $30 a day, a stroller $17 and a double stroller $26, plus tax. Mobility scooters aren't available during WinterFest.

## Free entry from 65

Anyone born on or before 31 December 1961 gets into WinterFest for free until 3 January 2027. You register on the Six Flags website beforehand and receive a QR code; you can't sign up at the park. At the gate you show ID with your date of birth on every visit. Free entry only applies at the park you registered for and only while spaces last. Parking isn't included, and anyone coming with you needs their own ticket.

## Which rides are running

25 rides operate during WinterFest, two of them thrill rides: [Psyclone](ref:canadas-wonderland/psyclone) and [Sledge Hammer](ref:canadas-wonderland/sledge-hammer). For kids there's [Snoopy's Space Race](ref:canadas-wonderland/snoopys-space-race), the [Woodstock Whirlybirds](ref:canadas-wonderland/woodstock-whirlybirds) and the [Character Carrousel](ref:canadas-wonderland/character-carrousel), among others. [Guardian](ref:canadas-wonderland/wonder-mountains-guardian) and [Snoopy's Racing Railway](ref:canadas-wonderland/snoopys-racing-railway) only run until 29 November, and only when the weather allows.

Leviathan, Yukon Striker and Behemoth sit out the winter, and in an Ontario December the wind in your face comes free anyway. [Leviathan](ref:canadas-wonderland/leviathan) is a Bolliger & Mabillard hyper coaster from 2012, 93 metres tall with a top speed of 148 km/h. [Yukon Striker](ref:canadas-wonderland/yukon-striker), a B&M dive coaster from 2019, drops 75 metres straight down and runs four inversions. [Behemoth](ref:canadas-wonderland/behemoth), from 2008, is 70 metres tall and reaches 124 km/h.

## Getting there

The address is 1 Canada's Wonderland Drive, Vaughan, ON L6A 1S6. By car, take Highway 400 to exit 33, Rutherford Road, then head north onto Canada's Wonderland Drive. The park hasn't published a parking price.

Without a car, ride TTC subway Line 1 to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre and change at the SmartCentres Place Bus Terminal to a YRT bus heading north on Jane Street. Get off at Jane St/Norwood Av; from there it's a five-minute walk. Since 1 November, YRT routes 320 and 360 have run to timetables built around the park's hours. During WinterFest the 360 from Finch GO Bus Terminal and Richmond Hill Centre also runs on Fridays. GO buses 47W and 52X only went to the park from 25 April to 30 August. From Pearson Airport, the UP Express gets you to Union Station in 28 minutes, and the TTC takes you on from there.

## Canada's Wonderland since 1981

Taft Broadcasting and the Great-West Life Assurance Company opened Canada's Wonderland on 23 May 1981. From 1994 to 2006 it was called Paramount Canada's Wonderland, and in 2006 Cedar Fair bought it. Since Cedar Fair and Six Flags merged on 1 July 2024, it has belonged to Six Flags Entertainment Corporation. The park has 18 roller coasters. This winter Six Flags also holds WinterFest at Carowinds, Six Flags Discovery Kingdom and Kings Island.
