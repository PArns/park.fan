[Kennywood](ref:kennywood), outside Pittsburgh, opens its 2027 season on Saturday, April 17, and spends the year marking three rides that arrived in 1927: the wooden Racer coaster, the Turtle and the Merry-Go-Round. The park announced opening day and its event calendar on November 17, together with the Sandcastle water park, which opens on May 29. It hasn't said yet what the centennial celebration will look like.

## Three rides from 1927

On the [Racer](ref:kennywood/racer?bare), two trains race side by side, yet there's only one track. Board on the right in the station and you come back on the left. Kennywood says no other coaster in the US runs a single continuous track like this. John A. Miller designed it and Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters built it. The track is 4,500 feet (1,372 metres) long, the highest point is 72.5 feet (22 metres), and the trains hit 40 mph (64 km/h). A lap takes 1 minute 32 seconds. Three 24-seat trains carry 1,400 riders an hour, and you need to be 46 inches (117 centimetres) tall. The name belonged to an earlier coaster first, which opened in 1910.

The [Turtle](ref:kennywood/turtle?bare) came to the park in 1927 as a Tumble Bug. Its cars look like turtles and run over hills and dips on a circular track. It's been the Turtle since Kennywood bought a sixth car in 1948, and the park says it's the last ride of its kind still running anywhere. In 1930 a stand called Lucky went up next to it. On its own history page, the park guesses someone already had a hunch it would be a survivor. There's no minimum height; children under 46 inches ride with a companion, and babies don't ride at all.

The [Merry-Go-Round](ref:kennywood/merry-go-round?bare) is the third carousel in the park's history, hand-carved in William Dentzel's workshop in Philadelphia. It carries 64 animals: 50 jumping and 12 standing horses, a lion and a tiger, with Dentzel's jesters and cherubs in between. Here too, children under 46 inches ride with a companion.

## Events from April to January

The season starts with Celebrate Kennywood Weekends from April 17 to May 16, built around the old rides, the park's food, souvenirs and memories of past visits. The park explicitly asks you to show up in matching Kennywood gear; nobody needs talking into the fries at Potato Patch.

The Bites and Pints Food & Drink Festival follows from May 28 to June 27, on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays and on Memorial Day, May 31. Around the Lagoon you'll find dishes from kitchens around the world, cocktails, craft beer and wine.

On July 4 the Independence Day fireworks go up over the Racer, right above one of the birthday rides. Every Friday and Saturday from July 9 to July 31, Kennywood puts on evening drone shows with designs made for the park.

From August 7 to August 22, the Fall Fantasy Parades run through the park every day, with hand-decorated floats and more than 130 local marching bands. Phantom Fall Fest, the Halloween event, runs on selected days from September 11 to October 31 and turns 25 itself in 2027. The park promises a few surprises for the anniversary. Once it's dark, the haunted houses and scare zones open as usual.

Holiday Lights starts its 16th season on selected days from November 13, 2027 to January 2, 2028, with more than three million lights, the tallest Christmas tree in Pennsylvania and visits with Rudolph.

## What 2027 season passes cost

For Black Friday, Kennywood is already selling 2027 season passes online at a discount. The Gold Pass costs $124.99 instead of $169.99, or eight payments of under $16 each. It's valid on every operating day, Phantom Fall Fest and Holiday Lights included, and comes with parking in the Preferred and VIP lots, a souvenir cup with free refills, two 2027 guest tickets and 15% off most food, shopping and Speedy Pass.

The Platinum Pass, $189.99 instead of $249.99, adds Sandcastle, Idlewild and SoakZone and Dutch Wonderland, with free parking at all four Pennsylvania parks, three guest tickets and 20% off. On top of that you get into Lake Compounce, Noah's Ark, Adventureland, Story Land, Living Shores Aquarium, Splish Splash and Water Country.

Buy Gold or Platinum now and Holiday Lights 2026 is included, plus a Holiday Lights guest ticket valid until December 20, 2026. That means a 2027 pass starts working six weeks before New Year.

Right now the Gold Pass sells for the price of the summer-only Silver Pass. The Bronze Pass costs $99.99 instead of $109.99 and covers most summer days, but not the Saturdays of July 10, 17 and 24, 2027, three of the drone show nights. None of the passes are refundable.

## Only Holiday Lights this winter

Holiday Lights 2026 runs until January 3, 2027. Until Friday, November 20, an undated ticket costs $19.99 instead of $49.99 and works on any operating day through the end of the event. Dated tickets currently start at $24.99. More than 20 rides are running. If your visit gets rained out, the Rainy Day Guarantee gets you a free ticket for another day.

## Sandcastle from May 29

The Sandcastle water park opens on Saturday, May 29. There are foam parties on Wednesdays from July 7 to August 18, Christmas in July from July 23 to 25, and from August 7 to 22 DJs play music from several decades at weekends. The Platinum Pass gets you in.

## Getting there

Kennywood is on Route 837 in West Mifflin, about 12 miles (19 kilometres) southeast of downtown Pittsburgh. The 61C bus stops at Lot D. Preferred Parking costs $25 and is included with Gold and Platinum; from the free lot at Gate A, you've got a longer walk to the entrance.
