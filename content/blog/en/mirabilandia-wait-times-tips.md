[Mirabilandia](ref:mirabilandia) in Savio near Ravenna is the biggest theme park in Italy by area. It opened on 4 July 1992 and has belonged to Parques Reunidos since 2006. The park itself cites 850,000 square metres and more than 40 attractions. The latest attendance figure we can find with a year and a source comes from the Themed Entertainment Association and covers 2012: 1.39 million.

The big rides are [Katun](ref:mirabilandia/katun), a Bolliger & Mabillard inverted coaster with six inversions, [iSpeed](ref:mirabilandia/ispeed), an Intamin launch coaster, and [Desmo Race](ref:mirabilandia/desmo-race), two tracks where you help set the pace yourself. There's also a water area around Rio Bravo and Autosplash, a 90-metre Ferris wheel and, since 2025, a Nickelodeon area. Our wait-time data for Mirabilandia starts in August 2026 and runs to today: 42 days of measurements, 23 of them in August, 17 in September and 2 in October. It doesn't cover a full year.

## From the Pineta di Classe to Parques Reunidos

The park was built next to the pine forest of Classe, on the coastal road SS 16 Adriatica. The idea came in the late 1980s from a partnership of Finbrescia (46 percent), San Paolo Finance (44 percent) and Publitalia '80 of the Fininvest group (10 percent). The first built the park, the third did the advertising. That's what the Italian Wikipedia says, and its article traces back to a single source and is itself flagged as insufficiently referenced.

The park opened on 4 July 1992. The German Wikipedia gives 8 July, the park's website 4 July, and we go with the park. Opening day brought, among other things, the wooden coaster Sierra Tonante and the white-water ride Rio Bravo. Rio Bravo is still running.

The first years went badly. Between 1994 and 1996, visits fell to around 600,000 a year according to the Italian Wikipedia. The German Wikipedia names the buyers as the Löffelhardt family, who also own [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand), and Giancarlo Casoli, an owner of the ride manufacturer S.D.C. It dates the takeover to November 1996, the English and Italian Wikipedia to 1997.

Investment followed. In 1997 the two 60-metre towers now called Oil Tower arrived. In 1998 came the wild mouse Pakal, now [Gold Digger](ref:mirabilandia/gold-digger), and the entrance area got its pirate theme, the Baia dei Pirati. In 1999 the Eurowheel Ferris wheel and the water ride Niagara, now [El Dorado Falls](ref:mirabilandia/el-dorado-falls), opened. Katun followed in 2000. In 2003 the water park Mirabilandia Beach opened, called Mirabeach since 2011.

Parques Reunidos bought the park in 2006. The German Wikipedia, citing the Spanish newspaper Cinco Días, puts the price at around 100 million euros. iSpeed replaced Sierra Tonante, which had stood idle since 2007 (opened in 2008 according to the park, 2009 according to Wikipedia). Master Thai (2011), Divertical (2012), the children's area Dinoland (2014), the western area Far West Valley (2016) and, in 2019, Ducati World with Desmo Race followed. The Nickelodeon partnership began in 2022, and Nickelodeon Land opened in 2025. EQT is looking for a buyer for Parques Reunidos; more in our post [Movie Park Germany's owner Parques Reunidos is up for sale](/news/parques-reunidos-sale-movie-park).

### Katun

[Katun](ref:mirabilandia/katun?full)

Katun is 1,200 metres long, 50 metres tall and runs for 2:22 minutes. According to RCDB the track has six inversions: a loop, a zero-g roll, a cobra roll and two corkscrews. Riders sit four across, a train has eight cars with 32 seats, and three trains run at the same time. RCDB gives the ride a capacity of 1,700 people an hour. The park says 110 km/h, RCDB 104.

The theme is the Mayan city of Sian Ka'an. In the Mayan calendar a katun is a period of 7,200 days, about 20 years, and at the start and end of the ride the train passes through a "Stargate". The park calls Katun the longest inverted coaster in Europe. You can ride from 140 centimetres and from age twelve.

### iSpeed

iSpeed is an Intamin launch coaster with a magnetic drive. According to the park it accelerates to 100 km/h in 2.2 seconds, reaches up to 110 km/h (RCDB) and climbs to 55 metres. The track is 980 metres long and has two inversions. The park says 120 km/h, and the ride takes 77 seconds.

iSpeed replaced Sierra Tonante in 2009, the wooden coaster from 1992. You can ride from 140 centimetres up to a maximum of 195 centimetres, and from age twelve.

### Divertical

[Divertical](ref:mirabilandia/divertical) is the park's water coaster, opened in 2012. An elevator lift takes the boat up, and the steepest section is 45 degrees. The park calls it the tallest water coaster in the world and describes it as a ride inspired by the offshore world. The sources disagree on the dimensions: RCDB lists 50 metres in height, 767 metres in length and 95 km/h, the English Wikipedia 60 metres. The minimum height is 130 centimetres, and children aged eight to ten need an adult with them.

### Desmo Race

[Desmo Race](ref:mirabilandia/desmo-race?full)

Desmo Race opened in 2019 in the Ducati area. It's two tracks side by side, each 525 metres long and 22 metres high, and the ride takes one minute at up to 80 km/h. You sit astride, and according to the park the ride is meant to feel like a Panigale V4. The park calls it interactive: you can accelerate and brake and so leave the other track behind. The manufacturer is Maurer Rides, and the type is called a Spike Coaster. Riders must be between 120 and 195 centimetres and weigh no more than 135 kilograms. Children under ten and under 140 centimetres ride with an adult.

In our data Desmo Race has the longest queue in the park, and the Flash Pass isn't valid there.

### Master Thai, Gold Digger and the family coasters

According to the park, [Master Thai](ref:mirabilandia/master-thai) is the first two-track coaster in Italy. Preston & Barbieri built it in 2011. It has a chain lift, no inversion and a Mobius layout in which two trains run against each other. The minimum height is 120 centimetres, and children aged six to eight ride with an adult.

[Gold Digger](ref:mirabilandia/gold-digger) is a wild mouse by L&T Systems: 361 metres, 14.5 metres high, 1:25 minutes. It runs in the western area from 90 centimetres. [Rexplorer](ref:mirabilandia/rexplorer) in Dinoland is the oldest coaster in the park that still runs, 250 metres long at 35 km/h, also from 90 centimetres. RCDB lists S.D.C. as the manufacturer, the German Wikipedia Mack Rides.

Two more run in the children's area: [PAW Patrol To The Rescue](ref:mirabilandia/paw-patrol-to-the-rescue), opened in 2005 as Leprotto Express, 135 metres and 34 km/h, and [Cowabunga Carts](ref:mirabilandia/cowabunga-carts), a Zierer Force 190 from 2025 with 192 metres, 40 km/h and a booster-wheel lift. Both are open from 90 centimetres, with younger children riding with an adult.

## Water rides, dark rides and towers

You get wet in four places. [Rio Bravo](ref:mirabilandia/rio-bravo) is the Intamin rafting ride from the opening year, in inflatable boats through a canyon with rapids, from 110 centimetres. [Autosplash](ref:mirabilandia/autosplash) opened the same year as an Intamin white-water ride. You sit in a Cadillac with tail fins, and at the end you drop 15 metres. [El Dorado Falls](ref:mirabilandia/el-dorado-falls) by Hopkins is a shoot-the-chute from 1999 with a 15-metre wave at the end. [Raratonga](ref:mirabilandia/raratonga) (2007, 3DBA) is an interactive boat ride where you shoot at totem poles. Autosplash, El Dorado Falls and Raratonga are open from 90 centimetres.

[Blu River](ref:mirabilandia/blu-river) is a Preston & Barbieri slide for two-person tubes from 1992, also from 90 centimetres.

The dark ride is [Reset](ref:mirabilandia/reset), built by Mack Rides in 2008. You drive through the ruins of a destroyed New York and shoot targets with laser guns. According to the English Wikipedia it was the first interactive dark ride in Italy. You can ride from 90 centimetres.

The tall ones include the two Oil Towers from 1997, 60 metres high. The [Eurowheel](ref:mirabilandia/eurowheel) is 90 metres tall and was the tallest Ferris wheel in Europe when it opened in 1999. According to the park it's now the second tallest after the London Eye. On a clear day you can see the Apennines, the Adriatic and San Marino. The Ferris wheel has no minimum height, and children under twelve ride with an adult. The [Aquila Tonante](ref:mirabilandia/aquila-tonante) is a Zamperla kite flyer (2016) where you fly face down, from 110 centimetres.

## The themed areas

Behind the entrance square, the Baia dei Pirati with the X Point for Flash Passes, the park splits into six areas:

- **Adventureland** (Katun, Master Thai, Rio Bravo) is the Mayan jungle.
- **Route 66** has the car theme of the American sixties: iSpeed, Divertical, Autosplash, Raratonga, Blu River, Reset and the Ferris wheel.
- **Far West Valley** (since 2016) is the western area with Gold Digger, Buffalo Bill Rodeo, Aquila Tonante, El Dorado Falls, Campo Sioux and Fort Alamo, the wooden playground.
- **Ducati World** (since 2019, 35,000 square metres) is, according to the park, the first themed area in the world about a motorcycle brand. It holds Desmo Race, the simulators, Kiddy Monster and the Diavel Ring.
- **Dinoland** (since 2014) is the area for small children with Reptilium, Raptotana, Rexplorer, Bicisauro and Monosauro.
- **Nickelodeon Land** (since 2025, 25,000 square metres) has areas for SpongeBob, PAW Patrol, Dora and the Ninja Turtles.

[Buffalo Bill Rodeo](ref:mirabilandia/buffalo-bill-rodeo) is a Zamperla Disk'O Coaster (2016), a spinning disc on 80 metres of track and 13 metres of height. It turns 14 times a minute and you can ride from 120 centimetres up to 190 centimetres. Ducati World also has simulators. [Motion Sphere](ref:mirabilandia/motion-sphere) costs extra and isn't included in admission. You can ride from 130 centimetres and up to 110 kilograms, and for children under 13 the "Motion" setting stays off.

## For small children: Nickelodeon Land and Dinoland

Children under 100 centimetres get in free. There are two areas for them.

**Nickelodeon Land** (2025, 25,000 square metres) has ten attractions, three restaurants, a shop, two meet-and-greet areas and a show stage, according to the park. The area is organised around four series. SpongeBob has Jellyfish Jam (from 100 centimetres, children under six and under 120 centimetres with an adult) and the Bikini Bottom Express (from 75 centimetres). PAW Patrol has the Adventure Bay playground, the PAW Patrol To The Rescue coaster, the water ride Rubble's Rapids and a carousel. There's also Dora's Train Adventure, Splish Splat as a water playground and, for the Ninja Turtles, the Cowabunga Carts. The park's website calls the area "Italy's Best New Family Attraction 2025".

In **Dinoland**, [Reptilium](ref:mirabilandia/reptilium) is open from 120 centimetres, while Raptotana (the spinning nests with Mama Velociraptor), Bicisauro and Monosauro are open from 90 centimetres. Children under ten need an adult with them on most rides there.

If you're coming with children, set height and weight in the access filter on the park's website and it shows which rides they can take.

## Shows

The most applauded show, according to the park, is the stunt show "Hot Wheels City, La nuova sfida". It has run in various versions since 2000, and since the partnership with Hot Wheels it includes an 18-metre loop that a car drives. "Pinocchio, il Musical" plays in the Coca-Cola theatre. For both shows you can book six reserved seats in the "Exclusive Experience" package. The package is aimed at adults, includes a ride with the stunt drivers in the car before the show and isn't available in the Halloween season.

For the Halloween season from 26 September to 1 November, the park's website lists these programmes: the Nickelodeon Halloween Party, "I misteri di Wondy" (new in 2026), Black Lake as a night show, R.I.P. Rest In Peace, Los Locos Fantasmas, Black Crow: the Musical and the meet & greet with the Nickelodeon characters.

## Halloween

Halloween runs from 26 September to 1 November in 2026. Admission includes Suburbia, which according to the park is the biggest horror zone of any theme park in Italy. It has more than 30,000 square metres, over 100 performers, five scare trails and, new this year, the Ossarium zone. Suburbia is open from 7 pm on 26 September, on all Saturdays in October and on 1 November.

Three horror houses cost extra: [The Walking Dead](ref:mirabilandia/the-walking-dead), [Paranormal Activity: Next of Kin](ref:mirabilandia/paranormal-activity-next-of-kin) (new in 2026, based on the film) and [Mr. Blackmoon's Hypnotic Circus](ref:mirabilandia/mr-blackmoons-hypnotic-circus). None of the three is included in park admission, and the Flash Pass isn't valid for The Walking Dead. Holders of a 2027 season pass get the Hypnotic Circus once a day for free in the 2026 season (from age 12, except on 31 October).

On 30 October there's the Scary Night: the park opens an extra session at 7:30 pm, with its own ticket. Season passes aren't valid that evening. Shuttle buses run from Lido di Savio/Lido di Classe station to the park at 7:45 and 8:45 pm, and back at 10 and 11 pm.

### Time of day

The water rides Autosplash, Rio Bravo and El Dorado Falls peak at 1 pm. In the evening their wait times drop clearly. iSpeed stays at a similar level all day, with a slight peak at 1 pm. ## A day in the park from 140 centimetres

Say you're at the entrance at 10:30 am and can ride everything. We have no analysis for Katun and Master Thai, so the plan follows the rides with data.

Start with Katun, Master Thai and iSpeed, which you'd ride before lunchtime anyway. Then Divertical, which at noon is still ahead of its climb. Between 1 and 2 pm, when Autosplash, Rio Bravo and El Dorado Falls peak, it's time for lunch. In our data Desmo Race is shortest from 1 pm and gets longer until 6 pm, so it's worth doing early in the afternoon. You ride the water rides in the early evening, after their peak. Autosplash and Rio Bravo are shortest from 8 pm, and on the October Saturdays the park is open until 10 pm.

You need two days if you're coming with children, want to do all of Nickelodeon Land and Dinoland, or add Mirabeach in summer. The water park has its own ticket.

## Flash Pass: priority through a separate entrance

Mirabilandia calls its fast pass the Flash Pass. It's an add-on to admission and doesn't replace it. According to the park you get priority through a separate entrance. It doesn't guarantee a seat in the front rows.

There are three versions. The **Gold Flash Pass** is a personal wristband with unlimited rides on the attractions with a Flash Pass entrance and costs from 49.90 euros online, from 59.90 euros in the park. The **Regular Flash Pass** is a transferable card with one ride per attraction, from 24.90 euros online, from 29.90 euros in the park. The **One Shot Flash Pass** is valid for one ride on a single attraction and is sold only in the park, from 5.90 euros, at the X Point, at the ticket office, in the Mayan Shop and at the Autosplash photo point.

Desmo Race, Blu River and The Walking Dead are excluded. Desmo Race is the ride with the longest queue in our table. The Flash Pass is valid on Katun, iSpeed, Divertical, Master Thai, Rio Bravo, Autosplash, El Dorado Falls, Gold Digger and most of the family rides. Passes bought online aren't valid on the day of purchase but on a visit day of your choice, while passes bought in the park are valid that day only. The number of passes is limited.

Where the wait is around a quarter of an hour, the pass doesn't save much. It does the most on the rides that come after Desmo Race in our table: Rio Bravo, iSpeed and Autosplash. The "VIP" package combines admission, the all-inclusive meal plan and a Regular Flash Pass.

## Tickets, prices and season pass

The adult day ticket (over 140 centimetres) starts at 27.90 euros online, and the earlier you book, the cheaper. According to the park you save up to 20 euros if you buy at least ten days ahead (offer until 25 October). At the ticket office the ticket costs 49.90 euros. The reduced ticket for children from 100 to 140 centimetres and for seniors over 60 costs from 27.90 euros online and 42.90 euros at the ticket office. Children under 100 centimetres pay nothing. The evening ticket from 5 pm costs 22.90 euros. The open-date ticket costs 49.90 euros (reduced 42.90) and is valid until the end of the season.

For visits from 19 September to 25 October 2026 there's a 40 percent Halloween discount online: adults pay 29.90 euros instead of 49.90 euros with the code 1HW2026, for a maximum of four tickets. The code is valid until 11 October and not on the day of purchase. A second day costs 6.90 euros, added at purchase, in the park before you leave or in the park-and-hotel package.

All tickets bought online include the good-weather guarantee: if it rains during your visit, you can come back for free. Admission covers all rides, shows and areas. Not included are the horror houses The Walking Dead, Paranormal Activity: Next of Kin and Mr. Blackmoon's Hypnotic Circus, the Motion Sphere simulators and Mirabeach.

On season passes there's an offer until 18 October: if you buy a pass for the 2027 season, you get into the park for the rest of the 2026 season free. The discount applies against the list price and only to new passes at the "new" rate.

## Getting there and parking

The park is on the SS 16 Adriatica, km 162, 48125 Savio (Ravenna). From the north and the south, take the A14 motorway to the Cesena Nord exit, then the E45 towards Ravenna to the Mirabilandia exit. From central Italy, take the A1 to Orte and then the E45. From the coast near Rimini, take the SS 16 Adriatica towards Ravenna.

Parking costs 9 euros for cars and motorcycles, and the ticket is paid at the entrance gate, even for a short stop. Motorhomes pay 18 euros for 48 hours, with water supply and waste water disposal but no electricity. The car park opens at least 30 minutes before the park and isn't guarded.

By train you reach Ravenna, Cesena or Rimini. The nearest station is Lido di Savio/Lido di Classe, from where a shuttle bus and bus line 176 run to the park. Line 176 also runs from Ravenna, and from Cervia, Lido di Savio and Milano Marittima. A Saca bus runs from Bologna and Modena. According to the park, a taxi from Lido di Savio station costs about 25 euros, from Cesena about 45 euros. Through the Discovera platform there's a package of return train travel and admission from several Italian cities.

## Hotels and food

There's no hotel in the park. The "Park + Hotel" offer runs through Mirabilandia Vacanze, the park's tour operator, and covers more than 200 partner hotels with three or four stars nearby. It includes a ticket for one to three consecutive days, bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to four days before arrival, and children up to and including age ten get free admission until 25 October. Prices start at 54.90 euros.

You eat at restaurants and kiosks, among them the Locanda del Faro, the Paddock Café, Goofy Goober's in Nickelodeon Land and the Bay Café. The park also has a McDonald's, Azuki Sushi and Italy & Italy. You can book the all-inclusive meal plan in advance, and it allows one meal every 60 minutes. Ristocredit is a credit you buy online and redeem at the till: 15 euros gets you 18 euros of credit, 20 euros gets you 24. Click & Collect lets you order ahead and pick up at your chosen time, without a table reservation. New in 2026 is the Coca-Cola Freestyle cup: it costs 15.90 euros online, you refill it free every 60 minutes at three stations (Autosplash, Rio Bravo, Dino's Pizza Time), over 100 flavours, and you keep it.

## What's new, and what has closed

2025 brought Nickelodeon Land and with it the Cowabunga Carts. New in 2026 are, among other things, Paranormal Activity: Next of Kin, the Ossarium zone in Suburbia, the show "I misteri di Wondy" and the Coca-Cola Freestyle cup. The monorail Mirabilandia Express (1992 to 2019), Colazione da Papere (2003 to 2023) and Casa Matta (2001 to 2024) have closed, according to the Italian Wikipedia. Sierra Tonante stood from 1992 to 2007.

In its 2027 season pass campaign, which started in early August, the park doesn't name a new ride for 2027.

## Mirabilandia FAQ

### How long are the wait times at Mirabilandia?

At most rides the median across our 42 measurement days since August 2026 is around a quarter of an hour. The queue is longest at Desmo Race, followed by Rio Bravo, iSpeed and Autosplash. The current wait time for each ride is on the park page.

### When is Mirabilandia least crowded?

According to our data so far, on a Friday, and less in September and October than in August. With five to nine days per weekday, that's a hint rather than a rule. Until the end of October the park is open on weekends only.

### Do I need the Flash Pass at Mirabilandia?

Not when waits are around a quarter of an hour. It pays off most on a busy day at Rio Bravo, iSpeed and Autosplash. At Desmo Race, the ride with the longest queue, it isn't valid.

### How tall do you have to be for Katun, iSpeed and Desmo Race?

140 centimetres for Katun and iSpeed, 130 for Divertical, 120 for Desmo Race and Master Thai and 110 for Rio Bravo. Autosplash, El Dorado Falls, Gold Digger and Rexplorer are open from 90 centimetres.

### How much does Mirabilandia cost?

The adult day ticket starts at 27.90 euros online and costs 49.90 euros at the ticket office. Children under 100 centimetres get in free. Parking costs 9 euros.

### When is Mirabilandia open?

The 2026 season runs from 2 April to 1 November. Until 24 October the park is open on weekends only, Saturdays from 10:30 am to 10 pm and Sundays until 6 pm.

### Can you do Mirabilandia in one day?

Yes, at the measured wait times the big coasters and the water rides fit into one day. With small children, Nickelodeon Land and Mirabeach you need two.

### How do you get to Mirabilandia without a car?

By train to Lido di Savio/Lido di Classe, from where a shuttle bus runs to the park, or from Ravenna on bus line 176.
