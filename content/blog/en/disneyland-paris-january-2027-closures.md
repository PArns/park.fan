Disneyland Paris is taking Mickey and the Magician off the schedule from 11 January 2027, because the Animagique Theater in [Disney Adventure World](ref:disney-adventure-world) is getting a major refurbishment. Next door in [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), [Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/indiana-jones-and-the-temple-of-peril) and [La Tanière du Dragon](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/la-taniere-du-dragon) close from 4 January. [Pirate Galleon](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/pirate-galleon) in Adventureland is already shut for the whole of January. Disney has put this on the show page and in its park hours calendar. Disney hasn't announced when Mickey will be back on stage.

## The last show day is 10 January

In the show, Mickey learns magic from characters who already know how: the Genie from Aladdin, Lumière from Beauty and the Beast and Rafiki from The Lion King. There's singing and dancing in between, and after about 30 minutes it's over.

The show won the Brass Ring Award from the industry association IAAPA in 2016. It's included in your Disney Adventure World ticket, but seats are limited. If you want to be sure of getting in, buy guaranteed access with its own queue. It's sold on the website, in the app or on the day at guest services up to an hour before the show starts. The show page doesn't give a price for it, only that children aged three and up pay the same as adults. The doors close five minutes before the start. Showtimes appear in the app up to 30 days ahead.

The show page names no replacement for the Animagique Theater and points to the rest of the entertainment in both parks. For the rest of January, the calendar has Minnie's Dream Factory, TOGETHER: a Pixar Musical Adventure and Goofy's Imaginary Orchestra in Disney Adventure World, plus the Disney Princess Cavalcade parade and, in the evening, the Disney Cascade of Lights show.

In Disneyland Park, The Lion King: Rhythms of the Pride Lands at the Frontierland Theater also takes a break in January. Its page gives a break from 7 January to 12 February 2027, but the calendar still has the show on until 10 January. If you're coming for the lions, plan around the earlier date.

## What's closed in January

These attractions in Disneyland Park are closed in January:

- Pirate Galleon in Adventureland: every day from 1 to 31 January
- Frontierland Playground in Frontierland: 1 to 3 January
- Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril in Adventureland: 4 to 31 January
- La Tanière du Dragon in Fantasyland: 4 to 31 January
- [Thunder Mesa Riverboat Landing](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/thunder-mesa-riverboat-landing) in Frontierland: 4 to 22 January
- Main Street Station on the [Disneyland Railroad](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/disneyland-railroad-main-street-station): 7 to 9 and 11 to 22 January
- [Le Carrousel de Lancelot](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/le-carrousel-de-lancelot) in Fantasyland: 12 to 14 January
- [Star Wars Hyperspace Mountain](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/star-wars-hyperspace-mountain) in Discoveryland: 25 and 26 January

Disney hasn't published opening hours for February yet, so there's no word on whether Indiana Jones, La Tanière du Dragon and Pirate Galleon reopen on 1 February. For the [Indiana Jones refurbishment](/news/indiana-jones-disneyland-paris-renovation-2027), Disney only ever spoke of several months. I wouldn't count on Indiana Jones during the February half-term either.

Families with small children lose the most in January, since La Tanière du Dragon, Pirate Galleon and Thunder Mesa Riverboat Landing have no height requirement. Beneath Sleeping Beauty Castle, La Tanière du Dragon keeps a defeated dragon chained up and asleep in a dark dungeon. Kids clamber across the deck of Pirate Galleon and look at the cannons. And the Molly Brown paddle steamer, which sails from Thunder Mesa along the Rivers of the Far West past Big Thunder Mountain, doesn't cast off again until 23 January.

In Disney Adventure World, only [Crush's Coaster](ref:disney-adventure-world/crushs-coaster) is closed in January. The coaster is being refurbished and is due back in summer 2027.

## What else is on in January

The Disney Enchanted Christmas season ends on 6 January, and Mickey's Christmas parade goes with it. From 7 January, the daytime parade Disney Stars on Parade is back in Disneyland Park after its Christmas break. In the evening, Disney Tales of Magic plays on Main Street, U.S.A. for about half an hour. Discoveryland is showing the 4D film [Mickey's PhilharMagic](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/mickeys-philharmagic).

## Opening hours in January 2027

Disney Adventure World keeps the same hours as Disneyland Park in January:

- 1 January: 9:30 am to 10 pm
- 2 and 3 January: 9 am to 10 pm
- Mondays to Thursdays from 4 January: 9:30 am to 9 pm
- Fridays: 9:30 am to 9 pm on 8 and 22 January, 9:30 am to 8:30 pm on 15 and 29 January
- Saturdays and Sundays from 9 January: 9:30 am to 10 pm

Guests at the Disney hotels and Les Villages Nature Paris, and Disneyland Pass Gold holders, get in an hour early, so from 8:30 am on most days and from 8 am on 2 and 3 January. Disney doesn't sell tickets at the entrance ticket booths. You get in with a dated ticket, or with an undated ticket or annual pass that you've registered for the day.

## When January is quieter

The Christmas holidays end in all three French holiday zones on 3 January, and the first day back at school is the day Indiana Jones and La Tanière du Dragon close. After that, schoolchildren in the Paris region are in class until 5 February, and their winter holidays start on 6 February.
