# A day that does not fit opens an assistant, not a footnote (REQUIREMENT)

One standing rule. It is indexed from the repo's [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), which carries the rule in one line and links here for the reasoning, the measurements and the counter-examples.

ten headliners and nine hours is a decision the app cannot make, and for a while it made it in a grey eleven-pixel clause („eine passt nicht mehr in den Tag") under a block drawn at **18:45 in a park that shuts at 18:00**. The information was there; there was nothing to answer it with. So both presses probe first (`needsFitHelp`) and open `PlannerFitAssistant` where anything would be left out, writing nothing until the last step. **Every hint is a measured difference between two plans, never advice**: `fitLevers` re-runs the whole optimiser once per free block without it and once with it cut to `SHORT_BLOCK_MIN`, and offers only what buys a ride — Phantasialand 2026-09-12 goes 9/10 → **10/10 without lunch**, Movie Park 2026-09-20 → 10/10 on **30 minutes instead of 60**, and 2026-10-03 says „dann passen 9 von 10" because that is what it is. Shortening is offered before dropping and dropping is not offered at all where half an hour already solved it; a lever is a **toggle**, so pressing it again puts the break back. The list is `fitOrder` and never a sort of its own — what is at the bottom of the screen is what the engine gives up first — with a tick (wanted at all) and a **pin** (`OptimizeInput.priority`) per row, both recomputing against the same search that runs on the press: on 2026-10-03 the default gives up Crazy Bats and River Quest, pinning those two moves it to Winja's Fear and Colorado Adventure. Two rules underneath. **A ticked wish is re-planned rather than parked past the gate** — every ride with a payload row goes to the engine as an ADD, which is what turns "which one falls out" from a remainder into a decision; `optimizeDay` alone may never delete somebody's entry, and this is the one place that is relaxed, in front of a list where the ride is named. And **the visitor's order beats the park's curation**: `isWanted` counts a ride named in `priority` as one somebody is there for, so a pinned filler no longer goes before an unpinned headliner (a no-op for every call site that predates it, since the only thing that ever passed `priority` was a list of headliners). The wizard asks the same question on a **step of its own** (`park → date → setup → headliners`) with the same two pieces inline, and `finish` reads the lunch block back out of `evaluateFit`'s answer rather than off a second piece of state, so the day filed is the day shown. The block on the axis says it too, in **two** shapes because the API reports a closing HOUR: past `closeMin + closeSlackMin` „Liegt nach Parkschluss", inside the hatched hour „Kann schon nach Parkschluss liegen", and nothing at all on a free block — telling somebody their dinner is after closing time is the app having an opinion about dinner. The result line is a bordered warning with an **„Anpassen"** button back into the assistant wherever something was left out. **And solving it may not take the screen away**, which is what the first version did: the wizard's block hung on the conflict alone, so the ride that made the day fit removed the thing used to fit it, and in the dialog the „fällt weg" marks simply went out with nothing saying the problem was over. Both carry a **state** now — the crowd tint with a warning mark while something is left out, `status-operating` with a check and „So passen alle 10 Bahnen in den Tag" once nothing is — and the wizard's block stays for as long as anything has been answered (`fitChoiceTouched`, derived, never latched, so a change of park or date cannot leave it standing). Measured over 35 park-days at six parks: 5 do not fit, 2 of them solved by one lever alone. `pnpm test:planner-fit` pins the model; `pnpm check:planner` walks the three steps against a seeded day that cannot fit.
