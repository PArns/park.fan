[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) is raising the price of a day ticket by €2.50 to €3.50 when its summer season opens on 20 March 2027. An adult will pay €70 to €79.50 online, and a day on the visitor car park goes from €10 to €12. Tickets for the new season have been on sale since 10 November 2026.

## What a day costs

The new prices apply all season until 9 January 2028, Halloween and winter included. The table shows online prices per person.

| Ticket                                       | 2026 season    | 2027 season    |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| Day ticket, children (4–11)                  | €56.50–65.00   | €59.00–68.00   |
| Day ticket, adults (12+)                     | €67.00–76.00   | €70.00–79.50   |
| Day ticket, seniors (60+)                    | €56.50–65.00   | €59.00–68.00   |
| 2-day ticket, children (4–11), seniors (60+) | €105.00–119.00 | €109.50–124.50 |
| 2-day ticket, adults                         | €127.00–143.50 | €132.50–150.00 |
| Toddlers (0–3)                               | free           | free           |

Each opening day falls into one of two price categories. On price category 1 days the higher prices in the table apply, on category 2 days the lower ones. The price calendar in the online ticket shop shows which category a day is in. The ticket office uses the same split.

With the 2-day ticket, your second visit can be any day within five days of the first. The park keeps a separate allocation of tickets back for that second day, so you're guaranteed to get in.

## Ticket office and parking

Every ticket costs €10 more at the ticket office than online.

The parking ticket covers a whole day on the visitor car park and still costs €10 until 19 March 2027. You can buy it in advance in the online shop. It's dated and only valid on the day you pick when you buy it. Enter your number plate at checkout and the barrier opens by itself on the way in and out; otherwise you scan the ticket.

Coaches park free on the visitor car park. Motorbikes and bicycles don't pay either and go to the "Reserviertes Parken" (reserved parking) area by the main entrance. Motorhomes and caravans longer than 5.5 metres park on the "Tagescamper" car park next to Silver Lake City. A day there currently costs €20, which you pay on site to a member of staff.

## What it means for a family

Take two adults and two children aged 4 to 11 who drive there and book everything online. On a price category 1 day their four day tickets come to €295, or €307 with parking. That's €15 more than in 2026, €2 of it for parking. On a price category 2 day the same four pay €258 for tickets and €270 with parking, €13 more than the year before.

The ticket office costs this family more than the whole price rise: buying the four tickets there adds €40 to the online price.

## Who pays less

If it's your birthday on the day of your visit and you're turning 12 or younger, you get in free. You'll need to show ID at the ticket office by the main entrance.

Groups, school classes and guests with disabilities have their own rates, which also go up on 20 March 2027:

- groups of 20 or more paying guests: €58.50 per person, previously €56.00
- school classes of 10 or more paying pupils: €40.00 per pupil, previously €38.50
- guests with disabilities aged 16 and over, online: €59.00–68.00, previously €56.50–65.00
- guests with disabilities aged 4 to 15, on site only: €40.00–49.00, previously €38.50–47.00
- blind guests (German disability card marked Bl) and wheelchair users with paraplegia (marked aG), on site only: €35.50–40.00, previously €34.00–38.00
- wheelchair users with tetraplegia: free

Hotel guests at the resort can enter the park before regular opening time, and the park gives them a best-price guarantee on their tickets. The ResortPass Silver and Gold annual passes can't be bought at all right now, because they've sold out.

## Vouchers and undated tickets

Undated tickets, voucher tickets and tickets from earlier seasons have to be assigned to a day before your visit at [europapark.de/umwandlung](https://www.europapark.de/umwandlung). If that day costs more than you paid, you pay the difference when you date the ticket, and the page shows the amount first. A voucher bought at 2026 prices can therefore cost extra for a day from 20 March 2027.

If you leave the dating until you're at the park, there's a €10 service fee per ticket. Undated tickets and voucher tickets are valid until the end of the third year after the year they were bought, so a 2026 voucher lasts until the end of 2029.

## Cheaper in March

From 7 to 19 March 2027, in the not quite two weeks before the season starts, the park opens for its Pre-Opening Weeks. An online day ticket then costs €59.50 to €70 for adults and €50 to €59 for children and seniors. That makes the dearest day in March cost the same as the cheapest day from 20 March. Our [post on the Pre-Opening Weeks](/news/europa-park-pre-opening-weeks-2027) lists the price of each day and what's open in those weeks.

## The 2027 season and opening hours

- Summer season: 20 March to 24 September 2027, daily from 9 a.m. until at least 6 p.m.
- Halloween: 25 September to 7 November 2027, daily from 9 a.m. until at least 6 p.m.
- HALLOWinter: 8 to 26 November 2027, daily from 11 a.m. until at least 7 p.m.
- Winterzauber: 27 November 2027 to 9 January 2028, daily from 11 a.m. until at least 7 p.m.
