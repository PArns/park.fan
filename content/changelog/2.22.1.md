## New

- Rides can pause during a season. Phantasialand's Phenies and River Quest, closed during
  Wintertraum, read "Paused during Wintertraum" with the dates instead of closed.
- Parky suggests a change to the trip you are planning right in the chat, such as a park on your
  arrival day, when it shortens the waits or brings in a park the plan left out. One tap applies
  it, "Another day" opens the swap for that park, and every change can be undone.
- The "Plan" menu's region scenes move while the menu is open: the rocket climbs from the space
  center, fireworks go off over Orlando, the Ferris wheel turns, blossoms fall in Tokyo and the
  Eiffel Tower sparkles.

## Improved

- The hotel step shows its areas on a real map with streets and the parks, and every step of the
  assistant opens at the top.
- The ticket card shows one ticket per resort: its park days, the days it is valid, whether a park
  hopper pays off and the shops that sell it, your own country's shop first. The 14-day tickets
  from UK shops sit in their own row as an alternative.
- The region hubs show their calendar one week at a time on the wait-time calendar's tiles, with a
  star on each park's quietest day of the week and the best park of each day on top.
- Comparing two days in a park's calendar names the season each day falls in, and the "Today in
  the park" panel names today's season.
- A day you change in your plan lights up briefly, and a change Parky makes can be undone like
  your own.
- "Back" and "Skip" in the assistant are easier to see, and days without a park are marked in the
  plan strip.

## Fixed

- On trips to Japan and Paris, the nights in each city showed a raw text key instead of "Osaka 4
  nights".
- A park closed for a whole trip, like Parc Astérix in March, no longer takes room from a second
  visit to another park.
- The park calendar now shows the closures we curate, as the region calendar already did.
