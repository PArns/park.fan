## New

- Search finds guides and news as well as parks and rides, three hits per group with the total in
  the heading. `/blog` and `/news` have a search field of their own that looks through every post,
  not only the page you are on.
- `/blog` and `/news` page through all posts, 20 to a page. `/de/blog` used to reach 12 of 45
  articles and now reaches all of them.
- A ride page opens with "… at a glance": type, maker and year, theme area, minimum height, the
  typical and the highest waits of the last 12 months, the quietest weekday and the record day.
  A line appears only where the park.fan data has the fact.
- The park statistics list the five busiest and the five quietest measured days, each with its
  crowd level and peak wait, linked to that month in the wait time calendar.
- The weather card shows gusts and visibility for the next hours. A row appears when gusts reach
  50 km/h or visibility drops below 1,000 m, and stays out of the way on a calm day.

## Improved

- The day planner works in quarter hours where the data has them, and it keeps its hourly plans
  unchanged where it does not.
- The top of a park page shows a week strip with each day marked quieter, average or busier for
  that park, and next to it the quietest and busiest weekdays and months. At Phantasialand Monday
  to Friday are quieter and Saturday and Sunday busier. The placeholder for the twelve-month
  forecast no longer prints "No forecast" twelve times.
- The headliner cards on the park page keep their order when the live waits update. On Fuji-Q
  Highland the page used to jump by 0.5 layout shift on a desktop screen.
- The news line on the home page runs the full width of the column from 1280 px, and the number of
  open parks moved into the world map panel.
- The imprint and the privacy notice are new. Photos by other people show their credit everywhere
  they appear.
- A ride with no queue, such as a carousel, says so as the reason for showing no outage figures.

## Fixed

- The home page no longer throws a hydration error on a phone or in Italian. On a 360 px phone it
  happened on 3 of 12 loads and on none of 24 after the fix. The Italian page showed it for a
  different reason, number formats that differ between server and browser, and none of 18 loads in
  six languages show it now.
- On `/compare` at 360 px the column "Quietest weekday" fits in the card again. The table was 431 px
  wide in a 326 px card and is 294 px now.
