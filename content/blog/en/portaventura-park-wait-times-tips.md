PortAventura World has three parks with their own tickets: PortAventura Park, Ferrari Land and the water park Caribe Aquatic Park. PortAventura Park has six themed areas: Mediterrània at the entrance, then Polynesia, China, México, Far West and SésamoAventura for small children. All prices and opening hours come from the park's website.

## How the park came about

Anheuser-Busch started the project and, according to El País, designed the park. In 1993 Variety reported that the brewer was planning it with the Spanish firm Grand Península under the name "Tibi Gardens". At the opening the Tussauds Group ran the park.

The park was inaugurated on 1 May 1995 and opened to paying guests on 2 May, the same day as Dragon Khan and the mine train El Diablo, according to RCDB. Jordi Pujol, then president of the Catalan government, rode Dragon Khan at the opening. At that point Tussauds held 40.01 per cent of the shares, La Caixa 33.19, Anheuser-Busch 19.9 and FECSA 6.7 per cent. The site covers 52 hectares. In 1998 Universal bought most of the Tussauds stake, and the park was called "Universal's Port Aventura". NBCUniversal began pulling out in 2004, and since 2005 the park has no longer carried the Universal name.

The resort with its hotels followed on 13 June 2002, Ferrari Land on 7 April 2017. According to Wikipedia the whole complex now belongs to Investindustrial and the investment firm KKR, which took just under half of the shares in 2013. PortAventura World reported around 5.5 million visits for 2023 (Vozpopuli) and 5.3 million for 2025, the year of its thirtieth anniversary (Diari de Tarragona), in each case for the resort with all three parks. According to Diari de Tarragona, about half the guests come from abroad, mainly France, the United Kingdom and Ireland. For the park alone, the TEA and AECOM Theme Index lists 3.975 million visits in 2023, up from 3.75 million in 2022, which put it sixth in Europe.

### New rides and removals since 1995

Templo del Fuego in the México area was built in 2001 and won a Thea Award from the Themed Entertainment Association in the attraction category in 2003. SésamoAventura, with the Sesame Street characters, opened on 8 April 2011. The dark ride Street Mission followed there in April 2019.

Fumanchú, a ride from the opening year, was taken down in 2012, and Shambhala's entrance stands in its place today. Tifón in Polynesia only ran until 1998. El Secreto de los Mayas replaced the Trono de Pacal in México, and Loco-Loco Tiki in SésamoAventura made way for Kiddie Dragons in 2017. The simulator Sea Odyssey from 2000 was renamed Ice Age in 2014 and Dino Escape 4D in 2017, and it has stood idle since 2020. REC Experience now runs in its rooms.

## The coasters in the park

Wikipedia counts ten coasters and five water rides at the resort, Ferrari Land included.

### Shambhala

Shambhala opened on 12 May 2012 and comes from Bolliger & Mabillard, the same manufacturer as Dragon Khan. It's a hypercoaster with no inversion: the lift hill is **76 metres** tall, the first drop measures 78 metres, and the train reaches **134 km/h** over 1,564 metres of track. Those are RCDB's numbers, and Coasterpedia gives the same ones. Three trains with 32 seats each carry 1,680 riders an hour. The first drop is 77.4 degrees steep, and a ride lasts three minutes. The minimum height is **1.40 m**, and there's no option to ride with an adult.

### Dragon Khan

Dragon Khan is 45 metres tall, has **eight inversions** and is also by Bolliger & Mabillard. The park's own website says that in 1995 the coaster had more inversions than any other in the world. The speed figures differ: the park advertises 110 km/h, while RCDB and Wikipedia list 104.6 km/h, which is 65 mph. The track is 1,269 metres long, and according to RCDB the ride lasts 1:45 minutes, with a first drop of 49 metres. The track was designed by the Stengel engineering office, and three trains with 28 seats each run on it. Guests from **1.40 m to 1.95 m** can ride.

### Furius Baco

Furius Baco is an Intamin launch coaster of the wing rider type, with four seats in a row, and opened on 5 June 2007. The train accelerates to **135 km/h**, in three seconds according to the park and in 3.5 according to RCDB. The track is 850 metres long, and Wikipedia says the ride has one inversion, an inline twist. The minimum height is 1.40 m and the maximum 1.95 m. The park places it in the Mediterrània area. Three trains with 24 seats each carry 1,500 guests an hour, and the ride lasts 55 seconds. The opening celebration with the motorcycle racer Valentino Rossi took place two days after the first day of operation.

### Stampida and Tomahawk

Stampida is the park's wooden coaster, built by Custom Coasters International. Since 17 March 1997 two trains have raced each other, one on the red track and one on the blue, each 953 metres long. RCDB gives 74 km/h, the park "up to 70", and the height is 25.6 metres. KumbaK trains have run on the rails since the 2007 season. Larry Bill designed the track, and a ride lasts 1:40 minutes. The minimum height is 1.20 m, with no option to ride with an adult.

Tomahawk, the children's coaster built by the same manufacturer in the same year, is 440 metres long and 13.4 metres tall and reaches 48 km/h, with a ride time of 1:10 minutes. Since 2015 it has run Mini-llennium Flyer trains from Great Coasters International, and the height range is 1.05 m to 1.85 m. Its track winds around Stampida's.

### El Diablo – Tren de la Mina

El Diablo is a mine train by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 2 May 1995 and, according to RCDB, is 1,008 metres long, with three lift hills of just over 16, 11 and 16.5 metres and a 3:10 ride time. A train holds 36 people. The ride is set in colonial-era Mexico, in an old silver mine that El Diablo shut down after miners vanished. The park says the cars are still made of wood. Since May 2025 RCDB has listed the ride as a virtual-reality hybrid, because guests can put on a headset. At **1.40 m** you can ride alone, at **1.10 m** with an adult.

### Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence

Uncharted is an enclosed Intamin launch coaster in the Far West area and opened on **17 June 2023**. The track is 673 metres long. PortAventura announced its agreement with Sony Pictures for a ride based on the Uncharted film in November 2022. Sally Corporation worked on it alongside Intamin, and according to Variety it cost 25 million euros. The car is launched five times and drops sideways once. Three cars with twelve seats each run on the track, and the highest point is twelve metres up. Anyone at least 1.10 m tall can ride with an adult, and the upper limit is 1.95 m. Penitence, the Western town where the ride is set, is also the scene of the new [Halloween pasaje](/blog/portaventura-halloween-2026).

### Rides for children

SésamoAventura has Tami-Tami (alone from 1.30 m, with an adult from 1.00 m) and Street Mission (from 1.20 m, with an adult from 0.90 m). Tomahawk (from 1.05 m) is on top of that. The park doesn't mention a rider switch for parents on its pages.

## Water rides and the tower

Hurakan Condor is an Intamin drop tower in the México area. The park says "more than 100 metres" tall, while Wikipedia gives a total height of 115 metres and an opening in 2005. Wikipedia and the Diari de Tarragona put the drop at about 86 to 87 metres. The minimum height is 1.40 m and the maximum 1.95 m.

## Shows and parades

From June to September the day ends with FiestAventura on the lake in Mediterrània, a show with drones, water, music and fireworks. The PortAventura Parade moves along the main street of Mediterrània, every evening according to the park, with more than 50 performers, Woody and the Sesame Street characters.

In Far West, Can Can Express plays in the saloon, where a railway tycoon called Andrew Dolar wants to have the building torn down, and West Gold Frenzy tells the story of a robbery in Penitence. The México area has México Lindo with dances from Veracruz and Jalisco during meals. Polynesia has Aloha Tahití, Pareos en Bora Bora and, in the evening, Noches de Fuego en Tahití with fire juggling. Magic Bubble in the Templo Mágico in China is a soap-bubble show, and the park says some bubbles are big enough to fit a person inside.

## When to go

On its website the park has a section called "Tips and recommendations". It says Saturdays and Sundays are the days families come, "and in general it's busier then". The ideal time is midweek, Tuesday to Thursday, as long as no public holiday falls on it. The park also advises checking the school holidays in Spain and France, since many French guests visit. It names summer, long weekends, Easter and Halloween as the busier periods.

For the 2026/27 school year, the TV broadcaster 3Cat lists the Catalan holidays as 12 October and 8 December. Christmas holidays run from 22 December 2026 to 7 January 2027, Easter holidays from 20 to 29 March 2027. Schools in the rest of Spain follow other dates.

### Halloween from 19 September to 15 November 2026

The park celebrates Halloween from 19 September to 15 November 2026. The five Pasajes del Terror are El Carnicero de Penitence (new), La Isla Maldita, La Muerte Viva, REC Experience and Angkor: La leyenda del Rey Caníbal. There's also the show "Resurrection at the Saloon" and the scare zone "La Maldición del Emperador". The Halloween Parade moves through Mediterrània, where Horror en el Lago also runs, México has El Día de los Muertos and China the Halloween Factory. Because the park itself lists Halloween as a busier time, a Tuesday or Wednesday pays off here. All the details on prices and age limits are in the [news post on Halloween at PortAventura](/blog/portaventura-halloween-2026).

### Christmas

According to the park's website, Christmas runs from 20 November 2026 to 6 January 2027. The programme includes the shows "Bubble Magic Christmas", "El bosque encantado", the "Circo de Navidad" (new), "Christmas Time" and the "Gran fiesta de Navidad", plus the Christmas parade, the arrival of the Three Kings as "La llegada de los emisarios reales" and "Ya es Navidad" in SésamoAventura. December opening hours are visible on the park's site only in the booking calendar.

## Tickets for Ferrari Land and Caribe Aquatic Park

In the park's words, the park ticket allows "one day of access to PortAventura Park", and nothing else. Ferrari Land and Caribe have their own admission tickets, or they're included in the combination tickets marked "2 parks" and "3 parks". The "1 day, 2 parks" ticket allows "one day of access to PortAventura Park and Ferrari Land on the same day". The three-park tickets include Caribe.

A day ticket for Ferrari Land alone costs from €21, one for Caribe from €32, in each case for adults. If you sleep in one of the resort's hotels, you get a day in Ferrari Land on top of park access.

The resort spent more than 100 million euros on Ferrari Land. The tallest ride is Red Force, an Intamin accelerator coaster 112 metres tall, with a top speed of 180 km/h and 880 metres of track. Other rides are Maranello Grand Race, Thrill Towers, Flying Dreams and Racing Legends. Taquilla lists Ferrari Land as open daily from 1 April to 31 December 2026, while a travel blog (El Viaje de Sofi) lists it open only on single days in November and December.

Caribe Aquatic Park is a water park with 16 attractions on around 50,000 square metres. According to Taquilla, its 2026 season ended on 20 September, and it's closed in October. In summer 2026, according to the Diari de Tarragona, Coral Bay opened there with the Leyenda Perdida and a water coaster from the Cyclone family.

## What's new in 2026

In summer 2026 **Makamanu Jungle** opened in Polynesia, an adventure trail in a former quarry: 135 metres long, with 18 huts up high, 20 rope tunnels, 3 net ladders and 4 slides. It includes the areas Forest for children up to 3, Nest, Trail and Summit, as well as an accessible path. The entrance leads straight from Far West, and there's no Express there.

In May 2026 the park also announced a Hard Rock Cafe at the entrance, with around 430 seats. According to the Diari de Tarragona, it replaces the LaLiga restaurant.

For 2027, the park hasn't confirmed anything new.

## A day in the park, roughly planned

If you're at the entrance when the gates open, go first to the three rides from the rope drop section: Hurakan Condor in México, Tutuki Splash in Polynesia and Silver River Flume in Far West. China has Shambhala and Dragon Khan, with lockers between the two. Far West has Stampida, Tomahawk, Uncharted and Grand Canyon Rapids, México has El Diablo and Templo del Fuego, and Mediterrània has Furius Baco. For children too small for the big rides, SésamoAventura has Street Mission and Tami-Tami.

## Getting there and where to stay

The address is Avinguda Alcalde Pere Molas, 43480 Vila-seca. From the A-7 motorway you drive straight into the car park. From Barcelona El Prat airport it takes a good hour according to the park, from Reus airport 15 minutes. Buses run by the company Plana connect the airports and the resort, and there are also taxis and rental cars from Avis, OK Mobility and Hertz. The Camp de Tarragona high-speed station has a bus connection, line L4.

By train, the Rodalies line R17 stops right at the resort, on the route from Barcelona-França. A Renfe combination ticket with admission costs from €41.95 for adults and €37.95 for juniors and seniors (Diari de Tarragona), and the park's ticket packages include the Rodalies return trip. The park's website carries a notice about construction work between Garraf and Sitges, with replacement buses between Barcelona and Sant Vicenç de Calders.

Parking costs from €16 for cars, from €21 in the preferred zone, from €16 for motorhomes and from €6 for motorcycles. If you leave the car park on the same day, your parking ticket lets you back in.

The resort has six hotels of its own, at these prices from per room and night: Gold River from €96, El Paso from €99, Caribe from €105, PortAventura from €110, Colorado Creek from €123 and the Mansión de Lucy from €275. Every night in a hotel brings an extra day of park access: one night means two days, two nights mean three days. A day in Ferrari Land comes on top. Off the grounds, four hotels of the Ponient chain are part of the offer: Vila Centric, Pirámide Salou, Dorada Palace and Marinada. The hotel page lists Early Access, half an hour before opening, only for stays from 23 March to 17 May, with no year given.

## Food, lockers, accessibility

You can't bring food and drinks into the park. There's a picnic area outside the entrance. Unpackaged water is free on request, and gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan dishes are on the menus.

For table service there's Iron Horse in Far West (burgers, ribs, steaks), Raco de Mar in Mediterrània (salads and rice dishes), the Vinosfera with tapas and the Hacienda El Charro in México. Self-service options include Bora Bora in Polynesia, La Cantina in México, Marco Polo in China (buffet) and Sichuan. In Ferrari Land, the Trattoria Maranello serves salads, pizza, pasta, meat and fish at the table. Pit Lane is quicker, with self-service, and there are also Paddock, Finish Line, Ice Cream Box and Stop & Go.

Lockers come in three sizes: small €8, medium €11 and large €15. There are also lockers at the rides, €2 for a rucksack, at four spots: between Shambhala and Dragon Khan, at the entrance to Furius Baco, next to Tutuki Splash and at the approach to Silver River Flume. After opening they're free for the next guest, and opening one again costs again.

A single stroller costs €12 to rent plus a €12 deposit, a double stroller €18 plus a €12 deposit, a wheelchair €12 plus €10. Guests with a recognised disability of 33 percent or more and proven reduced mobility get a pass for "Exclusive Access" to rides and shows, with up to four companions on the rides.

The park doesn't close in rain and doesn't refund tickets, but individual rides can be shut for safety in heavy rain, wind or thunderstorms.

## Frequently asked questions about PortAventura Park

### How tall does my child have to be to ride?

The lowest bar in the park is 0.90 m on Street Mission, when an adult rides along. Shambhala, Dragon Khan, Furius Baco, Hurakan Condor and El Diablo require 1.40 m when riding alone, Stampida 1.20 m and Tomahawk 1.05 m. Uncharted lets children from 1.10 m ride with an adult.

### How tall and how fast is Shambhala?

76 metres tall, a 78-metre drop, 134 km/h and 1,564 metres long. The ride has no inversion.

### Is Ferrari Land included in admission?

No. The "1 day PortAventura Park" ticket is valid for the park only. With the "1 day, 2 parks" combination ticket, Ferrari Land on the same day is included.

### Is Caribe Aquatic Park included in admission?

No, only in the three-park tickets and the Caribe tickets. In October the water park is closed anyway.

### Is Express worth it?

Express 5 Adrenaline covers seven rides and costs from €46, Express 10 from €59, Unlimited from €75. If you want to ride something twice, you need Unlimited, because Express 10 counts each ride only once.

### When is the park open in 2026?

According to the Diari de Tarragona, the season runs from 28 February 2026 to 6 January 2027. Halloween is from 19 September to 15 November, Christmas from 20 November to 6 January. Daily times are in the booking calendar on portaventuraworld.com.

### How do I get there without a car?

By Rodalies line R17 from Barcelona-França to the PortAventura World stop, by bus from the company Plana, or by taxi from the Camp de Tarragona station.

### Is there a single-rider queue?

The park's pages say nothing about it.
