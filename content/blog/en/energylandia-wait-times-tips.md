[Energylandia](ref:energylandia) is in Zator, in the Lesser Poland region, about 50 kilometres from Kraków and less than 20 from Wadowice and Oświęcim. It opened in 2014. By its own count it now has 133 attractions on 90 hectares, 20 of them roller coasters, while the RCDB counts 19. Even the lower figure beats every other theme park on the planet: Guinness lists Energylandia as the record holder, and the park's website calls it the "No. 1 Rollercoaster Park in the World".

The owner is Marek Goczał, whose businesses include the energy drink "Energy 2000". If the park's name makes you think of a can, you're not far off. In the 2026 ranking by the travel site Travelcircus, Energylandia came first out of 112 European parks, ahead of Europa-Park and Disneyland Paris.

## The rides by area

The park has seven themed areas: the Little Kids Zone (Bajkolandia in Polish), the Family Zone (Strefa familijna), the Extreme Zone (Strefa ekstremalna), the Water Park, Aqualantis, the Dragon Zone (Smoczy Gród) and Sweet Valley. Volana, the area built around Rokan, will be the eighth. In winter the park opens as Winter Kingdom, with an ice rink, snowtubing hills for adults and for children, and a new children's ride called Artic Loop.

### Extreme Zone

The tallest ride in the park is [Hyperion](ref:energylandia/pepsi-hyperion?bare), officially Pepsi Hyperion. Intamin built the mega coaster, and it first ran on 14 July 2018, the park's fourth birthday. The top of the lift is at 77 metres. The first drop falls 82 metres (park figure: over 80) at an angle of 85 degrees, and at the bottom the train reaches 141.9 km/h (park figure: 142). The 1,450-metre track has one inversion and forces of up to 4.8 G. Two trains of 28 seats each carry 1,300 riders an hour. When it opened for the 2018 season, Hyperion was the tallest and fastest mega coaster in Europe, by Energylandia's account.

[Mayan Zero Limitów](ref:energylandia/mayan-zero-limitow?bare) is a Vekoma Suspended Looping Coaster, the standard 689-metre model, and it opened on 12 September 2015. You hang below the track, climb to 33.3 metres, reach 80 km/h and go upside down five times, and the park quotes up to 5 G. The suffix in the name dates from 2026 and comes from a tie-up with Kanał Zero. "Zero limits" applies to the name only. At the entrance you still need to be 140 centimetres tall.

[Formuła](ref:energylandia/formula?bare) followed on 25 June 2016, a Vekoma Space Warp with a linear motor. The launch takes the train to 79.2 km/h in two seconds. Energylandia's figure is 100, which on a ride named after motor racing is maybe forgivable. It's 24.8 metres tall and 560 metres long, with three inversions and 3.8 G (park figure: 4.5).

The oldest coaster in the area is [Viking](ref:energylandia/viking?bare), a spinning wild mouse by SBF Visa, model MX52, which was already running on opening day, 14 July 2014. It's 11 metres tall (park figure: 13) and 310 metres long (park figure: 320), with a top speed of 43 km/h.

The Extreme Zone also has five rides that don't run on coaster track:

- Space Booster by Fabbri Group, 40 metres tall, up to 100 km/h and 4 G
- Tsunami Drop by SBF Visa, a 40-metre drop tower
- Aztec Swing by SBF Visa, with up to 4.5 G
- Space Gun by SBF Visa, 16 metres tall, 50 km/h and 3 G
- Apocalypto by Fabbri Group

### Dragon Zone (Smoczy Gród)

Energylandia built its fifth area, Smoczy Gród, for [Zadra](ref:energylandia/zadra-made-in-malopolska?bare). It covers five hectares and took eleven months to build, from November 2018 to August 2019. The coaster's full name is Made in Małopolska Zadra, and it opened on 22 August 2019. Rocky Mountain Construction built it with its I-Box track, and the first drop is a vertical 90 degrees.

Zadra is 62.8 metres tall (park figure: 63.8, and 63 elsewhere on its site). No hybrid coaster in the world is taller, and Iron Gwazi in Tampa matches it in the RCDB. Zadra reaches 121 km/h, goes upside down three times along 1,316 metres of track, and it's all over in 1 minute 50.

A month before Zadra, on 20 July 2019, two family coasters opened in the same area. [Frida](ref:energylandia/frida?bare) is a Vekoma junior coaster, 13 metres tall and 247 metres long, with a top speed of 44.6 km/h. [Draken](ref:energylandia/draken?bare) comes from Preston & Barbieri, a family coaster with 140 metres of track and a height of 7.3 metres. It runs at 34.4 km/h (park figure: 34.5).

Preston & Barbieri also made [Dragon Adventure](ref:energylandia/dragon-adventure?bare), a 270-metre ride at 3.5 km/h, which is about the speed of a slow stroll. The [Wonder Wheel](ref:energylandia/wonder-wheel?bare), a 53-metre Ferris wheel, has been turning in the Dragon Zone since 28 June 2022. [Fuego](ref:energylandia/fuego?bare) is in the Dragon Zone as well.

### Aqualantis

Aqualantis opened on the park's seventh birthday, 14 July 2021, with eight food courts and three shops. Its biggest ride is [Abyssus](ref:energylandia/abyssus?bare), a Vekoma Shockwave launched by linear motors. The track is 1,316 metres long, exactly as long as Zadra's to the metre. The highest point is 38.5 metres, the train reaches 100 km/h, and the RCDB counts four inversions. On Energylandia's site Abyssus is a "Double Coaster" with a ride time of up to two minutes and 4.5 G.

[Ekipa Light Explorers](ref:energylandia/ekipa-light-explorers?bare) opened the same day, a Vekoma Family Boomerang that's 24.2 metres tall, 238 metres long and reaches 60 km/h. The [Tidal Wave Twister](ref:energylandia/tidal-wave-twister?bare) by Zamperla covers 90 metres at 51 km/h. Only the park counts it as a roller coaster: the RCDB leaves it out, and Wikipedia lists it as a Disk'O, which makes it a flat ride.

Aqualantis also has the [Grotto Expedition](ref:energylandia/grotto-expedition?bare), [Submarine Dive](ref:energylandia/submarine-dive?bare), [Whirlpool Water Fight](ref:energylandia/whirlpool-water-fight?bare), [Magic Pump](ref:energylandia/magic-pump?bare), [Stormy Ship](ref:energylandia/stormy-ship?bare) and [Burning Engine](ref:energylandia/burning-engine?bare).

### Family Zone

In the Family Zone you hang beneath the track on [RMF Dragon](ref:energylandia/rmf-dragon?bare), a Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster. It opened on 4 June 2015 and is 19.3 metres tall (park figure: 20). Over 453 metres it takes the curves at up to 75.6 km/h and 3.5 G.

In the RCDB the [Boomerang](ref:energylandia/boomerang?bare) is a Vekoma Family Boomerang, Rebound model, opened on 29 April 2017. The Polish Wikipedia also gives April 2017, while Energylandia gives 2018 as the opening year. It has 205 metres of track (park figure: 200), a height of 23 metres (park figure: 25) and a top speed of 60 km/h (park figure: 70). A ride lasts 1 minute 36, forwards and then backwards along the same track.

Two coasters in the Family Zone date from the first year, 2014, and both are by SBF Visa. [Mars](ref:energylandia/mars?bare) is an MX610 family coaster, 150 metres long and 6 metres tall, with a top speed of 24.6 km/h (park figure: 24.7). [Frutti Loop](ref:energylandia/frutti-loop?bare), a Wacky Worm caterpillar ride, has 90 metres of track, a height of 3.7 metres and a top speed of 20 km/h.

These Family Zone rides run without coaster track:

- [Anaconda](ref:energylandia/anaconda?bare) by Intamin, 497 metres, 55 km/h, due to open in 2027
- [Toffifee Kopalnia Złota](ref:energylandia/toffifee-kopalnia-zlota?bare) by SBF Visa, 180 metres, 12 metres tall, 30 km/h, children aged 6 to 13 only with an adult
- [Jungle Adventure](ref:energylandia/jungle-adventure?bare) by Intamin, 486 metres at 20 km/h

The Family Zone also has [Atlantis](ref:energylandia/atlantis?bare), [Splash Battle](ref:energylandia/splash-battle?bare), the [Swiss Water Cups](ref:energylandia/swiss-water-cups?bare), the [Formula Autodrom](ref:energylandia/formula-autodrom?bare), the Muertos House, The Planes Ride and the [Viking Ride](ref:energylandia/viking-ride?bare).

### Little Kids Zone (Bajkolandia)

For children aged two to six there are 20 attractions in the Little Kids Zone and 28 across the whole park, by Energylandia's count. The biggest coaster here is [Śmiejżelki Energuś](ref:energylandia/smiejzelki-energus?bare), a Vekoma junior coaster from May 2015. It's 335 metres long and 13 metres tall, reaches 45.9 km/h (park figure: 45), and Energylandia adds 3 G.

[Happy Loops](ref:energylandia/happy-loops?bare) and the [Circus Coaster](ref:energylandia/circus-coaster?bare) arrived in 2017, both from SBF Visa, which the park lists as Visa Rides. Happy Loops is a spinning coaster, 7 metres tall, with a top speed of 29.9 km/h (park figure: 30) and 113 metres of track (park figure: 90). The Circus Coaster opened on 17 April 2017. It's a powered coaster with no lift hill, 32 metres of track and a top speed of 12.1 km/h (park figure: 12).

For the smallest children, who'll find even Energuś too fast, there's the [WRC Auto Zderzaki](ref:energylandia/wrc-auto-zderzaki?bare) bumper cars, the [Jeep Safari](ref:energylandia/jeep-safari?bare), [Magic Fly](ref:energylandia/magic-fly?bare), the [Farma Krasnali](ref:energylandia/farma-krasnali?bare) and the [Wyspa Skarbów](ref:energylandia/wyspa-skarbow?bare).

### Sweet Valley

Sweet Valley, the seventh area, opened on 27 April 2024 and covers four hectares. Vekoma built both of its coasters. [Choco Chip Creek](ref:energylandia/choco-chip-creek?bare) is a mine train, 1,200 metres long and 16.5 metres tall, with a top speed of 55 km/h. At 3 minutes 30, a ride lasts nearly twice as long as one on Zadra. [Honey Harbour](ref:energylandia/honey-harbour?bare) is a Kalypso family coaster, 254 metres long, 11.7 metres tall and 46 km/h fast, and you're back in the station after 56 seconds.

### Water Park

The Water Park has been open since summer 2016. It's included in admission, and Energylandia doesn't sell a separate ticket for it. In 2018 the park added Tropical Fun, with 14 slides and 150 single and double rings, and since then it says the Water Park is the largest outdoor water park in Poland. [Exotic Island](ref:energylandia/exotic-island?bare) followed in 2021 and [Bamboo Bay](ref:energylandia/bamboo-bay?bare) on 28 June 2022. There are lockers for your phone and wallet, so swimwear, a towel, flip-flops and sun cream can go in your bag in the morning.

### Height limits

The park sets a minimum and a maximum height for every attraction; on Hyperion and Speed, for instance, the cut-off is 195 cm. Where it says "with an adult", children between the two heights given can only ride with an adult.

- 140 cm and up: Hyperion (up to 195 cm), Zadra, Mayan Zero Limitów, Speed (up to 195 cm), Viking (also aged 12 and up), Tidal Wave Twister, Toffifee Kopalnia Złota, Atlantis (ages 4 to 12 with an adult), Rokan
- 140 to 195 cm: Space Booster, Space Gun, Aztec Swing, Apocalypto
- 130 to 195 cm: Tsunami Drop
- 130 cm and up: Dragon Adventure (children aged 2 to 13 with an adult)
- 120 cm and up: Formuła, Formula Autodrom, Draken (ages 4 to 13 with an adult), Mars, Frutti Loop and Happy Loops (each ages 4 to 12 with an adult), Circus Coaster (ages 2 to 13 with an adult)
- 120 to 140 cm with an adult: Abyssus, Choco Chip Creek, Anaconda, Jungle Adventure
- 120 to 130 cm with an adult: RMF Dragon
- 100 to 120 cm with an adult: Ekipa Light Explorers, Frida, Boomerang, Śmiejżelki Energuś, Honey Harbour
- Winter Kingdom: Artic Loop from 100 cm (up to 200 cm, ages 2 to 13 with an adult), snowtubing with no height limit (ages 3 to 7 with an adult)

### Winter Kingdom 2026/27

Energylandia is closed until 27 November 2026. From 28 November 2026 to 31 January 2027 it opens as Winter Kingdom, from 11:30 am to 6:30 pm on every open day. It's open on these days:

- November: 28 and 29
- December: 4 to 6, 11 to 13, 19 and 20, and 26 to 30
- January 2027: 2 to 6, 9 and 10, 16 and 17, 23 and 24, and 30 and 31

The park stays shut from 21 to 25 December and on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, so Father Christmas gets Christmas Eve off. Energylandia says Winter Kingdom has the largest light garden in Poland. The coasters that run in winter are Hyperion, Zadra, Mayan, Formuła, RMF Dragon, Choco Chip Creek and Energuś, plus the Space Booster and Aztec Swing. Abyssus, Speed, Viking, Boomerang, Happy Loops, Ekipa Light Explorers and Tidal Wave Twister only run in the summer season, and so do Apocalypto, Atlantis, Jungle Adventure and Toffifee Kopalnia Złota.

On top of the rides, winter brings a reindeer enclosure, meetings with Father Christmas, gingerbread baking, a workshop for writing letters to Father Christmas, a Christmas market, a winter parade and a Christmas menu.

The park recommends dressing in layers and wearing ski clothes. If you buy online by 27 November, your second Winter Kingdom ticket is half price, except for afternoon tickets and the Energy Pass. It's valid on any open day until 31 January.

## Energy Pass

Energylandia's fast pass is called the Energy Pass. It gets you onto every attraction on its list once, through a separate entrance marked "ENERGY PASS". It isn't an admission ticket, so you need a day ticket as well. The standard pass covers 14 attractions:

- Hyperion, Zadra, Mayan, Abyssus, Speed, Formuła, RMF Dragon, Choco Chip Creek, Śmiejżelki Energuś
- Toffifee Kopalnia Złota, Anaconda, Space Booster, Aztec Swing, Tsunami Drop

The reduced pass for children under 140 cm covers eight of them: Abyssus, Choco Chip Creek, Toffifee Kopalnia Złota, Śmiejżelki Energuś, Anaconda, Formuła, RMF Dragon and Tsunami Drop. In Winter Kingdom the standard pass covers Hyperion, Zadra, Mayan, Choco Chip Creek, Space Booster, Energuś, Aztec Swing, Formuła and RMF Dragon, and the reduced winter pass covers Choco Chip Creek, Energuś, Formuła and RMF Dragon.

In spring the Energy Pass costs more than the day ticket, and it only works once per ride. Since every shop in the park sells it, I wouldn't book it in advance. I'd check the waits at Zadra and Hyperion on the [park page](ref:energylandia) around midday and decide then.

## A day in the park

Say you're at the gate at 10 am on a Saturday in July without an Energy Pass. This is the order I'd ride in:

- Space Booster in the Extreme Zone
- Hyperion, Mayan and Formuła in the same area
- Abyssus and Ekipa Light Explorers in Aqualantis
- Speed, back in the Extreme Zone
- lunch, then Zadra and Frida in the Dragon Zone
- in the afternoon, Choco Chip Creek and Honey Harbour in Sweet Valley and RMF Dragon in the Family Zone
- on a warm day, Tropical Fun in the Water Park in the late afternoon

You can leave the park and come back on the same day. You get a wristband at the exit and show it with your ticket when you return. Dogs aren't allowed in, but assistance dogs are. There's a service desk for disabled visitors at the main entrance, and visually impaired guests can ride the coasters too.

Phones, cameras, food and alcohol aren't allowed on Hyperion and the other big rides, and you can't take a backpack on any coaster. Paid lockers are at the entrance, in the Extreme Zone and at Hyperion and Zadra. Smaller rides have shelves to leave things on, and the park takes no responsibility for them.

Besides children under 140 cm, the reduced price applies to seniors aged 65 and over, disabled visitors and pregnant women, while companions pay full price on the single ticket. Pregnant women can't go on any rides, though, and their reduced ticket is only sold at the ticket desk on showing the maternity record. Children up to three cost 1 zł, or €1 if you book in English, an exchange rate you won't get at any bank. Age is proven with an ID card or birth certificate.

Apart from the season pass, the birthday ticket and the bride-and-groom ticket are also only sold at the ticket desk. The latter lets a couple in wedding clothes into the park for a photo shoot, with at most two photographers. Groups of 15 or more book through the group department, on +48 33 486 15 00 or at grupy@energylandia.pl. Energylandia sells tickets online in its [ticket shop](https://tickets.energylandia.pl/en/), at the ticket desks and through partners.

## Parking

The car park is in front of the entrance and costs 30 zł for the whole day, or €8. Coaches park for free. A season parking card costs 149 zł and is only sold at the ticket desk, and there are charging points for electric cars.

## Getting there

The address for your satnav is al. 3 Maja 2, 32-640 Zator. The park says it's less than half an hour by car from Katowice and from Kraków. From Warsaw it's about 335 kilometres. You can reach the park by phone on +48 725 25 25 25 or by email at info@energylandia.pl.

The station for the park is Zator Park Rozrywki, and the timetables are on KOLEO. For Winter Kingdom, from 28 November to 12 December 2026, a train leaves Kraków Główny at 9:18 am, and the return train leaves Zator Park Rozrywki at 7:03 pm. The "Cho Cho Train" picks you up at the station at 10:25 am and takes you back at 6:20 pm. In the same period there's also a train from Oświęcim to Winter Kingdom. Buses run to Zator from Kraków and Katowice.

## Food

There are 67 restaurants and stalls in the park. You'll find pizza at Formula Pizza, burgers at Sunken Burger, crêpes at Octopus Crepe, waffles at Wacky Waffles, shakes at Sweet Shake and coffee at The Coffee Crab. Then there's the Palm Grill, the Hall of Columns, a Pierogarnia for pierogi, a Viking Kebab, the Kings Arthur Browar and the Restauracja Widokowa, a restaurant with a view.

You can't bring alcohol in, and anything you buy in the park you can only drink in the places set aside for it. Backpacks are allowed, and Energylandia suggests packing water and snacks.

## Where to stay

The park runs Route 44 Camping for caravans and motorhomes, opened in 2025, four stars and styled on Route 66. A night costs 199 zł (€50) in the low season and 249 zł (€63) from 26 June to 31 August 2027, booked through [route44.energylandia.pl](https://route44.energylandia.pl/).

Energylandia also recommends partner accommodation nearby:

- Hotel Aquila, 5 kilometres

## Rokan, Volana and Anaconda

[Rokan](ref:energylandia/rokan?bare) is a Bolliger & Mabillard wing coaster and, according to the park, the tallest and longest in Europe. It will be the first of its kind in Poland. The ride will stand 62 metres tall with 1,215 metres of track, and the trains are due to hit 115 km/h. The RCDB counts three inversions, and a ride is set to last 141 seconds. Three trains are planned, each with eight rows of four seats. Energylandia announced Rokan on 15 October 2026, after a teaser had been on its website since 8 October. Our [news on Rokan](/news/energylandia-rokan-wing-coaster) has a table of every B&M wing coaster in Europe.

Volana's theme is the legend of the Valley of the Monoliths, with Rokan as the valley's winged guardian. The area and the coaster open on the same day. Energylandia says spring 2027 and hasn't announced the date yet. Construction began in the first quarter of 2026, and the first track pieces from B&M arrived on site on 21 August 2026. Anaconda in the Family Zone is also due to open in 2027, and it's already on the Energy Pass list.

## History

Energylandia opened on 14 July 2014 with three coasters: Frutti Loop, Mars and Viking. The park is run by Energylandia Goczał Family sp. k. Until 2018 at least one new coaster arrived every year, and in 2015 there were three. The Water Park opened in summer 2016, and 2018 brought Speed and Hyperion, the two big Intamin rides. On 27 October 2018 Energylandia presented plans to expand the site to a total of 150 hectares.

In autumn 2019 the park also announced that it would open in winter from the 2020 season. Because of COVID-19 it then had to postpone the start of the 2020 season and didn't open until 6 June. Aqualantis followed in 2021 and the Wonder Wheel in 2022.

In 2025 more than two million visitors came, more than ever before, and the park welcomed its two-millionth on 18 October. The Polish Wikipedia gives 2.1 million for 2025. The park says it passed two million again in 2026 and that 19 million visitors have come since 2014. At the European Star Awards 2025, Energylandia came eighth among Europe's parks, Zadra fourth among steel coasters and Speed fifth among water rides.

## Frequently asked questions about Energylandia

### Where can I see Energylandia's wait times live?

The [Energylandia park page](ref:energylandia) on park.fan has the current wait at every ride and whether it's running. The park is closed until 27 November 2026, and wait times come back with Winter Kingdom from 28 November.

### How busy is Energylandia, and is there a crowd calendar?

The [wait-time calendar](ref:energylandia?calendar) shows the expected crowd level for every open day. We measure the longest queues at Zadra, Hyperion, Speed and RMF Dragon.

### What are Energylandia's opening hours?

Winter Kingdom runs from 28 November 2026 to 31 January 2027 on selected days from 11:30 am to 6:30 pm, but not from 21 to 25 December or on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day. The 2027 season starts on 3 April with opening from 10 am to 6 pm, and until 8 pm in summer from 26 June to 31 August.

### How late is Energylandia open in October?

During Halloween Time from 10 am to 6 pm, and until 7 pm on the Halloween Weekends. In 2027 the Halloween Weekends are 23 and 24 and 30 and 31 October, and the park closes from 1 November.

### When does Rokan open at Energylandia?

Energylandia says spring 2027 and hasn't given a date yet. Rokan opens together with the new Volana area.

### Is Rokan the tallest wing coaster?

In Europe it is, according to Energylandia: the Bolliger & Mabillard ride will be 62 metres tall and 1,215 metres long, which makes it the tallest and longest wing coaster on the continent.

### How many roller coasters does Energylandia have?

The park counts 20 and the RCDB 19, because it leaves out the Tidal Wave Twister. Rokan adds one more in 2027. No other theme park in the world has as many.

### How much is the Energy Pass?

159 zł in Winter Kingdom and 239 zł in summer 2027, less for children under 140 cm in both cases. It's valid once each on 14 attractions and doesn't replace the day ticket.

### How much is a ticket to Energylandia?

A day costs 149 zł in Winter Kingdom, or 84 zł from 3 pm. From April to June 2027 it's 209 zł and in summer 229 zł, or 169 and 189 zł for children under 140 cm. Children up to three pay 1 zł, and the Water Park is always included.

### How tall do you have to be for the roller coasters?

Hyperion, Zadra, Mayan, Speed, Viking and Rokan need 140 cm, Formuła, Draken and Mars 120 cm. Abyssus and Choco Chip Creek take riders from 120 cm, with an adult up to 140 cm, and Frida, Energuś and Honey Harbour from 100 cm with an adult.

### Can I bring my dog?

No, dogs aren't allowed in Energylandia for safety reasons. Assistance dogs are the exception.
