On the evening of Thursday 19 November, Europa-Park announced "Bring Your Friend" in its Facebook group "Europa-Park Resort Freunde", and it's valid straight away. Until 18 December, you can bring a friend into [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) for free on any day from Monday to Friday, once with Silver, twice with Gold.

## The free ticket is in the ResortPass portal

Your friend's ticket comes from the ResortPass portal at resort-pass.de. Log in with your MackOne account. The graphic that came with the announcement lists it as a "For friends" ticket at €0.00. There's a daily limit on these tickets, and some days may already be sold out. For a fixed date, such as 18 December, the last day of the offer, book your friend's ticket now, not the week before.

You book your own visit in the portal as usual, because as a day guest you need a reservation for every visit with the ResortPass. You can hold up to five days at once, even on the day itself while places are left, and cancel any you won't use. Once a day is fully booked, nobody on the phone or at the gate can get you in. Guests at the resort's hotels and at Silver Lake City already have their own reservation with the hotel booking.

## 21 days to choose from

The park is open every day throughout the offer. HALLOWinter runs until 27 November and Winterzauber starts on 28 November, both from 11 am until at least 7 pm. Hotel guests go in earlier. Because the announcement came out around closing time, Friday 20 November is the first full day of the offer. That leaves 21 weekdays up to Friday 18 December: six during HALLOWinter and 15 during Winterzauber.

Both passes get you in on all 21 days: Silver's blackout dates don't start until 26 December, and Gold has none.

## What your friend would pay otherwise

Every day of the offer falls in the cheaper of the two price categories, where an online day ticket costs €67 for adults and €56.50 for children aged 4 to 11 and for seniors aged 60 and over. At the ticket desk, add €10. With Gold, two adult friends save €134 between them, which makes the ticket price a thin excuse. Bringing a child under four wastes the offer, though, since they get in free anyway.

## HALLOWinter and Winterzauber

During HALLOWinter, Halloween pumpkins and Christmas trees share the park. From 28 November, Winterzauber adds:

- the Christmas market in the Historic Balthasar Castle Park
- an ice rink
- the Bellevue Ferris wheel
- a Christmas circus
- the Eurosat mapping show in the French area
- the "Alpin" children's ski school with snow tubes, and Skitty World Nordic, a cross-country skiing area for kids
- the "Monaco im Europa-Park" exhibition, new this winter

The other parks opening this winter are in the [overview of theme parks in winter 2026/27](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

## You can't buy a ResortPass right now

The allocation has run out, so the park isn't selling new passes until further notice, not by phone, email or at the ticket desk either. If you have one, you'll be told before it expires and can renew it in the portal during the last 14 days.

Silver is valid for day guests on more than 230 set days a year, Gold on every opening day, and Gold comes with two day tickets for [Rulantica](/news/rulantica-vinterglanz-2026), which you also reserve in the portal.
