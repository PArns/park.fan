[Adventureland](ref:adventureland-resort) in Altoona, Iowa, is building a new slide called Twisted Tiki at its Adventure Bay water park for summer 2027. It's the park's first slide where a family rides together in one raft. Adventureland announced it on 17 November, and construction has already started. Adventure Bay last grew in 2012, and the slide adds almost an acre (about 0.4 hectares) to the water park.

## Five storeys up, in a closed tube

The slide maker ProSlide is building Twisted Tiki. A raft holds up to three people, the start is five storeys up, and the tube runs for about 150 metres. It's enclosed all the way, so you won't see any of the Iowa sky on the way down. The tube twists through two 360-degree spirals, and coloured rings and light effects glow in the curves.

Adventureland hasn't published a height requirement for Twisted Tiki yet. Hyperlight, Bermuda Quadrangle and the Gang Plank & Pirates' Plummet slides all require 48 inches (122 cm).

> [!QUOTE]
> As our first-ever family raft slide, it gives our visitors a new way to experience the water park together, with thrilling twists, vibrant lights and plenty of surprises along the way. We can't wait to welcome guests aboard in summer 2027.
>
> Mike Lusky, general manager of Adventureland, in the [17 November 2026 press release](https://adventurelandpark.com/press/all-new-twisted-tiki-water-slide/)

There's no opening day yet. The park says the 2027 calendar and the year's events will follow soon.

## What 2027 season passes cost right now

The Black Friday sale takes up to $60 off 2027 season passes bought online. Adventureland hasn't given an end date; the sale runs "for a limited time". You can pay for any pass in eight instalments.

| Pass     | Black Friday price | Regular price | Includes                                                                      |
| -------- | ------------------ | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bronze   | $69.99             | $119.99       | summer, Sunday to Friday, 5% off food and merchandise                         |
| Silver   | from $129.99       | $169.99       | the full season with Oktoberfest and Phantom Fall Fest, half-price parking    |
| Gold     | from $159.99       | $219.99       | free parking, two friend tickets, one free tube rental a day at Adventure Bay |
| Platinum | from $199.99       | $259.99       | Neon Nights, early entry to Adventure Bay, ten more parks                     |

A 2027 day ticket costs $69.99 too. So the same money buys you either one day or a whole summer, except that Bronze leaves out Saturdays. Children aged three and under get in free. Both tickets and season passes include Adventure Bay whenever the water park is open, though at peak times the water park caps how many guests it lets in. If you had a season pass in 2024, 2025 or 2026, you save up to $80 when you renew. Platinum covers ten other parks as well, Noah's Ark Waterpark in Wisconsin among them.

## What else is at Adventure Bay

Children under 48 inches have to wear a life jacket in the Breaker Beach wave pool. The Caribbean Cruiser lazy river floats you through the water park, and on Reef Racer six people race each other side by side. Gang Plank & Pirates' Plummet are the fastest slides in Adventure Bay. For kids there's the pirate ship Shipwreck Shores and Kokomo Kove with its tipping buckets. The smallest slides at Kokomo Kove are open from 36 inches (91 cm), the rest from 40 inches (102 cm). Adventure Bay closes during thunderstorms as soon as lightning is detected within ten miles (16 km).

The last two additions were built around light too. Hyperlight, a slide with changing colours and surround sound, opened on Memorial Day weekend 2025. Neon Nights followed in 2026: on Fridays and Saturdays from 10 July to 15 August, more than ten attractions lit up in the evening, with roaming performers and music in between. Silver and Gold passes get you discounted tickets for it in 2027.

## Getting to Altoona

Adventureland is a few minutes from Des Moines, right off Interstate 80, at 3200 Adventureland Drive. So you reach the Caribbean flair the press release promises from an interstate exit in the Midwest. Parking is $21 a day, or $26 for Preferred Parking.
