## Improved

- About 30 KB less before the first paint (one font fewer), and the animation library out of the
  first download (about 40 KB compressed). Background photos on a computer are 30 to 40 % smaller.
- The guide page uses the real park header, weather card and statistics.

## Fixed

- An address for a park or ride that does not exist answered with a server error; it's a 404 page
  now.
- A closed park's ride list disappeared on phones.
- Times on park cards, showtimes and the best time on ride cards pushed the layout when they
  arrived.
