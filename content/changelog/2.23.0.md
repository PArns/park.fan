## New

- The homepage has a stop for the day planner below the trip regions: "Plan your park day ride by
  ride", with six parks to start a day at and "Plan with Parky" to plan it in the chat. Beside it,
  Parky's board draws a day that fills block by block once it reaches the middle of the screen.
  With a plan in your browser it shows your next planned day and "Open my plan".

## Improved

- "Optimise the day" and "Plan every headliner" start each ride on the five-minute mark instead of
  the next quarter hour, and a short queue no longer holds a full quarter hour. Across 23 park days
  with every headliner, the time between leaving one queue and joining the next fell from 23.7 to
  15.7 minutes, and the last queue ends about an hour earlier.
- The planner counts the walk between rides, so a day no longer crosses the park for a queue a
  minute shorter. At Phantasialand on Saturday 21 November, every headliner now means 1.0 km on
  foot instead of 1.8, with Winja's Force and Winja's Fear back to back instead of nearly five hours
  apart. A queue five minutes shorter still wins against a kilometre more of walking.
- After "Optimise the day", the result line also names the walking saved, for example "10 min less
  walking".
- The Parky scenes of the trip regions keep playing on the homepage, on a park page's trip planner
  tile and in the trip planner's assistant while you can see them, instead of stopping after one
  run.
- The call-to-action box in a post sits centred and is only as wide as its text and button.

## Fixed

- On the 23 headliner days we checked, all 177 transfers between two planned rides read "transfer
  tight". The verdict was measured against the forecast's spread for the whole day, which is often
  wider than the day's longest queue. "Transfer tight" now means you only make it at a brisk walk,
  and a day the planner ordered reads "transfer fine".
