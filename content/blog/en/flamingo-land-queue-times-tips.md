[Flamingo Land](ref:flamingo-land) is in Kirby Misperton, a small village between Malton and Pickering in North Yorkshire. A zoo, a theme park and a holiday resort share its 375 acres, a little over 150 hectares. The zoo and the rides run into each other so completely that in high season the park doesn't sell a zoo-only ticket at all. The suspended coaster Kumali is named after the lion who leads the pride in the zoo.

The Gibb family owns the park, Gordon Gibb is its general manager, and the slogan is "Wild Animals, Wilder Rides". Eight roller coasters are running, four of them with inversions. On top of those there's Cliff Hanger, a 54.9-metre drop tower, and Pterodactyl, a tower with swinging chairs.

## The rides by area

The park map splits Flamingo Land into nine areas: The Lost Kingdom, Dino-Stone Park, Flamingo 1, Metropolis, Splosh, Riverside 1, Children's Planet, Muddy Duck Farm and the zoo. The big rides are in six of them, and Muddy Duck Farm and Children's Planet are mostly for children.

### Metropolis

The park's newest coaster, and at 875 metres its longest, is in Metropolis. [Sik](ref:flamingo-land/sik?bare) opened on 2 July 2022, an Intamin multi-inversion coaster. A cable lift pulls the train up to 33 metres, the first drop is 31 metres, and at the bottom you're doing 87 km/h. After that the train goes upside down ten times: a loop, a cobra roll, a double corkscrew, four heartline rolls back to back and a fifth one. A ride takes 1 minute 5 seconds, and with two trains of 24 seats Sik is designed for 1,250 riders an hour. You need to be 1.40 metres to ride, and anyone over 1.95 metres stays on the ground.


The coaster was ordered in 2011 by Hopi Hari, a park in Brazil. It was never built there, and the parts sat unused for years. By way of Hopi Hari and Movie Animation Park Studios it ended up in Yorkshire, where RCS GmbH put it up. Sik takes its name from SikSilk, a clothing brand from Scarborough whose founders are friends of Gordon Gibb, and it has a shop of its own in Metropolis, the Sik Shop.

The [Runaway Train](ref:flamingo-land/runaway-train?bare) has moved house several times, and the Gallopers carousel in Metropolis used to stand somewhere else too, at Butlin's in Filey. Before the Zamperla family coaster came to Flamingo Land in 2007, it ran at American Adventure as Mini Mine Rush and at Gulliver's World. Until 2019 it stood in another spot in the park, which it had to give up for Sik, and in 2020 it moved to Metropolis. It's 4 metres tall and 80 metres long, a train has twelve seats, and the minimum height is 1.04 metres.

### The Lost Kingdom

At 35.9 metres, [Kumali](ref:flamingo-land/kumali?bare) is the tallest coaster in the park. Vekoma built it as a Suspended Looping Coaster to a design by Stefan Holtman, and it opened on 1 April 2006. The first drop measures 34.1 metres and the top speed is 88 km/h. Over 671 metres you hang upside down four times, in a loop, a cobra roll and a zero-g roll, at up to 4.7 g. Two trains of 20 seats each take 1 minute 32 seconds per ride, enough for 1,125 riders an hour. To ride you need to be at least 1.30 metres tall.


After its 30-metre lift hill, [Mumbo Jumbo](ref:flamingo-land/mumbo-jumbo?bare) drops at 112 degrees, tipping 22 degrees past vertical back underneath itself. That made it the steepest steel coaster in the world when it opened on 4 July 2009, until Timber Drop at Fraispertuis City arrived in July 2011 with 113.1 degrees. Alan Schilke designed it, and S&S Worldwide built it as an El Loco. The drop is followed by a dive loop and an in-line twist. You can ride from 1.22 metres, four to a single car.

Voodoo, a swinging ship by HUSS, has been rocking back and forth in the Lost Kingdom since 2003. You see the area's animals in Magombera Monkey Forest, at the Giraffe Encounter and from the [Daktari Express](ref:flamingo-land/daktari-express?bare). The train is a narrow-gauge replica of an American steam locomotive and takes 20 minutes to go round the Lost Kingdom's animal enclosure. It stops at three stations, by Kumali, at the Plaza and in the zoo, and on the way you pass giraffes, hippos, zebras and ostriches.

### Flamingo 1

[Velocity](ref:flamingo-land/velocity?bare) is the only motorbike launch coaster in Britain. You sit astride it like a motorbike, and a hydraulic launch takes you from 0 to 97 km/h in 2.8 seconds. Vekoma built the Motorbike Coaster to a design by Peter Clerx, and it started running on 2 July 2005. The track is 643 metres long and 17.4 metres high, with no inversions. Velocity runs a single train of 16 seats, which gets 518 riders an hour through, not even half of what Sik manages. You need to be between 1.40 and 1.98 metres.

[Hero](ref:flamingo-land/hero?bare) has been flying since 16 July 2013, a Volare flying coaster that Zamperla built to a design by Werner Stengel. Each car holds four people, a spiral lift takes them up to 15.2 metres, and over 391 metres of track they go through two heartline rolls at up to 41 km/h. The minimum height is 1.27 metres.


On Zamperla's [Navigator](ref:flamingo-land/navigator?bare), up to 40 riders sit on a disc that swings more than 18 metres high on a 32-metre half-pipe. A small version of it is the Vortex, which you can ride alone from 1.07 metres and with an adult below that.

On [Flamingo 1](ref:flamingo-land/flamingo-1?bare), the ride the area is named after, you drive an electric car yourself round a 380-metre figure-of-eight track. There are three lanes, the cars do about 16 km/h, and the track runs straight through a building called The Hub. Pink Elephants is here as well, and for the smallest there's the Frog Hopper from 0.90 metres and HMS Bouncy, an indoor climbing frame that only takes children up to 1.24 metres. The People Mover runs between Flamingo 1 and Muddy Duck Farm, alone from 1.07 metres.

### Riverside 1

[Cliff Hanger](ref:flamingo-land/cliff-hanger?bare) is the only S&S Combo Tower in Britain, two rides on one tower. On one you're shot upwards, on the other you free-fall down. The tower came to the park in 2002, and the minimum height is 1.32 metres. Riverside 1 also has the Entertainment Stage and the Balloon Race, which children can ride alone from 1.07 metres.

### Splosh

[Splash Battle](ref:flamingo-land/splash-battle?bare) came to Splosh in 2007, an area it shares with the Splish Splosh water play area, Crazy Combat Laser Battle and Swoosh Air. You ride in a submarine and fire water cannons at the other boats and at people standing along the edge. Preston & Barbieri built it, and you can ride from just 0.91 metres.

[Zooom!](ref:flamingo-land/zooom?bare) does without a lift hill, and it's the only coaster in Splosh. Zamperla built the suspended family coaster as an Air Force model, and it opened on 2 July 2011. Tyres push the train up to 9.8 metres, and the track is 172 metres long. Children ride alone from 1.07 metres, and with an adult below that.

### Dino-Stone Park

[Pterodactyl](ref:flamingo-land/pterodactyl?bare), from 2012, is a swing tower that lifts its seats up to 46 metres while they reach 40 km/h.

[Twistosaurus](ref:flamingo-land/twistosaurus?bare) is a small Zamperla spinning coaster that opened on 25 May 2013, with one train of four spinning cars that seat four each. The track is 175 metres long and 8 metres high, the top speed is 28 km/h, and the minimum height is 1.05 metres. The area also has animatronic dinosaurs, a dig site called Dino Dig and the Cyclosoar pedal-powered flyers.

### Muddy Duck Farm and Children's Planet

Muddy Duck Farm is where the Information Centre answers questions, and since 2001 it's also where the Little Monsters Mummy, Drac and Yeti have lived in the ghost train [Mischief Mansion](ref:flamingo-land/mischief-mansion?bare), a cast list that took about as long to name as it takes to read. Little Monsters Wacky Races uses replicas of the Ford Model T. For children the farm has a tractor ride, the Helitoys, the Jungle Carousel with elephants and teacups, and a pirate ship to climb on.

Children's Planet has a playground, a sand area, the Meerkat Encounter and the Picnic Planet picnic area.

With a plaster cast you can't go on most rides, and on the coasters not at all.

## The zoo

More than 140 species of reptiles, mammals and birds live in the zoo. The animal list on the website names 70 animals, among them the African lion, the white rhino, the black rhino, the hippo, the Rothschild's giraffe, the Sumatran tiger, the Cape fur seal and the mangabey. The zoo is a member of the zoo associations BIAZA and EAZA and supports a conservation project in the Udzungwa forest in Tanzania.

The newest enclosure is Lion Coast, home to the fur seals. The penguins live in Penguin Coast, where the keeper talk is at 3pm; the zoo also has the Treetop Walkway, Lily Bobtail Nature Discovery and the Peter Rabbit Adventure. The Zoo Monorail runs over baboons, meerkats, lemurs and emus. You can get closer to lemurs and penguins at the Lemur Encounter and the Penguin Encounter, both at extra cost.

At the keeper talks, the keepers tell you about their animals at the enclosures. In season they run at these times:

- 11am Magombera monkeys
- 11:30am flamingos
- 12pm hippos
- 12:30pm baboons
- 12:45pm black rhinos
- 1:30pm tigers
- 1:45pm red panda
- 2pm fur seals in Lion Coast
- 3pm penguins
- 3:30pm Meet a Creature

Dogs on a lead are allowed in the theme park, but not in the zoo, Children's Planet or the Splish Splosh water play area, an arrangement the lions presumably don't mind either. Assistance dogs can go everywhere. On selected days between early December and March you can visit just the zoo, at a reduced price.

## Christmas at Flamingo Land 2026

Santa only appears on the Christmas Experience Days, and between Christmas and New Year the park opens for Winter Opening:

- Christmas Experience Days: Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December, Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December, and every day from 19 to 24 December, 10am to 5:30pm, on Christmas Eve 10am to 4pm
- Winter Opening: 29 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, 10am to 4pm
- closed: 25 to 28 December

On every one of these dates a day costs £18 with the "Winter at Flamingo Land" ticket. Disabled visitors also pay £18, their carer gets in at no extra charge, and children up to three go free. The park doesn't say whether the visit to Santa and the present are included in that price. Annual pass holders need a separate Christmas Passport for the Christmas activities.

On the Christmas Experience Days the programme centres on the Christmas tree in Muddy Duck Square. A Peter Rabbit show starts there at 4:30pm, and the lights go on at 4:45pm. On Christmas Eve the park closes at 4pm, so before the switch-on. In Santa's Grotto, Santa sees 15 children at a time, from different families too, so Santa works in batches here. Afterwards the children pick a present in Santa's Magical Toy Shop. The programme also includes:

- gingerbread baking with Mrs Claus
- the Elf Hunt, a search for elves around the park
- pick & mix reindeer food you put together yourself
- a letter to Santa
- the Decoration Station and the Wishing Well
- meeting Peter Rabbit

No roller coasters run in December, so anyone who came for Mumbo Jumbo will have to make do with a steam train, a monorail and a ghost train. The Christmas page lists the Daktari Express, the Gallopers, the Helitoys, the Jungle Carousel, the Zoo Monorail, Mischief Mansion and the Muddy Duck Tractors, and says the list may change. With children under 1.07 metres you ride along on most of them.

In December the Muddy Duck Cafe and the Spud Stop are open from 10am to 5:30pm, and there are doughnuts and Crumble Heaven too. The Coach House opens on Fridays from 4pm to 9pm, on Saturdays from 12pm to 9pm and on Sundays from 12pm to 6pm, and it now does fish & chips to take away. The Street Food Kitchen is open from 6pm to 11pm, and so is The Club for resort guests, on Fridays and Saturdays from 4pm to 11pm.

Over Christmas you can stay in cottages and lodges from Friday to Sunday. Santa calls in person at the Lifestyle Lodge, the Mulberry Lodge, The Gatehouse and the Riverside Cottage. The package includes the zoo, the small children's rides on Muddy Duck Farm, the Splash Zone on Saturday and Sunday, and the Christmas Passport with the "PolarCoaster Express".

## VIP Ride Pass

The VIP Ride Pass gets you one ride each on six rides, through the exit instead of the queue.

- VIP Ride Pass: Mumbo Jumbo, Hero, Velocity, Kumali, Cliff Hanger and Sik
- VIP Ride Pass Junior Package: Pterodactyl, Voodoo, Twistosaurus, Splash Battle, Zooom! and Flamingo 1

You buy it on the day, at the VIP pass kiosk or the Queue Buster Outlet on the Plaza. It costs £50 per person. The VIP Ride Passes page gives no separate price for the Junior Package. You can ride in any order, but there has to be an hour between two rides, so all six take at least five hours. At busy times you may wait a little at the exit as well. The pass isn't transferable, and the park won't replace a lost or damaged card.

If a ride is closed all day, you can take a second VIP ride on another ride from the list once you've told the team. While Kumali is down for maintenance, that applies to one of the six. The annual pass gives no discount on the VIP Ride Pass.

Disabled visitors get a free Queue Pass with wristbands: orange for you, purple for your carer and up to four yellow ones for the family. That also takes you to the ride through the exit.

In low season the pass, at £50, costs more than admission at £30. I'd only buy it on a peak season Saturday or Sunday, and even then only after riding Hero and Mumbo Jumbo in the morning and seeing how long the queues for Sik and Velocity are at midday.

## A day in the park

Say you're at the entrance at 10am on a peak season Saturday, with no VIP Ride Pass. This is how I'd do it:

- at opening, Hero in Flamingo 1
- straight after, Mumbo Jumbo in the Lost Kingdom and, once it's running again, Kumali
- at 11am, the Pirates of Zanzibar show at the Black Heart pirate ship, if you want to see it
- back to Flamingo 1 for Velocity
- at 12pm the keeper talk at the hippos, then lunch
- in the early afternoon, Sik in Metropolis
- Cliff Hanger in Riverside 1 and Pterodactyl in Dino-Stone Park
- a round on the Daktari Express from the station by Kumali
- to finish, the zoo with Lion Coast and the penguins at 3pm

On Saturdays and Sundays Pirates of Zanzibar also plays at 3pm; on weekdays it's only at 11am. Peter Rabbit meets children at 11:40am and 1:45pm in Plaza Square. I'd leave the zoo until last, because on some days it stays open longer than the rides.

You can't bring alcohol into the park, it's taken off you at the entrance, and knives, weapons and fireworks are banned too. There may be bag checks and sniffer dogs at the entrance. The terms of admission say nothing about bringing your own food.

### Annual Pass

The Annual Pass costs £199 and is valid for 365 days from purchase. At £46 a day in peak season, it pays for itself from the fifth visit. It's tied to one person, a replacement costs £10, and you can't combine it with a stay in the Holiday Village. You get 10 per cent off food (not drinks) and in the shops. Paid extras such as animal encounters, the "Stalk in the Park" tour and the Christmas activities aren't included.

## Parking

Parking is included with every ticket, for cars, minibuses and coaches. Every car park is no more than a five-minute walk from the entrance. There are EV charging points in the main car park, and disabled spaces right by the entrance. If you nip back to the car during the day, a hand stamp gets you back in. Coach drivers of pre-booked groups get in free.

## Getting there

For the satnav, the postcode is YO17 6UX and the address Flamingo Land Ltd., Kirby Misperton, Malton, North Yorkshire. The park is on the A169 between Malton and Pickering, and you reach it via the A64 or the A170.

The nearest railway station is Malton. National Express coaches go as far as York, and from York station the Yorkshire Coastliner bus takes you to Flamingo Land without changing. Coach trips from the region are run by Family Outings from Teesside and Cleveland, Acklams Coaches from Hull and East Yorkshire, and Caledonian Breaks. Flamingo Land's phone number is 0800 40 888 40.

## Staying at the resort

The resort has the Lifestyle Lodge for up to six people, The Gatehouse, the Riverside Cottages in former farmhouses, Holiday Lodges and Holiday Homes in Gold, Platinum and Platinum Plus grades. Book direct through flamingoland.co.uk and park admission is included from opening on the day you arrive to closing on the day you leave. On top of that you get the Splash Zone with slides, spa pool, sauna and steam room, the gym, the evening entertainment, Wi-Fi and a parking space at your accommodation.

The Splash Zone is for overnight guests only, from March to November and on Saturday and Sunday in the Christmas package. A locker there takes a £1 deposit. The "Walk on the Wild Side" night safari costs extra. The resort also has a Leisure Centre with two pools, a fitness room, a sauna and a whirlpool, and a nine-hole golf course.

When you book you pay a £100 deposit for a Holiday Home and £200 for lodges and cottages, with the rest due 56 days before arrival. Dogs are only allowed in the dog-friendly accommodation and on dedicated pitches, for a fee. On the campsite you can pitch a tent or park a caravan.

## What's closed

Flamingo Land currently lists Kumali, the Navigator, the People Mover and Little Monsters Wacky Races as temporarily closed.

Bear's Lost River Ride, the log flume from 2004, is no longer on the park map as a ride; only the Lost River Gifts shop is. According to Wikipedia, the park is rebuilding it itself, with a smaller drop.

The coasters Go Gator and Dino Roller have been taken down. Flip Flop and Rotataur are missing from the park map too, and the Tea Cups ran until 2023.

## History

In 1959 the cinema owner Edwin Pentland Hick sold his chain of cinemas and bought a bankrupt country club in Kirby Misperton to open a zoo there. The Yorkshire Zoological Gardens started out at 9 acres, about 3.6 hectares, and a colony of flamingos was among the first animals.

In 1963 bottlenose dolphins arrived in Kirby Misperton, one of them called Sooty. A small funfair was added in the 1960s, in 1965 the company floated on the stock market as Associated Pleasure Parks, and in 1968 the zoo was renamed Flamingo Park Zoo. The same year it bought the orca Cuddles in Seattle, passing him on to Dudley Zoo in 1971.

In 1974 the park was making a loss, was remodelled and from then on was called Flamingo Land. Four years later its owner, Scotia Leisure Ltd, sold it to Robert Gibb, one of its former directors. When Robert Gibb died in a car accident in 1995, his son Gordon, then 18, took over with his sisters Vicky and Melanie. From 23 May 1993 to April 2010, Pleasure Island in Cleethorpes was its sister park; after that it carried on independently and closed at the end of 2016.

In 1990 the park put up the Corkscrew, which had stood at Spanish City in Whitley Bay and ran until 2011, and in 1997 a Wild Mouse, which made way for Hero in 2012. The Navigator arrived in April 2005, and the same year Velocity took the place of the Thunder Mountain coaster, at an estimated £7 million. The park spent more than £4 million on Mumbo Jumbo and £8 million on Hero, and the R&B singer Lemar came to Hero's opening party on 24 July 2013. Sik cost around £18 million.

On 22 May 2015 a footrest came off a moving car on Hero and hit two people in the queue. A 15-year-old was taken to hospital and a 26-year-old was treated at the scene, and the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) and the police investigated.

With Go Gator, Dino Roller, the Corkscrew, the Wild Mouse and Thunder Mountain, Flamingo Land has taken down ten roller coasters; the other five were called Bullet, Flying Trapeze, Magnum Force, Crazy Loop and Runaway Mine Train, whose train runs in Metropolis today.

### Where can I see Flamingo Land queue times live?

The theme park is closed until 4 December 2026. After that it's open on the Christmas Experience Days on 5, 6, 12 and 13 December and every day from 19 to 24 December, then again from 29 December to 3 January. No roller coasters run on those days.

### Is there a crowd calendar for Flamingo Land?

Yes, the [queue time calendar](ref:flamingo-land?calendar) on park.fan gives the expected crowd level for every opening day. Flamingo Land hasn't published its 2027 opening days yet; they'll be added as soon as the park announces them.

### What are the quiet days at Flamingo Land?

Queues build faster at weekends than on weekdays, especially at Hero and Flamingo 1. The cheapest days of the 2026 season were the low season ones, which in October meant the weekdays from 1 to 9 October.

### What are the average queue times at Flamingo Land?

Each ride's typical queue time is on its page on park.fan, split into weekdays and weekends. We measure the longest queues at Hero, at the Flamingo 1 electric cars and at Mumbo Jumbo, while at Sik you usually wait less.

### When is rope drop worth it at Flamingo Land?

An early start pays off most at Hero, ideally in the first half hour after the 10am opening and above all at weekends. Mumbo Jumbo has its shortest queue right at opening.

### How much is a ticket for Flamingo Land?

In 2026 a peak season day booked more than seven days ahead cost £46, a low season day £30 and a winter day in December and January £18. Children up to three go free, and from four they pay the full price. The Annual Pass costs £199.

### How much is the VIP Ride Pass?

Flamingo Land gives £50 per person. The pass covers one ride each through the exit on Mumbo Jumbo, Hero, Velocity, Kumali, Cliff Hanger and Sik, with an hour between two rides. The Junior Package covers Pterodactyl, Voodoo, Twistosaurus, Splash Battle, Zooom! and Flamingo 1.

### Is Flamingo Land open in winter?

Yes, on the Christmas Experience Days in December from 10am to 5:30pm, until 4pm on Christmas Eve, and for Winter Opening from 29 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, from 10am to 4pm. The roller coasters don't run then, but the Daktari Express, the Zoo Monorail, Mischief Mansion and the children's rides do.

### Can you visit just the zoo at Flamingo Land?

Not in high season, because the zoo and the theme park run into each other. On selected days between early December and March the park sells a zoo-only visit at a reduced price.

### How tall do you have to be for the roller coasters?

Sik and Velocity need 1.40 metres, Kumali 1.30 metres, Hero 1.27 metres and Mumbo Jumbo 1.22 metres. Twistosaurus takes riders from 1.05 metres, the Runaway Train from 1.04 metres, and on Zooom! children under 1.07 metres can ride with an adult.
