## New

- `/favorites` lists everything you have starred (16 September). The menu's "view all" used to lead
  to the top of the homepage, where the band sat two screens down. `/favorites` and `/alerts` are
  linked from the header menu in every state and from the footer.
- In the calendar's day view, each headliner's forecast shows how far it may be off, as a ± figure
  the way the planner writes it, and says when the model gave no range (15 September).
- The estimate for how long a ride stays down is drawn as well as written: a bar on a fixed scale
  from now to four hours, and a meter for how many outages of the same length were over within the
  hour (15 September).

## Improved

- Each section behind "More" in the header is a card with an icon, and the whole card is the link.
  The blog is back in the header bar as an entry of its own, "Backstage" (16 September).
- The footer's links sit in three named columns, content, tools and legal, where one row broke
  wherever the width ran out (18 September).
- With a mouse, a planner block moves in five-minute steps instead of fifteen, and the outline you
  drag shows its time however short the block is (18 September).

## Fixed

- On 18 September every map tile on every park page came back as "403 Access blocked". We had been
  loading the tiles from OpenStreetMap's own server, which is not meant for embedding. The park
  map gets its tiles from CARTO, credited to OpenStreetMap and CARTO.
