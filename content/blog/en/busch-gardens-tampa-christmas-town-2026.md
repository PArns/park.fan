Christmas Town opens at [Busch Gardens Tampa Bay](ref:busch-gardens-tampa) in Florida on Friday, November 13, and for the first time it comes with a drone show. The acrobatics show A Very Merry Cirque is new as well. The event runs on selected days until January 4, 2027, from opening to park close, and it's included with regular admission.

## The two new shows

The drone show is called Lights of Wonder, presented by GEICO. The drones fly to classic Christmas songs above Festival Field, whose entrance is near Pass Member Landing, on selected days from November 13 to December 31. On New Year's Eve the park also has new fireworks, and Festival Field is the best place to watch those too.

A Very Merry Cirque is an acrobatics show on selected days from November to January. Busch Gardens hasn't said yet which stage it's on.

## Event days and hours in November

Christmas Town runs on 14 days in November. On each of them the park opens at 10 am:

- Friday, November 13, and Sunday, November 15: until 9 pm
- Saturday, November 14: until 10 pm
- Thursday, November 19: until 8 pm
- Friday, November 20, through Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving): until 9 pm
- Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28: until 10 pm
- Sunday, November 29: until 9 pm

There's no Christmas program from November 16 to 18 or on November 30. The park's calendar also lists a Thanksgiving Feast on Thanksgiving Day.

Opening night has a program of its own on Cheetah Hunt Plaza. Radio station Mix 100.7 broadcasts live from there between 5 and 7 pm with Miguel & Holly and Brian Fink, and the Christmas tree lights up at 7.

## What's back

Christmas on Ice plays the Moroccan Palace Theater from November 13 to January 4, with figure skaters, which in Tampa's climate counts as a minor achievement all by itself. Seating is first come, first served, and once the theater is full the doors may close before the show starts. If you want a particular performance, get there early.

Outside Serengeti Overlook Restaurant, thousands of lights move to music in the Serengeti Light Show. Across the park, the Celebration of Lights strings up almost 70 miles (about 113 km) of lights, with stilt walkers in between. In the evening the Holly Jolly Express leaves from [Nairobi Station](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/serengeti-express-nairobi-station): a train with Christmas songs that crosses the 65-acre (26-hectare) savanna where the giraffes, rhinos and zebras live.

On selected nights in November and December the Ginja Ninjas breakdance on Jungala Plaza. The plot: Mrs. Claus's cookie recipe has gone missing. Whether they dance depends on the weather.

The Three Kings Journey at Dragon Fire Grill starts on November 21 according to the park's show page and on December 21 according to its event page; both say it ends on January 4. From December 4 to 12, the menorah between the Moroccan Palace and the cheetah habitat is lit every evening at 6 pm. On December 17 the Coca-Cola Holiday Caravan stops on Festival Field with a free sample while supplies last. For shopping, there's the new Christmas Town Market this year.

## For kids

Elmo's Christmas Wish starts ahead of the event, on November 6, and plays the Sunny Day Theater in Sesame Street Safari of Fun until January 4. On selected days you can take photos with the Sesame Street characters at 123 Smile.

Until December 24, Festival Walkway has life-size scenes from the Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer picture book, where you'll meet Rudolph and Bumble. Also until Christmas Eve, Santa sits in his study at Santa's North Pole Experience and poses for photos with kids. Until January 4, Mrs. Claus reads stories at the Canopy, and the princesses Ice, Sugar Plum and Poinsettia walk around the park.

## What costs extra

The Welcome Center and the holiday food stands open at 4 pm on event nights. A sampler lanyard with 5 or 10 tastings starts at $45, and pass members get 12 tastings for the price of 10. New this year is a $35 dessert sampler with a hot chocolate in a souvenir mug and four desserts, valid from November 13 to January 4. The Savory Sleigh hut serves braised short rib, pumpkin soup and eggnog bread pudding, among other things, and Holiday Spice has a beef Wellington.

The Rudolph & Friends Character Dine costs $42.99. Two VIP tours last four hours each:

- Holly VIP Tour, private, $150: front-of-line access to Santa, reserved seats at Christmas on Ice, a flamingo feeding and a meal
- Jolly VIP Tour, in a group, $125: bites at the huts, front-of-line access on selected coasters, a giraffe feeding on the Serengeti Safari and reserved seats at Christmas on Ice

## Tickets and parking

In the Black Friday sale a day ticket starts at $71.99 instead of the regular $150.99, or $111.99 with All-Day Dining. The Ultimate Bundle includes unlimited Quick Queue, except on [Iron Gwazi](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/iron-gwazi) and [Phoenix Rising](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/phoenix-rising-now-open). The 2027 Fun Card costs $89.99 and is good for unlimited visits until December 31, 2027, blockout dates aside, with the rest of 2026 thrown in for free. Parking starts at $35 plus tax.
