Holiday in the Park starts at [Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) in New Jersey on Saturday, 21 November. This year, for the first time, the park splits the event into four Christmas areas of its own. According to Six Flags' announcement, [Jersey Devil Coaster](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/jersey-devil-coaster?bare), [El Toro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/el-toro?bare) and [Runaway Mine Train](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/runaway-mine-train?bare) won't run at any point during the event. So New Jersey's own devil gets the holidays off, of all times.

## Dates and opening hours

On Friday, 20 November, passholders have the park to themselves from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m. at the Season Passholder Preview Night. Everyone else can come from the following day, and every Holiday day runs from 1 p.m. to 8 p.m.:

- November: 21, 22, 27, 28 and 29
- December: 5, 6, 12, 13, 19, 20, 23 and 26 to 31
- January 2027: 1, 2 and 3

The park is closed on Thanksgiving, 26 November, and on 24 and 25 December. New Year's Eve and New Year's Day are open, though. Six Flags' event overview gives 3 January 2026 as the last day, while in the park's calendar it's 3 January 2027. The water park and Wild Safari stay closed on every Holiday day.

After Fright Fest, the park still opens before the event from 5 to 8 November and on 14 and 15 November, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. each day, with no programme.

## The four areas and the shows

Six Flags calls this a return of the event and says it has redesigned it from scratch. At the entrance, in Glittering Gateway, there's a 65-foot Christmas tree, just under 20 metres. That's where the Tree Lighting Spectacular happens every evening, along with the Glistening Snow Show and its falling snow, which means New Jersey gets snow on schedule in at least one spot. Santa waits in Santa's Arctic North Pole, which also has fire pits and "S'mores with Santa's Helpers".

Coastal Christmas sits on Shoreline Pier, which the park has just rebuilt, among glittering palm trees. Sweet Street has the Sweet Tooth Stage with a silent disco. Outside the four areas, the Showcase Theater puts on "Holiday Road: LIVE on Tour!". More fire pits for s'mores burn behind the Big Wheel, and the mirror maze goes by Elf Craze Mirror Maze. There are shows on the main stage, and carolers walk the park.

Six Flags hasn't published times for the shows or the tree lighting yet. For food, the park so far only promises holiday dishes and drinks, with no menu and no prices.

## Which rides stay shut

Six Flags only lists the exceptions. Besides the three coasters, Tango and the rides in Bugs Bunny National Park won't run during Holiday in the Park. Many other rides are meant to operate, though there's no list. Whether they run depends on weather and temperature, Six Flags says. The park hasn't entered ride times for the Holiday days in its calendar yet. The park's own page for [Nitro](ref:six-flags-great-adventure/nitro?bare) lists it as temporarily closed, with no reason and no date. The rides coming in 2027 are in our roundup of [new theme park attractions for 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027).

## Tickets, parking and getting there

Holiday in the Park is included in the Gold Season Pass and in an active membership. The event page says that can also apply to buyers from the 2025 MVP Sale, to the All-Park Add-On, to the Prestige Pass and to Gold passholders at some other Six Flags parks on the East Coast. If you buy a 2027 Season Pass now, you get the rest of 2026 thrown in, Holiday in the Park included. Parking in the general lot is free with it.

The 2027 Gold Pass costs $99 instead of $115, and the price goes up from 2 November. The Prestige Pass is $249. Memberships cost $8 or $20 a month plus a $20 initiation fee. Six Flags adds a processing fee of up to $9.99 to all three.

So far the park's ticket page only lists day prices for the fall events, with none yet for a Holiday evening. General parking starts at $45 a day and Priority Parking at $65, each plus a fee. Six Flags doesn't say whether those prices also apply on Holiday evenings. For the satnav, the address is 1 Six Flags Blvd, Jackson Township, NJ 08527.

## John Reilly's letter

On 23 October, CEO John Reilly announced that [Goliath and other coasters with a similar lap bar will get seat belts](/news/six-flags-seat-belts-goliath). According to his letter, eight more rides at five other parks are closed as a precaution. Reilly doesn't say which parks and rides those are, and he doesn't name Great Adventure in the letter at all.
