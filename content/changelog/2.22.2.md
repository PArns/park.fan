## New

- "Avoid extreme weather" in the planner's parks step keeps theme park days off the hottest days
  where the waits allow, gives water parks the hottest ones and weighs the hurricane or typhoon
  season more. "When to travel" then ranks the hottest weeks lower. Parky sets it when you tell him
  the heat is too much.
- The plan shows the weather on top: a band across your trip with the hot days marked and the storm
  season under it. Each day card shows the day's typical high, and a forecast only where there is
  one.
- You can delete a trip on the region hub, and undo it for a few seconds.
- The homepage search lists the four trip regions under the parks near you, and the regions sit in
  one section right under the news.

## Improved

- The ticket card is about half as long. It compares two packages, the tickets for this plan and
  the 14-day tickets from UK shops, with one button per ticket and the official shops under each.
  Each resort's details fold into one line.
- The week view names the early entry for hotel guests on its block, labels the minutes as the
  day's peak wait, shows each park's hours and has a key under it.
- In the region hubs' week calendar each park marks its own quietest day of the week, and every
  chapter stays open.
- Parky's bar in the planners speaks for him in a bubble and lists the questions he will ask.
- The menu reads Guides, News, Planen, Parks, favourites and "More", and "More" no longer repeats
  the regions.
- Headings and the line under them no longer end with a full stop.

## Fixed

- Parky's body was missing from the trip assistant's header after the Plan menu had been opened.
- The hotel select in the hub's day sheet had no visible arrow.
- A trip region's page could show data from before the last update for up to a day.
- The trip assistant's last screen showed a diagram with cut-off lettering in place of a Magic
  Kingdom photo. Its photo stack now shows photos only, and a photo that fails to load leaves the
  print blank.
- The evening step named the beach at Disney's Polynesian Village Resort in English in every
  language, and the hotel step named Southern California's hotel areas in English.
