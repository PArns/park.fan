On 18 November Disneyland in Anaheim announced the dates for Disneyland After Dark 2027 on the Disney Parks Blog. Four separately ticketed parties run at [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park) between January and June. Sweethearts' Nite, Star Wars Nite and Pride Nite are back, and Disneylandia Nite joins them in April. Sweethearts' Nite goes on sale first, to everyone from 10 December.

## The nights

- Sweethearts' Nite: 24, 26, 28 and 31 January, 2, 4, 7, 9, 11 and 16 February
- Disneylandia Nite: 11, 13 and 15 April
- Star Wars Nite: 27 and 29 April, 4 and 6 May
- Pride Nite: 17 June

All 18 nights fall on a Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday. Valentine's Day isn't one of them, even though 14 February 2027 is a Sunday. If you want to take your sweetheart to the park that day, you'll need a regular day ticket, or you move Valentine's to Tuesday the 16th, the last Sweethearts' Nite.

## Hours and what the ticket includes

Each party runs from 9 p.m. to 1 a.m. local time, after the park has closed to day guests. A party ticket gets you in from 6 p.m., though, seven hours before the end, and until 9 you share the park with the day crowd.

The ticket covers every digital Disney PhotoPass photo taken of you during the party, with no cap. You collect them by scanning the QR code on your wristband in the Disneyland app. You also get a party map of your own, more scrapbook than navigation. Only selected attractions are open, and Disney often promises shorter waits on them. Disney hasn't announced which rides will be open in 2027 or what a ticket costs.

A ticket is valid only on the night you book, and because Disney sells a limited number for each night, be on Disneyland.com when sales open at 9 a.m. Pacific on 10 December.

## Tickets for Sweethearts' Nite

Tickets are sold through Disneyland.com. On Tuesday, 8 December, from 9 a.m. California time, Inspire Key holders can buy from a small allocation. A day later it's the turn of all Magic Key holders, again from a limited allocation. General sale opens on Thursday, 10 December, at 9 a.m. Pacific, which is 6 p.m. CET. Disney plans to announce on-sale dates and details for Disneylandia Nite and Star Wars Nite in early 2027, and for Pride Nite in the spring.

## Sweethearts' Nite

Disney has couples in mind, but says just as clearly that a best friend or the family counts too. Back again are the "Celebrate Love Cavalcade", which brings Mickey, Minnie and their friends down Main Street, U.S.A., and "Once Upon a Dream" in front of Sleeping Beauty Castle, where singers perform love songs from Disney films live. Afterwards you can meet royal couples there.

If you'd rather sing yourself, the Golden Horseshoe in Frontierland runs "Disney Duets", a karaoke for two. A DJ plays the Valentine's Dance Party at Tomorrowland Terrace, with Disney characters on the dance floor. For something slower, board the [Mark Twain](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/mark-twain-riverboat?bare), which becomes the Lou-Ray Jazz Club and cruises the Rivers of America in the dark with jazz on board. There are photo backdrops for four films, "Lady and the Tramp" among them, and WALL-E and EVE, the night's robot couple, light up the front of [Space Mountain](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/space-mountain?bare).

Disney sells the party merchandise in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge of all places, next to the [Millennium Falcon](ref:/parks/north-america/united-states/anaheim/disneyland-park/millennium-falcon-smugglers-run?bare), and one of the pieces is a headband with a Minnie bow.

Among the new snacks are fries with chicken and za'atar at Galactic Grill and elsewhere, and fried Hawaiian rolls at Little Red Wagon. Blue Bayou, River Belle Terrace and Carnation Café serve their own party dishes. Disney recommends booking those, with online reservations opening closer to the dates. Specialty items from the quick-service spots can be ordered through mobile order in the app before the party starts.

## Disneylandia Nite, Star Wars Nite and Pride Nite

Disneylandia Nite celebrates Hispanic and Latin American cultures, with characters from "Coco" and "Encanto". It takes almost the same April dates as Disney Channel Nite did in 2026, which isn't on the 2027 calendar.

Star Wars Nite in 2027 marks 50 years of Star Wars, and one of its four nights falls on 4 May. Disney asks guests to come in Star Wars-style costumes, within the park's costume rules.

At Pride Nite on 17 June, rainbow projections light up the park for the LGBTQIA+ community. It has run since 2023, and 2027 has one night where 2026 had two.

## Fewer parties than in 2026

Disneyland held five After Dark parties in 2026. Besides Disney Channel Nite, "70 Years of Favorites" ran for the first time in March, on two nights, and it's missing from the 2027 calendar too.

If you're planning a trip around Southern California, the [California itinerary guide](/blog/california-itinerary-disneyland-universal-san-diego) puts the party nights at Disneyland, Universal, Knott's and SeaWorld San Diego side by side, with the time day guests have to leave at each.
