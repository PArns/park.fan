[Efteling](ref:efteling) published the programme for Winter Efteling 2026/27 on 8
October 2026. It runs from Monday, 16 November 2026, to 31 January 2027. New are a
show in a mirror tent, a caterpillar coaster, a partly covered ice rink and, between
Christmas and New Year, an evening ticket for 33 euros.

## The evening ticket is valid from 4 pm

The evening ticket is on sale for 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. It's valid from
4 pm until closing and costs 33 euros. It isn't valid on 31 December.

Anyone driving pays 10 euros for parking if they buy the parking ticket together
with the evening ticket. Otherwise parking costs 15 euros a day, according to the
park's parking page. The announcement puts day tickets at 40 to 56 euros and doesn't
say which price applies on which day. The evening ticket is 7 euros cheaper than the
cheapest day ticket and up to 23 euros cheaper than the dearest.

Over the Christmas holidays the park stays open until 8 pm, except on 31 December.
Arriving at 4 pm leaves four hours. On days with an earlier closing time, there's
less. The car park can be entered until an hour and a half before closing, so until
6:30 pm at an 8 pm close. Otherwise the park opens from 11 am to 6 pm at the least,
often from 10 am to 7 pm. Tickets for visits up to 31 December are on sale. The dates
from January follow later.

## New Year's Eve is separate

Efteling is holding its own Oud & Nieuw on 31 December. Tickets aren't on sale yet;
the park's page lets you sign up by email to be notified. The accommodations at
Efteling are fully booked for that night.

Guests staying overnight get unlimited access to Winter Efteling. The park also
offers Christmas packages with breakfast or dinner.

## Cirque Miragio and Cirque Papillon

The new show Cirque Miragio runs on the Warme Winter Weide. It's on daily, lasts 20
minutes and is a musical acrobatics show in a nostalgic mirror tent with seating. It
tells what happens to the wicked queen after the Snow White fairy tale. The park says
nothing about showtimes.

The caterpillar coaster Cirque Papillon is new next to it. The announcement gives no
age limit or minimum height.

The ice rink is called De Gladde Piste. It's new and partly covered, and the skating
tent has seating. The park hasn't published what skating costs.

## The rest of the winter village

The Warme Winter Weide has been renewed in other ways, too. The Winterdorp shows new
and earlier Luville miniatures. For photos there's a sleigh with Pardijn, several
chalets run prize games, and there are winter food specials that are only on offer
during this period, plus souvenirs. At the fire pit you can see the nine-metre-high
Wonder Winterboom.

Pardoes greets guests in a new covered pavilion in the Hartenhof area, on the square
in front of Symbolica. The Nikkelen Nelissen from Symbolica join him. The Carrousel
Theater shows "De Man met de Toverlantaarn" until 3 January 2027, a musical with
singing, puppets and visual effects. On the Carnaval Festivalplein, Jokie & Jet
perform their winter show, and on the Max & Moritzplein there's the Anderberg
Liederfestival with Frau Schmetterling and Bakker Krümel. The Sprookjesbos adds a
winter walk, snow-covered mushrooms for photos and the Sprookjesboom winter show in
the open-air theatre.

On the Anton Pieckplein, the Anton Pieck Molen runs, along with the Carolling
Carousel trio and the game "Uyt den Losse Pols".

## What the park hasn't said yet

Still open are the ice rink's price, the age limits for the caterpillar coaster, the
showtimes for the show and the theatre, and when Oud & Nieuw tickets go on sale. That
Droomvlucht and Joris en de Draak are in maintenance over the same period is in the
[winter overview](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026). Why the park has so much room in
winter is explained in the
[Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).
