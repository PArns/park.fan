With the Black Deal, a Wintertraum day ticket for [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) costs €29 from Monday to Friday and €33 at weekends and in the Christmas holidays, on every day the park is open in December and January. The rest of November isn't included. The park's newsletter with the offer reached me this morning, and the ticket shop's calendar already shows the new prices. The park gives no end date for the offer; the shop says prices can go up at any time, and each date has a limited number of tickets.

Wintertraum itself runs until 24 January 2027, daily from 11 am to 8 pm. The day ends with the MAGIC SYMPHONY fireworks over Berlin, which are called off for safety reasons in bad weather.

## What each day costs

Adult prices (age 12 and up) in the ticket calendar, as of 21 November 2026:

| Days                                             | Adult day ticket |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| December, Monday to Friday, up to 22 Dec         | €29              |
| December, Saturday and Sunday                    | €33              |
| Christmas holidays, 23 Dec and 26 Dec to 5 Jan   | €33              |
| January from 6 Jan, Monday to Friday             | €29              |
| January, Saturday and Sunday                     | €33              |
| Saturday, 12 December                            | sold out         |
| 24 and 25 December, 1 January, 12 and 13 January | closed           |

The Christmas school holidays in North Rhine-Westphalia don't end until 6 January, but that Wednesday is already €29. From 25 January the park is closed.

## November stays more expensive

The Black Deal doesn't cover the November dates. Today, a Saturday, costs €69, more than two December Saturdays put together. Totensonntag, 22 November, is €45, and the days up to the end of the month cost between €35 and €42.

## What the Black Deal saves compared with 15 November

On 15 November the calendar showed different prices. Sunday, 27 December, was €42 then and is €33 now. New Year's Eve was €37 and Saturday, 9 January, €38; both are now €33. Weekdays barely change: Wednesday, 2 December, was €30 and is now €29.

## How to buy your ticket

Tickets are sold online only, since Phantasialand has no ticket office, and each one is fixed to the day you pick in the calendar. For the same day, the online shop sells them until 4 pm, at whatever that day's price is and only while it isn't sold out. So if the car is already on the A553 at a quarter to four and it turns out nobody bought tickets, you're still in time. At the gate you show the ticket on your phone; you don't need to print anything.

You can now also [buy the Quick Pass online](/news/phantasialand-quick-pass-online), from 11 am on the day of your visit, without queuing at Guest Services. It costs extra and only works with a day ticket.

Children aged 4 to 11 pay a lower price of their own, which the calendar only shows once you click on a day. Children up to three get in free with an ID card or birth certificate. Children up to eleven also get in free on their birthday if you book the visit in advance.

## When it's less crowded

At last year's Wintertraum, queues in the Christmas holidays were as long as in the August summer holidays, and the January weekends were very busy. Weekdays after the holidays were quiet, and those are exactly the days that now cost €29. For the cheapest day with short queues, I'd go for a weekday between 14 and 22 January. If it rains, [Winja’s Fear](ref:phantasialand/winjas-fear) and Winja’s Force keep running inside the Wuze Town building.
