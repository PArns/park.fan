A Disney Christmas starts at [Hong Kong Disneyland](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park) on November 17, 2026, and ends on January 3, 2027. New this year are a winter market in World of Frozen, in Arendelle, with treats and handmade goods, and a countdown performance by Mickey and friends on New Year's Eve. The Christmas concert in front of the castle is back for four evenings in November and December. This time the Hong Kong pianists Joyce Cheung and Oscar Tao play, and more than a hundred kids from the Hong Kong Children's Choir sing.

## Christmas in World of Frozen

In World of Frozen, Olaf talks about what he loves about Christmas, and Anna walks through the village. In Kristoff's Sven-tastic Surprise, Kristoff carves an ice sculpture for his reindeer Sven, and guests can lend a hand. Whether Sven would rather have a carrot is Kristoff's problem. Snow keeps falling in Arendelle after Christmas, and the market stays. In the same land, [Frozen Ever After](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/frozen-ever-after-presented-by-blue-cross) takes boats through the village and the forest up to the North Mountain.

## Main Street and the tree

On Main Street, U.S.A., Santa Goofy switches on the big Christmas tree, snow falls and drones fly a choreography above it. Carolers sing under the lights. The tree lighting ran in 2025 too, the park's 20th-anniversary year, as "A Holiday Wish-Come-True" and with projections added.

In the park you can also meet Mickey and friends, Duffy and friends, the Toy Story characters and Winnie the Pooh, all in their Christmas outfits.

## The concert in front of the castle

"Disney Christmas Live in Concert" plays in the evening in front of the Castle of Magical Dreams, and Mickey brings Christmas Goofy and other Disney characters along. Joyce Cheung is at the piano on November 21 and 22, Oscar Tao on December 5 and 6.

Until December 6, the park sells seating passes for the zones in front of the stage:

- Standard: HK$299
- VIP 2 with a waffle: HK$369
- VIP 3 with a soft-serve ice cream: HK$369
- VIP 1 with a combo meal at Golden Crocus Inn: HK$479

The VIP zones are right by the stage. A pass is good only on the day you picked and for one person, and park admission isn't included. Inside a zone you take any free seat, so whoever gets there first sits at the front. The park asks you to be at the entrance to the seating area 30 minutes before the start.

## New Year's Eve in the park

On December 31, the nighttime show Momentous plays at 9 pm. After it, a DJ takes over at the castle, Goofy and Max join the party, and for the countdown Mickey and friends put on a new performance.

For that one night there's the New Year's Eve Edition of Disney Premier Access, from HK$659 and again without park admission. The pass covers the show Mickey and the Wondrous Book, a viewing area each for the daytime show on the castle stage, for Momentous and for the countdown, and eight attractions:

- [Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/big-grizzly-mountain-runaway-mine-cars)
- [Ant-Man and The Wasp: Nano Battle](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/ant-man-and-the-wasp-nano-battle)
- [Mystic Manor](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/mystic-manor)
- [Iron Man Experience](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/iron-man-experience-presented-by-aia)
- "it's a small world"
- Frozen Ever After
- [Hyperspace Mountain](ref:hong-kong-disneyland-park/hyperspace-mountain)
- Mickey's PhilharMagic

The Explorer's Club Restaurant, which serves a dinner buffet over Christmas, also has a package that includes a spot with a view of Momentous.

## Tickets and getting there

Along with your ticket, you need a park reservation for the exact day of each visit. If you want two days, the Non-stop 2-Day Fun Ticket costs HK$100 more than a regular one-day ticket. Adults pay from HK$769, kids aged 3 to 11 and seniors 60 and over from HK$599. It's on sale until December 31, 2026.

On the MTR, you ride to Sunny Bay and change there to the Disneyland Resort Line, which runs straight to the resort. That gets you to the park in about 30 minutes from the airport, from Kowloon MTR station or from Hong Kong Station.
