[Energylandia](ref:energylandia) in Zator opens its Winter Kingdom on Saturday 28 November. Until 31 January 2027 the park is open on 28 days, always from 11:30am to 6:30pm. The programme runs from a light garden and an ice rink to snow tubing, a Christmas market, Santa and elf shows. Coasters run too, but only on a dry track.

## Which days the park is open

- in November on Saturday and Sunday, the 28th and 29th
- in December from the 4th to the 6th and the 11th to the 13th (Friday to Sunday), on the 19th and 20th, and from the 26th to the 30th (Saturday to Wednesday)
- in January from the 2nd to the 6th (Saturday to Wednesday), then on the weekends of the 9th and 10th, 16th and 17th, 23rd and 24th, and 30th and 31st

The gates stay shut from 21 to 25 December, on New Year's Eve and on New Year's Day. Last winter, Winter Kingdom ran from 29 November 2025 to 8 February 2026, so this time it ends just over a week earlier.

## What's on

The path through the light garden goes past 5 million lights and through light tunnels. At the end of the path stands a Christmas tree almost 17 metres tall, with glowing sleighs, snowmen and oversized baubles in front of it. It's lit for the first time on Saturday at 4pm, in a ceremony at the main entrance. The park says the lights are on all day, and you don't need an extra ticket for the garden.

The ice rink has a small and a large surface. Everyone has to wear a helmet and protective gloves, and children aged three to seven must be accompanied on the ice. On opening Saturday, DJs from the Energy 2000 club run a skating disco there from 1pm to 3pm. For snow tubing there are two hills, one for adults and one for kids.

New this year is Arctic Loop, a ride with rubber rings that turn slowly on the ground. It's open to children aged two to 13 and between 100 and 200 centimetres tall, and only with an adult. There's also a reindeer enclosure where, according to Energylandia, the reindeer can talk. From 12:30pm to 5:30pm you can decorate gingerbread and have your photo taken with Santa at the Lancelot Shop. Letters to him go in the second workshop, which stays open all day, so you can still write one after he clocks off at 5:30pm.

At 11:20am Santa, the elves and the mascots welcome the first guests outside the gate. In Teatr Egypt, elves put on two 15-minute shows, "Następny przystanek: Święta!" (Next stop: Christmas) and "Fabryka Elfów" (The elf factory). One is about Santa's trip to Lapland, which looks like it's about to go wrong, the other about elves who have to get all the presents finished in time. Elf René does magic five times a day in the Town Hall Theatre in "Elfastycznie!" (roughly: Elftastic). The day ends at 6:15pm with the "Santa Express" parade, led by Santa's train, in the Formuła area.

## Which rides are running

More than 30 attractions are open, from roller coasters to part of Bajkolandia for small children. The park's blog post about last winter promotes rides on [Hyperion](ref:energylandia/pepsi-hyperion) and [Zadra](ref:energylandia/zadra-made-in-malopolska) in a wintry setting. A coaster only runs when the track is dry, though, with no ice or snow on it. You'll see on the [park page](ref:energylandia) each morning whether Hyperion is running that day. After a night of frost, check again just before you leave.

## What winter costs

Energylandia sells tickets in euros and in złoty, with separate price lists in two shops. In the euro shop at tickets.energylandia.pl, a day at Winter Kingdom costs €38, or €33 reduced. The reduced price is for children under 140 centimetres, seniors from 65 and people with disabilities. From 3pm there's an afternoon ticket for €21, or €19 reduced. You don't need to book a date: the ticket is valid on any opening day until 31 January. It covers every attraction and show that's open that day, the ice rink included.

In the Polish shop at ticket.energylandia.pl, the winter ticket normally costs 149 zł, and during Black Weeks, until Sunday 29 November, only 99 zł. The offer is online only, with no more than ten tickets in all. These tickets only become valid on 4 December, though, so on the opening weekend you pay full price. The afternoon ticket isn't part of the offer. [Other parks](/news/black-friday-theme-parks-2026) have Black Friday tickets too.

Parking in front of the entrance costs 30 zł a day. For clothing, the park recommends layers, ski wear and even ski goggles with an elastic strap.

## By train from Kraków or Oświęcim

From 28 November to 12 December, a train runs to Zator Park Rozrywki station for Winter Kingdom. A little road train picks you up there and takes you back in the evening:

- from Kraków Główny at 9:18am, road train at 10:25am, back to the station at 6:20pm, train to Kraków at 7:03pm
- from Oświęcim at 10:27am, road train at 10:47am, back to the station at 6:45pm, train to Oświęcim at 7:27pm

So if you're heading back to Kraków, you'll catch the 6:15pm parade from behind at best.

## What happens in 2027

The 2027 summer season starts on 3 April. With the new Volana area, Energylandia is getting the [Rokan wing coaster](/news/energylandia-rokan-wing-coaster), though the park hasn't named a date yet. The rides that run only in summer are in our [Energylandia tips](/blog/energylandia-wait-times-tips).
