We measured every day from 24 to 31 December 2025, in Orlando and in Paris. In Orlando that week was busier than any other month of the year in **all seven
parks**: [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park), [EPCOT](ref:epcot),
[Hollywood Studios](ref:disneys-hollywood-studios),
[Animal Kingdom](ref:disneys-animal-kingdom-theme-park),
[Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida),
[Islands of Adventure](ref:universal-islands-of-adventure) and
[Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe).

In Paris the same week sat on the yearly average at
[Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), and at
[Disney Adventure World](ref:disney-adventure-world) it was **quieter than any
other month of the year**.

> **About the dates:** dates,
> times and prices come from the parks' official pages, linked in the sources below. Parks
> move evening dates around well into November.

## Walt Disney World from 13 November to 6 January

Disney runs its Orlando Christmas season from **13 November 2026 to 6 January
2027**. Two of the four parks add a separately ticketed evening event, and both
of them start before the season officially does: Very Merry Christmas Party on
8 November, Jollywood Nights on the 7th. The earliest party nights are reliably the cheapest ones.

### Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party on 25 nights

The Magic Kingdom party runs on **25 nights between 8 November and 22
December**, not nightly:

- November: 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27 and 29
- December: 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 22

It's a five-hour event, 7 p.m. to midnight, and the ticket gets you into the
park **from 4 p.m.** You don't need a separate day ticket and you don't need a
park reservation. Tickets run **$189 to $229 plus tax** depending on the date,
with $10 off for children aged three to nine. Annual Passholders and Disney
Vacation Club members save another $10, but only on selected nights from 8 to 20
November and from 29 November to 3 December.

The ticket includes Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade, the Minnie's Wonderful
Christmastime fireworks, the Mickey's Most Merriest Celebration stage show at
Cinderella Castle, snowfall on Main Street, and cookies and hot drinks at
set locations around the park at no extra charge. Five attractions get a holiday
version, among them [Space Mountain](ref:magic-kingdom-park/space-mountain) with
different music and different interior lighting, the Tomorrowland Speedway and
the Mad Tea Party, where spinning your teacup lands you on one of four holiday
combinations of lights and music. Monsters Inc. Laugh Floor plays a seasonal
version of its show. Jungle Cruise runs as Jingle Cruise all December, during the
day as well, no party ticket needed.

Only on party nights, the programme also has Frozen Holiday Surprise at
Cinderella Castle with Elsa, Anna, Olaf, Kristoff and the Snowgies, Santa's
Christmas Carnival in Storybook Circus with Santa, and the Club Tinsel
dance party on the Rockettower Plaza Stage in Tomorrowland. Two dessert buffets
cost extra and need a party ticket for the same night. At one you sit for the
fireworks on the terrace of Tomorrowland Terrace; at the other, Cast Members walk
you to a standing area in the East Plaza Garden in front of the castle. The party menu brings back, among others, the Carne Asada Loaded Fries at Pecos Bill Tall Tale Inn & Cafe.

The parade, the fireworks and the castle stage show are cancelled in wind and
storms, and Disney notes this under each of the three. In December that's rare
in Florida.

The party has existed since 1983. From 2002 to 2015 the castle stage had the show
Celebrate the Season, from 2003 to 2019 the fireworks were Holiday Wishes, and
from 2007 to 2019 about 200,000 LED lights hung on Cinderella Castle. Jingle
Cruise arrived in 2013. The party was not held in 2020 or 2021; in 2021 Disney
sold a Very Merry After Hours in its place. It has run again since 2022, and
Frozen Holiday Surprise since 7 November 2023.

### Festival of the Holidays and Candlelight Processional at EPCOT

The **EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays** runs from **27 November to
30 December** and costs nothing extra. Storytellers around World Showcase tell the Christmas customs of their pavilion: Las Posadas with Mariachi Cobre in Mexico, a
mischievous Barn Santa in Norway, Micha's Adventskalender puppet theatre in
Germany, La Befana in Italy, the story of the Daruma doll in Japan, a Hanukkah
storyteller between Morocco and France, Père Noël in France, Father Christmas in
the United Kingdom, and the Canadian Holiday Voyageurs with customs from the
regions of Canada. China uses the same slot for Lunar New Year, with a lion
dancer and a Si-Zhu trio.

On the CommuniCore Plaza Stage, JOYFUL! A Celebration of the Season plays daily
from 27 November to 30 December, a show of gospel, R&B and songs for Christmas
and Kwanzaa. You meet Santa in CommuniCore Hall until 24 December, and Goofy in
his red Santa suit in The Odyssey building.

The festival was called Holidays Around the World from 1996 to 2016 and has had
its current name since 2017.

The **Candlelight Processional** runs in the same window, but **every evening**,
three times a night, at 4:45, 6:30 and 8 p.m. at the America Gardens Theatre. A
celebrity narrator reads the Christmas story, backed by a massed choir, the
Voices of Liberty and a 50-piece orchestra. For 27 and 28 November the narrator
schedule still reads "To Be Announced". After that come Gary Sinise from 29
November to 1 December, then Benjamin Bratt, Whoopi Goldberg, Robin Roberts,
Jason Kelce, Neil Patrick Harris, Josh Groban, Lauren Daigle, Ralph Macchio,
Henry Winkler, Lacey Chabert, Jodi Benson, Simu Liu, and Eva Longoria closing
from 28 to 30 December. Bratt, Roberts, Kelce, Groban and Chabert narrate for the
first time in 2026.

Disneyland in
California held the first procession in 1958. Walt Disney World brought it to
the Magic Kingdom in 1971, the year it opened, with Rock Hudson as the first
narrator. Since 1994 it has been at the America Gardens Theatre at EPCOT, where
Phylicia Rashad read first. In 2020 it was cancelled at both resorts.

Admission is free, but a seat isn't guaranteed. The Candlelight Processional
Dining Package, a meal at one of the EPCOT restaurants, comes with a guaranteed
seat. [Living with the Land](ref:epcot/living-with-the-land)
runs as Glimmering Greenhouses in these weeks, with lights through the
greenhouses.

### Jollywood Nights at Hollywood Studios

**Disney Jollywood Nights** is the second ticketed event, and it runs longer
than the Magic Kingdom party, into the new year. Fifteen nights from **7
November 2026 to 5 January 2027**:

- November: 7, 14, 16, 21, 23 and 28
- December: 5, 7, 12, 14, 19, 21 and 23
- January: 3 and 5

Five hours again, 7:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m., with entry from 5:30 p.m. Tickets are
**$169 to $199 plus tax**, $10 off for children aged three to nine, and the
Passholder and DVC discount is valid on **all** nights here rather than selected ones.

Kermit and Miss Piggy host the show Disney Holidays in Hollywood at Theater of the Stars, with Tiana, Belle, Minnie and Mickey. Hyperion Theater has a sing-along to Tim Burton's The Nightmare Before Christmas, and in Glisten! figure skaters perform to holiday songs and original Jollywood songs. The characters of the night include Baloo and King Louie from TaleSpin, and Phineas and Ferb. There is live music in the courtyard of the Hollywood Tower Hotel at the Twilight Soirée at the Tip Top Club. New in 2026 are the Checkin' It Twice Lounge in Animation Courtyard, with a DJ and its own drinks, and, for the first time, George Sanderson and Needleman from Monsters, Inc.

Before Jollywood Nights, Hollywood Studios had The Osborne Family Spectacle of
Dancing Lights from 1995 to 6 January 2016. Jennings Osborne had started it in
1986 with 1,000 lights on his house in Little Rock, Arkansas; by 1993 there were
more than three million. Disney first set it up on Residential Street and from
2004 on the Streets of America, in the end with more than five million lights.
Disney announced its end in September 2015. Most of the lights went to Give Kids the World
Village in Kissimmee. Jollywood Nights has run since 2023.

Without an extra ticket, Sunset Seasons Greetings projects onto the Hollywood
Tower Hotel every evening, Toy
Story Land gets oversized decorations, and the Frozen sing-along show gets a
holiday finale with Olaf.

### Animal Kingdom and Disney Springs

Animal Kingdom has no evening event and the smallest programme of the four
parks. On Discovery Island the **Merry Menagerie** sets out hand-built puppets
of arctic animals, penguins, reindeer, foxes and polar bears. The Tree of Life
gets a holiday edition of its Awakenings each evening. There's a Christmas tree
of a little over 16 metres at the entrance, and the village of Harambe is hung
with garlands made from recycled materials and traditional African textiles.
Mickey and Minnie meet guests in holiday outfits at Adventurers Outpost.

Disney Springs runs the **Christmas Tree Stroll**, a walk past elaborately
decorated trees, plus Santa for photos. Neither costs admission.

## Universal Orlando from 14 November to 3 January

Universal pulled its season forward by a week this year and celebrates from **14
November 2026 to 3 January 2027**. That's 51 days, six more than in 2025. Unlike Disney, Grinchmas, the Wizarding World, the parade and the holiday
decorations at Epic Universe need no extra ticket; they're included with regular
park admission.

**Grinchmas** is where it always is, in Seuss Landing at Islands of Adventure,
with the "Grinchmas Who-liday Spectacular" stage show after the Dr. Seuss book,
the Grinch himself for photos, and the Who-Ville Tree Lot.

**Christmas in The Wizarding World of Harry Potter** runs across all three parks
at once for the first time: Hogsmeade at Islands of Adventure, Diagon Alley at
Universal Studios Florida, and the Ministry of Magic at Epic Universe. Hogsmeade
also gets "The Magic of Christmas at Hogwarts Castle" in the evenings, a
projection show onto the castle.

At Universal Studios Florida, **Universal's Holiday Parade featuring Macy's**
goes down the street every evening, with the oversized balloons from the New
York Thanksgiving parade and floats for Despicable Me, Shrek and Madagascar.
Santa lights the Christmas tree at the end of it.

At **Epic Universe**, in the park's second winter, SUPER NINTENDO
WORLD and How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk get holiday decorations for
the first time, and Celestial Park returns as a winter wonderland with lit trees
and holiday fountain displays at the Cosmos Fountain. Universal calls the Isle
of Berk version **Snoggletog**, the winter feast from the Dragons films.

You can also book the **Universal Holiday Tour**, two and a half hours
guided, with a meet-and-greet with the Grinch and his dog Max, reserved seating
for the Grinchmas show, a meeting with Santa, and optionally a reserved viewing
area for the parade. It needs a park-to-park ticket for the Studios and the
Islands, and it runs on selected dates from 14 November 2026 to 2 January 2027.

At CityWalk, the Red Coconut Club turns into the Green and Red Coconut Club again
for the season, with live music, seasonal cocktails and holiday decorations.

### Christmas at Universal since 2000

Grinchmas started in December 2000 in Seuss Landing at Islands of Adventure (opened 28 May 1999). The Grinch was played in that first
year by Mike Aiello, who now works in Universal's creative development team. The
show was then three acts at different times of day, performed outside the shop
All the Books You Can Read. In 2002 Universal combined the three into one show of
15 to 20 minutes.

The Macy's parade has run through Universal Studios Florida (opened 7 June 1990) since 2002. Until 2017 it was called Macy's Holiday Parade; then
it got its current name and new holiday floats and balloons.

In the same year, from 18 November 2017, the Wizarding World celebrated
Christmas for the first time (Hogsmeade had opened on 18 June 2010). The projection show on the castle then lasted about eight minutes and ran roughly every 20 minutes after sunset. Spectrum News 13 reported in 2017 that
work on it had taken a year and a half.

## Disneyland Paris from 7 November to 6 January

**Disney Enchanted Christmas** runs from
**7 November 2026 to 6 January 2027** across both parks.

At Disneyland Park, **Mickey's Dazzling Christmas Parade** goes round twice a
day, the Christmas tree on Town Square is **24 metres** tall and gets lit
several times a day, and the stage carries the "Let's Sing Christmas!"
sing-along with Mickey, Minnie, Donald, Daisy, Goofy and Clarabelle. Characters
stand in three places: Mickey and friends on Town Square, the princesses on
Central Plaza, Aurora and Prince Phillip in the castle courtyard. For the photo
with Santa you need a reservation through the virtual queue.

New this winter is Christmas in **World of Frozen**, the land Disney Adventure
World opened in 2026. Arendelle holds its first Christmas there, with garlands,
advent wreaths and the Arendelle Carollers, a Christmas choir. With children who know
[Frozen Ever After](ref:disney-adventure-world/frozen-ever-after), I wouldn't
plan Disney Adventure World as just a half day.

The parade is included with the park ticket. Disney sells a reserved spot for it
separately, while they last.

### Christmas in Paris since 1992

Disneyland Park opened on 12 April 1992 as Euro Disneyland, and in its first winter, from December 1992, it ran the show Le Noël de Mickey. That stayed
in the programme until January 2006. From 1998 to March 2012, Mickey et la Magie
de l'hiver played every winter. The tree-lighting ceremony on Town Square existed
in the 1990s and again since 2009. The evening show Disney Dreams! ran in a
Christmas version, for example in the season from 10 November 2013 to 6 January 2014.

In winter 2016/17 the season ran from 11 November to 8 January. The parade then had two extra floats, and that tree was already 24 metres
tall. In 2020 Christmas did not happen, because the park was closed from 30 October 2020 to 17 June 2021. Today's parade, in French Mickey et sa Parade
Étincelante de Noël, has run since 13 November 2021 with five illuminated floats.
For the 2023/24 season the City of Paris counted nearly 100 performers on stage
and backstage. Disney Adventure World was called Walt Disney Studios from 2002 to 2026.

## Hotels over Christmas

The gingerbread house in the Grand Floridian lobby, which dates back to 1999, is not there in 2026. Disney says the lobby needs
to stay clear for hotel guests and is putting smaller gingerbread displays around
the hotel instead. For rooms in the Disney Resorts Collection, Disney gives up to
20 per cent off on selected nights from 4 October to 24 December 2026. Children
aged three to nine eat free in 2026 when you book a package with a room at one
of these hotels and a dining plan for everyone aged ten and over.

Universal decorates each of its eleven hotels to its own theme, with menorah
lighting ceremonies, Christmas tree lightings and strolling carollers. Hotel
guests get into the parks earlier with Early Park Admission. US residents save up
to 30 per cent on five nights or more at participating hotels, and up to $300
more with park tickets in the package. Florida residents get the 2-Park
Unlimited Days Ticket for $199 plus tax, valid until 16 December.

In Paris, Christmas gift sets can be added to a stay at the participating Disney hotels. The Disneyland Hotel, five stars, stands at the entrance to Disneyland Park and is decorated for Christmas during the season, with a seasonal offer in its restaurants.

## New Year's Eve

**Disneyland Paris** has published its programme and splits the night between
the two parks. Disney Adventure World gets a fireworks and drone show built
around Frozen, weather permitting. Disneyland Park gets DJs, the characters, and
its own fireworks over Sleeping Beauty Castle at midnight. You pick which park
you'll be standing in when you buy the ticket.

**Universal Orlando** lists New Year's Eve across all three parks and CityWalk,
with DJs, live entertainment and a midnight countdown. Universal hasn't given dates, times or prices yet.

**Walt Disney World** has published no New Year's Eve programme on its Christmas
page. It may still come; Disney often publishes New Year's Eve in November.

## What I'd book in your place

November already gets you the full decoration. The week between Christmas and New
Year gets you the same programme at the highest waits of the year.

If you have to travel between Christmas and New Year, go to Paris.

If you want one ticketed evening in Orlando and you're watching the price,
take Jollywood Nights. It's cheaper than the Very Merry Christmas Party on
every date, the Passholder discount applies on all nights instead of selected
ones, and it runs to 5 January.

## Frequently asked questions

### Since when has Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party existed?

Since 1983. It was not held in 2020 or 2021 and has run again since 2022. In
2026 it takes place on 25 nights between 8 November and 22 December, with
tickets at $189 to $229 plus tax.

### Do you need an extra ticket for the Candlelight Processional?

No, it's included with EPCOT admission and runs three times a night from 27
November to 30 December at the America Gardens Theatre. A guaranteed seat comes
only with the Candlelight Processional Dining Package.

### Does Christmas at Universal Orlando cost extra?

Grinchmas, the Wizarding World, the Macy's parade and the decorations at Epic
Universe are included with regular park admission. Only the Universal Holiday
Tour costs extra.

### When is Christmas at Disneyland Paris in 2026?

From 7 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, at Disneyland Park and in World of Frozen
at Disney Adventure World. The parade, the tree and the shows are included with
the park ticket.
