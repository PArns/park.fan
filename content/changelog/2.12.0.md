## New

- 119 parks with enough measured days (as of 21 September) have a statistics page of their own:
  crowds by month and by weekday over two years, the rides ranked by typical wait, and the typical
  day hour by hour, with a note on how many opening days the numbers come from.
- The info card on a park page links to Google Maps and Apple Maps. Both links are built from the
  park's coordinates, so a park with no curated facts has a route to its gate as well: 19 of a
  40-park sample had none and showed no card before.
- This changelog. Until 21 September the record of what changed was a file in the repository, and
  nobody outside it read that file.

## Improved

- The live crowd badge in a park's panel opens the whole scale: all six levels with the
  percentages behind them, the current one highlighted.
- A ride closed for a refurbishment carries a badge that says so, beside its season badge, on the
  card and on its ride page. Before, a headliner behind hoardings until March looked like a ride
  having a bad morning.
- Efteling's steam train station carries a transport badge, so a queue for the next departure is
  not read as a queue for a popular ride.
- In the planner, the overview counts down to the next planned day, and a ride you tick off shows
  how the measured wait compared with the forecast.
- Eleven fixes to the planner on phones: the sheet rests flush under the header, and delete sits on
  the selected block's own card (20 September).
- Photos that are not our own carry a credit badge in their corner.
