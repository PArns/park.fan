## New

- A calendar of past waits on every ride page, seven columns on a computer and two on a phone,
  one tile per day with its lowest and highest wait (9 January).
- Its legend matches the park calendar's from 10 January: public holiday, bridge day, school
  holiday, park closed. Days the park was closed say so.
- The wait card on a ride page draws the day's queue as a line behind the number, with an arrow
  for the trend (11 January).
- Rides at Toverland, Bobbejaanland, Phantasialand and Walibi Belgium show a photo on their card,
  and the homepage photo started rotating through them.

## Improved

- Park, ride and show cards use one set of status badges.
