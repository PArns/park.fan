## New

- The trip planner is an assistant. Parky asks one question per screen: your dates, your parks,
  your hotel or route, how packed the days should be, your evenings and your tickets. Only the dates
  are needed. Every other step can be skipped, and from the day you leave a strip at the bottom
  already shows the plan. Answers are cards to tap instead of drop-down lists. The hotel areas
  show the walk, monorail or bus minutes to each park on a small map.
- The plan is a card per day, with the park's photo, how busy it gets, the peak wait and why the
  park is on that day. Days without a park are free days. A run of them is folded into one card
  that suggests parks to add.
- Each day card can pin the day, swap it with another day or move, free or remove the park.
  "Swap" shows the park's own minutes on every other day. Every change can be undone.
- Parky suggests up to three changes to your plan that shorten the waits, such as a park on your
  arrival day or a park hopper. One tap applies a suggestion, another opens the chat about it.
- If you already have tickets, you can start with them on the hub: "Already have tickets? Add
  them" opens the planner on its tickets step, and the tickets set your dates.
- A ride that only runs during a season, such as Phantasialand's ice rink during Wintertraum or the
  Halloween mazes at Movie Park Germany, reads "Only during Wintertraum" with the season's dates
  outside it. It no longer counts as closed.

## Improved

- The hubs for Orlando, Southern California, Japan and Paris explain the planner in four steps with
  screenshots, show the region's resorts with their parks and where to stay, and end with a call to
  plan. With a plan already made, the plan comes first.
- A park's wait-time calendar marks the seasons on the month chips and on the twelve months of the
  year ahead, so Movie Park's Halloween Horror Festival in October 2027 shows already.
- On trips of three or four days the plan no longer leaves a free day between two park days.
