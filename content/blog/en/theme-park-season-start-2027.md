Easter Sunday falls on 28 March in 2027, eight days earlier than in 2026. Theme parks build their calendars around the Easter holidays, so a lot of opening days move forward by a week. [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland) opens on 20 March instead of 28 March, [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) also on 20 March instead of 28 March, and Puy du Fou on 27 March instead of 4 April. If you spent spring 2026 waiting for the first warm days, in 2027 you’re more likely to queue at the gate in a woolly hat.

From 7 to 19 March, Europa-Park opens for its **Pre-Opening Weeks**, the first time in its history that it has opened two weeks ahead of the season, which makes it the earliest park in Germany. In France, Futuroscope near Poitiers starts its 40th-birthday season as early as 6 February. [Efteling](ref:efteling) is open every day in January and February.

Eleven of the 23 parks in this overview have a fixed date. Phantasialand, Movie Park, Toverland, Walibi Holland and Bobbejaanland haven’t released their 2027 calendars yet. Where the table has "online calendar", the park has put the date into its booking calendar without announcing it. Those same calendars are where the opening days in the park.fan crowd calendar come from.

This season’s new builds, among them the Buddenbrook-Turm at Hansa-Park and Supersonic 1887 at Nigloland, are in [New theme park attractions 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027).

## Parks open in January and February

After 24 January, only three theme parks from the table still have days in their calendars: Efteling every day, Plopsaland Deutschland with its winter season, and Futuroscope from 6 February.

### Efteling

Efteling in Kaatsheuvel says on its website that it’s open all year, and the park’s schedule lists every day up to 6 March 2027 as an opening day; it doesn’t reach any further for now. **Winter Efteling** runs until 31 January. From 4 January the park opens from 11:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 19:00 at weekends. In the quiet months it overhauls its big rides one after another, and its [maintenance list](https://www.efteling.com/en/park/information/in-maintenance) shows which one is out at the moment.

In February the roller coasters run at Efteling; Plopsaland Deutschland in Haßloch opens only its children’s areas in winter.

### Plopsaland Deutschland

[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) in Haßloch runs a winter season up to and including **14 March 2027**. Three parts of the park are open during that time: Majaland, the Plopsa Indoor hall and Die Schlümpfe Abenteuer. In January the park’s schedule lists Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, each from 11:00 to 18:00, plus every day up to 10 January. Expedition GeForce and the other big rides stay closed until the summer season, which starts on **19 March 2027**. That leaves four closed days in between, about as long as it takes to swap the Christmas decorations for Easter eggs. It also makes 19 March the earliest regular season start in Germany, three days before the Easter holidays begin in Rhineland-Palatinate.

### Phantasialand

Wintertraum at [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) runs up to and including **24 January 2027**, daily from 11:00 to 20:00. It’s closed on 1 January and on 12 and 13 January. After that, Brühl stays shut until spring, and the park hasn’t announced when it reopens. According to its schedule, the 2026 summer season began on 28 March, eight days before Easter. If the park sticks to the same pattern in 2027, the start would fall on 20 March, but that’s my arithmetic, not a date from the park.

### Rulantica

In Rust, the [Rulantica](ref:rulantica) water park next to Europa-Park stays open through January and February. Its schedule lists every day in January and February, from 9:30 to 22:00, and the Nordic Festival of Lights runs there from 11 January to 19 March. Europa-Park next door is closed during those weeks.

### Tripsdrill

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill near Cleebronn closes on 8 November. The Wildparadies wildlife park next door is open all year and puts on WinterWunderWald from **28 November 2026 to 31 January 2027**. Tripsdrill hasn’t named a date for the theme park yet. In 2026 it was open daily from 28 March.

### Futuroscope

Futuroscope near Poitiers takes a month off after 3 January and starts its season on **6 February 2027**, according to the park’s press release of 16 September 2026. For the opening, the Kinemax shows the IMAX film "ATHOS" about the Patrouille de France, and the **Nuits Magiques** light parade moves through the park on opening evenings until 27 March. Futuroscope turns 40 in 2027 ([Futuroscope at 40](/blog/futuroscope-40-years-nuits-magiques)).

A lot of Futuroscope’s attractions are films shown in indoor halls, so a February day there is less of a weather lottery than in a park full of roller coasters.

## The last winter days in January

Most winter programmes end in the first week of January. The last winter day at each park:

- **3 January**: last winter day at Walibi Belgium, Bobbejaanland, Bellewaerde, Plopsaland Belgium, Walibi Rhône-Alpes, Parc Astérix and Puy du Fou
- **5 January**: Movie Park’s Hollywood Christmas in Bottrop
- **6 January**: Disney’s Enchanted Christmas at Disneyland Paris
- **9 January**: Europa-Park for day visitors, on 10 January for hotel guests only
- **10 January**: WinterWonder at Legoland Deutschland
- **17 January**: Winter Feelings at Toverland
- **24 January**: Wintertraum at Phantasialand
- **31 January**: Winter Efteling, then regular operation

At Europa-Park, [Euro-Mir](ref:europa-park/euro-mir) runs for the last time on 9 January before it’s taken down, and at Walibi Belgium the wooden coaster [Loup-Garou](ref:walibi-belgium/loup-garou) does its final lap on 3 January to make room for a new roller coaster.

The winter programmes, the rides that stand still and the crowds between Christmas and New Year are in our overview [Theme parks in winter 2026/27](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026).

## Pre-season openings and previews

### Pre-Opening Weeks at Europa-Park

From **7 to 19 March 2027**, Europa-Park opens ahead of the season, from 9:00 to 18:00. The four-star hotels and Silver Lake City open as early as 6 March. The park itself says that not every attraction and show will run in those weeks, because it’s still getting ready for the season start. It doesn’t say which ones. On a few days the park stays closed for private events; in the online calendar those are Tuesday and Wednesday, 16 and 17 March.

The summer season proper starts on **Saturday, 20 March 2027**, eight days earlier than in 2026. In 2026 the park tried out a single Pre-Opening Day; in 2027 that grows into two weeks ([our news item on the Pre-Opening Weeks](/news/europa-park-pre-opening-weeks-2027)).

[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?full)

For March at Europa-Park we have fourteen measured days from spring 2026. March came in below April, and April level with July.

That tells you little about the Pre-Opening Weeks, because nothing like them existed in 2026. In the first two weeks of March 2027 only Hamburg has Easter holidays; every other German state starts on 22 March.

### Flying Cinema at Plopsaland Belgium

In De Panne the Flying Cinema opens on **13 March 2027**: a hall with a dome screen and gondolas floating beneath it, built by Mack Rides. Previews start as early as 19 December 2026, but only for Plopsa annual pass holders and guests of the park’s hotels and chalets. With a day ticket you won’t get in before 13 March. [Plopsaland Belgium](ref:plopsaland-belgium) hasn’t published when the rest of its 2027 season begins, and its schedule only lists days up to 3 January.

### The new hotel at Parc Astérix

Parc Astérix opens its fourth hotel, L’Odyssée, on **26 March 2027**, one day before the park’s first day ([about the hotel](/news/parc-asterix-hotel-odyssee-2027)). So the first hotel guests spend a night next to a park nobody is allowed into yet.

## Season start in Germany

### Legoland Deutschland

The online calendar for Legoland in Günzburg already runs to 31 October 2027. The first day is **20 March**, with opening hours of 10:00 to 18:00 to begin with. In 2026 the season started on 28 March. Bavaria’s Easter holidays run from 22 March to 2 April in 2027, so that first Saturday is the last one before the break.

[Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland?full)

### Hansa-Park

The online calendar of [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park) in Sierksdorf has **Thursday, 25 March 2027** as its first day, from 10:00 to 18:00, and daily from then on. In 2026 the first day was a Thursday too, 26 March. Schleswig-Holstein is the only German state whose 2027 Easter holidays don’t start until 30 March; they last until 10 April.

New for 2027 is the Buddenbrook-Turm in the Hansa-Garten, due "during the season" according to the park ([New attractions 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027)).

### Heide Park

[Heide Park](ref:heide-park) in Soltau says on its website that it’ll welcome guests back "for the Easter holidays". There’s no date. Lower Saxony’s Easter holidays run from 22 March to 3 April in 2027. According to its schedule, the park opened on Saturday, 21 March in 2026. Keep the same gap to the Lower Saxony holidays and you land on 20 March 2027, a date I wouldn’t book a hotel on just yet.

[Heide Park](ref:heide-park?full)

### Movie Park Germany

The last date in the calendar of [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany) in Bottrop is 5 January, the end of Hollywood Christmas, and there’s no day for 2027 yet. North Rhine-Westphalia’s Easter holidays begin on 22 March, and Bottrop still has no date to go with them.

## Season start in the Netherlands and Belgium

### Toverland and Walibi Holland

[Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum runs Winter Feelings until 17 January 2027, after which the park is closed until spring. Toverland has no 2027 date in its calendar yet.

[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) in Biddinghuizen has stayed closed in winter since the park dropped Bright Nights in April 2026. Its website calendar still only covers 2026. Season pass holders in Biddinghuizen spend the winter hitting refresh.

### Walibi Belgium

In Wavre the first weekend is Easter itself. According to the online calendar, [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) opens on **Saturday, 27 March 2027**, from 10:00 to 18:00, and stays open through Easter Monday. It’s closed on 30 and 31 March, and from 1 April it’s open on most days, without Loup-Garou.

[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium?full)

### Bobbejaanland

[Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) in Kasterlee is building an area with Nickelodeon characters right behind the entrance. According to VRT, three new family attractions open there "from April 2027". Bobbejaanland hasn’t announced when the rest of the park opens before then.

### Plopsaland Belgium and Bellewaerde

[Plopsaland Belgium](ref:plopsaland-belgium) has to be open on 13 March, or nobody gets into the new Flying Cinema. No calendar has any days before that. [Bellewaerde](ref:bellewaerde) near Ypres ends its Christmas season on 3 January and has no 2027 date yet.

## Season start in France

### Puy du Fou

Puy du Fou in the Vendée has announced its 2027 season from **27 March 2027 to 2 January 2028** and has been selling tickets for it since 6 October. The park’s calendar has closed days within those nine months. The Cinéscénie, the big evening show, plays on 28 evenings in 2027, Fridays and Saturdays between 5 June and 11 September, and Noël au Puy du Fou runs from 27 November 2027.

### Parc Astérix

[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) north of Paris also opens on **Saturday, 27 March 2027**, according to its online calendar, and stays open until 19:00. The first weekend is followed by four closed days, and it reopens on 3 April. In 2027 the park is rebuilding its Greece area ([about the new area](/news/parc-asterix-greece-2027)).

In our calendar, April 2026 came in above July at Parc Astérix.

### Disneyland Paris

The schedule for [Disneyland Paris](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park) lists both parks as open every day up to 3 January 2027 and doesn’t go any further yet. Disney’s Enchanted Christmas ends on 6 January. The resort hasn’t announced a winter break.

### Nigloland and Walibi Rhône-Alpes

[Nigloland](ref:nigloland) near Troyes runs Nigloween until 11 November and is building the launched coaster Supersonic 1887 for 2027 ([New attractions 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027)). The park hasn’t announced when Supersonic 1887, and the season with it, will start. [Walibi Rhône-Alpes](ref:walibi-rhone-alpes) runs "Merry ChristWAAAs" from 19 December to 3 January and has no 2027 calendar after that.

## Season start in Austria and Switzerland

### Familypark

[Familypark](ref:familypark) in St. Margarethen in Burgenland states on its website that it’s closed from 2 November 2026 to 19 March 2027. The first possible opening day after that is Saturday, 20 March. Opening hours for 2027 aren’t on the website yet.

### Conny-Land

Conny-Land in Lipperswil in Thurgau opens its 2027 season on **Thursday, 25 March** and stays open until 24 October, from 10:00 to 18:00, with the ticket desks closing at 17:00.

## How busy the first weeks will be

In 2027, the first opening days of almost every German park fall in the Easter holidays or just before them. German states set their own school holidays. The 2027 Easter breaks:

- **Bavaria**: 22 March to 2 April
- **Baden-Württemberg**: 25 March to 3 April
- **Lower Saxony**: 22 March to 3 April
- **North Rhine-Westphalia**: 22 March to 3 April
- **Rhineland-Palatinate**: 22 March to 2 April
- **Schleswig-Holstein**: 30 March to 10 April
- **Hamburg**: 1 to 12 March

In our crowd calendar for spring 2026, Legoland’s first five days, 28 March to 1 April, were "Low". From Maundy Thursday, 2 April, to 11 April the calendar showed "High" or "Very High", with "Extreme" on 8 and 9 April. From Monday, 13 April, through the Thursday after, it was "Very Low" every single day.

At Heide Park, 1 April 2026 was "Extreme", while on 14 and 15 April Soltau sat at "Very Low". At Phantasialand, the week after Easter, 6 to 11 April, was "High" every day, then "Low" from 12 April.

For 2027 that means the week after the Easter holidays, 5 to 9 April, is likely to be the first properly empty week of the year in Bavaria, Lower Saxony and North Rhine-Westphalia. The exception is Hansa-Park, because Schleswig-Holstein’s schools don’t break up until 30 March. In Sierksdorf the first days, 25 to 29 March, are a good bet instead, since only the other states are on holiday and Hamburg’s kids are already back at their desks.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Europa-Park open in 2027?

Europa-Park opens on 7 March 2027 for the Pre-Opening Weeks, which run until 19 March with a reduced line-up, from 9:00 to 18:00. It’s closed on 16 and 17 March. The summer season starts on Saturday, 20 March 2027.

### When does Phantasialand open in 2027?

Wintertraum runs until 24 January 2027. Phantasialand hasn’t published a date for its 2027 summer season yet. In 2026 the season began on 28 March, eight days before Easter.

### When does Heide Park open in 2027?

Heide Park says "for the Easter holidays" and hasn’t given a date yet. Lower Saxony’s Easter holidays start on 22 March 2027. In 2026 the park opened on 21 March.

### Which theme parks are open in January and February 2027?

Efteling is open all year. Plopsaland Deutschland runs a winter season with three areas until 14 March, Phantasialand’s Wintertraum lasts until 24 January and Toverland’s Winter Feelings until 17 January. Futuroscope starts its season on 6 February 2027.

### Which theme park in Germany opens first in 2027?

Europa-Park, with the Pre-Opening Weeks from 7 March 2027. The earliest regular season start belongs to Plopsaland Deutschland on 19 March, one day ahead of Europa-Park and Legoland.

### Why are many parks opening earlier in 2027 than in 2026?

Easter Sunday falls on 28 March in 2027; in 2026 it was 5 April. Many parks put their start just before the Easter holidays, which is why Legoland, Europa-Park and Puy du Fou open a week earlier in 2027.
