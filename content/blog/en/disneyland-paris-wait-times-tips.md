The big park has 43 attractions we get wait times for. The small one has
fourteen. So almost everyone visiting Marne-la-Vallée for the first time plans
the same day: the whole morning in
[Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), then a
quick hop over to the second park sometime in the afternoon, on the assumption
that there is not much queuing there anyway.

That is the wrong order. We have measured both parks for two years, and in
that time the crowd level in **Disney Adventure World** has been higher than
the big park's in every single month we have data for, and on every one of
the seven weekdays. Never the other way round. The smaller park spreads its
guests across a third of the rides, and you feel that at every barrier.

## One resort, two parks, one train station

Disneyland Paris opened on **12 April 1992**, back then still as Euro Disney
Resort, about 32 kilometres east of Paris in the new town of
Marne-la-Vallée. The site covers roughly **2,100 hectares**, a good half of
it built on. It is still the most visited theme park in Europe, and with
around 17,000 employees it is France's largest single-site employer.
(Somebody has to fry all those churros.)

Two parks stand on that site. [Disneyland
Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park) is the 1992 one: 56.7
hectares, five themed lands, the castle in the middle. The second park
followed on **16 March 2002**, was called Walt Disney Studios Park until
recently, and has been [Disney Adventure
World](ref:disney-adventure-world) since **29 March 2026**. The rename had
been announced back on 12 April 2024, and it took effect on the same day the
new World of Frozen area opened.

You pay for the two parks separately. A day ticket covers **one** of them,
and switching between the two means buying the two-park variant. It is the
one decision that day you cannot undo at a ticket window come afternoon.

## Disneyland Park: five lands and a dragon in the basement

The **Château de la Belle au Bois Dormant** is the only Disney castle built
for an audience that has real castles of its own. In California a silhouette
is enough; in Europe there is an actual one two hours away by car. So the
Paris castle went the other direction: exaggerated proportions, twisted
towers, trees made of stained glass on the upper floor. Underneath, in the
rock base, sits **La Tanière du Dragon**, a cave with a chained animatronic
dragon that moves and lets off steam. Some children do not want to leave.
Others will not go in at all.

**[Big Thunder
Mountain](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/big-thunder-mountain)**
is the largest version of this ride found in any Disney park, and the only
one built on an island. The train leaves the
shore station, dives into a tunnel under the Rivers of the Far West and only
resurfaces on the island, where the real track begins. Vekoma built it, it
opened on the park's first day, minimum height 102 centimetres. It shut for a
full year across 2015 and 2016 and came back with new brakes, fresh paint and
a handful of effects borrowed from California.

**[Phantom
Manor](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/phantom-manor)** is the
Paris Haunted Mansion, with one difference that fans make the trip for: the
Florida and California versions deliberately tell no continuous story. Paris
does:
Melanie Ravenswood has been waiting in her wedding dress ever since her
wedding fell apart, and her father Henry killed her suitors one after
another (a father of the bride with firm views on meeting the family), a plot the 2019 refurbishment spelled out clearly for the first
time. Six minutes in the Omnimover, 92 animatronics, plus Vincent Price's
English narration, parts of which came back into the ride in 2019. After
fifteen months of work, it reopened on 3 May 2019.

**[Star Wars Hyperspace
Mountain](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/star-wars-hyperspace-mountain)**
has only carried that name since 7 May 2017. It opened on 1 June 1995 as
“Space Mountain: De la Terre à la Lune”, after Jules Verne, with a cannon
launch. That launch is still there: the train goes from 0 to 71 km/h up the
mountain in 1.8 seconds. Three inversions, a kilometre of track, up to 5 g.
Of every Space Mountain worldwide, it is the only one with a launch and the
only one with inversions. Michael Eisner, Disney's boss at the time, called it
the park's saviour: on the back of its success, Disneyland Paris turned its
first profit after the loss-making early years.

And yet it does not top our ranking.


The longest queue in the park belongs to **Peter Pan's Flight**, a 1992 dark
ride that is over in about ninety seconds. Popularity only explains half of
that. The other half is throughput: Hyperspace Mountain runs four trains and
pushes almost 2,000 people through per hour, so it works its queue down all
day long. Little flying pirate ships with room for one family cannot do that. Spend the morning on
the coasters and you will spend part of the afternoon in the park's longest
queue, for ninety seconds of dark ride.

Three more rides cost you time that same morning. **[Pirates of the
Caribbean](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/pirates-of-the-caribbean)**
in Adventureland has been running since day one and lasts ten and a half
minutes, which makes it the longest ride in the park. **[Indiana Jones and
the Temple of
Peril](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/indiana-jones-and-the-temple-of-peril)**
opened on 30 July 1993 as the first roller coaster in a Disney park with an
inversion, built by Intamin, 600 metres of track, 58 km/h. It ran backwards
from April 2000 to November 2004. Its minimum height of 140 centimetres is
the highest of any Disney attraction worldwide, which regularly splits up
families on site. And
**[“it's a small world”](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/its-a-small-world)**
is exactly what you are afraid it is.

## Disney Adventure World: the new name and the missing ride

For a long time the second park was the part of the visit nobody looked
forward to. It opened in 2002 as a studio backlot, stayed too small for its
crowds for years, and anyone who saw it before 2022 probably remembers a lot
of asphalt. A renovation worth **€2 billion** has been running since
February 2018, and three pieces of it now stand: **Marvel Avengers Campus**
since July 2022, **World of Frozen** since 29 March 2026 with a 36-metre
mountain and a fjord in front of it, and a new path between the two that
finally connects the zones at all. A Lion King land has been announced.

The rides themselves are older than the renovation suggests. **[The
Twilight Zone Tower of
Terror](ref:disney-adventure-world/the-twilight-zone-tower-of-terror)** and
**Crush's Coaster** both date to 2007,
**[Ratatouille](ref:disney-adventure-world/ratatouille-laventure-totalement-toquee-de-remy)**
with its driverless ride vehicles to 2014, and the two Marvel rides,
**[Avengers Assemble: Flight
Force](ref:disney-adventure-world/avengers-assemble-flight-force)** and
**[Spider-Man W.E.B.
Adventure](ref:disney-adventure-world/spider-man-web-adventure)**, arrived
with the area in July 2022.

**[Crush's Coaster](ref:disney-adventure-world/crushs-coaster)** has the
longest queue of any ride in the park across the whole history we have
measured, on a broad data set and by a clear margin. It has been out of
service since **7 September 2026**. The park pulled it for its biggest
refurbishment since it opened: controls, sensors, communications technology
and sections of track are being replaced, alongside cleaning, painting and
repairs. The planned return is **summer 2027**, which leaves the East Australian
Current without passengers for around ten months.

You can see it in our data without needing a press release: the last live
wait time for this ride is timestamped **6 September, 20:21 UTC**. After
that it simply went quiet, while every other closed attraction in the park
kept dutifully reporting its status.

still printed on the menu that the kitchen stopped making in September. For

## How busy it really gets

Both parks feed us wait times continuously, over a two-year measurement
window and on a base of a good half-year of measured operating days each.
That is a broader base of data than some German park gets, even one with a
full guide of its own already on this site. How many days sit behind each
row, the tables state themselves.

```stats-widget slug=disney-adventure-world show=attractions

```

The gap between the two parks is stable across the whole window. Only in
December do typical wait times converge, and even then the crowd level in
Adventure World stays higher. That has little to do with popularity and a
lot to do with arithmetic: in 2023, Disneyland Park drew 10.4 million
visitors and the second park 5.7 million. Divide that across the attractions
that report wait times to us, and it works out to roughly **242,000 guests
per attraction per year** against **407,000**.

## When to go to a park that never closes

Disneyland Paris is open year-round, every single day, so it has none of the
classic off-season question that decides everything at a seasonal park. What
drives the crowds instead is French school holidays, which fall differently
from the German ones and are staggered by zone, plus British half-term
weeks, since a noticeable share of guests arrive via Eurostar.

## Premier Access, and what a queue costs there

The fast lane in Paris is called **Disney Premier Access**, sold per ride,
per trip. There is no fixed price: Disney charges different amounts
depending on the attraction, the date and the time of day, the same way
airlines price the school holidays. The floor in our database sits at €5 per person, with plenty of room above that for the
rides in demand. There is also a day variant covering every participating
attraction, which likewise swings a lot by date.

```glossary-widget slug=premier-access

```

Before you buy it, it is worth glancing back at the ranking further up. The
price tracks demand, and the highest demand does not automatically belong to
the rides where you would otherwise wait the longest. Pay for a coaster that
moves its queue perfectly well at lunchtime, then join the back of the line
for Peter Pan, and you have spent the money on the wrong queue.

## How I would plan the day

If you only have one day and one park, take Disneyland Park: more rides,
shorter queues, and the castle is the reason most people came in the first
place. Walk to the Fantasyland dark rides at opening, even if it feels wrong to
leave Hyperspace Mountain alone and climb into a flying pirate ship: once
their queues build, you will not catch up with them later in the day. Big Thunder
Mountain, Hyperspace Mountain and Indiana Jones sit well in the lunch hours,
while everyone else is eating. And if you are booking both parks, flip the
usual order and start in Adventure World, where our rope-drop analysis shows
Ratatouille and the Tower of Terror have the biggest payoff for an early
start.

## Practical: getting there, tickets, opening hours

**Getting there.** The **Marne-la-Vallée – Chessy** train station sits
between the two park entrances, a few minutes' walk from either. It is
served directly by TGV, Eurostar and ICE, plus the RER A line from central
Paris. From Cologne and Frankfurt, the fastest one-change connections run a
good four to four and a half hours; average travel times run noticeably
longer. By car it is about 32 kilometres from Paris, and parking costs
extra.

**Opening hours.** Year-round, daily. Hours shift a lot with the season and
are kept current on the [park
page](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park); on the day this was
written, it was 9:30 to 21:00.

**Tickets.** Dated tickets, with the price tied to the day you pick. There is
a one-park variant and a two-park one, and you decide at purchase, not at
the turnstile. Current prices for your date are on
[disneylandparis.com](https://www.disneylandparis.com/).

**Food.** Both parks have table-service restaurants that need a reservation,
plus plenty of quick service in between. Our database lists 35 dining venues
in Disneyland Park and 19 in Adventure World. At lunchtime the queue migrates
from the rides to the counters, and for a while the chip stand is the most
popular attraction in the park.

## Frequently asked questions about Disneyland Paris

### Is one day enough for both parks?

For an overview, yes. For the main attractions of both parks, no. Adventure
World, with fourteen attractions, is doable in half a day; Disneyland Park,
with 43, is not. If you want both in one day, start in Adventure World in
the morning and switch over at midday, not the other way round.

### Which of the two parks has longer queues?

Disney Adventure World, and that holds across our two-year window for every
month and every weekday we have measured. The reason is capacity: the
smaller park spreads a little over half the visitors across less than a
third of the rides. Current numbers for both parks are in the comparison
table above.

### Why is Walt Disney Studios Park now called Disney Adventure World?

The new name has applied since 29 March 2026 and had been announced since 12
April 2024. It arrived together with the World of Frozen area and ends the
studio theme, which had matched what was actually there less and less every
year since 2002. Nobody was going to buy a 36-metre Frozen mountain with its
own fjord as a film set.

### Is Crush's Coaster running right now?

No. The ride has been closed since 7 September 2026 for a roughly
ten-month refurbishment, with a planned return in summer 2027. Current
status is on the
[attraction page](ref:disney-adventure-world/crushs-coaster).

### What does Premier Access cost at Disneyland Paris?

The price is dynamic and depends on the ride, the date and the time. As a
floor, we track €5 per person per ride; for the most sought-after
attractions it runs several times higher on busy days. A day variant
covering every participating ride is also available.

### How do I get to Disneyland Paris without a car?

By train, to **Marne-la-Vallée – Chessy**, the station between the two park
entrances. TGV, Eurostar, ICE and the RER A all stop there. From the
Rhineland and the Rhine-Main area, a good four hours with one change is
realistic.
