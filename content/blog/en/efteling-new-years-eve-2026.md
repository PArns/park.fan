> [!UPDATE] 2026-10-26
> The New Year's Eve party has sold out, the [event page](https://www.efteling.com/nl/park/events/oud-en-nieuw) now says. The tickets for annual pass holders are gone as well, according to the [pass holder page](https://www.efteling.com/nl/park/abonnement/voordelen-abonnement/oud-en-nieuw). The event page takes sign-ups for a message when sales for 2027 open.

[Efteling](ref:efteling) has put tickets on sale for its New Year's Eve party on 31 December 2026, which runs from 7 pm to 1 am and costs €62 a head. The event page advertises rides in the dark and fireworks.

## What the ticket covers

The ticket includes an oliebol, a drink for the toast and the fireworks. On the event page the drink is a can of bubbly, on the pass holder page a glass.

Parking is included on New Year's Eve. On other days the Efteling car park costs €15, and guests at the hotels and holiday parks park free at their accommodation.

Each ticket is for one person aged four or over and admits them once. On day tickets, children up to and including three get in free with ID. Efteling doesn't say whether that also applies on New Year's Eve.

You can buy up to 19 tickets per order, and they can't be returned. The 14-day return period for online purchases doesn't apply to event tickets, and Efteling names the New Year's Eve ticket there explicitly.

## With an annual pass

An Abonnement, Efteling's annual pass, gets you 25 percent off. The ticket then costs €46.50 and is a separate product in the ticket shop, limited to six per order. Every one of those tickets has to be shown at the gate together with a valid pass.

The pass alone isn't valid that evening, so pass holders need a New Year's Eve ticket too. The discounted ticket can only be booked in the Dutch ticket shop; the English event page doesn't mention it.

## 31 December during the day

During the day Efteling is open from 10 am to 6 pm on 31 December, two hours shorter than from 19 to 30 December. The day ticket is sold separately and costs €56 for that date. The €33 evening ticket, valid from 4 pm between 21 December and 3 January, isn't sold for 31 December.

The day falls within Winter Efteling (16 November 2026 to 31 January 2027), and our [winter round-up](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026) lists the other parks open between Christmas and New Year. The park's maintenance list has no ride down for 31 December.

## How earlier New Year's Eves went

Efteling hasn't yet published which rides will run in the evening, what shows there will be or when the fireworks start in 2026.

For 2022 the park described the programme on its blog. From 11 pm DJs played at the Siervijver. The countdown began at 11:45 pm, followed by fireworks with laser effects, and then the DJs played again until 1 am. Tickets cost €49 including parking that year, and pass holders paid €36.75. Admission ran from 7 pm to 1 am in 2022 as well.

The official map for 2024/25 marked, among other things, a silent disco, "Grootmoeders Bingo", "Bubbels en bollen" and the best spot to watch the fireworks.

In 2022 the evening sold out on 3 December, just over three weeks after sales opened on 9 November. In 2018 the tickets were gone by 16 November.

## Staying overnight

Efteling wrote on 8 October that no accommodation was left for Oud & Nieuw.

The package includes entry to the party, unlimited park admission during the stay, park entry half an hour early and breakfast. In the holiday homes at Efteling Bosrijk and Efteling Loonsche Land you arrive on 30 December and stay three nights. Breakfast is delivered there. Hotel rooms at the Grand Hotel, the Wonder Hotel, Bosrijk and Loonsche Land start on 31 December for two nights, with breakfast and a three-course dinner, drinks not included.

Grand Hotel guests can pay extra to watch the countdown on the terrace of the Mystique restaurant-bar, with a snack and a drink.

## Getting there

Efteling is at Europalaan 1, 5171 KW Kaatsheuvel. By public transport, take the train to 's-Hertogenbosch or Tilburg and then, depending on direction, bus 300, 301, 136, 800 or 801 to the "Efteling" stop. From Brussels and Antwerp, Flixbus 811 runs direct to the park on Mondays and from Thursday to Sunday.

The free Lijn 7 shuttle links the bus station at the main entrance with the Wonder Hotel, Villa Pardoes, Loonsche Land and Bosrijk. It runs Wednesday to Sunday from 8 am to noon and from 4:30 pm to 7 pm.

Efteling lists no buses after 1 am on New Year's night. There's more on travel, tickets and hotels in our [Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).
