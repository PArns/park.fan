An annual pass is a bet that you will go more often than you plan to today. The stake is on the price
list, and the check is short: pass price divided by day ticket price, rounded up. Blocked days are the
harder part. The pass is not valid on them, and they are often exactly the days you have off.

This guide compares four parks: [Efteling](ref:efteling) in the Netherlands,
[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) in Rust, [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum and
[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl. The first three sell an annual pass. Phantasialand doesn't, and it is here anyway because there the question runs the other way: what does a year of visits
cost when every single one pays the day price?

All prices come from the parks' own pages and are dated 6 October 2026. The addresses are at the end.
Our wait times enter the text only through the widgets, never as a figure. They answer the second
question behind an annual pass: which days do you want to go, and are those the days the park is full?

## The four parks at a glance

| Park                                          | Annual pass             | Adult price      | Adult day ticket          | Blocked days                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| [Efteling](ref:efteling?bare)                 | Classic, Plus, Premium  | €240, €310, €400 | €40 to €56 (2026)         | 2027: Classic 68, Plus 25, Premium none         |
| [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?bare)           | ResortPass Silver, Gold | €325, €495       | €67 to €76 (2026 season)  | Silver only on over 230 set days, Gold none     |
| [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland?bare) | Magic Member            | €160             | €37 to €49.50 (base rate) | none, only days the park is closed or hired out |
| [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand?bare)       | none                    | not available    | €44 to €78 (October 2026) | not applicable                                  |

The day ticket ranges do not cover the same period. Efteling gives its range for the whole of 2026.
For Europa-Park it is the season from 28 March 2026 to 9 January 2027. For Toverland it is the base rate
in the ticket calendar from 6 October 2026 to 17 January 2027, so only the autumn and winter months. For
Phantasialand it is the October calendar of the ticket shop. None of the four parks has published
prices for 2027. Europa-Park has announced that tickets for the 2027 summer season go on sale on
10 November 2026.

## How the break-even works

The break-even is the smallest whole number of visits at which the annual pass costs no more than the same
number of day tickets. It hangs on two quantities, and both move. The first is the day price. Between the
cheapest and the dearest day the gap is €16 at Efteling, €9 at Europa-Park, €12.50 at Toverland and €34 at
Phantasialand. So I calculate with both ends and give a range. If you mostly go on Saturdays, you sit at
the upper end of the prices and the lower end of the visit count.

The second is the car. Each of the three parks with a pass sells a parking permit or discounts the
parking ticket. If you come by train, that part drops out and the "without car" columns in the tables
below apply.

Two things are missing from the sum because they cannot be put in a figure. One is the discount on food
and souvenirs, which depends on what you buy. The other is the visit you would not have made without the
pass. That is usually the real reason to buy, and it makes the break-even a floor: anyone who goes more
often with a pass also spends more on those days.

All passes are tied to a person. At Toverland the terms say so outright: one pass per person, not
transferable. A family adds up the passes member by member.

## Efteling: Classic, Plus and Premium

Efteling has sold three annual passes since 11 June 2024, differing in the number of days they are
valid. A discount for visitors aged 65 and over no longer exists.

| Tier    | Annual price | Monthly | Access            | Parking discount | Food, drinks, souvenirs | Friend tickets at half price |
| ------- | ------------ | ------- | ----------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Classic | €240         | €21     | at least 290 days | 40%              | no discount             | 2 per calendar year          |
| Plus    | €310         | €28.50  | at least 340 days | 50%              | 5%                      | 2 per calendar year          |
| Premium | €400         | €36     | all days          | 60%              | 10%                     | 4 per calendar year          |

All three tiers give 25% off CARO tickets and the New Year's Eve party, up to 25% off overnight stays in
the park and free or reduced entry to partner parks and zoos. Premium adds a keepsake and access to an
event for pass holders only. Paying monthly commits you for at least twelve months and costs more: twelve
months of Classic come to €252, which is €12 above the annual payment, and for Plus and Premium it is
€32 each. You set the start date of the pass when you buy, up to one month ahead.

### The blocked days in the park's calendar

Efteling publishes the blocked days as a calendar for Classic and Plus that you can subscribe to in
Google, Apple or Outlook. The figures below are counted from both calendars as of 6 October 2026.
Premium has no blocked days and does not appear in them.

| Period                         | Classic blocked | Plus blocked |
| ------------------------------ | --------------- | ------------ |
| 10 October to 31 December 2026 | 44 days         | 10 days      |
| Calendar year 2027             | 68 days         | 25 days      |
| Open days 2027                 | 297             | 340          |

The promised minimums hold. 297 open days on Classic are seven above the promised 290, and on Plus it is
exactly 340. Blocking falls mostly on weekends and in blocks of several weeks: in 2027, 42 of the 68
Classic days are a Saturday or Sunday, and 16 of the 25 for Plus. Monday to Friday account for 26 Classic
days.

The Classic blocked days in calendar order:

- Autumn and winter 2026: 10 and 11 October, 17 October to 8 November, 14 and 15 November, 22 and 29 November, 5 and 6 December, 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027
- January 2027: 10, 16, 17, 23 and 24 January
- Spring 2027: 6 and 7 March, 14 March, 20 and 21 March, 28 and 29 March, 6 and 7 May, 16 and 17 May
- Autumn 2027: 11, 12, 18, 19, 25 and 26 September, 2, 3, 9 and 10 October, 16 October to 7 November, 13 and 14 November, 21 November, 27 and 28 November
- December 2027: 4, 5, 12 and 14 December, 25 to 31 December

Plus blocks far fewer: 22 and 29 November, 5 and 6 December and 26 to 31 December 2026, and in 2027 10,
16, 23 and 24 January, 6 and 7 March, 20 and 21 March, 6, 7, 16 and 17 May, 21, 27 and 28 November, 4, 12
and 14 December and 25 to 31 December. The biggest difference is the three weeks from mid-October to
early November: 17 October to 8 November in 2026, 16 October to 7 November in 2027. On Classic the park is
shut to you in that time, on Plus it is open. Easter 2027 is the second example. On 28 and 29 March Plus
is valid and Classic is not. Ascension (6 May) and Whitsun (16 and 17 May) are blocked for both.

### What pays off

A day ticket at Efteling costs between €40 and €56 in 2026, plus €15 for parking per visit.

| Tier    | Price | Break-even without car | Break-even with car |
| ------- | ----- | ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Classic | €240  | 5 to 6 visits          | 4 to 6 visits       |
| Plus    | €310  | 6 to 8 visits          | 5 to 7 visits       |
| Premium | €400  | 8 to 10 visits         | 7 to 9 visits       |

"With car" counts the discount on the parking ticket as savings: €6, €7.50 and €9 per visit. The price
applies per person on every tier. Two adults on Classic pay €480.

The step from Classic to Plus costs €70, the step from Plus to Premium €90. Both steps buy days, not
anything on the day itself: from Classic to Plus, 43 days are added in the 2027 count, from Plus to
Premium 25. Per extra day, Plus costs €1.63 more than Classic and Premium €3.60 more than Plus. That's
less than any day price. What counts is not the price of a day but whether you want to come on those
days. If you never come in those weeks, you do not need the 43 days.

The weekday matters too. At Efteling the quietest and the fullest weekday are only one step apart, and
only Saturday sits one step above the rest, as the [Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands)
has it. Classic does block mostly weekends, but also 26 days from Monday to Friday. If you can come on
weekdays you lose little. If you have to come in the blocked stretches you lose a lot.

The park is a good one and a half to two hours' drive from Düsseldorf, a good two from Cologne and the
Ruhr. At that distance the number of visits is the question: three visits a year stay with the day
ticket, six or more are cheaper on Classic, and with the car and the parking discount the pass pays off
from the fourth to the sixth visit.

## Europa-Park: ResortPass Silver and Gold

Europa-Park sells the ResortPass in two tiers, both valid for one year:

| Tier   | Adults 12+ | Children 4 to 11, seniors 60+ | Access as a day guest            | Rulantica                |
| ------ | ---------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Silver | €325       | €275                          | on over 230 pre-set opening days | not included             |
| Gold   | €495       | €430                          | on all opening days              | two day tickets included |

As an overnight guest in the park's hotels and in Silver Lake City, both passes give entry on every day
of the stay. Both give €2 off at the evening cinema, free access to content on the VEEJOY streaming
platform and free or reduced entry to partner parks. A ParkingPass costs €39 extra on either tier. At a
€10 parking fee per day it is cheaper from the fourth visit by car. The printed card costs €5 extra on
Silver and comes with Gold.

At the moment no new pass can be bought. The park writes on its page that the allotment is used up and
the ResortPass is unavailable until further notice, also by phone, by e-mail or on site. Existing holders
are told about renewal before their pass ends. The sum for a new pass therefore only matters once sales
reopen, and when that is happening is not stated anywhere.

A day ticket costs €67 to €76 for adults and €56.50 to €65 for children and seniors.

| Tier   | Adults        | Children and seniors | Family of two adults and two children             |
| ------ | ------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Silver | 5 visits      | 5 visits             | 5 visits (€1,200 against €247 to €282 a day)      |
| Gold   | 7 to 8 visits | 7 to 8 visits        | 7 to 8 visits (€1,850 against €247 to €282 a day) |

For Silver the break-even is the same at both ends of the price range, five visits. Finding the cheap day
is therefore not enough. What counts is that you come five times on days Silver covers. Which days those
are is not on the price page but in the ResortPass portal. The page gives only the count: over 230 days.
The 2026 season runs from 28 March 2026 to 9 January 2027, which is 288 calendar days, less the two rest
days at Christmas. Silver leaves out part of the opening days, and the park chooses which, not you.

Gold costs €170 more, €155 for children. That is a little over two extra adult day tickets at the top
price. In return Gold is valid on every opening day, and the two Rulantica tickets are included. The
Rulantica ticket has a price of its own, which I have not counted here because it depends on the day and
the time slot.

Gold pays off if you do not want to fix days in advance or if you visit the water world twice. Silver
pays off if you come five times and can plan your visit days around the park's calendar. If you come only
two to four days a year, stay with the day ticket.

One date that helps pass holders: the park has announced the Pre-Opening Weeks from 7 to 19 March 2027,
two weeks before the season opens. On the ResortPass page the park calls them the best start to the 2027
season, with the cheapest tickets of the year. Day reservations for them go through the ResortPass portal.
That is the first date on which you could use a new pass.

According to our [Europa-Park guide](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips), the park is emptiest on a Sunday
or Thursday in May or June and fullest on Saturday. Which days Silver covers is set by the park in the
portal. The widget shows which days in the coming weeks are quiet.

## Toverland: Magic Member

Toverland in Sevenum has the cheapest annual pass of the four, and the simplest. It is called Magic
Member.

| Group                       | Annual price | Monthly (minimum twelve months) | Parking per year | Parking per month |
| --------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Adults and children 140 cm+ | €160         | €14                             | €50              | €4.50             |
| Children 90 to 140 cm       | €120         | €10.50                          | €50              | €4.50             |
| Seniors 60+                 | €130         | €11.50                          | €50              | €4.50             |

Paying monthly comes to €168 instead of €160. Included is entry on all opening days, 5% off food, drinks
and souvenirs at the permanent outlets, discounts at other parks and zoos and birthday offers. Entry to
the Halloween Nights comes at no extra charge, while the Haunted Experiences cost extra. A child under
90 cm gets a free pass if a parent holds an annual pass. If the child outgrows the height class, the
difference is payable for each remaining month.

### The terms

The price page lists no blocked days. The pass terms, version of 21 March 2024, say: the pass entitles you
to one day visit per opening day. Days on which the park is closed or hired out are excluded and are
listed on the opening-times page. On busy days the park may refuse entry without refunding the price.
There is no blocked-day list like Efteling's.

Further points from the terms that matter for the purchase:

- One pass per person, not transferable, with a photo that is checked at the entrance.
- Bought at the desk, it runs for one year from the date of purchase. Bought online, it runs from the date of collection, provided it is collected within two months.
- Online purchases carry a 14-day right of withdrawal as long as the pass has not been used or collected.
- The park sends a renewal invitation two months before expiry and may adjust the price at expiry.
- With monthly payment the pass can be cancelled after the first twelve months, with one month's notice.
- The parking pass covers one car per day, for one person in the family, and is not valid in the last hour before closing.

### What pays off

In the ticket calendar the base rate for adults and children 140 cm and over lies between €37 and €49.50.
Anyone who books online at least seven days ahead gets a €7 early-bird discount, a shorter lead time gets
€2. Toverland has four price levels, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum, set by the opening hours and
programme of the day.

| Day price                           | Break-even for €160 | Break-even with car (pass plus parking €210) |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| €37, cheapest base rate             | 5 visits            | 5 visits                                     |
| €49.50, dearest base rate           | 4 visits            | 4 visits                                     |
| €30, cheapest day with early bird   | 6 visits            | 5 visits                                     |
| €42.50, dearest day with early bird | 4 visits            | 4 visits                                     |

The "with car" column sets a parking fee of €13.50 per day against the annual parking at €50. Annual
parking pays for itself after four visits by car.

In 2026 the park sold a Summer Pass for the first time: €79 for adults, €59 for children from 90 to
140 cm, unlimited visits from 1 July to 30 September. Whether it returns in 2027 the park has not said.
If you come only in summer, you pay €81 less than for the annual pass.

Which days at Toverland are quiet is in the [Toverland guide](/blog/toverland-troy-wait-times-tips):
there is hardly any difference between weekdays, and the Christmas holidays stand out. A pass without
blocked days therefore helps you less in choosing a day than one at a park with a clear weekday pattern.

## Phantasialand: no annual pass

Phantasialand took its Club card off sale during the pandemic, and the last cards ran out in summer 2022.
The park's ticket-shop FAQ mentions neither an annual pass nor a Club card, and tickets exist only dated
and online. Why it stayed that way and what fans did about it is in the
[Phantasialand guide](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips).

What remains is the day price, and it jumps. In the ticket shop's October calendar a ticket for adults
from 12 years costs between €44 and €78. The gap between the cheapest and the dearest day is €34, which is
77% on top of the cheap day. Three visits a year cost between €132 and €234, and the difference comes
entirely from how early you book and which day you take. An annual pass would smooth that out, but there
is none. That leaves the choice of day, and cheap and empty do not always coincide. At Phantasialand a
Saturday is noticeably fuller than a Tuesday.

## Blocked days against full days

An annual pass with blocked days is good when the blocked days are the full days. Then you do not pay for
days you want to avoid anyway. It is bad when the blocked days are the days you have off and the park is
empty.

At Efteling that holds only in part. Classic blocks mostly weekends and holiday stretches. Because the
weekday makes little difference there, it does not block only full days, and with 26 blocked weekdays in
2027 quiet ones are lost too. At Europa-Park Saturday is the fullest day, and whether Silver blocks it is
decided by the park in the portal. At Toverland there are no blocked days, and the weekday hardly
matters. At Phantasialand it stays with the day price.

The widget sets the quietest weekday of the four parks side by side. If your weekday is in the column and
the pass is valid on it, the pass fits your plan.

```park-comparison-widget slugs=efteling,europa-park,attractiepark-toverland,phantasialand show=quietest

```

The column is what an annual pass does not buy. It sells access, not the right day. If you can come
during the week, Classic at Efteling and Magic Member at Toverland give you the most freedom for the
smallest price. If you are free only on weekends, Plus or Premium suits Efteling better, and Gold suits
Europa-Park.

## Who needs which pass

This is my reading of the figures above, not a buying tip. I know neither where you live nor which days
you have free.

Efteling with Classic works if you can come outside the blocked weeks and are at the park at least six
times a year. If you want to go between mid-October and early November, you need Plus. Between Christmas and New Year you need
Premium, because Plus is blocked then too. Premium lifts the 25 blocked days Plus has in 2027 and only pays off from eight to ten
visits. Below four visits none of the three tiers pays off.

Europa-Park with Silver works if you come five times or more and can live with the days the park sets.
Whether you can get a pass at all is decided by sales, not by the sum. Gold is the pass for anyone who
does not want to fix days in advance, and for families who visit the water world twice.

Toverland has the simplest sum. From four to six visits Magic Member is cheaper than the day ticket, and
by car from four to five.

At Phantasialand there is nothing to calculate except the day. A visit on the cheapest day costs €44, on
the dearest €78.

## When to buy

An annual pass runs for one year from purchase or from its start date. At Efteling you set the start and
can place it up to one month ahead. If you start in October, you begin in the middle of the blocked weeks:
a Classic from 10 October 2026 has 44 blocked days by the end of the year. The calendar year 2027 has 68
in all.

No park has named prices for 2027 yet. At Efteling the 2026 prices stand until the park names new ones. At
Toverland the park may change the price only when the pass expires. The ResortPass is not on sale, and
for 2027 season tickets the date is 10 November 2026. As soon as a park publishes new prices, this guide
will be updated.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a theme park annual pass worth it?

From five to six visits at Efteling on Classic, from five visits at Europa-Park on Silver and from four
to five at Toverland, in each case against the day ticket price. Phantasialand has no annual pass.

### How many blocked days does the Efteling annual pass have?

According to the park's calendar the Classic is blocked on 68 days in 2027, Plus on 25 days and Premium on none.

### Can you still buy the Europa-Park ResortPass?

Not as of 6 October 2026. The park writes that the allotment is used up and the ResortPass is unavailable
until further notice.

### Does Toverland have blocked days for the annual pass?

According to the pass terms of 21 March 2024, no. Only days on which the park is closed or hired out are
excluded. The park may refuse entry on busy days.

### Does Phantasialand still have an annual pass?

No. The Club card ended during the pandemic, and the last cards ran out in summer 2022.
