[Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) in Buena Park, near Los Angeles, runs Knott's Merry Farm from 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. This winter's new show is "Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies" on ice in the Walter Knott Theater, with Snoopy, the Peanuts gang and a company of figure skaters.

## The new ice show in the Walter Knott Theater

A vocal quartet sings Christmas standards, a line of Follies dancers high-kicks across the stage, and the skaters glide in between with Snoopy and his friends. The theater seats about 2,000, which is a decent crowd for a beagle. Showtimes are only in the Six Flags app, and the schedule changes from day to day.

Six Flags can't quite settle on the name. The press release of 4 November calls it "Snoopy's Christmas Follies", while the park's event guide says "Snoopy's Merry Christmas Follies". I'm going with the guide, since that's the park's own program.

Merry Farm has had a Snoopy ice show in past years too, most recently "Snoopy's Night Before Christmas", according to Wikipedia. The Follies are a new production, and the guide prints "All new!" above the description.

## Lights, shows and Santa

Thousands of lights, miles of garland and giant snowflakes hang all over the park, and every evening there's a park-wide Holiday Lighting Moment. In Ghost Town, Snow and Glow sets the lights on Main Street and Schoolhouse Road dancing to Christmas music while snow comes down. In Calico Park, the "'Tis the Season Tree Lighting Ceremony" switches on the Christmas tree.

Also on the schedule:

- Home for the Holidays: a musical revue with dancing and a winter set on the Calico Mine Stage
- The Gift of the Magi, 1885 and A Calico Christmas Carol: two plays after O. Henry and Charles Dickens in the Bird Cage Theatre
- Krazy Kirk and the Hillbillies at Wagon Camp, the Wandering Wranglers on the Calico Park Stage and the Fireman's Duo at the Fireman's BBQ Stage
- the Calico Carolers in the streets of Calico, and holiday bands in Fiesta Village

Ghost Town becomes the Christmas Crafts Village, with dozens of local artisans. Myles Freedman blows glass there every day, and Nathan Howard carves with a chainsaw.

You'll find Santa in Santa's Christmas Cabin, which is the Wilderness Dance Hall made over. It opens every day about half an hour after the park, and photos cost extra. There are cookies, hot chocolate, hot toddies and mulled wine, with alcohol only for guests 21 and over with ID. On some days, kids can decorate a cupcake in Mrs. Claus' Kitchen for a small fee and take it home.

The park is open every day of the event except 25 December. Thanksgiving, 26 November, is no exception, with hours until 10 p.m. These are the times from the park's calendar:

## What your ticket covers and what costs extra

Shows, lights, selected activities and most rides are included with admission. Food, drinks, souvenirs and some experiences cost extra. A date-specific one-day ticket starts at $65 plus tax online, and kids two and under get in free. You don't need a reservation, but tickets aren't refunded for rain either.

The 2027 Season Pass costs $120 and already covers the rest of 2026; renewing costs $110. On a payment plan it's $30 down and ten payments of $9. The pass beats two day tickets at the lowest price by your second visit, and it takes care of your Christmas present to yourself as well. Fast Lane, which takes you past the regular line on participating rides, starts at $75 for one day and is limited per day.

The holiday menu has a Cranberry Bacon Jam Cheeseburger with crispy onions at Sutter's Grill, a Hot Chocolate Churro from the Gourmet Churro Factory, and a flatbread with cranberry, mint and goat cheese at Mrs. Murphy's. The Merry Farm souvenir cup is $13.99, and each refill $3.99.

The Knott's Hotel sits right next to the park and sells packages that include admission. For Black Friday, Six Flags is giving a third night free when you book two at participating hotels, and the Knott's Hotel is one of them.

## Getting there and parking

The park is at 8039 Beach Boulevard in Buena Park. Parking is $40, or $60 in the Grand Preferred Lot closest to the park, and the park doesn't take cash. The lots open about an hour before the park. Without a car, take Metrolink to Buena Park station on Lakeknoll Drive and an OCTA bus down Beach Boulevard from there. The Karmel Shuttle runs from hotels in Anaheim, Garden Grove and Buena Park if you book it ahead. The park puts John Wayne Airport in Orange County 20 minutes away and LAX 30.

Over at the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, the [holiday season with Santa in Toontown](/news/disneyland-resort-holidays-2026) starts as early as 13 November.

## Merry Farm dates back to 1985

From 1923, the Knott family sold berries, jam and pies at a roadside stand. Walter Knott opened Ghost Town next to it in 1941, and the whole place has been called Knott's Berry Farm since 1947. Snoopy has been the park's mascot since 1983, the year Camp Snoopy was built, and two years later Gary Salisbury came up with Merry Farm. The family sold the park to Cedar Fair in 1997, and Cedar Fair merged with Six Flags on 1 July 2024. This fall the park still had Scary Farm running; there's more on it in our roundup of [Halloween in the USA](/blog/halloween-usa-theme-parks-2026).

Six Flags is celebrating in eleven parks this winter. [Six Flags Great Adventure](/news/six-flags-great-adventure-holiday-in-the-park-2026) and Six Flags Over Georgia have redesigned Holiday in the Park, and Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia has seven new shows from 26 November. Two offers in the press release don't apply at Knott's: the new free admission for guests 65 and over, and the Black Friday ticket that adds a day in the spring and summer 2027 season.
