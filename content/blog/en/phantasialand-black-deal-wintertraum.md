In the Black Deal, a Wintertraum day ticket for [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) costs €29 from Monday to Friday and €33 at weekends and in the Christmas holidays. That applies on every open day in December and January; the rest of November isn't included. Phantasialand announced the Black Deal today in its newsletter, and the ticket calendar already shows the new prices. The park gives no end date for the offer. The shop says prices can go up at any time, and each day has a set quota of tickets. For Saturday, 12 December, it's already used up.

Wintertraum itself runs until 24 January 2027. Opening hours are 11 am to 8 pm, and only until 6 pm on New Year's Eve. The day ends with the MAGIC SYMPHONY fireworks over Kaiserplatz in Berlin. If wind or weather cancels the fireworks, the Good Night Wish, a show of music and light, runs instead.

## What each day costs

Adult prices (age 12 and up) in the ticket calendar, as of 21 November 2026:

| Days                                             | Adult day ticket |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| December up to 22 Dec, Monday to Friday          | €29              |
| December, Saturday and Sunday                    | €33              |
| Christmas holidays, 23 Dec and 26 Dec to 5 Jan   | €33              |
| January from 6 Jan, Monday to Friday             | €29              |
| January, Saturday and Sunday                     | €33              |
| Saturday, 12 December                            | sold out         |
| 24 and 25 December, 1 January, 12 and 13 January | closed           |

In North Rhine-Westphalia, 6 January is still a school holiday, but the calendar already lists it at €29.

## November stays more expensive

Today, a Saturday, costs €69, more than two December Saturdays put together. Totensonntag, 22 November, is €45, and the days up to the end of the month cost between €35 and €42.

## What the Black Deal saves compared with 15 November

Weekends and the holidays get the biggest cut. Sunday, 27 December, still cost €42 on 15 November and is now €33, as are New Year's Eve (previously €37) and Saturday, 9 January (previously €38). Weekday prices hardly move; Wednesday, 2 December, was €30 on 15 November.

## How to buy your ticket

Phantasialand only sells tickets online, and each one is fixed to the day you pick in the calendar. For the same day, the online shop sells them until 4 pm, at whatever that day's price is and only while it isn't sold out. So if the car is already on the A553 at a quarter to four and it turns out nobody bought tickets, you're still in time. At the gate you show the ticket on your phone.

You can now also [buy the Quick Pass online](/news/phantasialand-quick-pass-online), from 11 am on the day of your visit, without queuing at Guest Services. It comes on top of the day ticket and costs from €5 depending on the ride. The Ultimate, which gets you one ride through every Quick Pass entrance, is €80.

Children aged 4 to 11 pay less, for example €27 on Thursday, 14 January. Their price only appears in the calendar once you click on a day. Children up to three get in free with an ID card or birth certificate. On their birthday, eleven-year-olds and younger go free as well, as long as you reserve the visit online in advance and bring ID.

## When it's less crowded

Going by our wait-time measurements at last year's Wintertraum, queues in the Christmas holidays were as long as in the August summer holidays, and the January weekends were very busy. On weekdays after Epiphany, the park was quiet. For the cheapest day with short queues, I'd pick a day from Monday to Friday between 14 and 22 January; the forecast for every Wintertraum day is in the [Phantasialand crowd calendar](ref:phantasialand?calendar). If your January day turns out wet, you can still ride [Winja’s Fear](ref:phantasialand/winjas-fear) and Winja’s Force indoors.
