The gate of [Parque Warner Madrid](ref:parque-warner-madrid) opens at **noon**.
In our calendar almost every operating day of 2026 starts then. Depending on the
season it closes at 8, 9 or 10pm, and at midnight in July and August. If you’re
standing in the car park at ten, you’ll wait two hours, because the car park
opens an hour before the park does.

The park is in San Martín de la Vega, about 30 kilometres south of the capital,
and has five themed areas built on Warner Bros. films and comics. Two of its
seven coasters are the reason to come: Superman, there since the opening in
2002, and Batman: Gotham City Escape, since 2023. Almost everything else in the
park has short queues. Batman doesn’t.

## How the park came about

Madrid wanted a park like the one Disney built in Paris. Disney had looked at
Spain beforehand and picked Paris, and the region got Warner instead. The
contract went to **Six Flags**, then a subsidiary of Time Warner. The agreement
with the Comunidad de Madrid was signed in **January 1999**, construction began
in spring 1999, and the Spanish Wikipedia puts the total cost at **368 million
euros**.

The park opened on **6 April 2002**. The evening before, Bo Derek and
Christopher Lambert had inaugurated it with a gala so rainy that the programme
and the TV broadcast were cancelled. It was first called Warner Bros. Movie
World Madrid, became Warner Bros. Park Madrid in 2004 and has been Parque Warner
Madrid since 2006.

The operators had planned for three million guests a year and soon lowered that
to one million. The Spanish Wikipedia gives the high admission price, the
parking fee and the ban on bringing your own food as reasons. In the first year,
long queues came on top of that, because the staff didn’t know the site yet, and
56 of them quit. In 2006 the debt stood at 240 million euros. The Comunidad sold
its shares to the construction firm Fadesa for 25 million euros, and **Parques
Reunidos** took over management for ten years. After that the park made its
first profits, according to the same source.

In 2019 it had 2.26 million guests, which puts it among the twenty most visited
parks in Europe. Parques Reunidos now belongs to the investment firm EQT, which,
according to Expansión, wants to put the group up for sale in September 2026
([more on that](/blog/parques-reunidos-sale-movie-park)). For a visit that
changes nothing for now.

The site covers 31.2 hectares. 3.2 hectares go to the Hollywood Boulevard
entrance area, which has no coaster, and roughly seven each to Movie World
Studios, DC Super Heroes World and Old West Territory. Cartoon Village has 5.5
hectares. Our park page lists 45 attractions and experiences, including
Halloween pasajes that only run for part of the year; the park itself talks
about 33 attractions.

Over the years the additions were mostly smaller ones. 2004 brought the first
Halloween days and more street shows, 2005 the Scooby-Doo dark ride, and the
park celebrated its first Christmas season in 2006. In 2009 Oso Yogui and
Correcaminos Bip Bip opened, in 2011 Cartoon Village got four more rides,
including the Flintstones traffic school, and in 2013 the Academia de Pilotos
Baby Looney Tunes. Parque Warner Beach followed in June 2014, a DC area with six
water attractions was added there in 2018, and the Aquaman Nighttime Spectacular
in 2019. Then four years passed before Batman.

## Superman and Gotham: DC Super Heroes World

DC Super Heroes World has the coasters people come for.
**[Superman: La Atracción de Acero](ref:parque-warner-madrid/superman-la-atraccion-de-acero)**
has run since the opening and, according to the Spanish Wikipedia, was the first
floorless coaster in Europe. Bolliger & Mabillard built it. On this type there
is no floor under the seats, and your feet dangle free.

```glossary-widget slug=floorless-coaster

```

According to the Roller Coaster DataBase the ride is **50 metres** tall, 1,100
metres long and **100 km/h** fast, has seven inversions and takes 2:26 minutes
for the circuit. The ride’s own page says 55 metres, the page for the themed
area 65. You can ride from **132 cm**.

The youngest and most important coaster is
**[Batman: Gotham City Escape](ref:parque-warner-madrid/batman-gotham-city-escape)**.
Intamin built it, it opened on **13 May 2023**, and it cost more than 20 million
euros, one of Parques Reunidos’ biggest projects of that year according to the
Spanish Wikipedia. It’s the first multi-launch coaster in Spain. Linear motors
accelerate the train three times, and the 1:51 minutes of ride time hold four
inversions. The top hat is 45 metres tall, and the top speed is 103 km/h
according to the RCDB, 104 according to the park.

```glossary-widget slug=launch-coaster

```

The ride is open to riders from **140 to 195 cm**, and the park points out that
because of the restraints, body girth counts too; if you’re unsure, ask the
staff at the station. It also stands in the middle of the park, with the track
running over the guests’ walkways. The Spanish Wikipedia lists several awards
for 2023, among them Best Coaster of the Year at the Park World Excellence
Awards, and for 2025 46th place among steel coasters at the Golden Ticket
Awards.

**[Shadows of Arkham](ref:parque-warner-madrid/batman-arkham-asylum)** is the
older Batman coaster, an inverted coaster by Bolliger & Mabillard with five
inversions, a height of 32 metres and 80 km/h. It used to be called Batman: La
Fuga, later Batman: Arkham Asylum. The park advertises a ride with a
virtual-reality headset, which the RCDB has listed since 2017. Minimum height
**132 cm**.

The landmark is the tower. **[La Venganza del Enigma](ref:parque-warner-madrid/la-venganza-del-enigma)**
is a combo tower by S&S and, according to the park, 115 metres tall. It runs in
three modes that change over the course of the day. In Space mode the car is
shot upward with compressed air, in Turbo mode it’s pulled up slowly and
dropped, and in Combo mode one follows the other. Minimum height **132 cm**,
maximum 195 cm.

```glossary-widget slug=drop-tower

```

The area also has the Lex Luthor Invertatron, Mr. Freeze’s flying chairs (from
100 cm) and the Joker bumper cars (from 105 cm). If you stay under 132 cm, you
can’t ride any of the three big coasters or the tower anywhere in the area.

## The other areas

**Movie World Studios.** The area has
**[Stunt Fall](ref:parque-warner-madrid/stunt-fall)**, a Giant Inverted
Boomerang by Vekoma that opened on **8 August 2002**. The train is pulled
backwards up a tower, drops through the station, runs a cobra roll and a loop
and is pulled up again at the other end. Then the track runs a second time,
this time backwards. The RCDB lists 54 metres of height, **99 km/h**, three
inversions and 4.5 g; the page for the themed area talks about 65 metres and
115 km/h. Minimum height **137 cm**, maximum 195 cm. The Hotel Embrujado (a
Vekoma Madhouse, from 90 cm) and the Cine Tour (a calm ride with no minimum
height) suit children.

**Old West Territory.** [Coaster Express](ref:parque-warner-madrid/coaster-express)
is a wooden coaster by the Roller Coaster Corporation of America, built to a
design by the engineering firm Stengel. Until 2004 it was called Wild Wild West,
after the Warner film. According to the RCDB it’s 36.6 metres tall and 1,394
metres long, runs at 95 km/h and has two helices of 590 and 520 degrees. The
park calls it the longest wooden coaster in Europe. In 2009 it stayed closed for
a season because problems had turned up in the structure, and afterwards it got
metal supports. The Spanish Wikipedia records complaints about strong
vibrations that can strain your back and neck, and about glasses and phones
that get lost on the ride. From **121 cm**.

Río Bravo (Intamin, from 100 cm) is a log flume based on the 1959 film of the
same name. The boat travels for a good five minutes through a rebuilt canyon and
an abandoned settlement before dropping 22 metres into a lake. The Cataratas
Salvajes (from 110 cm) and the Carros de la Mina (from 120 cm) are smaller.

```glossary-widget slug=log-flume

```

**Cartoon Village.** This is the area for families with smaller children. The
**[Tom & Jerry](ref:parque-warner-madrid/tom-y-jerry)** coaster is 360 metres
long and 8 metres tall and comes from Zierer. It had actually been bought for
Six Flags Fiesta Texas and was never built there, because the annual budget had
run out. From 100 cm, or with an adult up to 120 cm.
**[Correcaminos Bip Bip](ref:parque-warner-madrid/correcaminos-bip-bip)** is a
Mack Youngstar from 2009, 16 metres tall and about 60 km/h fast, also from
100 cm. On the **[Rápidos ACME](ref:parque-warner-madrid/rapidos-acme)**
(Intamin, 2004, from 110 cm) round boats take you down a rapids course, and in
**[La Aventura de Scooby-Doo](ref:parque-warner-madrid/la-aventura-de-scooby-doo)**
you shoot at ghosts on an interactive dark ride, with no minimum height.

**Hollywood Boulevard.** The entrance area has no coaster, but it does have
pavement slabs with the names of film stars, a replica of the Chinese Theatre
with a 3D cinema and the stage of the welcome show with the Looney Tunes.

## With children under 120 cm

With no minimum height, but with an adult up to 105 or 110 cm, you can ride La
Aventura de Scooby-Doo, Looney Tunes Correo Aéreo, La Captura de Gossamer,
Emergencias Pato Lucas, A Toda Máquina and the Convoy de Camiones. At the Cine
Tour in Movie World Studios that applies up to 120 cm. From 100 cm the two small
coasters, Río Bravo, the Mr. Freeze chair and the Hotel Embrujado join in, and
from 110 cm the Rápidos ACME. On most of these rides, children between 100 and
120 cm can only go with an adult. Batman is out for this group. At the other
rides the queue is usually short, as the table further down shows.

## Which ride for whom

If you only ride one big coaster, take Superman. It has seven inversions and no
floor, and its queue sits in the middle of the park’s range. If you want the
most intense ride, go to Stunt Fall. The RCDB lists 4.5 g, and the ride runs
through the same track twice, once backwards. Batman is the most modern coaster
and the one with the longest wait times, and if you’re coming for it, you’re
best off planning a pass. Coaster Express is a wooden coaster with a rough ride
and 1.4 kilometres of track.

Shadows of Arkham is the least conspicuous of the big names. The coaster is
compact, has five inversions on 823 metres, and you rarely queue there. You can
ride the tower from 132 cm. If you’re smaller, you’ll see it from below.

## Shows and what’s new in 2026

The programme runs all day, and the times are in the park’s app. The Superstars
Parade for the 20th anniversary moves through the streets as a procession, and
in July and August the Aquaman Nighttime Spectacular, a night show that
premiered in 2019, closes the day. For the theatres with a limited number of
seats the park advises being there 30 minutes before the start and choosing the
shows it recommends as less crowded.

New in the 2026 season is the **DC Super-Villains Stunt Show** in the Teatro
Especialistas, a car stunt show with characters from the DC universe. There’s
also the Don Gonzales Taquería on Hollywood Boulevard, a restaurant themed on
Speedy Gonzales where everything is gluten-free. Oso Yogui, Spain’s first splash
battle ride in 2009, is listed under closed attractions on our park page.

## The Pase Correcaminos

```glossary-widget slug=virtual-queue

```

The park sells its express system as the **Pase Correcaminos**, after the Looney
Tunes Roadrunner. You log in on the website with the e-mail address of your
booking, pick a ride, and a counter runs down. At zero and green, you go to the
pass entrance and show the QR code. Only one ride is active at a time, one
account manages up to ten passes, and the pass doesn’t replace admission.

There are four tiers. Silver (from €35.90) is unlimited and halves the wait, but
excludes Batman. Gold (from €45.90) is valid once, Batman included. Platinum
(from €75.90, currently €65.90 online) is unlimited with almost immediate
access. The One-Pass (€8) covers a single ride on one chosen coaster.

If you’re coming mainly for Batman, I’d buy the One-Pass or Gold. Silver saves a
little time at Superman, Venganza del Enigma and Coaster Express, but those are
exactly the places where the queues are short anyway, and the pass skips the
one long queue in the park. If you’d rather save the pass money, ride Batman
around 4pm, when the queue is a few minutes shorter than usual, and still plan
time for it.

## When to go

```stats-widget slug=parque-warner-madrid show=months,weekdays

```

The weekday makes a big difference here. Saturday sits at the top by a clear
margin, and Wednesday and Thursday are at the bottom. Monday is in second place,
with Sunday and Tuesday in the middle. Monday rests on few measured days,
because the park often closes it in autumn.

In the months, August stands out. July is low, even though the park is open
every day from noon to midnight in both months, and August is clearly higher. We
don’t know why. September is quiet. October has six measured days so far and is
the quietest month; December also has six days and an unusually high value (as
of 8 October 2026). Both figures are thin.

Here’s what the 2026 calendar looks like. From April to June the park is mostly
open, sometimes until 8pm, sometimes until 10pm. In July and August it runs
daily until midnight. In September 8pm and 10pm alternate, and in October
Mondays and Tuesdays keep dropping out. In November it opens only on weekends
and on two Mondays, from noon to 8pm. According to the park, the Christmas
season runs from 21 November to 10 January and also has a few weekdays in
December 2026 and nine days in January 2027. In February it’s practically
closed. What actually applies is in the calendar on the
[park page](ref:parque-warner-madrid).

The park gives a rain guarantee. If you’ve bought your ticket on parquewarner.com
and it rains for two hours in total, interruptions included, you can swap the
ticket for free within two months. The swap is handled at customer service in
the park, at least an hour before closing. It doesn’t apply to groups, Parque
Warner Beach and the Scary Nights. More on other parks in the rain is in the
[rain guide](/blog/theme-parks-in-the-rain).

### Halloween until 2 November

The Halloween season runs from **26 September to 2 November 2026**. During the
day there’s decoration, parades and the new family attraction ACME Factory,
where you repair a factory with the Coyote. On top of that come four Pasajes del
Terror (La Monja, Crime Alley: Night of Chaos, IT Experience, Expedientes
Warren) and two scare zones. The park sells the pasajes individually or as a
bundle. The musical La Maldición de Drácula is new as well.

The **Halloween Scary Nights** are a separate ticket for the evening. In 2026
they take place for the fifth time, on 9, 16 and 23 October from 10pm to 3am and
on 31 October from midnight to 5am, with entry two hours earlier each time. The
park recommends them from age 12, accompanied. The programme has eight pasajes,
six scare zones and a DJ, and Venganza del Enigma, Superman, Batman Gotham City
Escape, Shadows of Arkham and the Joker bumper cars keep running. Slasher Royale
is an extra experience from 18 with an ID check and was sold out on 8 October.
How the event compares with others in Europe is in the
[Halloween guide 2026](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026).

## Tickets, prices and annual pass

All prices are the park’s own as of 8 October 2026. The day ticket costs from
**€32.90** online and **€62.90** at the ticket office (from 140 cm). Children
from 100 to 140 cm pay from €32.90 online and €56.90 at the ticket office,
children under 100 cm nothing. The junior price also applies to seniors from 65,
large families and people with a disability from 33 percent. For people with a
disability the companion gets in free. The two-day ticket starts online at
€42.90, and with Parque Warner Beach together a day costs from €44.80.

Parking costs **€16** for a car, €19 covered, €17 for motorhomes and €7 for
motorcycles, and the park advises buying it online. You can’t bring food and
drink, which the consumer organisation FACUA has objected to. The Spanish
Wikipedia counts around 20 food outlets. The All Inclusive menu costs €63 online
for adults and €55 for children, the Express menu €12.90. The restaurants open
at 1pm, and the peak is between 2 and 3:30pm.

The **Bono Parques** are the annual passes of the Parques Reunidos group, valid
until the end of 2027. The Bono Oro costs €219, with parking €294, and is valid
in twelve parks. The Bono Platino (€329) is valid in 27. The Oro also gives 50
percent off at Parque Warner Beach. How that compares with other annual passes
is in the [annual pass comparison](/blog/theme-park-annual-pass-comparison).

## Services in the park

The park has 13 accessible toilets with changing tables, a left-luggage office
and a pick-up service for purchases, where the shops keep the bags until the
exit. The first-aid service is on Calle Nueva York between Stunt Fall and the
stunt show theatre. Smoking is only allowed in 16 designated zones. At Repsol
solar benches you charge your phone with solar energy, and the park also rents
out power banks. The official app shows wait times, shows and the status of the
Pase Correcaminos. Its wait times are the park’s own figures, and our tables
come from our own measurements.

## Getting there from Madrid

**By car.** A-4 heading south, exit 22, then signposted. According to the
Spanish Wikipedia the car park holds more than 10,000 cars. The park advises
arriving before opening, because queues form at the entrances, and the car park
opens an hour before the park.

**By train and bus.** Cercanías line C-3 goes to Pinto, where La Veloz’s line 413
takes guests to the gate. Line 412 runs from the Villaverde Bajo interchange
straight to the park. The park also sells admission with a bus from the Méndez
Álvaro bus station and from Príncipe Pío; for the Scary Nights, SAMAR buses run
from Méndez Álvaro with a return the same night.

**By car sharing.** WiBLE rents cars with parking at the park included, for €45
a day.

## Where to sleep

There’s no hotel at the park itself. It sells packages of admission and an
overnight stay in partner hotels in the surrounding towns, and for visits until
2 November it advertises a free second day (booking until 12 October). If you
sleep in Madrid, you drive 30 kilometres. That’s more practical when the visit is
a side trip in a Madrid holiday.

## A day in the park, roughly planned

Be in the car park at 11am, inside at noon. If you use the rope drop window, go
to the Rápidos ACME in Cartoon Village first and then ride Superman, Shadows of
Arkham and Stunt Fall while the queues are short. Batman comes either with the
One-Pass or Gold, or around 4pm. Take your lunch break shortly before 2pm or
after 3:30pm, when the restaurants are emptier. Coaster Express and Venganza
del Enigma fit into the late afternoon, the water rides into the warmest hour. On
days with a midnight closing, the park says wait times drop noticeably in the
last two hours, and that’s when you catch up on what you skipped.

## Frequently asked questions about Parque Warner Madrid

### When does Parque Warner open?

In our calendar almost always at noon, with closing between 8pm and midnight. In
winter the park is mostly closed, and in November it only opens on weekends. The
published calendar is on the [park page](ref:parque-warner-madrid).

### How much does admission cost?

From €32.90 online, €62.90 at the ticket office. Children under 100 cm pay
nothing. Parking costs €16. Current prices are on
[parquewarner.com](https://www.parquewarner.com/horarios-y-precios/precios).

### Which ride has the longest wait?

Batman: Gotham City Escape, by a wide margin. It isn’t included in the Pase
Correcaminos Silver.

### How tall is Batman: Gotham City Escape?

45 metres, with three launches, four inversions and around 103 km/h. Minimum
height 140 cm, maximum height 195 cm.

### When is Parque Warner least busy?

On a Wednesday or Thursday, especially in September and October. The quietest of
the next operating days are on the [park page](ref:parque-warner-madrid).

### Can you bring food?

No. The park allows neither food nor drinks from outside.

### How many roller coasters does Parque Warner have?

Seven: Batman: Gotham City Escape, Superman, Shadows of Arkham, Stunt Fall,
Coaster Express, Tom & Jerry and Correcaminos Bip Bip.

### Is there a discount for people with a disability?

From a degree of 33 percent you pay the junior price, and the companion gets in
free. According to the park, both get priority access to the rides with a
wristband or a card. Noise-cancelling headphones are available against a
deposit, in two sizes and in limited numbers.

## What I would tell you about Madrid

As a day trip from Madrid, the park is worth it mainly for three coasters:
Batman, Superman and Stunt Fall. If you come with children under 120 cm,
Cartoon Village has a decent programme and little queueing. If you go on a
weekday in September or October, you get the quietest days.

In July and August the park closes at midnight. If you’re coming without a car,
check the timetables of lines 412 and 413 beforehand, which the bus operator can
change. And if you want to ride Batman, decide at home whether you’ll buy a pass
for it.
