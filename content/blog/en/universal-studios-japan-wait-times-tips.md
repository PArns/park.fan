If you want to get into Super Nintendo World at [Universal Studios Japan](ref:universal-studios-japan),
you'll need an Area Timed Entry Ticket on busy days. There are two ways to get
one: in advance with one of the Express Passes, or on the day in the park's app.
Since **5 January 2026**, tickets at the park are only handed out through the
app. On quiet days the park says you can walk in without one.

The two rides with the longest waits in the park are both in Super Nintendo
World. In our measurements since October 2024,
[Mine Cart Madness](ref:universal-studios-japan/mine-cart-madness-2) is first,
followed by [Mario Kart: Koopa's Challenge](ref:universal-studios-japan/mario-kart-koopas-challenge-2).

## The park's history

Universal Studios Japan opened on **31 March 2001** in Osaka's Konohana ward. It
was Universal's first park outside the US, built for 1.7 billion US dollars.
Planning began in December 1992 with the founding of Osaka Universal Planning
Inc., the framework agreement with MCA followed in February 1996, and
construction started in 1998.

In its first year more than **11 million** guests came, and the park reached
ten million faster than any park before it. By 2009 attendance had dropped to 8
million a year. On **15 July 2014**, **The Wizarding World of Harry Potter**
opened at a cost of 45 billion yen. The park counted 13.9 million guests in
2015 and almost 14.9 million in 2017, then 4.9 million in 2020 because of the
pandemic and 12.4 million again in 2022. For 2024, English Wikipedia gives 16
million guests, which puts it third in the world behind the Magic Kingdom and
Disneyland.

USJ LLC runs the park. Comcast bought out the remaining shares in 2017 for 2.3
billion US dollars, and since 2 March 2017 the park has belonged entirely to the
group through NBCUniversal. In August 2026, USJ, Nippon Steel and the city of
Osaka are in talks about a neighbouring former factory site of around 60,000
square metres.

The latest big addition was **Super Nintendo World**, which opened on **18 March
2021** and cost 40 billion yen. Donkey Kong Country has been part of it since
**11 December 2024**. The park now has ten themed areas: Hollywood, New York,
San Francisco, Minion Park, Jurassic Park, Amity Village, Universal Wonderland,
WaterWorld, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter and Super Nintendo World.

## Height requirements, companions, metal detector

Every ride has a height requirement for guests riding alone and a lower one if
a companion rides along. The companion has to be at least middle-school age and
meet the ride's own requirements. On rides with a safety bar, staff also check
that the bar closes on each guest, and anyone who doesn't fit properly can't
ride.

On **The Flying Dinosaur**, **Hollywood Dream – The Ride** and its backwards
version, **Backdrop**, you can't take anything on board, and you go through a
metal detector before boarding. Anyone who can't pass it skips these three
rides. On most of the other rides, phones, cameras, umbrellas and keys are only
allowed if they're secured, stay with a companion or go in a locker.

## Super Nintendo World

You enter through a warp pipe into Princess Peach's castle, and Bowser's
castle sits on the other side. The area has three rides, plus the game played
with the Power-Up Band.

**[Mario Kart: Koopa's Challenge](ref:universal-studios-japan/mario-kart-koopas-challenge-2)**
has been the main attraction since 2021. You walk through Bowser's castle to
your vehicle, then four people sit in two rows for about five minutes and drive
through tracks from the game, firing shells at opponents. The park says it's
the world's first interactive Mario Kart ride in a theme park. Height
requirement 122 cm, or 107 cm with a companion.

**[Yoshi's Adventure](ref:universal-studios-japan/yoshis-adventure-2)** is the
gentlest ride in the area. Two people ride on Yoshi and use Captain Toad's map
to look for three eggs, with a view over the Mushroom Kingdom from the top of
Mount Beanpole. The ride takes about five minutes, and the park sells a photo
service. Height requirement 122 cm, or 92 cm with a companion. A child between
92 and 106 cm can therefore ride only this one ride in Super Nintendo World.

**[Mine Cart Madness](ref:universal-studios-japan/mine-cart-madness-2)** opened
with Donkey Kong Country on 11 December 2024 and is a roller coaster. The cart
is fired out of a barrel cannon at the Golden Temple, runs through the jungle
and jumps a gap where the track has collapsed. According to English Wikipedia,
Universal uses a new design for it, the "Boom Coaster": an arm connects the
wheels to the cart above, so the cart seems to lift off the rail, and all of
that happens without a screen. Four people ride in two rows, the ride lasts
about two minutes and you can get wet. Height requirement 122 cm, or 107 cm
with a companion. The ride has a single rider queue.

The **Power-Up Band** costs extra and is sold from the cart in front of Super
Star Plaza. It collects coins, keys and points and connects to the park's app.
In the Key Challenges you collect three keys around the area and face Bowser
Jr. at the end, and that final battle is meant for guests aged five and over.
For the fifth anniversary, a celebration runs from 18 March 2026 to 11 January
2027, with Super Star decorations, a meet and greet with Yoshi in several
colours and an anniversary band.

## The Wizarding World of Harry Potter

The area has two rides and the Hogwarts Castle Walk, which runs from 1
September 2026 to 17 January 2027.

**[Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey](ref:universal-studios-japan/harry-potter-and-the-forbidden-journey-2)**
is a dark ride with motion simulation. Four people sit in one vehicle, and the
ride lasts about five minutes. The park says you no longer need 3D glasses since
the refurbishment, but you now feel heat and cold when the dragon and the
Dementor appear. Height requirement 122 cm, also with a companion. Bags stay
outside, and there are lockers.

**[Flight of the Hippogriff](ref:universal-studios-japan/flight-of-the-hippogriff-2)**
is a short family coaster that runs through Hagrid's pumpkin patch and past his
hut. The ride takes about two minutes, and a train holds 16 guests. Height
requirement 122 to 195 cm, or 92 cm with a companion. There are no lockers
here, and bags can't come along.

## Hollywood

Two rides share one track. **[Hollywood Dream – The Ride](ref:universal-studios-japan/hollywood-dream-the-ride-2)**
opened on **9 March 2007**, built by Bolliger & Mabillard as a hyper coaster: 44
metres tall, a 43-metre drop, 90 km/h, 1,200 metres of track and about two and
a half minutes of ride time. It cost 50 million US dollars, the park's second
largest investment after the Spider-Man ride (120 million). According to
English Wikipedia, attendance rose quickly after it opened, and the park's net
profit later grew by 63.6 percent.

Before the ride, every guest picks a piece of music that plays during it. For
the 2026 Halloween season you can choose tracks by King Gnu, Ado and Sandaime J
SOUL BROTHERS, or Kenshi Yonezu's theme song for the film "Chainsaw Man – Reze
Arc". A train has 36 seats in nine rows. Height requirement 132 cm, also with a
companion.

**[Hollywood Dream – The Ride: Backdrop](ref:universal-studios-japan/hollywood-dream-the-ride-backdrop-2)**
is the same ride backwards. This version first ran from 15 March to 7 July 2013
and stayed afterwards. You take the 43 metres of the first drop with your back
to the direction of travel, so you can't see where you're going or what comes
next. Same height requirement, same rules at the boarding area.

**[Space Fantasy – The Ride](ref:universal-studios-japan/space-fantasy-the-ride-2)**
is an indoor coaster with spinning cars. It opened in 2010 and won a THEA Award
for outstanding achievement in 2011. The spaceship passes Earth, Saturn and
comets and plunges into the sun at the end. Eight people sit in four rows, and
the whole visit takes about ten minutes. Height requirement 122 cm, or 102 cm
with a companion. Because the ride is in a hall, it runs in any weather.

## Jurassic Park and Amity Village

**[The Flying Dinosaur](ref:universal-studios-japan/the-flying-dinosaur-2)**
opened on **18 March 2016**. It's a Bolliger & Mabillard flying coaster in a
special design: 46 metres tall, a 38-metre drop, 100 km/h, 1,124 metres of track
and five inversions. A pterosaur grabs the riders from behind and pulls them up,
which is how the park describes it. According to English Wikipedia it's the
second-longest flying coaster in the world; only F.L.Y. at Phantasialand, which
opened on 17 September 2020, is longer. There are four trains with 32 seats
each, and the ride takes about three minutes. Height requirement 132 to 198 cm,
and 132 cm with a companion too. As at Hollywood Dream, all bags stay outside,
and the lockers are at the boarding area.

**[Jurassic Park – The Ride](ref:universal-studios-japan/jurassic-park-the-ride-2)**
is one of the rides from the 2001 opening. 25 people sit in a boat and plunge
25.9 metres to escape a Tyrannosaurus. You get wet. The ride takes about seven
minutes, with a height requirement of 122 cm, or 107 cm with a companion. For
its 25th anniversary the ride had extra effects from 8 July to 30 September 2026.

**[JAWS](ref:universal-studios-japan/jaws-2)** in Amity Village is a boat tour
of about seven minutes, with 48 guests in eight rows of six. A shark attacks
the boat, and you can get wet. Height requirement 122 cm, with no limit if a
companion rides along. From 11 September 2026 to 31 January 2027, the ride runs
in the evenings with extra effects for its 25th anniversary, from 6:30 pm until
9 October and from 6:00 pm until park closing after that.

## Minion Park and Universal Wonderland

Minion Park is home to **[Despicable Me: Minion Mayhem](ref:universal-studios-japan/despicable-me-minion-mayhem-2)**,
which opened in 2017. A dome screen and a projection fill the room, and
according to the park you become a Minion in a vehicle designed by Gru. Each
vehicle holds eight people, two rows of four, and the whole thing takes about 25
minutes. Height requirement 122 cm, or 102 cm with a companion, and a single
rider queue since 21 December 2019.

**[Freeze Ray Sliders](ref:universal-studios-japan/freeze-ray-sliders-2)** is a
race on frozen ground where you can get wet: four people, a ride of about a
minute and a half, height requirement 122 cm, or 92 cm with a companion. Since
2025 there's also **[Illumination's Villain-Con Minion Blast](ref:universal-studios-japan/illuminations-villain-con-minion-blast)**,
a game with blasters in which you take on the Vicious 6. It lasts about seven
minutes, the height requirement is 122 cm, and there's no limit with a
companion.

Universal Wonderland opened in March 2012, and its rides are for small guests:
**[The Flying Snoopy](ref:universal-studios-japan/the-flying-snoopy-2)** (122
cm, or 92 with a companion, two people per car),
**[Hello Kitty's Cupcake Dream](ref:universal-studios-japan/hello-kittys-cupcake-dream-2)**
(122 cm, no limit with a companion, five people per cup) and, since 2025,
**[Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure](ref:universal-studios-japan/snoopys-flying-ace-adventure)**,
an indoor coaster that replaced Snoopy's Great Race. Sesame Street Fun World,
with Elmo and Big Bird, closed on 10 May 2026.

### Seasons and special dates in autumn 2026

The autumn season is called "Universal Extreme Autumn" in 2026 and runs from
**10 September to 8 November**. At its centre is **Halloween Horror Nights**
from 11 September to 8 November, the 15th edition. The attractions include
"Lights Out: Nightmare Isolation", which the park says is its first attraction
for guests aged 18 and over that requires a consent form, "Resident Evil
Requiem: The Dive" and "Factory of Fear: Zombie Tour". Guests up to 12 years old
need their parents' consent, and the park advises against it for children up to
five. According to the park's notice, no child up to 14 and no middle-school
student may enter "Resident Evil Requiem: The Dive" because of its extreme
content.

During this time Hollywood Dream plays tracks by Japanese bands, and Jurassic
Park runs in the dark as "The Ride in the Dark". For the Express Pass, the park
has put together Halloween packages with four attractions, for example with
Factory of Fear, Chainsaw Man: The Chaos 4-D or Resident Evil Requiem: The
Dive. Our [Halloween guide](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026) covers the other
parks in the season.

The "Frieren: Beyond Journey's End Story Walk" in Hollywood runs from 30 May
2026 to 11 January 2027 and requires an Attraction Timed Entry Ticket from the
app. "ONE PIECE Premier Summer 2026" runs from 30 July to 19 November 2026, and
the street show "Wicked: The Witches of Oz" from 20 February to 27 December 2026.

## Express Pass: packages, prices, time slots

A Studio Pass gets you into the park and on all the rides. You buy the Universal
Express Pass on top of it, and it gives you access to a shorter queue at selected
rides.

The park doesn't list prices on its own site; they're in its ticket shop. As of
8 October 2026, the authorised ticket shop JTRWeb (usjticketing.com) carries more
than twenty packages, each with a price starting from a minimum amount that goes
up depending on the day of the visit. The packages are valid only on selected
days and together with a Studio Pass. All prices include tax:

| Express Pass Premium                                      | all rides | 41,000 to 44,000 | included                       |

According to the ticket shop, the packages marked "included" in the last column
come with a guaranteed Area Timed Entry Ticket for Super Nintendo World; with
the others you have to get the ticket in the app on the day. The "Race &
Minecart Special" package includes Mario Kart, Mine Cart Madness, Forbidden
Journey, Villain-Con Minion Blast and The Flying Dinosaur. Packages with
Hollywood Dream or the dinosaur ride have a height requirement of 132 cm.

If one of the pass's rides is out of service, the park says you use the pass's
other rides instead, except for rides with a fixed time. The ticket lists for
each ride whether a time applies. You have to show the whole screen with the QR
code, and an altered ticket isn't valid.

For guests without a pass, the park runs a single rider queue at Mine Cart
Madness, Mario Kart, Forbidden Journey, Minion Mayhem, Hollywood Dream, Space
Fantasy, The Flying Dinosaur, Jurassic Park and JAWS. You don't sit with your
group, but you fill a gap where a single seat is free.

### The app: time slots, Yoyakunori, standby

Before you can buy a time slot, your ticket has to be registered in the app, and
for groups that means the tickets of every member.

The **Area Timed Entry Ticket** applies to areas such as Super Nintendo World.
Once the day's allotment is gone, the park issues the **Standby Entry Ticket**,
and it says that depending on the crowds, this can stop without warning. Even
with a ticket, the ride inside the area can already be fully booked.

**Yoyakunori (Reserve & Ride)** reserves a ride time for selected rides, in the
park for example at The Flying Snoopy and Freeze Ray Sliders. Until your
reserved time, you can ride other rides or eat. The **Attraction Timed Entry
Ticket** applies to individual rides, including the Frieren walk. Both are only
available in the park and can't be transferred, and each group can pick only one
ride. If you uninstall the app before your ride time, the ticket becomes
invalid. The app requires iOS 17 or Android 9.

## Tickets and prices

All prices are listed with tax in the ticket shop and depend on the day of the
visit. For visits from 1 September 2026, the park uses day-based pricing: every
day starts at its lowest level, and the price can rise when demand is high.
According to the park, buying early is usually cheapest.

| Ticket                                                 | Adults from |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| 1 Day Studio Pass                                      | 8,400 yen   |
| 1.5 Day Studio Pass (entry on the first day from 3 pm) | 13,600 yen  |
| 2 Day Studio Pass                                      | 16,000 yen  |

A child is anyone aged 4 to 11 on the day of the visit. If you start middle
school in April, you still pay the child price until 31 March and the adult
price from 1 April. A Studio Pass doesn't allow re-entry once you've left; only
the annual pass does, and the park says that can only be booked in Japanese.
Resale is prohibited, and tickets from resellers aren't accepted. For some
tickets for visits from 1 September 2026, the date can be changed.

## Getting there

**Universal City** station is on the JR Yumesaki Line (also called the Sakurajima
Line), run by JR West. It opened on **1 March 2001**, a month before the park.
The line is 4.1 kilometres long and starts at Nishikujō, and Universal City is
the third station, 3.2 kilometres away. Some trains run directly from Tennōji,
Kyōbashi and Ōsaka, alternating with others outside rush hour. The park lists
travel times from eight starting points, including Umeda and Osaka Station,
Shin-Osaka, Namba, Tennōji and the airports at Itami and Kansai.

By boat, the Captain Line runs from Kaiyukan West Wharf to Universal City Port in
about ten minutes. Buses and taxis stop at the Bus & Taxi Plaza.

If you drive, there are around 2,800 car spaces. The fee depends on the day, and
the park keeps a fee calendar. The area with the shortest walk to the entrance
costs 1,000 yen extra. Motorcycles over 51 cc pay 1,000 yen, vehicles 5.6 metres
or longer pay 6,000 yen and have to be reserved four days ahead. Vehicles 2.3
metres or taller report at the barrier. For dropping off guests, there's a zone
with 20 minutes of free stopping.

## Staying near the park

The park lists seven partner hotels. The **Park Front Hotel at Universal Studios
Japan** is in front of the main gate and closest to the park, and **The Singulari
Hotel & Skyspa** connects directly to the station. The **Hotel Keihan Universal
Tower** has what the hotel says is Osaka's first natural hot spring, with a view
from the 31st floor, and a restaurant with an airship theme. The **Hotel Kintetsu
Universal City** has Sesame Street rooms, and **Hotel Universal Port** and **Hotel
Universal Port Vita** (opened in July 2018) are decorated with Minions. The
**Oriental Hotel Universal City** completes the list. If you stay at a partner
hotel, you can buy Studio Passes on the day of check-in and the day after.

## Food

Super Nintendo World has Kinopio's Café, Yoshi's Snack Island and Pit Stop
Popcorn, plus Jungle Beat Shakes in the Donkey Kong area. For the anniversary
there's a popcorn bucket that plays music and lights up. The park has its own
page for allergies, and in the restaurants you can reserve Priority Seating in
advance.

## What's new and what has closed

Since the end of 2024, the Donkey Kong area, the Detective Conan show,
Illumination's Villain-Con Minion Blast and Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure have
been added. The Spider-Man ride, which opened in 2004, closed on 22 January 2024. Backdraft and T2-3D have been closed since 2020, and Sesame Street Fun
World since 10 May 2026.

## Frequently asked questions about Universal Studios Japan

### How much does admission cost?

A 1-day ticket for adults costs from 8,400 yen including tax, and a 2-day ticket
from 16,000 yen. The exact price depends on the day of the visit, because since
1 September 2026 the park has priced tickets by demand.

### Do I need a ticket for Super Nintendo World?

Depending on the crowds, yes: the park then requires an Area Timed Entry Ticket,
which you can get in advance with an Express Pass or on the day in the app. On
days with few visitors, you can get in without one.

### How tall does my child need to be for the rides?

Most rides have a limit of 122 cm, and with a companion it drops to between 92
and 107 cm depending on the ride. Hollywood Dream and The Flying Dinosaur
require 132 cm, also with a companion.

### How much does the Express Pass cost?

An Express Pass 4 starts at 10,800 yen and an Express Pass 8 at 23,900 yen. The
Premium package for all rides costs from 41,000 yen, and the Unlimited version
from 99,000 yen. Prices depend on the day of the visit, and only some of the
packages include the time slot for Super Nintendo World.

### When is Universal Studios Japan least busy?

The weekday makes hardly any difference. In our measurements from October 2024
to October 2026, July has the lowest crowd level, and March and December are
higher.

### When is Halloween Horror Nights 2026?

From 11 September to 8 November 2026, the 15th edition. Guests up to 12 years
old need their parents' consent, and the park advises against it for children up
to five.

### How do I get from Osaka to Universal Studios Japan?

Take the JR Yumesaki Line to Universal City, two stops from Nishikujō. Some
trains run without a change from Osaka, Tennōji and Kyōbashi.

### Can I leave the park and come back later?

Not with the Studio Pass. The park doesn't allow re-entry; only the annual pass
does.
