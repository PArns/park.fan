[Liseberg](ref:liseberg) in Gothenburg opened the
[Radiobilarna](ref:liseberg/radiobilarna) for the last time on Sunday, 4
October. The bumper-car ride had run since 1927 and is the park's oldest ride
concept. The building will be demolished and a new attraction will take its
place.

## Why the building is going

Liseberg's announcement of 29 September gives room for new things as the reason
for the demolition. CEO Andreas Andersen says there that the park has to keep
developing to stay attractive and sometimes has to make space for that. The announcement does not say what will replace the Radiobilarna.

The park stopped operating the ride after its first Halloween weekend. From 2 to
4 October, every guest with park admission rode for free.

## Since 1927

The Radiobilarna opened on 12 May 1927 at 7 p.m. For the first five years the
entrepreneur Arnold Neble ran the ride. After that Liseberg took it over for
5,000 kronor.

Since then it has stood in four different places in the park, and it moved for
the last time in 1998. Eight different car models have been used over the
years. According to the park, more than 300,000 guests a year rode it most
recently. The announcement also names Michael Jackson and Stevie Wonder among
the riders.

## What stays

The small children's bumper cars "Tuta & Kör" in Kaninlandet stay in the park
unchanged.

## What is still open

Liseberg has not decided whether the Radiobilarna will be rebuilt elsewhere in
the park. The park is looking into whether that is possible. It has not said
which attraction will go into the old building.
