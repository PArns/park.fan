## New

- Every address starts with the language, `/en/` or `/de/`, and a switch in the header changes it.
  The old addresses without one redirect.
- Pages go from continent to country and city down to a single park and ride. A park page has
  tabs for attractions, shows, restaurants, the calendar and the map, and each tab has its own
  link.
- Each ride shows its status (open, down, closed, under refurbishment), its wait with an arrow for
  the trend, a small chart of the last hours behind it, and the return time where a park runs a
  virtual queue.
- The park header shows today's opening hours on the park's own clock, counts down to opening,
  closing and the next show, and names the hour that is usually busiest.
- The crowd calendar colours each day by the expected crowd, with public holidays and bridge days
  marked.
- The map places every ride with its wait and can show where you are. "Parks near you" asks for
  your location and lists the closest parks with their distance and hours.
- Search opens with Ctrl+K or ⌘K and finds parks, rides, shows and restaurants. It also sits in
  the middle of the homepage, cycling through park names as examples.
