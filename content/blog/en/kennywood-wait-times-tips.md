[Kennywood](ref:kennywood) sits on a bluff above the Monongahela River in West Mifflin, and a streetcar company built it as a day out at the end of its line. The US once had plenty of these trolley parks, and 13 of them are still operating. Kennywood has been a National Historic Landmark since 1987, the highest level of heritage protection in the country, and the only other amusement park that holds it is Rye Playland.

There are eight roller coasters in the park, three of them wooden and dating from the 1920s. The youngest and tallest of the five steel coasters is Steel Curtain, in an area that belongs entirely to the Pittsburgh Steelers. The park is so much a part of everyday life in Pittsburgh that "Kennywood's open" is what locals tell you when your fly is down.

I haven't been to Kennywood myself, so the details here come from the park, RCDB, Wikipedia and our own measurements. If you go now, from mid-November you'll find Holiday Lights, with more than three million lights and a new ice show.

## The rides by area

On its 2026 park map, Kennywood divides the grounds into ten areas: Entrance Plaza, Good Time Midway, The Lagoon, Steelers Country, Kiddieland, Kennyville, Kennywood Junction, Lost Kennywood, Kenny Lane and Area 412. The grounds cover 80 acres, a little over 32 hectares, with more than 40 rides and attractions on them.

### Good Time Midway

[Sky Rocket](ref:kennywood/sky-rocket?bare), a launch coaster by Premier Rides, has been firing trains out of the Good Time Midway since 29 June 2010. A linear motor gets the train to 50 mph (80 km/h) in three seconds, then it heads straight up into a top hat 95 feet (29 metres) high. The 640 metres of track after that hold a cutback, a zero-g roll and a corkscrew, and after 1:05 minutes you're back in the station, the shortest ride of the big coasters in the park. Sky Rocket has two trains with twelve seats each. From May 2018 it stood idle for more than a year and returned on 13 July 2019, the same day Steel Curtain opened.

The [Jack Rabbit](ref:kennywood/jack-rabbit?bare) is Kennywood's oldest coaster and has run since 18 June 1920. John A. Miller laid the wooden track into a natural ravine, which is why a lift hill of only 40 feet (12 metres) is enough for a 70-foot (21-metre) drop, taken as a double dip in two stages. Over 650 metres the train reaches 45 mph (72 km/h), and the ride lasts 1:36 minutes. By the park's account, the Jack Rabbit was one of the first coasters with upstop wheels under the rail to keep the train on the track. In 1920 it cost $50,000.

The [Old Mill](ref:kennywood/old-mill?bare) is older still. It's been here since 1901, the oldest surviving ride in the park, and according to Kennywood no dark ride on water anywhere in the world has run longer without a break. It has gone by plenty of names over the years, "Panama Canal", "Tour Around the World" and, from 2004 to 2020, "Garfield's Nightmare". In 2020 the park restored it.

The [Kangaroo](ref:kennywood/kangaroo?bare) in the Good Time Midway dates from 1962. After the 2020 season Kennywood took it down, renovated it and put it back up in 2022.

### The Lagoon

The layout around the Lagoon has barely changed since the early days, and three rides from 1927 to 1930 stand close together there: Racer, the Merry-Go-Round and the Auto Race. Paddle boats go out on the water in the middle. They cost extra and won't be back until 2027.

[Racer](ref:kennywood/racer?bare) looks like two wooden coasters racing side by side. In fact it's one continuous track, and if you board on the right you come back on the left. There's no other coaster in the US with a track like it, Kennywood says, and RCDB lists Racer as a Möbius coaster. John A. Miller designed it, and Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters built it in 1927. At 72.5 feet (22 metres), Racer is the tallest of the three wooden coasters, yet at 40 mph (64 km/h) it's also the slowest. The track is 4,500 feet (1,372 metres) long, and a lap takes 1:32 minutes. With three trains of 24 seats each, Racer gets through 1,400 riders an hour. An earlier coaster called Racer stood in the park from 1910 to 1926.

William Dentzel built the [Merry-Go-Round](ref:kennywood/merry-go-round?bare) in 1927. It carries 64 animals, among them 50 jumping horses, a lion and a tiger.

According to Kennywood, the [Auto Race](ref:kennywood/auto-race?bare) from 1930 by the Lagoon, the [Turtle](ref:kennywood/turtle?bare), [Noah's Ark](ref:kennywood/noahs-ark?bare) and the Kangaroo are the last of their kind, so anyone riding them is sitting in a museum piece with a lap bar.

### Steelers Country

The Pittsburgh Steelers have had an area of their own since 2019, Steelers Country, and its ride is [Steel Curtain](ref:kennywood/the-steel-curtain?bare). S&S Worldwide built the steel coaster to a design by Joe Draves, and it opened on 13 July 2019 where the Log Jammer flume used to be. Steel Curtain is 220 feet (67 metres) tall, and the park says no coaster in Pennsylvania is taller. RCDB gives 75 mph (121 km/h) and eight inversions, Kennywood 76 mph (122 km/h) and nine. The highest inversion is 197 feet (60 metres) above the ground and was the highest in the world when the coaster opened. The train needs two minutes for its 4,000 feet (1,219 metres), and two trains with 24 seats each are in service.

In July 2023 Kennywood closed Steel Curtain to add extra footings and supports. The coaster sat out the whole 2024 season and came back in May 2025. The weight limit is 300 lbs (136 kg), and there's a test seat, which a Steelers linebacker would probably want to try before anyone else.

The End Zone Cafe, the Punts Bar and a Steelers merchandise shop are in the same area.

### Kenny Lane

[Phantom's Revenge](ref:kennywood/phantoms-revenge?bare) opened in 1991 as Steel Phantom, an Arrow Dynamics steel coaster with four loops. Its 80 mph (129 km/h) and its 225-foot (69-metre) drop were world records for speed and drop height at the time, the park says. Over the winter of 2000/01, D.H. Morgan rebuilt it, took the loops out and set the big drop a little lower. It's been called Phantom's Revenge since 2001. For its 25th birthday in 2026 the cars run alternately in green and purple, the station has been renewed, and the queue plays the original ride sounds, re-recorded.

The train climbs 160 feet (49 metres) and reaches 85 mph (137 km/h). RCDB puts the longest drop at 228 feet (69.5 metres), while Kennywood gives 232 feet (71 metres) for the second drop. The two disagree on the length as well, with 3,200 feet (975 metres) at RCDB and 3,365 feet (1,026 metres) from the park. Along the way the train passes twice through Thunderbolt's wooden structure. The ride lasts 1:57 minutes, and each of the two trains seats 28.

Kenny Lane also has the Musik Express and the Pirate, which swings up to 60 feet (18 metres) high and to an angle of 75 degrees. The bumper cars of Potato Smash are shaped like the fries from the Potato Patch.

### Kennyville

Thunderbolt stands in Kennyville and started out as the Pippin, a wooden coaster by John A. Miller that opened on 4 May 1924. In 1968 Andy Vettel expanded it into Thunderbolt. The first drop goes into a ravine, and the biggest ones don't come until the end, with a fall of 90 feet (27 metres) even though the highest point is only 70 feet (21 metres) above the ground. Over 3,250 feet (991 metres) the train reaches 55 mph (89 km/h), and the ride lasts 1:41 minutes. National Amusement Device built the trains, which the park calls Century Flyers from the 1950s. In 1974 the New York Times called Thunderbolt the "King of the Coasters". The coaster is missing from the Phantom Fall Fest 2026 park map, but its page on the park's website has no closure notice.

In [Ghostwood Estate](ref:kennywood/ghostwood-estate?bare), an interactive dark ride from 2008, you fire "Ghost Blasters" at ghosts in an eight-room mansion and collect points.

Kennywood has rebuilt Noah's Ark, which dates from 1936, twice: in 1969 it got the blue whale, and in 2016 a renovation. The Turtle has run since 1927 and got its sixth car and its turtle shapes in 1948.

[Raging Rapids](ref:kennywood/raging-rapids?bare), the Intamin river rapids ride from 1985, uses a natural slope and won't reopen until 2027. Outside Raging Rapids and Pittsburg\* Plunge there are drying stations where, for a small fee, you can get blown dry in about three minutes, a service you'd otherwise expect at a dog groomer's.

### Lost Kennywood

Lost Kennywood, opened in 1995, was the biggest single expansion in the park's history. It sits where the Sunlite Pool used to be, and it was modelled on Pittsburgh's Luna Park, which closed in 1909.

At its centre is [Pittsburg\* Plunge](ref:kennywood/pittsburg-plunge?bare), a shoot-the-chute from 1995 that also won't reopen until 2027. Three boats holding 20 people each plunge 50 feet (15 metres) into the pool. The name is missing its h because that's how the city used to be spelled.

The [Exterminator](ref:kennywood/exterminator?bare) is a wild mouse by Reverchon with spinning cars. It's run since 18 April 1999 inside a building dressed up as a factory taken over by mice. The highest point is 42.7 feet (13 metres), the cars reach 29.1 mph (47 km/h) on 420 metres of track, and a ride lasts 1:48 minutes. Nine cars with four seats each take 900 riders an hour through the building.

[Black Widow](ref:kennywood/black-widow?bare) has swung and spun its riders up to 146 feet (45 metres) since 2012, at up to 68 mph (109 km/h). It replaced the Pitt Fall drop tower, which ran from 1997 to 2011. SwingShot, also in Lost Kennywood, has flung riders at more than 50 mph (80 km/h) since 2006. The Wave Swinger is a Zierer ride and takes riders up to 220 lbs (100 kg).

The [Whip](ref:kennywood/whip?bare) came to Kennywood in 1919 with twelve cars, and in 1926 the park replaced it with today's model, which has 16. That one turned 100 in 2026 and got a new fence and an anniversary logo for the occasion.

### Area 412

The name Area 412 combines Area 51 with Pittsburgh's 412 area code, so since 2023 the area has been all about aliens. [Spinvasion](ref:kennywood/spinvasion?bare), the first Zamperla Gryphon in the US, has spun there since it opened. In Cosmic Chaos, a UFO has ridden up a U-shaped track to 50 feet (15 metres) since 2007.

### Kiddieland and Kennywood Junction

Kiddieland has been in its current spot since 1927, on the edge of the bluff on the river side of the park. The smallest coaster runs there, [Lil' Phantom](ref:kennywood/lil-phantom?bare), a Moli-Coaster by Molina & Son's from 1996. Its five-car train carries ten kids, and the lap is over after 15 seconds. Kenny's Karousel, with 16 jumping and 10 standing horses and two chariots, has been part of Kiddieland since 1924 and won't reopen until 2027. The Red Baron, the Steel City Choppers and the Wacky Wheel are among the rides spinning nearby.

Kennywood Junction arrived on the season's opening day, 12 April 2025. Kennywood describes it as a small neighbourhood of a steel town with five family rides: Olde Kennywood Railroad, Fire Bustin' Brigade, Coal Haulin' Convoy, Parker's Cloud Cruisers and Kenny's Cargo Drop.

The locomotive of the [Olde Kennywood Railroad](ref:kennywood/olde-kennywood-railroad?bare), the Gimbels Flyer, ran at the 1939 New York World's Fair and has been at Kennywood since 1945, and the Kenny Express is a new addition. From the train you look out over the Monongahela River and the Edgar Thomson Works, Pittsburgh's oldest steelworks. In winter it goes by Gingerbread Express.

## Height requirements

Kennywood gives the limits in inches. You can get measured at the Safety Center at the main entrance or at the Guest Service Center by Noah's Ark.

- 52 inches (132 cm): Steel Curtain (196 cm at most), Sky Rocket (with a companion only, up to 140 cm), Black Widow, Thunderbolt
- 50 inches (127 cm): Musik Express
- 48 inches (122 cm): Phantom's Revenge, Aero 360, Cosmic Chaos, Spinvasion, SwingShot, Potato Smash as a driver
- 46 inches (117 cm): Racer, Wave Swinger, Exterminator (with a companion only, up to 150 cm)
- 43 inches (109 cm): Raging Rapids (with a companion only, up to 130 cm)
- 42 inches (107 cm): Jack Rabbit and Kangaroo (both with a companion only, up to 122 cm), Kenny's Cargo Drop, Potato Smash as a passenger (with a responsible person up to 122 cm)
- 39 inches (99 cm): Pirate (with a companion only, up to 122 cm)
- 36 inches (91 cm): Pittsburg\* Plunge (with a companion only, up to 117 cm), Red Baron and Steel City Choppers (142 cm at most), Wacky Wheel, Whippersnapper and Kenny's Karousel (132 cm at most), Whirlwind (122 cm at most)
- no minimum, with a companion under 46 inches (117 cm): Auto Race, Ghostwood Estate, Merry-Go-Round, Noah's Ark, Old Mill, Olde Kennywood Railroad, Paddle Boats, Turtle, Whip
- no minimum, with a companion under 42 inches (107 cm): Crazy Trolley, Turtle Chase
- no minimum, with a companion under 36 inches (91 cm): Lil' Phantom, Coal Haulin' Convoy, Dizzy Dynamo, Fire Bustin' Brigade, Parker's Cloud Cruisers

A companion has to be at least 14 and tall enough to ride alone. Babies aren't allowed on Auto Race, Ghostwood Estate, Noah's Ark, Old Mill, the Paddle Boats, the Turtle or the Whip. With Parent Swap, two adults ride one after the other while one of them stays with the child.

### Holiday Lights 2026

Holiday Lights, which started in 2011, runs in 2026 from 13 November to 3 January 2027, on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays until mid-December and every day after that. The park is closed on 24 and 25 December, and on Thanksgiving, 26 November. It's open on these days and at these times:

- in November, the 13th to 15th, 20th to 22nd and 27th to 29th
- in December, the 4th to 6th, 11th to 13th, 18th to 23rd and 26th to 31st
- in January 2027, the 1st to 3rd
- Fridays from 5 to 9 p.m., 18 December included
- Saturdays and Sundays from 3 to 9 p.m.
- the Friday after Thanksgiving and every day from 19 December, from 3 to 9 p.m.

The Christmas tree is just under 100 feet (almost 30 metres) tall and carries more than 250,000 lights. The park says there's no taller Christmas tree in Pennsylvania either, which gives Kennywood two of the state's height records at once. It's switched on every evening at 5:45 p.m. between Aero 360 and the Pizza Warehouse.

New for 2026 is "Finding Christmas: A Whimsical Journey on Ice", an ice show with singing, dancing and BMX stunts on the Garden Stage. Also on the schedule:

- Finding Christmas: 6:45 and 8:30 p.m., plus 4:30 p.m. on days the park opens at 3
- A Broadway Christmas, also on the Garden Stage: 6:15 and 7:45 p.m.
- the Holiday Reflections light show at the Lagoon: every half hour from 6 to 8:30 p.m.
- the Snowy Sing Along until 3 January
- performances by local groups until 22 December
- "'Twas the Night Before Christmas", read by well-known Pittsburghers, on the Kennyville Stage from 20 November to 22 December

In Christmastown a walk-through takes you to Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer and his friends Clarice, Bumble and Sam the Snowman. Back again are the Christmas village in Kennywood Junction, the Gingerbread Express, Santa in the Celebration Station and the Jingle Bell Jamboree dance party. From 20 November there's a model railway in the Penny Arcade, a fire burns by Noah's Ark, and a pop-up post office takes letters to Santa.

More than 20 rides operate, some of them only above 40 °F (4 °C):

- coasters: Jack Rabbit, the oldest in the park, and Lil' Phantom, the smallest; the other six don't run in winter
- classics: Merry-Go-Round, Noah's Ark, Turtle, Kangaroo, Olde Kennywood Railroad as the Gingerbread Express
- other rides: Musik Express, Pirate, Potato Smash, Spinvasion
- for kids: Coal Haulin' Convoy, Crazy Trolley, Dizzy Dynamo, Fire Bustin' Brigade, Kenny's Cargo Drop, Parker's Cloud Cruisers, Red Baron, Steel City Choppers, Turtle Chase, Wacky Wheel, Whirlwind

Holiday Lights is nominated for best theme park holiday event in the USA Today 10Best awards. Voting runs from 2 to 30 November, and the winners are announced on 3 December. At Holiday Lights, guests aged 15 and under need a companion aged 21 or over with ID the whole time.

### The 2026 and 2027 seasons

From 18 April to 10 May 2026 the park held its first Celebrate Kennywood Weekends, on Saturdays and Sundays, with food built around the potato. The Bites and Pints food festival followed from Friday to Sunday between 22 May and 28 June, and on Monday 25 May, with stands from France and Germany. For the 250th birthday of the US, Zambelli set off what the park calls the biggest fireworks show in Kennywood's history on 4 July, and on the weekends from 11 to 26 July more than 300 drones went up.

Local marching bands have walked in the Fall Fantasy Parades since 1955, more than 180 of them today. In 2026 the parades ran every day from 1 to 17 August and on 22 August. Phantom Fall Fest, the Halloween event since 2021, ran on selected days until 1 November, and in the 2026 USA Today 10Best awards it came fourth among theme park Halloween events.

After 3 January 2027 there are no operating days in the calendar until the end of May, and Kennywood hasn't published dates for summer 2027 yet. The park marks Raging Rapids, Pittsburg\* Plunge, the Paddle Boats and Kenny's Karousel as "Reopens in 2027".

## Speedy Pass

Speedy Pass is a virtual queue on your phone. Depending on the tier, the virtual wait is as long as the regular one or shorter:

- Standard: the virtual wait is as long as the regular one, but you can spend it somewhere else.
- Premium: up to 50 percent less waiting
- Elite: up to 90 percent less waiting
- One Shot: immediate entry at a single ride


Speedy Pass Thrills covers Aero 360, Black Widow, Exterminator, Jack Rabbit, Kangaroo, Old Mill (Saturdays and Sundays until 6 p.m.), Phantom's Revenge, Racer, Sky Rocket, Spinvasion, Steel Curtain (Elite and Premium only), SwingShot and the Turtle. The booth is just past the entrance tunnel, next to Sky Rocket.

During Phantom Fall Fest, Speedy Pass Thrills cost from $10 for a set date and from $50 for any day, and for the six haunts there was Speedy Pass Chills from $35. For Holiday Lights, the Speedy Pass is marked "Coming Soon" on the Extras page, and the Holiday Lights page says it'll cover selected rides. Of the Speedy Pass rides, Jack Rabbit, Kangaroo, Spinvasion and Turtle run in winter.

The Gold Pass gets you 15 percent off Speedy Pass Thrills in the park and Platinum 20 percent, though not on One Shots. Kennywood introduced the Speedy Pass in 2022 as the successor to the V.I.P. Coaster Tour.

I'd ride the morning without a Speedy Pass and decide in the afternoon. If the queue at Steel Curtain is long by then, only Premium or Elite will help, because Standard doesn't save you a single minute at the ride itself.

## A day in the park

Say you're at the gate at opening on a summer Saturday and you haven't got a Speedy Pass. This is the order I'd ride in:

- at opening, Steel Curtain in Steelers Country
- then Phantom's Revenge in Kenny Lane and, if it's running, Thunderbolt in Kennyville
- Ghostwood Estate in Kennyville
- the Exterminator and Black Widow in Lost Kennywood
- Jack Rabbit and the Old Mill in the Good Time Midway
- at lunchtime, fries from the Potato Patch in Kennyville
- in the afternoon, Racer and the classics by the Lagoon, plus Sky Rocket
- from 2027, Raging Rapids and Pittsburg\* Plunge to finish, when the queues at the water rides are shorter

For a Holiday Lights evening on a weekend I'd arrive at 3 p.m., ride Jack Rabbit first while it's still light, and then catch the early performance of Finding Christmas. After the tree lighting, A Broadway Christmas fits in between two runs of Holiday Reflections at the Lagoon.

You can bring bags of up to 15 inches (38 cm) per side into the park, plus diaper bags and medical bags. Outside food and drink aren't allowed, apart from a sealed bottle of water and food for special dietary needs. There's a weapons check at the entrance. The park is cashless, so you pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and free machines turn cash into a card.

## Tickets

The regular price for Holiday Lights is $49.99. Kennywood sells these tickets online:

- any day, in the flash sale until 8 November: $19.99
- with a set date: from $24.99
- any day: $44.99
- groups of 10 to 99 people: $17.99 until 16 November
- students with a university email address: $19.99

Kids aged three and under get in free, and the discounted prices are only available online. Kennywood isn't selling day tickets for summer 2027 yet, and no ticket can be refunded. Every ticket does come with a Rainy Day Guarantee, though: if the weather ruins your visit, you get a free ticket for another day.

The 2027 season passes are already on sale:

- Silver for $99.99 instead of $129.99: admission in summer 2027, one guest ticket, 10 percent off food and merchandise
- Gold for $124.99 instead of $169.99: the rest of 2026 including Holiday Lights, all of 2027 with Phantom Fall Fest and Holiday Lights, Preferred and V.I.P. Parking, a souvenir cup, two guest tickets and 15 percent off
- Platinum for $189.99 instead of $249.99: Holiday Lights 2026 and all of 2027 at Kennywood, Sandcastle, Idlewild & SoakZone, Dutch Wonderland and seven parks in other states, parking at all four Pennsylvania parks, three guest tickets and 20 percent off

For a single winter evening I'd buy the dated ticket and only go for the Gold Pass once you know you're coming back in 2027.

Under Bronze, Kennywood advertises "Get a Silver Season Pass for the Price of a Bronze", and the page doesn't show a Bronze price of its own. Bronze holders are blocked out on 10, 17 and 24 July 2027. You can pay for the Silver, Gold and Platinum passes in eight instalments. The seven other Platinum parks are Lake Compounce in Connecticut, Noah's Ark Waterpark in Wisconsin, Adventureland in Iowa, Story Land, the Living Shores Aquarium and Water Country in New Hampshire, and Splish Splash in New York. The pass holder discount on food doesn't apply to alcohol, to Auntie Anne's or to Rogue BBQ.

## Parking

Parking at Gate A is free, but it's further from the entrance. All-Day Preferred Parking gets you closer from $20, paid online in advance, and you drive in at Gate A or B from Kennywood Boulevard. Gate B also has the spaces for vehicles with a disability permit, and Gate C is for short stops, pick-up and drop-off. Buses and RVs park free at Gate D. Online, Kennywood lists V.I.P. Parking as "coming soon", and Gold and Platinum holders will use their own entrance for it at Gate E, from Hoffman Boulevard.

## Getting there

The address for your GPS is 4800 Kennywood Boulevard, West Mifflin, PA 15122. The park is on Route 837, about 12 miles (19 km) southeast of downtown Pittsburgh by Kennywood's count. By car, take I-376 to exit 77, Edgewood-Swissvale, turn left and follow the arrows for Kennywood along South Braddock Avenue. After about 3 miles (5 km), past the Rankin Bridge, turn left onto Route 837, and after 1.6 miles (2.6 km) the parking lot is on your right.

Without a car, Pittsburgh Regional Transit's 61C bus runs via Oakland, Squirrel Hill, Homestead, Munhall and West Mifflin to the park and on to Duquesne and McKeesport. When the park is open, the bus stops in the parking lot at Lot D, and otherwise on Kennywood Boulevard. A ride costs $2.75 in cash.

## Food

The Potato Patch has sold its fries since 1973. They're cut fresh from Idaho potatoes, and you choose your topping from cheddar, bacon, vinegar, gravy or seasoned salt. Small Fry's in Lost Kennywood is completely gluten-free and sells Potato Patch fries too. The park doesn't fry in peanut oil.

In 2026 the Centennial Grill opened in Lost Kennywood next to the Whip, with cheesesteaks and potato tots. The new Big Dipper ice cream stand next to Noah's Ark is the park's nod to the Dipper, a coaster that ran from 1948 to 1984.

At the Golden Nugget you get vanilla ice cream in a cone, dipped in chocolate and topped with sprinkles or chopped peanuts and a cherry. The stand survived the Ghost Ship fire in 1975. Lucky has been around since the 1930s and sells funnel cakes these days. Kandy Kaleidoscope has homemade fudge and hand-dipped candy apples, and the Star Refreshment Stand deep-fries Oreos. The Parkside Café was originally the park's casino, and the Carousel Burger Co. in the Good Time Midway is housed in the pavilion of the original 19th-century Merry-Go-Round.

The other stands are 1898 Coffee Co., Auntie Anne's, the Beer Garden, Dippin' Dots, Fire-Breathing Sausage, Jeeters' Pub, the Midway Refreshment Stand, the Pagoda Creamery, Pedro's, the Pizza Warehouse, Rogue BBQ and Snack-A-Saurus.

For Holiday Lights 2026 there's turkey dinner, chicken pot pie, red velvet funnel cake, peppermint soft serve, candy cane milkshakes, cranberry orange cinnamon rolls and hot chocolate with everything on top. Dinner with Santa at the End Zone Cafe isn't included in a park ticket and takes place on selected days from 20 November to 23 December. It costs $29.99 from age ten, $24.99 for ages four to nine and nothing for three and under, plus tax in each case, and pass holders pay $24.99.

## History

In 1818 Charles Kenny bought the land above the Monongahela River. Around the time of the Civil War, "Kenny's Grove" was a popular picnic spot. In December 1898 Anthony Kenny leased the grounds to the Monongahela Street Railway, which the banker Andrew Mellon had a stake in. Mellon chose the name Kennywood, and George S. Davidson, the railway's chief engineer, designed the layout. The park opened on 30 May 1899.

The first coaster, the Figure Eight Toboggan, arrived in 1902. In 1906 A.S. McSwigan and F.W. Henninger took over the park, and their families ran it for a whole century, by the 2007 season in the fourth and fifth generations. When the Turtle arrived in 1927, it ran under the name Tumble Bug at first. The streetcar line Kennywood was once built for ran until 15 September 1958, when route 68 switched to buses.

Kenny Kangaroo first appeared as the mascot in 1974. In 1980 came the first steel coaster, Laser Loop, which ran until 1990. The families bought Idlewild in Ligonier in 1983 and built the Sandcastle water park in Homestead in 1989. In 1988 WQED aired Rick Sebak's documentary "Kennywood Memories". A Pennsylvania state historical marker has stood for the park since 5 August 1992, and the 2009 film "Adventureland" was shot at Kennywood.

In December 2007 the families sold Kennywood and its sister parks to Palace Entertainment, a subsidiary of Parques Reunidos in Madrid, and the park changed hands in 2008. On 18 March 2025 Herschend Family Entertainment and Parques Reunidos signed the agreement under which Herschend took over all of Palace Entertainment's US parks, Kennywood included. The general manager of Kennywood and Sandcastle is Ricky Spicuzza, who has worked at the park since 2002.

For its 125th anniversary in 2022 and 2023, Kennywood renovated the whole park, and in 2023 Area 412 replaced the Volcano Valley area. In 2024 Jeeters came back as a mascot, with a pub of his own, and the Gran Prix bumper cars became Potato Smash. Thomas Town, which opened on 27 July 2018 as an extension of Kiddieland, lost its Thomas theme after the 2024 season and became Kennywood Junction. RCDB lists eleven coasters at Kennywood that are gone, among them Teddy Bear (1935 to 1947), Tickler (1931 to 1952) and Wild Mouse (1958 to 1960).

## Frequently asked questions about Kennywood

### Where can I see Kennywood wait times live?

The [Kennywood park page](ref:kennywood) on park.fan has the current wait times for the park's rides and, for each ride, whether it's running right now. All eight coasters are included.

### What rides are open at Kennywood today?

You'll find each ride's status live on the [park page](ref:kennywood). During Holiday Lights, from 13 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, more than 20 rides operate, Jack Rabbit and Lil' Phantom among them, but none of the other coasters. Raging Rapids, Pittsburg\* Plunge, the Paddle Boats and Kenny's Karousel won't reopen until 2027.

### How busy is Kennywood today?

Kennywood is closed from 2 to 12 November 2026. From 13 November it's open on Holiday Lights days, and the expected crowd level for each day is on the [park page](ref:kennywood).

### Is there a crowd calendar for Kennywood?

Yes, the [wait-time calendar](ref:kennywood?calendar) has the expected crowd level for every operating day. Kennywood itself names Sundays in May, Memorial Day, weekdays in late June and all of July as the quietest times.

### What are Kennywood's hours?

During Holiday Lights, Kennywood is open on Fridays from 5 to 9 p.m., on Saturdays, Sundays and the Friday after Thanksgiving from 3 to 9 p.m., and every day from 19 December from 3 to 9 p.m., but not on Thanksgiving or on 24 and 25 December. The last day is 3 January 2027.

### How long is the wait for Steel Curtain?

The current wait time is on the [Steel Curtain](ref:kennywood/the-steel-curtain?bare) page on park.fan. By our measurements it pays to ride Steel Curtain right at opening. The Speedy Pass covers it only in the Premium and Elite tiers.

### How long is the wait for the Exterminator?

The current wait time is on the [Exterminator](ref:kennywood/exterminator?bare) page on park.fan. By our measurements an early start helps here too, if not by much. The Exterminator is included in Speedy Pass Thrills.

### How much is a ticket for Kennywood?

For Holiday Lights, dated tickets cost from $24.99 online and any-day tickets $44.99, otherwise $49.99. Kids aged three and under are free. For summer 2027 only season passes are on sale so far, and the 2027 Gold Pass for $124.99 includes Holiday Lights 2026.

### How much is the Speedy Pass at Kennywood?

During Phantom Fall Fest, Speedy Pass Thrills cost from $10 for a set date and from $50 for any day. Kennywood has announced it for selected rides at Holiday Lights but hasn't given a price yet. Premium cuts the wait by up to 50 percent, Elite by up to 90.

### How tall do you have to be for the coasters?

Steel Curtain, Sky Rocket and Thunderbolt require 52 inches (132 cm), and Steel Curtain takes riders up to 196 cm. Phantom's Revenge takes riders from 122 cm, Racer and the Exterminator from 117 cm, and the Jack Rabbit from 107 cm. Lil' Phantom has no minimum height, and kids under 91 cm ride with a companion.
