## New

- A show goes into the day planner as an entry of its own, fixed to the start time of the
  performance.
- "My favourites" names the quietest open day of the next 14 under each park.
- The footer offers to install park.fan as an app, where the browser supports it.

## Improved

- The planner counts the walk to a show and away from it. After a show or a free block it no
  longer adds the time for leaving the exit and for getting to the next ride.
- Every ride is counted as 5 minutes in the planner.
- "Add to plan" no longer puts a ride after closing time. It opens the fit assistant with the ride
  you pressed.
- The glossary has 3D players for block brake, trim brake and turntable.
- The park page has one sentence under "Crowds now" that says what the crowd level means. The height
  filter also sits in the "With kids" block.
- 103 cities with more than one park have an introduction sentence. The Germany country page shows
  the ranking guide.
- Hub and tool pages have one primary button and the same header.
- The location banner on a phone is 147 to 181 px high instead of 202 to 252 px. The home page
  loads 373 KB of script on a first visit on a phone, down to 329 KB, and the planner photos go
  through the image optimiser.
- An unknown path shows the 404 page with the header and the footer.
- The planner page says in its lead that the planner is free.

## Fixed

- The favourites band no longer hides a remove button. The alert threshold shows in full, in 24
  of 24 rows where 14 were cut off, and Escape no longer pulls the focus out of a field.
- The planner handle responds where it is drawn, and on an iPhone SE held sideways the time axis
  is visible: 252 px high, where it was 3 px.
- The rope drop card stays on one line in French, Italian and Spanish. The monthly table no longer
  cuts off a number on a phone: 11 rows ran over at 320 px, none do now.
- "My favourites", "How park.fan works" and "Contribute" no longer jump while loading. On a phone
  the layout shift score of "My favourites" fell from 0.4214 to 0, and the guide's from 0.9882 to
  0.0118.
- The Europa-Park page opens its blog section with the Europa-Park guide again. A dark strip
  before the footer is gone.
