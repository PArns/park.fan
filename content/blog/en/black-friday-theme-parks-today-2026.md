It's Black Friday, and today is the last day for the Black Deal on Wintertraum tickets at [Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand), for Six Flags' 2027 Silver Passes in North America and for the Black Nights at Fårup Sommerland's hotel. The Phantasialand hotels, Walibi Belgium and Energylandia follow on Sunday. On Monday, 30 November, the annual pass deals at Heide Park, LEGOLAND Deutschland and Walibi Holland end. FORT FUN only started its Black FUN-Days today. If you're still choosing an annual pass, our [overview from Monday](/news/black-friday-theme-parks-2026) compares them.

## Germany

Phantasialand's Black Deal ticket is only valid on the day you book, and every day has its own allocation. Wintertraum runs until 24 January 2027, mostly from 11 am to 8 pm, and until 6 pm on New Year's Eve. The park doesn't say what time the deal ends, and its website warns that prices can go up at any time. €29 still gets you in from 7 to 11 and from 15 to 18 December, and on most weekdays in January. 12 December is sold out, and the other December days now cost €31 to €37 in the shop, with 5 and 6 December the dearest. The park is closed on 24 and 25 December, on 1 January and on 12 and 13 January.

Matamba and Ling Bao take the BLACKDEAL code on top of their already reduced early-booking prices. You can only use it on the dates marked black in the booking calendar, and the button on the hotel pages fills it in for you. Ling Bao includes the pool and spa, and both hotels include breakfast. The Hotel Charles Lindbergh page doesn't mention the code. Fantissima tickets get you the show and a four-course dinner on selected dates from January to June. The extra €10 off the already discounted advance tickets is taken off the shop price. The tile on the park's homepage still advertises "from €89", while the ticket shop has the new price.

The Merlin Platinum annual pass is the same pass at [Heide Park](ref:heide-park) and at [LEGOLAND Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland); LEGOLAND just crosses out a higher regular price. Renewing a Basic or a Gold costs as much as buying new, and renewing a Platinum costs €99. The Basic has 35 blackout days at Heide Park and 36 at LEGOLAND, while the Heide Park Gold has two. With the Heide Park Gold you can buy up to four friend tickets at €29 each and get 15% off overnight stays. At Heide Park, parking for Basic and Gold holders is a €35 add-on. LEGOLAND's Gold also works at PEPPA PIG Park and at WinterWonder.

Until Monday, both parks also sell overnight stays with two days' entry for 25% less. At Heide Park it's from €60 instead of €80 per person at the Adventure Hotel or the Holiday Camp, with breakfast and dinner buffet, for stays until 30 June 2027. You can't arrive from 26 to 29 March, from 6 to 8 May or on 15 and 16 May 2027, and there's no cancellation cover. At LEGOLAND the package costs from €72 instead of €96 per person, with breakfast and parking, for stays from 20 March to 30 June 2027.

The [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany) annual pass is valid from the day you buy it, so it covers [Hollywood Christmas](/news/movie-park-hollywood-christmas-2026) too. If you already have one, you can only renew it at the park, not in the web shop: from 27 to 29 November and from 4 to 6 December at the info desk or the Visitor Welcome Center. Bronze then costs €74.90, Silver €96.90, Gold €111.90 and Platinum €129.90.

FORT FUN says its 2027 day ticket is more than 40% below the gate price, and the allocation is limited. It gets anyone 90 cm and taller in on every regular opening day in 2027 except the Hallow Eve SPECIAL. The Basic annual pass at €84.90, the Action at €109.90 and the Family at €329 run for 365 days from the day you first use them. A night at the Abenteuercamp with breakfast and day tickets costs 10% less; you pay when you arrive and can cancel for free.

Tripsdrill sells its vouchers for the day pass and the two-day pass only in the online shop, and you can redeem them at both the Erlebnispark and the Wildparadies. At the Natur-Resort you save on the daily rate for nights until 18 March 2027, with breakfast and the Wildparadies included and the Erlebnis packages left out.

A banner on the [Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland) page advertises "only €16 per ticket", which is for Majaland Deutschland in Haßloch. You use both tickets on the same day, on an opening day of the winter season until 14 March 2027. The Majaland Pass covers unlimited visits over the same period, but it's in your name.

## Netherlands and Belgium

At [Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland), groups of four or more get the Family & Friends deal: €60 per person for Explore and €76 for Experience. The offer page gives 30 November 2027 as the end date, which surely means this year. The [Avonturenpark Hellendoorn](ref:avonturenpark-hellendoorn) Flex-Ticket works on one regular opening day until 23 July 2027, with no reservation. On its offer page, Hellendoorn crosses out €33.50, a euro more than the regular price in the shop.

Slagharen's Dagticket Winter is for visits from 28 November to 3 January. The Goud and Platinum annual passes run until 9 January 2028, and selected winter stays are up to 35% cheaper until 14 December.

The [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) Walibi Pass runs for a year from 5 December 2026. The park sells no more than 18,000 passes at the Black Week price in total, and the premium passes are 15% off: Gold €344.25, Ruby €450.50, Diamond €620.50.

At [Bellewaerde](ref:bellewaerde), the Combi pass for the park and aquapark and the park pass are sold out, but some of the 500 aquapark passes are left. You buy the pass as a voucher and activate it at the ticket desk by 3 January 2027. The day tickets are for Bellewaerde Kerst from 5 December to 3 January, for the park from 27 March to 30 May 2027 and for the aquapark until the end of February 2027. There are 5,000 of each, with five per order at most.

For Plopsaland De Panne, Plopsa sells two nights in a villa at Plopsaland Village, with unlimited entry to the park and to Plopsaqua De Panne, and parking. The villa for four costs from €608 instead of €811 and the one for ten from €880 instead of €1,467. You arrive on a Monday, Wednesday or Friday, in the low and shoulder season until the end of 2027. For the Plopsaqua pools in De Panne and Landen-Hannut and the Plopsa Indoor parks in Hasselt in Belgium and Coevorden in the Netherlands, there's a family afternoon ticket for three to six people from €49, from 1 pm. It doesn't include Plopsaland itself.

The [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) Black November passes run for 365 days from purchase and aren't valid on the days marked red in the calendar. Anyone who had a 2026 pass also gets a personal €5 code through the Bonus Club, which you can combine with the Black November price.

## Rest of Europe

[Energylandia](ref:energylandia)'s Black Weeks prices also cover reduced tickets and two- and three-day tickets, ten per order at most and only on ticket.energylandia.pl. All tickets are valid from 4 December, and the [Winter Kingdom](/news/energylandia-winter-kingdom-2026) ticket until 31 January 2027.

Parc Astérix calls Black Friday "Vendredi Gaulois". The Billet Givré gets you in on one day of your choice from 19 December to 3 January, can't be refunded or exchanged, and children under three get in free. Without a fixed day, the Christmas ticket costs €57. Adults and children pay the same for Nigloland's spring ticket, which runs from 27 March to 2 May 2027, and the code NIGLOBLACK gets you the hotel discount.

PortAventura World's Black Days tickets with the code BLACKPAW26 are limited to 1,000, for adults only, and cover several days or several parks. They're blocked from 5 to 8, on 12 and 13 and from 19 to 31 December, and from 1 to 6 January. With Hotel Roulette you find out your four-star hotel the day before you arrive, and the booking can't be refunded. The Bonos for Parque Warner and Parque de Atracciones already cover the rest of 2026, and numbers are limited.

[Mirabilandia](ref:mirabilandia)'s 2027 passes cost the same new or as a renewal. Bronze is only valid until 24 October 2027 and not from 9 to 22 August. Park and hotel together are 10% cheaper until Tuesday, and tickets without a fixed date are up to €15 below full price. For Gardaland's Black Friday package you arrive between 30 March and 30 June 2027, just not on 1 May or 2 June.

Thorpe Park takes £20 off with the code SAVE20 for nights in the Shark Cabin between 20 April and 12 May 2027, with a day of Unlimited Fastrack. Chessington knocks off £30 with SAVE30, for selected nights in February and from 5 to 7 March 2027. You get the Alton Towers short-break price for nights from 22 January to 7 March 2027, and the LEGOLAND Windsor one for nights from 27 December to 18 March 2027. The Merlin Annual Pass is only at the Black Friday price as a new purchase, not as a renewal. The Paultons Park break deal is for nights until 1 November 2027 and includes parking and cancellation cover.

The new Essential season pass at [LEGOLAND Billund](ref:legoland-billund) isn't valid from 26 June to 8 August 2027 or on 17 more days in March, May and October. All 2027 passes already include December 2026. Fårup Sommerland only takes phone bookings for the Black Nights until 4 pm today, on 96 73 92 07. The [Gröna Lund](ref:grona-lund) Guldkortet gets you in all summer 2027, concerts included.

## USA and Canada

At Six Flags, the 2027 Silver Pass costs as much as a single day ticket today, so it's paid for itself from your second visit:

- $99: [Kings Island](ref:kings-island)
- $89: [Cedar Point](ref:cedar-point)
- $79: [Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain), [Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure)
- $75: [Dorney Park](ref:dorney-park)
- $69: [Carowinds](ref:carowinds), [Kings Dominion](ref:kings-dominion), [Six Flags Fiesta Texas](ref:six-flags-fiesta-texas), [Six Flags Over Texas](ref:six-flags-over-texas), [Six Flags Over Georgia](ref:six-flags-over-georgia), [Six Flags Great America](ref:six-flags-great-america)
- $59: [Six Flags Discovery Kingdom](ref:six-flags-discovery-kingdom), [Six Flags New England](ref:six-flags-new-england)
- $49: [Six Flags Darien Lake](ref:six-flags-darien-lake), [Frontier City](ref:six-flags-frontier-city)

The pass is mostly valid until Labor Day 2027. At Magic Mountain and Fiesta Texas it covers all of 2027, at Over Texas the rest of 2026 plus all of 2027, and at California's Great America the whole 2027 season. Most parks add a fee of up to $9.99 plus tax. At Kings Island, the bundle of Silver Pass and season dining and drinks plan costs $199 instead of $348.

Most parks count down to midnight Eastern Time, which is 6 am on Saturday in Germany, so an order placed tonight still goes through. Six Flags Great America and Fiesta Texas run an hour longer, Discovery Kingdom until 11:59 pm Pacific Time, 8:59 am Saturday in Germany. At [California's Great America](ref:great-america), the banner for the $65 pass says "Ends 11/27", but the countdown on the park's page ran out on Thursday at 11:59 pm Pacific Time, 8:59 am today German time. In early November Six Flags still gave 30 November as the end date; the park pages now say the 27th.

The [Canada's Wonderland](ref:canadas-wonderland) Silver Pass already includes WinterFest 2026. [Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) isn't discounting its passes. There, and at Cedar Point, Carowinds, Kings Dominion, Darien Lake and Schlitterbahn New Braunfels, the third hotel night is free if you book by Monday; at Cedar Point it's for nights from 1 May to 30 June 2027.

Five parks have left Six Flags and now run as Enchanted Parks: [Worlds of Fun](ref:worlds-of-fun), [Valleyfair](ref:valleyfair), [Michigan's Adventure](ref:michigans-adventure), [Mid America Adventure](ref:mid-america-parks), formerly Six Flags St. Louis, and [Great Escape](ref:six-flags-great-escape). Until Monday they're selling 2027 passes and tickets for less, the Explorer pass at Mid America Adventure from $55.99 and the Any Day Ticket at Worlds of Fun for $35.99. The two higher passes, Legend and Elite, work at all eight Enchanted Parks.

At [SeaWorld Orlando](ref:seaworld-orlando) and [Busch Gardens Tampa Bay](ref:busch-gardens-tampa), annual passes cost $198 instead of $279 for Silver, $222 instead of $315 for Gold and $351 instead of $549 for Platinum today. The SeaWorld Fun Card is valid until the end of 2027, with blackout days and without parking.

The [Lake Compounce](ref:lake-compounce) Gold includes parking. Gold and Platinum at [Kennywood](ref:kennywood) come with a guest ticket for Holiday Lights until 20 December. [Silver Dollar City](ref:silver-dollar-city) throws free refills in with the Gold for Black Friday, and the ten-month payment plan is only on offer until Monday. [Dollywood](ref:dollywood) counts you as a new customer if you haven't had a pass since the 2022 season. Until Monday, the park adds cinnamon bread, a TimeSaver ride and up to 30% off at the resort to Silver, Gold and Diamond.
