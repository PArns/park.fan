> [!UPDATE] 2026-11-27
> You can't combine the FUNTIME code with the Black Week price. Walibi now says so on the [sale page](https://www.walibi.be/nl/tickets/pass), next to the Walibi, Aqualibi and Red&Blue passes. A Walibi Pass runs for a year from 5 December, the first day of Walibi Winter. The five passes that include Aqualibi run for a year from the day you buy them. Walibi is selling no more than 18,000 annual passes in the sale.

[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) in Wavre is selling its annual passes at up to 35 percent off until Sunday, 29 November. During Black Week the basic Walibi Pass costs €100.75 instead of €155, and the three Premium passes, Gold, Ruby and Diamond, are 15 percent cheaper. The sale opened in the webshop today, while the park itself stays closed until Walibi Winter.

Walibi has put a waiting room in front of the shop, and a notice in it promises the same price on every day of the sale, so if the queue is long today you can close the tab and try again on Thursday. You only get into the shop when it's your turn, and you have to accept functional cookies for that. Once you're in, you have ten minutes to place your order, and if the clock runs out you're sent to the back of the queue.

| Pass            | List price | Black Week | Discount |

| Red&Blue        | €240       | €156.00    | 35%      |
| Premium Gold    | €405       | €344.25    | 15%      |
| Premium Ruby    | €530       | €450.50    | 15%      |

The list prices have applied since 1 March 2026.

## What each pass includes

| Pass     | Walibi | Aqualibi | Parking | Speedy                  | Haunted houses | Shops and restaurants |
| -------- | ------ | -------- | ------- | ----------------------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| Walibi   | yes    | no       | no      | no                      | no             | −10%                  |
| Aqualibi | no     | yes      | no      | no                      | no             | −10%                  |
| Red&Blue | yes    | yes      | no      | no                      | no             | −10%                  |
| Gold     | yes    | yes      | no      | 4 rides per visit       | no             | −10%                  |
| Ruby     | yes    | yes      | yes     | once per ride per visit | yes            | −20%                  |
| Diamond  | yes    | yes      | yes     | unlimited               | yes            | −20%                  |

Every pass except the Aqualibi Pass also works at the Nocturnes (the evening openings), at Halloween and during Walibi Winter. All six get you discounts at partner parks and on guest tickets for friends. Walibi sells Gold, Ruby and Diamond in limited numbers, so for once the names from the jewellery box are more than packaging. Gold holders use the Speedy One entrance three times per visit and the one at Tiki-Waka once. Ruby and Diamond holders take the Speedy entrance at 18 rides, Tiki-Waka, Kondaa and Loup-Garou among them.

With the Walibi, Aqualibi, Red&Blue and Gold passes you pay for parking on every visit, €13 per car. Pass holders can buy a parking pass in the park for €40 that takes two number plates, and from your fourth visit by car it costs less than paying each time. If an order has five Walibi, Aqualibi or Red&Blue passes in it, the code FUNTIME takes half off the fifth. Walibi's pass page doesn't say whether the code works on top of the Black Week price.

For most people making [the drive to Wavre from Germany](/blog/walibi-belgium-wait-times-tips), I'd take the basic Walibi Pass. A day ticket for 2027 costs from €45 and a Walibi Winter day €57 at the full price, so the pass has paid for itself after two winter days or three visits next season. Red&Blue only beats the Walibi Pass if you go to Aqualibi next door at least three times, since a visit there costs from €27.50 for anyone 1.20 m or taller. Children under one metre don't need anything for either park.

## When the pass starts

The pass is valid for twelve months from the date you buy it. Because the park is closed right now, though, the [pass terms](https://www.walibi.be/nl/reglement/pass-reglement) say your year only starts on the next opening day of the park the pass is for. It isn't valid on sold-out days or on days the park is hired out privately. It can't be cancelled either.

## Walibi Winter and the last rides on Loup-Garou

The next date in the park is the Walibi Winter Run on Friday, 4 December, a timed evening run through the lit-up park. The day after, the fourth edition of [Walibi Winter](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026) begins, and it runs until 3 January 2027. There's a toboggan run, an ice show and a Christmas market, the 4D cinema shows a Christmas film, and from Saturday, 19 December, you can skate on a 630 m² rink under a roof. An early-bird day ticket costs €45 instead of €57. Pass holders never need to book.

On 3 January, [Loup-Garou](ref:walibi-belgium/loup-garou) runs for the last time in its current form. After that, Rocky Mountain Construction [rebuilds the wooden coaster into a new ride](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027). It comes back in 2028 as part of Festival World, an area themed on New Orleans.

The 2027 season starts on Saturday, 27 March, from 10 am to 6 pm. The park is open until 29 March and then again from 1 to 4 April. By car you take exit 6 on the E411 motorway between Brussels and Namur, and by train you get off at Bierges-Walibi station, 150 metres from the entrance.
