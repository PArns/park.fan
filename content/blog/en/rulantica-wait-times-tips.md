Europa-Park hibernates from 11 January to 19 March 2027. Every coaster and
themed area is shut. [Rulantica](ref:rulantica) next door stays open all that
time, every day from 9:30 a.m., with the air in the hall at 30 to 34 degrees
and the water at 27 to 32.

How busy it gets then is something we've measured. The queues at the slides are
short, though at a few of them you'll still notice one.

## History and construction of Rulantica

"Rulantica" is a made-up word from **Rust** and **Atlantic**. Manfred Gotta
coined it, and the operator, Europa-Park, had the trademark secured in April 2014. By 8 July 2014 it was settled that the project would become a water park,
and in October 2014 the trade site Parkerlebnis reported it would turn out
smaller than first announced. The project was approved in March 2016, building
of the Krønasår hotel began in May 2016, and work on the water park itself
started on 14 September 2017.

Costs grew during construction. When building began, the Badische Zeitung put
them at around €150 million, after the topping-out of the hall in December 2018 the
figure was €160 million, and by the opening on **28 November 2019** it had risen
to €180 million, according to Wikipedia. Even at the €150 million of 2017,
Wikipedia calls Rulantica the most expensive investment in Europa-Park's
history. The hall is 20 metres high, has a roof area of 10,500 square metres and
encloses around 300,000 cubic metres of air. The mast holding up the wooden roof
is made of spruce and weighs 50 tonnes. Solar cells shade the car parks and
supply about a quarter of the electricity.

Rulantica is an invented island in the Norwegian Sea, home to trolls, merfolk and
Vikings, and its mascot is **Snorri**, a blue sixtopus, an octopus with six
arms. A musical ran at the Europa-Park Teatro in May 2018, and Coppenrath
published three novels by Michaela Hanauer. The Krønasår hotel opposite is
designed like a natural history museum. The restaurant Bubba Svens houses the
"Wiking Saga", the boat in which Burghard Pieske crossed the Atlantic.

The water world shut during the pandemic and reopened on **10 June 2020** with a
hygiene plan. The sauna and wellness area Hyggedal followed in October 2020. New
outdoor areas totalling 9,000 square metres were added in 2021, the water
carousel Tønnevirvel in 2022, and in 2023 the slide tower Nordiskturn with
Vikingløp. Since early summer 2025 the outdoor area has had a pool of its own,
the Svømmepøl. According to TEA and AECOM, around 1.2 million guests visited
in 2023, and at the start the Süddeutsche Zeitung gave 6,000 guests a day as
the upper limit.

## The themed areas

Rulantica is divided into themed areas. The park map lists them all, and these
are the main ones.

**Rangnakor** is the "city on stilts", four metres above the ground. The big
slides run down from here, and the Comfort Hyddas look out over the whole hall.
**Vinterhal** is the ice palace, built around the sea serpent Svalgur, frozen
solid, whose body carries the slides. The **Frigg Tempel** has the heated
outdoor pool with its swim-up bar, and the water carousel stands here too.
**Lumåfals** is the cave with a waterfall, a wave pool and a lazy river, and the
**Skog Lagune** has bubble loungers under pine trees.

For little ones there's **Trølldal**, with shallow water and small slides, and
the **Skip Strand**, where you can climb on a sunken three-master and the
remains of a steamer. The **Nordiskturn** holds the water world's biggest slide
complex, 30 metres up.

Three areas are outdoors and tied to the season: **Svalgurok** with ten slides,
**Dynstrønd** with a beach and VIP yachts, and the **Snorri Strand** with a
water playground. The park gives May to September for them and says on its
pages that they're available daily again from 1 May 2027. Everything this guide
covers indoors is open all year.

ProSlide built eight of the ten slides, and Aquarena the two drop slides. The

carousel, through 95 on the smallest slides, up to the 140 of Dugdrob and
Vildfål. For Hugin, Munin, Stormvind and Svalgur Rytt, the park's page says that
children between 110 and 125 centimetres may ride only with an adult. Stormrok
is outdoors and closed until 1 May 2027.

### Dugdrob and Vildfål: the drop capsules

The two tallest slides stand side by side in Rangnakor, and they're twins. You
step into a glass capsule, a countdown runs, and the floor drops away. You fall
twenty metres down the tube, over a stretch of 121 metres. Sections of acrylic
glass are built into the tube, so you can see outside as you fall. The capsule
holds one person. The theoretical maximum is 150 guests an hour per slide, the
lowest figure of any slide in the table.

For safety reasons, burkinis and swim shirts aren't allowed on either slide.
Glasses are out here too, and action cameras on head mounts are banned at
Dugdrob, Vildfål and Isbrekker. The park offers action photos instead, at
Dugdrob, Vildfål and the Schabernakker I and II slides.

### Hugin and Munin: the duel

The two slides run in parallel, are known together as Odinrås and are 148 metres
long. You ride in a two-person tyre. Every so often the dividing wall between
the tubes is open, so you see the tyre next door, and the runout decides who was
ahead. Children under eight who reach 110 centimetres ride with an adult, and so
does anyone under 125 centimetres. At 300 guests an hour per slide, the duel on
paper clears people faster than the drop capsules.

### Svalgur Rytt and Vinter Rytt: the sea serpent and the half-pipe

[Svalgur Rytt](ref:rulantica/svalgur-rytt) has one of the biggest tyres in the
complex. Up to four people ride 208 metres together through the body of the
frozen sea serpent. At 960 guests an hour it's the slide with the highest
capacity on paper. The park admits children from 110 centimetres, and those
under 125 centimetres need an adult with them.

Right next to it is [Vinter Rytt](ref:rulantica/vinter-rytt), the half-pipe. The
start in the four-person tyre is gentle, then comes a steep descent, and the tyre
shoots up the wall on the far side. It's the only slide in the complex with a
weight limit, 320 kilograms per tyre. The minimum height is 125 centimetres, and
you have to be ten. In our data these two slides top the wait times. The table
further down shows how big the gap is.

### Stormvind: the drum

In [Stormvind](ref:rulantica/stormvind) the tyre sets off slowly, followed by a
quick descent. The high point is a circular drum where the tyre turns for at
least one full lap before the next tube takes it. It's 136 metres long and 15
metres high, with a two-person tyre and 360 guests an hour. The park admits
children from ten years and 110 centimetres, and those under 125 centimetres
need an adult.

### Isbrekker: the springboard

Isbrekker is two jump slides in the ice palace, four metres high and 13.5 metres
long. At the end, according to the park, you're catapulted into the pool. The
minimum height is 125 centimetres and the age ten, it's for swimmers only, and
anyone under ten rides only under the supervision of a guardian. It handles 240
guests an hour.

### Vikingløp: head first

The park calls [Vikingløp](ref:rulantica/vikinglop) in the Nordiskturn the
biggest speed slide in Europe. Eight racing lanes run side by side. You lie on
a mat head first and race the guests in the other lanes. At the bottom there's a
grandstand where spectators cheer on the winner. Under the park's rules your
arms and elbows stay on the mat, and so do your legs and knees. In the bends you
hold the mat with both hands, and shortly before the finish you lift your feet.
Glasses aren't allowed here. The theoretical maximum is 600 guests an hour, the
minimum height is 110 centimetres, and the minimum age is ten.

## Water without a slide

If you're after something different once you've climbed stairs for three hours,
here it is.

[Snorri's Saga](ref:rulantica/snorris-saga) is a current channel. You sit in a
single tyre, lean back and drift through the whole water world, past the
merfolk, through a colourful underwater cave, under the city on stilts and
along the shipwrecks. The minimum height is 125 centimetres and the age eight;
younger children ride with an adult. Unlike the slides, the ride is suitable
for pregnant women.

[Tønnevirvel](ref:rulantica/tonnevirvel) is a spinning ride from MACK Rides. The
type is called Twist 'n' Splash, and according to the park this is the first
one in a water park anywhere in the world. Nine round boats for up to six people
each spin on three underwater platforms, and every passenger gets a water
pistol. People who stay on dry land can join in the shooting too. A ride lasts
three minutes, and the theoretical capacity is 1,080 guests an hour. The
carousel has since been moved under a roof and runs all year. The minimum
height is 90 centimetres and the age three, with an adult for anyone under 120
centimetres.

The [Vildstrøm](ref:rulantica/vildstrom) is a raging river. Children under 125
centimetres or under eight may use it only with a life jacket and an adult.
There are loungers on the bank for anyone who needs a break from the rapids. The
**Surf Fjørd** is the wave pool in the middle of the water world, and the
**Hoppablad** is an obstacle course on the water where you can't wear glasses.

**Snorri Snorkling VR – The New Adventure** is by the Skog Lagune. You get VR
diving goggles with a snorkel and swim through sunken shipwrecks to the
mermaids' village in a pool 1.10 metres deep, while the goggles show the scene
to match. A ride takes six minutes, and two pools side by side hold up to four
people. It's an add-on that costs **€6**, bookable with your ticket or on the
spot if there's room. You don't have to book a fixed time slot. Swimmers aged ten
and over and 125 centimetres tall are admitted, and the park gives the capacity
as 40 guests an hour. Wheelchairs can't get in, and if you wear glasses you take
them off, or wear them under the VR goggles if the fit allows. According to the
park the station is open daily from 12:00 to 9:00 p.m.

## When to go

Our measurement series for Rulantica covers 287 days between 6 October 2025 and
6 October 2026. That's enough for the months from January to September.
December and October rest on only seven days each, so I read them as a hint,
not a result.

July and August are the busiest months, August by a long way. December is as
busy as the summer. January and March were the quietest, and February and May
were quiet too. Among the weekdays only Saturday stands out. Monday to Friday
barely differ in our data, and Sunday sits close to them.

Europa-Park is closed from 11 January to 19 March 2027 while Rulantica is open
every day, and January and March are among our quietest months. In our calendar

## The order for a day at Rulantica

The waits are too short for a trick at a single slide to pay off. A fixed order
still makes the day calmer.

Before opening, overnight guests of the Europa-Park Erlebnis-Resort and holders
of the Adventure Ticket can go in from 9:00 a.m., half an hour ahead of everyone
else. If you want the drop capsules in Rangnakor, you can ride them in that
half hour.

In the morning you then head to Vinterhal for Svalgur Rytt and Vinter Rytt, the
two slides with the longest waits, and after that to Stormvind and Isbrekker.
Despite its eight lanes, Vikingløp in the Nordiskturn has clearly shorter waits
in our data than the two four-person-tyre slides.

Around midday, Snorri's Saga, the Surf Fjørd and the bubble loungers of the Skog
Lagune are worth a stop between slide rounds. You can't bring food in. What you
bring goes in a locker and gets eaten in the lobby, for example at the Kelpie
Café. In the Halloween season there are shows at the Skip Strand between 3:30
and 5:30 p.m. (as of 8 October 2026).

In the evening, the evening ticket applies from 5:00 p.m. and the Moonlight
ticket from 7:00 p.m., both until 10:00 p.m. The evening ticket costs less than
the day ticket. In our data the waits only drop after 9 p.m.

## With kids

Strollers aren't allowed in the water world, for safety reasons. The park asks
you to leave them in the car, and it keeps a limited number of lockers that
fit strollers. As a tip it suggests an infant carrier in which your toddler can
settle down at any time. There are changing rooms, one of them on the first
floor, run with dm and babylove. The park lends life jackets in three children's
sizes free of charge. Parents are responsible for supervision, and the park
recommends judging your child's swimming ability realistically and putting on a
jacket if in doubt.

**Trølldal** and the **Snorri Strand** are made for the youngest. Trølldal has
shallow water and small slides, **Kullerbuller** is a climbing and rolling
playground, **Tommelplums** is a slide for the smallest, and at **Schabernakker**
two slides race against each other. According to the park's data, all three
have a minimum height of 95 centimetres, and Tønnevirvel starts at 90. Outside
you also get **Snorri's Rutscheplads** with three slides and the play castle
**Snorri's Spelaborg**.

For children in between there are beginner slides. The beginner slides
**Skiprok**, **Vragrok**, **Istapprok** and **Isrok** start at 100 centimetres,
and **Översnurra**, **Översnabb**, **Överstor**, **Falrok**, **Spiralrok**,
**Slalomrok** and **Vågorrok** at 110. A child of 118 centimetres is tall enough
for everything up to Vikingløp (from age ten), and for Hugin, Munin, Stormvind
and Svalgur Rytt only with an adult, but not yet for the drop capsules and
Vinter Rytt.

The park states at each attraction whether swimming skills are needed: Isbrekker
is "for swimmers only", and so is Snorri Snorkling VR.

## Hyggedal: the sauna above the water

The sauna and wellness area **Hyggedal** covers over 1,000 square metres and sits
above the restaurant Lumålunda, with a view across the whole hall. It has three
clothing-free saunas with regular infusions, a steam bath, two outdoor terraces
and a bar of its own. One of the saunas is the **KOTA Sauna Chalet** on the
outdoor terrace. You get in only with the **Hyggedal Rulantica Ticket** (€52 to
€68), which includes standard admission. If you already have a day ticket, you
can add access by calling 07822 77 6655, or on the spot if there's room.

Children under 12 aren't allowed in, and the saunas, steam bath, bucket showers
and outdoor terraces are open only from 16. When infusions happen is posted
daily on screens on site. There are also two comfort options: the **Hygge Sofa**,
a daybed for two from €52, and the **Hygge Supreme Hydda** from €490, with a
private sauna, bathroom, daybed for two, a seating area for up to four and
personal service. Anyone who books one gets a ticket guarantee for their
companions, even when online sales are sold out.

You reach the **panoraama – the Rulantica Bar** on the first floor through the
lobby, and you don't need admission for it.

## Comfort options at a glance

There are two tiers in the hall, both bookable all year. According to the park,
the prices on the detail pages are on top of the Rulantica ticket. The **Comfort
Sofa** (from €52 a day) is a daybed for two in an alcove in the middle of the
hall. The **Comfort Hydda** (from €350 a day) stands in Rangnakor and has room
for up to four people, entry from 9:00 a.m., personal service and a welcome
drink at the Skål Bar, plus access to the Hydda Loft with a sauna, shower and
sun terrace. There are eight of these huts, equipped alike and themed
differently.

If you want more, you can rent a day suite in the Nordiskturn. The **Rulantica
Svit** (from €590 a day, up to four people) has a private loggia, a sauna, a
wellness tub and its own access to Vikingløp. The two-storey **VIP Svit** (from
€750 a day, up to six people, over 70 square metres) comes with a balcony and a
panorama sauna on the fifth floor. In summer, Strand Eggs, Strand Sofas and three
VIP yachts add to the outdoor offer; for 2027 they can be booked from the end
of 2026.

## Tickets and prices

The park gives a price range for each ticket. The prices apply to visits up to
19 March 2027. The tickets are day-based, and the park names no time slot.
Because capacity is limited, it advises buying online. At the box office,
tickets are sold only if there's capacity left, and for a **€6 surcharge**.

- Day ticket: €38 to €54, from 9:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
- Evening ticket: €35 to €46.50, from 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.
- Moonlight ticket: €28 to €34, from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.
- Hyggedal Rulantica Ticket: €52 to €68, with access to the sauna and
  relaxation area.
- Adventure Ticket: €59 to €72, with one of the three Rulantica novels and entry
  from 9:00 a.m.
- Best Price ticket: €28 to €48, only together with an overnight stay in the
  Europa-Park Erlebnis-Resort.
- Groups: from 20 paying people €38 to €45, school classes from ten paying
  pupils €28 to €33.
- Voucher ticket: from €51.

Then there are the small items. The locker costs **€1 a day** and can be booked
with your ticket or on site; it's included for hotel guests aged twelve and
over. As a day guest you can't rent towels, only buy them, and the park
recommends bringing your own. You pay with the **RulaBand**, a transponder
wristband that is also your entry card and locker key. Purchases are charged to
the band and paid when you leave. Wi-Fi is free. If you're visiting Europa-Park
anyway, the **ResortPass Gold** includes two Rulantica day tickets.

On its site the park lists the Parkscout plus Award 2020 to 2025 ("Europe's best
water park") and the EWA Professional Award 2025 for innovative offer design.
Snorri Snorkling VR won the 2022 Golden Wave Award from the trade magazine
Schwimmbad+Sauna in the "Smart Technology" category.

## Rulantica and Europa-Park

Both belong to the same operator and sit at the same motorway exit. Europa-Park
is open in its summer season from 28 March to 25 September 2026. Halloween
(until 1 November) and HALLOWinter (2 to 27 November) follow, and from 28
November 2026 to 9 January 2027 the Winterzauber. Then it pauses until 19 March.

Rulantica stays open through those weeks. If you're in Rust in February, there's
nothing to do at Europa-Park, but plenty in the water world. If you come in
summer, you combine the two. The park sells the **Ride & Slide Ticket** for that
(from €75, only in certain periods) and hotel packages. "Experience it all"
includes one day at Europa-Park, one day at Rulantica and an overnight stay with
breakfast, from €192 per person in a five-bed room. In the off-season there's
"2 for 1" (two nights and two days at Rulantica from €160 per person in a
double room) and "Kids for free" for two adults with up to three children from
€320.

Which wait times and quiet days we've measured at Europa-Park is covered in the
[Europa-Park guide](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips).

## Getting there, parking, staying overnight

Rulantica is at Roland-Mack-Ring 1 in 77977 Rust, opposite the Krønasår hotel.
By car, take **exit 57b Rust on the A5**. The visitor car park costs **€10** a
day and can be booked in advance in the ticket shop. Motorhomes pay €20, can
enter from 8:00 a.m. and have to be out by 11:00 p.m. EV charging stations are in
row 10, and for short stops there's a kiss-and-ride area at the coach park with
a 30-minute limit.

Without a car, the park lists train and bus, plus the EuroCity-Express between
Frankfurt and Milan. Scheduled buses stop at the main entrance of Europa-Park,
and from there the Europa-Park & Rulantica bus (line 7231/7200) takes a few
minutes to the water world. Airports are 64 kilometres away at Karlsruhe/Baden-Baden
and at Strasbourg, 20 at Black Forest Airport Lahr, 90 at EuroAirport Basel and
175 at Stuttgart; partner companies offer shuttles.

If you stay overnight, you can choose between six Experience hotels: El Andaluz,
Castillo Alcazar, Santa Isabel, Colosseo, Bell Rock and Krønasår. A free shuttle
runs between the hotels and Rulantica, every 15 minutes at peak times and
otherwise every 15 to 30. The Rust bus takes guests of Silver Lake City to the
water world every 30 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Overnight guests also
get the earlier entry at 9:00 a.m.

## Food, rules and the small print

Dining includes the Kelpie Café in the lobby (8:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m.), the
Snekkjas in the city on stilts, the Lumålunda at the Surf Fjørd and the Tempel
Krog at the Frigg Tempel. There are also the Skogbar in the middle of the
lagoon, the Svømme-Bar at the outdoor pool and the Hygge Sauna-Bar for Hyggedal
guests. There are vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and at the stations
next to Snekkjas and in the Strand Kök you mix your own soft-serve ice cream
with toppings.

These rules apply in the water world:

- Swimwear has to be made of special swimming fabrics.
- Glasses aren't allowed on Hoppablad, Dugdrob, Vildfål, Vikingløp and
  Isbrekker, and there are racks at the slides.
- Action cameras on head mounts are allowed everywhere except at Dugdrob,
  Vildfål and Isbrekker. Smartphones may come along as long as nobody is
  disturbed by the filming.
- Smoking is allowed only in designated areas outside, animals are banned, and
  glass bottles belong in the locker.
- Wheelchair users can get around on most paths, and there are water wheelchairs
  in limited numbers. The VR station isn't accessible.

## Closures, Halloween and Nørd Havn

From **16 to 27 November 2026** Rulantica is closed for maintenance, and so on 24
and 25 December. On 31 December the water world closes as early as 7:00 p.m.
The park keeps updating these dates, so check the opening hours page once more
before you book.

The Halloween season runs until **15 November 2026**. It brings decorations of
pumpkins, skeletons and corn stalks, three shows at the Skip Strand ("Der Schatz
der Geisterwikinger", "Die Geisterprüfung von Rangnakor" and the puppet show
"Snorris Spøg-Køken"), and the **Rulantica Vibes**, an evening with a DJ in the
heated outdoor pool Frigg Tempel. In summer there's the Midsummer festival, and
in August summer night parties with opening until midnight.

Next up is **Nørd Havn**, a two-storey building with a restaurant in the style of
the Nordic trading world downstairs and lounging areas upstairs, indoors, in the
conservatory and outside. The park gives the end of 2027 as the date.

## Frequently asked questions about Rulantica

### Is Rulantica open in winter?

Yes. The water world is open daily all year from 9:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., except
from 16 to 27 November 2026 (maintenance) and on 24 and 25 December. The pools
are 27 to 32 degrees and the air 30 to 34. The outdoor areas Svalgurok,
Dynstrønd and Snorri Strand are open only from May to September.

### How much does admission to Rulantica cost?

The day ticket costs €38 to €54. The evening ticket from 5 p.m. is €35 to
€46.50, and the Moonlight ticket from 7 p.m. is €28 to €34. Parking is €10 and
the locker €1 a day. With an overnight stay in the Europa-Park Erlebnis-Resort
there's the Best Price ticket from €28.

### When is Rulantica least busy?

In our data January and March were the quietest months, and February and May
were quiet too. July, August and December are the busiest, and among the weekdays
only Saturday stands out. On the day itself the waits only drop after 9 p.m.
December and October rest on seven measured days each, so the answer is less
certain there.

### Do you need a time-slot ticket for Rulantica?

The park names no time slot, and the tickets are day-based. Because capacity is
limited, it advises buying online; at the box office, tickets are sold only if
there's capacity left, and for a €6 surcharge. You don't have to book a time
slot for Snorri Snorkling VR either.

### Are children allowed in the sauna?

No. Hyggedal is closed to children under 12, and the clothing-free saunas, the
steam bath, the bucket showers and the terraces are open only from 16.

### What are the minimum heights for the slides?

From 95 centimetres on the smallest slides up to 140 centimetres at Dugdrob and
Vildfål. The park admits children from 110 centimetres to Hugin, Munin,
Stormvind and Svalgur Rytt, and those under 125 centimetres only with an adult.
Vinter Rytt and Isbrekker require 125 centimetres. The Tønnevirvel water
carousel starts at 90.

### Can you bring food?

No. For hygiene reasons, food isn't allowed in the water world. It has to go in
a locker, and you can eat it in the lobby at the Kelpie Café, for example. Drinks
are allowed, just no glass bottles.

### Can you do Rulantica in one day?

Yes. With a day ticket from 9:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. and the short waits, you
can fit in all the big slides, the water carousel, the current channel and the
lagoon.

## Rulantica when Europa-Park is closed

Rulantica is open daily from 9:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., in January even while
Europa-Park next door is shut. In our data January is one of the quietest
months.
