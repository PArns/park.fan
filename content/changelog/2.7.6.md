## New

- Park pages say when to go: the quietest weekdays, the next quiet days, and waits by month and by
  weekday from the park's own history (6 April).
- Headliners get a section of their own, and each ride has a chart of today's waits with the low,
  high and trend.
- A badge marks rides that only run part of the year, and the park page shows how many are out of season.
- The homepage reports how accurate the predictions are (the average error, how many were made and
  how many rides they cover) and counts down to the next training of the model.
