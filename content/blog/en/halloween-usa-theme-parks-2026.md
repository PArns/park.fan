Five big Halloween events in the USA are still running until October 31 or November 1. Every one of them costs extra for the evening, and each has a different program: horror houses in Orlando and Buena Park, Fright Fest at Six Flags, candy trails at Disney. The overview for Europe is in the [Halloween guide](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026), and Cedar Point in Ohio has its own post on [HalloWeekends](/blog/cedar-point-halloweekends-2026).

## Halloween Horror Nights in Orlando

[Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida) runs 49 nights between August 28 and November 1, according to Universal's press release. Ten houses are on the program:

- Stranger Things
- Sinners
- Hellraiser
- Ozzy Osbourne: Prince of Darkness
- Evil Dead Burn
- Jack and Oddfellow: Chaos & Control
- INVASION: Alien Abduction
- H.R. Bloodengutz Presents: A Halloween Fright-Tacular!
- MADLANDS: Caged Cannibals
- Cybergoria

There are also four scare zones: Fortnitemares, Infernal Carnival of Nightmares, Downtown Clowntown and Sideshow of Decay. The press release does not give ticket prices. They are on the park's website.

## Scary Farm at Knott's Berry Farm

[Knott's Berry Farm](ref:knotts-berry-farm) in Buena Park near Los Angeles holds **Knott's Scary Farm** on select nights from September 17 to October 31. There are ten mazes, and **Inked** and **Unearthed** are new. There are also five scare zones and three shows, and the magic show **Occultum** is new. According to the park, a ticket for one night starts at $65 and the Scary Farm pass costs $164, both online only. The park does not recommend the event for children under 13.

## Fright Fest at Six Flags

Fright Fest runs at Magic Mountain and Great Adventure from September 18 to November 1, on select nights.

[Six Flags Magic Mountain](ref:six-flags-magic-mountain) in California opens the event at 6:30 p.m., Friday to Sunday, and from October 8 on Thursdays too, according to the park. The mazes cost from $25 for one night, the pass for the whole season from $69, and the bundle with park admission from $74. **Final Destination: Death's Playground** and **Wolves' Blood** are new.

[Six Flags Great Adventure](ref:six-flags-great-adventure) in New Jersey has eight mazes, including the new **JASON: Blood Reign** and **Final Destination: Death's Playground**. The bundle with admission starts at $80, the season pass for the mazes at $75. From October 8, Fright Fest also runs on Thursdays. Every guest needs their own ticket.

Both parks advise against Fright Fest for children under 13.

## Halloween parties at Disney

The Disney parties are separate from the park day. If you want to go, you buy a separate ticket.

[Magic Kingdom Park](ref:magic-kingdom-park) in Florida holds **Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party** on select evenings between August 7 and October 31. The party starts at 7 p.m., and guests with a ticket can enter the park from 4 p.m. In October the dates, according to Disney, are the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 9th, 13th, 15th, 16th, 18th, 22nd, 23rd, 25th, 27th, 29th and 31st.

[Disney California Adventure Park](ref:disney-california-adventure-park) in Anaheim holds **Oogie Boogie Bash** on 33 evenings between August 18 and October 31, from 6 to 11 p.m. With a ticket you can enter the park three hours before the start. New this year is **Madame Leota's Swinging Wake**, a street party for the Haunted Mansion. Costumes are allowed, and according to Disney the waits at select attractions are often shorter.
