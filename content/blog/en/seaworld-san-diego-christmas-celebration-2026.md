The Christmas Celebration at [SeaWorld San Diego](ref:seaworld-san-diego) begins on Friday, November 13, and runs every day through January 3, 2027, all of it included in regular park admission. Five things on the program are new this year, among them the family show Baby Shark's Christmas Adventure and the Rock the Holiday Christmas Parade. More than a million lights go up around the park, and every evening it snows on the SkyTower Lawn and at Wild Arctic.

## What's new

Baby Shark's Christmas Adventure sends Baby Shark and friends to the North Pole, with the audience singing and dancing along. If you have small kids, you've known the song by heart for years whether you wanted to or not, so now at least it gets a Christmas outing. The show has sing-along moments and a small mission that families solve together.

Dolphin Island Christmas at Dolphin Stadium mixes Christmas with music from Hawaii and the Pacific, some of it sung in the islands' own languages. Solstice, the youngest dolphin in the pod, spends her first Christmas at the park.

At North Pole Storytime, Paige the Elf, the North Pole's librarian, tells how Santa once came to SeaWorld San Diego, and she pulls volunteers from the audience up on stage to help. DJ the Elf spins remixed Christmas songs at a dance party. The Rock the Holiday Christmas Parade winds through the whole park with floats, Mrs. Claus and her elves, and North Pole toymakers and bakers turn up along the route.

Coca-Cola Lane is new too, a red-and-white path of lights set to music. The Twinkle Tree Café pours new cocktails and alcohol-free mocktails; for the cocktails you have to be 21.

## What's back

Clyde and Seamore return to Sea Lion Stadium with their Christmas Special, along with sea lions, otters and their trainers. In Toymaker Trouble with Mrs. Claus and the Elves, head toymaker Sparky loses his Christmas spirit just before the presents go out, and Mrs. Claus and three elves have to get it back for him. A Stocking Full of Soul brings Christmas songs and dancing to the Nautilus Amphitheater, and Donner and Blitzen's Lazer Light Show plays at the park entrance every night.

On Holiday Plaza, a 30-foot (9-meter) Christmas tree lights up each evening. The SkyTower turns into the 320-foot (98-meter) Tree of Lights, and the palm trees are lit like candy canes. You'll find Santa at Santa's Cottage on the SkyTower Lawn; two of his reindeer stand by Rentals, and Rudolph is in the Holly Jolly Marketplace. The fireworks show A Christmas Wish happens only twice, on Saturday, December 12, and on New Year's Eve.

## Hours in November

The event page gives November 13 as the first day. The park calendar lists that Friday as the Christmas Celebration Pass Member Preview, an early look for annual pass holders, with the park open from 10 am to 9 pm. From Saturday, November 14, the calendar says Christmas Celebration. The program runs during regular park hours, local time:

- Saturday, November 14, and Sunday, November 15: 9 am to 7 pm
- Monday, November 16, to Thursday, November 19: 10 am to 6 pm
- Friday, November 20: 10 am to 8 pm
- Saturday, November 21, to Saturday, November 28, Thanksgiving included: 9 am to 9 pm
- Sunday, November 29: 9 am to 8 pm
- Monday, November 30: 10 am to 6 pm

## Tickets, parking and extras

Prices depend on the day. In the Black Friday sale, a date-specific day ticket starts at $59.99 instead of the regular $130.99, and SeaWorld hasn't said when the sale ends. The Any Day Ticket, good for one day of your choice through March 31, 2027, costs $64.99. The 2027 Fun Card, at $77.99, is valid from now until the end of 2027, so it already covers the whole Christmas Celebration. Kids two and under get in free. A two-park ticket with Sesame Place San Diego costs $104.99, and All-Day Dining adds $40 to a ticket. Reserved parking starts at $37, and annual pass holders get standard parking included.

The Festive Food & Seasonal Sips Lanyard, with 5 or 10 tasting portions, starts at $45. Dine with Orcas & Santa runs from November 13 to Christmas Eve, from $59.99 for adults and $39.99 for kids. The New Year's Eve Family Bash costs $74.99, or $39.99 for kids. For the Snowflake Seeker's scavenger hunt, you buy a card at The Christmas Shoppe and hand it back there once it's filled in to collect a prize.

## Getting there

SeaWorld San Diego is at 500 Sea World Drive in San Diego. Without a car you can get there on the MTS Trolley and buses; a day pass costs $6 in the Compass Cloud app.

## Coasters and last year

In 2025 the new piece of the Christmas event was a SeaWorld San Diego Sea Lion Christmas, and the Black Friday ticket cost $49.99 back then.

If you want a ride between the shows and the lights, there are four coasters to pick from:

- [Emperor](ref:seaworld-san-diego/emperor), a floorless dive coaster with a 90-degree drop and a top speed of 60 mph (97 km/h): 52 inches (132 cm), maximum 78 inches (198 cm)
- [Manta](ref:seaworld-san-diego/manta), a launch coaster: 48 inches (122 cm)
- [Electric Eel](ref:seaworld-san-diego/electric-eel), with several launches: 54 inches (137 cm)
- [Arctic Rescue](ref:seaworld-san-diego/arctic-rescue), a straddle coaster with three launches over 2,800 feet (853 meters) of track and up to 40 mph (64 km/h): 48 inches (122 cm)
