## New

- A ride page's header lists its facts with labels (manufacturer, ride type, number of track
  elements) and its measurements in your units: switch to °F and a speed reads in mph, a height in
  feet, and a minimum height of 140 cm as 55 in (29 July).
- Rides and the glossary link both ways. A ride's profile lists its track elements in the order
  you ride them, each linked to its term, and a term page lists the rides that have it. The
  glossary grew to 262 terms (28 July).
- Park, ride and region pages say how far away you are.

## Fixed

- Blog posts that name a park or a ride showed its status from the day the post was built. The
  Phantasialand guide had shown all twelve coasters it names as closed in the middle of an
  operating day.
- Without location permission, "Parks near you" placed you at the data centre your request went
  through, so a visitor in Geilenkirchen got the parks around Frankfurt or London (31 July).
