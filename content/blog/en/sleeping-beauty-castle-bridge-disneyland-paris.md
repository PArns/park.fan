The bridge to [Sleeping Beauty Castle](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/sleeping-beauty-castle) in [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park) at Disneyland Paris is reopening to guests after almost six months of renovation, timed for the castle's 35th anniversary. The park announced it on 12 October without giving a date. The bridge had been closed since May 2026. It's now paved with granite instead of concrete, and a compass rose of natural stone and brass is set into its centre.

## Granite where the concrete was

Disneyland Paris took up the old stamped concrete and laid granite setts, which are easier to maintain. There are 2,735 of them, laid to follow the curve of the arch, and Disney compares the job to a jigsaw puzzle.

The balusters of the parapet, in Champagny limestone, were rebuilt from scratch and waterproofed against spray from the fountains below. The wooden posts of the balustrade are new, and the drawbridge has a new deck of tropical hardwood.

The structure that carries the decorative pieces was rebuilt too, along with the arches, the corbels and the ornamented spandrels between the arches. What looks like masonry on the side walls and under the arches is artificial stone: a paste sprayed onto a metal frame and shaped into stones by hand. Each piece was then hand-painted in five shades, with light and dark gradations, to match the stone of the castle. Patina, fresh paint and new planting came last.

## The compass rose

The compass rose is natural stone inlaid with brass, in the colours of the three good fairies from "Sleeping Beauty". Merryweather's blue rays and Flora's red ones run in opposite directions. That's the scene where the two of them squabble over the colour of Aurora's dress while she dances with Prince Philip. A green arc for Fauna, the one who keeps the peace in the film, joins the two colours.

At the centre is a brass medallion with Aurora's crown, ringed by thorns. A brass fleur-de-lis points north. It recalls the four brass letters that marked the points of the compass on the bridge when the park opened and were taken out during a renovation in 2010. Every brass piece is worked on three levels with different finishes, made by hand by a craftsman who specialises in it.

Since June 2024 one of the castle windows has referred to the same scene: in the evening it keeps changing colour.

> [!QUOTE]
> This renovation was made possible thanks to the involvement of numerous trades and the wealth of their expertise. We called upon experts in the restoration of historic monuments to take care of Sleeping Beauty Castle, which is, in its own way, a landmark of Disneyland Paris. Our aim was to restore the bridge to its former glory, whilst preserving the details that give it its character, so that it may continue to form an integral part of the Castle for many years to come.
>
> Stefano Servi, Stage Architect at Walt Disney Imagineering Paris, in [Disneyland Paris's announcement of 12 October 2026](https://news.disneylandparis.com/en/sleeping-beauty-castle-bridge-refurbishment/)

## The castle at 35

The castle was unveiled on 12 October 1991, half a year before the park, at an event called "Rendez-Vous au Château" for the press, cast members and their families. Disneyland Paris opened on 12 April 1992. For the resort's fifth birthday in 1997 the castle wore carnival decorations for "The Hunchback of Notre Dame".

The first major renovation of the whole castle since 1992 took place in 2021. Nine French firms worked on it, among them carpenters, roofers, painters and metalworkers. The roof and its timber frame went to Le Bras Frères, a company that usually restores historic monuments. Adrien Beaugendre, a roofer there who was named "Best Craftsman in France" for his roofing in 2015, said in a piece published by the park that the frame and the slates were done exactly as on a real French château.

Since June 2024 more than 200 new LED floodlights have lit the castle from all sides. Disney Tales of Magic, a show projected onto the Main Street facades and the castle, has run in the evenings since 2025. On 14 October the park calendar has it starting at 10 pm.

## Inside the castle

The castle is 167 feet tall, about 51 metres, with towers under royal blue roofs. It's modelled on the castle in the animated "Sleeping Beauty" and on real French châteaux and historic buildings. The trees around it are clipped into cubes.

Stairs lead up to the first floor and [La Galerie de la Belle au Bois Dormant](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/la-galerie-de-la-belle-au-bois-dormant), which retells the fairy tale in ornate storybooks, handmade Aubusson tapestries and nine stained-glass windows. The windows are by Paul Chapman, who has also restored windows at Notre-Dame de Paris. One of them shows Aurora and Philip kissing.

Below the castle is [La Tanière du Dragon](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park/la-taniere-du-dragon), a dark dungeon where a defeated dragon lies chained to the rock. Every so often it wakes up, puffs smoke and roars.

The gallery and the dragon's lair stayed open throughout the work, and so did La Boutique du Château and the Boutique Merlin l'Enchanteur. On 14 October the gallery and the lair are open from 8:30 am to 8:15 pm.

## Around the castle this autumn

The castle stands at the end of Main Street, U.S.A. In front of it is Central Plaza, where the paths split off to the other four lands. The castle itself belongs to Fantasyland, home to Peter Pan's Flight, Blanche-Neige et les Sept Nains, Les Voyages de Pinocchio and Le Carrousel de Lancelot, among others.

The Disney Halloween Festival runs until 1 November, and its decorations stretch from Main Street to the foot of the castle. At the Royal Castle Stage, thorns, ravens and the crests of Disney villains wind around the gates, and a ruin has been built at the foot of the castle as a photo backdrop. The Théâtre du Château beside the castle is dressed as a haunted ruin as well. On 31 October a special programme runs until 11 pm with its own fireworks, and that's the only day of the year guests may come into the park in costume.

Disney Enchanted Christmas follows from 7 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, with Main Street decorated and a 24-metre Christmas tree. From 14 November Minnie and Mrs. Claus appear at the Royal Castle Stage. On New Year's Eve fireworks go up over Sleeping Beauty Castle at midnight. How busy Paris got between Christmas and New Year is in our look at [Christmas and New Year's Eve in Orlando and Paris](/blog/christmas-new-year-orlando-paris-2026).

On 14 October the park calendar lists these as closed for refurbishment: Buzz Lightyear Laser Blast, Pirates' Beach, the Pirate Galleon, the Frontierland Playground, and the Frontierland Depot and Discoveryland Station stops of the Disneyland Railroad. The Plaza Gardens Restaurant on Main Street has been shut since April 2026 for a rebuild that's due to last until early 2027.
