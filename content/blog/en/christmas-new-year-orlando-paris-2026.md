We measured every day from 24 to 31 December 2025, in Orlando and in Paris. In Orlando that week was busier than any other month of the year in **all seven
parks**: [Magic Kingdom](ref:magic-kingdom-park), [EPCOT](ref:epcot),
[Hollywood Studios](ref:disneys-hollywood-studios),
[Animal Kingdom](ref:disneys-animal-kingdom-theme-park),
[Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida),
[Islands of Adventure](ref:universal-islands-of-adventure) and
[Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe).

In Paris the same week sat on the yearly average at
[Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), and at
[Disney Adventure World](ref:disney-adventure-world) it was **quieter than any
other month of the year**.

> **About the dates:** this is where things stood on 4 October 2026. Dates,
> times and prices come from the parks' official pages, linked in the sources below. Parks
> move evening dates around well into November, so check again before you book.

## Walt Disney World: 13 November to 6 January

Disney runs its Orlando Christmas season from **13 November 2026 to 6 January
2027**. Two of the four parks add a separately ticketed evening event, and both
of them start before the season officially does: Very Merry Christmas Party on
8 November, Jollywood Nights on the 7th. The earliest party nights are reliably the cheapest ones.

### Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party: 25 nights

The Magic Kingdom party runs on **25 nights between 8 November and 22
December**, not nightly:

- November: 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27 and 29
- December: 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 22

It's a five-hour event, 7 p.m. to midnight, and the ticket gets you into the
park **from 4 p.m.** You don't need a separate day ticket and you don't need a
park reservation. Tickets run **$189 to $229 plus tax** depending on the date,
with $10 off for children aged three to nine. Annual Passholders and Disney
Vacation Club members save another $10, but only on selected nights from 8 to 20
November and from 29 November to 3 December.

The ticket includes Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade, the Minnie's Wonderful
Christmastime fireworks, the Mickey's Most Merriest Celebration stage show at
Cinderella Castle, snowfall on Main Street, and cookies and hot drinks at
set locations around the park at no extra charge. Five attractions get a holiday
version, among them [Space Mountain](ref:magic-kingdom-park/space-mountain) with
different music and different interior lighting, the Tomorrowland Speedway and
the Mad Tea Party. Jungle Cruise runs as Jingle Cruise all December, during the
day as well, no party ticket needed.

The parade, the fireworks and the castle stage show are cancelled in wind and
storms, and Disney notes this under each of the three. In December that's rare
in Florida.

### EPCOT: Festival of the Holidays and the Candlelight Processional

The **EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays** runs from **27 November to
30 December** and costs nothing extra. Storytellers around World Showcase tell the Christmas customs of their pavilion: Las Posadas with Mariachi Cobre in Mexico, a
mischievous Barn Santa in Norway, Micha's Adventskalender puppet theatre in
Germany, La Befana in Italy, a Hanukkah storyteller between Morocco and France,
Père Noël in France, Father Christmas in the United Kingdom. China uses the same
slot for Lunar New Year, with a lion dancer and a Si-Zhu trio.

The **Candlelight Processional** runs in the same window, but **every evening**,
three times a night, at 4:45, 6:30 and 8 p.m. at the America Gardens Theatre. A
celebrity narrator reads the Christmas story, backed by a massed choir, the
Voices of Liberty and a 50-piece orchestra. Disney has already published almost
the whole 2026 narrator schedule: Auliʻi Cravalho opens on 27 and 28 November,
then Gary Sinise, Whoopi Goldberg, Robin Roberts, Jason Kelce, Neil Patrick
Harris, Lauren Daigle, Ralph Macchio, Henry Winkler, Lacey Chabert, Jodi Benson,
Simu Liu, and Eva Longoria closing from 28 to 30 December. Two slots, 2 and 3
December and 13 and 14 December, still read "To Be Announced".

Admission is free, but a seat isn't guaranteed. The Candlelight Processional
Dining Package, a meal at one of the EPCOT restaurants, comes with a guaranteed
seat. [Living with the Land](ref:epcot/living-with-the-land)
runs as Glimmering Greenhouses in these weeks, with lights through the
greenhouses.

### Hollywood Studios: Jollywood Nights on 15 evenings

**Disney Jollywood Nights** is the second ticketed event, and it runs longer
than the Magic Kingdom party, into the new year. Fifteen nights from **7
November 2026 to 5 January 2027**:

- November: 7, 14, 16, 21, 23 and 28
- December: 5, 7, 12, 14, 19, 21 and 23
- January: 3 and 5

Five hours again, 7:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m., with entry from 5:30 p.m. Tickets are
**$169 to $199 plus tax**, $10 off for children aged three to nine, and the
Passholder and DVC discount is valid on **all** nights here rather than selected ones.

Without an extra ticket, Sunset Seasons Greetings projects onto the Hollywood
Tower Hotel every evening, Toy
Story Land gets oversized decorations, and the Frozen sing-along show gets a
holiday finale with Olaf.

### Animal Kingdom and Disney Springs

Animal Kingdom has no evening event and the smallest programme of the four
parks. On Discovery Island the **Merry Menagerie** sets out hand-built puppets
of arctic animals, penguins, reindeer, foxes and polar bears. The Tree of Life
gets a holiday edition of its Awakenings each evening. There's a Christmas tree
of a little over 16 metres at the entrance, and the village of Harambe is hung
with garlands made from recycled materials and traditional African textiles.

Disney Springs runs the **Christmas Tree Stroll**, a walk past elaborately
decorated trees, plus Santa for photos. Neither costs admission.

## Universal Orlando: 14 November to 3 January

Universal pulled its season forward by a week this year and celebrates from **14
November 2026 to 3 January 2027**. That's 51 days, six more than in 2025. Unlike Disney, Grinchmas, the Wizarding World, the parade and the holiday
decorations at Epic Universe need no extra ticket; they're included with regular
park admission.

**Grinchmas** is where it always is, in Seuss Landing at Islands of Adventure,
with the "Grinchmas Who-liday Spectacular" stage show after the Dr. Seuss book,
the Grinch himself for photos, and the Who-Ville Tree Lot.

**Christmas in The Wizarding World of Harry Potter** runs across all three parks
at once for the first time: Hogsmeade at Islands of Adventure, Diagon Alley at
Universal Studios Florida, and the Ministry of Magic at Epic Universe. Hogsmeade
also gets "The Magic of Christmas at Hogwarts Castle" in the evenings, a
projection show onto the castle.

At Universal Studios Florida, **Universal's Holiday Parade featuring Macy's**
goes down the street every evening, with the oversized balloons from the New
York Thanksgiving parade and floats for Despicable Me, Shrek and Madagascar.
Santa lights the Christmas tree at the end of it.

At **Epic Universe**, in the park's second winter, SUPER NINTENDO
WORLD and How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk get holiday decorations for
the first time, and Celestial Park returns as a winter wonderland with lit trees
and holiday fountain displays at the Cosmos Fountain. Universal calls the Isle
of Berk version **Snoggletog**, the winter feast from the Dragons films.

You can also book the **Universal Holiday Tour**, two and a half hours
guided, with a meet-and-greet with the Grinch and his dog Max, reserved seating
for the Grinchmas show, a meeting with Santa, and optionally a reserved viewing
area for the parade. It needs a park-to-park ticket for the Studios and the
Islands, and it runs on selected dates from 14 November 2026 to 2 January 2027.

## Disneyland Paris: 7 November to 6 January

**Disney Enchanted Christmas** runs from
**7 November 2026 to 6 January 2027** across both parks.

At Disneyland Park, **Mickey's Dazzling Christmas Parade** goes round twice a
day, the Christmas tree on Town Square is **24 metres** tall and gets lit
several times a day, and the stage carries the "Let's Sing Christmas!"
sing-along with Mickey, Minnie, Donald, Daisy, Goofy and Clarabelle. Characters
stand in three places: Mickey and friends on Town Square, the princesses on
Central Plaza, Aurora and Prince Phillip in the castle courtyard. For the photo
with Santa you need a reservation through the virtual queue.

New this winter is Christmas in **World of Frozen**, the land Disney Adventure
World opened in 2026. Arendelle holds its first Christmas there, with garlands,
advent wreaths and the Arendelle Carollers, a Christmas choir. With children who know
[Frozen Ever After](ref:disney-adventure-world/frozen-ever-after), I wouldn't
plan Disney Adventure World as just a half day.

The parade is included with the park ticket. Disney sells a reserved spot for it
separately, while they last.

December 2025. Before that we didn't measure in Orlando or Paris at all; since
January 2026 we have 28 to 31 days a month. **For November 2025 we have no
figures for any of the nine parks**, and none for the first half of the
Christmas season either.

At Disneyland Park the week between Christmas and New Year sits where an ordinary
summer month sits. At Disney Adventure World it's quieter than any other month of the year.

One reason is probably capacity. Magic Kingdom has 37 active attractions,
Disney Adventure World has 14, and the Paris parks spread their guests across
two entrances on one resort.

In that week Orlando lives off
guests who flew in and booked a week; Paris sits two hours by train from a large
share of its visitors, and a day trip on 28 December competes with the rest of
the family holidays.

## New Year's Eve

**Disneyland Paris** has published its programme and splits the night between
the two parks. Disney Adventure World gets a fireworks and drone show built
around Frozen, weather permitting. Disneyland Park gets DJs, the characters, and
its own fireworks over Sleeping Beauty Castle at midnight. You pick which park
you'll be standing in when you buy the ticket.

**Universal Orlando** lists New Year's Eve across all three parks and CityWalk,
with DJs, live entertainment and a midnight countdown. Dates, times and prices
aren't on the page, and they aren't in the 6 August press release either.

**Walt Disney World** has published no New Year's Eve programme on its Christmas
page. Disney lists Magic Kingdom, Hollywood Studios, EPCOT, Animal Kingdom,
Disney Springs, the hotels and Blizzard Beach there, and 31 December appears in
none of those sections. It may still come; Disney often publishes New Year's Eve in November.

## What I'd book in your place

**November already gets you the full decoration.** In
Orlando both evening events start before the season officially does, Universal
celebrates from the 14th, and Paris from the 7th. The week between Christmas and New
Year gets you the same programme at the highest waits of the year.

**If you have to travel between Christmas and New Year, go to Paris.** There the same week was no outlier upwards, and at Disney Adventure World it
was the quietest time of the year.

**If you want one ticketed evening in Orlando and you're watching the price,
take Jollywood Nights.** It's cheaper than the Very Merry Christmas Party on
every date, the Passholder discount applies on all nights instead of selected
ones, and it runs to 5 January, which is two weeks after the Magic Kingdom party
has finished.

Very Merry Christmas Party happens on 25 of the 45 days in its
window, Jollywood Nights on 15 of 60.
