Saturday 24 October 2026, the last Saturday of the autumn half-term in the southern Netherlands.
Our calendar puts [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland?bare) in Sevenum on Extreme, the top of our six levels. On Wednesday 7 October, seventeen days earlier, the same park sat on Very Low. No ride
had opened in between and none had closed. The school bell had just been quiet for a week.

Toverland is in North Limburg, on the A67 between Eindhoven and Venlo, less than twenty kilometres
from the German border. Two school calendars count here: the Dutch south region's and North
Rhine-Westphalia's (NRW), the German state around Düsseldorf, Cologne and the Ruhr. We measured which one makes the queues longer: every ride's wait time every five minutes since 24 December 2025, on 250 days up to 28 October 2026.

All of this comes from a single season, with one Easter and one autumn. Where a finding rests on
four or five days, I'll say so.

## When is Toverland quiet?

Toverland is quietest on a weekday outside the school holidays, ideally a Monday, Tuesday or
Wednesday in May, June or September. On those school days between April and the end of October
2026, our calendar showed Low or Very Low on 46 of 71 days, and at the big rides you queue for over
a third less than on a Saturday. Saturday is the busiest day of the week: 25 of the season's 30
Saturdays came in at High or above. Sundays are noticeably quieter. Avoid the autumn half-term in
the southern Netherlands, because in 2026 every day of it was Very High or Extreme. The May
holiday, the summer holidays from late July and German public holidays such as 1 May and Corpus
Christi also fill the park. A German school holiday on its own, while Dutch schools are open,
changes little. In winter Toverland only opens on Wednesdays and at weekends, and the quietest days
we've measured were Wednesdays in January and February.

## How we measure crowds

Every day gets a level in our calendar, from Very Low to Extreme. At Toverland we work it out from
the waits at [Booster Bike](ref:attractiepark-toverland/booster-bike),
[Pixarus](ref:attractiepark-toverland/pixarus) and
[Expedition Zork](ref:attractiepark-toverland/expedition-zork). Normal means as busy as an average
Toverland day. Low and Very Low are quieter, High, Very High and Extreme busier.

The level measures Toverland against itself, and an Extreme day in Sevenum isn't an Extreme day in Kaatsheuvel. Toverland had 1,137,000 visitors in 2025, according to the park, spread across forty-odd attractions. In the comparison below it sits next to Efteling, Walibi Holland and Phantasialand.

Queues are short here on an ordinary school day, which is why one busy week stands out so much: it doesn't take many extra cars with German or Brabant number plates
before the level jumps two steps.

## Which day of the week

Saturday is Toverland's busiest day, by a wide margin. Sundays sat lower from April to October: 14 of 30 reached High, and 11 stayed Low
or Very Low. At the headliners you queue almost a fifth less on a Sunday than on the Saturday
before. If it has to be the weekend, Sunday is the better pick.

On weekdays a lot depends on the holidays, so the table only counts days with no school holiday in
the Netherlands or in NRW, and no public holiday on either side of the border.

| Day       | Days, April to September 2026 | Low or Very Low | High or above |
| --------- | ----------------------------: | --------------: | ------------: |
| Monday    |                            12 |               9 |             1 |
| Tuesday   |                            13 |               8 |             1 |
| Wednesday |                            13 |              10 |             2 |
| Thursday  |                            12 |               6 |             5 |
| Friday    |                            15 |               8 |             6 |
| Saturday  |                            13 |               1 |            12 |
| Sunday    |                            13 |               6 |             5 |

Monday to Wednesday are the best days of the week. In an ordinary school week, Thursday and Friday reached High or above about four times as often as Monday to Wednesday. I don't know why Thursday in particular, and it isn't Corpus Christi: the public holidays are already left out of the table.

In winter there's less choice. From January to late March 2026 Toverland only opened on
Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays, and in winter 2026/27 that's the pattern again outside the
Christmas holidays. Of the seven Wednesdays outside the southern school holidays we measured in
those months, six were Low or Very Low. Four of the five quietest days since we started measuring
Toverland are such Wednesdays: 28 January, 4 and 11 February and 11 March. The fifth, of all days, is a holiday: Sunday 22 February, the last day of the southern spring half-term.

## Which month

```stats-widget slug=attractiepark-toverland show=months

```

**January to March.** The park opens three days a week and the queues are short. The southern
spring half-term, 14 to 22 February 2026, passed Toverland by: all eight measured days came in at
Low or Very Low. Weekends varied: 17 and 18 January and 21 March were Very High, the weekend of 24
and 25 January Very Low. The emptiest days of the year are in winter, then, only on Wednesdays and weekends and with North Limburg's January weather.
November isn't in the chart yet, because we've only been measuring since late December 2025.

**April.** The busiest month after July, August and October, mostly because of Easter and the
German and Belgian Easter holidays.

**May and June.** Together with September, the best stretch of the year. On 35 weekday school days
with no holiday of any kind, Toverland was Low or Very Low 24 times. You do have to plan around the
public holidays, though: 1 May, Ascension Day, Whitsun and the German Corpus Christi all fall in
these months, and most of them were busy.

**July and August.** The summer holidays, and August was the busiest month of the year.

**September.** After the summer holidays, weekdays are quieter than at any other point in the
season: 13 of the 17 weekdays between 2 and 30 September were Low or Very Low. All four Saturdays in September still came in at High or above, as if half of Limburg had realised on
Friday evening that summer was nearly over.

**October.** On weekdays it stayed quiet until the half-term, Low or Very Low on
five of six measured days. Halloween starts on 3 October, weekends fill up, and in the half-term
Toverland gets busier than in summer.

**November and December.** Halloween runs until Sunday 8 November 2026, then the park closes until
28 November, the first day of Winter Feelings.

## School holidays in the southern Netherlands

Sevenum is in the Dutch south region, along with the rest of Limburg, North Brabant and Zeeland. For
the table we took every holiday of the 2025/26 school year, with every day the park was open and we
have a level for.

| South region holiday, 2025/26 | Days measured | High or above | Low or Very Low |
| ----------------------------- | ------------: | ------------: | --------------: |
| Christmas, 20 Dec to 4 Jan    |            11 |             3 |               5 |
| Spring half-term, 14–22 Feb   |             8 |             0 |               8 |
| May holiday, 25 Apr to 3 May  |             9 |             7 |               1 |
| Summer, 11 Jul to 23 Aug      |            44 |            30 |               6 |
| Autumn half-term, 17–25 Oct   |             9 |             9 |               0 |

The May holiday is busy, King's Day on 27 April came in at High, and Friday 1 May was even the busiest day we've measured at Toverland: the
Netherlands were on holiday, Germany and Belgium had Labour Day, and all three seemed to be driving
to Sevenum.

Summer started cautiously. In the first week of the southern summer holidays, 13 to 17 July, the
park was at Normal or below on four weekdays. The central region's holidays began on 18 July and NRW's on 20 July, the same weekend, so the two can't be told apart this summer. From that Monday weekday queues were about two thirds longer than the week
before. Of the 25 weekdays between 20 July and 23 August, 20 were High or above.

The autumn half-term was the busiest. Three of the five busiest days of our whole year of measurements fall in that week: 19, 22 and 24 October. On weekdays you queued over three times as long at the headliners as
in the week of 5 October. Halloween and the holiday came together: on eight of the nine days the
park stayed open until 11 p.m. for the Halloween Nights.

The north region had its autumn half-term a week earlier, 10 to 18 October. On weekdays you'd never
have known in Sevenum: from 12 to 16 October the park was Very Low, Very Low, Normal, Normal and
Low. Groningen and Friesland are just a bit too far for a day out at Toverland.

![A performer on the balcony above the "The Dollhouse" lettering | During Halloween Nights Villa Fiasko becomes "The Dollhouse", with a performer on the balcony. | right](/media/toverland/villa-fiasko-4x3.jpg)

The 2026/27 dates are already set, and the south region's and NRW's overlap in a few places.

| Holiday              | Dutch south region               | North Rhine-Westphalia   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Christmas 2026/27    | 19 December to 3 January         | 23 December to 6 January |
| Spring 2027          | 13 to 21 February                | none                     |
| Easter 2027          | none, but Easter Monday 29 March | 22 March to 3 April      |
| May and Whitsun 2027 | 24 April to 2 May                | 18 May                   |
| Summer 2027          | 24 July to 5 September           | 19 July to 31 August     |
| Autumn 2027          | 23 to 31 October                 | 23 October to 6 November |

Summer 2027 will run differently from 2026. NRW starts five days before the south region, and the
south region carries on five days longer. From Wednesday 1 to Sunday 5 September 2027 Limburg is
still on holiday while NRW is back at school. We didn't have days like that in 2026. The closest is the first week of the 2026 summer holidays, when only the south and north regions were off and four weekdays stayed at Normal or below.

## Do German school holidays matter?

With the border this close, you'd expect so. To test it, we looked for days when only NRW was on
holiday and Dutch schools were open. In 2026 there were seven such weekdays, four at Easter and three in late October.

| Weekdays in 2026    | Who was on holiday                      | Crowds at Toverland                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 30 March to 2 April | NRW only, first week of Easter holidays | Very Low, Very Low, High, High     |
| 7 to 10 April       | NRW and Flanders                        | Very High, Very High, High, Normal |
| 26 to 28 October    | NRW only, last week of autumn holidays  | Very Low, Low, Normal              |

In the first Easter week and after the Dutch half-term, weekday queues were about as long as on an
ordinary school day in the season. As long as only the German schools are
shut, you barely notice at Toverland. In the second Easter week, when Flanders was off too, waits nearly doubled.

On a German public holiday all of NRW is off at once, parents included, and Toverland fills up straight away.

| Day in 2026        | What was on                                          | Crowds at Toverland |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Friday 1 May       | Labour Day in Germany and Belgium, May holiday in NL | Extreme             |
| Tuesday 26 May     | Whitsun school day off in NRW                        | Very High           |
| Thursday 4 June    | Corpus Christi (Fronleichnam) in NRW                 | Very High           |
| Friday 5 June      | bridge day after Corpus Christi                      | High                |
| Saturday 3 October | German Unity Day, first day of Halloween             | Very High           |

Corpus Christi is the cleanest example. In the Netherlands 4 June was an ordinary Thursday, and the
other Thursdays in May and June were mostly Low or Very Low. On Corpus Christi you queued almost
twice as long at the headliners as on an ordinary school day. A holiday that applies on both sides
of the border did less: Ascension Day, Thursday 14 May, stayed at Normal, Whit Monday reached High,
and Liberation Day on 5 May, a public holiday only in the Netherlands, stayed Low.

For planning 2027: Corpus Christi falls on Thursday 27 May, the NRW Whitsun school day off on
Tuesday 18 May. All Saints' Day, a public holiday in NRW and Belgium, falls on a Sunday with
Halloween Nights in 2026, and on Monday 1 November in 2027, in the middle of the NRW autumn
holidays. Easter Monday 2027 is 29 March, in the second week of the NRW Easter holidays.

## And the Belgians?

Flanders is further away than NRW, but Belgian holidays are in our calendar too, and twice in 2026
they fell on days when the Netherlands and NRW were at school.

| Weekdays in 2026 | Who was on holiday                            | Crowds at Toverland                           |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 13 to 17 April   | Flanders only, second week of Easter holidays | Low, Low, Low, High, Very High                |
| 1 to 3 July      | Flanders only, start of summer holidays       | High, High, Very High                         |
| 6 to 10 July     | Flanders and the Dutch north region           | Very High, High, Very High, Very High, Normal |

With only Flanders off at Easter it stayed Low until Wednesday and filled up on Thursday and Friday, just like an ordinary school week. From 1 to 10 July,
the last school days in the south region, weekdays were busier than in the first week of its own
summer holidays, when you queued about a third less at the headliners. The holiday calendars alone don't explain it, and for July our calendar is a better guide than the school bell.

## Halloween and the end of the season

From Saturday 3 October 2026 Toverland runs Halloween, by day and on a number of evenings. For
crowds it's mostly the weekends: the first four Saturdays of October came in at Very High or
Extreme, all of them with Halloween Nights until 11 p.m. On weekdays before the half-term you
hardly noticed. A Wednesday in the first week of October is one of the best days of the year as a
result: the park is already decorated, the queues are short, and the pumpkins look at you as if
they'd have preferred a Saturday too.

![Four glowing pumpkin heads, two of them in witches' hats, in a corn field | A pumpkin field along the Halloween Nights route. | left](/media/toverland-halloween/04-kuerbisse-maisfeld.jpg)

After the half-term it dropped straight back: Monday 26 to Wednesday 28 October were Very Low, Low
and Normal. On Saturday 31 October, Sunday 1 November and Saturday 7 November the park stays open
until 11 p.m. one last time. For those evenings our calendar shows High and Very High, for the
weekdays in between Normal to High. The last day of the season is Sunday 8 November. From 9 to 27
November Toverland is closed, which gives the park three weeks to clear away the pumpkins and put
up the fairy lights.

## Winter Feelings and the 2027 season

On Saturday 28 November 2026 Toverland reopens as Winter Feelings, until Sunday 17 January 2027. Open days:

- 28 November to 16 December: Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays
- 19 December to 3 January: every day
- 6 to 17 January: Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays

On Wednesdays the park is then open from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., at weekends until 7 p.m., in the
Christmas holidays mostly until 8 p.m., and on 24 and 31 December and 1 January until 6 p.m. On 15
of those days [pay per ride](/news/toverland-pay-per-ride-2026) applies: the park charges no
admission, and you pay per ride with €2 credits.

![A lit swing carousel turning inside a hall, the seats blurred by the motion | Djinn in the covered hall of the Land van Toos, which stays dry even in January. | wide](/media/toverland-halloween/05-djinn-land-van-toos.jpg)

For most Winter Feelings days our calendar shows High, even though last winter's Christmas holidays stayed far quieter than the summer. The busiest days then were Christmas Day and 29 and 30 December, while the weekend of 3 and 4 January was Very Low. Very High is on most Saturdays in December and around Christmas. Free admission on Wednesdays and weekends didn't exist at Toverland last winter, and for those 15 days our forecast has no previous year to go on.

The park hasn't published any opening days after 17 January 2027 yet. In 2026 it opened on
Wednesdays and at weekends in January, February and March, and every day from Monday 30 March, the first day of the NRW Easter holidays.

![A carved ram's head at the Maximus' Blitz Bahn station | The station of Maximus' Blitz Bahn, which ran for the last time on 17 May 2026. The Swinging Bobsled takes its place. | right](/media/toverland/maximus-blitzbahn-4x3.jpg)

Where
Maximus' Blitz Bahn stood in the Wunderwald, closed since 17 May 2026, the Swinging Bobsled is due to
open in the 2026/27 winter season: a two-seater bobsled that swings out sideways at certain points.
Its predecessor had the longest typical wait in the park for as long as we measured it, and the
bobsled keeps the same queue line through Maximus's chalet. There's more on the ride in our [guide to Troy and Toverland](/blog/toverland-troy-wait-times-tips).

## Which rides to do first

At 10 a.m., when Toverland opens, most queues are at their shortest of the day.
[Troy](ref:attractiepark-toverland/troy) is the exception: its queue peaks around 11, and around
midday and in the last hour it's shorter than at opening. Most other rides only fill up after
lunch, peaking around 2 p.m.

```hourly-profile-widget slug=attractiepark-toverland top=8

```

1. **Head to the Magische Vallei at opening.**
   [Booster Bike](ref:attractiepark-toverland/booster-bike) has the longest typical queue in the park
   over the year, and it grows in the very first hour. At 10 a.m. it's still short.
   [Dwervelwind](ref:attractiepark-toverland/dwervelwind) is in the same area.
2. **Avalon before 11.** [Fēnix](ref:attractiepark-toverland/fenix) and Pixarus are shortest at 10
   a.m. and then stay about the same until late afternoon. Expedition Zork in the Wunderwald belongs
   in the morning too, because its queue grows until mid-afternoon.
3. **Troy around midday or at the end of the day.** While the rest of the park has lunch, the queue
   is shorter than at 11, and again in the last hour.
4. **Not the water rides at 2 p.m.** [Djengu River](ref:attractiepark-toverland/djengu-river) peaks
   then. Before noon or in the last hour you're on faster, and you get wet either way.

When that last hour is depends on the day. From May to October 2026, outside the holidays, the park closed at 5.30 p.m. on weekdays and 6 p.m. at weekends, at 7 or 8 p.m. in the summer holidays and at 11 p.m. on Halloween Nights evenings. On a Wednesday in September that makes it the hour from 4.30 p.m.

![The wooden Trojan horse with the queue in front of it | Troy in Ithaka. The Trojan horse stands at the entrance, the track behind it among the trees. | left](/media/toverland/troy-4x3.jpg)

With no fast pass on sale at Toverland, the order is your only tool against the queues. On a Wednesday in
September you'll hardly need the order. On a Saturday in the half-term it saves you a good part of
the day.

## How to use the park page and the crowd calendar

On the [Toverland park page](ref:attractiepark-toverland?bare) you see every ride's wait, updated every five minutes, and which rides are closed, for maintenance, for the winter or just for the day. During Winter Feelings that second half matters more.

The [Toverland wait-time calendar](ref:attractiepark-toverland?calendar&bare) gives every day a
colour. For past days that's the measured level, for days to come a forecast. The forecast takes
into account holidays in the Netherlands, Belgium and NRW, public holidays, the park's opening hours
and the weather forecast. If a neighbouring region is on holiday, the day says so.

How to plan with it:

- Look at the colour of the whole week first, not a single day. Low on a Tuesday next to Very
  High on a Thursday is perfectly normal at Toverland.
- Watch the neighbours' holidays. If a day carries a German public holiday, it's nearly always
  busier than the days around it.
- Check again the evening before. For a day months away, the forecast only rests on the weekday,
  the holidays and the public holidays. A day ahead the weather comes in, and in Limburg that changes
  faster than your rain jacket dries.

## Frequently asked questions about Toverland crowds

### What's the quietest day at Toverland?

A Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday outside the school holidays. On those days between April and
September 2026, Toverland was Low or Very Low on 27 of 38 days. In winter, when the park only opens
on Wednesdays and at weekends, Wednesday is the quietest day.

### Is Toverland busy during the autumn half-term?

Yes. During the southern Dutch autumn half-term, 17 to 25 October 2026, every day was Very High or
Extreme. From Monday to Friday, waits at the headliners were more than three times as long as in
early October before the holiday. In 2027 the southern autumn half-term runs from 23 to 31 October.

### Do German school holidays make Toverland busier?

Only slightly. When only North Rhine-Westphalia was on holiday and Dutch schools were open, as in
late October and the first Easter week of 2026, queues were about as long as on an ordinary school
day. German public holidays do make a difference: on Corpus Christi, 4 June 2026, waits were almost
twice as long.

### Is Toverland busy at weekends?

On Saturdays almost always: 25 of the 30 Saturdays from April to October 2026 came in at High or
above. On Sundays you queue almost a fifth less than on Saturdays.

### When is Toverland closed?

After Halloween, from 9 to 27 November 2026. From 28 November to 17 January 2027 the park opens for
Winter Feelings, on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays and every day in the Christmas holidays. The
park hasn't published any opening days after 17 January 2027 yet.

### How long are the wait times at Toverland?

On an ordinary school day you queue over a third less at the headliners than on a Saturday. Booster Bike has the longest typical queue over the year, and on most days Troy is shorter around midday than at 11 a.m. Live wait times are on our Toverland park page, and the [averages by ride, month and weekday](/parks/europe/netherlands/sevenum/attractiepark-toverland/average-wait-times) on a page of their own.
