From today you can test the park.fan iPhone app on TestFlight, Apple's programme for beta versions. We're handing out 50 places in the public beta for now. The app only runs on iPhones with iOS 26 or later, the iPhone Duo included. Once every place is taken, TestFlight tells you the beta is full when you open the link.

## How to join

First get the TestFlight app from the App Store. Then open the [invitation link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/W3b54H4U) on your iPhone, tap "Accept" and then "Install". You don't need a park.fan account. Each beta build runs for 90 days in TestFlight, and if you like, TestFlight installs the next one for you.

## What the app does

### Before your visit

The crowd calendar shows you the expected crowds and the headliners' peak wait for every day, months ahead. The day planner puts your rides and shows in the order with the shortest expected waits, walking time included.

![The day planner for Friday 27 November at Phantasialand as a grid: ten rides at the top with 240 minutes of waiting and 25 minutes of walking, below them F.L.Y., Winja’s Fear and Winja’s Force one after another with the walks between. | Your park day, planned around the queues | center | small](/media/app/en/05-planner.webp)

### In the park

Open the app in a park and it greets you with today's best times, the rides around you and your plan.

![The park.fan app’s Start page inside Phantasialand: over the park’s photo “Welcome to Phantasialand” with the opening hours, below it tiles for the park, the map, what is next and the compass, and today’s best times. | Live wait times, right where you stand | center | small](/media/app/en/01-start.webp)

You see live wait times, with breakdowns and closures, and for every ride how its day has gone so far.

![Phantasialand’s park page in the park.fan app, “Attractions” tab: the headliners Crazy Bats and Black Mamba at 35 minutes each, crowds “high”, with their curve for the day and today’s best time. | Every ride with its day so far | center | small](/media/app/en/02-park.webp)

The compass points to the rides in your plan and tells you how far away they are, and "Take me there" walks you to the entrance in metres and minutes. The park map shows every attraction, show and restaurant, and in approved parks the toilets and drinking water too.

![The compass inside Phantasialand: the rides around your position as dots with their wait in minutes, Black Mamba pinned, with the distance and the “Take me there” button below. | Take me there | center | small](/media/app/en/06-compass.webp)

### Lock Screen and notifications

Your lock screen and the Dynamic Island show the waits of your favourite rides and the next stop in your plan. Notifications only come if you switch them on, for example when a ride drops below the wait you set.

### Parky

Parky answers your questions in the app too. He knows your plan, rearranges it if you ask and shows you the way to the next ride. Before your first message leaves the iPhone, the app asks you.

![Parky greets you in the chat: it is Phantasialand today and he has your plan with him. Three questions to tap below. | Ask Parky | center | small](/media/app/en/07-parky.webp)

You don't need an account, you won't see ads, and nobody tracks you. Favourites and plans stay on your iPhone. The app speaks the same six languages as the website. The [app page](/app) walks through every screen of the app in order.

## Bugs and wishes

Take a screenshot in the beta and TestFlight offers to send it to us with a comment. If the app crashes, TestFlight asks whether you want to send us the report. We read both. Everything else about the app, from requirements to common questions, is on the [app page](/app).
