## New

- A star on every park, ride, show and restaurant keeps it in your favourites, shown in a section
  of their own on the homepage (7 January). They live in a cookie on your device for a year.
- Ride pages have a calendar of past waits, one tile per day with that day's lowest and highest
  wait, seven columns on a computer and two on a phone (9 January). From 10 January its legend
  matches the park calendar's: public holiday, bridge day, school holiday, park closed.
- The wait card on a ride page draws the day's queue as a line behind the number (11 January).
- Rides at Toverland, Bobbejaanland, Phantasialand and Walibi Belgium got photos on their cards,
  15 in all, and the homepage hero started showing them too.
- The imprint and privacy policy pages, in English and German (8 January).

## Improved

- The footer lists popular parks in Germany, the USA, France and Japan (8 January).
- Favourites and nearby parks show a placeholder while they load and refresh every five minutes.

## Fixed

- A park you had just added to your favourites showed a distance of 0 km.
- The favourites section and the homepage hero jumped as they loaded.
