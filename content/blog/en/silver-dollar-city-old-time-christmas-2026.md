An Old Time Christmas starts at [Silver Dollar City](ref:silver-dollar-city) in Branson, Missouri, on November 7, and it opens with a new parade: the Stars, Lights & Christmas Nights Parade, twice as long as the one it replaces. The event runs until January 2, 2027, and the parade comes with regular admission.

## The new parade

A vintage car carrying the Grand Marshal leads the way. Rudolph and his friends follow, and Yukon Cornelius and the Misfit Toys are new this year. In the "Home for the Holidays" scene a horse-drawn sleigh rolls past with carolers on board, while children dressed as Christmas ornaments walk alongside. At the North Pole, elves collect letters to Santa, so if you've brought yours you can hand it over from the curb, and Mrs. Claus shakes sprinkles out of a giant shaker. Santa and his reindeer fly above the crowd. Trumpeters, a choir and an illuminated nativity float close it out.

Whether "twice as long" means running time or the length of the procession, the announcement doesn't say. The park hasn't published a route either. What it has named are the two VIP areas, "North Pole" at the Grand Exposition Garden Pavilion and "South Pole" at the Red Gold Courtyard.

River City Scenic built the floats in a warehouse near Cincinnati. The music is by Mike Avila, who has worked on about 20 major parades, among them shows for Disney, the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade and the Rose Parade.

The parade steps off twice on every operating day, at 6 pm and 8 pm local time. Up to 2025 its slot belonged to Rudolph's Holly Jolly Christmas Light Parade, and back then the event ran from November 1 to January 4.

## Operating days and hours

The park is open on 44 days during the event. Until mid-December it stays shut on most Tuesdays and Wednesdays. It's closed on:

- November 10, 11, 17, 18 and 24
- December 1, 2, 8, 9, 15 and 16
- December 24 and 25

On Saturdays and on the Friday after Thanksgiving, November 27, hours are 11 am to 10 pm. On the other days the gates usually open at 1 pm and close at 9 pm, with a 10 pm close on the Fridays of December 4, 11 and 18 and on New Year's Day. Thanksgiving itself, November 26, runs from 4 pm to 10 pm. Breakfast from 8:30 am is only served from March to October.

The park itself says Saturdays in November and December are often very busy, especially when the weather is mild.

## What the ticket covers and what costs extra

A one-day ticket costs $95 plus tax and is good on any operating day through January 2. Book online at least 24 hours ahead and selected days start at $79; a second day within five operating days adds $20. Children aged three and under get in free, and the prices for ages 4 to 11 and for guests 65 and over only show up at checkout. On the four Merry Mondays, November 9, 16, 23 and 30, the online ticket is $79.

Until November 2 the park is selling the 2027 Silver Season Passport for $119.99 instead of $174.99. It's valid from opening day on March 12, 2027, and new buyers get a $47.50 day ticket for the 2026 Christmas season, except on Saturdays and November 22.

Admission includes every ride and show, along with the Marvel Cave tours apart from the Lantern Light Tours. These cost extra, each plus tax and on top of park admission:

- VIP Parade Experience: $45, with a meet and photo with Rudolph, a reserved spot, hot chocolate and a bag of kettle corn, good for the performance you booked; children under three sit on a parent's lap for free
- reserved show seating: Frontier Fa-La-La Follies $15, A Dickens' Christmas Carol and Heart of Christmas $30 each, plus a $1 fee per seat
- Lunch With Santa in Reunion Hall: $24.99 for ages 12 and up, $19.99 for 11 and under
- Christmas Tasting Ticket: $38 for five tasting portions, with the All-Day Mug another $17

Most show seats stay free, first come, first seated.

## Lights, shows and Santa

More than 6.5 million lights hang in the park, 1.5 million of them in Christmas in Midtown alone. A crew of nine starts putting them up every year on July 1, which in the Ozarks is not a day anyone spends thinking about string lights by choice. On The Plaza stands the Grand Fir, a living Shasta fir from Oregon, 54 feet tall. It's lit at 5:15, 6:30 and 7:30 pm in a ceremony that takes 20 minutes. At Joy On Town Square, an eight-story tree runs a light and music show every 15 minutes.

A Dickens' Christmas Carol plays for the 25th time, 60 minutes with a live band in the Opera House, usually at 1:30, 3:30 and 5:30 pm. Audio description is available for blind and low-vision guests. Heart of Christmas in Red Gold Heritage Hall changes its guest act through the season: Lindley Creek until November 27 and on December 3, Jeff & Sheri Easter from November 28 to 30, the Triumphant Quartet from December 4 to 7, The Isaacs from December 10 to 14, and Rhonda Vincent from December 17 to the end. The Living Nativity in the Dockside Theater lasts 15 minutes and plays five times a day.

Santa receives visitors in Santa's Cottage in the Carousel Barn, but only until December 23; after that, as everyone knows, he's booked elsewhere. The press release lists the Frisco Sing-Along Steam Train as a Christmas tradition, yet the train isn't on the park's Christmas calendar, and its own page has the season ending on October 31.

## Which rides run in winter

[Thunderation](ref:silver-dollar-city/thunderation), the 1993 Arrow Dynamics coaster, is in its final season in 2026. The park hasn't announced its last day yet. The wooden coaster [Outlaw Run](ref:silver-dollar-city/outlaw-run), the spinning coaster [Time Traveler](ref:silver-dollar-city/time-traveler), WildFire, PowderKeg and [Marvel Cave](ref:silver-dollar-city/marvel-cave) all operate through January 2.

Every outdoor coaster has its own temperature limit. Thunderation still runs at 32°F (0°C), Outlaw Run needs 37°F (just under 3°C) and Time Traveler 41°F (5°C), which makes the oldest ride the toughest of the three. [Fire In The Hole](ref:silver-dollar-city/new-fire-in-the-hole) is indoors and isn't on the temperature list. If the weather cuts your visit short, the ticket window hands out a free ticket for another operating day in the current season.

## Getting there and parking

The park is at 399 Silver Dollar City Parkway in Branson, reached via MO-76 and Indian Point Road. Standard parking is free, and a tram or bus takes you to the entrance. Preferred Parking for $22 or Premier Parking for $67, tax included in both, puts you closer.

## Built around a cave since 1960

Marvel Cave has drawn tourists since 1894 and is today the largest cave chamber in Missouri. In 1950 Hugo Herschend leased it together with his wife Mary and their sons Jack and Peter, and on May 1, 1960 the family opened an 1880s-style Ozark village around the cave entrance. That village has grown into a park with more than 40 rides and attractions on 61 acres.

In spring 2027 the family coaster Miner's Mountain Express from Mack Rides opens there; more on that in [new theme park attractions for 2027](/blog/new-theme-park-attractions-2027). For Christmas 2027 the park promises 7 million lights and a new production called Christmas In The Ozarks.
