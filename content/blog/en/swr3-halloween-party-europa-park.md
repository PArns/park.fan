[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?bare) and the radio station SWR3 hold the **SWR3 Halloween Party** on Saturday, 31 October 2026. Only guests aged 18 and over are admitted. The event ticket costs €39 and the VIP ticket €185, each plus a processing and shipping fee. The headliner is DJ Jaden Bojsen in the Europa-Park Arena.

## Times and programme

The park is open to party guests from 18:00, and so is the event area with the food court. The party venues open at 20:00 and the night ends at 02:00. According to the event page, payment is cashless.

The event page lists this for the Europa-Park Arena:

- 20:00 to 21:30: DJ MylesMusic
- 21:30 to 22:15: Michael Jackson tribute show
- 22:15 to 23:30: SWR3 DJ BeOne
- 23:30 to 01:00: Jaden Bojsen
- 01:00 to 02:00: SWR3 DJ BeOne

The Europa-Park Dome hosts a silent disco from 20:00 to 02:00 with the SWR3 DJs Michael Leupold, D White and fabioless. Last entry to the Dome is 01:30. "Das Studio" has the NightLiveBand alternating with SWR3 Deejay Bene from 20:00 to 01:00. The 4D cinema runs karaoke from 21:00 to 01:00. Food and drink are at Mario-Botta-Platz, which the event page gives as open from 18:00 to 01:00.

## What the tickets include

The event ticket covers entry to the park from 18:00 and to the party in the Confertainment Center. Neither the event page nor the press release says whether rides run during that time. The event page only states that all attractions close at park closing and cannot be reached afterwards. It does not say when the park closes that evening.

The VIP ticket includes, per person:

- a two-level VIP area with its own entrance, cloakroom and toilets
- a welcome drink and drinks, with cocktails and long drinks from 20:00
- a buffet from 18:00 to 21:00 and a midnight snack from about 23:30 to 01:30
- access to the Arena from 20:00 to 02:00 and a goodie bag

VIP guests pay for food and drink outside the VIP area themselves.

## Tickets and overnight stay

Tickets are sold in advance at swr3tickets.de and europapark.de, and according to the press release also at the box office on the night. Neither the event page nor the press release says how well the tickets are selling; only the ticket shop shows what is still available. Guests who cannot get a room in the resort can book the "Sleep & Party" package at the VOYA Hotel in Ringsheim. It includes the party ticket but not the journey there and back.

## And Traumatica?

The party is its own ticket and not part of [Traumatica](/blog/traumatica-ten-years-europa-park). According to our post on its tenth edition, the horror event runs until 7 November; the party's event page does not mention it. How the other parks celebrate Halloween is in the [Halloween overview](/blog/halloween-theme-parks-2026).
