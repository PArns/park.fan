In December, Universal Orlando starts selling the first annual and seasonal passes that include [Epic Universe](ref:universal-epic-universe). If you want to use one of them at the new park, you'll have to reserve each visit in advance, and the first day you can book is January 4, 2027. The resort announced the overhaul of its pass program on November 19.

Until now, no annual pass covered Epic Universe. From October 24, 2024, passholders could buy a separate day ticket for it before everyone else, and they got 10% off the park's Darkmoor Monster Makeup Experience. Epic Universe opened at around 8 a.m. on May 22, 2025, with five worlds: Celestial Park, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic, SUPER NINTENDO WORLD, How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk and Dark Universe. That gave the resort four theme parks, which is the reason Universal gives for the new passes. The other three are [Universal Studios Florida](ref:universal-studios-florida), [Islands of Adventure](ref:universal-islands-of-adventure) and the [Volcano Bay](ref:universal-volcano-bay) water park.

## Who gets to upgrade first

Current passholders go first. During an upgrade window that opens in the coming weeks, they can switch to a pass with Epic Universe, as long as their pass is active and, in Universal's words, "in good standing". The window runs in phases by pass type, and Epic Universe reservations should already be bookable in each phase. Universal plans to publish the dates in early December.

What an upgrade costs depends on when your current pass expires. If you're already in your renewal window, you pay a difference of $125 to $410, depending on the pass type, and renew straight into a pass with Epic Universe. Everyone else gets a special price based on their pass type and the unused value left on it. Universal hasn't published which difference goes with which tier yet.

General sales start later in December, once every phase is over. There's no exact date for that yet.

## Prices and the new tiers

Florida residents pay from $329.99 for the new passes and from $449.99 for one with Epic Universe, plus tax. Universal will announce prices for everyone else and for each tier from December, along with the sale dates.

Every tier gets a new name. Premier becomes Platinum, Preferred becomes Gold, Power becomes Silver, and the Seasonal pass will be called Bronze. Each tier keeps its own set of benefits, as it does now, and you combine it with the parks you want on the pass. Universal hasn't said yet what Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze get you for parking, Express or discounts. It's also open whether Volcano Bay will be one of the parks you can pick. The water park is closed for the winter until April 1, 2027 anyway.

The UOAP Lounge, passholder-only sweepstakes and Passholder Appreciation Days stay. What's new is that every pass goes digital: entry and benefits run through the digital pass, and Universal will phase out the plastic cards over time, without giving a date. Passholders also become Pass Members, as they already are at Universal's other US parks. The announcement itself still calls them passholders throughout, though.

## No reservation, no Epic Universe

With an annual or seasonal pass that includes Epic Universe, you reserve every single visit there in advance. Universal wants to control how many people come into the park this way. You can't hold more than two reservations at once, so stockpiling a whole month of Epic days won't work. Spots are limited, go to whoever books first and depend on your pass's blockout dates. Universal hasn't published those dates yet, and it's just as open whether the Bronze seasonal pass will get the same Epic days as the annual passes.

I'd put those two reservations on days when Epic Universe is quieter.

## Who the pass is for

The prices announced so far only apply to Florida residents, so the pass mainly suits people who live there, come several times a year and can live with the reservations. For a holiday from Europe, I'd wait to do the maths until Universal publishes the remaining prices.
