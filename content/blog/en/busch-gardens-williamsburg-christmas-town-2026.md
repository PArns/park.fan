Christmas Town opens at [Busch Gardens Williamsburg](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg) in Virginia on Friday, November 13, and this year it brings a new concert, The Queens of Christmas. [Verbolten: Forbidden Turn](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/verbolten), the coaster the park rebuilt this spring, joins the event for the first time, though right now it's closed. Christmas Town runs until January 3, 2027, from opening to close on every event day, and the park hangs more than ten million lights for it.

## Event days and hours

The park is closed from November 9 to 12. Three preview days come first, then a second break from November 16 to 18. From Thursday, November 19, Christmas Town opens every day until the end, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Eve included. That makes 49 days. All times are local:

- Preview on Friday, November 13: 5 pm to 10 pm
- Previews on Saturday, November 14, noon to 10 pm, and Sunday, November 15, 2 pm to 10 pm
- Monday to Thursday from November 19 to December 10: 4 pm to 9 pm, from 2 pm on November 25, and 3 pm to 9 pm on Thanksgiving (November 26)
- Friday to Sunday through December 20: Fridays 2 pm to 10 pm (from 5 pm on November 20, from noon on November 27), Saturdays noon to 10 pm, Sundays 2 pm to 10 pm
- Monday, December 14, to Thursday, December 17: 3 pm to 10 pm
- December 21 through New Year's Day: 2 pm to 10 pm, from 3 pm on Christmas Day and from noon on December 26 and 27
- Saturday, January 2, and Sunday, January 3: 2 pm to 9 pm

If you're coming on Thanksgiving, you've got until 3 pm for the turkey. The park's restaurants are open on December 25 as well.

## What's new

The Queens of Christmas are a trio singing Christmas songs like "This Christmas" and "Santa Baby" on the stage at the Italy Pavilion. Monday to Thursday through December 10 they usually go on at 5 pm, 7 pm and 8:30 pm. From December 14 they play four times a weekday starting at 4 pm, and on Saturdays such as November 21 up to five times starting at 1:30 pm. The concert isn't scheduled on the six Wednesdays from November 25 to December 30. A reserved seat costs $5.99.

Verbolten: Forbidden Turn opened on May 30, 2026, as a new version of Verbolten with animatronics and special effects. The park hasn't announced when it'll run again.

Wisemen, which played Il Teatro di San Marco in 2025, isn't coming back in 2026.

## Shows and lights

Christmas Town Live! A Holiday Special plays indoors at Das Festhaus in Oktoberfest, styled as a 1960s TV Christmas special. A reserved seat there costs $9.99. Gloria!, the Christmas story at the Abbey Stone Theatre in Ireland, and the musical Scrooge No More! at the Globe Theatre in England are indoors too. Once the Christmas story at the Abbey Stone Theatre has started, nobody else gets in. For both shows a reserved seat costs $5.99 and an Insider Tour $24.99, with a look at the theater's history and a meeting with the cast.

Outside, on the stage in Sesame Street Forest of Fun, Elmo, Cookie Monster, Rosita, Grover and Zoe perform Elmo's Christmas Wish, in a modified version on Mondays and Wednesdays. The ice show 'Twas That Night at the Royal Palace Theatre in France, based on the poem "A Visit From St. Nicholas", is due to start on November 27 according to its show page. The park's daily schedule, however, doesn't list it on a single day through January 3.

In Oktoberfest, a 50-foot (15-meter) Christmas tree lights up for the Celebration of Lights, and at Polar Pathway: Spectacle of Lights more than a million LEDs blink in time with the music. Carolers sing in England, and in France the Merry Misfits play with vocals, guitar and cajón. Santa's Workshop is in the Wilkommenhaus in North Pole.

## Which coasters are running

Twenty-five rides operate during Christmas Town, among them five coasters and the swing Finnegan's Flyer. Height requirements:

- [Apollo's Chariot](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/apollos-chariot), 210-foot (64-meter) drop: 52 inches (132 cm)
- [The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/all-new-the-big-bad-wolf-the-wolfs-revenge): 42 inches (107 cm), maximum 77 inches (196 cm)
- [DarKoaster](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/darkoaster), fully enclosed: 48 inches (122 cm)
- [Finnegan's Flyer](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/finnegans-flyer): 48 inches (122 cm)
- Verbolten: Forbidden Turn, currently closed: 48 inches (122 cm)
- [Grover's Alpine Express](ref:busch-gardens-williamsburg/grovers-alpine-express): 41 inches (104 cm), or 38 inches (97 cm) with a companion

Alpengeist, Griffon, InvadR, Loch Ness Monster, Pantheon and Tempesto aren't on the Christmas Town list. Loch Ness Monster and Alpengeist are closed from November 9, Tempesto and Pantheon temporarily. InvadR closes on November 30, and the park hasn't announced whether the wooden coaster runs on Christmas Town evenings until then. The water rides Le Scoot, Roman Rapids and Escape from Pompeii are closed for the season, as are Bert and Ernie's Loch Adventure and Chug a Tug, and the Rhine River Cruise is temporarily closed.

## Tickets and membership

Without a membership you buy a regular day ticket for a Christmas Town date. At the gate it costs $91.99 on those days and $121.99 on the season's other days. All prices are plus tax and fees. The Christmas Town Any Day Ticket, good for any one day through January 3, costs $56.99, or $101.99 with All-Day Dining. If you're coming more than once, the Christmas Town Fun Card costs $89.99 and is valid on every event day. The Christmas Bounce Ticket combines three days of Christmas Town with Colonial Williamsburg, parking included. It's $112.05 for ages 13 and up and $95.05 for ages 6 to 12, and kids five and under go free.

Active Basic, Unlimited, Premier and Platinum Memberships include Christmas Town, as do the College Pass and the 2026 Military Pass. With Unlimited, Premier and Platinum you park for free and have no blockout dates. The Basic Membership is blocked out on November 21, 22, 28 and 29, December 5, 6, 12 and 13, and December 19 to 26, so on most weekends and over Christmas.

Parking costs from $35 a day online, and Multi-Day Parking is $65.

## Quick Queue and food

Winter Quick Queue, one ride per attraction, starts at $29.99 and covers Apollo's Chariot, Big Bad Wolf, Finnegan's Flyer and seven other rides. Unlimited Plus starts at $49.99 and works without limit on the same ten rides, plus one ride each on DarKoaster and Verbolten. While Verbolten is closed, you lose that one ride. The Christmas Town Scenic Pass, at $49.99, promises priority on the Skyride, the Christmas Town Express and the Rhine River Christmas Cruise. The cruise is temporarily closed, so for now the pass only works on the Skyride and the Christmas Town Express.

Santa's Fireside Feast in Killarney, Ireland, starts at $45 and needs a reservation. While you eat as much as you like, Santa reads a Christmas story and Mrs. Claus stops by. All-Day Dining adds $45 to a day ticket and allows one visit every 90 minutes to Das Festhaus, Marco Polo's Marketplace, Trappers Smokehouse, Les Frites or German Pretzels and Beer. Das Festhaus serves a "German Christmas Dinner", which in an area called Oktoberfest surprises nobody anymore. Flavored hot chocolates and deep-dish cookies are only around during Christmas Town.

## Getting there

The park is at 1 Busch Gardens Boulevard in Williamsburg; by car, take I-64 to exit 243A. It's 40 miles (64 km) from Norfolk, 55 miles (89 km) from Richmond and 155 miles (249 km) from Washington, D.C. The airports in Richmond (RIC), Norfolk (ORF) and Newport News-Williamsburg (PHF) are each 45 minutes away at most, and the Williamsburg station, served by Amtrak and Greyhound, is about ten minutes away.

## Verbolten and Corkscrew Hill

In the ride's backstory, the company Black Forest Motor Tours has new operators in 2026, and signs that don't belong there have suddenly turned up along the route through the Black Forest. Forbidden Turn is a drop-track coaster that reaches 53 mph (85 km/h) on more than 2,780 feet (850 meters) of track and drops 88 feet (27 meters) toward the Rhine River. The park has announced Return to Corkscrew Hill for 2027.
