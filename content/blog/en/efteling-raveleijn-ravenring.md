On **1 December 2026**, [Efteling](ref:efteling) reopens its Raveleijn themed
area after almost a year. The area has been completely renovated and comes back
with a new park show, the **Ravenring** attraction, a new inn and a new shop.
The park announced it in a press release on 1 September 2026. It says it will
share details of the reopening celebration later.

## The show

The new show is called "Raveleijn: Battle for the City" and is built on stunts.
Efteling developed it with the French theme park Puy du Fou. The story is set
ten years after the victory over the dragon: during the wedding of Thomas and
Samira, a dark force wakes up and a fight breaks out for the city. The show runs
several times a day, and the park hasn't given showtimes yet.

## The Ravenring

The Ravenring stands in the camp outside the city walls. It's a horizontally
rotating wheel from Vekoma with 18 winged gondolas modelled on the ravens of the
Raveleijn story. **36 riders** sit in it per cycle, and the minimum height is
**1.00 m**.

A lever between the seats lets each rider decide how steeply the gondola tilts.
A ride lasts about a minute and a half, and the gondolas rise to as much as five
metres. At the end you find out which of the five magic elements suits you:
water (blue), fire (red), wood (green), earth (brown) or metal (purple).

Guests with a disability can ride if they can transfer into the gondola. With
the SafeStrap, a safety strap from the park, guests with a prosthetic leg can
ride too.

## Inn and shop

The inn De Verslaeghen Draek serves breakfast, lunch and dinner all day. The
menu draws on recipes and ingredients that travellers brought back from around
the world, and you can order a slice of Thomas and Samira's wedding cake. The
shop In den Ravenkorf sells costumes, toys, stationery and decorations, and you
can have your photo taken there with the heroes of Raveleijn.

## The money and the date

Efteling is investing **20 million euros** in total: 10 million in the show, 5
million in renovating the area and 5 million in the Ravenring.

Winter Efteling runs from 16 November 2026 to 31 January 2027. Raveleijn opens 15
days after it starts, so the area stays closed for the first two weeks. Opening
hours and maintenance dates for other rides are in our
[winter overview](/blog/winter-theme-parks-2026), and the rest of the park is
covered in the [Efteling guide](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands).
