[Universal Studios Singapore](ref:universal-studios-singapore) sits on Sentosa island as part of Resorts World Sentosa, a 49-hectare resort that also holds hotels, a casino and the Singapore Oceanarium. The park itself covers 25 hectares, which makes it the smallest of the Universal parks. Its seven zones form a ring around a lagoon. From the entrance in Hollywood you walk through New York, Sci-Fi City, Ancient Egypt, The Lost World and Far Far Away to Minion Land, which the park map puts right back next to Hollywood.

The longest waits in our measurements are at [Battlestar Galactica: Human](ref:universal-studios-singapore/battlestar-galactica-human) and [Battlestar Galactica: Cylon](ref:universal-studios-singapore/battlestar-galactica-cylon), two coasters built as a duel. Resorts World Sentosa calls them the tallest dueling roller coasters in the world. On inversions the Cylons win five to nothing; Human stays upright the whole way round.

The newest zone is Illumination's Minion Land, which opened on 14 February 2025. I haven't been to Singapore myself yet. Ride details, prices and dates come from Resorts World Sentosa and the RCDB, parts of the history from English Wikipedia, and the wait times from our own measurements since April 2026.

## The rides and shows by zone

```map-widget slug=universal-studios-singapore

```

Battlestar Galactica, Revenge of the Mummy, Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure, Canopy Flyer and Enchanted Airways have all been running since the park's first day, 18 March 2010, and the Spielberg show in New York has been on for just as long. WaterWorld has no return date yet, and the rapids ride takes a break from December. Show times for Trolls Hug Time Jubilee, Donkey Live and the rest, and the slots for meeting the characters, are only in the park's app, which also shows the current waits on the day.

### Hollywood

No ride runs in Hollywood, but this is where the fireworks go up over Lake Hollywood. The Universal Fireworks Spectacular isn't on every night, and its music comes from Jurassic Park, Shrek and Despicable Me.

The theatre runs Trolls Hug Time Jubilee, and out on the street the Universal Singers perform Mel's Mixtape. Hollywood is also where you meet the Sanrio characters.

### New York

On [Sesame Street Spaghetti Space Chase](ref:universal-studios-singapore/sesame-street-spaghetti-space-chase) the cars hang below the track, and inside the building you pass twelve Sesame Street characters. Intamin built the dark ride to a design by Universal Creative, and it opened on 1 March 2013. The spot was first meant for a walkthrough called Stage 28, which was cancelled.

```glossary-widget slug=suspended-dark-ride

```

At [Lights, Camera, Action! Hosted by Steven Spielberg](ref:universal-studios-singapore/lights-camera-action-hosted-by-steven-spielberg) you sit in a hall while special effects let a hurricane hit New York around you.

The climbing game Ready, Set, Rescue costs extra. Tickets are sold only in the park and only for the same day.

### Sci-Fi City

Vekoma built Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon to a design by Peter Clerx, as a launch coaster with two tracks. It's 42.7 metres tall and reaches 90 km/h, and after a minute and a half you're back in the station. Human's track is 982 metres long and Cylon's 971, and Cylon fits its five inversions into a cobra roll, a corkscrew, a loop and a zero-g roll. The park map shows part of the ride sitting over the lagoon.

The trains climb to the top on a lift hill driven by linear motors. Each train holds 16 riders in eight cars of two seats each. The original trains had rows of four; the two-seaters arrived in 2015.

```glossary-widget slug=launch-coaster

```

[TRANSFORMERS The Ride: The Ultimate 3D Battle](ref:universal-studios-singapore/transformers-the-ride-the-ultimate-3d-battle) is an indoor ride where a motion simulator moves your vehicle while the 3D scenes play out around you. Universal Creative, Oceaneering International and Industrial Light & Magic designed the ride. The roughly four-minute ride had its world premiere on 2 December 2011 with Michael Bay. Hollywood didn't get the same ride until May 2012, and Florida and Beijing came later.

[Accelerator](ref:universal-studios-singapore/accelerator) spins you round in cups under a roof. Bumblebee himself joins the band Bumble B-Boys on stage for Bumble Beats.

### Ancient Egypt

Resorts World Sentosa sets this zone in 1920s Egypt, English Wikipedia in the 1930s, and the mummy inside Revenge of the Mummy probably doesn't mind either way.

All 671 metres of [Revenge of the Mummy](ref:universal-studios-singapore/revenge-of-the-mummy) are indoors, and Premier Rides built the coaster. It's 13.5 metres tall and reaches 64 km/h. In just under two minutes the Mummy packs in launches by linear motor, a turntable and a 50-degree drop, without ever turning you over. A train has 16 seats, and according to RCDB the ride cost 20 million US dollars. From 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, the BTS projection Lightscape plays on the facade every evening from 7:30 pm.

[Treasure Hunters](ref:universal-studios-singapore/treasure-hunters) puts you behind the wheel of a vintage car on an outdoor track.

### The Lost World

The Lost World has two parts, Jurassic Park and WaterWorld. The round boats of [Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure](ref:universal-studios-singapore/jurassic-park-rapids-adventure) seat nine people, three by three. Hafema made the rapids ride and Universal Creative designed it. Next to it stand drying booths, the Drying Pods, and nobody puts those next to a ride where you stay dry. Resorts World Sentosa says the ride is closed from 1 December 2026 to 30 June 2027; English Wikipedia gives 31 July 2027 as the end.

```glossary-widget slug=river-rapids

```

The cars of [Canopy Flyer](ref:universal-studios-singapore/canopy-flyer) hang below the track in fours and swing out on the curves. The family coaster is a Swing Thing from Setpoint, and a chain pulls the cars up.

[Dino-Soarin'](ref:universal-studios-singapore/dino-soarin) takes small children gently round in a circle. WaterWorld, the stunt show based on the film, is marked "Temporarily unavailable" on the zone's page. The zone also has Raptor Encounter, the show with Dr. Rodney, and Dolores, an Aquilops who wanders among the guests.

### Far Far Away

[Puss In Boots' Giant Journey](ref:universal-studios-singapore/puss-in-boots-giant-journey) opened on 8 April 2015 for the park's fifth birthday. For about two minutes you hang below the track and pass 23 animatronic figures. Zamperla built the family coaster: 17.3 metres tall, 40 km/h and 428 metres long, with no inversions. A spiral lift takes the nine-seat cars to the top, and 875 guests ride per hour.

The zone's second coaster is [Enchanted Airways](ref:universal-studios-singapore/enchanted-airways), a Vekoma junior coaster that Peter Clerx designed, just like Battlestar Galactica. The track is 335 metres long and a train has 16 seats. Tyres push the train up the lift hill. Nobody taller than 205 centimetres may ride, so basketball players are banned from the children's coaster of all places.

[Shrek 4-D Adventure](ref:universal-studios-singapore/shrek-4-d-adventure) is a film with in-theatre effects, and [Magic Potion Spin](ref:universal-studios-singapore/magic-potion-spin) is a small indoor Ferris wheel for children. In Donkey Live, the donkey from Shrek puts on a show with the audience. Outside you can meet Puss in Boots at Fortune Favors the Furry, Hiccup and Astrid from How to Train Your Dragon, and Shrek and his friends at Happily Ever After.

### Illumination's Minion Land

Minion Land stands on the former site of the Madagascar zone. Page Thompson of Universal Destinations & Experiences says this gives the Minions a place of their own in every Universal park. Universal Creative, Illumination and Resorts World Sentosa built the zone, which splits into Minion Marketplace, Gru's Neighborhood and Super Silly Fun Land.

[Despicable Me Minion Mayhem](ref:universal-studios-singapore/despicable-me-minion-mayhem) replaced the boat ride Madagascar: A Crate Adventure, which ran until 2022, and moves its audience on a simulator. [Buggie Boogie](ref:universal-studios-singapore/buggie-boogie), a carousel with a Minion dance party under a roof, exists only in Singapore. [Silly Swirly](ref:universal-studios-singapore/silly-swirly) is an outdoor spinning ride. Between the rides there are two game booths, the shows Minion Dance Party and Minute of Minion Mayhem, and Gru and Lucy posing for photos.

### Height requirements

- Battlestar Galactica Human and Cylon: from 125 cm
- Revenge of the Mummy: from 122 cm
- Sesame Street Spaghetti Space Chase: from 110 cm and at most 205 cm, with a companion from 110 to 122 cm
- Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure: from 107 cm, with a companion from 107 to 122 cm
- Transformers: from 102 cm, with a companion from 102 to 122 cm
- Puss In Boots' Giant Journey: from 102 cm, with a companion from 102 to 122 cm
- Despicable Me Minion Mayhem: with a companion from 102 to 122 cm
- Canopy Flyer and Silly Swirly: from 92 cm, with a companion from 92 to 122 cm
- Enchanted Airways: from 92 cm and at most 205 cm, with a companion from 92 to 122 cm
- Magic Potion Spin: under 110 cm with a parent or guardian
- Treasure Hunters, Accelerator, Dino-Soarin', Buggie Boogie, Shrek 4-D Adventure and the Spielberg show in New York: under 122 cm with a companion

Resorts World Sentosa prices the day ticket by how busy it expects the park to be, as Non Peak, Peak or Super Peak. Every day in December 2026 is priced as Super Peak. The days from 25 to 28 November are Non Peak, and several days in early January 2027 are Peak.

### Opening hours until New Year's Eve

Hours are out up to New Year's Eve; January 2027 isn't published yet. Until then the park opens at 10 am every day and stays open:

- on 25, 26, 29 and 30 November: until 8 pm
- on 27 and 28 November: until 7 pm
- on 1 and 2 December: until 7 pm
- from 3 to 13 December: until 8 pm
- from 14 to 20 December and from 22 to 31 December: until 9 pm
- on 21 December: until 6 pm, because BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Night Out takes over the park that evening

### Weather on Sentosa

```weather-widget slug=universal-studios-singapore

```

Universal Studios Singapore doesn't close for bad weather, and you won't get a refund on your ticket either. When there's a risk of lightning, the outdoor rides stop until the storm has passed, Battlestar Galactica and Canopy Flyer included. Under a roof you can still ride Revenge of the Mummy, Transformers, Sesame Street Spaghetti Space Chase, Minion Mayhem, Accelerator and Buggie Boogie, and see Shrek 4-D Adventure, the Spielberg show, Donkey Live and Trolls Hug Time Jubilee.

### Christmas, BTS and the 2026/27 events

Resorts World Sentosa celebrates the Season of Joy from **27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027**, and in the park the programme is called A Universal Christmas. The park turns Hollywood Boulevard into the Dazzling Dessert Studios, with a ten-metre Christmas tree that looks like it's made of confectionery. Santa and Mrs. Claus pose for photos with guests, and Hello Kitty and Kuromi appear only at the Superstar Boutique. In New York the park sets up The North Pole Jingle Jam. The similarly named [new show Universal Jingle Jam](/news/universal-studios-singapore-a-universal-christmas-2026) is a separate thing and brings Poppy from Trolls, Sesame Street characters and the Minions onto one stage, with falling snow.

There are fireworks on 4, 5, 11 and 12 December, from 16 to 20 December, from 22 to 25 December and on 1 and 2 January 2027. On 21 December there are none for day guests. That night, from 8 pm to 1 am, BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Night Out runs with selected rides, DJ JAGI and fireworks. The early-bird ticket costs 58 Singapore dollars (SGD) and includes admission. From 16 to 22 December, a regular adult day ticket also gets you into the BTS Fan Village at the Resorts World Convention Centre for free.

Halloween Horror Nights 14 ran on 18 nights from 25 September to 1 November 2026, at SGD 68 on non-peak and SGD 78 on peak nights. During the day, Trick or Thrills ran from 1 September. In 2026 the park had its own Chinese New Year programme from 7 February to 3 March, and Resorts World Sentosa hasn't announced one for the park in 2027 yet. For the whole resort it has announced the Celebration of Spring, from 21 January to 11 April 2027.

## Universal Express and the VIP tour

Universal Express costs from SGD 80. It lets you skip the regular queue once at every participating ride and show, though the Express page doesn't name them. For the first two hours after opening you also get your own lane into the park. Universal Express 3 costs from SGD 60 and covers three rides or shows of your choice once each, with the same entrance lane.

```glossary-widget slug=express-pass

```

Every Express pass is valid only on the date you pick. Online you can buy one only once a park ticket is in your basket, and it's the same at the park. Express doesn't get anyone under 125 cm onto Battlestar Galactica either. And while Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure is closed from 1 December, the pass costs the full price.

The VIP Experience is a guided tour of about five hours in English or Mandarin, with VIP access to at least eight attractions. On the group tour you get a SGD 25 meal voucher. The private tour comes with a four-course lunch, a private character meet-and-greet and a photo package. It also brings Universal Express Unlimited on some rides before and after the tour, a reserved seat at some shows and 10 percent off food and shopping. The VIP tour page says the private tour takes up to ten people, while the park's FAQ says up to twelve, in groups of no more than five. Children up to seven have to come with an adult aged 18 or over.

## A day in the park

I'd start at opening with Battlestar Galactica and then ride Transformers in Sci-Fi City; the Mummy is one zone further on.

Until 30 November, the rapids ride comes before the Mummy; from December it's closed and the Mummy moves up. Canopy Flyer is on the way through The Lost World, and Far Far Away has Puss In Boots' Giant Journey and Enchanted Airways right after it.

I'd save the shows and indoor rides for midday and for thunderstorms: Shrek 4-D Adventure, Minion Mayhem and, on the way back, the two in New York. On a fireworks night in December the day ends at Lake Hollywood. Leave on a day ticket and you're out for the day; only hotel guests with a stamp, their ticket and room key get back in after a break at the hotel.

## Tickets, getting there and food

### Tickets and prices

The One-Day Ticket is for a fixed date. Adults are 13 and over, children 4 to 12. Online it costs:

- Non Peak: SGD 76 for adults, SGD 69 for children
- Peak: SGD 83 for adults, SGD 69 for children
- Super Peak: SGD 89 for adults, SGD 69 for children

The day ticket has no senior rate. Students who book three day tickets get a fourth free. It covers every attraction except separately ticketed events such as Halloween Horror Nights. It can't be refunded, exchanged or transferred. At the gate, day tickets are sold only while they last. Since 1 April 2023 you no longer need a separate reservation on top of the ticket.

Instead of an annual pass, the park sells a Season Pass for SGD 190 for adults and SGD 170 for children. Until 3 January 2027 you get seven months for the price of six. The pass includes priority entry and 10 percent off in the park, plus four SGD 5 vouchers, two for food and two for shopping. Holders can also buy Express passes on site from a separate allocation, while it lasts.

For the Season of Joy, the 2-Park Pass with Festive Perks costs SGD 168 for adults and SGD 148 for children. It also covers the Singapore Oceanarium and includes one Universal Express ride. At Trolls Hug Time Jubilee, Donkey Live and Shrek 4-D Adventure you're let into the theatre first, and you get a reserved spot at Universal Jingle Jam and the fireworks.

### Getting to Sentosa

From HarbourFront MRT station, walk into the VivoCity mall to Lobby L, Level 3. The Sentosa Express leaves from there for Resorts World Station between 7 am and midnight. The ride costs SGD 4, which also covers your entry to the island. You can pay with EZ-Link, contactless or at the counter. Children under seven ride free with an adult, and certain TransitLink concessions pay SGD 2.

On foot, the Sentosa Boardwalk starts at VivoCity, Lobby F, Level 1, with no island entry fee. Part of it has been closed since 22 May 2025 for the RWS Waterfront Development. Sentosa Development Corporation plans to reopen it in 2030; until then you walk through a new covered walkway.

Bus 123 runs every 15 minutes from 6 am to 11:45 pm to the Transport Hub on basement level B1 of Resorts World, at the normal bus fare, which goes up with distance. The RWS8 bus from HarbourFront, MRT exit C, costs SGD 1 there and nothing on the way back.

The cable car runs from HarbourFront via Sentosa Station and Imbiah to Sensoryscape, where you change to the Sentosa Express for Resorts World Station. It runs from 8:45 am to 10 pm, with last boarding at 9:30 pm. On the island, the resort shuttle from 8 am to 11 pm and Sentosa buses A and B from 7 am to 11 pm are free.

The best taxi drop-off is B1 in front of the Resorts World casino, where the approach has no toll. Grab, Gojek, TADA and the CDG app all work. Resorts World books airport transfers on request, and a Toyota Alphard costs SGD 80 per trip.

### Parking

By car you pay a fee at the entrance to Sentosa that depends on the time of day:

- from 7 am to 11:29 am: SGD 6
- from 11:30 am to 1:30 pm: SGD 2
- from 1:31 pm to 5 pm: SGD 6
- from 5:01 pm: SGD 2

That cheap midday slot is a small consolation for anyone who slept through rope drop. Motorcycles aren't allowed on the island and park at VivoCity. Hotel guests drive on free with a booking or a room key.

The car park for the theme park is the B1 East Car Park. From Monday to Thursday the first hour costs SGD 6.50, from Friday to Sunday and on public holidays SGD 9.70. After that, every half hour costs SGD 1.10. The daily cap is SGD 13.10 from Monday to Thursday and SGD 16.30 from Friday to Sunday. The first ten minutes are free, and valet parking costs SGD 3.

### Food and park rules

- Hollywood: Mel's Drive-In, Pops Popcorn Delight, Starbucks, Superstar Café
- New York: KT's Grill, Loui's NY Pizza Parlor, Me Want Cookie!
- Sci-Fi City: Frozen Fuel, Planet Yen, StarBot Café with ramen, Stardots
- Ancient Egypt: Cairo Market, Oasis Spice Café with Indian food, Pharaoh's Dessert Oasis
- The Lost World: Discovery Food Court with Singaporean dishes, Fossil Fuels, Jungle Bites
- Far Far Away: Friar's Good Food, Goldilocks Fried Chicken
- Minion Land: Super Hungry Food Stand, Pop-A-Nana, Ice-Cream Stand

Mel's Drive-In, Oasis Spice Café, Friar's Good Food and Goldilocks are halal-certified. The Super Hungry Food Stand exists only in Singapore. The park prefers contactless payment and doesn't accept NETS.

You can't bring your own food or drinks. Baby food, infant formula and food for guests with special dietary needs are the exceptions, and bags are checked at the entrance. Costumes and masks that cover the whole face are banned, so if you were planning to come as a Minion, stick to the yellow T-shirt. Pets stay outside, service dogs don't. Drones are banned, and so are cameras for film productions.

There are lockers next to the VIP Reception, in The Lost World, in Ancient Egypt and in Sci-Fi City, plus large ones for suitcases at the entrance, all for a fee. There are no showers. You can hire strollers and wheelchairs just to the left inside the entrance. Purchases can be picked up for free at That's A Wrap, three hours after you buy them. At the game booths, two coupons cost SGD 10 and five coupons SGD 20.

## Staying at Resorts World Sentosa

On its grounds Resorts World Sentosa has the Equarius Hotel, the Equarius Ocean Suites, the Equarius TreeTop Lofts, the Equarius Villas, The Laurus from Marriott's Luxury Collection, Crockfords Tower, Hotel Ora and Hotel Michael. The cheapest rooms start at SGD 454 at the Equarius Hotel, SGD 502 at Hotel Ora, SGD 849 at Hotel Michael and SGD 1,017 at Crockfords Tower. Hotel Michael is being renovated right now, and its pool and gym are closed.

Hotel guests can go back into the park, the Oceanarium and Adventure Cove Waterpark as often as they like on the same day. For that they need a stamp, a valid ticket and their room key. From two nights at Hotel Ora or the Equarius Hotel, Resorts World Sentosa sells packages with two adult day tickets from SGD 850. For the Season of Joy, The Laurus offers a package from two nights with Universal Express and admission for two to the park and the Oceanarium.

## History of Universal Studios Singapore

On 16 October 2006, Genting International and Star Cruises unveiled their bid for an integrated resort on Sentosa. The plan included a Universal park with 22 attractions, at least 16 of them rides designed specially for Singapore. On 8 December 2006 the government announced that the consortium had won the tender for the second of the two casino resorts. Ground was broken on 16 April 2007. The operator puts the whole resort at SGD 6.59 billion, built in less than three years.

From 14 to 21 February 2010, Resorts World Sentosa held a preview week for Chinese New Year, every evening from 5 pm to 9 pm, for SGD 10 admission. Not a single ride was running, and the tickets still sold out within two days. From 13 March 2010, staff and their families were allowed in. On 18 March 2010 the park opened to everyone at 8:28 am. English Wikipedia counts 16 attractions running at the soft opening; the 2006 plan had promised 22, and today there are 24. The operator counted more than two million guests in the first nine months. The official grand opening followed on 28 May 2011. The night before, a gala hosted 1,600 guests, Jet Li and Paula Abdul among them.

Battlestar Galactica barely ran in that first year. On 25 March 2010, a week after the start, the ride closed with a technical problem. In August 2010 Resorts World Sentosa named the cause: fatigue cracks in the seat-post brackets of the Human trains, because the loads and vibration were higher than expected. The ride reopened on 21 February 2011 and closed again on 21 July 2013. After a thorough inspection it came back on 27 May 2015 with new two-seat cars.

At opening the park had a Madagascar zone. Its boat ride, Madagascar: A Crate Adventure, officially launched on 16 May 2011. The first Halloween Horror Nights took place on 21 October 2011. By 2015, Transformers, Sesame Street Spaghetti Space Chase and Puss In Boots' Giant Journey had been added. English Wikipedia puts the cost of Transformers at 100 million US dollars. On 24 January 2017 the park welcomed its 25-millionth guest, a visitor from Gyeongju in South Korea, according to Resorts World Sentosa.

From 6 April to 1 July 2020 the park was closed because of the pandemic. The show Turntables, the Amber Rock Climb climbing wall and Fairy Godmother's Juice Bar never reopened. The Madagascar zone closed on 27 March 2022. Its carousel, King Julien's Beach Party-Go-Round, followed on 26 May 2022 and came back with Minion Land, redesigned as Buggie Boogie. Ground was broken for Minion Land the same day, at the time with 2024 as the target.

Genting announced Minion Land and Super Nintendo World together on 3 April 2019. Super Nintendo World is part of the second phase of the RWS 2.0 expansion, and Genting Singapore's 2025 annual report says construction is on schedule, without giving an opening date.

Resorts World Sentosa is wholly owned by Genting Singapore, part of the Genting Group. Genting Singapore runs the park under a licence from Universal Destinations & Experiences, a division of NBCUniversal and so of Comcast. Around 4.2 million guests came in 2015 and around 4.5 million in 2019, according to the TEA and AECOM Theme Index. In 2020 the pandemic brought that down to just under 1.1 million, and in 2022 it was back to 2.1 million. For 2023, Universal Studios Singapore no longer appears among the 20 most-visited parks in Asia-Pacific.

## Frequently asked questions about Universal Studios Singapore

### When is Universal Studios Singapore least busy?

In September and November, going by our measurements since April 2026. July and August were well above that. Resorts World Sentosa rates every day of December 2026 as Super Peak. For a single day, the [crowd calendar](ref:universal-studios-singapore?calendar) gives the expected crowd level.

### What are the opening hours of Universal Studios Singapore in December 2026?

The park opens at 10 am. It closes at 7 pm on 1 and 2 December, at 8 pm from 3 to 13 December and at 9 pm after that until New Year's Eve. On 21 December it closes at 6 pm, because BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Night Out runs in the evening.

### How much does Universal Express cost?

From SGD 80 for one go at every participating ride and show, or from SGD 60 for Universal Express 3 with three. You buy it together with a park ticket, for one date.

### How tall do you have to be for Battlestar Galactica?

At least 125 centimetres, for both Human and Cylon. Revenge of the Mummy needs 122 centimetres.

### Is Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure open?

Until 30 November 2026, yes. From 1 December 2026 to 30 June 2027, Resorts World Sentosa says the rapids ride is closed.

### What's on for Christmas 2026?

A Universal Christmas runs from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with the new show Universal Jingle Jam, Christmas decorations on Hollywood Boulevard and fireworks on 15 nights between 4 December and 2 January. A regular day ticket covers all of it.

### How do I get to Universal Studios Singapore?

Take the MRT to HarbourFront, then the Sentosa Express from Level 3 of VivoCity to Resorts World Station. For SGD 4 you're already on the island. The park's address is 30 Sentosa Gateway.

### Can I leave the park and come back?

Not with a day ticket. Resorts World Sentosa hotel guests can go back in on the same day, with a stamp, their ticket and their room key.
