Christmas Candylane opens at [Hersheypark](ref:hersheypark) in Pennsylvania on November 13, and for the first time in 20 seasons it'll run without Hershey Sweet Lights. The drive-through light show isn't coming back in 2026; the park says it wants to put its effort into growing Candylane instead. So if you've only ever seen the lights through a windshield, this year you'll be seeing them on foot. New this year is the Fire & Ice Experience, where lights and open flames go off in time to a soundtrack written for it.

## Dates and prices

Candylane runs from November 13 to January 3, 2027, mostly on weekends until mid-December and almost every day after that:

- Fridays 5 pm to 9 pm, Saturdays noon to 9 pm, Sundays noon to 8 pm
- Thanksgiving week: Wednesday, November 25, and Thursday, November 26, 5 pm to 9 pm; Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28, noon to 9 pm
- December 21 and 22, 5 pm to 9 pm; December 23, noon to 9 pm
- Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, noon to 4 pm
- December 26 to 30 and January 1 and 2, noon to 9 pm; January 3, noon to 8 pm

Every ticket gets you into both Hersheypark and ZooAmerica North American Wildlife Park. The price covers the holiday rides and TREEville. By the park's own count, it has put up more than five million lights.

Online prices, all plus taxes and fees:

- 1-Day Any Day Ticket: $66.99 instead of $89.95
- 2-Day Any Day Ticket: $50 per day instead of $65
- Junior/Senior Any Day Ticket: $54.99 instead of $89.95
- Family 4-Pack: four tickets and free parking for one car, $57 per person instead of $99.95

All four are valid on any public operating day through January 3, 2027. The park has also announced a separate Candylane presale and more ticket deals, with no date yet. The address is 100 W. Hersheypark Drive, Hershey, PA 17033.

## What's new in 2026

Besides Fire & Ice, [Twin Turnpike](ref:hersheypark/twin-turnpike-classic-cars-presented-by-sunoco-featuring-christmas-overlay?bare) gets four new Christmas cars and new light displays along the track. The coaster [Laff Trakk](ref:hersheypark/laff-trakk?bare) becomes Holly Jolly Laff Trakk for the season and runs with a new holiday soundtrack. The Estate of Evil, a house from the Dark Nights Halloween event, holds a Holiday Open House: its "staff" have decorated the rooms for Christmas, with decorations meant to unsettle you more than put you in a festive mood.

TREEville has grown, and you'll find it at The Shore. You walk between trees styled as Hershey candy, with lights, lasers and fog, and two snack stands sit along the path.

At Mary N. Bright Airfield, Santa poses for photos with all nine reindeer, and you can hand over your wish list while you're there. The Chevrolet Music Box Theatre has the show "Twinkling Thru Time," with Christmas hits from several decades. Breakfast with Santa takes place at The Chocolatier restaurant on selected days between November 27 and December 24. The breakfast needs its own ticket. Nobody in Hershey will be surprised to find chocolate in the buffet here and there, and there are activities for kids too.

## Coasters in winter

The park lists 46 rides for the holiday season, among them the coasters [Candymonium](ref:hersheypark/candymonium?bare), [Wildcat's Revenge](ref:hersheypark/wildcats-revenge?bare), [Twizzlers Twisted Gravity](ref:hersheypark/twizzlers-twisted-gravity-now-open?bare), [Reese's Cupfusion](ref:hersheypark/reeses-cupfusion?bare), Wild Mouse and Jolly Rancher Remix, plus Kissing Tower and Starship America. Monorail, Tea Cups, Music Express and Red Baron close as early as 5 pm.

Whether a ride runs in the cold, in wind or with snow on the track is decided by the park together with its manufacturer, and maintenance can take a ride out too. Under the park's Winter Weather Promise, Guest Services will give you a free voucher for another visit if weather keeps most of the season's major rides closed for more than 60 minutes of your visit. It's good on any operating day through June 30, 2027.

## Fall Weekend on November 7 and 8

Before Candylane, the park opens for an extra weekend between seasons, on November 7 and 8, noon to 8 pm both days. More than 40 rides are planned to run. Six of them are coasters, with all four big ones from the winter lineup. The four tickets above work on these two days too.

ZooAmerica is open from 10 am to 4:30 pm on both days. With a park ticket you can walk in over the bridge in the Kissing Tower Hill area while the park is open. In the Aquatheatre inside Hersheypark, "Our Friends from the Sea" shows California sea lions and harbor seals at 1:30 pm and 3:30 pm. At the Touch & Feed program at 3 pm, you pay $125 per person to feed a seal or sea lion and scratch its back. That includes a souvenir photo, a behind-the-scenes tour and a better seat at the show.

For food, 1906 Grill, 8-Cut Crust and Chickie's & Pete's are among the places open, and King Size Shakes and funnel cakes cover dessert. A 2027 Season Pass at the Full, King or Giant Size tier already works for the rest of 2026, Fall Weekend included. Until November 1, the park promises its lowest price for the pass, so it won't get any cheaper after that.
