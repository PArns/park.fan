## New

- The crowd calendar is a month grid of our own, with month-by-month navigation and a link for
  each month (30 January).
- Days without published opening hours are grey and say so; days the park is confirmed closed are
  red (3 February).
- A park out of season is marked on its card and page, with the date it reopens (31 January).

## Improved

- The crowd scale got new colours and an icon per level, and its middle level is called "Normal"
  instead of "Moderate" (1 February).
