## New

- Ride cards name the best time to ride, from 22 April with "now" or the minutes until then,
  and tell an optimal slot from a good one.
- Where a park has not published its hours, the hours card shows an estimate, marked as one
  (18 April).

## Improved

- Today's wait chart is drawn in 15-minute steps (20 April).
- The weather strip scrolls, with day and night icons, on a glass card (16 April).
- The glossary reached 219 terms (20 April).
