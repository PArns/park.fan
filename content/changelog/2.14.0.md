  Planning several parks in one trip, you can let the crowd forecast pick the day for each of
  them. A ride that closed for good keeps its page, with the date it closed, and its park lists it
  and finds it in the ride search. Map popups show top speed, height and ride time, and the park
  page loads 57 KB less script on a first visit.
mode: published

## New

- "Spread parks over days" on the planner page takes up to 8 parks and a window of up to 31 days
  and gives each park its quietest free day by the crowd forecast. Parks in the same country stay
  together, a switch adds a free travel day when the country changes, and a park whose day is
  forecast high or busier is offered a second day next to it. Days you already planned stay where they are, and
  nothing changes until you press "Accept proposal".
- A ride that closed for good keeps its page instead of answering "not found". X2 at Six Flags
  Magic Mountain, closed on 13 July 2026, shows the closing date, our news article about it, its
  typical waits and its ride profile. Links to it from a post carry a "Closed permanently" badge.
- The park page lists those rides under the ride list, apart from the ones still running, and the
  ride search finds them: typing "x2" on Six Flags Magic Mountain used to return no ride at all. A
  year after closing a ride leaves the park page, and its own page stays.
- A ride's popup on the park map shows top speed, height and ride time wherever we have the figure;
  at Phantasialand that is 8 of 40 attractions.
- The top 10 on a park's statistics page names the area and the ride type under each name, without
  making the rows any taller.
- Inside a post's glossary box, a ride linked from a definition shows its live wait or its status,
  the same chip as a ride named in the text. In the Europa-Park guide that puts a badge on Toutatis,
  The Ride to Happiness and Schwur des Kärnan.

## Improved

- The day planner's code loads when you open it, unless a saved plan needs it right away. On a
  phone's first visit that is 57 KB less script on a park page and 78.5 KB less on the park list.
- An old link to a park page's `#calendar` cost 12 requests and 121.9 KB before it moved on to the
  calendar page. It moves on before anything loads now: 9 requests and 92.8 KB, the same as opening
  the calendar page directly.
- "Nearby parks" asks for its parks' status every 30 minutes instead of every 5, and only while
  it is on screen.
- "Nearby parks" arrives with the rest of the page instead of pushing the content below it down.
  On the Phantasialand calendar page the layout shift score fell from 0.2472 to 0.0058.
- The skip-the-line card on a phone takes 151 px at Magic Kingdom where it took 217, so the first
  headliner row is on screen without scrolling.
- A post without a cover image shows the park.fan pin on a dark ground lit in the logo's colours,
  in every place a cover goes: the article header, cards, the news list and the header menus.
- "How park.fan works" went from about 4,000 words to 2,200 in German and from eleven chapters to
  ten. The trip planner's page explains what you can do with the planner in six chapters, and the
  chapter on groups, which only the German page had, is there in all six languages.
- With "reduce motion" on, the planner's sheet on a phone appears and settles without animating.
- In the footer, the popular parks use the same headings and 44 px tap targets on phones as the
  columns beside them. Their links were rows 20 px tall.
- The entry for 2.12.0 on this page covered five weeks. It is split into 2.11.1 to 2.11.6 and a
  2.12.0 holding only what landed on 20 and 21 September.

## Fixed

- The close button of a side sheet on a phone held sideways was 16 px. It is 44 px now, the same as
  with the phone upright.
- Rope drop badges and the headliner bar showed waits off the five-minute grid every other wait
  uses: 56 of 336 tips and 6 of 7 evening tips on 1 October, across 210 parks.
- The "Backstage" menu sorted posts by their last update but printed the day they were published,
  which on 1 October read 24 Jul, 17 Jul, 28 Sep. It prints "Updated" with the date it sorts by.
- Opening a shared plan could leave wait-time alerts tied to a trip that had expired, and the
  alert switch showed them as off the next time you came back.
