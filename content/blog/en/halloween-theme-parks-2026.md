At the big parks, **Halloween** is a season of its own, about six weeks long, with its own tickets and its own age limits.

> [!NOTE]
> **Dates, age limits and prices as of 29 September 2026.** PortAventura has
> been running since 19 September, Traumatica since its preview on 23
> September, and Movie Park, Disneyland Paris and Alton Towers joined in on 26
> September. Everything else starts in October. Many prices depend on the
> evening and go up as it gets closer.

## The big horror events (evenings, often 16+)

The hard core of the season is its evening events, with elaborate walk-through spook houses
(**mazes** or **haunted houses** in the jargon), scare zones full of live
performers and, in some places, an age limit that’s meant seriously. Almost everywhere, the mazes are _not_ included in the day ticket. They’re booked separately, and on busy evenings the good slots go early.

![Abandoned trailer with wooden crates in the morning fog | A lone trailer in the morning fog. | wide](/media/halloween-2026/beispiel-wohnwagen.jpg)

### Movie Park Germany

The **Halloween Horror Festival** at [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany)
in Bottrop calls itself the “biggest Halloween event in Europe”. A title like that is hard to check, because every park counts
differently. According to the park, the **28th edition**, under the motto “Legends Never Die”, has over **300 monsters**, nine horror houses and four scare zones. In the evening, performers with chainsaws roam the “Streets of New York”.

Exactly one house is new. **Jason Universe** (16+) brings Jason Voorhees to Bottrop and
works its way through parts two to six of the series in 14 scenes. It replaces
Secrets of St. Elmo, which had run since 2020. The other eight come from
previous years, two of them reworked. The **Ahoj-Brause Horror Lab**, still staged as a factory tour, has
been given extra scares and a new storyline around a taste test; the true crime
house **Murder Museum** has a pathology scene. Alongside them run **Blood Moon
Trailer Park**, an abandoned, decaying trailer park five hours' drive north of
Los Angeles on the edge of Yosemite, plus _A Quiet Place_, _Hell House_, _Final
Stop_, _The Slaughterhouse_ and _Circus of Freaks_. New on stage is **“PHOBIA –
The Live Horror Game Show”** in Studio 7, where the audience plays along instead
of watching. Also new are the fire and dance show **“El Despertar”** and a
séance with hypnotist Stephan Nölle, alongside the monster parade and DJ sets
from Max Bering.

Movie Park’s mazes run in a continuous stream.

It runs on **22 selected evenings from 26 September to 8 November 2026**, mostly
Friday to Sunday; by day the park is open as usual from 10 a.m., and the horror
areas start in the early evening. From September the
[park](/blog/movie-park-germany-wait-times-tips) only opens Thursday to Sunday
anyway. The horror evening comes with the regular park
ticket, the **mazes come on top**, and your own costumes or masks aren’t allowed
on any evening, only children up to 1.40 m may dress up. One date I’d put in the diary separately is **17 October**, when the event day
**Haunters Against Hate** runs for the fifth time.

[Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany?full)

Bottrop opened one of its Halloween houses early this summer. **Slaughterhouse** ran under the “Summerween” label every weekend in August,
€5 a head.

Dates, maze line-up and ticket tiers are on the
[official Halloween Horror Festival page](https://www.movieparkgermany.de/en/halloween).

### Europa-Park

By day, from 26 September to 1 November, [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) has a colourful autumn backdrop for families with more than 180,000 pumpkins and no scares. By night, **Traumatica**
takes over, the “Festival of Fear”, and in 2026 it has two birthdays at once:
**20 years of horror and 10 years of Traumatica**.

In 2007, Europa-Park’s Michael Mack and pop singer **Marc Terenzi** cooked up the first “Terenzi Horror
Nights” on the American model. Terenzi carried the event until 2012, then their
paths diverged, although he came back for one more season in 2015. From 2013 to
2016 it simply ran as “Horror Nights”, before reinventing itself as
**Traumatica** in **2017**. Since then Traumatica has won “Best Live Horror Attraction” (European
Travel Awards 2024), the Board of Directors Award from the American Haunted
Attraction Association (2025) and, at the British ScareCON awards, the 2025
prize for its roaming performers and the 2026 one for best scream park event in
Europe.

Traumatica is **strictly 16+**, and nobody younger gets in, not even with a parent’s signature (individual experiences like **THE HOTEL** and the club **THE FORBIDDEN** are even 18+). There are **eight horror attractions**, plenty of performers, stage shows and the club, open for dancing from 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. The preview ran
on **Wednesday 23 September** and the premiere was on **Friday 25 September**.
After that it runs Friday to Sunday until **7 November 2026** (except Sunday 27
September), plus three Thursdays: 22 and 29 October and 5 November.

Doors open at 5:45 p.m., the food court at 6 p.m. and the horror worlds at 7 p.m.; closing is at 11:30 p.m.
Admission runs on tiered passes, from the **Event Pass** (the price depends on
the evening; from €45) through the **Shoxter Pass** with
priority access (from €96, now sold out) to the **Psycho Pass** (18+, all-inclusive, €349). Extra charges apply for the
full-contact experience THE HOTEL (€29), the club THE FORBIDDEN (€10) and a
short horror VR (€6).

For the anniversary the **Vampire’s Club** came back, for exactly four nights:
25 and 26 September (over), 2 and 3 October. On those evenings it takes the place of
THE FORBIDDEN, and Marc Terenzi is on stage there himself.

In fog-shrouded alleys, whole groups of performers hunt down individual visitors, and there are pyro shows.

By day, the [queues in Rust](/blog/europa-park-wait-times-tips) are short for a park of its size.

[Europa-Park](ref:europa-park?full)

### Walibi Holland

[Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) is in Biddinghuizen, about two hours’ drive
north-west of Bottrop.

The **Halloween Spooky Days** are the harmless daytime version, with autumn decorations for children. The **Halloween Fright Nights** are the evening event, in 2026 with four haunted houses, two walkthroughs, four scare zones and two fright areas. **Haunted houses** are the classic walk-through spook houses you go through in a group. The **experiences** are Walibi’s own invention, for tiny groups or single visitors, with performers right up close, and the park explicitly promises that something will _happen_ to you. An experience costs €20 to €25 in advance, on top of your park ticket.

In **The Clinic**, reception looks like a normal clinic, and then you’re
strapped to a stretcher and pushed through the rest lying down, facing nothing
but the ceiling. Flickering fluorescent tubes and mouldy tiles race past above
you, then blinding surgical light, a syringe, the screech of a bone saw
attending to your chest, a heart monitor going off the rails, a panicking
nurse. At the end come the toe tag and the solid slam of a morgue drawer.

Or **Below**, running since 2017 and, according to Walibi at its opening, the
most exclusive and intense haunted house the park had ever built. It starts with you taking off your shoes and trousers and climbing into
a pair of waders, because you descend into a sewer system and wade through
**knee-deep water**, supposedly hunting a vermin infestation. The performers
come storming at you through real waterfalls.

![Performer in a welding mask and leather apron in front of a container door | The butcher is already waiting. | left](/media/halloween-2026/beispiel-metzger.jpg)

**Slaughterhouse**, running since 2022, is a meat processing plant in which
you take the role of the raw material. First you’re
“inspected” in a cattle-wagon-style waiting room, then strapped into a seat
hanging from a ceiling rail. It is modelled on a meat hook; you can’t use your arms, and your legs hang at grabbing height. You get shorn,
a captive bolt pistol is set against your chest, hands are laid on with
enthusiasm, and at the end you get your **stamp**: “quality control passed,
premium meat.”

All three experiences are back
in 2026, along with **The Unhappy Hour** and a backstage tour. Two zones are
new: **Camp Dead End**, a lakeside campsite with a slasher on the loose, and
**Halloween Town**, where the monsters sing, dance and play with fire in the
evening.

In 2026, Spooky Days and Fright Nights fall on **different days** for the first time. Until 2025 they shared a
date, pumpkins by day and chainsaws by night. According to the park, the day
was often so full of people already waiting for the evening that families with
small children were squeezed out, and the performers had to keep switching
between child-friendly and full Fright Nights mode. The **Spooky Days** for six-
to twelve-year-olds shrink from 18 days to five as a result: **14, 15, 19, 20
and 21 October**, all weekdays in the Dutch autumn holidays, which run from 10
to 18 or from 17 to 25 October depending on the region. The **Fright Nights**
get every weekend between **3 October and 1 November**, plus Friday the 16th,
Thursday the 22nd and Friday the 23rd of October. Halloween itself falls on a Saturday in 2026 and is one of them. On those 13 days the park is open from
10 a.m. to 11 p.m., and the first haunted house opens at 1 p.m. The price
depends on the evening, and the quieter ones save you up to €10 a ticket.
Recommended age is 16 and up, but nobody checks.

The trailer “Back to Reality” has been online since 19 August and spends a minute on a pleasant Eddie. The horror clown
presses a pink cuddly toy into a boy’s hands by Speed of Sound, hands out ice
cream and candy floss, poses laughing for photos and dances through the park
until he is standing in a sea of pink plush. Then he wakes up drenched in sweat
in his lair, axe still in hand. “Relax, het is bijna Halloween.” The story
carries on as a show on the Main Stage at 2 and 3 p.m. on Fright Nights days.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fFPGRQAs-Xk

Last year’s spot, “Souls for Sale”, showed
a terrified woman in a glass box, auctioned off as “object 666” before she
vanished through the floor and the glass filled with blood. With **889
complaints** it became the most-complained-about Dutch ad of the year; the
Reclame Code Commissie found it incompatible with human dignity. Walibi pulled
it on 22 August 2025, first temporarily and four days later for good.

### Bobbejaanland

Belgium’s [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) opened its largest spook house
ever in 2025, and it carries on into 2026: **“Paranormal Activity: Next of
Kin”**, built in cooperation with film studio Paramount in the former
“Forbidden Caves.” It has **520 square metres, seventeen scenes and two
floors**, with 14 performers indoors and out. The story follows a
documentary filmmaker searching for her missing mother, an Amish village and a sinister family. That brings Bobbejaanland to
**seven spook houses**; alongside the Paramount house run classics such as
_Wrecked_, _Alice in Horrorland_ and _Texas Butcher_. Each one costs extra,
Next of Kin from €14, the others from €7 to €9.

Halloween in Kasterlee runs on 20 days from **10 October to 15 November 2026**,
but all seven spook houses are only open at the same time from 31 October to
8 November. Before that it’s just the Paranormal Activity house and _Alice in
Horrorland_, with _Wrecked_ added on 24 and 25 October, and the final weekend,
14 and 15 November, goes back to those three. The park stays open until 9 p.m.
on five evenings: 31 October and 2, 4, 6 and 7 November. New this year is the
scare zone **Deadbolt Creek**, a former mining town full of factories and steam
pipes, where a giant mechanical sheriff takes over after sunset.

As the park’s only Halloween offer of the summer, the spook house **Bazaar Bizarre** was meant to run through July, August and the September
weekends as an exception. After less than four weeks the park shut it again on
26 July because too few tickets had sold. It’s back in the
Halloween line-up from 31 October. Bottrop, part of the same
[Parques Reunidos](/blog/parques-reunidos-sale-movie-park) group, saw its summer
haunt through in August. The group’s owner EQT is now trying to sell it.

[Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland?full)

### Toverland

[Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) in Sevenum in the Netherlands (for
many people in NRW the nearest Benelux park of all) expands its **Halloween
Nights** year after year. In 2026 there are **13 evenings between 3 October
and 7 November** (the more harmless “Halloween Days” run on 17 days during the
day), with 170 performers, six scare zones included in admission and six paid
haunted experiences, among them **“Unmasked”**, introduced in 2025, an
experience with its own scare zone. The recommended age for the Nights is 12,
and the park says it goes for atmosphere rather than severed limbs and lots of
blood.

![Performer with a tentacle mask and hand bell in front of the Toverland Halloween Nights banner | The night watchman of the Toverland Halloween Nights. | left](/media/halloween-2026/toverland-cthulhu.jpg)

New for **2026** is **“Entwined: The Maze”**, a **seven-hectare maize field** outside the park, grown all summer
behind the Fenix coaster, with walls metres high and, according to the park,
_twice the size of the entire Avalon area_. It replaces the old rat labyrinth “Trapped” (2019–2025). The park asks: _are you a match for the evil that dwells in the densely grown maize fields?_ The rule is that whoever stops moving gets caught.

Toverland markets Entwined as the **“biggest Halloween experience in Europe”**.
Sevenum is a good hour’s drive from Bottrop.

The Fear Pass is the one extra I would pay for at Toverland. For €69.95
you get one-off, faster access to all six experiences plus your own spot for
the parade that starts every evening at 6 p.m. Bought separately, the
experiences cost €6.50 to €13 per time slot, Entwined alone €13, and each
night has only a limited number of Fear Passes. On busy evenings it decides whether you manage three or all six. For 2026, though, the Fear Pass is
sold out for every Halloween Night; the experiences are still sold one by one.

[Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland?full)

### Walibi Belgium

![Clown in a fez at the knife-throwing stall by day | A clown in a fez at the knife-throwing stall. | right](/media/halloween-2026/beispiel-fez-clown.jpg)

The second Walibi, [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium) in Wavre, turns its
autumn into **“Ibilaw”**, which is “Walibi” backwards. “Walibi” itself is an abbreviation,
assembled from the three neighbouring municipalities **Wa**vre, **Li**mal and
**Bi**erges. At Halloween the park becomes a mirror-image Walibi, built since 2024 around the mascot **Bill** and his “grim kingdom.”

The programme has four walk-through spook houses, **Mine Blast**, **Silence**, the **Aquarium** (Dock World’s old public
aquarium, where something still lurks) and, new,
**The Grand Hotel**, a 1930s luxury palace that replaces The Curse of Amun. Then
there are scare zones like the clownish **Psycho Circus**, running for the last time in 2026 as “The Final Act”, or **Arachnophobia** with spiders.
Every spook house except Mine Blast costs **extra**, around **€6.50 to €8.50 per
house** as a timed ticket; if you hate queueing, take the **Speedy Pass** as a
fast lane.

![Rusted old truck with moss on its bodywork, a rig with satellite dishes behind it | A real Austin, not a prop build. | wide](/media/halloween-2026/beispiel-endzeit-truck.jpg)

Ibilaw has no age limit, unlike Traumatica with its strict 16+; the park only
advises against the spook houses for under-16s. The monsters stay in a fenced-off area between Wave Swinger and
Buzzsaw, the rest of the park belongs to the families, and children under 12
get an **anti-monster button** at the entrance. Ibilaw comes with regular park
admission (dated tickets €46 in advance, €49 on the long days), and season
passes are valid too. If you come on one of the long days with a season pass
or an undated ticket, you have to book online first; only Ruby and Diamond
passes are exempt.

Ibilaw runs on 20 days from **10 October to 11 November 2026**, with the park
open until 10 p.m. on ten of them: 17, 24 and 25 October, 29 October to 2 November, and 7 and
11 November. Besides the Grand Hotel, the new arrival is the scare zone
**Hecatomb**, a tribal ritual right next to Kondaa.

[Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland) is a good
hour’s drive away; if you’re heading to Belgium anyway, put both on one
weekend.

[Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium?full)

### Plopsaland Deutschland

From south-west Germany, Haßloch in the Palatinate is closer than Bottrop.
[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland), formerly Holiday Park,
runs its **Halloween Fright Nights** on ten evenings, every Friday and Saturday
from **2 to 31 October 2026**, until 10 p.m. Two of its six horror houses are
new, **NEXUS AI** and **Lost: Deep in the Woods**. The three scare zones come
with admission, the houses don’t: from €3 a house with a time slot, or €49 for
all six as a bundle. The recommended age is 16, but unlike at Traumatica, younger guests get in with a parent or guardian.

[Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland?full)

### Heide-Park

[Heide-Park](ref:heide-park) in Soltau does both.
Halloween runs **from 10 to 31 October 2026**, with family-friendly décor,
shows and a trick-or-treat trail for children by day. The evenings only get
scary on **five long days** (Sat 10th, Sat 17th, Sat 24th, Fri 30th and Sat
31st October), when the rides run from noon to 9 p.m. and the park closes an
hour later. From about 4 p.m. a scare zone and **three horror mazes, 16+ with
ID checks**, open up: _Parasomnis_ in an abandoned chapel, the _Grand Hotel
Morton_ in 1920s style and _SubTerra_, a masked harvest banquet. Each maze needs
its own timed ticket at €9, or €22 for all three as a bundle.

_Zauberwald_ (the magic forest) is for families with children aged 3 to 10, and _Mission Meeresgrund_ runs in a Halloween version
from late afternoon, recommended from age 10. On the five long days there is a
fireworks show in the evening with a light display on the lake. _Parasomnis_
has extra effects compared with 2025, and about 80% of the attractions run
until 9 p.m. on the long days.

[Heide-Park](ref:heide-park?full)

### Parc Astérix

[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) north of Paris runs its **Peur sur le Parc** from **3 October to 8 November
2026**, family-friendly by day and, on twelve selected evenings, as
**Nocturnes** with considerably more edge: 10, 16, 17, 21, 22, 23, 24, 28, 29,
30 and 31 October plus 7 November, each running 7 p.m. to 1 a.m. on a separate
ticket (€53 online, €58 at the gate). New for 2026 are the fear zone **“Les
Bas-fonds du Souk”** in the Egypt area, the night-time walk **“La Forêt sans
Retour”** and an illusion show in the arena.

[Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix?full)

### Disneyland Paris

At [Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), the
opposite of Bottrop, the **Disney Halloween Festival** runs from **26 September
to 1 November 2026**, with pumpkins on Main Street, the Mickey's Halloween
Celebration parade and villains to pose with, this year including Dr. Facilier
again. All of it comes with a regular ticket, and there’s no maze. On **31
October** the park stays open until 11 p.m., with fireworks, and it’s the one
day of the year when adults may come in costume too.

[Disneyland Park](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park?full)

### Alton Towers

[Alton Towers](ref:alton-towers) runs **Scarefest** on
selected days from **26 September to 1 November 2026**: 26 and 27 September,
2 to 4 October, 9 to 11 October, and then every day from 16 October to 1
November. On those days the park is open until 9 p.m., which is 10 p.m. in
Central Europe. In between, for example from 5 to 8 October, it closes at 4
p.m. and there is no Scarefest. From Manchester Airport it’s a good hour by
car.

New this year is the maze [Final Exhibit](ref:alton-towers/final-exhibit): the
forgotten collection of a man the park only calls “The Curator”. It’s recommended from 12, like all four
thrill mazes. The other three are COMPOUND, Edge of the Forest and Altonville
Mine Tours: Tiny’s Revenge. The mazes aren’t included in admission and run with
timed slots. The Dark Hollow scare zone needs no slot, and for families there
are two walkthroughs from age 6: Trick O’ Treat Town and Amigos in the
Afterlife.

The roller coasters run in the dark, too. In early October the sun sets in
Staffordshire shortly before 7 p.m., so if you stay until 9 you ride for a good
two hours after sunset. The park names
[The Smiler](ref:alton-towers/the-smiler),
[Nemesis Reborn](ref:alton-towers/nemesis-reborn) and
[Wicker Man](ref:alton-towers/wicker-man) explicitly, the wooden coaster that
heads straight for a burning figure six storeys high. Also new at
[Scarefest](/blog/alton-towers-scarefest-2026) is the dinner “Dine with the
Dead”.

[Alton Towers](ref:alton-towers?full)

### PortAventura

[PortAventura Park](ref:portaventura-park) on Spain’s Costa Dorada has been
celebrating Halloween since 19 September and keeps going until **15 November
2026**, so almost two months. At no extra cost there’s the Halloween parade,
Truco o Trato, Día de los Muertos, new characters in the park, the new show
**Resurrection** in the saloon and, in the evening, the scare zone **La Maldición del Emperador**. For horror there are the **Pasajes del Terror**, walkthroughs with performers,
and none of them is included in park admission. New is the fifth one,
**El Carnicero de Penitence** (from €10): in the Western town of Penitence next
to [Uncharted](ref:portaventura-park/uncharted) people are going missing, the
sheriff suspects the butcher, and you walk through butcher’s shop and
slaughterhouse to help him. The other four are **La Isla Maldita** (from €12,
ghost pirates), **REC® Experience** (from €8.90, based on the Spanish film
series and only at PortAventura), **La Muerte Viva** and **Angkor** (each from
€6.90). For more than one there is the Halloween Passport:
Passport 2 (El Carnicero and REC) from €11.90, Passport 5 with all five from
€30.90. According to the ticket page, all five have the same age limit: from 12
if accompanied, and from 14 on your own.

[PortAventura Park](ref:portaventura-park?full)

Gardaland on Lake Garda has a
[new maze](/blog/gardaland-magic-halloween-2026) this autumn that you walk
through in silence.

## Parks without Halloween

### Phantasialand

[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) in Brühl offers **no Halloween event.**

From **14 November 2026**,
**Wintertraum** runs into January (24 January 2027), with an ice rink,
winter shows and evening illumination. The
[Quick Pass](/blog/phantasialand-wait-times-tips) has worked completely
differently since the 2025/26 season.

[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand?full)

### Efteling

[Efteling](ref:efteling) in Kaatsheuvel in the Netherlands even states it
officially: **“De Efteling viert geen Halloween”**, Efteling does not celebrate
Halloween, though there’s plenty to shiver at anyway.

Its **Herfst** (autumn) brings long evenings until 8 p.m. at weekends and in
the autumn holidays, and a dark, gothic core in the Huyverwoud, which the park
itself calls its creepiest corner. That’s where **Danse Macabre** stands, the
death-dancing successor to _Spookslot_, which closed in 2022 after 44 years.
Danse Macabre received a **Thea Award** from the Themed Entertainment
Association in spring 2026. If you have children with you or don’t want jump scares, go to Kaatsheuvel in autumn.

**Danse Macabre is closed for maintenance until 30 September
inclusive, and again from 26 October to 6 November 2026.** Max & Moritz and the
Gondoletta are also down from 26 to 30 October, Baron 1898 earlier, from 5 to 9
October. That splits the NRW autumn holidays (17 to 31 October) into two weeks
with different drawbacks. In the first, everything runs, but the central and
southern Netherlands are on holiday too, and our crowd calendar expects high
crowds on every day from 17 to 25 October, in the first
half of October otherwise only medium ones from Tuesday to Thursday. In the
second, three attractions are missing, Danse Macabre among them, and the
calendar expects high crowds there on every day as well. If Danse Macabre is why you’re going, take
the first week anyway.

Efteling has a [visitor cap](/blog/efteling-disney-of-the-netherlands) of five
million, and in winter there is the Winter Efteling.

[Efteling](ref:efteling?full)

## What goes wrong the first time

1. **Where 16+ is a limit, it is enforced.** Traumatica, most of Movie Park’s
   horror houses and the Heide-Park mazes check ID and won’t let younger
   visitors in even _with_ parents.
   At Walibi Holland, 16+ is only a recommendation, Plopsaland lets younger guests in with a parent or guardian, and Toverland recommends 12+.
2. **Fast lanes are worth it here.** [Toverland’s](ref:attractiepark-toverland) **Fear Pass** or [Walibi Belgium’s](ref:walibi-belgium) **Speedy Pass** pay for themselves on busy Halloween evenings, because a Halloween evening is shorter than a normal park day.

The weekends and 31 October are the busiest, and that’s also the forecast in our
crowd calendar for almost every park in this guide. It’s still a forecast, though, because our measurements only go back to late
December 2025 and don’t include a Halloween October yet. For midweek days and the fringe weeks of the season, the forecast is noticeably lower.
