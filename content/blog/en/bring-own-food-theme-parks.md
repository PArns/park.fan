Before every day trip with kids, the question comes up: can the sandwiches, the fruit and a water bottle go into the park?
The answer sits in the park's house rules, and hardly anyone reads those before they go. We read them for fifteen parks in
Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium and France, each on the park's own site: park regulations, house rules, visitor rules or
the official FAQ. Everything was retrieved on 9 October 2026.

The result is messier than the question sounds. Six parks allow your own food in so many words. Those are Heide Park,
Efteling, Toverland, Walibi Holland, Futuroscope and Disneyland Paris, the last with a restriction. Four more only say
where to eat it, which assumes you've brought it: Bobbejaanland, Walibi Belgium, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland.
Europa-Park, Phantasialand, Hansa-Park, Legoland Deutschland and Movie Park are silent on your own food. Their rules ban
grills, cooking equipment or alcohol, but sandwiches and fruit appear nowhere.

This guide goes through the parks country by country, quotes the line each verdict rests on, and sums up at the end what
the house rules have in common. Prices for food in the park are missing because the pages we read give none. Wait times and
opening hours are on each park's page at park.fan.

## The fifteen parks at a glance

only" means the park mentions picnics or food you've brought and only sets the place or the amount. "Unregulated" means the
pages we read leave the topic out.

## Germany

### Europa-Park

The park rules of [Europa-Park](ref:europa-park) mention catering nowhere. What they do cover sits in section 3: grilling
and cooking equipment are banned (3.12). Drinking large amounts of alcohol or high-proof spirits is banned (3.20), and
anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs can be barred from the grounds (3.21). Large suitcases belong in the lockers
at the main entrance (3.7). A sentence that allows or bans sandwiches is missing.

The park itself names picnic areas on its "Tipps für Ihren Besuch" page, in the historic Balthasar castle park in the
Germany themed area, next to the Wodan Timburcoaster. It's the only line about eating from your backpack that we found on
europapark.de, and it only says where you can sit down. For questions the same page lists the guest service with a phone
number and an e-mail address.

The Rulantica water world has an explicit rule, covered in the water parks section below. In the theme park itself the
question stays open.

### Phantasialand

[Phantasialand](ref:phantasialand) lists its bans in two documents. The "Taschen- und Personenkontrollen" sheet names what stays
outside: sharp or dangerous objects such as knives and scissors, pepper spray and stun guns, pyrotechnics, alcohol in any
packaging, weapons of any kind, plus skateboards, roller skates, inline skates, bicycles, tricycles and Segways. The entry
terms add that detectors are used at the entrances and bags are checked at random.

Sandwiches, fruit, water bottles and coolers are missing from both lists, and so is glass. The alcohol line stands out
because it names the packaging. A sealed beer or a bottle of wine falls under it too. Whether the park tolerates food from
home, both pages leave open.

### Heide Park

[Heide Park](ref:heide-park) answers the question directly in its help center. Under "Darf ich Speisen und Getränke
mitbringen?" it says you can bring your own food for the day and eat it in the park, and that glass bottles should stay at
home. The article was last updated on 15 May 2024.

The park and business rules dated 7 August 2026 add that grilling is banned across the whole grounds. People under the
influence of alcohol or drugs can be refused entry. A second help center article, last changed on 22 April 2025,
recommends own food to guests with severe intolerances such as coeliac disease. The reason is cross-contamination: the park
says it can't rule it out completely when it prepares food, and the kitchen can't cater to special requests in the park.

### Hansa-Park

The park rules of [Hansa-Park](ref:hansa-park), signed in Sierksdorf on 2 March 2026, set out several bans in section 3.
"Essen und Trinken sind in den Attraktionen und in den Indoorshows untersagt", so eating and drinking is banned in the
rides and the indoor shows. Bringing alcohol or drugs is banned. Grilling is banned across the whole grounds, and grills and
cooking equipment have to stay outside. The sample list of dangerous objects names pistols, knives, knuckledusters and laser
pointers, and glass bottles as well.

The sentence that allows your own food is missing here too. What's documented is what rules a picnic out, namely grills,
stoves and glass. The line about the rides applies to every guest, including one who bought the food in the park.

### Legoland Deutschland

The park and business rules of Legoland Deutschland are part of the general terms and conditions on legoland.de. They cover
cooking and grilling, in more detail for the accommodation: private cooking utensils and cooking appliances are banned in
all the resort's lodgings, and food that needs heating can be neither stored nor prepared in guest rooms. For the whole site
the rule reads "Das Entfachen von offenem Feuer sowie die Nutzung von Grills sind auf dem gesamten Gelände des LEGOLAND
Deutschland Resorts grundsätzlich verboten", so open fire and grills are banned everywhere on the grounds.

The terms stay silent on food and drink in the park, on glass and on cooler bags. If you want to know what applies at the
gate of [Legoland Deutschland](ref:legoland-deutschland), ask the park.

### Movie Park Germany

For regular operation of [Movie Park Germany](ref:movie-park-germany) we found no catering rule. The "Tipps für Deinen
Besuch" page describes the restaurants in the park and leaves out food from home. We haven't read the park rules that the
footer links to.

There is a rule for the Halloween Horror Festival, and it's strict. According to the festival FAQ, alcohol and drinks of any
kind in glass bottles are banned. Deodorant sprays are banned too, roll-on and stick are fine. Person and bag checks take
place before entry. The FAQ gives these rules for the duration of the festival.

### Plopsaland Deutschland

The park regulations of [Plopsaland Deutschland](ref:plopsaland-deutschland), last modified in February 2025, deal with
your own food in several articles. Article 17 bans bulk packs of food or drink and allows picnics only at the designated
areas. Article 2 bans camping, barbecuing and picnicking on the park grounds and the car parks. Article 10 bans smoking,
e-cigarettes, eating and drinking in all attractions and queues. Article 8 bans bringing or trading alcohol in the park.

Taken together, that leaves small amounts, eaten at the designated picnic areas, away from the car park and the queue.
Glass and cooler bags don't appear in the regulations, and neither does a size for "bulk pack". If in doubt, ask at the
entrance.

## The Netherlands

### Efteling

[Efteling](ref:efteling) has the clearest wording of the lot. Article 9 of the park regulations is headed "Eigen eten en
drinken" and begins "Het is toegestaan om eigen eten en drinken mee te nemen naar het park", which means bringing your own
food and drink is allowed. The place rule follows: when it's busy, the park asks you to eat what you've brought at one of
the picnic areas instead of in the restaurants and on their terraces. The park's FAQ repeats the same sentences under the
question "Mag ik zelf eten en drinken meenemen naar de Efteling?".

Article 9 leaves the amount, the packaging and glass open. Article 10 treats alcohol and 0.0 drinks alike: the park sells
0.0 drinks to adults only.

### Toverland

The "Practical information" page of [Toverland](ref:attractiepark-toverland) has a "Food & drinks" section with an "Own food
& drink" block. It has two lines. Toverland offers plenty of catering, but you may bring your own food. And the microwaves at
the Waldstube and at Katara Plaza are free to use. The baby care section mentions the same microwaves for bottles.

For everything else Toverland points to its park regulations, available at the entrance and as a download. The "Practical
information" page leaves out glass and coolers. If you want to be sure, download the regulations beforehand.

### Walibi Holland

The "Facilities" page of [Walibi Holland](ref:walibi-holland) says under "Picnicking": "It is allowed to bring your own
food and drinks to Walibi. Picnic areas are indicated on the park." The park rules tighten that in two places. Glass is on
the list of items that stay outside, next to weapons, fireworks, laser pens, masks and costumes, and self-defense items. And
alcohol is banned even if you brought it yourself.

A third rule concerns the place: areas outside the designated paths and picnic areas are off limits. If you want to sit down
on a free lawn to eat, you do it only where the park map shows picnic spots.

## Belgium

### Bobbejaanland

The park regulations of [Bobbejaanland](ref:bobbejaanland), version 02/2026, deal with food in three places. Article 6, the
list of bans, says large food and drink packs may only enter the park with the management's explicit permission. The list
is worded openly. Article 11 bans entering attractions and shops with food, drinks, cigarettes or e-cigarettes, and
instructs: "Picknick kan enkel geconsumeerd worden op de daartoe voorziene plaatsen in het park", so picnics may only be
eaten at the places provided in the park. On the car park, camping, barbecuing and picnicking are banned.

The text doesn't define "large". Glass and coolers appear nowhere in the regulations.

### Walibi Belgium

The park regulations of [Walibi Belgium](ref:walibi-belgium), last updated on 28 January 2026, have their own article 11
titled "Picnics". Picnics are banned in the restaurants and on their terraces and allowed only in the areas the park map
shows. Article 3 bans alcoholic beverages in the park. Bags, boxes and containers over 55 cm wide, 40 cm long and 20 cm deep
stay outside. The rule doesn't name coolers, but the size limit caps them.

Eating and drinking is banned in the attractions for safety reasons and in the queues for reasons of hygiene. Aqualibi has
its own, stricter rule, covered in the water parks section below.

## France

### Parc Astérix

The Règlement intérieur of [Parc Astérix](ref:parc-asterix) lists banned items in article 6.1. They include alcoholic
drinks the park didn't sell itself, objects made of glass, sharp or blunt objects, and any kitchen appliance, "notamment
réchaud, barbecue, glacière", so stoves, grills and coolers. The list is worded openly. Article 6.2 bans eating and drinking
in the attractions for safety and in the queues for hygiene. And it says: "Les pique-niques sont autorisés au sein du Parc
Astérix uniquement dans les zones signalées comme prévues à cet effet."

A picnic is therefore allowed, in marked zones and without a cooler. The page gives no date for this version, only a 2026
copyright in the footer.

### Disneyland Paris

The help page of [Disneyland Park in Paris](ref:/parks/europe/france/paris/disneyland-park), "Can I bring food into the
Disney Parks?", answers "Yes" and names two things. There's a picnic area between the car park and the park entrance. And
meals that need specific equipment, such as a cool box, a table or bulky containers, are banned in Disneyland Park and Walt
Disney Studios Park.

The Theme Parks Rules say the same thing in other words: group picnics with a cool box, stove, folding tables and containers
are banned in the parks. Alcoholic drinks and glass bottles are on the list of items that stay outside. Anyone can use the
outdoor tables of the restaurants, but guests who bought food in the parks get priority. The request not to eat and drink in
attractions, theatres and queues is in the rules as well. So if you arrive with your own food, you eat at the picnic area in
front of the entrance and go in afterwards.

### Futuroscope

The FAQ of [Futuroscope](ref:futuroscope) asks "Is it possible to picnic in the Futuroscope park?" and answers "Picnics are
permitted in all areas of the Futuroscope park - enjoy the lawns!" The restrictions follow. Bags are checked at the entrance
for safety, bulky bags are best avoided, and rigid iceboxes are banned. You leave them in your vehicle, and you can leave
the park and come back during the day. The park has several takeaway outlets.

Futuroscope opens up picnics the furthest, on all lawns and not only at marked spots. The answer contains a sentence that
ends with "Sharp objects, drinks glasses, glass bottles," and then breaks off. The page doesn't show the rest, so we don't
count that sentence.

## The water parks: food from home stays outside

Four water parks have a clear rule, and all four say the same.

| Aquascope (Futuroscope)   | Bringing in picnics, food or drinks from outside is strictly prohibited; only what's bought at the bar    | [Règlement de l'Aquascope, 24 Dec 2025](https://collectivites.futuroscope.com/sites/default/files/inline-files/Reglement_Aquascope_24-12-25.pdf) |

Rulantica has a second rule: drinks without a sturdy container have to stay in the catering areas (3.14), and glass bottles,
porcelain and jars are banned (3.5). And at the Aquascope every exit is final: "Toute sortie de l'Aquascope est
définitive." If you step out to eat, you don't get back in.

## What the house rules have in common

Across the fifteen parks and four water parks, five things can be counted, each with the parks that spell it out.

Glass is banned in six places, namely Heide Park (glass bottles), Hansa-Park (glass bottles on the list of dangerous
objects), Walibi Holland (glass), Parc Astérix (objects made of glass), Disneyland Paris (glass bottles) and Rulantica (glass
bottles, porcelain, jars). Add the Halloween Horror Festival at Movie Park, with the same ban for drinks in glass bottles.
The other pages never mention glass.

Coolers are banned in three parks, in so many words: at Parc Astérix as a kitchen appliance, at Disneyland Paris as equipment
a meal needs, and at Futuroscope as a "rigid icebox". A soft cooler bag appears in the sources as neither banned nor allowed.
Walibi Belgium caps bags and boxes at 55 × 40 × 20 cm.

Grills and cooking equipment are banned in seven parks. That covers Europa-Park, Heide Park, Hansa-Park, Legoland Deutschland,
Plopsaland Deutschland, Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris (there as a stove at group picnics). Bobbejaanland bans grilling,
camping and picnicking on the car park.

Alcohol from your backpack is a topic in eight parks. Phantasialand, Hansa-Park, Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland,
Walibi Belgium and Disneyland Paris ban it outright, Parc Astérix bans what the park didn't sell, and Europa-Park bans large
amounts and high-proof drinks.

Eating in the attractions is banned wherever a park mentions it: Hansa-Park, Plopsaland Deutschland, Bobbejaanland, Walibi
Belgium, Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris. If you buy or bring a sandwich, you eat it before the ride.

## The backpack for a day on your own food

The texts we read add up to a short list. A bottle made of plastic or metal works, because glass is on the ban list in six
sets of rules. The food goes into a backpack or a soft bag. Rigid coolers are banned at Futuroscope, coolers fall under the
bans at Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris, and Walibi Belgium only lets in bags up to 55 × 40 × 20 cm. Stove, grill and
alcohol stay at home.

You eat at the picnic areas. At Efteling that applies when it's busy, at Walibi Holland, Walibi Belgium, Bobbejaanland, Parc
Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland it applies always, and at Walibi Holland you stay on the designated paths to get there. At
Disneyland Paris the picnic area lies between the car park and the entrance, at Futuroscope any lawn counts. At Europa-Park
the park names the spots in the historic Balthasar castle park. Picnicking on the car park is banned at Bobbejaanland and
Plopsaland Deutschland.

Toverland provides free microwaves at the Waldstube and at Katara Plaza, for bottles among other things. If you have a
severe intolerance such as coeliac disease, Heide Park gives you a written recommendation to bring your own food. In the
other parks we read no such line. There you ask the park before you travel. This guide doesn't cover the allergens of the
restaurants.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you bring your own food into a theme park?

Six of the fifteen parks we read say so in their rules or FAQ: Heide Park, Efteling, Toverland, Walibi Holland, Futuroscope
and Disneyland Paris. Disneyland Paris excludes meals that need a cool box or a table. Four more parks allow picnics only at
designated areas: Bobbejaanland, Walibi Belgium, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland. Europa-Park, Phantasialand,
Hansa-Park, Legoland Deutschland and Movie Park leave your own food out of the texts we read. As of 9 October 2026.

### Can you take glass bottles into a theme park?

Heide Park, Hansa-Park, Walibi Holland, Parc Astérix, Disneyland Paris and the Rulantica water world at Europa-Park spell out
the ban in their rules, and so does the Halloween Horror Festival at Movie Park. Efteling, Toverland, Bobbejaanland, Walibi
Belgium, Plopsaland Deutschland, Phantasialand and Legoland Deutschland leave glass out of the texts we read. A bottle of
plastic or metal is fine in all fifteen parks.

### Are coolers allowed in theme parks?

Not at Parc Astérix, Disneyland Paris and Futuroscope. Parc Astérix counts the cooler among the banned kitchen appliances,
Disneyland Paris excludes meals that need a cool box, and Futuroscope bans rigid coolers and has you leave them in the
vehicle. Walibi Belgium lets in no bags or boxes over 55 × 40 × 20 cm. The other parks don't mention coolers.

### Can you bring your own food into a water park?

No, in all four water parks we read: Rulantica, Aqualibi, Plopsaqua De Panne and the Aquascope ban food from home.
Rulantica and Plopsaqua give hygiene as the reason. At the Aquascope every exit is also final.

### Where can you eat food you've brought?

Where the park allows it. At Efteling at the picnic areas when it's busy, and not in the restaurants or on their terraces.
At Walibi Belgium, Bobbejaanland, Parc Astérix and Plopsaland Deutschland only at the designated picnic areas. At Disneyland
Paris in the picnic area between the car park and the entrance. At Futuroscope on any lawn.

### Can you bring alcohol into a theme park?

Mostly not. Phantasialand, Hansa-Park, Plopsaland Deutschland, Walibi Holland, Walibi Belgium and Disneyland Paris ban it
outright. Parc Astérix bans alcohol the park didn't sell itself, and Europa-Park bans large amounts and high-proof drinks.
Phantasialand names alcohol in any packaging.

### Can you grill in a theme park?

No. Grills and cooking equipment are banned at Europa-Park, Heide Park, Hansa-Park, Legoland Deutschland, Plopsaland
Deutschland, Parc Astérix and Disneyland Paris. Bobbejaanland bans grilling, camping and picnicking on the car park. If you
want to picnic, you do it cold, at the designated areas.

### Where can you find a park's rule?

In the house rules, which are called park regulations, Parkordnung, park rules or Règlement intérieur depending on the park,
and often in the FAQ. The links to all the sources are in the table at the top and in the list below.

## What's missing from this guide

The selection ends at fifteen parks in four countries. Austria and Switzerland are missing. Even among the parks we read,
not all have written their catering rule down publicly, which affects five rows of the table. At Movie Park we couldn't read
the park rules themselves, only the page on the Halloween Horror Festival and the visitor tips.

We give no prices for food and drink in the park because none of the texts we read states any. For allergies and intolerances
the same holds as for the rules: the park answers them, not this guide. If you want to be sure before you travel, ask the
park's guest service and get the answer in writing.
