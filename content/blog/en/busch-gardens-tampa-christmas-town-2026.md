Christmas Town opens at [Busch Gardens Tampa Bay](ref:busch-gardens-tampa) in Florida on Friday, November 13, and this year it comes with a new drone show. The acrobatics show A Very Merry Cirque is new as well. The event runs until January 4, 2027, though not every day, and on event days it lasts from park opening to close. It's included with regular admission.

## New this year

The drone show is called Lights of Wonder, presented by GEICO. The drones fly to classic Christmas songs above Festival Field, whose entrance is near Pass Member Landing, on some event days through December 31. On New Year's Eve the park puts on new fireworks, best seen from Festival Field as well.

A Very Merry Cirque plays on some event days between November and January, on a stage Busch Gardens hasn't named yet. There's also a new Christmas Town Market for shopping.

## Event days and hours in November

Christmas Town runs on 14 days in November. On each of them the park opens at 10 am:

- Friday, November 13, and Sunday, November 15: until 9 pm
- Saturday, November 14: until 10 pm
- Thursday, November 19: until 8 pm
- Friday, November 20, through Thursday, November 26: until 9 pm, with a Thanksgiving Feast on Thanksgiving Day (the 26th)
- Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28: until 10 pm
- Sunday, November 29: until 9 pm

There's no Christmas program from November 16 to 18 or on November 30.

On opening night, radio station Mix 100.7 broadcasts live from Cheetah Hunt Plaza between 5 and 7 pm with Miguel & Holly and Brian Fink, and the Christmas tree there lights up at 7.

## What else is on

Christmas on Ice plays the Moroccan Palace Theater for the whole event, with figure skaters, which in Tampa's climate counts as a minor achievement all by itself. Seating is first come, first served, and if the theater fills up, latecomers can find the doors shut before the show has even started.

Outside Serengeti Overlook Restaurant, thousands of lights move to music in the Serengeti Light Show. Across the park, almost 70 miles (about 113 km) of lights hang for the Celebration of Lights, with stilt walkers in between. In the evening the Holly Jolly Express leaves from [Nairobi Station](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/serengeti-express-nairobi-station): a train with Christmas songs and views over the 65-acre (26-hectare) savanna where the giraffes, rhinos and zebras live.

On some nights in November and December the Ginja Ninjas breakdance on Jungala Plaza, weather permitting, to a story about Mrs. Claus's missing cookie recipe.

The Three Kings Journey at Dragon Fire Grill runs until January 4. The park's show page has it starting on November 21, its event page on December 21. From December 4 to 12, the menorah between the Moroccan Palace and the cheetah habitat is lit every evening at 6 pm. On December 17 the Coca-Cola Holiday Caravan stops on Festival Field with a free sample while supplies last.

## For kids

Elmo's Christmas Wish starts ahead of the event, on November 6, and plays the Sunny Day Theater in Sesame Street Safari of Fun until January 4. On some days you can take photos with the Sesame Street characters at 123 Smile.

Festival Walkway has life-size scenes from the Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer picture book until December 24, and you'll meet Rudolph and Bumble there. Santa poses for photos with kids in his study at Santa's North Pole Experience until the same day. Mrs. Claus reads stories at the Canopy and the princesses Ice, Sugar Plum and Poinsettia walk the park right up to the last event day.

## What costs extra

The Welcome Center and the holiday food stands open at 4 pm on event nights. A sampler lanyard with 5 or 10 tastings starts at $45, and pass members get 12 tastings for the price of 10. New this year is a $35 dessert sampler with a hot chocolate in a souvenir mug and four desserts, valid for the whole event. The Savory Sleigh hut serves braised short rib, pumpkin soup and eggnog bread pudding, among other things, and Holiday Spice has a beef Wellington.

The Rudolph & Friends Character Dine costs $42.99. Two VIP tours last four hours each:

- Holly VIP Tour, private, $150: front-of-line access to Santa, reserved seats at Christmas on Ice, a flamingo feeding and a meal
- Jolly VIP Tour, guided, $125: bites at the huts, front-of-line access on some coasters, a giraffe feeding on the Serengeti Safari and reserved seats at Christmas on Ice

## Tickets and parking

In the Black Friday presale a day ticket starts at $71.99 instead of the regular $150.99, or $111.99 with All-Day Dining. The Ultimate Bundle includes unlimited Quick Queue, except on [Iron Gwazi](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/iron-gwazi) and [Phoenix Rising](ref:busch-gardens-tampa/phoenix-rising-now-open). The 2027 Fun Card costs $89.99 and is good for unlimited visits until December 31, 2027, blockout dates aside, with the rest of 2026 thrown in for free. Parking starts at $35 plus tax.

Busch Gardens belongs to SeaWorld Parks & Entertainment. A two-park ticket with SeaWorld Orlando starts at $93.99 for both parks, as long as both visits fall within six months of purchase.
