# A wait time is never typed into a post (REQUIREMENT)

Every table of wait times in a post is a widget fence, `ride-waits-widget` or
`hourly-profile-widget`, never typed numbers. What stays typed is what does not move: a coaster's
label and build type are stable facts, the minutes never are.

- **A sentence next to a widget must not name a figure the widget renders.** Write the shape („gut
  eine halbe Stunde", „ab Mittag etwa die Hälfte"), which stays true while the number moves.
- **A table's order is its claim**: a park's ranking is ordered by the data, a hand-picked list keeps
  the author's order because the sentence under it depends on it, and only the hand-picked list
  applies a 60-day floor, because two numbers side by side invite subtracting them.

## Why

Four articles carried 22 hand-maintained tables across six languages, Europa-Park's 8 × 10 hour
matrix among them, eighty numbers per locale. They had already drifted with nothing to detect it: the
Efteling post said 34 minutes for Joris en de Draak where the park page said 35, and two tables in
one Toverland post disagreed by a minute because they were typed a week apart.
